For shipped orders, we hand your parcel to the carrier within two business days after payment, Monday through Friday, excluding holidays. Tracking is added to your eBay order. Please see each listing for available services, shipping charges, and the estimated delivery date; handling time is separate from carrier transit time.

For local-pickup listings, arrange collection through eBay Messages. The listing identifies the pickup area. Pickup items are not offered for parcel delivery unless the listing specifically includes that service.

Buying more than one item? Message us through eBay before checkout to ask whether the pieces can be packed safely together. Combined shipping depends on the items, destination, and available checkout options. Please do not assume that every order can be combined.

Where eBay International Shipping is offered, the checkout and order details explain the international service and charges.
