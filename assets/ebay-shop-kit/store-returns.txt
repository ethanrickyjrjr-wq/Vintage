We accept returns within 30 days of delivery or collection. Start your return from your eBay purchase history and follow the instructions and return address provided there.

For a change of mind, the buyer pays return shipping or arranges the return of a local-pickup item. Please return the item in the condition received, with its included parts and accessories.

If an item arrives damaged, faulty, incorrect, or not as described, we cover return shipping and resolve the return through eBay. The applicable eBay Money Back Guarantee remains in effect.

For orders handled by eBay International Shipping, use the return process in your eBay order; eBay manages those returns under its program terms.
