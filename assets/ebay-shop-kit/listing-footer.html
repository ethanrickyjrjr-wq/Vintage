THE DETAILS MATTER

Please read the item details and measurements and look through all photos for condition and signs of wear. Have a question? Send us a message through eBay; we're happy to help.

SHIPPING & PICKUP

Shipped orders go to the carrier within two business days after payment, Monday through Friday, excluding holidays. Tracking is added to your eBay order. Shipping costs and estimated delivery dates appear in the listing. For items offered with local pickup, collection is arranged through eBay Messages.

RETURNS

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery or collection. Buyers pay return shipping for a change of mind. If an item arrives damaged, faulty, incorrect, or not as described, we cover return shipping. Start a return from your eBay purchase history. For orders handled by eBay International Shipping, follow the return instructions in your order.
