moonwalk_reference.webm is the original silent Commons excerpt.
moonwalk-driver.mp4 is trimmed to 4.5 seconds starting at 1.25 seconds,
cropped to 480x480 at x=80,y=0, converted to 24 fps and H.264.
The reference is used to drive an AI mannequin animation. Credit does not
imply endorsement. Keep this attribution when sharing derivatives.

moonwalk-driver-side.mp4 uses source seconds 2.0-5.6, slowed by 1.25,
the same square crop, and conversion to 24 fps. No optical flow is used.

wan-animate-modular-raw.mp4 is the model's untouched continuous output.
mustache-moonwalk-draft.mp4 is a remux with title and source metadata.
mustache-moonwalk-draft.gif is resized to 480 pixels with palette
optimization at about 15 fps and infinite repeat. These are drafts:
mustache visibility and the loop seam have not passed visual review.

Character references and START_HERE_CODEX.md came from Ricky's handoff:
https://drive.google.com/file/d/1UjY-oTTxzwx1YkAaVnI4lAr2vL3FESeJ/view
The old rejected GIF in that ZIP is deliberately not copied or used.

November 8 refinement inputs:
mannequin_full_body_wide.png is a built-in ImageGen edit of the original
full-body composite, supported by mannequin_reference.jpeg and pants_reference.jpeg.
It widens the neon room and preserves the mustached mannequin and outfit.
The complete edit prompt is in wide-reference-prompt.txt.
moonwalk-driver-facing.mp4 edits the continuous RichKaz driving footage:
the mannequin head is composited at manually keyed neck positions, the
shot is scaled to 80 percent with margins, and playback is slowed to
108 frames / 24 fps / 4.5 seconds. No new motion frames are interpolated.
driver-facing-tracking.json records the source, positions and edits.

Finished shareable files, November 8:
mustache-moonwalk-final.mp4 and mustache-moonwalk-final.gif use actual
continuous generated frames from candidates/local-20261108T170457Z/candidate.mp4.
Model: Wan-AI/Wan2.2-Animate-2-14B-Distilled-Diffusers, revision
59e4141466bcb1bf9733eca1bc78be6891c9fbdf, run locally in NF4, six steps,
seed 57, 384 square, conditioned on the widened reference and facing driver.
The untouched model output is 4.5 seconds / 108 frames / 24 fps.
Finishing uses source frames 46-97, u2netp character mattes, normalized
head/foot framing, a fixed room, a soft reflection and a four-frame
character-only dissolve. The resulting two-second cycle repeats twice
in the four-second MP4 and GIF. No pose-grid animation, reverse bounce
or optical-flow frame synthesis is used.
disco-background.png is a built-in ImageGen edit removing the mannequin
from the widened room. Its complete prompt is in background-plate-prompt.txt.
loop-finishing.json and final-verification.json record the finishing and checks.
