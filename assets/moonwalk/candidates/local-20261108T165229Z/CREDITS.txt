moonwalk_reference.webm is the original silent Commons excerpt.
moonwalk-driver.mp4 is trimmed to 4.5 seconds starting at 1.25 seconds,
cropped to 480x480 at x=80,y=0, converted to 24 fps and H.264.
The reference is used to drive an AI mannequin animation. Credit does not
imply endorsement. Keep this attribution when sharing derivatives.

moonwalk-driver-side.mp4 uses source seconds 2.0-5.6, slowed by 1.25,
the same square crop, and conversion to 24 fps. No optical flow is used.

wan-animate-modular-raw.mp4 is the model's untouched continuous output.
mustache-moonwalk-draft.mp4 is a remux with title and source metadata.
mustache-moonwalk-draft.gif is resized to 480 pixels with palette
optimization at about 15 fps and infinite repeat. These are drafts:
mustache visibility and the loop seam have not passed visual review.
