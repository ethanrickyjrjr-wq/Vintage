## Frequently asked questions
#### What's a credit?
**AI credits** power Photoroom's most advanced AI tools, like video ads, product videos, model shots, AI backgrounds and edits with AI. The number shown in your plan is your monthly AI credit pool, shared across your Space and renewed each billing cycle. Different tools consume different amounts of credits, with simpler generations using fewer credits and more advanced ones using more. The monthly credit amount included with your plan may vary depending on your country or region.
#### What videos can I make with Photoroom?
Video ads and product videos. Video Ad Maker turns your product photos into video ads with actors, music and voiceover, ready to run on Meta, TikTok and YouTube. You can also animate your product photos into product videos for your product pages and listings. Both are included in Max and Ultra and use your plan's credits. A video ad uses more credits than a product video.
#### What counts as an export?
Exports include every image you save from Photoroom, whether from the editor, Batch, or a Shopify sync. Each image counts as 1 export, and the number shown in your plan is your monthly export allowance, shared across all export methods and renewed each billing cycle. API usage isn't included: Pro, Max and Ultra don't include any API allowance, and the API has its own plans. Export limits apply to visual assets up to 2K resolution.
#### What's the difference between Core tools and Credit AI tools?
Core tools like Background Remover, Shadows (Presets), Product Fixer, Retouch, AI Fill, AI Expand/Resize, and Image Enhancer, are included on every paid plan with no credit cost. Use them as much as you need within fair-use limits.
Credit AI tools for video ads, product videos, model shots, AI backgrounds and edits with AI, draw from your AI credit pool because they're more resource-intensive to run.
#### What happens if I exceed my export limit?
You'll get a notification at 80% and again at 100% of your monthly quota. Once you hit your limit, exports pause until your next billing cycle or you can upgrade to a higher plan anytime to unlock more.
#### Can I change my credit tier mid-month?
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately: you get the new plan's credits right away, and your billing cycle resets to that date. Downgrades take effect on your next renewal — you can keep using your current plan's credits until then.
#### Do I need an API plan?
Only if you're automating Photoroom into your own workflow; most users can ignore this. The API has three plans of its own: Basic (Remove Background API), Plus (Image Editing API) and Enterprise.
#### Do Photoroom app plans include API access?
No. Pro, Max and Ultra subscriptions don't include any API allowance. If you need both, you can subscribe to a self-serve app plan and a self-serve API plan in parallel.
On Photoroom's new Enterprise pricing, you get a committed wallet in EUR, USD or GBP that you can spend across both apps and APIs.
#### What payment methods do you accept?
You can exclusively use your credit card for payments on desktop. However, on the mobile app, payments are processed through the payment method you've established on either the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.
#### Will I be charged VAT/GST?
No, VAT/GST is already included.
#### How do I cancel my Photoroom subscription or trial?
Go to your account settings in the app and click on “Manage Subscriptions”. You can cancel your subscription (Pro, Max, Ultra or API) with one click.
#### Can I upgrade or downgrade my subscription?
Navigate to your account settings within the app and select 'Manage Subscriptions.' From there, you can modify your subscription.
#### What is your refund policy?
We refund customers up to one week after their purchase. Depending on the platform you initially used to subscribe, you'll get a different way to request a refund. Please find all the information in our guide on “[How to get a refund](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/8370245-request-a-refund)”.
#### Can I upgrade or downgrade my API subscription?
Yes, you can cancel, upgrade or downgrade your subscription at any time.
  
Cancel: If you cancel, your subscription will stop at the end of your current billing period. Your remaining credits will be preserved for 6 months. You can use them if you subscribe again.  
  
Upgrade: Upgrading your plan takes effect immediately: after confirmation, your payment method is charged, you immediately get the credits from the larger plan, and your renewal date gets updated to the current date. For instance, when upgrading from 1000 credits/month to 5000 credits/month, you immediately get 5000 credits and your plan will renew in exactly one month (unless you cancel).
  
Downgrade: If you're downgrading your plan you will not be billed immediately. Instead, your current plan stays active until the end of the current billing period, and only then does the new plan take effect. For example, when you downgrade your 10000 credits per month plan to the 5000 credits per month on June 10, and your next billing date is July 5, then you will be billed for it on July 5. This is also when the new rollover limit applies, so make sure you use the remaining credits until then.

## Frequently asked questions
#### What is usage-based Enterprise pricing?
Enterprise gives you one shared annual balance across Photoroom apps and API. You pay for the exports and AI operations you use, with negotiated pricing for Visual Agents configured for your business.
#### Can I use my Enterprise balance across Photoroom apps and API?
Yes. Your Enterprise balance is shared across Photoroom web, mobile apps and API, so you don’t need separate plans or budgets for each setup.
#### What counts as usage?
Usage is based on exports and AI operations. Cleanup tasks such as background removal, basic shadows, retouching, resizing and upscaling are included in the export fee, while generative AI and premium models have an additional operation fee.
#### How is Visual Agents pricing calculated?
Visual Agents configured for your business can have a negotiated price based on the workflow they run. Usage outside your configured Visual Agents is charged from the same Enterprise balance at standard rates.
#### What happens if I use my full Enterprise balance?
Your workflows won’t automatically stop when you reach your committed balance, so production can continue without interruption. You can add more budget through your account team when needed.
#### Can I track my Enterprise usage?
Yes. Enterprise admins can see their current balance and track usage, giving you visibility into how your budget is being used across Photoroom.
#### Is Enterprise billed monthly or annually?
Enterprise is based on an annual contract, with your committed balance funded at the start of the contract.
#### Which currencies does Enterprise support?
Enterprise contracts can be funded in USD, EUR or GBP.
#### How is Enterprise different from an Ultra subscription?
Ultra is designed for individuals and teams using Photoroom’s apps. Enterprise adds a shared balance across apps and API, tailored Visual Agents, dedicated onboarding and support, and Enterprise-level security and SLAs.
#### What is outcome-based pricing with Enterprise Guarantee?
For eligible workflows, outcome-based pricing lets you agree on product fidelity criteria upfront and pay for accepted outputs that meet them. Photoroom checks the outputs and handles retries and fixes when they don’t pass.
#### Who is eligible for Enterprise Guarantee?
Enterprise Guarantee is initially designed for eligible Food and Fashion businesses on annual Enterprise contracts. We test the workflow on your product visuals and agree on clear pass/fail criteria before the guarantee starts.
