Official pages read during this session:

- [Business policies](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/business-policies/business-policies?id=4212): reusable payment, shipping and return settings; edits can propagate to linked listings; existing policies can be selected on mobile.
- [Store customization](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/ebay-stores/manage-ebay-store?id=4090): logo, billboard, About copy, policies and Store sections. Image dimensions and character limits informed the kit.
- [Store plans](https://www.ebay.com/sellercenter/selling/how-to-sell/set-up-your-store) and [fees](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/store-selling-fees?id=4809): US Starter and Basic plan prices; category and tier differences matter.
- [Handling time](https://www.ebay.com/sellercenter/shipping/preparing-your-shipment/handling-time): carrier scan, not merely label creation, ends handling time.
- [Return shipping](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/managing-returns-refunds/return-shipping-for-sellers?id=4703) and [handling returns](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/managing-returns-refunds/handling-return-requests?id=4115): responsibility depends on the reason; native return deadlines still apply.
- [Local pickup](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/posting-items/setting-postage-options/local-pickup?id=4181): payment, private collection arrangements and pickup confirmation codes.
- [eBay International Shipping](https://www.ebay.com/sellercenter/shipping/ebay-international-shipping): program handling differs from self-managed international shipping and returns.
- [Shipping discounts](https://www.ebay.com/help/ship-track/ship-track-selling/ebay-postage-discounts?id=4168): calculated combination rules require application to listings.
- [Selling templates](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/selling-tools/selling-templates?id=4097): Seller Hub supports reusable templates.
- [Messages](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/selling-tools/schedule-time-away?id=4194) and [Time Away](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/selling-tools/time-away?id=5137): folders, reviewed AI suggestions and separate away response/sales settings.
- [Feedback policy](https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/feedback-policies/feedback-manipulation-policy?id=4231) and [links policy](https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/listing-policies/links-policy?id=4248): no feedback references in descriptions, no positive-feedback incentives, and restrictions on off-eBay links.
- [Top Rated criteria](https://export.ebay.com/en/growth/seller-performance/top-rated-seller/): the approved baseline should not be presented as qualifying for Top Rated Plus.

The policy names, carrier starting choices, manual combined-shipping review, response targets and Starter monthly recommendation are our operating recommendations. They are not observations of account settings. The implementation and copy live in [the setup guide](../docs/ebay-setup.md) and `assets/ebay-shop-kit/`.
