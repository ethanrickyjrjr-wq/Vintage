#  About Us 
Bluebox is the premier online marketplace for resellers, offering access to a unique selection of used, surplus, or discontinued inventory.  
  
Our products are carefully sourced to ensure that items without a home find their second life, preventing them from becoming environmentally damaging waste or economically taxing exports.  
  
Our mission is to serve resellers and small businesses in search of exceptional deals on a wide range of products, including jewelry, clothing, shoes, and more. We aim to be the definitive resource for sourcing resale inventory, providing an affordable and sustainable shopping solution from the comfort of your home or business.  
  
At Bluebox, we are driven by a commitment to sustainability and community support. Each purchase not only offers you access to great deals but also contributes to a broader mission. Every purchase made through Bluebox directly contributes to Gulfstream Goodwill Industries’ efforts to make a significant impact within our community. The proceeds are instrumental in funding our wide range of initiatives, including job training, housing, and educational programs. These efforts are designed to empower individuals, foster economic development, and promote environmental sustainability.
