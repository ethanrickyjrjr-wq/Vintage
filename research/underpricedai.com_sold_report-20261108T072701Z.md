## What condition does to price
On the same item, used sold for a median 81% of new. For the middle half of items it was 61% to 111%, and on 31% of items used sold for more than new.
From 20,653 eBay US searches with at least 5 new and 5 used sales in the last 180 days. The spread is wide, so check your own item's sales.
## How this report is made
Figures come from completed eBay US sales we collect every day, matched to each category by what the listing is. The median is the middle sale: half sold for more, half for less. "Most sold" is the middle half of sales, drawn as the green band, with the median as the mark.
A category appears only when it had at least 100 sales in the month. The number of sales reflects how much we collected, not how busy the market was, so read it as how much data a price rests on. For the same reason we do not compare months: the mix of items we collect in a category shifts, and that moves a median more than the market does.
It is a market snapshot, not a price for your item: condition, model and size move a single item a long way. For that, [scan it free](https://underpricedai.com/register) and see the sold listings that match it.
