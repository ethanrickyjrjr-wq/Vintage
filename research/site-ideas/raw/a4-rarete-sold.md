# Sold Archive
The Sold Archive is a reference library of vintage and archival listings that have already sold at The Rareté. These pages remain published so collectors, stylists, and researchers can study prior sourcing—houses, seasons, silhouettes, and documentation practices—without treating archived listings as current inventory available to purchase.
Each sold listing keeps the product-level notes that accompanied it when live, including season or runway references where they were documented on the individual page. The Sold Archive does not replace the live [Clothing](https://therarete.com/collections/clothing), [Dresses](https://therarete.com/collections/dresses), [Bottoms](https://therarete.com/collections/bottoms), or [Shoes](https://therarete.com/collections/shoes) edits, and it should not be read as a promise that a pictured piece can be reordered from stock. Archived listings help explain what The Rareté has previously sourced and how those pieces were documented for collectors and researchers.
If you are looking for a similar designer, season, or silhouette, [submit a Sourcing Request](https://therarete.com/pages/sourcing-requests) describing what you need. Pieces still available for purchase or stylist consideration appear across the live collections and, where relevant, the [Archival Collection](https://therarete.com/collections/archival-collection). For Pull-to-Purchase-or-Rent terms on available garments, see the [policy page](https://therarete.com/pages/pull-to-purchase-policy). Availability language on live collections remains separate from this archive view: sold means sold, and purchase paths begin from current listings only. An overview of The Rareté's approach appears on the [About](https://therarete.com/pages/about) page.
We keep sold pieces visible here as a reference, not a waitlist. Each one was reviewed the same way as anything currently listed: [how to authenticate vintage designer clothing](https://therarete.com/blogs/news/how-to-authenticate-vintage-designer-clothing) explains that process, and [what archive fashion actually means](https://therarete.com/blogs/news/what-is-archive-fashion) is a good primer if you're using this archive for research rather than to shop.

## Frequently asked questions
### What is the Sold Archive?
+
A reference library of vintage and archival listings that have already sold at The Rareté, kept visible for collectors and researchers.
### Can I purchase pieces from the Sold Archive?
+
No. Archived listings are sold reference material, not current inventory available to purchase from stock.
### Why keep sold listings online?
+
Archived listings help collectors and researchers understand previous sourcing—houses, seasons, silhouettes, and how pieces were documented.
### Where do I shop pieces that are still available?
+
Browse live collections such as Clothing, Dresses, Bottoms, and Shoes, or the Archival Collection for stylist-oriented pieces.
### Can The Rareté help me find something similar to a sold piece?
+
Yes—submit a Sourcing Request describing the designer, season, or silhouette you want.
### Do sold pages still show runway or season notes?
+
Yes. Product-level documentation that existed when the piece was live remains on the individual sold listing.

From rare runway garments to sculptural silhouettes and collectible accessories, each item is a study in timeless design - curated for collectors, stylists, and anyone chasing something truly one-of-a-kind.
