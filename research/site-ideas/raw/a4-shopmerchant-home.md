## Private Appointments
Experience MERCHANT with a private appointment tailored to you. Come for a one-on-one styling session, bring a friend or two, or book a fun girls’ night.
Whether you’re dressing for a wedding, Bar or Bat Mitzvah, or another special occasion, we’ll help you find an outfit that feels like you.
Your appointment opens up more of our curated collection. We’ll pull pieces from the studio floor, additional stock, and our selection of high-end dress rentals to bring together the perfect look.
### Rentals
From black-tie evenings to a wild night out, MERCHANT rentals let you dress for the occasion without committing to a one-time purchase. Explore our collection of designer dresses and gowns for weddings, galas, and cocktail parties, alongside playful statement pieces for nights that call for something unexpected.
### The Collection
Discover a thoughtfully curated mix of vintage and current fashion, one-of-a-kind pieces, and special finds. Each piece is handpicked for its style, character, and craftsmanship.
### Styling
Enjoy personal styling to create a complete look, find something for a special occasion, or refresh your wardrobe—or simply browse and see what catches your eye. Your MERCHANT experience is yours to make.
### Consignment
Give your fabulous pieces a new chapter at MERCHANT. Book a consignment appointment so we can review your items together, discuss terms, and set clear expectations for the process.
