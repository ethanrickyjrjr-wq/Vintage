## What even is this place?
Maximalism
Maximalism is what happens when your imagination refuses to fit inside a tiny little neutral-colored box.
It's a world where leopard print gets along with florals, grandma's china hangs out with funky modern art, and your favorite weird little thrift store find becomes the star of the show.
Kitsch
Kitsch is the glitter-covered cherry on top of your personality sundae. 🍒✨
It's the vintage treasures, the quirky collectibles, the conversation pieces, and the wonderfully weird things that make someone stop and say, “Wait…where did you find that?!”
Because life is too short for boring stuff.
