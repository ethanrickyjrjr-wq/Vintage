# 1970s
I carry a very large inventory of authentic vintage / antique lighting.
On this page, ALL my lights will download, so you will not need to click Page 2, Page 3, and so on. Just scroll down and enjoy shopping! The sorting default is by era, so lighting from the 1890s and 1900s are listed first, then 1910s, 1920s, and so on. So, if you are interested in a specific decade, all such lights will be grouped together. Or, you can change the default sorting by clicking the dropdown menu.
If you have specific needs the links in the main menu will be helpful. Please note that each link offers a drop-down to help further refine your search.
A specialty here at  _The Old Above_ is whole house sets. So, if your home is missing all its original fixtures, just click [MATCHING SETS](https://theoldabove.com/category/matching-sets/).
Enjoy Shopping!
