# How to Create a Listing 
Before you can create a listing, you must [register as a seller](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015672808?segment=selling). Review our [Seller Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/sellers/?ref=list&segment=selling#allowed) to make sure your items fit into Etsy’s handmade, vintage, or craft supply categories.
To create a listing on Etsy.com: 
  1. Sign in and go to **Shop Manager**.
  2. Select [**Listings**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/listings?segment=selling).
  3. Select **Add a listing**.
  4. Fill in the relevant information in the listing creation form. 
  5. When you’re finished, select **Publish**. The listing is now live in your shop.


To create a listing on the Etsy Seller app: 
  1. Go to **Listings**. 
  2. Select **+** in the top right corner and choose **Copy an existing listing** or **Start from scratch**. 
  3. Select a category that best describes your listing. If you don’t see a good match, choose**Select a different category** to see more options. 
  4. Upload photos and/or videos that show off your item. You’ll be able to [edit your uploads](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360016260113-How-to-Edit-Your-Listing-Photos?segment=selling) later in the process. 
  5. Enter relevant and required information for your listing. Select **Next** and **Back** to navigate between sections.
  6. When finished, select **Publish**.


Once published, it may take up to 48 hours for your listing to appear in Etsy search results and categories.
If you’d rather save the listing for later, select **Save as Draft** instead. 
[Learn how to copy a listing to create multiple listings at once. ](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000337307?segment=selling)

## Photo & video
You can add up to 20 photos and 2 videos that are 5-15 seconds in length to each listing. Select **Edit** to adjust the image thumbnail, crop the photo to the aspect ratio of your choosing, or delete the photo. Select **Apply** when finished. [Learn about best practices for images in your Etsy shop.](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015663347?segment=selling)
## Category
Based on the photos and/or videos you provided, this section will be prefilled. Confirm the selections are correct, and make updates as needed.
  * **Find a category** : The category determines which sections of Etsy’s marketplace your item appears in. Type the category of your item into the search bar, then under **Results** , select the option that is the closest match for what you’re listing.
  * **What type of item is it?:** Select whether you’re listing a physical item or digital files.
  * **When was it made?:** Select when you made the item you’re selling.


## Item details
This is what shoppers see when browsing for items on Etsy, so be sure to follow best practices for [getting your items found in search](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/the-ultimate-guide-to-etsy-search/366469415790). From here you can add:
  * **Title** : A listing’s title can be up to 140 characters long. Consider using less than 15 words when including the traits that make your item special. Clearly state what the item is and any key details. Avoid repetition as well as subjective words (for example, “perfect”). There’s no need to add information about price or shipping to your title. Only include holidays, special occasions, or recipient types if they’re essential to what the item is (for example, “Halloween costume”). 


  * Your listing's unique URL is based on the title you enter when you first publish the listing. Once you've published the listing, the URL won’t changeagain, even if you edit the title later. If you make major changes to your listing, consider creating a new listing so the URL better reflects what's being sold.


  * **Digital files** : Add up to 5 files for buyers to download when purchasing a digital item from your shop. This section only appears if you have indicated you are listing digital files.
  * **Description** : Describe the details of your item so buyers know what they’re getting. When possible, include [keywords](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/382774281517) buyers would use when searching for an item like this. [Learn about best practices for listing descriptions](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/26330089019?segment=selling).


## Item options
  * **Variations:** Add variations to your listing if you’d like to offer different options for the item, like size or color. You’ll only be able to add variations to an item once you’ve selected a category for the item in the **Details** section. Variations are unavailable for digital listings. [Learn how to add variations to your listings.](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015664047?segment=selling)
  * **Personalization** : Select **Add Personalization** if you want to offer the ability to customize your item. Then, add in any personalization instructions you want buyers to see, set a limit on the number of characters a buyer can request when customizing the item, and select if you want to make personalization optional or mandatory for this item. [Learn more about personalization.](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000344528?segment=selling)
  * **Attributes:** Give details about the qualities of the item, such as the color, material, size dimensions, etc. The attributes listed under this section will change depending on the category you selected. [Learn more about how to use attributes for your listings](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115014502508-How-to-Use-Attributes-When-Listing-an-Item?segment=selling).
  * **Tags** : Add up to 13 tags that describe your item. Try to include [keywords](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/382774281517) buyers would use when searching for an item like this. This helps your item appear in shoppers’ search results. [Learn how using tags can help you get found in search](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000336307-Using-Tags-to-Get-Found-in-Search?segment=selling).


## Pricing & shipping
### Price and inventory
  * **Price** : Set the amount buyers will pay for your item here. The price will appear in your [preferred currency](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000336747?segment=selling). When you enter a price on a listing, a profit estimate appears. This can be either a single price or a range, depending on your variation settings. Select the dropdown to see more details.
  * **Quantity** : Add the amount of the item you have for sale. Learn more about [how item quantity works with listing fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000344908?segment=selling).
  * **SKU:**[ Create a custom SKU](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015691707?segment=selling) (Stock Keeping Unit) number for the item to help keep track of your inventory.


### Shipping, processing, and returns
Apply accurate processing and shipping information to your listings so buyers have realistic expectations on when the order will arrive. If the listing is for a digital item, buyers will download the files you uploaded immediately after purchase. 
To create and set your listing’s processing profile on Etsy.com: 
  1. While creating your listing, go to **Processing profile**.
  2. Select **Create**.
  3. Select if your item is **Made to order** or **Ready to ship**.
  4. Choose the processing time from the **How much processing time do you need?** dropdown.
  5. Select **Apply**.


To create and set your listing’s processing profile on the Etsy Seller app: 
  1. While creating your listing, go to **Processing and shipping** and select **Processing profile**.
  2. Select **Create new**.
  3. Select if your item is **Made to order** or **Ready to ship**.
  4. Choose a time from the**Processing time** dropdown.
  5. Select **Save**.


If you already have processing profiles, Etsy will pre-fill this section for you. 
To change your listing’s pre-filled processing profile on Etsy.com: 
  1. Go to **Processing profile**.
  2. Select **Change profile**. 
  3. Select **Apply** on the processing profile you wish to use, or select**Create new** to add another.


To change your listing’s pre-filled processing profile on the Etsy Seller app: 
  1. Go to **Processing and shipping**.
  2. Go to **Processing profile** and select the pencil icon to edit. 
  3. Select the processing profile you wish to apply and select **Save**. To add a new processing profile, select **Create new**.


[Learn more about processing profiles.](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015588087?segment=selling)
To add shipping information to your listings: 
  1. In the **Shipping option** section, choose **Select Profile**. 
  2. Choose one of your existing shipping profiles and select Apply or fill in the required information to create a new shipping option. For calculated shipping, you must enter the **item weight** and **item size** (when packed).
  3. If the item will be sent with an international shipping label, you can enter the **Tariff Number** to prefill a customs form.


Select **Change** if you need to apply a different shipping profile to your listing. You can also select the three dots next to a shipping profile to **Edit** or **Duplicate** it.
### GPSR manufacturer and safety information
If you’re a “trader” selling to European Economic Area (EEA) states or Northern Ireland (NI), you may need to include manufacturer and safety information to comply with the [General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR)](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/28211364687383?segment=selling). 
If you’re listing a physical item, you can also provide **Customs information** that is used to prefill a customs form when you purchase an international shipping label on Etsy.
## How it's made
These are the most basic aspects of your listing, including who made the item, what it is, and when it was made. Add any [production partners](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000336547-Working-with-Production-Partners-on-Etsy?segment=selling) involved in the creation of this item.
## Settings
Adjust settings to how your listing appears and how it will renew.
  * **Shop section** : Select which of your shop sections you want the listing displayed in or create a new one. [Learn more about how to organize your listings with shop sections](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000345048-How-to-Create-and-Manage-Shop-Sections?segment=selling).
  * **Feature this listing** : Switch the toggle button on to display the listing at the top of your shop’s homepage.
  * **Renewal options** : Choose if you want your listings to automatically renew after they expire, or if you’d rather renew them manually. See our [policy page](https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees) to learn more about listing fees.


## **Questions about listings?**
[Ask the Community](https://community.etsy.com/forum/shop-management-302/)
You can edit all parts of a listing at any time. There's no cost to edit a listing.
If you’re having trouble creating or editing listings, select an issue to learn how to resolve it:
My listing is deactivating after each sale.
If you find your listing isn’t renewing properly, or says it **Needs Review** when you are making edits, you’ll need to clarify some parts of your shipping preferences. 
Etsy requires sellers from all origin countries to provide an [estimated delivery date](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360020601674?segment=selling) to save a shipping profile and listing. 
For each listing, you will need to have the following information completely filled out:
  * **Domestic shipping service**
    * If **Other** is selected, you must provide a delivery time.  

  * **Cross-border shipping service**
    * If **Other** is selected, you must provide a delivery time.
    * Cross-border services will be required of all sellers in all countries who ship internationally.
  * If you offer **shipping upgrades, shipping service** or **delivery time**.
  * **Origin ZIP/postal code/postcode/PIN, which the ioptem is shipping from**
  * A processing time to indicate how long you need to get an order ready and handed off to your shipping carrier. [Learn how to set a processing time](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015588087?segment=selling).


You are required to add the above information to listings/shipping profiles whenever you:
  * Create a new listing
  * Duplicate a listing
  * Edit an existing listing
  * Create a new shared or custom shipping profile
  * Duplicate a shipping profile
  * Edit an existing shipping profile


I’m having trouble uploading an image to a listing 
**Your image may be too small or too large**
We recommend your images be at least 2000 pixels wide. You may want to use image editing software to resize your images before uploading them to Etsy.
Files bigger than 300k may time out, especially on a slower internet connection. Try using a smaller file or compressing your file to reduce its size.
**If you are seeing the error message “Listings look best with photos at least 2000 pixels wide”**
This error appears when the images uploaded to a listing are less than 2000 pixels wide. While this error is only a suggestion and you should be able to continue publishing, having your listing images be at least 2000 pixels wide will result in better quality images for buyers.
If you’re experiencing a different issue with image uploads, [check the steps in this Help Center article](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000337987?segment=selling).
I don’t see the type of listing variation I want to offer 
You can create a new variation type by selecting **Create a new variation** from the **Add or Edit variations** popup window. However, buyers won’t be able to filter their Etsy search results based on custom variations.
For example, if you want to offer a unisex size variation, you can choose **Create a new variation** and type in “Size.”
I’m seeing the error “Listing preview doesn't accurately reflect price and quantity on variations” 
This is normal, and you'll see the variations and price differences you added when you publish your listing. Try publishing your listing. You can see how buyers can interact with variations from the published listing.
Can I set different prices based on a buyer's location?
If you’re a seller outside the US and India, you can set up a different listing price for orders shipping within your country and orders shipping outside your country. [Learn more to set up domestic & global pricing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/4403156582039?segment=selling).
What can I sell on Etsy? 
Everything listed for sale on Etsy must be handmade, vintage, or a craft supply. Items sold on Etsy must also follow our [Prohibited Items Policy](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360024112614-What-Can-I-Sell-on-Etsy-?segment=selling#h_e46ecc87-a2ce-442f-b4ed-0c97aa4fcb38).
**Handmade items**
Handmade items are items that are made and/or designed by you, the seller. If you sell handmade items, you must:
  * Physically make or create the original designs for your items.
  * Include each person involved in making your items or running your business in the [About section](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015628487?segment=selling) of your shop. If you work with a [production partner](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000336547?segment=selling), you must disclose that production partner in your relevant listings.
  * Use your own [photographs](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/listing-image-requirements/253962679005) in your listings.


Read our full [Handmade Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/handmade).
**Vintage items**
Vintage items must be at least 20 years old. Newer, vintage-style, or collectible items aren’t allowed in this category.
[Learn more about selling vintage items on Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/vintage-items-on-etsy/242665563649).
**Craft supplies**
Craft supplies on Etsy are tools, ingredients, or materials whose primary purpose is for use in the creation of an item or special occasion. Craft supplies may be handmade, commercial, or vintage. Party supplies may also be sold as craft supplies.
[Learn about what can be sold as a craft supply](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/craft-supplies/239327031264).
My listing was removed by Etsy 
If your listing was removed by Etsy for a violation of Etsy’s policies, check your email inbox for a message from Etsy. In the email you received about this listing(s), you’ll be told the reason it was removed.
If you’re unable to locate an email about your listing:
  * Make sure you’re using the email address registered with the shop that the listing was associated with
  * Check your spam or junk mail


Also be sure to review [Etsy’s policies](https://www.etsy.com/legal/sellers) to ensure that your items can be listed on our marketplace. 
I want to delete a listing 
You can deactivate or delete a listing from your Listings page. Check the box next to the listing, then choose **Deactivate** or **Delete**. If you deactivate a listing, you can reactivate it later. If you delete a listing, it is permanently deleted.
