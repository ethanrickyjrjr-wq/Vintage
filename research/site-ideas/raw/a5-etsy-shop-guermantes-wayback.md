G U E R M A N T E S
Guermantes Vintage was founded in February 2012. The name Guermantes refers to Marcel Proust's In Search of Lost Time, and the philosophy of the shop is influenced by Proust's treatment and understanding of memory. Vintage clothing is imbued with essential memory, not only personal (for example in the ownership of a particular individual) but cultural memory as well. Its details - seams, hemline, buttons, silhouette - all reflect its era and the way the world was at the time it was made. Much like Proust's narrator biting into a Madeleine, experiencing vintage clothing is an immediate portal to memory, a window into a personal and a cultural past.  
  
In Search of Lost Time was first published in English translation over several years, between 1922 and 1931. Though Guermantes Vintage spans the 1910 through the early 1960s, my clear focus is on my stock of exceptional 1920s and 1930s era vintage.
