##  About jessjamesjake 
Sales 5,987
On Etsy since 2008
authentic vintage fashion for all ladies
I started jessjamesjake in 2008 in Denver, CO, focusing on bringing vintage to everyday life in a way that is eco-friendly and fashion-forward. We now live in Albany, NY. I specialize in clothing and jewelry from the 1920s - 1990s, and take pride in stocking vintage for every woman: all shapes and sizes! I handpick quality vintage apparel in flattering shapes, charming prints, beautiful fabrics, and feminine silhouettes, focusing on our modern day sizes, from XXS/Petites - XXL/Plus (and everything in between),
