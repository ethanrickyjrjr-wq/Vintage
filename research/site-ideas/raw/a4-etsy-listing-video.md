# How to Add Listing Videos 
Listing videos give shoppers a better idea of your products, and can also help improve how your [listings appear in Etsy search](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/25869947521175?segment=selling). You can add up to 2 videos per listing.
**To add videos to a listing on Etsy.com:**
  1. On [Etsy.com](https://www.etsy.com/), select the [**Shop Manager**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/dashboard) icon.
  2. Select [**Listings**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/listings).
  3. Choose the listing you’d like to add videos to or create a new listing.
  4. Go to **Photo and video**.
  5. Drag and drop files or select **Upload** and choose the short listing videos you would like to add.


**To add a video to a listing in the Etsy Seller app:**
  1. In the Etsy Seller app, select **Listings**.
  2. Choose the listing you'd like to add videos to.
  3. Select **Listing** to see the listing details.
  4. Either select the plus sign under **Photo and video** or select **View** **all** then **Add a video**.
  5. Choose the short listing videos you would like to add. 


## Listing video size and formats
We accept most common video formats for listing videos. The maximum size is 100 MB and file types are:
  * MP4
  * MOV
  * FLV
  * AAC
  * AVI
  * 3GP
  * MPEG


Your video may be 3-15 seconds long and will not contain any audio once uploaded to your listing. While the minimum video resolution is 500px, the ideal video resolution is at least 1080px. You can add two videos per listing. To delete a video, select that video and select the trash can icon.
The video aspect ratio should be 2:1 or 1:2. No matter what kind of device you use to record your video, it’ll most likely already be in a format that is accepted by the video tool.
