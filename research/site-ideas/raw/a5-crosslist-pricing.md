## Frequently asked questions
Everything you need to know about our pricing and refund policy.
Can I try Crosslist® risk-free?
Yes, you can try Crosslist® completely risk-free before subscribing.
We offer a 3-day money-back guarantee on your first purchase, as long as you’ve created fewer than 20 listings within the app — whether manually or through import.
This gives you plenty of freedom to explore Crosslist® and experience [its powerful features](https://crosslist.com/features), while helping us prevent misuse of the platform.
Your refund covers both your subscription plan and any add-ons. For annual plans, a small $9.99 processing fee applies, while monthly and quarterly plans have no fees deducted to cover credit card processing costs.
If you’d like a refund, simply contact our support team through the chat widget inside the Crosslist® application (not on the landing page) within 72 hours of purchase. Canceling your subscription alone will not initiate a refund.
In which countries is Crosslist® available?
Crosslist® is currently available in the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia.
At this time, we do not support users residing in the European Union.
Which payment methods do you support?
We support all major credit cards, as well as Google Pay and Apple Pay.
All payments are processed securely through our trusted payment provider, Stripe.
What is your refund policy?
Crosslist® offers a 3-day money-back guarantee on your first purchase, provided you’ve created fewer than 20 listings within the app — including through manual creation or import.  
  
This limit helps prevent misuse of the platform while still giving new users enough flexibility to explore Crosslist® and experience its features.
The refund covers both your subscription plan and any add-ons. For annual plans, a $9.99 processing fee applies, while monthly and quarterly plans have no fees deducted (to cover the credit card processing costs).
To request a refund, you must contact our support team through the chat widget inside the Crosslist® application (not on the landing page) within 72 hours of purchase. Merely canceling your subscription does not initiate a refund.
Please note that the guarantee applies only to your first purchase, and recurring payments are non-refundable.
Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
Yes, you can cancel your Crosslist® subscription at any time directly from within the Crosslist® app.
There’s no need to contact support — simply follow the steps outlined in our [cancellation guide](https://docs.crosslist.com/service/subscription/managing-your-subscription#cancelling-your-subscription).
Once canceled, your plan will remain active until the end of your current billing period.
If you believe you’re eligible for a refund under our 3-day money-back guarantee, please contact us through the chat widget inside the Crosslist® application (not on the landing page) so our team can review your request.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan at any time?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your Crosslist® subscription at any time directly from within the Crosslist® app.
There’s no need to contact support — simply follow the steps outlined in our [managing your subscription guide](https://docs.crosslist.com/service/subscription/managing-your-subscription).
When you upgrade, the change takes effect immediately, and you’ll gain instant access to the features included in your new plan.
When you downgrade, the change will apply at the end of your current billing period, allowing you to continue using your existing plan until it expires.
Can I save money with a quarterly or annual plan?
Yes, you can save money with a quarterly or annual Crosslist® plan.
Quarterly subscriptions include a 10% discount, and annual plans give you 2 months off compared to paying monthly.
Explore all Crosslist pricing discounts and plan options on our [pricing page](https://crosslist.com/pricing).
Are there any limits on how many listings I can create or import?
Yes, Crosslist® plans include listing limits based on the plan you choose.
Bronze includes 200 listings, Silver includes 500 listings, Gold includes 1,000 listings, and Diamond includes unlimited listings.
These limits apply to the total number of listings you create or import into the Crosslist® app.
Once a listing has been created, you can post, relist, and delist it across supported marketplaces as often as needed.
View all Crosslist plan limits and features on our [pricing page](https://crosslist.com/pricing).
Is Crosslist® worth the investment?
Yes, Crosslist® is absolutely worth it for most resellers.  
  
The biggest reason is simple: more exposure leads to more sales. By helping you list on [12+ marketplaces](https://crosslist.com/marketplaces), Crosslist® puts your inventory in front of far more buyers, which means more chances to sell and more ways to grow your revenue.
For many sellers, only a few extra sales per month are enough to cover the cost of a [cross listing app](https://crosslist.com/).
On average, Crosslist® users see up to 156% more sales and save over 80% of their time, making the return well worth it.
Instead of spending hours manually posting the same items over and over, you can grow faster and make more from the inventory you already have.
