James Veloria is a vintage clothing store in New York City that sells vintage clothing curated by Collin James Weber and Brandon Veloria Giordano. 
Inspired by innovative design and the use of bold colors and patterns, we collect with a very specific vision and critical eye to find the most unique and exciting clothing from the past and present. We specialize in collectible, iconic pieces from throughout fashion history with an emphasis on clothing and accessories from Japanese and European avant-garde designers.
Our clothing is sourced from a large network of closets all over the world to continually add to our carefully curated stock while maintaining an accessible price point.
