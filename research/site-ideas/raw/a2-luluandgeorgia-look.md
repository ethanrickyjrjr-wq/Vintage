### What is the Dining Details collection at Lulu and Georgia?

### How do I style a dining room with pieces from Lulu and Georgia?
Start with the rug: place it under the dining table with enough overhang (18–24 inches on each side) for chairs to remain on the rug when pulled out. An 8x10 suits most dining tables for four; a 9x12 for six or eight. Next, layer in the lighting—a pendant centered over the table at 30–36 inches above the surface. Finish with table styling: a low vase with dried stems, candlesticks, and seasonal linens. The Orb Candlestick and Wrinkle Vase by Sarah Sherman Samuel make a strong organic centerpiece pair.
