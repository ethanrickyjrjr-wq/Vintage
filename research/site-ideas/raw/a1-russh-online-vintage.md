Finding forever secondhand is exactly what the environment needs. So here's your guide to [vintage](https://www.russh.com/tag/vintage/) shopping online, courtesy of the minds at _RUSSH._ From pre-loved designer bags to archival gowns, these are the online vintage stores to know. And you don't even have to leave your front door.
### 1. [Shy Online](https://shy-online.com/?utm_source=ig&utm_medium=social&utm_content=link_in_bio&fbclid=PAZXh0bgNhZW0CMTEAc3J0YwZhcHBfaWQPOTM2NjE5NzQzMzkyNDU5AAGnxKqlkPE2S_fA1LNBrRqIJbyV65cpZKPlzy-VZQ4ZfKJe8yt8A8B-Tfi0MVA_aem_gI3bT3oKos556L_khktyww)
> View this post on Instagram
> [A post shared by shy (@shy___online)](https://www.instagram.com/p/DauFyNOj8RS/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading)
**Known for:** Niche luxury pieces at great prices
Our Social Media Coordinator and Writer Samantha's go-to vintage shop. Having purchased from them on multiple ocassions you can be assured the quality, efficiency and selection at Shy Online is like no other. They do monthly drops promoted on their Instagram, and have some of the nichest yet affordable selections from [Tom Ford](https://www.russh.com/tag/tom-ford/) by Gucci, Saint Laurent, CHANEL, and Yohji Yamamoto. Once live, you can purchase their pieces [via their website](https://shy-online.com/?utm_source=ig&utm_medium=social&utm_content=link_in_bio&fbclid=PAZXh0bgNhZW0CMTEAc3J0YwZhcHBfaWQPOTM2NjE5NzQzMzkyNDU5AAGnxKqlkPE2S_fA1LNBrRqIJbyV65cpZKPlzy-VZQ4ZfKJe8yt8A8B-Tfi0MVA_aem_gI3bT3oKos556L_khktyww).
### 2. [EVOIIA](https://evoiia.com/)
> View this post on Instagram
> [A post shared by EVOIIA (@_evoiia)](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DKV6msiTmPC/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading)
**Known for:** Luxury pieces at great prices, wearable basics, great condition pieces
This Sydney-based online store is a treasure trove of vintage finds – from [Prada](https://www.russh.com/tag/prada/) to MiuMiu to Dior and YSL. They stock footwear, garments, sunglasses and even some swim, and all for very reasonable prices. Everything is in great condition, and lovingly curated by one very passionate vintage lover. They have also started doing pop-ups across [Sydney](https://www.russh.com/tag/sydney/) and [Melbourne](https://www.russh.com/tag/melbourne/) – with the next set for Sunday 6 July at the Secondlife Markets in Melbourne.
### 3. [Studded Petals](https://www.studdedpetals.com/)
> View this post on Instagram
> [A post shared by Studded Petals Vintage (@studdedpetals)](https://www.instagram.com/p/CnctUYPuUP0/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading)
**Known for:** Pristine Vivienne Westwood, 90s Jean Paul Gaultier, Anna Sui, Dior by Galliano and Fendi.
I do not gatekeep, but if I was in the business of doing so, I'd keep Studded Petals close to my chest. This is my current favourite vintage shop; not only does it carry a diverse range of sizes (a rare thing in the vintage game), but it's true archival pieces at a fraction of the cost at other stores.
### 4. [Sororité Vintage](https://sororitevintage.com/shop)
> View this post on Instagram
> [A post shared by sororité. (@sororitevintage)](https://www.instagram.com/p/Cr_pi-kSK7b/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading)
**Known for:** A shoe collection to rival Carrie Bradshaw's and vintage [French](https://www.russh.com/tag/french/) lingerie
With over 700k followers on Instagram, this is one of those situations where if you like something, you're going to have to work fast. Sororité Vintage has earned its hefty following for doling out vintage [La Perla](https://www.russh.com/tag/la-perla/) and Chantall Thomas bras, slips and bustiers, a sprinkling of vaguely 90s and Y2K designer party dresses, and the true drawcard, it's magnificently curated and regularly updated collection of vintage designer heels. Think Prada mules, Manolo Blahnik slingbacks, [Chanel](https://www.russh.com/tag/chanel/) ballet flats and [Jimmy Choo](https://www.russh.com/tag/jimmy-choo/) sandals.
### 5. [Los Feliz](https://losfelizshop.com/)
> View this post on Instagram
> [A post shared by Los Féliz (@losfelizshop2)](https://www.instagram.com/p/CtFICmfqdGP/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading)
**Known for:** Vintage Plein Sud, Ungaro, Escada, [Moschino](https://www.russh.com/tag/moschino/) and Kenzo.
I've been a stalwart of Nadia Pape's beloved Barcelona born and based store for many years now. I love its unique perspective, artful and distinctly southern European. For fans of Maryam Nassir Zadeh.
### 6. [Kiko Vintage](https://kikovintage.com.au/)
> View this post on Instagram
> [A post shared by kiko.vintage (@kiko.vintage)](https://www.instagram.com/p/Cru-Y9Yr405/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading)
**Known for:** A genre-spanning range of vintage designer, from Commes Des Garcons and Hysteric Glamour to [Vivienne Westwood](https://www.russh.com/tag/vivienne-westwood/) and Dior.
[Kiko Vintage](https://www.russh.com/ari-kiko-how-to-buy-designer-vintage-guide/) is one of those IYKYK local names. Beloved by Sydney models, creatives, musicians and artists alike, every month Kiko Vintage releases a drop of designer vintage from the 70s up until the 2000s. The styles are always changing, but you can always count on there being a heavy flush of colour, handbags and a sensibility as sweet as the candy that accompanies your purchase in the mail. Read our [interview with Kiko Vintage founder, Arielle De Bono](https://www.russh.com/ari-kiko-how-to-buy-designer-vintage-guide/), here.

> [A post shared by Dani Michelle (@danixmichelle)](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DLF7Bw5yx_M/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading)
**Known for:** one-of-a-kind vintage deals, limited-time price drops, regularly updated inventory
This French resale site has been going since 2009 (and has the support of fashion conglomerate [Kering](https://www.russh.com/tag/kering/) too). Vintage inventory on this site is user-generated, authenticated by their in-house team. You'll find a great range of designer labels across all categories here, with shipping available to most destinations.

> [A post shared by Toots Vintage (@tootsvintage)](https://www.instagram.com/p/CgcOhAIg_y6/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading)
**Known for:** Statement ballgowns, evening dresses, tulle and silk chiffon.
A NYC store helmed by Michael Phillips, if you have an event you want to wear something extravagant too, look no further. Also a fan of the cute floral brooches.

Founded in 2019, LovedbyChi is an Italian-inspired small business that specialises in luxurious vintage pieces from the 60s to the early 2000s.
### 10. [Cara Mia Vintage](https://caramiavintage.com/)
> View this post on Instagram
> [A post shared by Cara Mia Vintage (@caramiavintage)](https://www.instagram.com/p/Cq6cDlpvNsO/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading)
**Known for:** Singular gowns, Gaultier mesh, hot pants and flair.
Another staple in the diets of vintage-obsessed Australians, Cara Mia has operated for over a decade now, sourcing deadstock designer that would send a shiver down your spine. As seen on [Bella Hadid](https://www.russh.com/tag/bella-hadid/) and FKA Twigs.
### 11. [Eveliina Vintage](https://eveliina-vintage.com/)
> View this post on Instagram
> [A post shared by 🌸 Eveliina Vintage 🌸 (@eveliinavintage)](https://www.instagram.com/p/CpXsMujAHR0/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading)
**Known for:** Whimsical vintage silk dresses sourced from all decades.
If you've ever loved a [Reformation](https://www.russh.com/tag/reformation/) or [Realisation Par](https://www.russh.com/tag/realisation-par/) dress, Eveliina Vintage slips in as a secondhand alternative. Offering the kind of floral-patterned maxi dresses you hope to fall in love wearing in summer, there's also a dedicated section for brides-to-be, with 1920s lace, drop waists, puff sleeves and all your girlish fantasies. Plus it comes approved by [Adwoa Aboah](https://www.russh.com/tag/adwoa-aboah/) and Devon Lee Carlson.
### 12. [Re-SEE](https://www.resee.com/)
> View this post on Instagram
> [A post shared by RESEE.com (@resee)](https://www.instagram.com/p/C57_vteoNkG/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading)

Parisian based Re-SEE is the brainchild of two fashion veterans, Sofia Bernardin and Sabrina Marshall, from _Vogue_ and _Self Service_ Magazine. Offering rare and classic luxury vintage with the vision of keeping great fashion in circulation. Every piece on Re-SEE is personally curated by editors, fully authenticated by experts and restored when necessary by artisans.
### 13. [Shop Vintage Collection](https://www.instagram.com/svc_______/)
> View this post on Instagram
> [A post shared by SHOP VINTAGE COLLECTION (@svc_______)](https://www.instagram.com/p/CuAEWNVtSF4/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading)

As Bella Hadid's personal favourite for vintage blazers, we're not surprised items sell out in record time. A treasure trove of boyish blazers, leather coats and masculine shirting, you'd better not only follow, but turn on post notifications to snag a beautiful, affordable piece.
### 14. [The Vintage Bar](https://www.thevintagebar.com/)
> View this post on Instagram
> [A post shared by The Vintage Bar (@the_vintage_bar)](https://www.instagram.com/p/Cum0oSQNhro/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading)
**Known for:** Large selection, Scandi fashion, luxury bags
With a large curation of luxury vintage and secondhand items, there is something for everyone. Established in 2017, the Scandinavian treasure trove is overflowing with authenticated luxury bags and accessories and has a penchant for sustainability. The Vintage Bar are also available to consign should you have a [Fendi](https://www.russh.com/tag/fendi/) Baguette collecting dust.
### 15. [Jerome Vintage](https://www.instagram.com/jerome_vintage/)
> View this post on Instagram
> [A post shared by JEROME VINTAGE (@jerome_vintage)](https://www.instagram.com/p/Cqn5dwVsE_S/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading)

Look no further for chic vintage designer co-ords then Jerome Vintage. Offering an expansive range of vintage every day staples, there is little room to go wrong.
### 16. [1st Dibs](https://www.1stdibs.com/)

You'd be mistkaen for thikning that he cult online resal eplatform 1stDibs is just for [homewares](https://www.russh.com/tag/homewares/) – in fact, they also list jewellery and rare [vintage fashion](https://www.russh.com/tag/vintage-fashion/) pieces too. If you're looking for something extra special, or one-off, this is the place we'd be going for a vintage engagement ring, birkins or ballgowns.
### 17. [Mori Market](https://www.instagram.com/mori_market/)

Another locally curated shop via Instagram style vintage store. Mori Market offers a selection of sweet basics at an affordable price point. Kind of like op-shopping but without having to trawl through stained t-shirts to find a gem. If you're not following for the garments themselves, do so for the mood imagery.
### 18. [Blue Spinach](https://bluespinach.com.au/)

A Sydney vintage institution if there ever was one. Blue Spinach, the previously cobalt blue corner store on Liverpool St in Darlinghurst has always been home to some of the most covetable vintage pieces, and now you can shop online.
### 19. [Trading in Style](https://www.tradinginstyle.com/)

### 21. [What Goes Around Comes Around](https://www.whatgoesaroundnyc.com/)

> [A post shared by What Goes Around Comes Around (@whatgoesaroundnyc)](https://www.instagram.com/p/C7ncH3ou-he/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading)
**Known for:** Luxury bags, pre-loved watches, vintage jewellery
If you didn't already know, now you do. The number one destination for luxury vintage bags and goods, What Goes Around Comes Around is an NYC institution. Peruse the site and see for yourself why.
### 22. [Retrostar](https://www.retrostar.com.au/)
> View this post on Instagram
> [A post shared by Retrostar Vintage Clothing (@retrostarvintage)](https://www.instagram.com/p/C5AYtoESxOX/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading)
**Known for:** warehouse sales, vintage tees
The best place in Southern Victoria to pick up a sweatshirt for layering. Head down to Swanston street for all your sundried, retro wares. The best part? There's plenty to pick from.
### 23. [The RealReal](https://www.therealreal.com/)
> View this post on Instagram
> [A post shared by therealreal (@therealreal)](https://www.instagram.com/p/CsyvGlPOITd/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading)
**Known for:** accessibility, authenticated goods, range
The OG of luxury consignment and we all know it. The RealReal is like one giant, chaotic vintage store, housing hundreds of brands and covetable items. If you remember it on the runway, The RealReal likely has it.
### 24. [Art Haus](https://arthouseco.com.au/collections/vintage)
> View this post on Instagram
> [A post shared by art haus (@arthauscollections)](https://www.instagram.com/p/CwPIMRFvSpH/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading)
**Known for:** Distinctive pieces of jewellery, from vintage [Cartier](https://www.russh.com/tag/cartier/) watches to one-off statement bracelets.
The passion project of photographer and creative director, Natalie Cottee, Art Haus is a well-curated source of vintage treasures collected from across the globe.
### 25. [Shrimpton Couture](https://www.shrimptoncouture.com/)
> View this post on Instagram
> [A post shared by Cherie Balch (@shrimptoncouture)](https://www.instagram.com/p/C7R0mIIOWK1/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading)
**Known for:** Archival pulls, period pieces, and referential fashion in spades
This is for the fashion elite. And that's not just affixed to the price tag, but rather, the rich history and top-of-the-shelf vintage curation. Everything tells a story, and this is the story of couture.
### 26. [Lende Vintage](https://thelende.com/)
> View this post on Instagram
> [A post shared by LENDE (@lendestore)](https://www.instagram.com/p/CquGOJHvGnQ/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading)
**Known for:** archival specialist fashion, delectable Marant shoewear, having very limited stock - so move with a swiftness.
This is the vintage store for the girls who love geometric, sculptural pieces. I am one of them, and will invariably end up on The Lende whenever I'm on the action hunt for archival [Margiela](https://www.russh.com/tag/margiela/) or Helmut Lang.
### 27. [Miishka](https://miishka.com/collections/miishkavintage)
> View this post on Instagram
> [A post shared by MIISHKA (@miishka)](https://www.instagram.com/p/C465Iu6S0R_/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading)
**Known for:** contrast colours, extensive designer purses, and knits
Miishka's mission is pretty straightforward. I've hovered over their Tom Ford for [Gucci](https://www.russh.com/tag/gucci/) [mules](https://www.russh.com/tag/mules/) for a criminal amount of time in my inbox. They're slinging designer goods in near-perfect condition, at a just price in an unjust economy. I'll clink to that.
