# Schedule, edit, and start a show
[ ![Whatnot Support](https://help.whatnot.com/system/photos/36667494453133/BLACKLOGO.png) Whatnot Support ](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/9778927885581-Schedule-edit-and-start-a-show) August 10, 2026 at 2:38 PM
A show is a live stream where you can sell items, connect with your audience, and share the moment in real time.
You can schedule and host shows from Seller Hub in the app or on [whatnot.com](http://whatnot.com). Scheduling in advance gives you time to prepare listings, promote your show, and build an audience. 
Review Whatnot’s [Community Guidelines](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360061197472) before going live to keep shows fair, safe, and enjoyable for all.

## Schedule a show
  1. From the app or [whatnot.com](http://whatnot.com), open **Seller Hub**
  2. (On web) Open the [**Shows**](https://www.whatnot.com/dashboard/lives) page on the left 
  3. Select **Schedule a Show**
  4. Enter your show name, date, and time
  5. Choose a **category**
     * Shows only appear in the selected category
     * If selling multiple product types, choose a primary category
  6. Select a primary **selling format**
  7. Publish your show


**Note:** Selling formats and tags must be accurate to comply with our Gaming Policy.
**Optional:**
  * Add a **thumbnail** or preview video 
    * Use a vertical image or video (9:16 ratio)
    * Recommended size: 1080 × 1920 pixels
    * If none is added, your profile photo will be used
  * Add**tags** to improve your show’s discoverability
  * Add **moderators**
  * Set a **repeat schedule**
  * Adjust [**shipping settings**](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/36860251881869) for this show specifically
  * Adjust **content settings** : 
    * **Explicit content:** Flag if your stream has strong language
    * **Mute words:** Automatically hide specific words from the chat
  * Choose **Public** or [**Private**](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/6218017419021-Schedule-and-host-a-private-show) visibility


**Tips:**
  * [Add listings ](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/9779149424269-How-to-list-products-on-Whatnot)and stock your show with inventory so buyers can browse and [place bids in advance](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/14933026908301).
  * Share your show link early so people can [bookmark](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/9780885421069) it and get notified when you go live.
  * Add [Show Notes](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/15463542041229) so buyers know what to expect.
  * Use Whatnot’s [Show Thumbnail Template](https://www.canva.com/design/DAF5mTWV2AY/Q8ivOyrVLIrJf7-UrZ0NdA/view?utm_content=DAF5mTWV2AY&utm_campaign=designshare&utm_medium=link&utm_source=publishsharelink&mode=preview) for clean, consistent visuals.


## Edit or cancel a show
  1. From the app or [whatnot.com](http://whatnot.com), open **Seller Hub**
  2. (On web) Open the [**Shows**](https://www.whatnot.com/dashboard/lives) page on the left 
  3. Find your upcoming show
  4. Tap **More ⋯** icon
  5. Select **Edit Show** or **Cancel Show**


If you need to change the date or time, you can edit and reschedule instead of canceling.
## Start your show
  1. From the app or [whatnot.com](http://whatnot.com), open **Seller Hub**
  2. (On web) Open the [**Shows**](https://www.whatnot.com/dashboard/lives) page on the left 
  3. Open your show 
  4. Select **Start Show**


## End your show 
Closing the app or browser only pauses your show. End your show manually to finish it. 
  1. From your live show, tap or click the **More ⋯** icon
  2. Select **End Show**
  3. Confirm 


**Tips:**
  * [Raid another seller’s show](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/11429333286669) to end yours and send your audience to their stream.
  * You can [restart your show](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/12955560391309) within 2 hours of ending it.
