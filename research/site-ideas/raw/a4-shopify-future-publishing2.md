# Future publishing
Future publishing allows you to hide parts of your online store until a specific date and time. You can set up future publishing for products, collections, blog posts, and pages. Your online store publishes the content at the dates and times that you specify.
#### Note
Verify that the date and time in the **Store defaults** section of your [**General**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/general) settings page is set to your time zone so that your products publish at the correct time.

## Limitations for future publishing
Future publishing isn't available for the Shop app. Scheduled content will display on supported channels only when published.
You can't set a future publishing date for individual product variants. If you need to control which variants are available on a sales channel or catalog, then you can [manage variant publishing](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/variants/publish-variants) instead.
## Set a publish date for a product
You can set a publish date for a product for your online store and for some sales channels.
#### Caution
Products must be in **Active** status for future publishing to work. If a product is in **Draft** status at the scheduled publish time, then the product won't be published to your online store or sales channels.
#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to [**Products**](https://admin.shopify.com/products).
  2. Click a product name.
  3. In the **Product status** section, do the following:
    1. Verify that the status is set to **Active**.
    2. In the **Publishing** section, click the calendar icon next to **Online Store**.
    3. Choose the date and time that you want the product to publish to your online store. The default time is the next `:00` or `:30` point in the day, but you can type in any valid hour and minute combination into the time field (for example: `1:23 p.m.`).
    4. Optional: If applicable, click the calendar icon next to a sales channel and choose the date and time that you want the product to publish to the sales channel.
  4. Click **Schedule publishing**.
  5. Click **Save**.


To view the scheduled publishing date and time, go to the product and then in the **Publishing** section, click **Online Store** or another sales channel. From here, you can also:
  * Click the pencil icon to edit the scheduled date and time, and then click **Save**.
  * Click the trash icon to delete the scheduled date and time, and then click **Save**.


## Set a publish date for a collection
You can set a publish date for a collection.
#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to **Products** > [**Collections**](https://admin.shopify.com/collections).
  2. Click a collection name.
  3. In the **Publishing** section, click the calendar icon next to **Online Store**.
  4. Choose the date and time that you want the collection to publish to your online store. The default time is the next `:00` or `:30` point in the day, but you can type in any valid hour and minute combination into the time field (for example: `1:23 p.m.`).
  5. Click **Schedule publishing**.
  6. Click **Save**.


To view the scheduled publishing date and time, go to the collection and then in the **Publishing** section, click **Online Store**. From here, you can also:
  * Click the pencil icon to edit the scheduled date and time, and then click **Save**.
  * Click the trash icon to delete the scheduled date and time, and then click **Save**.


## Set a publish date for a blog post or page
You can set a publish date for a blog post or a page.
#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to **Online Store** > [**Pages**](https://admin.shopify.com/pages).
  2. From your Shopify admin, go to either **Online Store** > [**Pages**](https://admin.shopify.com/pages) or **Content** > [**Blog posts**](https://admin.shopify.com/articles).
  3. In the **Visibility** section, click the calendar icon.
  4. In the **Set visibility date** dialog, choose the date and time that you want the item to publish to your online store. The default time is the next `:00` or `:30` point in the day, but you can type in any valid hour and minute combination into the time field (for example: `1:23 p.m.`).
  5. Click **Set visibility date**.
  6. Click **Save**.


To view the scheduled publishing date and time, go to the blog post or page and then in the **Visibility** section, check the date and time listed next to **Hidden**. From here, you can also do the following actions:
  * To edit the scheduled date and time, click the pencil icon and then click **Set visibility date** > **Save**.
  * To delete the scheduled date and time, click the trash can icon and then click **Save**.
