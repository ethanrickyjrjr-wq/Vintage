# Introduction Page
![Header Image](https://frutigeraeroarchive.org/images/themes/default/introduction_banner.jpg?v=1.1)
Welcome to the Frutiger Aero Archive, a digital museum and tribute to old 2000s aesthetics, notably Frutiger Aero which it is named after and is primarily representing, but also other styles such as Y2K, Vectordelia (also called Frutiger Metro), and others. On this site you will find large collections of [wallpapers](https://frutigeraeroarchive.org/wallpapers), [music](https://frutigeraeroarchive.org/music) from a variety of styles popular during the era, [old videos](https://frutigeraeroarchive.org/videos), [flash games](https://frutigeraeroarchive.org/flash_media), [PNG images](https://frutigeraeroarchive.org/resources/foregrounds#tableOfContents) to make your own collages, a [huge list of customization software](https://frutigeraeroarchive.org/resources), and many more goodies. We have active community spaces in our IRC chatroom at #frutiger-aero-archive on [Libera.Chat](https://libera.chat/), as well as our [Web Forum](https://forum.frutigeraeroarchive.org/) and a [1.6.4 Minecraft Server](https://frutigeraeroarchive.org/minecraft), come say hi and make new friends! 
For those not familiar with it, Frutiger Aero is an aesthetic widely used in technology and graphic design in the mid 2000s up to its demise in around 2013. The term was coined retrospectively in 2017 by [Sofi Xian](https://sofi.pics/about) of the [Consumer Aesthetics Research Institute](https://cari.institute/). During the era of its prime, the style didn't have a specific name, but its web counterpart was often referred to as ["The Web 2.0 style"](https://snap2objects.com/2007/11/20/how-to-destroy-the-web-20-look/) by critics, as it was the main visual style of the new [social web](https://psu.pb.unizin.org/ist110/chapter/1-6-the-www-and-web-2-0/), in contrast to the static websites of Web 1.0. This look, and by extension Frutiger Aero were mainly popularized by Apple's [Aqua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aqua_\(user_interface\)) style, who first used skeuomorphism (the mimicking of real life objects) in the user interface of [Mac OS X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mac_OS_X_10.0) and the [Apple website](https://www.webdesignmuseum.org/gallery/apple-2000) as far back as the year 2000. 
"Frutiger Aero" derives its name from the [Frutiger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrian_Frutiger#Frutiger) font designed by [Adrian Frutiger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrian_Frutiger), which came to inspire many humanist sans-serif typefaces such as Microsoft's [Segoe UI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Segoe#Segoe_UI) and many other fonts used in computers in the early 2000s, and [Aero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windows_Aero) the visual design and user experience guidelines of Windows Vista. The acronym "Aero" stands for "Authentic, Energetic, Reflective, and Open." 
You probably have seen this style before in the glossy UI of Windows Vista and 7, the PS Vita, the Wii and Wii U, the PlayStation 3, or even on advertisements, stock images, and certain toys such as the [iDog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IDog) by Hasbro. Its main characteristics are bright and vibrant colors, glossy textures, glass/transparent materials, aurora borealis, [skeuomorphism](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Skeuomorphism), bubbles, bokeh, macro photographs of grass, futurism, and humanism. It succeeded the Y2K style, which was named after the [Year 2000 problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Year_2000_problem). 
For a more in depth look into the historical context and meaning of the aesthetic, go take a look at the [History](https://frutigeraeroarchive.org/history) section. You can also scroll down for some visual examples. 
For more information about the site, and to receive the latest updates via the **RSS feed** , head to the [Site Info](https://frutigeraeroarchive.org/site_info) page. You can also check my [blog](https://frutigeraeroarchive.org/blog/home) for writings on Frutiger Aero and old web related things, also available with RSS.
