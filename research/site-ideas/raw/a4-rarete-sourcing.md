#  Sourcing Requests 
Sourcing Requests is how clients ask The Rareté to look for a specific archival or vintage designer piece that isn't currently listed in the shop, such as a particular house, a specific runway season or look, a garment type, or a piece a client has seen referenced elsewhere and would like help locating.
Because each piece The Rareté carries is a one-of-a-kind vintage garment rather than restockable stock, not every request can be fulfilled, and submitting a request does not guarantee that a matching piece will be found.
This service sits alongside The Rareté's current listings across [Clothing](https://therarete.com/collections/clothing), [Dresses](https://therarete.com/collections/dresses), and single-designer collections such as [Chloé](https://therarete.com/collections/chloe) and [Valentino](https://therarete.com/collections/valentino), and the [Archival Collection](https://therarete.com/collections/archival-collection), which is geared toward stylist pulls, editorial use, and production. Clients working on a specific project, including stylists and production teams, are welcome to describe their timeline when submitting a request. For a Sourcing Request or any other question, clients can reach The Rareté directly through the [Contact page](https://therarete.com/pages/contact).

## Frequently asked questions
### What is a Sourcing Request?
+
A request for The Rareté to look for a specific archival or vintage designer piece, such as a particular house, season, look, or garment type, that isn't currently listed.
### Does submitting a request guarantee The Rareté can find the piece?
+
No. It's a search request, not a guarantee or a reservation.
### What information should I include in a Sourcing Request?
+
Include the designer, season or era, garment type, and any reference images or details that help describe the piece you're looking for.
### How long does sourcing take?
+
Timelines vary because each piece is a one-of-a-kind vintage garment rather than restockable inventory, and not every request can be fulfilled.
### Can stylists and production teams use this for a project deadline?
+
Yes; clients working on a specific project are welcome to describe their timeline when submitting a request.
### What if I'd rather browse current inventory first?
+
Explore Clothing, Dresses, or single-designer collections such as Chloé and Valentino, or the Archival Collection for stylist and editorial pulls.
The Rareté offers a refined edit of vintage and archival fashion, spotlighting era-defining pieces from the 1970s through the early 2000s. From rare runway garments to sculptural silhouettes and collectible accessories, each item is a study in timeless design - curated for collectors, stylists, and anyone chasing something truly one-of-a-kind.
