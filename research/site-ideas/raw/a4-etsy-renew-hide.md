# How to Renew or Hide Your Listings 
If an item has been purchased or its listing has expired, you can start selling it again by renewing the listing. New listings are set to renew automatically when they expire by default. You can choose to turn off automatic renewal so your listings can be renewed manually when they expire.
Just like posting a new listing, there will be a [$0.20 USD fee](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115014483627?segment=selling) charged to your Payment account each time you renew a listing. While this will give it a new listing date and a new expiration date (4 months from the renewal date), the renewed listing will still keep its original view count, number of favorites, and web address. 
If you don’t want your sold out listings to be visible to buyers anymore, you have the option to hide it instead of renewing it. 
## How to renew sold items
Renewing individual items 
  1. On Etsy.com, go to your **Shop Manager** and select **[Orders& Shipping](https://www.etsy.com/your/orders/sold/completed)**. On the Etsy Seller app, tap **Listings**.
  2. Select the gear icon next to the item you want to renew. On the Etsy Seller app, tap the three dots on the top of the listing. 
  3. Choose **Renew** and confirm your selection.

If you’re [selling an item with variations](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015664047-How-to-Add-Variations-for-Your-Listings?segment=selling) like different sizes or colors, each option will automatically have their quantity set to “1” when listings are renewed. 
Renewing multiple items 
  1. Go to your **Shop Manager**.
  2. Go to **[Listings](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/listings)**.
  3. Check the box for all the items you want to renew.
  4. Choose **Renew** and confirm your selection.


## How to renew active listings
Renewing active listings from the Shop Manager 
  1. Go to your **Shop Manager**.
  2. Go to **[Listings](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/listings)**.
  3. Check the boxes for the items you want to renew.
  4. Choose **Renew** and confirm your selection.


You can also select **Renew** from the gear icon next to the item.  
  

Renewing directly from the item’s listing page 
  1. Sign in to your Etsy account.
  2. Find your item’s listing page. There will be renewal options only visible to you.
  3. Choose **Renew** and confirm your selection.


## How to manage your renewal settings
You can choose if you want to automatically renew all of your expired listings or just a select few. Automatic renewal is the default option selected on new listings, so make sure that you double check the settings for any listings you want to renew manually. You can also manage your listings in the following ways:   

Setting listings to automatically renew 
  1. Go to **Listing Details**.
  2. Look for **Renewal options**.
  3. Select **Automatic**.


Renewing expired listings 
In an individual listing, you can check the box next to **Renew these listings automatically** in the renewal window. You can also renew multiple listings through the following steps:
  1. Check the boxes next to each item you want to manage.
  2. Select **Editing options**.
  3. Choose **Change renewal options**.
  4. Select **Automatic**.


If your shop is in [vacation mode](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015662947?segment=selling), your listings will still auto-renew if you've chosen this option.
Turning off automatic renewal 
  1. Go to your **Shop Manager**.
  2. Go to **[Listings](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/listings)**.
  3. Check the boxes next to each item with automatic renewal set up.
  4. Select **Editing options**.
  5. Choose **Change renewal options.**
  6. Select **Manual**.


## How to hide my sold listings
If you don’t want buyers to be able to see your sold out listings, you can choose to hide them. You will still be able to view the page, but buyers won't be able to access it on your public shop homepage.
  1. Go into **Settings** from your **Shop Manager.**
  2. Select **[Options](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/options)**.
  3. Navigate to the **Sold Listings** section.
  4. Select **No, hide them.**
  5. Hit the **Save** button.


The sales numbers of hidden listings will still be reflected in the tally of items sold seen in your shop header.
## How to find the expiration date for your listings
  1. Go to your **Shop Manager**.
  2. Go to **[Listings](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/listings)**.
  3. Select **Active**. The expiration date will be listed for each item.
  4. Use the sort feature to sort by expiration date.


You can also sign up to receive reminder emails when you have items that will expire within seven days.
  1. Go to **[Emails](https://www.etsy.com/your/account/email)** in your **Account settings**.
  2. Check the box next to **My listings are about to expire.**
  3. Hit the **Save** button.
