# How to Customize Your Shop's Appearance 
You can define your brand and show buyers what makes your shop unique by customizing your shop’s appearance. To give your shop more visibility, use creative elements like banners, strategically arrange the items of your shop, and feature items you want to showcase to buyers.

##  **How to customize your shop banner**  

Shop banners are the largest branding asset for your shop. This image appears when shoppers view your shop on the standard view of the website as well as on mobile devices.
To add your shop banners:
  1. On [Etsy.com](https://www.etsy.com), go to your [**Shop Manager**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/dashboard).
  2. Select the [pencil icon](https://www.etsy.com/shop/me/edit) next to your shop name under **Sales Channels**.
  3. Select **Banner to showcase your brand and listings** at the top of the page. 
  4. Choose the type of banner you want. 
  5. Choose **Done**.


You have 2 styles of banner to choose from: 
  * **Mini Banner:** A narrow image with a minimum size of 1200 x 160 pixels.
  * **Big Banner:** A large image with a minimum size of 1200 x 300 pixels.


If you do not want a banner for your shop, select **None**. This will move your listings higher up on the page and your logo will be the most prominent branding on your shop homepage. 
More advanced shop banner options, such as the Carousel banner or Collage banner, are available to [Etsy Plus subscribers](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360001730447?segment=selling). 
## **How to edit your order receipt banner**
You can personalize the receipts buyers receive when ordering from your shop by adding a custom banner to them.
To manage your order receipt banner:
  1. On [Etsy.com](https://www.etsy.com), go to your [**Shop Manager**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/dashboard).
  2. Choose **Settings**.
  3. Choose [**Info & Appearance**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me).
  4. Select **Choose file** next to **Order receipt banner**.


## **How to add a logo**
Adding a logo helps visually represent your shop, your brand, and what you sell. It’s prominently displayed at the top of your shop for buyers to see.
To add or edit a logo: 
  1. On [Etsy.com](http://www.etsy.com/), go to your [**Shop Manager**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/dashboard). 
  2. Select **Settings** , then select [**Your shop**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/settings/your-shop).
  3. Under **Logo** , select **Add** to add a logo or **Edit** to edit your logo.
  4. To add a logo, select **Add file** or drag and drop your file.
  5. Select the square (crop) icon to crop your image. Select the x to remove your image.
  6. Select **Save**.


You can also add or edit your logo in your [Shop home visual editor](https://www.etsy.com/shop/me/edit), which can be found by going to **Sales Channels** in your [**Shop Manager**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/dashboard) and selecting the pencil icon next to your shop name. You can also access your Shop home visual editor within [**Your shop**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/settings/your-shop) settings.
A good logo should: 
  * Reflect your shop’s personality 
  * Look good both big and small 
  * Use simple graphics or illustrations 
  * Keep any text short and easy to read 
  * Avoid long words or busy backgrounds


Your shop should have a logo to represent your business to buyers. Not having a logo may impact your [visibility in Etsy search](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/25869947521175?segment=selling).
## **How to arrange items in your shop**
Thoughtfully arranging the order of items in your shop is a great way to entice shoppers to browse your products. Much like a display in a physical shop, the way your products are arranged in your Etsy shop can make a big impression about your brand. 
To rearrange items in your shop:
  1. On [Etsy.com](http://www.etsy.com/), go to your [**Shop Manager**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/dashboard). 
  2. Select **Settings** , then select [**Your shop**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/settings/your-shop).
  3. Under **Listing order** , select **Edit**.
  4. Choose which listings are shown first in your shop. Select **Custom** or **Most recent**.
  5. Select**Save**.


If you select **Custom** , you'll need to set the order of your listings using your [Shop home visual editor](https://www.etsy.com/shop/me/edit). Your Shop home visual editor can be found by going to **Sales Channels** in your [**Shop Manager**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/dashboard) and selecting the pencil icon next to your shop name. You can also access your Shop home visual editor within [**Your shop**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/settings/your-shop) settings. Select **Rearrange items**.
To move listings between pages, select the **Move to page** dropdown menu on the individual listing. 
When listings are renewed, they'll go back to their custom location. New listings go to the top of your shop.
It could take up to 10 minutes for your listings to rearrange on your public shop homepage.
Tips for arranging your products in your shop:
  * Be cohesive with imagery and style.
  * Update arrangements seasonally to reflect shopping trends.
  * Keep a full shop to avoid looking sparse. 
  * Use the [best thumbnail](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360016260113?segment=selling) image for each product. 


## **How to feature items**
**Featuring items** is a way to showcase 4 listings you especially want shoppers to see when they visit your public shop homepage.
These items will appear just above your shop announcement.
A buyer can choose the listing image in the featured row or in its place with the rest of your items to get to the same listing page.
To feature your items: 
  1. On [Etsy.com](https://www.etsy.com/), go to your [**Shop Manager**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/dashboard).
  2. Choose [**Listings**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/listings).
  3. Select the star icon on the four listings you want to feature. You can only feature active listings. Choosing more than four items adds them to a queue so they'll be displayed if the items before them get removed from your shop.
  4. In the **Listing status** sidebar menu, find **Featured listings** and select **Manage.**
  5. Drag and drop the listings in the order you want them displayed. 
  6. Select **Done.**


The changes you make will take effect in your shop immediately.
