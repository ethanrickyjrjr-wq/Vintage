##  Founded in 2016... 
Lily Kaizer dreamed of a place where brides and seasoned vintage collectors could come to find magical pieces in a magical setting. Lily envisioned a destination where you could not only try on, but also receive the expert guidance and elevated experience of high-end bridal salons.
With a love for designer vintage, couture, and standout pieces from the ‘30s through the ‘00s, Lily’s approach was groundbreaking in the bridal space and vintage industry.
Over the past decade, Happy Isles has grown. Now, Lily and a passionate team of stylists are here to help you find the vintage dress of your dreams in both[ **Los Angeles**](https://thehappyisles.com/pages/los-angeles "https://thehappyisles.com/pages/los-angeles") and [**New York**](https://thehappyisles.com/pages/new-york "https://thehappyisles.com/pages/new-york").
