## Shop Vintage Lighting at Pamono
At Pamono, shopping for vintage lighting is like taking a stroll through all the brightest spots in design history. We’ve got all the greatest hits and the lesser-known treasures, from the era when electric lamps first became common in modern homes, right on through the end of the 20th century. We even have some antique [candelabras](https://www.pamono.eu/catalogsearch/result/?q=candelabra) rewired to function in contemporary environments.
Early standouts from our vintage lighting collection include minimalist works by [ Bauhaus](https://www.pamono.eu/bauhaus-products) stars like [Wilhelm Wagenfeld](https://www.pamono.eu/designers/wilhelm-wagenfeld) and [Marianne Brandt](https://www.pamono.eu/designers/marianne-brandt), as well as opulent French [ Art Deco sconces](https://www.pamono.eu/art-deco-sconces) and ceiling lights, not to mention the honest simplicity of German industrial spotlights and pendants. Our vast midcentury modern selection includes classics from design geniuses like [Christian Dell](https://www.pamono.eu/designers/christian-dell), [Greta Magnusson Grossman](https://www.pamono.eu/designers/greta-magnusson-grossman), [Angelo Lelli](https://www.pamono.eu/designers/angelo-lelli), [Verner Panton](https://www.pamono.eu/designers/verner-panton), and [Gino Sarfatti](https://www.pamono.eu/designers/gino-sarfatti), as well as visionary manufacturers like [Arteluce](https://www.pamono.eu/makers/arteluce), [Artemide](https://www.pamono.eu/makers/artemide), [Fog & Mørup](https://www.pamono.eu/makers/fog-morup), [Flos](https://www.pamono.eu/makers/flos), [Louis Poulsen](https://www.pamono.eu/makers/louis-poulsen), [Lyfa](https://www.pamono.eu/makers/lyfa), [Stilnovo](https://www.pamono.eu/makers/stilnovo), [Jieldé](https://www.pamono.eu/makers/jielde), and more. And, of course, lots of masterpieces from the always brilliant Scandinavian designer [Poul Henningsen](https://www.pamono.eu/designers/poul-henningsen). The 1960s and ’70s brought biomorphic, mushroom-shaped lamps in vividly hued aluminum, acrylic, and plastic, as well as intricate, blown-glass chandeliers by Murano. Lighting design in the 20th century rounded out with experimental table lamps, floor lamps, and hanging lamps by postmodern pioneers like Albert Fraser, [Ingo Maurer](https://www.pamono.eu/designers/ingo-maurer), and [Memphis](https://www.pamono.eu/makers/memphis) designers like [Ettore Sottsass](https://www.pamono.eu/designers/ettore-sottsass) and Martine Bedin.
No matter which style you desire, we’ve got you covered. At Pamono, you’ll find thousands of vintage lights, lamps, [ chandeliers﻿﻿](https://www.pamono.eu/chandeliers) and more from the dawn of the industrial revolution, right on through to today—with an emphasis on the ever-popular, beyond timeless works of midcentury modernism. Every object tells a story. Make one yours today.
