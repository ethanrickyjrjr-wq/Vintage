## 1. Selling Basics
Keeping in mind these basic requirements will set you up for success on Etsy. 
**a. What can be sold on Etsy**
Etsy is a unique marketplace. Buyers come here to purchase items that they might not find anywhere else, or to spark their own creativity. As such, everything listed for sale on Etsy must be made, designed, handpicked, or sourced by a seller. 
To learn about what items are allowed on Etsy, review Read about Etsy’s Creativity Standards [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20261027222419/https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity).
If you sell on Etsy, you need to provide the following information for each of your items:
  1. An accurate representation of how the item was made, by whom, and where it is being shipped from. 
  2. An accurate description of any [shop members](https://web.archive.org/web/20261027222419/https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000336867-Roles-for-Shop-Members?segment=selling) who are involved in making your items or running your business in the [About section](https://web.archive.org/web/20261027222419/https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628487-How-to-Edit-Your-Shop-s-About-Section?segment=selling) of your shop.
  3. If you work with a production partner, you must disclose that production partner in your relevant listings. Read more about [working with production partners](https://web.archive.org/web/20261027222419/https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000336547-Working-with-Production-Partners-on-Etsy?segment=selling). 
  4. Your own photographs or video content —not stock photos, artistic renderings, or photos used by other sellers or sites. Read more about [listing image requirements](https://web.archive.org/web/20261027222419/https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/listing-image-requirements/253962679005), including limited exceptions for custom items and items made with production partner assistance.
  5. If an item is created through the use of artificial intelligence, you must disclose this in your relevant listings. Read more about our stance on AI creations [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20261027222419/https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1275449912004). 


For information on how to list personalized items, please see [this Help article](https://web.archive.org/web/20261027222419/https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000344528). 
We encourage you to be transparent about how your craft and party supplies were made and where your materials come from. You can disclose whether your items have social or environmental attributes, such as [organic](https://web.archive.org/web/20261027222419/https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/organic-materials/242696248617) or [recycled](https://web.archive.org/web/20261027222419/https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/recycled-content/239326729662). Dropshipping and reselling are not allowed on Etsy, except in the specific cases outlined in [this Help article](https://web.archive.org/web/20261027222419/https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/23948763872151), such as craft supplies.

  3. Purchasing from a seller for the sole purpose of leaving a negative review;
  4. Maliciously clicking on a competitor's ads in order to drain that member's advertising budget, also known as "click fraud."

## 3. Feedback, Cases and Your Success
**a. Reviews**
Reviews are a great way for you to build a reputation on Etsy. Buyers can leave a [review](https://web.archive.org/web/20261027222419/https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115013197687), including a one to five star rating and a photograph or video of their purchase, within 100 days from the earlier date of their item being delivered (where the shipping carrier’s delivery record is available) or their item’s max estimated delivery date. If an estimated delivery date is not available, the review window opens after the order’s [processing time](https://web.archive.org/web/20261027222419/https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015588087) and shipping time have elapsed. Buyers of digital items can leave a review for 100 days from the first time they download the item or within 12 months of purchase (whichever happens first). Buyers can [edit](https://web.archive.org/web/20261027222419/https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015521588) their review, including the photograph or video, any number of times during that 100 day period.
On the rare occasion you receive an unfavorable review, you can reach out to the buyer or leave a response.
