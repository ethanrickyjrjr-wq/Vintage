# How to Add a Shop Announcement and Shop Title 
Your shop announcement appears under the listings on your shop homepage. You can update your announcement to alert shoppers to important information like a sale or if you are away on vacation. 
To add or edit your shop announcement on the Etsy Seller app: 
  1. Select **More** from the navigation bar. 
  2. Scroll to **Shop settings**. 
  3. Select **Shop appearance**. 
  4. Select **Shop announcement**. 
  5. Add your announcement. 
  6. Choose **Save**. 


To add or edit your shop announcement on Etsy.com:
  1. On [Etsy.com](https://www.etsy.com/), go to your **[Shop Manager](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/dashboard).**
  2. Select the pencil icon next to your shop name under **Sales Channels**.
  3. Go to the **Announcement** section.
  4. Select **Add a shop announcement** or **Edit**.
  5. Add your announcement.
  6. Choose **Save**.


Keep your announcement short and sweet. You can add more detailed information to your [About](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015628487?segment=selling) section, [public profile](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015651948?segment=selling), [shop policies](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115014372467?segment=selling), and item descriptions.
If your shop isn't open to the public yet, you won’t be able to add an announcement until you open your shop. [Learn more about setting up your shop](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015672808?segment=selling).
### **Why doesn’t the date posted on my shop announcement match when I made it?**
If your shop’s language is not set to English, the date may not update to reflect when you made your new shop announcement. You can manually add the date to the first line of your shop announcement so buyers will see when you made it.
##  **How to add a shop title**
Your shop title is a brief bit of text you can use to describe your shop, and is one aspect of your shop that you can use to influence Search Engine Optimization (SEO). 
When you enter a new shop title or edit an existing one, you'll be able to preview how it will look in Google search results.
[Learn more about SEO](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015663987?segment=selling).
To add or edit your shop title:
  1. On [Etsy.com](http://www.etsy.com), go to your [**Shop Manager**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/dashboard)
  2. Select the pencil icon next to your shop name under **Sales Channels**. 
  3. Right below your shop name, select **Add a shop title**. 
  4. Describe your shop in one sentence, or choose **Edit** if you've already got a title.


If your shop isn't open yet, you won’t be able to edit your shop title. [Learn more about setting up your shop](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015672808?segment=selling).
  * Shop titles can be up to 55 characters long.


  * They appear right under your shop name on your shop homepage.


  * Many sellers make it a tagline or slogan, or they state what they sell.
