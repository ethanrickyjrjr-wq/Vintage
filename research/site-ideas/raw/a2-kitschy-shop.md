## Where these come from
Everything here was dug out of an estate sale, a thrift store, or somebody's very confused garage. No wholesale pallets. No drop-shipped nonsense. Just one woman, a full tank of gas, and a deeply unreasonable amount of restraint the next morning.
You can't buy taste this good at a big box store. You can, however, buy it right here.

## The sweet spot around here: 80s through Y2K.
Leg warmers, jelly shoes, butterfly clips, tiny sunglasses, and every other glorious relic your childhood refuses to let go of.
