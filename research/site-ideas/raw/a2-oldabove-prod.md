## Description
**I’m very pleased to offer this dazzling 1960s Hollywood Regency crystal chandelier!**
WOW!
Brass crystal chandeliers were wildly popular in the 1950s, 1960s, and 1970s, and many iterations are easily found.
But this chandelier is…different:  
It’s large: a whopping 24-inches wide.  
It has eight arms (rather than the more common 5 arms).  
Each arm has TWO candles! So, 16 candles total! I’ve never before offered a 16-candle chandelier!  
The curvaceous arms are richly detailed.
The crystals are hand-cut and polished.
The brass, including the ceiling canopy, is cast brass. The brass isn’t polished bright and retains its original blackened finish.
The vertical wire is new, and cloth-covered like the original. Nice.
This is a very special chandelier.
**NOTE: The upper S-curve arms will be unscrewed for shipping. They will easily be reattached.**
**This fixture is ready to hang, and comes with all the mounting hardware needed!**
**DETAILS** : The fixture accepts candelabra base 25W bulbs (not included). Clear flame-style LED bulbs would look well.  
**COLOR** : See above.  
**SIZE** : See above. As well, the fixture hangs down about 30-inches. I can remove up to 7-inches of chain; I can add chain.  
**CONDITION** :  
I love writing this: there’s no damage.  
Most of the sockets and their wiring are original (and in fine shape) with new paper insulators.  
The candle covers are new.  
The crystals are new, high-quality, and hand-cut and polished. Gorgeous.  
**SHIPPING** : Insured shipping in the Continental US will be…FREE!
