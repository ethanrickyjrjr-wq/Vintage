##  Automate Hiding or Publishing products by Catalogs (Markets group), Sales channels or Status 
Improve buying experience for your customers by hiding Products they can't buy! The main challenge faced due to sold-out products is that customers may lose interest in shopping when they look at sold-out products, which may lead to a destructive impact on sales. Hence it's essential to manage sold-out products to have good sales. The app facilitates merchants to automate the process of hiding and publishing the products.

  * Automatically hide sold-out products and publish when they are back in-stock. 
  * Hide/Publish by Catalogs (Markets group), Sales channels, Products status 
  * Configurable to hide from search, collections but accessible via direct url 
  * Hide products with no images
