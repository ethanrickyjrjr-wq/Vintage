Founder Eeva Musacchia started Eveliina Vintage in the early 1970’s in Helsinki Finland. From there she grew to having a store front in Miami Beach, simultaneously showing in NYC trade shows. At the same age as Eeva was when she started the brand, her twin daughters Emilia & Amanda joined Eveliina Vintage. Split between Miami and New York City, the ladies show in London, Los Angeles and New York while taking on clients by appointment to shop poolside in Miami Florida. Eveliina pieces are rare, romantic, one of kind finds for every day use.
