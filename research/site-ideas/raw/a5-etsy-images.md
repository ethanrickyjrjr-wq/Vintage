## **File type requirements**
All images in your shop should be one of these file types: .jpg, .gif, .png, .svg, or .heic. These are the only image file types Etsy supports. 
Animated .gif files & transparent .png files are not supported. If a file contains transparency, the transparent parts of the image will appear black on Etsy.

### **Listing images**
We recommend your listing photos have a width and height of at least 2000 pixels or more. Your first listing photo should have a width and height of at least 635 pixels to avoid showing up lower in searches.
You can usually find an image’s dimensions and resolution in the image details on your phone or on your computer.
Images larger than 1MB in file size may not finish uploading, especially on a slower internet connection. If you're finding that your photo uploads are taking longer than normal, [follow these troubleshooting steps](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015650068?segment=selling).
### **Logo**
The recommended size for logos is 500 x 500px. [Learn more about uploading a logo.](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663247-How-to-Manage-Your-Shop-Photos-and-Banners?segment=selling)
### **Profile photo**
The recommended size for profile photos is 400 x 400px. [Learn more about uploading a profile photo.](http://www.etsy.com/help/article/66?segment=selling)
### **Team logo**
The recommended size for team logos is 170 x 100px.
[Learn more about Etsy Teams](http://www.etsy.com/help/article/332).
### **Order receipt banner**
The minimum required size for order receipt banner photos is 760 x 100px. [Learn more about uploading an order receipt banner here.](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015663247-How-to-Manage-Your-Shop-Photos-and-Banners?segment=selling)
### **Mini shop banner**
The minimum required size for mini shop banners is 1200 x 160px. The recommended size is 1600 x 213px. [Learn more about uploading a mini shop banner here.](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015663247-How-to-Manage-Your-Shop-Photos-and-Banners?segment=selling)
### **Big shop banner**
The minimum required size for big shop banners is 1200 x 300px. The recommended size is**1** 600 x 400px.[Learn more about uploading a big shop banner here.](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015663247-How-to-Manage-Your-Shop-Photos-and-Banners?segment=selling)
### **Carousel banner**
The recommended size for carousel banners is 1200 x 300px. [Learn more about uploading a carousel banner here.](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015663247-How-to-Manage-Your-Shop-Photos-and-Banners?segment=selling)
### **Collage banner**
The size recommendation depends on the number of images you want in your collage:
  * For 2 images, the minimum required size is 600 x 300px.
  * For 3 images, the minimum required size is 400 x 300px.
  * For 4 images, the minimum required size is 300 x 300px.


The [carousel and collage banners](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360001730447?segment=selling) are only available to sellers subscribed to Etsy Plus. [Learn more about Etsy Plus](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360001589928?segment=selling).
## **Image authenticity requirements**
Your listing images should help buyers understand exactly what they will receive:
  * All images must accurately reflect the item’s true appearance, including color, condition, scale, and materials. 
  * Your listing must include at least one unedited photo of the actual item against a real background, meaning the photo hasn’t been edited or changed in any way. Some [exceptions](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/listing-image-requirements/253962679005) may apply depending on the types of items you sell.
  * Outside of your required unedited photo, you can still use photo-editing tools, including AI, to adjust lighting or color, crop or resize images, or change backgrounds.
  * You can't use editing to hide flaws, conceal damage, or edit an item’s appearance to make it look different from what the buyer will receive. 
  * You can’t use images that create false expectations about what the buyer will receive. 
  * If you sell functional guides like patterns, recipes, or instructions, you’ll also need to show an actual result made using your instructions, not an AI-generated or simulated result, so buyers know what to expect.


Beyond these requirements, here are a few best practices to keep in mind when using AI in your listings:
  * Make your first listing photo the clearest, most realistic representation of the item buyers will receive. Because it’s the first image buyers see, take extra care to make sure it accurately represents your item and isn’t misleading.
  * If you use AI to edit any of your listing photos, note whether images were edited in your listing description. This gives buyers more context about how your listing images were created and helps you be transparent about your use of AI.

## **How to resize images**
You can find websites that help you resize images online. Use a website, Photoshop, or a similar software to resize your images.
If you decrease the size of an image, it won’t lower the resolution. However, if you increase the size of an image, you may lose resolution and pixelation may occur.
Images may appear less sharp after being uploaded due to compression of the image files. We do this in order to improve the overall performance of the page. Image sizes are optimized for mobile displays.
### **Color mode & profile**
If your image appears in the wrong colors on Etsy.com, convert your images to sRGB using an image editing software before uploading to ensure that the colors you see on the site will match the originals. We convert images to use the sRGB color profile (CMYK color profile is for print only).

### **Image orientation**
The first photo in a listing should be horizontal (landscape) or square. This ensures that the center focal point of the image appears in the [cropped thumbnail views](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360016260113?segment=selling).
Your first listing photo also dictates the shape of the photos that follow. Uploading the same shape photos for all listing photos will create a more cohesive browsing experience.
### **Image rotation**
Etsy auto-rotates listing photos, but keep in mind that this depends on information included by your camera in the image file. 
If your listing images are still uploading sideways, try using a photo editing service to rotate your images, or retake the photo while holding the camera in a different position. Remember that the orientation of your subject matter should match the orientation of the camera.
### **Listing image thumbnails**
When creating or editing a listing, use the listing thumbnail adjustment tool to set and preview how your listing image may look in different places across Etsy. Make sure that your thumbnail images have enough of a border that they can be cropped to square, portrait, and landscape thumbnails without losing some of the product.
Our recommendations are:
  * Try to capture images of your item further back than you think you’ll need. This will give you the ability to crop in without losing parts of your item. Make sure the most important part of the item is focused in the center. 
  * Position your image so your subject is clear in all 3 views used across Etsy.
  * Avoid square crops. Upload horizontal or landscape images.


### **Tips for listing images**
Take your original picture from a far enough vantage point where you can see the entire product with extra negative space around your product.
Taking horizontally oriented picture means you’re able to use more of the available space within the thumbnail frame. Vertical images will require more information to be cropped out. 
Use the placement tool when adding images to your listings to perform fine-tuning adjustments on what appears within your thumbnail on your listing. 
Your final image will most likely be cropped down from the original image that was initially created. Confirming the image was produced at the highest resolution would be advised to meet the minimum suggested size for listing images.
