George Foreman (42), named one of the 25 greatest fighters of all time by Ring Magazine, fought Evander Holyfield (28) in the "_Battle for the Ages_ " this month in Atlantic City, New Jersey. He lost on points but did go the full 12 rounds to the surprise of many boxing experts. On November 5 1994 Big George would win back the Heavyweight title by knocking out Michael Moorer in Las Vegas, Nevada—he was 45, the oldest fighter ever to win the crown. Even George admits that now he's better known for the grill than he is for his boxing. It's a shame because without doubt he is one of the best. George was interviewed in the [December 1995](https://www.vintageplayboymags.co.uk/90s/Dec/05.htm) issue.
