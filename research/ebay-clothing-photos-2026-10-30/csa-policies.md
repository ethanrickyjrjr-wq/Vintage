## Required item specifics in Clothing, Shoes & Accessories
Get your listings in front of more buyers
Starting July 20, 2011, new and revised listings in Clothing, Shoes & Accessories must include the following item specifics: Brand, Style, Size Type and Size. Required item specifics may vary by category—use the look-up table below to view the required item specifics by category and exceptions. [Learn more](https://pages.ebay.com/sellerinformation/news/clothingshoesaccessories.html) about the new requirements.  
  
New item specifics values are available now—start using them in your listings. Add item specifics to existing listings to get them in front of more buyers. Get general [tips and tools to edit listings](https://pages.ebay.com/sellerinformation/howtosell/EditingTipsandTools.html).
