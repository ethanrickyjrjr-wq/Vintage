## Findings

**Photos (eBay's own pages):** up to 24 per listing; first photo is the search thumbnail, whole item centred and face-on; 1600 x 1600 recommended, 500 x 500 minimum, 1:1 or 16:9; natural light, no flash, no filters; solid background, white or light for dark clothes and black or dark for light clothes; front, back, labels, closeups of unique features, and closeups of every flaw; a ruler or coin if size is unclear; no added text, logos, borders or watermarks; no stock photos for used items. eBay's own example is a denim jacket on pure white: front, back, sleeve, collar with label, front buttons, back buttons.

**Condition for pre-owned clothing (vintage clothing categories included):** Pre-owned - Excellent, Good, Fair. Signs of wear and every imperfection must be shown and described. A wrong condition can cost search placement and cause returns and negative feedback.

**Title:** brand, type, colour, size, material, in a logical order, no acronyms, no ALL CAPS, no asterisks. eBay's example: "Lululemon Mens Cashmere V-Neck Sweater Medium Blue Long Sleeve Size M". 80 characters.

**Description and sizing:** condition, size, colour, material, brand, anything notable; blended fabric with each percentage; flaws with photos; measurements and brand sizing information, in inches and centimetres. eBay's measurement examples: men's shirts pit to pit and sleeve from the centre of the neck to the cuff; dresses waist and length; pants and shorts inseam; shoes length and width.

**A live example (viewed in Chrome, 2026-10-30):** "Vintage 1970s Lace Boho Prairie Gunne Sax Maxi Dress Wedding Regency", $450, 42 watchers, Pre-owned - Excellent. Main photo: the whole dress on a mannequin beside a window in natural light, with a gallery of 6 or more thumbnails.
