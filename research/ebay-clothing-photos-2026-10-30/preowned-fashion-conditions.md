# List with pre-owned conditions
## Learn how to describe your pre-loved fashion listings like a pro.
## Why accurate conditions matter
### Build trust
Buyers can shop with confidence when they clearly understand an item’s condition.
### Help increase visibility
Optimize your listings to ensure they appear in the correct search results.
### Reduce returns
Manage buyer expectations about an item's condition and minimize the likelihood of returns.
![A group of clothing items and a chair](https://ir.ebaystatic.com/pictures/aw/pics/sneakers/Condition_8a5a127671.png)
## Choose a pre-owned condition
  * 1
### Pre-owned - Excellent
Like-new items that have been previously worn, but with little-to-no signs of wear.
  * 2
### Pre-owned - Good
Gently-used fashion items with light signs of wear, with or without visible flaws.
  * 3
### Pre-owned - Fair
Significantly visible flaws, heavy signs of wear, and/or missing or damaged components.

###  How do I revise my listings to select the new item condition options? 
You can revise your listings from [_Selling Activity_](https://www.ebay.com/mys/overview) in My eBay or your [_Active listings_](https://www.ebay.com/sh/lst/active) in Seller Hub. You can also revise your listings in bulk using one of our [_bulk listing tools_](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/listings/listing-tips/bulk-listing-tool?id=4160) or within Seller Hub.
How to bulk edit in Seller Hub:
  1. Check the [pre-filtered list](https://www.ebay.com/sh/lst/active) of your items.
  2. Go to Seller Hub and sign in to your account.
  3. In the Listings tab, use the filters to narrow down your active listings by category: Footwear, Handbags, Watches, Jewelry.
  4. After filtering, select the listings you want to update and click the “Edit” button.
  5. Make bulk edits in the "Revise Listings" page.
  6. If you have a lot of edits to make, you may find it easier to reorder the columns so the required fields are on the same screen without scrolling. In the editing interface, select "Customize Columns" to reorder, add, or remove columns as needed.Once the "Condition" column is visible, choose the column header to sort your listings by item condition (e.g., New, Pre-Owned, etc).


###  What happens if the item condition I’ve selected doesn’t accurately describe my item? 
Selecting the correct item condition is your responsibility and crucial for your listings’ success. If the item condition is inaccurate, your listings might not appear in relevant search results, and you could experience increased returns or negative feedback from buyers, which can impact your seller standards.
###  What if a buyer wants to return an item because the item condition wasn’t as expected? 
In this case, please work with your buyer to resolve the issue and avoid a ‘case closed without seller resolution.’
