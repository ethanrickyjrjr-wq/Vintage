# List and manage products on Marketplace Connect
After you connect your Shopify store to a marketplace, you can list your products to that marketplace. After you list your products, you can manage them in the **Listings** section of Marketplace Connect.
Review the following listing modes that you can use to manage your Marketplace Connect product listings:
  * [Single edit mode](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/marketplaces/marketplace-connect/products/manage/list-and-edit-products#single-edit) lets you add or make changes to an individual product, and is best suited for smaller product catalogs.
  * [Bulk edit mode](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/marketplaces/marketplace-connect/products/manage/list-and-edit-products#bulk-edit) lets you list and manage multiple products or entire catalogs on a marketplace in bulk.
  * [Attribute mapping mode](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/marketplaces/marketplace-connect/products/customize#attribute-mapping) lets you customize how all your products display across all your listings for a marketplace.


If you have existing listings on your connected marketplaces, then refer to [_Link existing marketplace listings to your Shopify products_](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/marketplaces/marketplace-connect/products/link-existing).
For additional support with listing products to Marketplace Connect, you can use the [chat feature in the app](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/shopify-marketplace-connect).
