## Frequently Asked Questions
How much does FLUF Connect cost? +
Starter is $9/month, Seller $29/month, Pro $149/month, and Priority is custom. Every plan has unlimited crosslisting. Paying yearly saves 40%.
What payment methods does FLUF Connect accept? +
All major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), processed securely by Stripe.
Can I change or cancel my plan at any time? +
Yes, from your billing settings. No contracts or fees. Upgrades apply now and you pay the difference; downgrades apply at renewal. If you cancel, your listings and data are kept.
Do I pay per listing or per crosslist? +
No. One monthly price covers unlimited listings on unlimited marketplaces.
Is FLUF Connect cheaper than List Perfectly or Vendoo? +
For many resellers, yes. Every plan includes unlimited crosslisting, inventory sync and Vinted, from a lower starting price. Check each provider's site for their current pricing.
Does FLUF Connect support Vinted? +
Yes, on every plan. Automated relisting for Vinted starts at Seller.
