# Frequently asked questions related to Shopify [​](https://docs.crosslist.com/knowledge-base/marketplaces/shopify/shopify-faq#frequently-asked-questions-related-to-shopify)
## Does Crosslist use metadata or variants on Shopify? [​](https://docs.crosslist.com/knowledge-base/marketplaces/shopify/shopify-faq#does-crosslist-use-metadata-or-variants-on-shopify)
Crosslist uses **variants** , not metadata.
When importing listings from Shopify, Crosslist takes the **first variant** available. At the moment, Crosslist does **not support multiple variants** within a single listing.
When posting to Shopify, Crosslist creates a listing as a **variant** and includes the relevant elements such as **size** , **condition** , and other attributes as defined in the Crosslist app.
Currently, Crosslist does not support Shopify metadata.
