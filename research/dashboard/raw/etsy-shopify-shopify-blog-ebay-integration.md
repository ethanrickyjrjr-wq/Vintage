## Connecting your Shopify store to your eBay account
Several third-party apps help you integrate your eBay account with your Shopify store. Those apps, available in the [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/search?q=ebay+integration&st_source=autocomplete&surface_detail=autocomplete_searches "shopify apps ebay integration"), all differ slightly but offer some of the following benefits:
  * Real-time syncing of inventory and sales.
  * Consolidation of inventory and order management processes between Shopify and eBay.
  * Exposure to more potential customers on eBay’s global marketplace.
  * Automation and bulk import features to save time.
  * Single app access to multiple marketplaces and [online selling sites](https://www.shopify.com/blog/online-selling-sites) to integrate with your Shopify store.
  * Access to global eBay marketplaces with unique customers in each region.

## Shopify eBay integration apps
Browse these apps to compare features, prices, and benefits. Choose the best one to connect your Shopify store to the eBay sales channel.
### 1. [CedCommerce eBay Integration](https://apps.shopify.com/ebay-integration)
CedCommerce is a bulk listing tool that allows Shopify store owners to manage their inventory and sales across both their online store and the eBay marketplace. The app’s features include the capability to import eBay listings into Shopify, to sync fulfillment details between Shopify and your eBay store, and more.
**Cost:** Free to $89/month, depending on plan.
**Noteworthy 5-star review:** “I have had a great experience with the app and the customer service is excellent. I am new to selling on eBay but putting products from my store to eBay is easy.” —Reeves Fitness
**Key features:**
  * List up to 10 products with the free plan and manage and sync up to 100.
  * Use the bulk product upload feature to automate workflow.
  * Sync inventory across Shopify and eBay in real time.
  * Create and apply templates to efficiently add listings.
  * Connect multiple eBay accounts to the app.


### 2. [Shopify Marketplace Connect (formerly Codisto)](https://apps.shopify.com/search?q=etsy)
Convert your Shopify store into an omnichannel platform with Shopify Marketplace Connect’s automation features that sync with popular online marketplaces like eBay, Amazon, Target, and Walmart.
**Cost:** Free with your Shopify plan up to 50 orders per month, then a 1% transaction fee per order up to $99/month.
**Noteworthy 5-star review:** “I have been using Shopify apps: Marketplace Connector for a few months now and I am very impressed with its performance. It has helped me to expand my online business to multiple marketplaces, such as Amazon, eBay, and Etsy, without any hassle.” —mobilemasters
**Key features:**
  * Connect eBay and other marketplaces with one app.
  * Sell globally with automated currency conversion.
  * Use bulk listing features to create new listings or link existing ones.
  * Sync inventory, orders, pricing, and product details in real time.
  * Manage eBay orders directly from Shopify.  



### 3. [eBay Importer by Infoshore](https://apps.shopify.com/epi)
With just a few clicks, eBay Importer by Infoshore enables store owners to easily import eBay listings into Shopify. Automatically import all active listings from your eBay account to your Shopify store, including images and variants.
**Cost:** Free to $59.99/month, depending on plan.
**Noteworthy 5-star review:** “I have used the app to manage the inventory from my existing eBay store and it has proved to be a convenient tool for updating prices and listings into Shopify.” —Soul Fashion
**Key features:**
  * Synchronize inventory and prices between the eBay channel and Shopify.
  * Fetch all eBay listings and import them in bulk, from five on the free plan to 5,000 on the Gold plan.  

  * Try the free plan to test the app without risk.
  * Customer support.

## Shopify eBay integration FAQ
### How do I import my eBay listings into my Shopify store?
You can import eBay listings into your Shopify store using a partner app from the Shopify App Store. There are several apps that integrate with Shopify and your eBay seller account to sync inventory and orders. These eBay Shopify apps offer the ability to bulk import eBay orders and listings with a few clicks. Many also offer automation that minimizes friction for online sellers by auto-importing and syncing listings.
### Is there an eBay app that syncs with Shopify?
There are several apps that connect your eBay account to your Shopify store to manage orders and inventory between the two sales channels. The app that's best for your business depends on your needs and budget. Consider whether you need it to import orders or offer real time sync, or if you’re looking for a free app to test selling on eBay with Shopify. Compare features and plans of each app for the best fit.
### What are the benefits of integrating my Shopify store with my eBay account?
When you integrate your Shopify store and your eBay account, you get more brand exposure across multiple sales channels. Widen your reach by selling your products both through your own store and eBay marketplaces globally. This omnichannel approach can give your business instant exposure to new audiences and potential customers.
