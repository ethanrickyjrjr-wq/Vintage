## FAQs
[](https://www.vendoo.co/mobile-app)
### Does Vendoo offer an app for Android and IOS users?
Yes, Vendoo's mobile app is available for both Android and iOS users to manage online selling apps, providing a seamless crosslisting experience across various devices.
[](https://www.vendoo.co/mobile-app)
### How much is the Vendoo mobile app?
The Vendoo mobile app is available for free download. For users who already have a Vendoo subscription plan, the mobile app can be used as part of their existing plan at no extra cost, offering full access to all the features included in their subscription whereas other online selling apps charge extra.
[](https://www.vendoo.co/mobile-app)
### What Vendoo features are available in the Vendoo mobile app?
The Vendoo app includes these features: background remover, sale detection, template and label manager, multi-quantity items management, a photo editor, comprehensive inventory management where you can create new items, delist and relist, and 8 different marketplace connections. You get the features of top selling apps in one.
[](https://www.vendoo.co/mobile-app)
### How does the sale detection feature work on mobile?
Wherever you go, Vendoo will detect your sales. However, your computer must be on and connected to the marketplaces for sale detection and auto delist to work in the mobile app.
