## FAQ
### Plans and listings
Which plan do I need?
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f60c731c7ffa0706798ef87/6a02edbb168e7e4e5ae1a0b9_7d0c44e7f9f397acfb5cb026b89a734e_chevron.svg)
It depends on how many stores you connect and how many live listings you have. Essential covers 1 eBay account and up to 100 listings. Growth covers 2 eBay accounts and up to 500 listings. Professional covers unlimited eBay accounts and 2,000 to 50,000+ listings. It's also the only plan that connects Shopify, Amazon, Etsy, Walmart, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, TikTok Shop and Facebook.
How are listings counted?
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f60c731c7ffa0706798ef87/6a02edbb168e7e4e5ae1a0b9_7d0c44e7f9f397acfb5cb026b89a734e_chevron.svg)
Every active listing on every connected store and account counts once. An active listing is one that is live on the marketplace, whatever its quantity. Drafts and ended listings don't count.  
  
Example: 300 listings on Shopify, 300 on one eBay account and 200 on a second eBay account make 800 listings, so you'd need Professional.  
  
You can check your count at any time under Listings Limit Usage on your Billing page.
I sell on several eBay sites. Do I pay for each one?
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f60c731c7ffa0706798ef87/6a02edbb168e7e4e5ae1a0b9_7d0c44e7f9f397acfb5cb026b89a734e_chevron.svg)
No. Your plan limits only the number of eBay accounts you connect and your number of live listings. If one eBay account sells on several sites, like eBay US, eBay Motors, eBay UK or DE, you can connect all of them at no extra charge. Their live listings still count toward your listing limit.
How much is Professional above 2,000 listings?
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f60c731c7ffa0706798ef87/6a02edbb168e7e4e5ae1a0b9_7d0c44e7f9f397acfb5cb026b89a734e_chevron.svg)
Professional comes in tiers of 2,000, 5,000, 10,000, 25,000 and 50,000 listings. Choose your tier on the Professional plan above to see its price. Selling more than 50,000 listings? Contact us for a custom plan.
What happens if I reach my listing limit?
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f60c731c7ffa0706798ef87/6a02edbb168e7e4e5ae1a0b9_7d0c44e7f9f397acfb5cb026b89a734e_chevron.svg)
Your existing listings, sync and orders keep running. You won't be able to add or import new listings until you upgrade or end some listings. We never change your plan or charge you without your approval. Your new limit applies as soon as you upgrade.
How many teammates can I add?
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f60c731c7ffa0706798ef87/6a02edbb168e7e4e5ae1a0b9_7d0c44e7f9f397acfb5cb026b89a734e_chevron.svg)
Essential includes 1 user, Growth includes 2 and Professional includes unlimited users. Helpdesk agents are added separately.

### Billing
Is there a discount for paying yearly?
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f60c731c7ffa0706798ef87/6a02edbb168e7e4e5ae1a0b9_7d0c44e7f9f397acfb5cb026b89a734e_chevron.svg)
Yes. With yearly billing, Essential is $16/month instead of $19, Growth is $29.90 instead of $39.90 and Professional is $59 instead of $79. You can switch to yearly from your Billing page at any time.
Do subscriptions renew automatically?
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f60c731c7ffa0706798ef87/6a02edbb168e7e4e5ae1a0b9_7d0c44e7f9f397acfb5cb026b89a734e_chevron.svg)
Yes. Monthly and yearly plans renew at the end of each period using your saved payment method. Your next billing date is shown on your Billing page.
Why is my invoice different from last time?
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f60c731c7ffa0706798ef87/6a02edbb168e7e4e5ae1a0b9_7d0c44e7f9f397acfb5cb026b89a734e_chevron.svg)
Usually for one of three reasons: a first-period discount or migration credit has ended, your live listings moved you into a higher tier, or you added an add-on like Helpdesk or AI credits. Every charge is listed on your Billing page, and you can download each invoice from Billing History. If something looks wrong, contact us before the due date.

Credit cards, PayPal and direct debit.

### Cancel, pause and refunds
How do I cancel?
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f60c731c7ffa0706798ef87/6a02edbb168e7e4e5ae1a0b9_7d0c44e7f9f397acfb5cb026b89a734e_chevron.svg)
Open Billing from your account menu and click Cancel Subscription. You can cancel your whole subscription or just one add-on, like Helpdesk. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've already paid for.
Can I pause instead of cancelling?
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f60c731c7ffa0706798ef87/6a02edbb168e7e4e5ae1a0b9_7d0c44e7f9f397acfb5cb026b89a734e_chevron.svg)
Yes, for free. Click Cancel Subscription and choose to pause. Your plan pauses for 1 month and you aren't charged. All your data is kept, and your plan resumes automatically. You can reactivate early at any time.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f60c731c7ffa0706798ef87/6a02edbb168e7e4e5ae1a0b9_7d0c44e7f9f397acfb5cb026b89a734e_chevron.svg)
Cancelling deletes your 3Dsellers data, such as your listing templates, Auto Messages and drafts. Your listings and orders on eBay and other marketplaces stay live. Because your templates are deleted, their designs stop showing on your listings, so switch to a plain description before you cancel if you want to keep a clean look. If you just need a break, pause instead and keep everything.
Can I get a refund?
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f60c731c7ffa0706798ef87/6a02edbb168e7e4e5ae1a0b9_7d0c44e7f9f397acfb5cb026b89a734e_chevron.svg)
Monthly payments aren't refundable, but you can cancel at any time before your next renewal. If a yearly plan renews and you didn't mean to keep it, contact support within 24 hours of the charge for a refund.
How do I delete my account?
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f60c731c7ffa0706798ef87/6a02edbb168e7e4e5ae1a0b9_7d0c44e7f9f397acfb5cb026b89a734e_chevron.svg)
Contact support from your account email and ask us to delete your account. We'll permanently delete your 3Dsellers data and confirm by email. Your listings and orders on eBay and other marketplaces aren't affected.
