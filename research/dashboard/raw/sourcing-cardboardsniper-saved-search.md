# eBay saved search alerts not working? Check email and app delivery
If eBay saved search alerts are missing, first check whether the saved search finds the listings you expect. Then check that search’s email setting, the registered email address and spam folder. For app alerts, check both eBay and device notification permissions. Separate a search-matching problem from a delivery problem before changing filters or recreating the search.

## Start with the symptom
These checks help isolate the problem; they are not a guaranteed fix or evidence of an eBay-wide outage. Test one saved search at a time and keep a note of the exact filters.  
| What you see  | What to check next  |  
| --- | --- |  
| Search opens with no relevant listings  | Keywords, category, condition, price and location filters  |  
| Relevant new listings appear, but no email arrives  | Per-search email setting, account address and mailbox filtering  |  
| Email arrives, but no app alert appears  | eBay app preferences and device notification permission  |  
| App alerts arrive late  | Connection, battery restrictions and device focus settings  |  
| Several searches fail despite correct settings  | Record examples and ask eBay support to investigate  |  
## Confirm the search and its email setting
Open Saved Searches in My eBay, then open the affected search. Check the envelope icon: it controls email for that search. eBay says saved-search email is enabled by default and goes to the registered address; do not assume every saved search has the same setting.
  * [eBay: saved searches and email controls](https://www.ebay.com/help/buying/search-tips/saved-searches?id=4051)


## Check whether the filters match what you want
Run the saved search manually. As a diagnostic, remove one restrictive filter and compare the results. For cards, a different spelling, language, grade or category can exclude a relevant listing. A search that returns no matches may be behaving correctly.
Keep the original search while testing a revised version. eBay notes that saving edits creates a new saved search. Confirm the revised filters before removing an older search so you do not lose a useful setup.
## Check email delivery separately
Confirm that you are checking the mailbox registered to the account. Review communication preferences, then look in junk or spam. eBay recommends checking junk mail when notifications are enabled but missing.
Our troubleshooting suggestion: also inspect mailbox rules, blocked senders, forwarding and available storage. Search for older eBay messages to see whether they were delivered elsewhere. These are possible email-delivery causes, not a diagnosis that your provider is blocking eBay.
  * [eBay: email and notification preferences](https://www.ebay.com/help/account/changing-account-settings/notifications?id=4203)


## Check iPhone or Android app notifications
Enable eBay notifications in the phone’s settings, then review notification choices inside the eBay app. Both layers matter. On Android, eBay also identifies battery-saving settings as a possible source of delayed notifications.
As a separate diagnostic, confirm the app is signed in to the intended account and check connection and focus settings. App delivery and email delivery are different paths; one working does not prove the other is configured correctly.
  * [eBay: app notification setup and delay troubleshooting](https://www.ebay.com/help/account/changing-account-settings/notifications?id=4203)


## If alerts still do not arrive
Record the affected search, filters, notification channel and an example listing you expected it to match. Note when you checked and whether a manually opened search showed that listing. Give eBay support those details rather than only saying that alerts are broken.
You can test a newly saved version after recording the original setup. Recreating a search is a diagnostic step, not a guaranteed reset for an unknown delivery problem. Avoid repeatedly deleting working searches.
## What timing can you rely on?
The current eBay help pages linked here do not establish a universal once-per-day limit or an exact delivery-time guarantee for every saved-search alert. Do not treat a missing message as proof of a daily schedule, and do not assume changing a setting guarantees immediate delivery. A matching listing and the time a notification reaches your device are separate observations.

## Frequently asked questions
### Why did my eBay saved-search emails stop?
+
Possible causes include the search email setting, changed matching conditions or mailbox filtering. Check the search manually and inspect the delivery settings before assuming an outage.
### How often does eBay send saved-search alerts?
+
The help pages reviewed for this guide do not specify a universal daily cap or a guaranteed delivery time. Check matching and delivery separately rather than relying on an assumed schedule.
### Why do I get email but no app notification?
+
Email and app permissions are separate. Check notification choices inside eBay and the device-level permission; also inspect settings that could delay or silence the app.
### Should I delete and recreate the saved search?
+
Record the original filters and test a new version first. Recreating a search may help isolate a configuration problem, but it is not a guaranteed fix for delivery issues.
