# Shopify Marketplace Connect app requirements
To use the [Shopify Marketplace Connect app](https://apps.shopify.com/marketplace-connect), your Shopify store needs to meet the following requirements:
  * You need a Shopify [online store](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store), and your store access can't be set to [private mode](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/themes/password-page).
  * You need to have a [valid payment provider](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments) activated in your Shopify admin.
  * You need to have [shipping rates](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/setup/shipping-rates) set up for the countries that you want to sell to, and accept payments in a currency that's supported in that country.
  * You need to have a verified seller account for the sales marketplace that you want to connect your Shopify store to. Review the following guides to setting up a seller's account for the supported marketplaces:
    * For Amazon, refer to the Amazon Seller Central page for the region that you want to sell in. For example, [_Become an Amazon seller_](https://sellercentral.amazon.com) for [Amazon.com](https://www.amazon.com), and [_Become an Amazon seller_](https://sellercentral.amazon.ca) for [Amazon.ca](https://www.amazon.ca). Ensure that you sign up for an Amazon seller's account in the correct country that you want to sell in.
    * For Target Plus (United States only), go to the [Marketplace Connect app in your Shopify admin](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/shopify-marketplace-connect), or [install the Marketplace Connect app](https://apps.shopify.com/marketplace-connect), then in the **Target Plus** section of the app, click **view requirements here** to view the **Target Plus Seller Requirements**. You need to complete the [_Target Plus™ Seller Application_](https://targetteam.tfaforms.net/3) form, and then get approved to become a seller by Target Plus.
    * For Walmart (United States only), refer to [_The Beginner's Guide to Selling on Walmart Marketplace_](https://marketplace.walmart.com/guide-to-walmart-marketplace).
    * For eBay, refer to [_How to register as a seller on eBay_](https://export.ebay.com/en/first-steps/how-use-ebay-seller-account/how-to-create-seller-account).
    * For Etsy, refer to [_Start selling on Etsy_](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/sections/4406539602455-Start-Selling-on-Etsy).


#### Note
New connections to Etsy can't be made at this time with the Shopify Marketplace Connect app. If you're already selling on Etsy through Marketplace Connect, then you can continue to use the app.
Some marketplaces have additional requirements to sell on their platforms. Before you connect to a marketplace, you should review their Terms of Service agreements and documentation.
For additional support, you can use the [chat feature in the Marketplace Connect app](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/shopify-marketplace-connect).
