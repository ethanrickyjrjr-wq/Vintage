#  Follow Search Results 
Following a specific search, category, or seller is an easy and convenient way to stay updated when new items related to your search are listed on 1stDibs. If you follow a single item or "save" it, you'll be updated when the item goes on sale or is sold. We'll also send weekly emails, letting you know when there are new arrivals in the categories you follow.
## How do I use the Follow feature?
  1. You'll see the _Follow_ button as you browse our site. Once you click it, your search, the category, or the seller will be added to the Following tab under My Favorites. There, you can view everything you follow, along with all new arrivals
  2. To follow a specific search, start by using the search bar
  3. You'll see the _Follow_ button once the page loads, displaying all the items related to your search


![014_Zendesk.jpeg](https://support.1stdibs.com/hc/article_attachments/27552916922011)
Relatedly, when you're browsing and want to save a single item, simply click the heart icon, which can be found next to the item name. This will add the item to the Items tab under My Favorites. You can learn more about how to save your favorite items [here](https://support.1stdibs.com/hc/en-us/articles/14517442029979).
![015_Zendesk.jpeg](https://support.1stdibs.com/hc/article_attachments/27552916923035)
To view what you follow in My Favorites, simply select the heart icon in the top-right corner of the page.
