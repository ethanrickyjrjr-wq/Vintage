# Goodwill Shutters Online Marketplace GoodwillFinds
Published: April 1, 2025By Nicole Silberstein  

![Featured Image](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Goodwill-signage-JHVEPhoto.jpg)
JHVEPhoto-stock.Adobe.com
[](https://www.facebook.com/sharer/sharer.php?u=https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/news/goodwill-shutters-online-marketplace-goodwillfinds/151013/) [](https://twitter.com/intent/tweet?url=https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/news/goodwill-shutters-online-marketplace-goodwillfinds/151013/&text=Goodwill%20Shutters%20Online%20Marketplace%20GoodwillFinds&via=YourTwitterHandle) [](https://www.linkedin.com/shareArticle?url=https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/news/goodwill-shutters-online-marketplace-goodwillfinds/151013/&title=Goodwill%20Shutters%20Online%20Marketplace%20GoodwillFinds)
[**GoodwillFinds.com**](https://www.goodwillfinds.com/), the online marketplace operated under a license agreement with [**Goodwill Industries**](https://www.goodwill.org/), has ceased operations. A message on the platform’s homepage states that the site has stopped accepting orders as of March 28, and redirects customers to the auction-style resale site [**ShopGoodwill.com**](https://www.shopgoodwill.com/).
### **Complicated Competitive Dynamics**
GoodwillFinds.com was launched in [October 2022](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/topics/digital-commerce/goodwill-online-resale-new-marketplace-goodwillfinds-thrift-store) in a bid by the **120** -year-old thrift store chain to grab a larger piece of the growing [online resale](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/topics/consumer-trends/tariffs-ai-social-trends-create-powerful-new-pathways-for-resale) market. The site was operated as a B2B2C marketplace where participating local Goodwills would list items on the site and ship the inventory directly to customers. This model can often provide better margins for operators than an **Ebay** -style auction marketplace like ShopGoodwill.com.
But it appears that **the new marketplace failed to find traction, both within the Goodwill organization and among consumers** , who have [complained](https://www.reddit.com/r/Flipping/comments/1jnk842/goodwillfindscom_is_shutting_down_cant_say_im/?rdt=48452) in recent months about unreasonably high prices and expensive shipping costs.
Because Goodwill Industries operates under a federated store model — essentially the nonprofit equivalent of a franchise system — it was up to each of the **150+** regional Goodwill affiliates across the country to decide if they wanted to participate in the new venture. Many of them already were selling via ShopGoodwill.com, which was [launched **25** years ago](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/features/retail-success-stories/how-resale-sustainability-and-its-3300-stores-support-goodwills-key-mission-jobs?__hstc=240025116.58f8cff5e2be1757ff942c5e496a4aee.1741219200463.1741219200464.1741219200465.1&__hssc=240025116.1.1741219200466&__hsfp=79048079) by Goodwill of Orange County and now boasts participation from more than **130** local Goodwills around the country. A little under**three** years in, GoodwillFinds had not yet come near to that level of organizational participation in its marketplace.

![RTP Newsletter](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/news/goodwill-shutters-online-marketplace-goodwillfinds/151013/)  
](https://cta-service-cms2.hubspot.com/web-interactives/public/v1/track/redirect?encryptedPayload=AVxigLLs%2BXIL1Td%2Fe3dnTeqC4OH0X62y6nbwre8P77VSU4ywUlfsSLzZNBpyA7Ki1qxomBG8mGyVtZTsqds7Lh4GHOnoh8w03VT7lg2d%2BXznVrpyhklxN1hh5GFTaopH%2BaaQZIhbG%2FwFT56q4%2Bp3iqI0LLEBYq8H%2BBSQJXcDX1iHMWOPkic%2FL%2Fu%2FKJ1phspbEI8%3D&webInteractiveContentId=207998848949&portalId=23836458%22%20target=)
“We want to express our sincere gratitude for your loyalty and support of GoodwillFinds over the past few years,” reads the message on the site’s homepage. “Goodwill shoppers can continue to go to [www.ShopGoodwill.com](https://www.shopgoodwill.com/) for great finds from **130+** Goodwill sellers. You can also keep supporting Goodwill by shopping at your local Goodwill store.”
All outstanding orders from GoodwillFinds will still be fulfilled, according to the website.
### **Online Resale is a Tough Nut to Crack**
It’s no secret that online resale on the lower end of the price point spectrum is [challenging to run profitably](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/topics/digital-commerce/resale-is-hot-but-when-it-comes-to-making-money-many-brands-are-out-in-the-cold). GoodwillFinds faced many of the same challenges as [its peers](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/topics/loyalty/thredup-nixes-rewards-points-as-struggle-to-improve-unit-economics-continues) while also contending with the complicated nuances of being a nonprofit and the unique structure of the Goodwill organization.
