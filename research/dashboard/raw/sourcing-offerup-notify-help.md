#  Get notified about items you want 
Updated 1 year ago
Looking for a specific item? Want to know when similar items get posted? Set alerts to get notified when new items are available that match your interests. There are two kinds of alerts you can set so you never miss out on a good deal—search alerts and similar item alerts. 
## Set search alerts
Set a search alert to get notified when new listings are posted that match your search criteria. This type of alert is great if you're hunting for a good deal on a certain kind of item, but aren’t looking for a specific color, brand, size, or model. This alert is also helpful if you’re looking for related items or accessories.
For example, if you search “iPhone 15”, you can save this search term as an alert. When you do, you’ll get notified any time a new iPhone 15 is posted, plus other iPhone models, and items related to iPhones like phone cases or screen protectors.
[Set a search alert in the app](https://help.offerup.com/hc/en-us/articles/360031995332-Get-notified-about-items-you-want#tab-content-40) [Delete a search alert in the app](https://help.offerup.com/hc/en-us/articles/360031995332-Get-notified-about-items-you-want#tab-content-41)
  1. Open the OfferUp app and log in to your account.
  2. On the **Home** page, type a search term into the **Search** bar.
  3. Submit your search to view your results.
  4. On the **Search Results** page, set your search alert by tapping the![search_alert_bell.jpg](https://help.offerup.com/hc/article_attachments/25660572143124)**Bell** icon in the top right corner. Or, tap the floating **Set Search Alert** button in the feed.
  5. You'll see a "**Search Alert Set** " confirmation message. Also, the![search_alert_checked.jpg](https://help.offerup.com/hc/article_attachments/25660593328148)**Bell** icon at the top of the feed will turn green and show a check mark.


  1. Open the OfferUp app and log in to your account.
  2. On the **Home** page, tap your profile picture in the top right corner.
  3. On the **Account** page, tap **Search Alerts**.
  4. Under **Saved Search Alerts** , tap **Delete** next to the term you want to remove.
  5. On the **Delete Saved Search Alert** pop-up, tap **Delete** again to confirm.


## Set similar item alerts
Set a similar item alert to get notified when a specific item gets posted to the feed. This type of alert is great if you’re looking for a particular product model.
For example, if you’re looking at a listing for an iPhone 15, but you don’t want to buy this exact one because it’s the wrong color or storage capacity, set a similar item alert instead. This will ensure that you get notified any time iPhone 15s are posted to the feed.
[Set a similar item alert in the app](https://help.offerup.com/hc/en-us/articles/360031995332-Get-notified-about-items-you-want#tab-content-82) [Remove a similar item alert in the app](https://help.offerup.com/hc/en-us/articles/360031995332-Get-notified-about-items-you-want#tab-content-83)
  1. Open the OfferUp app and log in to your account.
  2. From the **For Sale** feed, open a listing for an item you’re interested in.
  3. Scroll down to **Get Notified About Similar Items** (located below the **Description** details).
  4. Tap **Set Alert**.
  5. You’ll see an “**Alert Set** ” confirmation and the message will change to read “**You’ll be notified about similar items** ”.


  1. Open the OfferUp app and log in to your account.
  2. Open the listing where you set the similar item alert.
  3. Scroll down to the message “**You’ll be notified about similar items** ”.
  4. Tap **Remove Alert**.


Alternatively, when you get notified about a similar item, tap the notification to open the feed, then tap the ![similar_item_checked.jpg](https://help.offerup.com/hc/article_attachments/25660593336980) **Bell** icon in the top right corner to remove the alert.
