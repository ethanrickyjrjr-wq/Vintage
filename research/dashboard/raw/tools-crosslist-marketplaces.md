## Frequently asked questions
Everything you need to know about the platforms & marketplaces we support.
What is Crosslist®?
Crosslist® is a [cross listing app](https://crosslist.com/) that lets you create one listing and publish it across [11+ online marketplaces](https://crosslist.com/marketplaces), including eBay, Poshmark, Depop, Mercari, Whatnot, and more.
Rather than manually recreating the same listing on every platform, Crosslist® helps you cross post from [one universal form](https://crosslist.com/features/one-universal-form), saving time and getting your products in front of more buyers faster.
When an item sells, [autodelisting](https://crosslist.com/features/autodelisting) removes it from your other marketplaces automatically, so you never oversell. And with our [iOS and Android apps](https://crosslist.com/features/mobile-app), you can list, edit, and manage your inventory from anywhere.
It’s one of the easiest ways to grow your reach and scale your resale business across multiple marketplaces.  
  
Discover all [Crosslist® features](https://crosslist.com/features) or see how it compares with other cross listing tools like [Vendoo](https://crosslist.com/crosslist-vs-vendoo), [Nifty](https://crosslist.com/crosslist-vs-nifty), [List Perfectly](https://crosslist.com/crosslist-vs-list-perfectly), [PrimeLister](https://crosslist.com/crosslist-vs-primelister), [Flyp](https://crosslist.com/crosslist-vs-flyp) or [Zipsale](https://crosslist.com/crosslist-vs-zipsale).
In which countries is Crosslist® available?
Crosslist® is currently available in the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia.
At this time, we do not support users residing in the European Union.
Do I need an account on each marketplace to use Crosslist®?
To use Crosslist®, you must have active and fully verified accounts on every marketplace you plan to list to. Ideally, you should have already posted a few listings manually to ensure all verification steps have been completed successfully.
For eBay, you’ll also need to set up [business policies](https://docs.crosslist.com/knowledge-base/marketplaces/ebay/ebay-policies) — this is a mandatory requirement for all third-party applications that integrate with eBay’s official API, even if you’re using a personal account.
Setting up business policies takes only a few minutes and applies to both personal and business sellers. It’s simply part of eBay’s integration process and does not require you to register as a business.
Are all marketplaces included in every plan?
Yes, all [marketplaces](https://crosslist.com/marketplaces) and their regional variants are included in every Crosslist® plan.
No matter which plan you choose, you’ll have access to the full range of supported marketplaces and country versions without any restrictions.
Can I use multiple accounts for the same marketplace?
Yes, you can use multiple accounts for the same marketplace with Crosslist®.
This is especially useful if you manage different stores or sell under multiple profiles. You can easily connect and switch between accounts within the app, allowing you to cross list, import, and manage listings across all your marketplace accounts without restrictions.
Will Crosslist® automatically adapt my listings for each marketplace?
No, Crosslist® does not automatically sync or adapt your listings across marketplaces.
We’ve chosen not to implement automatic syncing because, in practice, it often creates more problems than it solves — if a mistake is made, it can be instantly replicated everywhere, making it difficult to track and correct.
Instead, Crosslist® allows you to manually [update listings in bulk](https://crosslist.com/features/update-listings), without needing to [relist](https://crosslist.com/features/delist-relist) them.
This approach gives you full control over your inventory while still saving time, since all updates are processed in the background and you can continue working on other tasks.
Some marketplaces or selling platforms I use aren’t listed. Will you add them later?
Yes, we’re continuously expanding the list of supported marketplaces and selling platforms.
You can view which marketplaces we plan to add on our [public roadmap](https://feedback.crosslist.com/en/roadmap).
If a marketplace you use isn’t listed, you can also submit it as a feature request so other users can vote on it — the most requested platforms are prioritized for future updates.

No matter which plan you choose, you’ll have access to the full range of supported marketplaces and country versions without any restrictions.
Can I use multiple accounts for the same marketplace?
Yes, you can use multiple accounts for the same marketplace with Crosslist®.
This is especially useful if you manage different stores or sell under multiple profiles. You can easily connect and switch between accounts within the app, allowing you to cross list, import, and manage listings across all your marketplace accounts without restrictions.
Will Crosslist® automatically adapt my listings for each marketplace?
No, Crosslist® does not automatically sync or adapt your listings across marketplaces.
We’ve chosen not to implement automatic syncing because, in practice, it often creates more problems than it solves — if a mistake is made, it can be instantly replicated everywhere, making it difficult to track and correct.
Instead, Crosslist® allows you to manually [update listings in bulk](https://crosslist.com/features/update-listings), without needing to [relist](https://crosslist.com/features/delist-relist) them.
This approach gives you full control over your inventory while still saving time, since all updates are processed in the background and you can continue working on other tasks.
Some marketplaces or selling platforms I use aren’t listed. Will you add them later?
Yes, we’re continuously expanding the list of supported marketplaces and selling platforms.
You can view which marketplaces we plan to add on our [public roadmap](https://feedback.crosslist.com/en/roadmap).
If a marketplace you use isn’t listed, you can also submit it as a feature request so other users can vote on it — the most requested platforms are prioritized for future updates.
