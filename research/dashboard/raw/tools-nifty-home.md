## FAQs
What is Nifty?
Nifty is a platform that helps resellers spend less time managing their listings, make more sales, and easily track their profits. Nifty combines an AI-powered crosslister that works across all major marketplaces, plus automation tools with bots specifically for Poshmark, eBay, Mercari, and Depop.
What marketplaces does Nifty support?
Nifty’s crosslisting and analytics features support Poshmark, eBay, Mercari, Depop, Etsy, and Whatnot. Automation runs on Poshmark, eBay, Mercari, and Depop. More marketplaces coming soon!
How does Nifty work?
Nifty automatically connects to your existing marketplaces, syncs all of your listings into a unified inventory, and accurately identifies matches across platforms. Then, Nifty helps you create listings for marketplaces where your items aren't listed yet, using AI to make the process easier.
How does Nifty help me make more money?
Nifty makes it faster to crosslist on multiple marketplaces, helping you get seen quicker. Nifty includes bots for each marketplace, allowing you to share items, relist to maximize visibility, and send timed offers to interested buyers. Finally, Nifty lets you track your sales data and business expenses, surfacing key insights about your performance, trends, and opportunities across your entire inventory.
Is Nifty the same as Auto Posher?
Yes – Nifty was originally known as Auto Posher, but we changed our name in 2025 to reflect that we support much more than just Poshmark automation now! In fact, Nifty is the only solution on the market to offer crosslisting, cloud-based automation, and sales analytics in one.
