### Question / discussion
Hi all, I'm hoping to get some guidance on the correct path here. We run a print-on-demand production partner app. Sellers connect their own shop through OAuth, upload their own designs, and we print and ship their orders and write tracking back to Etsy. So far we've had one app at a time, all on one account:
  * ninjapod-dtf-print-on-demand: Personal App, approved. We requested Commercial Access and it was banned within 24 hours.
  * ninja-print-on-demand: Personal App, approved. We requested Commercial Access and it was banned within 24 hours.
  * ninja-print-on-demand (Seller App): currently approved and working for testing on our own shop.

Support email couldn't tell us more, so I have a few questions for anyone on the Etsy team who can help:
  1. Is a POD production-partner app an allowed commercial use case?
  2. Is there anything specific we should fix before applying again?
  3. Since the Seller App can't be upgraded, what's the right next step for this account?

We'd rather get it right than keep resubmitting. Thanks!  |
