# Managing Email and Notification Preferences
July 16, 2026
Copy for LLM
You can control which email notifications you receive from HiBid, including outbid alerts, auction reminders, and saved search updates, from your account settings.
# Accessing Your Notification Settings
  1. Log in to your HiBid account.
  2. Click your name in the top right corner, then select **Manage Emails and Saved Searches**.


# Available Email Preferences
From this page, you can manage the following email preferences:
  * **Send notification when outbid on an item** – get an email when someone outbids you.
  * **Subscribe to email list** – receive HiBid's general email updates.
  * **Subscribe to saved searches email** – get emails matching the items in your saved searches.
  * **Send reminders for auction events** – get reminder emails for auctions you're registered in or watching.


# Turning a Notification On or Off
Click the empty checkbox next to any option to enable it. Once enabled, the checkbox turns into a blue box with a white checkmark, confirming you're subscribed to that email. Click it again to clear the checkmark and unsubscribe.
