# Which Sellbrite Plan is Right for Me?
Learn which Sellbrite plan best meets your needs.
Written by Elliot K
August 13, 2026
Sellbrite offers a free plan for small businesses processing fewer than 30 orders per month. Paid plans starts at just $29 per month. 
Visit our [Pricing Page](https://www.sellbrite.com/pricing/) for more details on available plans and features.
Paid plans also include enhanced features such as in-app chat support and improved synchronization speeds for more frequent updates.
## How to choose the right Sellbrite plan
When selecting a plan, consider the following:
  1. **Order Volume**  
Choose a plan that matches your monthly order volume. If your order count exceeds the plan limit during your billing cycle, you'll automatically be upgraded to the next order volume tier.
  2. **Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA)**  
If you use Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) and want to make your FBA inventory available on other sales channels, you can add the FBA add-on for an additional monthly fee.
  3. **Monthly vs. Annual Billing**  
You can choose between monthly or annual billing. Annual plans are paid upfront for 12 months and include a discount equivalent to 2 months free. Annual subscriptions require a one-year commitment and are non-refundable.


## Sellbrite for Shopify (SFS)
Sellbrite for Shopify (SFS) is a specialized version of Sellbrite designed specifically for Shopify merchants. With SFS Shopify acts as your source of truth for product data and inventory, and automatically syncs data automatically to Sellbrite.
**Important:** Data from a standard Sellbrite account cannot be transferred to an SFS account. If you wish to switch to SFS, you will need to create a new account.  
For more information, visit our [Sellbrite for Shopify page](https://www.sellbrite.com/integrations/sellbrite-for-shopify/).
* * *
## Managing Your Subscription
If your order volume exceeds the limit of your plan, your subscription will automatically be upgraded to the next tier. To downgrade your plan, go to **Settings** > **Billing Settings** > **Your Subscription** and make the changes manually.
