# Saved searches

Save valuable shopping time by creating saved searches for the items you regularly search eBay for.
We'll send you email alerts when new items matching your search are listed. Your searches will also appear in your feed when you're signed in or you can find them by selecting **Saved** on the eBay homepage.
## How to save searches
After you search for an item, select **Save this search** at the top of the search results page.
To view the listings in the searches you saved:
  1. Go to [Saved searches - opens in new window or tab](https://www.ebay.co.uk/myb/SavedSearches?MyEbay&gbh=1&CurrentPage=MyeBayAllFavorites).
  2. Choose **View items**.


## How to edit or delete your saved searches
You can update your saved searches by adding or removing filters, such as price, colour, or type of listing. To edit your saved searches:
  1. Go to [Saved searches - opens in new window or tab](https://www.ebay.co.uk/myb/SavedSearches?MyEbay&gbh=1&CurrentPage=MyeBayAllFavorites).
  2. Select the search you'd like to edit.
  3. Add or remove filters, then select **Save this search**.


Keep in mind that when you edit an existing search, a new saved search will be created with the filters you've selected.
To delete a saved search:
  1. Go to [Saved searches - opens in new window or tab](https://www.ebay.co.uk/myb/SavedSearches?MyEbay&gbh=1&CurrentPage=MyeBayAllFavorites).
  2. Select the blue heart on the search you'd like to delete.

[Go to Saved searches - opens in new window or tab](https://www.ebay.co.uk/myb/SavedSearches?MyEbay&gbh=1&CurrentPage=MyeBayAllFavorites)
**Tip**  
You might need to update your saved search if a product's name or other details change.
## Unsubscribing from saved search emails
By default you'll receive email alerts to your registered email address whenever new items match your search or if there are updates to your saved search. You can unsubscribe from these emails at any time.
  1. Go to [Saved searches - opens in new window or tab](https://www.ebay.co.uk/myb/SavedSearches?MyEbay&gbh=1&CurrentPage=MyeBayAllFavorites).
  2. Select the envelope icon on the search you'd like to stop receiving emails about.


If you change your mind, you can select the envelope icon to receive emails about this saved search again.
## How to remove a saved seller
Here’s how to remove a saved seller:
  1. Go to **Saved Sellers** in My eBay.
  2. Select the blue heart to the right of the seller you’d like to remove.
