## What is the best app for Vinted sellers to track profit?
There is no single best app for Vinted sellers: the right tracker depends on whether you crosslist. **Margeo** imports Vinted Pro and wallet CSV sales and reports overall net profit with revenue by channel for French micro-BIC sellers (Pro and Business CSV exports add per-sale estimates after item cost and commission); **Vendoo, List Perfectly and Crosslist** prioritize crosslisting with lighter profit analytics; a spreadsheet stays enough below a few sales per week.
## Crosslisting and profit tracking solve different problems
You may need one workflow or both:
  * **Crosslisting** — publish a listing to several marketplaces and, depending on the product and marketplace, detect sales or delist copies. Vendoo, List Perfectly, Crosslist, Sell The Flip and FLUF focus on this job.
  * **Profit tracking** — connect purchase cost, bundle allocation, sale proceeds, fees and shipping to an item so you can see what remains. Margeo and Suivi Vinted focus on recorded costs; Vinteer says it adds Vinted sales and stock synchronization.


These products are not direct substitutes. A crosslisting tool can save time publishing; a profit tracker can show whether a sale was worthwhile. Some sellers use both.

## What “profit per item” should include
A useful item-level result starts with sale proceeds and accounts for the costs attached to that item:
  * **Allocated purchase cost** — including the item’s share of a bundle.
  * **Marketplace fees and shipping** — use the values for the actual platform, label and sale.
  * **Other recorded expenses** — such as packaging or paid promotion.
  * **Local tax rules** — these depend on where you live and your business status.


For France-based micro-entrepreneurs, Margeo provides a separate micro-BIC estimate alongside inventory and sales tracking. Its overall net-profit total is not an item-level or marketplace-level profit report; the English workflow does not localize taxes for other countries.
## When a spreadsheet is still fine
A spreadsheet may be enough if you sell on one marketplace, buy items individually and only need a simple record. A dedicated inventory tool becomes more useful when you split bundles into items, track the same stock across channels, or want costs and sales connected without rebuilding formulas.
Margeo allocates purchase-bundle cost across its items and records fees and shipping. Crosslisting tools address listing distribution; check each plan’s analytics if you also want to compare sales performance.
