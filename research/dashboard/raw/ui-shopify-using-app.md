# Using the Shopify app for iPhone and Android
After installing the [Shopify app](https://www.shopify.com/install/detect), you can log in to your account and start running your store from your mobile device. If you want to know how to do a task in the Shopify app, then search for the task in the Shopify Help Center and select **iPhone** or **Android**.
For the best experience, make sure that your staff have the [permissions](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/users/roles/permissions) that they need, and that your software is up to date.
#### Tip
Throughout the Shopify Help Center, if a task can be done from your mobile device using the Shopify app, then you can select instructions for iPhone or Android using the ⌄ icons to expand the steps.

## User permissions on the Shopify app
Your users can log in to the Shopify app using their own Shopify credentials. If the user has [limited permissions](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/users/roles/permissions), then they can view only the areas of the Shopify app that they have the permissions for. For example, if they don't have permission to view the **Home** page of the Shopify admin, then the **Home** button won't display in the Shopify app.
To view the **Orders** screen in the Shopify app, staff need only the **Orders** > **View** permission. To open a customer's profile from an order, staff also need the **Customers** > **View** permission.
## Make sure that your mobile software is up to date
Make sure that your mobile device is running the latest versions of the operating system and the Shopify app. Software updates enhance your experience and help you avoid problems caused by out-of-date software.
Android devices automatically install updates by default, but it's possible for you to switch the option off.
iPhone
#### Steps:
  1. From your iPhone's home screen, tap **App Store**.
  2. Tap the profile icon, and then tap **Apps**.
  3. Locate the Shopify app, and then tap **Update**. If **Update** isn't displayed, then your Shopify app is up to date.

Android
  1. From your Android device's home screen, tap **Play Store**.
  2. Tap the profile icon.
  3. Tap **Manage apps & device** > **Manage**.
  4. Tap the **Shopify** app.
  5. Tap **Update**. If **Update** isn't displayed, then your app is up to date.


## Activate automatic updates on your mobile device
To make it easier to keep your software up to date, you can activate automatic updates. After you activate the option, software updates are downloaded and installed automatically. After you set your Shopify app for automatic updates, you might also want to review whether your mobile device updates on mobile data and set it for Wi-Fi only.
#### Steps:
iPhone
  1. From your iPhone's home screen, tap **Settings**.
  2. Tap **App Store**.
  3. Under **AUTOMATIC DOWNLOADS** , activate **App Updates**.

Android
  1. From your Android device's home screen, tap **Play Store**.
  2. Tap the profile icon.
  3. Tap **Manage apps & device** > **Manage**.
  4. Tap the **Shopify** app.
  5. Tap ![Vertical menu](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/MenuVerticalIcon.svg).
  6. Activate **Enable auto-updates**.

The Shopify app uses your device's preferred language. The Shopify app supports the following languages:
  * Bulgarian
  * Chinese (Simplified)
  * Chinese (Traditional)
  * Croatian
  * Czech
  * Danish
  * Dutch
  * English
  * Finnish
  * French
  * German
  * Greek
  * Hindi
  * Hungarian
  * Indonesian
  * Italian
  * Japanese
  * Korean
  * Lithuanian
  * Malay
  * Norwegian (Bokmål)
  * Polish
  * Portuguese (Brazil)
  * Portuguese (Portugal)
  * Romanian
  * Russian
  * Slovak
  * Slovenian
  * Spanish
  * Swedish
  * Thai
  * Turkish
  * Vietnamese


If the Shopify app doesn't support any of the preferred languages on your device, then the app uses English.
You can change the preferred language in your device's settings.
#### Steps:
iPad
  1. Close the Shopify app.
  2. In your iPad, tap **Settings** > **General** > **Language & Region**.
  3. Tap **Add Language** , and then select your preferred language from the list and tap **Done**.
  4. You can then sort the languages in this list in order of preference.

iPhone
  1. Close the Shopify app.
  2. In your iPhone, tap **Settings** > **General** > **Language & Region**.
  3. Tap **Add Language** , and then select your preferred language from the list and tap **Done**.
  4. You can then sort the languages in this list in order of preference.

Android
  1. Close the Shopify app.
  2. In your Android device, tap **Settings** > **System** > **Languages & input** > **Languages**.
  3. Tap and hold your preferred language, and then drag and drop it into the **1** position. If your preferred language isn't on the list, then tap **Add a language** and select a language.


### Activate English language override
  1. From the [Shopify app](https://www.shopify.com/install/detect), tap ![Menu](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/MenuIcon.svg), and then tap ![Settings](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/SettingsIcon.svg) **Settings**.
  2. Tap **Language settings**
  3. Activate the language override by tapping the toggle button.


## Change the Shopify app icon on iOS
You can set an alternative icon for the Shopify app on your iOS device, such as a special summer or winter icon. This setting is available only for iOS users.
#### Steps:
  1. From the [Shopify app](https://www.shopify.com/install/detect), tap ![Menu](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/MenuIcon.svg), and then tap ![Settings](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/SettingsIcon.svg) **Settings**.
  2. In the **App settings** section, tap **App icon**.
  3. Tap the icon that you want to display instead of the default icon.
  4. Tap **OK** to confirm.


## Change account notifications on the Shopify app
#### Note
When you activate notifications in the Shopify app, make sure to activate notifications in the main settings of your device.
You can activate push notifications for security updates and multi-factor authentication from the Shopify app on your mobile device.
#### Steps:
  1. From the [Shopify app](https://www.shopify.com/install/detect), tap ![Menu](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/MenuIcon.svg), and then tap ![Settings](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/SettingsIcon.svg) **Settings**.
  2. In the **App settings** section, tap **Account notifications**.
  3. Activate the notification by tapping the toggle button next to each option.


## Activate store notifications on the Shopify app
#### Note
When you activate notifications in the Shopify app, make sure to activate notifications in the main settings of your device.
You can set up push notifications in the [Shopify app](https://www.shopify.com/install/detect) to get instant alerts on your phone about the following important store updates:
  * Critical alerts
  * Fulfillments
  * New orders
  * Setup tips
  * Timeline mentions


iPhone
#### Steps:
  1. From the [Shopify app](https://www.shopify.com/install/detect), tap ![Menu](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/MenuIcon.svg), and then tap ![Settings](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/SettingsIcon.svg) **Settings**.
  2. In the **App settings** section, tap **Store notifications**.
  3. In the **Push notifications** section, activate the relevant options.


### Activate the Shopify app badge
If you activate push notifications for orders, then you can also activate the Shopify app badge to see how many open orders you have from your iPhone's home screen.
The Shopify app badge shows the number of open orders for one store only. If you're logged in to [more than one account](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/logging-in) with the Shopify app, then the badge shows the number of open orders for the last account to have the option activated.
#### Steps:
  1. From the [Shopify app](https://www.shopify.com/install/detect), tap ![Menu](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/MenuIcon.svg), and then tap ![Settings](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/SettingsIcon.svg) **Settings**.
  2. In the **App settings** section, tap **Notifications**.
  3. In the **Orders** section, make sure that push notifications are activated.
  4. Activate the **Badge app icon** option.

Android
You can use the [Shopify app](https://www.shopify.com/install/detect) to activate push notifications so that you receive an alert on your phone whenever an order is placed with your store.
#### Steps:
  1. From the [Shopify app](https://www.shopify.com/install/detect), tap ![Menu](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/MenuIcon.svg), and then tap ![Settings](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/SettingsIcon.svg) **Settings**.
  2. Tap **Store notifications**.
  3. From the **Push notifications** screen, activate the notification by tapping the toggle button beside each option.
