## **Seller Hub Reports features**
The [Reports](https://www.ebay.com/sh/reports/) tab in Seller Hub makes it easier for high-volume sellers to manage their eBay business in bulk. Using templates, sellers can create and upload files to add, revise, relist, and end listings.
Here are some of the many things you can do with Seller Hub Reports:
  * Quickly create listings in bulk
  * Create, download, and schedule reports
  * Fulfill orders
  * Mark items as dispatched
  * Find items that have been paid or posted
  * Manage feedback
  * Identify and fix errors in your listings in bulk


If you previously used File Exchange you will find its core functionality is now in Reports, including the ability to create active listings reports, sold reports, revise price and quantity reports. All commonly used reports and templates are backward compatible. For ease of access, you can bookmark this new tab or select Reports from the Seller Hub [Overview](https://www.ebay.com/sh/ovw) tab.
![Activate](https://export.ebay.com/_next/image/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fexport.ebay.com%2Febayhub_media%2Fmedia%2FActivate_BaiEKvf.svg&w=1920&q=90)
Templates and reports created or scheduled in the legacy File Exchange tool will not be carried over to the new Reports tool. To generate new templates and reports in Seller Hub, follow the instructions below.
## **Getting started**
If you're a first time user of Reports, all you need to get started is an eBay account with access to Seller Hub. Business sellers are automatically opted in to Seller Hub. Private sellers just need to have made at least one sale to get access.
**Inconveniences you might face:**
  * You will have to schedule brand new reports for your order management logistics
  * If you have been using File Exchange via the URL link in a programmatic way be aware that this will no longer be available. To integrate programmatically, you will have to migrate to the [Feed API](https://developer.ebay.com/api-docs/sell/feed/overview.html)


![guide](https://export.ebay.com/_next/image/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fexport.ebay.com%2Febayhub_media%2Fmedia%2Fguide_vNQrGhJ.svg&w=1920&q=90)
Learn more on how to save time with uploading your listings in a bulk using Reports tab in [this article](https://export.ebay.com/en/marketing/seller-hub/uploading-your-listings-in-bulk-using-reports-tab/).
From the Download section, you can generate, download, and keep track of reports on your orders and listings. Reports are very useful for a number of reasons, including record-keeping, auditing and order fulfillment. For example, you can create a scheduled order fulfillment report to automatically update shipping details at a frequency that suits you, saving you a lot of manual work.
## **How to download reports**
**To download orders or listings report:**
  1. Go to the [Reports](https://www.ebay.com/sh/reports) tab in Seller Hub.
  2. From the menu on the left, select **Download**.
  3. Select the **Download report** button.
  4. Choose **Orders, Listings** or **Marketing** from the **Source** options.
  5. Select the **Type** of report you want to download (e.g. All orders, Awaiting shipment, etc.).
  6. If applicable, select the **Date Range** for the report (from 1-90 days).
  7. The right side of the window displays a summary of the report columns.Check that the selected parameters are correct and select **Download**.

## **How to schedule automated reports**
**To download orders or listings report:**
  1. Go to the [Reports](https://www.ebay.com/sh/reports) tab in Seller Hub.
  2. From the menu on the left, select **Schedule**.
  3. Select the **Schedule report** button.
  4. Choose **Orders** or **Marketing** from the **Source** options.
  5. Select the **Type** of report you want to schedule (e.g. All orders, Awaiting shipment, etc.).
  6. Select the **Frequency** of your reports (Hourly, Daily, Weekly or Monthly).
  7. Select a **Run Time**.
  8. Select an **Ends on date** for the report or select **No end date**.
  9. On the right side of the window, you will see a summary of report columns. Check that the selected parameters are correct and select **Save**.
