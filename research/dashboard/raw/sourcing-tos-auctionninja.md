## **16. EXTENDED BIDDING ("SOFT CLOSE")**
AuctionNinja's online auction system features extended bidding (a.k.a. "soft close"). This feature is built into the coding of AuctionNinja and is fully automated. It is the closest way to mimic the actions of an auctioneer (during a live auction) in an online/automated format. To illustrate, during a live auction the auctioneer typically won't hammer at the first bid. The auctioneer gives bidders opportunities to increase their bids until he/she determines there is no further interest in the lot before hammering to the winning bidder.
Therefore, _AuctionNinja's automated system will automatically extend the closing time of a lot (auction item) whenever a new bid is placed within the last five minutes of its scheduled closing to allow other interested bidders an opportunity to increase their bid_ (if they wish to do so). This may occur multiple times on any given lot depending on bidder interest. Once all interest and bidding in a lot ceases, the clock will run out and the lot will close.
