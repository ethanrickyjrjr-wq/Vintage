#  Treasure Tracker 
  * 8 months ago
  * Updated


This feature simplifies the estate sale experience for you by scanning Traditional and Marketplace sales for your saved search terms. If a sale within the specified distance includes a match, we notify you via email.

### Track a New Treasure Page
On this page, you can enter a search term and adjust your Treasure Tracker notification settings:
![](https://estatesales.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/23083551217553)
  1. Log into your EstateSales.NET account.
  2. Click **Treasure Tracker** , located on the menu on the left side of the screen (Please note that on a mobile device, this menu is located in the top right corner of the screen).
  3. Click **New Saved Search** to add search terms.
  4. Adjust Filters and click **Add Saved Search**.

### Adding a Save from the Search Bar
You can add a saved search when searching for items directly. After searching for an item, click **Save Search**. This will add it to your Treasure Tracker.
![TreasureTracker1.png](https://estatesales.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/43989618477329)

### Tips for Entering Search Terms
These special characters allow for custom searching:
  * + Signifies AND operation
  * | Signifies OR operation
  * - Negates a single word
  * "" Wraps a number of words to signify a phrase for searching
  * * At the end of a word signifies a prefix query
  * ( ) Signify precedence
  * ~N After a word signifies fuzziness and after a phrase signifies slop amount


* * *
### Words That Can't Be Saved
These words can't be used for Treasure Tracker as they are too common:
  * "all"
  * “auctions”
  * “antique”
  * “antiques”
  * “estate”
  * “estatesale”
  * “estatesales”
  * “online”
  * “onlineauction”
  * “onlineauctions”
  * “sale”
  * “sales”
  * “vintage”
