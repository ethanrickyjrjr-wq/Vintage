## Frequently asked questions
### Is the Shopify app free?
The app is included with your Shopify plan. No separate download fee or subscription. Tap to Pay and payment links use your standard Shopify Payments processing rates.
### What is Quick Sale?
The fastest way to sell on Shopify. Accept payments directly on your phone with Tap to Pay, or create a payment link to share via text or DM. No storefront or hardware required.
### Do I need hardware to accept card payments?
No, Tap to Pay turns your phone into a card reader. Customers can tap their card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay directly on your screen. No dongles, setup cost, or extra equipment to carry.
### What countries support Tap to Pay?
Tap to Pay is available in select countries including the US, UK, Canada, and Australia. See the [full list](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-admin/shopify-app/quick-sale/create-quick-sale#considerations). In markets without Tap to Pay, you can use payment links instead for the same professional checkout, no hardware needed.
### How do I get paid from mobile sales?
The same as any Shopify sale. Tap to Pay and payment link transactions process through Shopify Payments and deposit to your bank on your normal payout schedule, typically 2-3 business days. You can track every payment in the app.
### What can Sidekick do in the app?
Answer questions and take actions by voice or text. Ask it to check today's sales, find a customer's order, update a product price, explain a Shopify feature, and more.
