### What is a cross listing App?
A cross listing app, like Vendoo, is a powerful tool for resellers to manage and post their inventory across multiple online marketplaces from a single platform. Using these types of apps allows resellers to save time, reduce manual errors, and reach a broader audience to scale profits. It simplifies listing, updating, and managing inventory, making reselling more manageable and less time-consuming.
[](https://www.vendoo.co/cross-listing-app)
### How does a crosslisting app work?
It works by integrating with various online marketplaces. With Vendoo, you can import your inventory, create listings, and crosspost them to multiple marketplaces with a few clicks.
