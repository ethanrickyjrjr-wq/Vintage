Inventory, expenses, mileage, profit: tracked. Your Coach reads them every night and tells you what to fix first.

Command Center
## Your AI Coach
Personalized insights from your own data, not generic advice. Every night the Coach re-reads your inventory, sales, expenses, and mileage, then hands you one verdict: what to fix first, ranked by dollars at stake.
Every insight opens the exact items to act on, and you can ask the Coach anything about your business in chat. The only reselling tracker with one, and it's included free on every plan. [Read the announcement](https://getflippd.com/blog/posts/meet-your-coach-the-ai-that-reads-your-reselling-business-ev/).
![Flippd Coach screen with the daily verdict and a ranked fix](https://getflippd.com/images/coach/coach-verdict.webp?v=ba5de159a4)
## Snap Decisions
Snap one item or a whole pile while you're sourcing. Flippd identifies what's in the photo, checks active eBay listings, and tags each item Buy, Too low for you, or Loses money after fees and shipping, with the most you should pay.
Matching sets are priced as one lot, with the singles alongside for comparison. Set your platform, minimum profit, and shipping once, then add a buy to inventory in one tap. [Read the announcement](https://getflippd.com/blog/posts/snap-decisions-know-if-its-worth-buying-in-one-photo/).
![Snap Decisions screen with a set of teacups priced as one lot and the best play to buy it for $18 or less](https://getflippd.com/images/snap/snap-decisions-phone.webp?v=b714bfaa3c)

Scan any product barcode and instantly look up its details in a large product database. Title, brand, category, and product image are filled in automatically. Just add your cost and save.

Every sale still happens with you, in person, and Checkout marks items sold in your inventory on the spot.

## Storage Location Management
Never lose track of your inventory again. Organize items by storage locations and find them instantly with QR code scanning.
Nest locations three levels deep (room → shelf → bin) and create as many as you need. Scan any location to see everything in it, including what’s nested inside.
![Storage location management with QR codes](https://getflippd.com/images/screens/storage.webp?v=1d106567cb)

## Import everything. Export anything. Always yours.
## AI-Powered Bulk Import
Import hundreds of items in seconds from eBay, Mercari, Poshmark, or any custom spreadsheet. Our AI intelligently maps your data fields, no matter the format.
Stop wasting hours on manual data entry. Whether it's a platform export or your own custom tracking sheet, Flippd handles it automatically.
![AI-powered bulk import from spreadsheets and platforms](https://getflippd.com/images/screens/imports.webp?v=1ad23c08ea)
## Always Own Your Data
Your business data belongs to you, always. Export your inventory, sales, expenses, and mileage anytime to an Excel workbook, and download your P&L and Schedule C as CSV or PDF.
No lock-in, no restrictions. Back up everything or take it with you whenever you want.
![Export your data to an Excel workbook](https://getflippd.com/images/screens/export.webp?v=0101297cf1)
## Data Health
One scan checks every item, sale, and expense for the gaps that quietly distort your numbers: no cost recorded, sales with no price or platform, dates out of order, listings with no photos, duplicates, and uncategorised expenses.
It's the perfect first stop after importing the spreadsheet you've been maintaining for years. Each issue shows how many items it affects, and you tap in to fix or dismiss them one by one, so your reports and coach tips are built on data you can trust. Nothing is ever changed without you.
