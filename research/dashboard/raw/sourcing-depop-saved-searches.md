#  Saved Searches 
You can save up to 10 searches, including size, brand, and price, to make it easier to find it next time. Your saved searches will automatically appear when you start typing into the search bar.
To save your search, just tap the star in the search bar. You can also opt in to start receiving notifications for new items. You’ll get a weekly push notification for all your saved searches.
To turn off notifications:
**iOS**
  * Go to **My Depop,** then **My account**
  * Under **Settings,** choose **Preferences,** then scroll down to **Notifications**
  * Choose **Push notifications** and toggle off **New results for saved search**


**Android**
  * Go to **Settings**
  * Scroll down to **Notifications**
  * Choose **Push Notifications** and toggle off **New results for saved search**


Once a search is saved, it can’t be edited, so you’ll need to delete it and start again. To do this, either swipe left on your saved search or tap the star in bold in the search bar and confirm.
We currently notify you of all new items worldwide; however, depending on your location, you might not be able to see items outside your country.
