#  Quick start guide for eBay 
1 year ago Updated
Congratulations, you’ve just installed Shopify Marketplace Connect and now have the easiest way of selling on eBay and more!
Marketplace Connect allows you to create & manage marketplace listings directly from Shopify whilst automatically keeping your inventory & orders synced for easy fulfillment.
[Basic Tutorial video - connection and link listings](https://youtu.be/1gVlzUUkxMo)
### Overview Screen
On the Overview Screen, you will see notification cards that serve as shortcuts to other pages of the app. By clicking on these notification cards, you can easily navigate to the respective pages or sections to take necessary actions.
![](https://www.shopifymarketplaceconnecthelp.com/hc/article_attachments/11697266689935)
### Marketplaces Screen
This can be found at the top right of the overview screen
The Marketplaces screen provides you with a convenient way to connect your online marketplaces such as Amazon, eBay, Walmart, and Etsy. You can effortlessly oversee your connected marketplaces where you can add or remove your connections.
### Navigation Menu
The navigation menu on the left will guide you toward the different views in Shopify Marketplace Connect.
**Listings**
The Listings view features the 3 listing modes and can be used to create and manage listings.
[Single & Bulk edit listing for ebay](https://www.shopifymarketplaceconnecthelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/9805002770447)
**Mapping**
Map Shopify Product Attributes or Shopify Marketplace Connect grid columns to your eBay listings.
**Orders**
The Order screen lets you review and manage inbound marketplace orders before they are transferred to Shopify based on the order transfer settings you have selected.
**Settings**
The Settings view lets you review the linked listings, synchronization settings, order import settings, and more depending on the marketplace selected. 
**FAQ**
In addition to the support articles available in the Help Center, you can also view a curated selection of help guides directly from within the Marketplace Connect app.
Connect Your First Marketplace
To get started, simply select Add new eBay connection from the Home/Marketplaces screen and follow the prompts to associate your eBay account.
![](https://www.shopifymarketplaceconnecthelp.com/hc/article_attachments/11697266690575)
Connecting your eBay account:
[eBay account](https://www.shopifymarketplaceconnecthelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/7240099379343)
### Listing Products
Once you have connected your eBay account, you can edit the marketplace values settings using any of the three listing modes in the Listings View.
Once you have confirmed your marketplace settings, simply enable and publish your products to set them live for sale on eBay.
![](https://www.shopifymarketplaceconnecthelp.com/hc/article_attachments/9857346963727)
![](https://www.shopifymarketplaceconnecthelp.com/hc/article_attachments/11697605710351)
For further assistance, please reach out to marketplace-connect@shopify.com
