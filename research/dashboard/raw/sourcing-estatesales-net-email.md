#  How to Receive Email Notifications About Upcoming Sales 
  * 1 year ago
  * Updated


If you’d like to stay informed about upcoming estate sales in your area, it’s simple! All you need to do is create a User account. This will give you access to free email notifications whenever sales are posted near you.
* * *
### Follow these steps to get started:
  1. Visit the Homepage 
     * • Head to [EstateSales.NET](https://www.estatesales.net/).
     * • Look for the **red "Get Free Sale Notifications"** button—it’s right on the homepage and easy to spot.
  2. Sign Up for Free 
     * • Click the button to begin creating your account.
     * • You’ll be asked to provide some basic information, like your email address and the area where you want to receive notifications.
  3. Customize Your Preferences 
     * • Once your account is set up, you can choose the types of sales you’re interested in by customizing your notification settings.
  4. Stay in the Loop 
     * • That’s it! You’ll now receive email alerts about estate sales in your chosen area, ensuring you never miss a sale.
