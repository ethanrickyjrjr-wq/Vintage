## Frequently Asked Questions
What platforms does Flippd work with?
Flippd works with any selling platform: eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Facebook Marketplace, Depop, Etsy, Amazon, flea markets, consignment shops, or any combination. It's platform-agnostic by design.
Can I track mileage for sourcing trips?
Yes! Flippd includes built-in mileage tracking for tax-deductible business miles. Log thrift store runs, estate sales, garage sales, and post office trips. Export reports at tax time.
Is Flippd better than using spreadsheets?
Yes! Flippd is purpose-built for resellers. Add items with photos from your phone, track across platforms, calculate profit automatically, and access your data anywhere. No formulas to maintain or files to sync.
How much does Flippd cost?
Experience every feature free: up to 20 inventory items, plus 25 expenses and 30 mileage entries a month. No credit card required. Upgrade to Flippd Pro for unlimited access when you're ready.
