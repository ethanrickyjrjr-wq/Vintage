# Bookmark a show, listing, or search
[ ![Whatnot Support](https://help.whatnot.com/system/photos/36667494453133/BLACKLOGO.png) Whatnot Support ](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/9780885421069-Bookmark-a-show-listing-or-search) February 19, 2026 at 11:47 AM
FollowNot yet followed by anyone
Bookmarking lets you save upcoming shows, specific listings, and product searches you’re interested in so you can find them later and get notified about them. 
**Tip:** If you want to be notified anytime a seller goes live (not just for a single show or product), you can **Follow** the seller from their profile.
**In this article:**

  * [Bookmark a search](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/9780885421069-Bookmark-a-show-listing-or-search#h_01KG83R0W25D3M90P7KXPC0S9Y)
  * [View your bookmarks](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/9780885421069-Bookmark-a-show-listing-or-search#h_01KG83R0W42VGQSSC6RA8FDQV3)
  * [Remove a bookmark](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/9780885421069-Bookmark-a-show-listing-or-search#h_01KG83R0W6MVGZ804FBYHY4J90)


## **How bookmarks work**
When you bookmark something: 
  * It’s added to your [**Saved** list](https://www.whatnot.com/?activityTab=saved) under the **Activity** tab
  * You’ll get a notification when a bookmarked show or product goes live, or new results match a saved search (make sure [notifications are enabled](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/articles/9429224729101))
  * Bookmarking is anonymous, so sellers and other buyers can only see the total number of bookmarks, not who saved them

![](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/article_attachments/43118228783117)
## **Bookmark a show or product**
  1. Open the show or product listing you want to save.
  2. Tap or click the **bookmark** icon to save it. 


## **Bookmark a search**
Save a product search to be notified when new matching products are listed.
  1. Run your product search (for example, by keyword, category, or brand).
  2. On the search results page, tap or click **Save this Search.**
  3. **Confirm** that you want to receive notifications. 


**Note:** You can’t save searches for Shows, Categories, or Users.
## **View your bookmarks**
You’ll see all your saved shows, products, and searches in one place.
  1. From Whatnot's home screen, open your **Activity** tab. 
  2. Select **Saved** to view your list. 


## **Remove a bookmark**
When you remove a bookmark, it'll disappear from your Saved list, and you’ll no longer receive notifications about it. 
  1. From Whatnot's home screen, open your **Activity** tab. 
  2. Select **Saved.**
  3. Remove the bookmark:
     * _For saved shows or products:_ Tap the **bookmark icon** again. When the icon changes from solid to empty, the bookmark is removed.
     * _For saved searches:_ Tap the **menu icon** on the left, then **Delete this search**(mobile app) or the**trash icon**(web).
