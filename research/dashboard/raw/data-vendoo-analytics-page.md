## Vendoo Analytics FAQs
[](https://www.vendoo.co/reselling-analytics)
### What is Vendoo Analytics?
Vendoo Analytics is a powerful tool designed to help resellers track their sales, analyze trends, and manage inventory in real-time. It provides detailed reports and insights to help you make data-driven decisions and optimize your reselling business.
[](https://www.vendoo.co/reselling-analytics)
### How does real-time sales tracking work?
Our real-time sales tracking feature updates your sales data as transactions happen. This allows you to monitor your performance instantly and respond quickly to market changes, ensuring you never miss an opportunity.
[](https://www.vendoo.co/reselling-analytics)
### Can I customize my dashboards in Vendoo Analytics?
Yes, Vendoo Analytics offers customizable analytics. In addition to the general data provided, you can assign custom labels to your items to create custom analytics by group.
