# How much does Flipwise cost?
[](javascript:window.print\(\) "Print this article")
Flipwise charges you based on the total number of items it considers “under management” in your store. To [calculate the size of your store](https://help.flipwise.app/article/28-how-does-flipwise-calculate-the-size-of-your-store), Flipwise considers the total number of active listings plus the number sold over the last 30 days.
Here are the pricing tiers:
  * 0-24 listings - free
  * 25-99 listings - $9.99
  * 100-499 listings - $19.99
  * 500-999 listings - $29.99
  * 1,000-1,999 listings - $39.99
  * 2,000-4,999 listings - $49.99
  * 5,000-9,999 listings - $59.99
  * 10,000-19,999 listings - $69.99
  * 20,000+ listings - contact us
