# How To Download a CSV Inventory Spreadsheet and Sales Report
How to download your CSV inventory report and sales report with Vendoo, for inventory management, book-keeping, and tax preparation.
Written by Ben Martinez
Updated over 2 weeks ago
# **Download Inventory & Sales Reports from Vendoo as a CSV File**
### With Vendoo, you can say goodbye to spreadsheets and tedious book-keeping methods. 
### You can easily **download all of the information stored in Vendoo in a csv.**
There are two ways to access the CSV export tool. You can find it under the blue “multi-action” button on the top right-hand side of your Vendoo inventory screen, or you can find it under the [settings menu](https://web.vendoo.co/app/settings/).

In your account settings, you can select a **custom date range** for your CSV information, or select **all inventory.** Keep in mind that the Start and End dates are based on the dates your items were **created**.

This information is logged _as you use Vendoo._ Vendoo centralizes your inventory information in one place. **Book-keeping and tax time just got _much easier_** _!_ 🔥
