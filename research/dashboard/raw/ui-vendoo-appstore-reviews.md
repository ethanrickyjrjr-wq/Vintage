  * ### PLEASE READ
Jun 12
Jengrace061186080707
I subscribed to Vendoo specifically because I needed a way to merge and manage existing listings across multiple marketplaces. Unfortunately, I discovered after paying that the Import & Merge feature is not available to all users and is still being rolled out in beta. This was not clearly disclosed before purchase.Without access to this feature, the process of merging existing listings is incredibly time-consuming. Instead of automatically matching listings across marketplaces, I would have to manually copy and paste links from each marketplace into every individual listing. For sellers with a large inventory, this could take weeks of work.Had I known this key feature was not available to all subscribers, I would not have signed up. The platform may work well for some users, but the lack of transparency regarding feature availability was extremely disappointing and made the service far less useful than advertised.
MORE
Developer Response
We’re sorry for the frustration and appreciate you sharing this feedback. Import & Merge is currently in beta, and it is labeled with a Beta badge in Vendoo to make its availability clear. The feature is also presented during onboarding. However, if the “I have not crosslisted items” option is selected, Import & Merge is not activated automatically, since it’s intended for users who already have crosslisted inventory. That said, our Support team can manually activate the feature if needed, so users can restart the process and avoid having to paste marketplace URLs for every individual item. We understand how important this feature can be for sellers with a large inventory, and we’re continuing to improve it as we roll it out more broadly.
