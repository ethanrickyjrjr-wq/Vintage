# 👋 Welcome to Crosslist! [​](https://docs.crosslist.com/#welcome-to-crosslist)
Crosslist helps you create, import, publish, and manage product listings across multiple online marketplaces from one place.
You can also track sales, manage orders, and use analytics to monitor your performance and make more informed decisions.
Whether you’re listing brand-new inventory, expanding existing listings to additional platforms, or managing your day-to-day selling activity, the guides below will help you get set up and start using Crosslist.
Find an answer quickly using the search function.
[Watch demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LN2-tMALb3I)
## Getting started [​](https://docs.crosslist.com/#getting-started)
The following articles will help you get started quickly:
  1. [Listing new inventory](https://docs.crosslist.com/getting-started/listing-new-inventory)
  2. [Cross listing existing inventory](https://docs.crosslist.com/getting-started/cross-listing-existing-inventory)
  3. [Setting up shipping profiles](https://docs.crosslist.com/getting-started/shipping-profiles/)


**Listing new inventory** is for users whose products haven’t been listed on a supported marketplace yet. You’ll learn how to create listings in Crosslist and publish them to your chosen marketplaces.
**Cross listing existing inventory** is for users who already have active listings on a supported marketplace. This guide explains how to import those listings into Crosslist and publish them on additional platforms.
**Setting up shipping profiles** explains how to configure your preferred shipping methods in Crosslist. This guide is recommended for **all users** , regardless of whether you’re creating new listings or importing existing inventory.
Once your inventory is set up, you can use Crosslist to manage and update your listings, keep track of sales and orders, and review analytics that provide insight into your selling activity and performance.
