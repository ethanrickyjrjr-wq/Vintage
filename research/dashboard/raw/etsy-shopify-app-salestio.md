##  Import Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Kaufland, and Walmart orders. Sync prices, stock, and fulfillment. 
Import Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Kaufland, and Walmart orders into Shopify and sync fulfillment and tracking back to each marketplace. Process marketplace orders through the shipping, warehouse and invoicing apps you already use. Keep inventory and prices synced from Shopify. Create listings from your catalog, mapping metafields to category requirements and setting custom prices, quantities and descriptions. Walmart and Kaufland support order and fulfillment sync only. 
Import Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Kaufland, and Walmart orders into Shopify and sync fulfillment and ... more
  * Import Amazon(FBM & FBA), eBay, Etsy, Walmart and Kaufland orders into Shopify 
  * Sync fulfillment and shipment tracking from Shopify back to each marketplace 
  * Sync Amazon, eBay and Etsy stock and prices using quantity and pricing rules 
  * Create Amazon, eBay and Etsy listings with custom product descriptions 
  * Map Shopify metafields to listings, including eBay Motors fitment (KType, ePID)
