# Select a distribution method
Install AI ToolkitAsk about this pageCopy MD
Before you can share your app, select a distribution method. You can't change the method after you select it, so review the [capabilities and requirements](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/distribution#capabilities-and-requirements) before continuing.
* * *
## 
[Anchor to Select a distribution method](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/distribution/select-distribution-method#select-a-distribution-method)Select a distribution method
Distribution card availability
The **Distribution** card appears only for apps that belong to a Partner organization. If you created the app in your store's organization, then install it from the app's **Installs** card instead. [Learn how to install your app](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/dev-dashboard/create-apps-using-dev-dashboard#step-3-install-your-app).
**Distribution card availability :**
The **Distribution** card appears only for apps that belong to a Partner organization. If you created the app in your store's organization, then install it from the app's **Installs** card instead. [Learn how to install your app](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/dev-dashboard/create-apps-using-dev-dashboard#step-3-install-your-app).
After you review the available methods, complete the following steps to make your selection:
  1. Open your app in the [Dev Dashboard](https://dev.shopify.com/dashboard/).
  2. On the app's **Home** , find the **Distribution** card and click **Select distribution method**.
  3. Select a distribution method, and then confirm the selection.


After you choose, the same card links to what you need next: **Manage Shopify App Store listing** for public distribution, or **Manage custom install link** for custom distribution.
If you selected **Public distribution** , then you can start creating your Shopify App Store listing, and submit your app for review when it's ready. See [submitting an app to the Shopify App Store](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/app-store-review/submit-app-for-review).
If you selected **Custom distribution** , then generate an install link and send it to the store owner.
* * *
## 
[Anchor to Install a custom app on multiple stores](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/distribution/select-distribution-method#install-a-custom-app-on-multiple-stores)Install a custom app on multiple stores
A custom app can be installed on multiple stores when those stores belong to the same Plus organization.
  1. After you select **Custom distribution** , enter the store's `myshopify.com` or `admin.shopify.com` [domain](https://help.shopify.com/manual/domains).
  2. Optional: to limit your app to a single store, clear **Allow multi-store installs for one Plus organization**.
  3. Click **Generate link** , and then copy the install link.


Send the install link to the store owner so that they can install the app. For example, you can share it by email.
Note
If you created your custom app before July 26, 2023, then [contact Shopify Support](https://help.shopify.com/support) to enable multi-store installs.
