# Shopify app
![Shopify app icon](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/apps/shopify-app/shopify-60px-icon.png)
[Shopify app](https://www.shopify.ca/install)
Install the Shopify mobile app for your device.
With the [Shopify app](https://www.shopify.com/install/detect), you can manage your store from your iPhone or Android device. Simply download the app and log in to your Shopify account to get started running your business.
Download and install the Shopify app that is designed for the operating system on your device or computer:
  * [Shopify for iPhone](https://itunes.apple.com/app/shopify/id371294472?mt=8)
  * [Shopify for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.shopify.mobile)


From the Shopify app, you can [see how your store is performing](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-admin), [manage your orders](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment), and [update your catalog](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products). You can manage and update your online store with the [mobile theme editor](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-admin/shopify-app/mobile-online-store#theme-editor). [Notifications](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/managing-order-details#activate-push-notifications) let you know when a new order comes in or somebody mentions you in a [Timeline comment](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-admin/productivity-tools/timeline). You can even [capture payments](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/payments/capturing-payments) and [create fulfillments](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/fulfilling-orders) from the app. On Apple Watch, you can [view store metrics](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-admin/shopify-app/watch-app) and [ask Sidekick questions](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-admin/shopify-app/watch-app#sidekick) using voice mode.
The Shopify app works on Android devices and iOS devices including iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. The Shopify app supports the following OS versions:
  * iOS: Version 18 or later
  * iPadOS: Version 18 or later
  * Android: Version 11 or later
  * watchOS: Version 10 or later


### iOS permission requirements
If you update your device to iOS 15.1 or higher and use third-party Shopify apps, then you might need to activate **Cross-Website tracking** in your iOS settings. **Cross-Website tracking** lets third-party Shopify apps that you install in your Shopify admin stay connected in Shopify Inbox, Shopify Point of Sale, and the Shopify app.
To activate **Cross-Website tracking** , complete the following steps:
  1. On your iOS device, open the **Settings** app.
  2. Tap **Shopify** , **Shopify POS** , or **Shopify Inbox**.
  3. Activate **Allow Cross-Website Tracking**.
