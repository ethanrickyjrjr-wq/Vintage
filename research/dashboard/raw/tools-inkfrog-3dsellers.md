5 Best Inkfrog Alternatives After the Shutdown
If you used Inkfrog, you've had to find a new way to manage your eBay listings: the platform shut down on June 1, 2026. Choosing the right replacement still matters. Your listings, templates, images, and workflows are all at stake, and choosing the wrong alternative could cost you weeks of productivity and potentially thousands of dollars in lost sales. 
In this guide, we'll help you make the best choice for your business by walking you through the top 5 alternatives to Inkfrog available to eBay sellers in 2026. ## Why Did Inkfrog Shut Down?
Inkfrog, owned by Wix, announced on April 29, 2026 that it would permanently shut down on June 1, 2026. While the company hasn't provided detailed reasons for the shutdown beyond calling it a "difficult decision," the closure leaves of thousands of eBay sellers looking for new listing management solutions. The announcement came with just over one month's notice, leaving sellers a tight window to export their data and migrate to a new platform.
### When Did Inkfrog Shut Down?
Inkfrog shut down completely on June 1, 2026. May 31 was the last day to export listing data, and there's no grace period or data recovery option; inkfrog.com now redirects to Wix.
### What Happened to Listings After Inkfrog Shut Down?
Your live eBay listings didn't disappear when Inkfrog shut down. However, if you used Inkfrog templates with Inkfrog-hosted design elements like banners, headers, store logos, or promotional graphics, those images are no longer hosted and will no longer appear on your templates. Your listings will still function, but they'll lose their professional branded appearance and display broken image icons where your custom design elements used to be. 
Additionally, if you were using Inkfrog to sync inventory across multiple channels like Shopify, Amazon, or BigCommerce, that sync has stopped working. Without inventory sync, you open yourself up to the risk of overselling. Finally, all your saved draft listings, templates, master profiles, bulk revision rules, and listing workflows stored in Inkfrog will be inaccessible after the shutdown.

### 2. Wonderlister: Multi-Channel Listing Management
Wonderlister is an eBay listing management solution that effectively offers two separate products for eBay listing management: a Windows desktop application and a cloud-based online version. Both focus on bulk editing capabilities and multi-account management for sellers who want extensive control over listing data. 
**Key features include:**
  * Desktop version for Windows with offline functionality and local database storage, while cloud-based online version accessible from any browser
  * Bulk editing across multiple eBay accounts simultaneously
  * Automated listing scheduling and auto-relist functionality
  * Custom templates with full HTML control


Wonderlister has been a major player in the eBay seller community for years, and while it remains a powerful tool, it has struggled to keep up with the evolving needs of eBay sellers today. For starters, it lacks any AI tools to make workflows smarter and more efficient, relying instead on rules and automations. Additionally, the desktop version requires SQL database setup during installation, effectively tying you to a specific Windows computer, and while the online version eliminates platform restrictions and allows remote team access, it shares similar limitations in terms of features and functionality. 
Wonderlister's pricing starts at $25 per month for basic plans across both desktop and online versions. While Wonderlister offers flexibility in deployment options, the dated interface, relatively complex setup, and steep learning curve make it less accessible than more modern alternatives.

### 3. CrazyLister: Template-Focused eBay Listing Tool
CrazyLister is a cloud-based listing tool built around applying visual templates to eBay and Amazon listings. The system handles basic listing creation, bulk editing, and multichannel publishing while focusing primarily on template design and mobile optimization. 
**Key features include:**
  * Drag-and-drop template editor with a library of pre-made designs
  * Mobile editor for controlling how listings display on phones and tablets
  * Import from eBay, Amazon, or CSV files with automatic channel compliance checking


CrazyLister handles the basics of listing management well, but lacks more advanced tools for inventory management and complex automation. The bulk editing capabilities focus primarily on applying templates rather than product data management, which is done exclusively with CSV uploads, and there are no AI tools. The template library, while extensive, tends to include dated designs that don't reflect current ecommerce aesthetics. The mobile editor, while unique, addresses a narrow use case that more advanced tools handle by creating responsive templates from the start. 
Pricing starts at $25 per month for up to 500 listings, rising to $60 per month for up to 5000 listings. Add-ons like removing the CrazyLister footer or background branding cost an additional $5 per month each.

Frooition operates differently from the other options on this list. The company is effectively a design agency that also provides basic listing management software. 
**Key features include:**
  * Custom-designed eBay templates built specifically for your brand
  * Professional design services rather than DIY templates
  * eBay SEO optimization services
  * Managed migration service for transitioning from other platforms
  * Custom pricing based on service level


The service-based model means comparatively higher costs compared to the other tools on our list. While Frooition handles template design and migration work for you, monthly costs can vary depending on the level of service required. The company heavily promotes itself as an alternative for Inkfrog users, its unclear costs and relatively limited features make it less suitable for sellers who need a true eBay selling manager.

### 5. SixBit: Desktop-Based Listing Software
SixBit is a Windows desktop application that has been serving eBay sellers since 1997. The software takes a database-driven approach to listing management, storing all listing data locally on your computer rather than in the cloud. 
**Key features include:**
  * Desktop database that stores complete listing history
  * Multi-user support for teams working in the same office
  * Detailed profit and loss reporting with historical data
  * Consignment selling features for sellers who work with consignors
  * Customizable forms and views for data entry


The desktop-only nature of SixBit is both its strength and its limitation. While local data storage provides speed and control, it also means you can't access your listings from mobile devices or work remotely. The software has a steep learning curve and an outdated interface. The software is also Windows-only, excluding Mac users entirely. 
Pricing starts at $29.99 per month and can be as high as $99.99 per month for enterprise-level plans. While the pricing is competitive, the platform limitations and technical requirements make it best suited for Windows users who work primarily from a single location and need extensive historical data capabilities.
