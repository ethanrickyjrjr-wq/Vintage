## How searches, prices and alerts work.
Something else on your mind? Write to support@crawlbench.com and a real person will answer.
### What can I search for?
Anything listed on Facebook Marketplace: cars, homes, rentals, electronics, furniture, cameras, tools, collectibles and more. Describe it, set a price and pick the cities.
### What is a Day Pass?
Free for 24 hours, with no credit card. One search for one person at Growth speed, with the price check on every listing and alerts on every channel. The 24 hours start when your first search goes live. When it ends, any plan starts with 3 days free, card required.
### How do you know the usual price?
We compare each listing with similar ones in the same area: the same model, year and condition, or the same size and neighborhood for homes and rentals.
### How fast are alerts?
Busy cities are checked every minute on Scale, every 2 minutes on Pro, every 5 on Growth and every 10 on Starter. A search that fits in one round checks every city. On a wider search, quiet cities are checked less often. You only hear about listings that match your search. Listings found in the same check arrive together in one alert.
### Which cities do you cover?
52 countries and 3,514 cities, 209 of them in the United States. That is the United States and Canada, 33 countries in Europe, the United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia, Türkiye and Egypt, India and Pakistan, Hong Kong, 4 countries in Southeast Asia, Australia and New Zealand, Brazil and Guyana, Nigeria and Zimbabwe. Each search covers any cities in one country.
### How do I contact a seller?
From your own Marketplace account. The assistant reviews each listing, suggests questions and a first offer, and drafts a message you can copy.
