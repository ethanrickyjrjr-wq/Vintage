## Turn your Marketplace notifications on or off
  1. Click your profile picture in the top right of Facebook. 
  2. Select **Settings & privacy**, then click **Settings.**
  3. Click **Notifications** in the left menu.
  4. Click **Marketplace**.
  5. Click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-toggleonsettings-desktop&density=1)**Toggle** next to a notification type to turn it on or off.
