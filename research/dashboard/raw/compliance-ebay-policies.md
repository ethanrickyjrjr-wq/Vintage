# Policies
In order to align eBay's developer community with eBay policies and ensure violations (and resulting eBay actions) do not occur, developers should become familiar with all relevant eBay policies.
  * [Rules and Policies](https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/default/ebay-rules-policies?id=4205)
  * [User Privacy Notice](https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/member-behavior-policies/user-privacy-notice?id=4260)
  * [User Agreement](https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/member-behaviour-policies/user-agreement?id=4259)


Additionally, ensure you are familiar with and that your application complies with the [eBay API License Agreement](https://developer.ebay.com/join/api-license-agreement).
Violations or facilitation of violations of eBay policies may result in a range of actions, including:
  * Listing cancellation
  * Limits on user account privileges
  * User account suspension
  * Forfeit of eBay fees on cancelled listings
  * Suspension of developer's access to the eBay Web Services
  * Termination of developer's eBay Developers Program membership


Please contact [Developer Technical Support](https://developer.ebay.com/support/developer-technical-support) for questions related to our policies.
