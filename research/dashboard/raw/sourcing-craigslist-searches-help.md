## Saved Searches
You can save any search you make for future use by clicking the "save search" link near the top right of a search results page.
Saved searches are accessible from your account page.
[Log in to your account](https://www.craigslist.org/about/help/account/login) to view, sort, rename and follow saved searches.
Saved searches cannot be edited; you must create a new one and delete the old one.
[Search alerts](https://www.craigslist.org/about/help/account/features/alerts) send an automated email when new postings match one of your saved searches.
