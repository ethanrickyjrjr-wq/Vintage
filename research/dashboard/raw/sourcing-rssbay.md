##### Instructions
Enter your search term and the various options. Hit the 'create rss feed' button and enter the link in your favourite rss-reader! 
##### eBay Site (Global ID)
Select the eBay Site you want te search
##### Item Location
Select the country you want to filter on, default is world wide
##### Category ID
A numerical id of the category you want to filter on. The category id can be found in the eBay category url, for example:
`https://www.ebay.com/b/Sports-Trading-Cards-Accessories/_212_/bn_1859819`
Here, 212 is the category id.
##### Buying Formats
Either Auction, Buy it now or both
##### Condition
The condition you would like to filter on. Read more about the various conditions [here](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/listings/creating-managing-listings/item-conditions-category?id=4765)
##### Price Range
Enter a minimum and a maximum price
##### Seller
The id of the seller you want to filter on, see [here](https://partnerhelp.ebay.com/helpcenter/s/article/How-can-I-find-a-Seller-ID?language=en_US) how to find a sellers id
##### Time Frame
Select one of the options and the amount off time
## What is RSS?
RSS is an acronym for Really Simple Syndication. It is used to stay up to date on new articles on blogs, news and many more things where you might want to receive instant updates on content. To do this, many websites provide RSS feeds themselves. For ebay, RSS feeds where available to keep track of new listings, but they were unfortunately discontinued.
## Why create eBay rss feeds?
If you are looking for a specific item to buy on eBay, it is very important to be on top of new listings. Checking the eBay search results periodically by hand is very time consuming. By creating rss feeds of your eBay searches, you are notified instantly on new items being listed matching your criteria.
## How to use eBay rss feeds?
After you have created your feed, you must enter the url in your favorite rss-reader. This can be an online reader, like feedly or inoreader or a desktop rss reader like Thunderbird or Feedlab.
## Rss feeds for eBay Sellers
By creating rss feeds of your own listings as a seller, you can increase the visibility of your listings and increase the amount of eyes that are on your items. It is also a great way to promote your store.
