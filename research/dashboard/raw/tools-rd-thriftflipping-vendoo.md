# Vendoo Pricing 2026: Plans, Free Tier, Add-Ons & Your Real Monthly Cost
Published August 15, 2026 · 7 min read
Vendoo’s headline price looks friendly — plans starting under $10 a month, even a free tier. But ask actual Vendoo users what they pay and the numbers come back higher, because the plan price is only part of the bill. Here’s the full 2026 breakdown: every tier, the add-on system that catches people off guard, and the real monthly cost for different seller sizes.
## The Plan Tiers
Vendoo prices by how many **new items you list per month** (unused slots don’t roll over). All plans include the core: cross-listing to 10+ marketplaces (eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Etsy, Grailed, Facebook Marketplace, Whatnot, and more), inventory management, and image hosting.  
| Plan  | Monthly Price  | New Items/Month  | Background Removals  |  
| --- | --- | --- | --- |  
| Free  | $0  | 5  | 3  |  
| Starter  | $8.99  | 25  | 25  |  
| Simple  | $19.99  | 125  | 125  |  
| Plus  | $29.99  | 250  | 250  |  
| Pro  | $49.99  | 600  | 600  |  
| Advanced  | $99.99  | More volume  | Scales up  |  
| Expert  | $149.99  | Highest tier  | Scales up  |  
## The Add-Ons: Where the Real Price Lives
On monthly billing, three features most resellers consider essential are **paid add-ons** :
  * **Importing** ($4.99/mo) — pulls your existing listings in from marketplaces. Without it, you re-create your inventory by hand.
  * **Bulk Delist & Relist** ($4.99/mo) — the refresh workflow that keeps listings ranking; core to how most people use a cross-lister.
  * **All Marketplaces** ($4.99/mo) — unlocks cross-posting beyond the base allotment of platforms.


The bundle of all three runs **$11.99/month** — and on annual billing, add-ons are typically included, which is Vendoo’s not-so-subtle nudge toward the yearly commitment (usually around two months free).
## So What Do You Actually Pay?  
| Seller Profile  | Realistic Setup  | True Monthly Cost  |  
| --- | --- | --- |  
| Testing the waters  | Free plan, no add-ons  | $0 (5 items is genuinely tiny)  |  
| New reseller, ~25 listings/mo  | Starter + bundle  | ~$20.98  |  
| Part-timer, ~100 listings/mo  | Simple + bundle  | ~$31.98  |  
| Serious side hustle, ~250/mo  | Plus + bundle  | ~$41.98  |  
| Full-time, 500+/mo  | Pro + bundle  | ~$61.98  |  
That $31.98 middle row is the honest number to compare against competitors’ headline prices — it’s what a typical part-time Vendoo user pays for a fully functional setup.
#### ⚠️ Hard Truth
A cross-lister at $30+/month has to EARN its seat. If you list 40 items a month, Vendoo costs you roughly 75 cents per listing — and if cross-listing only nets you a couple of extra sales, the tool is eating your margin, not growing it.
#### ✅ The Fix
Run the math before subscribing: most sellers report cross-listing lifts sales meaningfully once inventory is on 3+ platforms, but the lift has to exceed the subscription. Start on the free plan or Starter, measure one real month, and upgrade only when the slot limit — not the marketing — forces it.
**🌱 Pro Tip:** Vendoo’s free plan is small, but it’s a genuine no-card way to learn the workflow. List your five best items across three platforms this week and you’ll know within days whether the multi-platform lift is real for YOUR inventory.
## Worth It? Depends Which Seller You Are
  * **Strong fit:** phone-first sellers (Vendoo’s mobile app is a real differentiator), sellers who want built-in analytics and background removal, and anyone listing 100+ items monthly across 3+ platforms.
  * **Weak fit:** under ~25 listings a month (cross-list manually, keep the money), and bargain hunters — cheaper tools exist if you can live with their trade-offs, which we cover in the [budget cross-lister comparison](https://thriftflipping.com/blog/article-169-vendoo-vs-crosslist-vs-flyp.html).
