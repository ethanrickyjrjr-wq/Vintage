## Plans and pricing FAQs
Get answers to our most asked questions about our plans.
**How does your pricing work?**
Pricing is tiered based on monthly orders, and overages apply if that number is exceeded. Linnworks Listing, Linnworks WMS, and Skuvault Enhanced Warehouse are also based on your number of orders. Linnworks Forecasting is a flat fee.
**How do I decide between packages?**
Both our plans help fast-growing retailers reduce manual work, cut human error, and get accurate inventory data to improve efficiency, increase sales, and deliver great customer experiences.  
  
Our SkuVault Core package is most popular with retailers making the switch from spreadsheets to get organized in the warehouse fast. It’s only available in North America. The Linnworks package is ideal for retailers who need one centralized platform to manage ecommerce from listing through to shipping and fulfillment, with automated order management built in.
**Are quality control, serialization, and other advanced warehouse features available in my region?**
Quality control, serialization, product substitution, and 3PL inventory management with a client portal are part of SkuVault Core, which is only available in North America. All other features are available globally.
**Should I invest in onboarding? How much is it?**
We recommend investing in onboarding, which is a one-off fee calculated based on your needs, the timing of your implementation, and the resources you have available internally.
