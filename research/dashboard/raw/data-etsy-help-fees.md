# What are the Fees and Taxes for Selling on Etsy? 
When you sell on Etsy, you incur fees for using certain services. 
Your balance from sales, fees, and taxes is calculated and displayed in your Payment account. [Learn how to manage your Payment account](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015747228?segment=selling).
## **How fees appear in your Payment account**
### **Sale-based fees**
When you make a sale, certain fees and taxes are tied to that specific sale. They stay in your Pending balance alongside the earnings from that sale. When those earnings clear, the sale’s net amount (earnings minus these fees) moves to your Current balance together. These include:
  * Transaction fees (including shipping and gift wrap transaction fees)
  * Payment processing fees
  * Regulatory operating fees
  * Offsite Ads fees
  * VAT or other applicable seller taxes


Your payment account also shows taxes and government fees paid by the buyer. These are included in the total amount paid by the buyer and then subtracted back out, they are not deducted from your listing price.
### **Real-time fees**
Other fees and purchases, such as Etsy Ads, shipping labels, listing and renewal fees, and subscriptions like Etsy Plus, are deducted from your Current balance when they’re charged. 
If your Total balance is negative at the end of the month, you’ll have an amount due on the first of the next month. When your outstanding balance is due, you’ll receive an email from Etsy.
## **Currency conversion**
All fixed-cost fees, like listings and Pattern, are published in USD. You can see the fixed USD cost for a fee in its description. Etsy converts fees from USD to your payment account currency at the market rate at the time the fee is reflected in your payment account. This conversion may change if currency exchange rates change.

## **Set-up fee**  
| **Fee name**  | **What it means**  |  
| --- | --- |  
| Set-up fee  |  - You will be charged a one-time, non-refundable fee for opening your shop.  - Cost varies by location. - If applicable, the set-up fee will be displayed and charged as part of the shop set-up process. - If you're using Payoneer, the Etsy onboarding fee will be in USD. Taxes may apply. - [Learn more about the set-up fee.](https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/#:~:text=Set%2DUp%20Fee,the%20shop%20set%2Dup%20process)  |  
## **Listing fees**  
| **Fee name**  | **What it means**  |  
| --- | --- |  
| Listing  |  - $0.20 per listing. - Fee is added to your Payment account when an item is listed. - Has to be paid regardless of resulting sales. - A listing expires after 4 months.  |  
| Auto-renew expired  |  - $0.20 per listing. - Fee added when a listing is auto-renewed. - Has to be paid regardless of resulting sales. - A listing expires after 4 months. When a listing is auto-renewed, the 4-month listing period starts over.   |  
| Auto-renew sold   |  - $0.20 per each additional quantity.  - If you sell multiple quantities of a listing in one transaction you’ll owe a multi-quantity fee for each additional quantity. - If you sell more than one (1) item in an order, you've already been charged for listing that first item. We only charge you $0.20 each for the extra quantity you sell. [Learn more about multi-quantity items](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000344908?segment=selling).  |  
| Private listing fee  |  - $0.20 per private listing. - Fee is added to Payment account if the specified buyer purchases the item. [Learn more about custom listings](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015663107?segment=selling).  |  
## **Shipping label fees**  
| **Fee name**  | **What it means**  |  
| --- | --- |  
| Shipping labels  |  - Dependent on your selections when purchasing Etsy Shipping Labels. - Etsy Shipping Labels are available to [sellers in certain countries](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360001967188?segment=selling).  |  
## **Transaction fees**  
| **Fee name**  | **What it means**  |  
| --- | --- |  
| Transaction  |  - 6.5% of the total order amount in your designated listing currency. This fee applies to shipping and gift wrapping (if you charge your customers for those). Fee amount is converted to your Payment account currency at the market rate where your listing and Payment account currencies differ. - Different from the [Etsy Payments processing fee](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015628847?segment=selling). - Doesn’t include transaction fees incurred with Pattern. - Charges for shipping and gift wrapping fees will be itemized separately on your Payment account.  |  
| Square manual  |  - When you sell items in-person using Square that are _not synced_ from your Etsy shop inventory, there is a $0.20 USD fee per transaction. - Square will charge additional processing fees that vary by the type of sale. You can find more information on the [Square Fees and Payments FAQs](https://squareup.com/en/us/help/article/6109-fees-and-payments-faqs).  |

| --- | --- |

Learn more about [Offsite Ads](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000338367?segment=selling) and read [Etsy’s Advertising & Marketing Policy](https://www.etsy.com/ie/legal/advertising/?ref=list) for more details.

## **Etsy Payments and deposit fees**  
| **Fee name**  | **What it means**  |  
| --- | --- |  
| Payment processing fees  |  - Payment processing fees are charged on each transaction made through Etsy Payments.  
- A set rate plus a percent of the total sale price of the item. This rate and percent vary by country.   
- Taken from the item’s total sale price, including its shipping fees, and sales tax if you charge tax on the listing.  
[Learn more about payment processing fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015628847?segment=selling).  |  
| Deposit fees  |  - Only applies to sellers who accept payments through Etsy Payments. - Only applicable in certain countries. - If your deposit is above the deposit minimum but below the fee threshold, you will be charged a deposit fee.  
  
[Learn more about deposit fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360002120927?segment=selling).  
 |  
| Currency conversion  |  - If you list items in a currency other than the currency of your payment bank account, Etsy converts your funds from Etsy Payments to the currency of your payment account on your behalf. - A 2.5% fee is assessed when currency conversion is required.  |  
|  Regulatory Operating fee   |  -For sellers in the UK, France, Italy, India, Spain, Türkiye, Vietnam, and Canada -Percentage is charged on your order total, which includes item price, shipping, and gift wrap (excludes tax collected by Etsy). [Learn more about this fee and percentages.](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/1500011073202?segment=selling)  
 |
