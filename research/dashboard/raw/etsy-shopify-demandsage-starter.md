# Shopify Starter Plan (2026) – $5/mo Pricing & Features
By  [ Amandeep Singh  ](https://www.demandsage.com/author/aman/ "View all posts by Amandeep Singh") /  June 17, 2026 
The Shopify Starter plan is the most popular and affordable option for beginners, priced at just $5 per month. But is it any good? What features does it offer? Are they enough to run a full-scale business?
While the Shopify Starter plan is ideal for beginners, its features enable you to connect directly to your business’s social media handles, thereby expanding your offline e-commerce sales. 
But it is still not the ideal solution you may need. When compared against the Shopify Basic Plan, which offers a live store with a website, the Shopify Starter plan lacks several key features. Learn more about what this Shopify Start plan hits and misses in this detailed Shopify starter plan guide.
## **Shopify Starter Plan – Price, Features, and Details To Know**
Here is a snippet of what this Shopify starter plan offers for a $5 per month price in 2026:  
| Parameters  | Details  |  
| --- | --- |  
| **Price**  | Selling on social media with order management and analytics features  |  
| **Discount (New Users)**  | 3-day free, then $1/mo for 3 months   |  
| **Transaction fees**  | 5% transaction with Shopify Payments   |  
|  **Features**  | Sell products on social media, take payments, and monitor sales with analytics and reports  |  
| **Best For**  | Selling on social media with order management, and analytics features  |  
| **Customer Support**  | 24×7 chat support  |  
| **Biggest Drawback**  | No access to a fully functional online store  |  
Before you get bogged down in Shopify’s technical terms, here is a [Shopify Glossary](https://www.demandsage.com/shopify-glossary/) to simplify things for you. 
**Do you know? 💡** There are about over [6.81 million Shopify merchants](https://www.demandsage.com/shopify-statistics/) globally, and if you are planning to be a part of the Shopify community, the $5/month Shopify Starter plan can be a great way to kick things off!
### **Shopify Starter Plan – The Pros & Cons**
One of the quickest ways to start an online business has some drawbacks. Find the good and the bad about the Shopify Starter plan listed below:
**Pros**
  * Sell for just $5/month. 
  * Unlimited products and bandwidth.
  * Shopify order management and reports feature.
  * Sell in-person with Shopify POS Lite. 
  * Sell directly into messages on any social media. 
  * In-app integration tools (limited)


**Cons**
  * Does not offer a fully functional Shopify store. 
  * Limited freedom to customize and brand the website. 
  * High transaction fees at 5%.
  * No access to staff accounts. 
  * Marketing automation features are unavailable.


Shopify Starter plan has a lot of benefits for independent sellers. At the same time, the downsides are there in equal amounts, as it lacks a lot of features that a normal online storefront has. If you want to start a fully-fledged online store, it’s better to compare all the [Shopify plans](https://www.demandsage.com/shopify-pricing/) and get the right option that suits your business needs. 
## **Who Is The Shopify Starter Plan Best Suited For?**
This lightweight Shopify plan does not fall within the range of the primary Shopify Plans. It is for merchants wanting to start their e-commerce business and set up a Shopify store without a website. It is not for someone willing to build a proper store.
![Shopify Starter Plan](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%20846%20423'%3E%3C/svg%3E)
**Note:** The Shopify Starter Plan is strictly for new Shopify merchants and those currently in their trial period. 
### Is the Shopify Starter Plan Enough To Start a Store?
To start a store on social media. Yes. This plan is sufficient, but **to start a fully functional Shopify store** with product listings, themes, and other features, you **need to upgrade to the Shopify Base plan or above subscription**.
Check out more below!
## **What Makes The Shopify Starter Plan Special?**
Shopify offers a range of useful features, including POS selling access, analytics tools, and more, for just $ 5 per month.
**Have a look:**
  1. **Unlimited listings** – The Shopify Starter plan allows you to add unlimited listings for the store created on social media or messaging apps of your choice.
  2. **Basic Analytics –** with access to basic analytics features, you can keep track of your orders, sales, and the profits you are making. 
  3. **Shopify Fulfillment network access** – Shopify has a special network that can handle storing, packing, and delivering. The Shopify Starter plan also gives you access to this network.
  4. **Shopify POS Selling** – Merchants can use the Shopify POS feature for in-person or local market selling by accessing the Shopify POS Lite plan.

The Shopify Plan in 2026 is still priced at only $5 per month. The plan provides easy access to Shopify, allowing you to start selling products quickly.

### **Hidden Costs & Payment Methods for Shopify Starter Plan**
When budgeting for your first online business, it’s important to understand all potential costs. Besides the $5/month fee and 5% transaction charge with Shopify Payments, you may encounter additional expenses for payment gateways and currency conversion if selling internationally. 
Some payment providers charge setup or monthly fees, and rates may differ depending on the region. Make sure to check Shopify’s accepted payment methods and any restrictions based on your country, so you avoid surprises down the road. This transparency will help you keep your startup costs in check and plan for a profitable launch.
### **Shopify Starter Plan Features Explained**
In addition to the standard features, here is a brief overview of the key features of the Shopify Starter Plan. 
**1. Sell Anywhere**
With the starter Shopify plan, you can sell anywhere and on social media networks like Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook, TikTok, Etsy, LinkedIn, etc. 
With access to POS Lite, you can sell your products in person. Accepting payments is easy, too, with an option to accept payments using just a smartphone. 
**2. Reports And Analytics**
Though the price of the starter plan is cheap, it is definitely not cheap in terms of its reporting features. The Starter plan provides access to analytics pages, finance reports, product analytics, live view, acquisition, inventory, behavior, marketing, order, sales, retail sales, profit, and customer reports.
**3. Shopify Order Management**
One of the key features of Shopify is its easy inventory management. It offers a smooth dashboard to keep track of inventory. Stock keeping inside Shopify is one of the easiest methods for storing and categorizing your products.
**Note:** Shopify retired its free “Link in Bio” integration tool, Linkpop, in mid 2025. So, Shopify Starter users can no longer use or update their existing accounts. For similar integration, users can check out paid apps in the Shopify App Store.

After reviewing the Shopify Starter plan and its features, I noticed how it is more than just affordable for a beginner. This plan allows you to sell without much stress about the investment, and you can focus more on making your brand accessible across your social media network.
![Shopify Starter Plan Review](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201600%20914'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

## **Can You Upgrade Your Shopify Starter Plan?**
Any seller using the Shopify Starter plan can upgrade their plan to access more advanced features. This will give them the ability to create a proper online storefront and allow their customers to browse through the catalog and purchase. 
However, it is technically impossible to downgrade from the seller Shopify plans to the Starter plan, since you are running an online business with advanced features. If you need Starter plan features, you have to create a new account. 
### **Conclusion: The Shopify Starter Plan Allows Selling Online at just $5/mo**
Subscribe to a Shopify Starter plan to give your products and services an affordable platform to sell!
If your goal is to create a simple social media sales channel for your products without getting bogged down in complex e-commerce operations, you should definitely start with the Shopify Starter plan. 
But be aware that the Starter Shopify plan won’t allow you full access to Shopify features or the creation of a website store. Eventually, as your business grows and you need a full-fledged e-commerce website, you will have to upgrade your plan to Shopify Basic or above for advanced features.
