## Frequently asked questions
Does Zipsale Support Multi Quantity items
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/61b0715c63f194d4847ad8ff/61b0b362b88848fce1394a44_dropdown-line.svg)
Zipsale is designed for single quantity items, and currently does not support multiple quantity item listings 
What Platforms are part of the Auto delisting feature?
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/61b0715c63f194d4847ad8ff/61b0b362b88848fce1394a44_dropdown-line.svg)
Currently Ebay, Etsy, Depop, Poshmark, shopify, Woo, Asos and Vinted are all part of the auto delisting feature   
  
Facebook is the only marketplace where we do not autodelist for you.

Can I use Zipsale on mobile?
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/61b0715c63f194d4847ad8ff/61b0b362b88848fce1394a44_dropdown-line.svg)
Zipsale has been designed to be used on a computer. Zipsale's interface is available on mobile as well and can be used to upload images, for example. For the best experience, we would recommend performing most functionalities on PC, though.
Can I use Zipsale outside of the UK?
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/61b0715c63f194d4847ad8ff/61b0b362b88848fce1394a44_dropdown-line.svg)
Currently, Zipsale is made to be used in the UK. You can still ship your items internationally, if you choose that option when listing an item.
What is the refund policy?
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/61b0715c63f194d4847ad8ff/61b0b362b88848fce1394a44_dropdown-line.svg)
Users are eligible for refunds or account credits on a situational basis if Zipsale is negligent in any way or fails to provide users with the services they have paid for.  
Users are no longer eligible for a refund if they have already begun using their allotted items for the month. We also do not grant refunds for personal reasons.
What is the cancellation policy?
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/61b0715c63f194d4847ad8ff/61b0b362b88848fce1394a44_dropdown-line.svg)
You can cancel your subscription at any time. In order to cancel your subscription, please contact us via the orange help button at the bottom of the screen and we'll be happy to assist you. 
How do I subscribe to a plan on Zipsale?
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/61b0715c63f194d4847ad8ff/61b0b362b88848fce1394a44_dropdown-line.svg)
Please go to [**your subscription page**](https://web.zipsale.co.uk/users/settings/billing/)and pick a plan that suits you the best. You can [**upgrade**](https://zipsalehelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4414080160529),[**downgrade**](https://zipsalehelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4413282737553-How-do-I-downgrade-my-subscription-) or [**cancel your subscription**](https://zipsalehelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4413282648721-How-do-I-cancel-my-subscription-) at any time. 
The billing cycle will update in 1 month from your subscription date. If you cancel your subscription, it will remain active for the remainder of your billing cycle. After that, your payments will be cancelled.
