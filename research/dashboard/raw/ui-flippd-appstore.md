Know what you really made on every flip. Flippd tracks your inventory, sales, fees, expenses and mileage, and a free AI Coach reads your numbers and tells you what to fix first. YOUR COACH, FREE ON EVERY PLAN Each night the Coach reads your sales, inventory and costs and gives you a short list of next steps: what to relist, what is sitting too long, where margins are slipping. SOURCE SMARTER • Snap Decisions: snap an item or a whole pile and get a quick buy-or-pass read, with what you'd keep after fees and shipping • Check what similar items are listed for on eBay with Price Research • Work out fees, shipping and margin with the profit calculator before you buy INVENTORY THAT KEEPS UP • Add items in seconds with photos, or scan a barcode from a database of nearly 800 million products • Every item gets a stock number; add your own SKU if you use one • Storage locations up to three levels deep so you can find any item fast • Print labels and QR codes, including on thermal label printers • Split a pallet or lot cost across its items with Bulk Purchases • Import from a spreadsheet and export your data any time SPOT WHAT'S STUCK • Aging Inventory shows what has been listed too long • The Death Pile collects items you've written off, so you can clear or relist them • Records to fix finds duplicates, missing costs and bad dates REAL PROFIT, NOT GUESSES • Net profit on every item after cost, platform fees and shipping • Sales reports and trends that show what is working • Set Goals and follow your progress EXPENSES, MILEAGE AND TAX TIME • Snap a receipt, or a whole stack, and Flippd fills in the expense for you • Set up recurring expenses like booth rent or subscriptions • Keep proof-of-purchase receipts attached to the items they paid for • Automatic drive detection logs sourcing trips; US sellers get the IRS mileage rate and an audit-ready log • Profit and loss and Schedule C reports for tax time STOREFRONTS Share a catalog of your items with a link or QR code at a booth or market. Every sale still happens with you, in person. MAKE IT YOURS • Your own platforms with your own fees, plus your own categories and sources • Custom fields for the details you track • Hide the features you don't use • Light and dark mode, in six languages EVERY PLATFORM, ONE PLACE Track what you sell on eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Whatnot or anywhere else, and mark each sale on the platform it sold on. With Pro, connect eBay to bring your listings and sales in automatically. That sync is one-way: Flippd reads from eBay and never changes your eBay store. Items on other platforms you add yourself. Flippd is not a cross-lister: it never posts, edits or ends listings. APPLE WATCH, WIDGETS AND SIRI Check sales and track trips on Apple Watch, see your week in a widget, and use Siri to add an item, open mileage tracking or ask the Coach. One account works on iPhone, iPad and the web. FREE AND PRO Flippd is free to start, with every core feature and the AI Coach included. The free plan limits how many items, expenses and trips you can add. Pro, an optional auto-renewing subscription, adds: • Unlimited items, expenses and mileage • One-way eBay sync that brings in your listings and sales • Storefronts • Team access, so a partner or helper can work in your account • More Coach chat questions each day Terms of Use: https://getflippd.com/terms-of-use Privacy Policy: https://getflippd.com/privacy-policy
