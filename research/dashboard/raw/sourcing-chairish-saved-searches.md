#  My Saved Searches 
Finding that perfect piece can take time, so saving your searches makes it easy to pick up where you left off. Here's how to save and manage your searches on Chairish.
  * [Saving a Search](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44253725332753-My-Saved-Searches#h_01KQAPJVFA9KWY6R65DKCZDQD8)
  * [Managing Saved Searches](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44253725332753-My-Saved-Searches#h_01KQAQBDA22GX6H2N7EF0XF9T2)


## Saving a Search
* * *
To save a search, click the **Follow This Search** button on the left side of the page after performing a search. You can also apply advanced search filters by making selections from the various fields along on the left side of the page before following a search. 
![Search4.png](https://support.chairish.com/hc/article_attachments/44367103319697)
We will notify you by email when a new item in your saved search becomes available or goes on sale. You can update your [Email Settings](https://www.chairish.com/account/messaging-preference) to manage how frequently you receive saved search emails.
## Managing Saved Searches
* * *
You can manage your saved searched by clicking the **Heart** icon in the top right corner of the page and then selecting **Saved Searches**.
![Search5.png](https://support.chairish.com/hc/article_attachments/44367103320849)
Click the **Unfollow** link to the right of a saved search to unsave it.
