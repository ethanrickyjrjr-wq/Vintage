#  Why is my Sell Through Rate showing as N/A? 
![](https://secure.gravatar.com/avatar/182f425689c878447c61795cc302c647?default=https%3A%2F%2Fassets.zendesk.com%2Fhc%2Fassets%2Fdefault_avatar.png&r=g)

There's a couple reasons this could happen. Sell through rate is calculated as number of items sold in a month/number of items purchased in a month. The two most common reasons you could be seeing that your rate is "N/A" are:
  1. You haven't purchased any inventory items during the time filtered. This means the number can't be calculated
  2. You've applied filters to the sale detail
