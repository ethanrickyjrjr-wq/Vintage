## Pushover Pricing
### Individual Pricing
To use Pushover for yourself or a small group, it's just a **$4.99 USD one-time purchase** on each platform ([iPhone/iPad](https://pushover.net/clients/ios), [Android](https://pushover.net/clients/android), and [Desktop](https://pushover.net/clients/desktop)) where you want to receive Pushover notifications. You can try it out **free for 30 days** by downloading our [iPhone/iPad](https://pushover.net/clients/ios) or [Android](https://pushover.net/clients/android) apps, or by using our [Desktop](https://pushover.net/clients/desktop) app right from your browser. Multiple devices of the same platform are covered under the same one-time purchase, so you can use Pushover on your iPhone and iPad, or an Android phone and Android tablet without paying anything extra. There are **no subscription fees** for individuals using Pushover. 
[Get Pushover for **Android**](https://pushover.net/clients/android) [Get Pushover for **iPhone/iPad**](https://pushover.net/clients/ios) [Get Pushover for **Desktop**](https://pushover.net/clients/desktop)
### Team Pricing
For companies or organizations, our **[Pushover for Teams](https://pushover.net/teams)** service is **$5 USD per-user, per-month** and comes with extra features like centralized management, priority message queuing, and priority support. Each Pushover for Teams user can use Pushover on all of our supported platforms (Android, iOS, and Desktop) without any extra fees. 
[Learn about **Pushover for Teams**](https://pushover.net/teams)
### Sending Notifications
All Pushover users can send notifications to their own devices or to any other user's devices through our [API](https://pushover.net/api), [E-mail Gateway](https://support.pushover.net/i29-e-mailing-notifications-to-your-devices), or any Pushover-enabled app or service. Each user can send up to **10,000 messages per month for free**. Users needing to send larger amounts of notifications can easily [upgrade sending capacity](https://support.pushover.net/i13) through our website.
