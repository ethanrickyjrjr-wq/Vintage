# Installing the mobile application [​](https://docs.crosslist.com/knowledge-base/settings-and-preferences/mobile-application#installing-the-mobile-application)
Crosslist allows you to **create and manage listings** directly from your **mobile device**. This is especially helpful when adding new inventory, as you can take photos instantly using your device’s camera.
You can also **post** , **import** , and **delist** listings through the mobile app, making it easy to manage your workflow on the go.
Our mobile app is available for both **iOS** and **Android**. Download it using the links below:
## iOS [​](https://docs.crosslist.com/knowledge-base/settings-and-preferences/mobile-application#ios)
[Download from the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/crosslist/id6756124351)
## Android [​](https://docs.crosslist.com/knowledge-base/settings-and-preferences/mobile-application#android)
[Download from the Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.crosslist.app)
