# Vendoo Inventory Management Tools
Vendoo offers reseller inventory management, organization, filters, spreadsheets, and tools for multi-platform e-commerce resellers.
Written by Ben Martinez
Updated over 2 weeks ago
# **How to Use Vendoo’s Inventory Management Tools**
## Vendoo offers superior inventory management designed for multi-platform sellers!
### 📲 Inventory management is also available on the Vendoo mobile app!
You can view your items, status, quantities, and essential information about your inventory from the**Inventory Page.**
### Your inventory is divided into three columns based on their drafted, listed, or sold status: 
[![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/we4l9nms/2719290799/9c1872bb9497f1d784c059fcdbcb/image.png?expires=1793044800&signature=1b3bc2fe49453a903b7ef0dd0b0b2c30cb15ebff3c05a06af9a2b3347ec97fa3&req=dicmH8t3nYZWUPMW1HO4zVP7%2FV60LkjwbhkS7MxZtw2XdvMISMebyWPv%2Bpd9%0AUUlIU0SXwYUo3uQPj8M%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/we4l9nms/2719290799/9c1872bb9497f1d784c059fcdbcb/image.png?expires=1793044800&signature=1b3bc2fe49453a903b7ef0dd0b0b2c30cb15ebff3c05a06af9a2b3347ec97fa3&req=dicmH8t3nYZWUPMW1HO4zVP7%2FV60LkjwbhkS7MxZtw2XdvMISMebyWPv%2Bpd9%0AUUlIU0SXwYUo3uQPj8M%3D%0A)
Here, you can click each marketplace icon to take you directly to the live listing!
### You can view,**search, sort, and filter** your inventory in many ways. 
View by status, title, SKU, price, alphabetically, platform(s) listed, platforms not listed, date created, date modified, stalest, and even by custom labels that you assign to items!
[![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/we4l9nms/2719291478/744850f198f9d822ae880fb89e1a/image.png?expires=1793044800&signature=9851b8cd85d212bd321ffe2cc56e67b13277db6f47196b4b9f2982a06e059717&req=dicmH8t3nIVYUfMW1HO4zUCS6Tgeb3b31Dcp0c5wfF2dXEvvAt7JC2iJLjHd%0AoEXHsZnBOm%2FFnTaKczM%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/we4l9nms/2719291478/744850f198f9d822ae880fb89e1a/image.png?expires=1793044800&signature=9851b8cd85d212bd321ffe2cc56e67b13277db6f47196b4b9f2982a06e059717&req=dicmH8t3nIVYUfMW1HO4zUCS6Tgeb3b31Dcp0c5wfF2dXEvvAt7JC2iJLjHd%0AoEXHsZnBOm%2FFnTaKczM%3D%0A)
You can even add color-coded [custom labels ](https://youtu.be/i94eG9qGoLw)to your items to keep organized and get enhanced analytics. 
Vendoo even has advanced filters for enhanced inventory management:
[![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/we4l9nms/2719292113/ad90a8364b4bae638e231f696020/image.png?expires=1793044800&signature=e286f262a78d577596f4bd2df5da75498bfd767bc80f20e21dfa6361f90324a1&req=dicmH8t3n4BeWvMW1HO4zZg6BCiA7AsHiKBh62DxdkPT3zDx6QindYW%2B%2FXmr%0AaTXGWq25iPAcvi4FTYc%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/we4l9nms/2719292113/ad90a8364b4bae638e231f696020/image.png?expires=1793044800&signature=e286f262a78d577596f4bd2df5da75498bfd767bc80f20e21dfa6361f90324a1&req=dicmH8t3n4BeWvMW1HO4zZg6BCiA7AsHiKBh62DxdkPT3zDx6QindYW%2B%2FXmr%0AaTXGWq25iPAcvi4FTYc%3D%0A)
Learn more about Vendoo's advanced filters and how to use them [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dfyMxcWb4l8). 
⭐ The best part is, you no longer need to keep an inventory spreadsheet; Vendoo does this for you! Download your customizable **[full inventory report CSV](https://help.vendoo.co/en/articles/6260286-how-to-download-a-csv-file-of-my-inventory)** spreadsheet at any time. 
### Managing your inventory just got _so much easier!_ It is easy to find your items, and Vendoo keeps track of what is listed and where!
