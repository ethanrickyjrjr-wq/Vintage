Direct eBay sync
## Ditch the spreadsheet.
Every active and sold listing syncs automatically. You add only what eBay can’t see: your cost, where you bought it, and where it’s located.
  * Every cost captured. Selling fees, shipping label costs, and refunds all import on their own, so your profit reflects what you actually kept.
  * Two years of history. Flipwise backfills two full years of past sales the moment you connect, so your trends and tax numbers are ready from day one.
  * Always in sync. New sales, relists, returns, and cancellations show up on their own, in real time. No manual updates.
  * Import past data. Bring your existing inventory and sales history over from a spreadsheet or another inventory management tool, so you start with a full picture, not a blank slate.

##  Automations 
Keep the algorithm happy with automatic relisting, repricing, delayed promotions, and more.
### Ending & relisting
1h before auto-renew
Ends and relists your fixed-price items just before eBay’s auto-renew — a new listing ID, a fresh start date, and higher search placement, all on the same free listing credit.
### Repricing
by a % you set
Lowers the price by a percentage you choose on each relist, with a maximum reduction and timing you control — so aging inventory drops in price automatically until it sells.
### Delayed promotion
on a window you set
Promotes items only once they’ve gone unsold for a window you choose, so you stop paying ad fees on listings that would have sold anyway.
### Error detection
the moment it happens
Surfaces duplicate shipping charges, fee refunds, failed relists, and shipping-label cost adjustments — things that otherwise slip by silently.
### Auto-reconciliation
always accurate
Detects returns, cancellations, and refunds and applies them across your records, so your numbers always stay right.
### Sale & location alerts
the moment it sells
Tells you the instant something sells — and exactly where it’s stored — so you can find it and get it shipped fast.

Tax-ready finances
## Ready for tax season.
Your tax report generates automatically and updates in real time all year, so the moment the 1099-K lands, it’s ready to hand off — not a weekend of panic.
  * Tax reporting Always up to date and ready in one click. Supports cash or accrual and lays your numbers out to make filing with software like TurboTax (or handing off to your accountant) easy, with no messy eBay exports to wrangle.
  * Guided data checks Built-in helpers flag any product missing the data taxes need and walk you through adding it, so your report is complete and accurate before you file.
  * Expense ledger Log expenses and mileage into Schedule C categories to track your deductions and keep more of what you earn.
  * Income statement A real profit-and-loss across every marketplace and channel, for any date range, so you always know exactly where you stand.
