SC 1.4.3 (Level AA): text contrast at least 4.5:1; large-scale text at least 3:1; large-scale = at least 18 point, or 14 point bold. Ratios are thresholds (4.499:1 fails). Exceptions: inactive UI, pure decoration, invisible text, logotypes.
