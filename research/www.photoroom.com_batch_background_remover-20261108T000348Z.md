## Frequently asked questions
#### How can I remove backgrounds from multiple images at once?
Bulk background removal tools apply the same process to an entire set of images instead of requiring each file to be edited manually. This helps teams prepare large catalogs quickly and keeps cutouts consistent across SKUs. Photoroom removes backgrounds in bulk using AI that detects the product and keeps edges clean. You upload your images, select background removal and export all cutouts at once.
#### Does removing backgrounds in bulk affect image quality?
Some tools can leave rough edges, miss small details or remove parts of the product when processing batches. Quality depends on how accurately the product is detected. Photoroom uses AI trained specifically for product photography to isolate the subject and keep edges stable. Each file receives consistent treatment so your catalog maintains clear, reliable visuals.
#### Can I use background-removed images for marketplaces?
Yes. Many marketplaces require clean or neutral backgrounds, and bulk background removal helps speed up preparation for large product sets. Photoroom produces clean cutouts that work well for marketplace listings, color swaps or PDP layouts. You can also combine background removal with other bulk tools to complete marketplace-ready workflows.
#### What types of product photos work well with bulk background removal?
Background removal works best when the product is clearly visible and not blending with the background. Items like apparel, accessories, home goods, packaged products, beauty items, electronics and shoes typically produce strong results. Photoroom processes a wide range of product photos in batch and adapts to different shapes and textures to maintain accurate cutouts across your catalog.
#### Can I remove backgrounds from thousands of images in one workflow?
Large product catalogs often require frequent background cleanup, especially during seasonal updates or major product launches. Photoroom supports high-volume background removal and applies the same process across the entire batch. You can upload large sets and get clean results without splitting files or repeating steps.
