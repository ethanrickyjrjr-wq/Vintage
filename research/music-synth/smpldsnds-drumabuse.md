# Drum Abuse Vol.3: LinnDrum & 909 Birth
[](https://github.com/smpldsnds/drum-abuse-vol3#drum-abuse-vol3-linndrum--909-birth)
Hip-hop and house foundations: LinnDrum II, TR-909, DMX wave
Single-shot drum samples from **38 drum machines** released 1982–1983, extracted from the [Synth Abuse Drum Machine Sample Collection](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ay-U9eYKmGA).
  * **741 MB** (9394 samples, `.wav`, loops excluded)
  * 25 instrument categories: Agogo, Bongo, Cabasa, Clap, Clave, Click, Conga, Cowbell, Crash, Cymbal, FX, Guiro, Hi-Hat Closed, Hi-Hat Open, Kick, Perc, Ride, Rim, Shaker, Snare, Tambourine, Timbale, Tom, Whistle, Wood Block
  * Source: [Drive download](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1eacxX1GtQWqJP3dmZYbYvEPuGtxMtLff/view)
  * Thanks to <https://www.youtube.com/@synthabuse>


## Files in this repo
[](https://github.com/smpldsnds/drum-abuse-vol3#files-in-this-repo)
  * `pack.json` — pack metadata
  * `index.json` — array of machine summaries
  * `instruments/<slug>.json` — flat list of samples per instrument category, across all machines
  * `machines/<machine_id>.json` — per-machine detail (sample sets, instruments, sample lists)
  * `samples/<machine_id>/...` — the `.wav` files and cover images
