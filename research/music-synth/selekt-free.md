# Free samples for commercial music — royalty-free and cleared
Looking for free cleared samples you can actually release? Every sample on Selekt is free of royalties and clearance — CC0, public domain, and CC-BY — so you owe nothing per use and need no licensing budget, and you can **browse and stream all 100,000+ free with no signup**. We are honest about what “free” covers: free to listen, free of royalties and clearance, free tools, and free CC0 packs, with a $5/mo plan that only unlocks downloading — never the licensing, which is always yours. And because every file is sourced from a verified license, screened, and signed, you carry the proof of clearance with each one. Built for producers and composers releasing music commercially.

**Free cleared samples** means two things at once: free to browse, stream, and use without royalties or per-use fees, and [cleared](https://selektaudio.com/free-samples#cleared) — sourced from a verified open license and screened against a 150M+ recording database, with a signed certificate behind every file. Exactly what “cleared” covers is defined in full [below](https://selektaudio.com/free-samples#cleared).
## What “free” means here — spelled out
“Free” gets stretched a lot, so here is exactly what it means on Selekt, with no fine print waiting to surprise you:
**Free to browse and listen.** Stream the entire catalog — over 100,000 loops, one-shots, and stems — with no account and no paywall on previews.
**Free of royalties and clearance.** The licensing itself is free: CC0 and public-domain audio carries no royalties, no per-use fees, and no clearance budget. Use it in commercial work and you owe nothing for the music.
**Free of attribution, mostly.** CC0 and public-domain samples need no credit at all. CC-BY samples simply ask for a credit (still no royalty), and the certificate tells you exactly how.
**Free tools and packs.** The producer tools — [free online mastering](https://selektaudio.com/tools/mastering) (no upload, no signup, runs right in your browser), [key & BPM detection](https://selektaudio.com/tools/key-detector), [stem separator](https://selektaudio.com/tools/stem-separator), and [copyright check](https://selektaudio.com/tools/copyright-check) — are free with no signup, and the [Drum Kit](https://selektaudio.com/tools/drum-kit) and [Sound Lab](https://selektaudio.com/sound-lab) ship real CC0 / public-domain packs at no charge.
**Free to start downloading.** A free trial lets you download and try it for real. Early Access ($5/mo) then includes 50 downloads a month. We are upfront about that line: the $5 is for access to the catalog and the AI tools — the samples are always royalty-free.
## How far we go to clear every sample — origin-verified, fingerprint-screened, certified
We start at the source. Every sample comes from a **verified open license** — CC0, public domain, and CC-BY audio pulled from named archives like the Library of Congress / Citizen DJ, the Internet Archive, Freesound, and Musopen — where the license is confirmed at the origin archive itself, not asserted by an anonymous uploader. That is the first signal, and it is where most sample libraries stop.
We go further. Each file is then **screened against 150M+ commercial recordings** by ACRCloud audio fingerprinting, and anything that matches a known commercial release is rejected before it ever reaches the catalog. That gives every [cleared](https://selektaudio.com/free-samples#cleared) sample **two independent signals** working together: free at the source, and no match to a known commercial release. Each download also ships with a **signed certificate** logging the source, the license, and the screening result, independently verifiable — concrete evidence you can hand to a distributor, sync library, or label.
This is the most precaution you will find on a free sample, and we are confident saying so. Most libraries confirm a license and stop there; they do not fingerprint-screen, and many hand the same loop to thousands of producers — so the same files carry more exposure elsewhere, not less. We do both signals and sign the result on purpose, because going further is the responsible thing to do. It is honest, responsible confidence rather than a legal guarantee — no honest process can promise the impossible, so we built the most thorough one there is. That, backed by a certificate you can verify yourself, is the strongest footing available and your best bet for releasing with confidence. ## Every catalog sound has a documented clearance path.
Catalog candidates come from named CC0, public-domain, and CC-BY sources. Open uploads and other archive material are fingerprint-screened against more than 150 million commercial recordings; a match means the file is withheld. Qualifying public-domain recordings instead use documented source authority and publication-date eligibility, or source-curator attestation. The specific recording matters, not just the age of its song.
Each eligible download includes a signed certificate recording the named source, rights basis, clearance-path evidence, required attribution, and a tamper-evident audit chain. That gives creators and collaborators evidence they can inspect independently instead of a label that merely says “royalty-free.” It reduces uncertainty; it is not a legal guarantee.
