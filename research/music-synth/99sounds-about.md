#  About Us 
99Sounds is an independent sound design label and a free sound effects repository that was launched in 2014 by Tomislav Zlatić, the founder of Bedroom Producers Blog.
## Our Mission
Our mission is to provide sound effects and sample libraries of commercial quality for free.
Our sound libraries and plugins will always be 100% royalty-free and offered as such for both commercial and non-commercial use.
99Sounds is a collaboration and promotional space for talented sound designers worldwide, allowing them to share their work with the audio community.
Since the label launched in 2014, we have featured free sounds from dozens of talented creators. 99Sounds hosts a variety of free sounds, from [cinematic sound effects](https://99sounds.org/cinematic-sound-effects/) and [drum samples](https://99sounds.org/drum-samples/) to field recordings and [rain sounds](https://99sounds.org/rain-and-thunder/).
[![Tomislav Zlatic, 99Sounds and Bedroom Producers Blog founder.](https://99sounds.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/tomislav-zlatic-1.jpg)](https://99sounds.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/tomislav-zlatic-1.jpg)Tomislav Zlatić, 99Sounds and Bedroom Producers Blog founder.
The [Releases](https://99sounds.org/releases/) page showcases the entire collection of free sound effects available for download on the 99Sounds website. The page is regularly updated with new releases, so it’s worth checking back frequently.
All the sounds hosted on 99Sounds are royalty-free, which means you can use them in your music and video production projects, game development, and other creative endeavors. View full details on our [License page](https://99sounds.org/license/).
We released some amazing feedback from our visitors over the years, including messages of support from [Darude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darude) and Torley.
  * “Went through the sounds, and I think I’ll find some use for at least some of them! Very generous of you to share!” **Ville Virtanen, Darude**
  * ‘Wow! This is looking really promising. I recently heard of 99Sounds via BPB, and I’ve just downloaded “Rain And Thunder” to use in some granular synthesis experiments.” **Torley**


We also develop [VST plugins](https://99sounds.org/vst-plugins/). 99Sounds has released three plugins so far, with more plugins coming soon.
Subscribe to our mailing list for updates when we release a new sound library or plugin.
Our blog features sound design tutorials and our curated selection of the best VST plugins and music production tools.
If you’re a talented sound artist who wants to release a project on 99Sounds or have any inquiries, don’t hesitate to [contact the 99Sounds team](https://99sounds.org/contact/).

### A Bit About Me
Hey there, I’m Tomislav. I’ve always been into cinematic sound design, especially crafting [SFX](https://99sounds.org/what-is-sfx/) that sounds larger than life.
With a degree in engineering from Belgrade University, I’ve mixed a bit of a tech background and a lot of passion for synthesis and music to carve out my sound design techniques.
I also write about music production software on my main website, Bedroom Producers Blog. I like to keep my articles informative and with a sprinkle of fun.
[![That is me recording rain sounds in a park.](https://99sounds.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/rain-sounds-recording.jpg)](https://99sounds.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/rain-sounds-recording.jpg)That is me recording rain sounds in a park.
I also produce music and do a lot of field recording. Most of the sounds I record end up in free sound libraries here on 99Sounds.
Off the clock, you might catch me shooting hoops on a local basketball court, playing tennis, or whipping up a mean smashburger at home.
### Learning the Ropes
While I’m proud of my Engineering degree from Belgrade University, I picked up a big chunk of what I know about sound design on my own.
There’s much to be said for diving in headfirst and learning as you go!
