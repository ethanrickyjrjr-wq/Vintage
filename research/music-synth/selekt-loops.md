## Loops you can actually flip
Most loop packs get bought thousands of times — the same eight bars end up in a thousand beats. These are different: they are cleared parts meant to be **taken apart and recombined** , not played straight. Chop a guitar loop into one-shots, layer a percussion loop under your own kit, re-sing a vocal phrase — the certificate follows the result, and the arrangement is yours alone. ## Loops, answered Are these loops really free to use?
    Yes. Every loop here is CC0, public domain, or CC-BY. All three permit commercial reuse without royalties; CC-BY asks for creator credit, and the certificate tells you exactly what attribution is required. Each download ships with a certificate naming the source so you have the receipt on file. How are loops different from one-shots?
    A loop is a phrase that repeats cleanly — a drum break, a bassline, a chord progression — so you can drop it in and build around it. A one-shot is a single hit or note. These pages are loops only; one-shots live in the Sound Lab and Drum Kit tools. Can I chop, pitch, and rearrange these?
    That is the whole idea. Because the source is cleared, you can chop a loop into one-shots, pitch it, time-stretch it, and combine it with your own parts or other catalog loops — and the certificate still covers the result. Nobody else is starting from this exact arrangement. Where do these loops come from?
    They are sourced from cleared CC0, public-domain, and CC-BY creators and archives, then screened against a copyright database and enriched (BPM, key, tags) before they reach a bucket. We assigned every loop its bucket ourselves, so a card lands here because it belongs — not because of a fuzzy tag match.
Want more than loops? Build a kit in the [Sound Lab](https://selektaudio.com/sound-lab), split your own track with the [stem separator](https://selektaudio.com/tools/stem-separator), or browse [the whole catalog](https://selektaudio.com/explore).
*** What “cleared” means.** Every sample starts from material that's already free to use:
  * **Public domain** — copyright has expired or never applied, so the work belongs to everyone.
  * **CC0** — the creator waived all rights, effectively placing the work in the public domain.
  * **CC-BY** — the creator allows commercial use as long as you credit them (your certificate shows who and how).


Every catalog sound must then pass the clearance screen appropriate to its source. Open uploads and other archive material are checked by audio fingerprinting before admission. Qualifying public-domain collections are verified through source authority and the recording's documented first-publication-date eligibility, because a documented public-domain recording does not need to be treated like an unknown upload. The certificate records the source, license, and screening path used. That is strong, verifiable evidence — **not a legal guarantee** ; you remain responsible for confirming rights before commercial release. See our [Terms](https://selektaudio.com/terms).

How copyright screening works
## Every catalog sound has a documented clearance path.
Catalog candidates come from named CC0, public-domain, and CC-BY sources. Open uploads and other archive material are fingerprint-screened against more than 150 million commercial recordings; a match means the file is withheld. Qualifying public-domain recordings instead use documented source authority and publication-date eligibility, or source-curator attestation. The specific recording matters, not just the age of its song.
Each eligible download includes a signed certificate recording the named source, rights basis, clearance-path evidence, required attribution, and a tamper-evident audit chain. That gives creators and collaborators evidence they can inspect independently instead of a label that merely says “royalty-free.” It reduces uncertainty; it is not a legal guarantee.

### What Selekt is
Selekt is a library of **cleared** sound — real, finished recordings, every one free to use — plus the tools to shape it into yours. Other sites’ sample packs hand everyone the same shortlist of “best” picks — so everyone’s working from the same sounds. Selekt opens the whole cleared crate, so you dig out the gems nobody else is flipping.
### What to expect
Expect range: a two-note stab, a long loop, a field recording, a strange little texture. These are finished recordings — we just don’t filter them down to a highlight reel. Like digging through crates of records, the win is finding the one piece that clicks for _your_ track.
### Why it works
Cleared sound plus tools to reshape it means a part nobody else is using. Everything is CC0 or public domain, screened for copyright, and certified per download — safe for commercial work.
