##  [](/help "Back to help") What is ShopGoodwill.com about? 
**ShopGoodwill.com** is the e-commerce platform for Goodwill organizations across the United States and Canada. Established in 1999, it was the first online auction site created, owned, and operated by a nonprofit organization. Today, more than 135 Goodwill organizations list and auction unique items on the site, including art, antiques, collectibles, apparel, jewelry, electronics, and more. 
  

Revenue generated through ShopGoodwill.com directly supports Goodwill’s mission to enhance people’s dignity and quality of life through learning and the power of work. On average, 85 cents of every dollar spent goes back to local Goodwill organizations and their community programs, including job placement services, vocational training, educational advancement, financial literacy, childcare, and self-advocacy programs. Many of these services support individuals facing barriers to employment.
  

Since its inception, purchases made on ShopGoodwill.com have supported more than 7.5 million individuals in finding employment. When you shop on ShopGoodwill.com, your purchases help change lives and strengthen communities. 
  

ShopGoodwill.com was created by and continues to be owned and operated by Goodwill of Orange County, California (https://www.ocgoodwill.org).
