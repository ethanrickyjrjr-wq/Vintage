#  What does ‘buyer’s premium’ mean? 
  * 1 year ago
  * Updated


Auction companies add a buyer’s premium to the winning bid to offset auction costs. At MaxSold, the buyer’s premium is 18% (see section 7 of the [MaxSold Terms and Conditions](https://maxsold.com/terms-and-conditions)).
This premium is also a taxable item, which is why the buyer's premium is added to the hammer price, and then sale tax is applied.
