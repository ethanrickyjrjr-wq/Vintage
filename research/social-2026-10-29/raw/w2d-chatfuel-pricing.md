## Frequently asked questions
How many contacts can I store?
You can keep your customer base in Chatfuel and manage contacts from one workspace.
Can my team use the inbox?
Yes. Your team can handle live conversations and follow up when automation needs a human.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. You can cancel a paid plan at any time from your billing settings.
Are Meta’s WhatsApp fees included?
Meta charges for WhatsApp messages separately from your plan. Chatfuel pays Meta for you, and the fees come out of the same wallet as your plan’s credits, so you get one bill. See [WhatsApp pricing](https://chatfuel.com/whatsapp-pricing) for what Meta charges in 2026.
What uses credits?
Three things are metered: AI replies, WhatsApp templates and lead events reported to Meta. Inbox, contacts, bookings, flows and every channel are part of every plan.
Do unused credits roll over?
No. Your plan tops the balance up on each renewal, and what you didn't spend doesn't carry over. Pick the plan by what you'll actually use.
Can I connect integrations?
You can connect tools like Google Sheets, Zapier, Stripe and Chatfuel API.
Do I need technical skills?
No. You can launch with templates and build flows visually.
How fast can I launch?
Most teams can publish a first automation in minutes and improve it over time.
How does annual billing work?
Annual billing lowers the monthly equivalent price and is paid for the year.
What happens when my trial ends?
Your trial comes with a starting balance to test the bot. When it ends, pick a plan here. Your bot and its settings stay.
What if I run out of credits?
Buy a credit pack or turn on auto-reload, and the bot keeps running. Bigger packs cost less per credit, up to 30% off. Need more every month? Move up a plan or contact us and we will help you choose the right setup.
Every Chatfuel plan includes AI-powered chatbots, a shared team inbox, CRM, integrations with tools like Google Sheets and Zapier, and official WhatsApp API access. Start with a free trial and launch your first automation in minutes.
