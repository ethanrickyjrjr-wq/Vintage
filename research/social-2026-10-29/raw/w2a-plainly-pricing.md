## FAQs about pricing
### Is Plainly compatible with After Effects?
Yes, Plainly is fully compatible with After Effects and is built on an infrastructure that leverages After Effects. You create video templates in After Effects, then upload them to Plainly for easy video versioning and personalization. We also have an After Effects plugin that connects After Effects directly with Plainly.
### Do I need to know After Effects to use Plainly?
Technically, no. You don’t need to be an expert in After Effects to use Plainly. However, you or someone on your team will need to create the video template in After Effects first. Once the template is ready, you can upload it to Plainly and easily generate variations from there.
### How are you counting video minutes?
Each second you export will be counted towards your monthly limit. We’re not rounding to a minute, and we’re not looking at rendering time, only the duration of your exported videos.
### What's video retention and cloud storage space?
Video retention refers to the period your rendered videos are stored on our servers before they are automatically deleted. Cloud storage space is the amount of online storage allocated to your account for your projects. If you reach your storage limit, you may need to delete older projects or upgrade your plan.
### What's premium support?
With any paid plan you will receive email support which is lightning-fast. With higher plans, you will be able to access training sessions from our team and support through Slack or online meetings.
### Can you create custom templates for me?
Yes, we can. Our creative team can help you with your custom templates.
### Can you create integrations for me?
Plainly already has over 12 native integrations to many of the most popular tools. On top, you can create custom integrations using our API. However, if you need a custom workflow or a new integration, our technical team will be happy to assist.
### What does layer scripting do?
Layer scripting allows you to go beyond just changing values of the dynamic layers and set up conditional rendering, time based rules or change properties of layers such as font, duration, start time and more. Read more about scripting in Plainly [here.](https://Layer%20scripting%20allows%20you%20to%20go%20beyond%20just%20changing%20values%20of%20the%20dynamic%20layers%20and%20set%20up%20conditional%20rendering,%20time%20based%20rules%20or%20change%20properties%20of%20layers%20such%20as%20font,%20duration,%20start%20time%20and%20more.%20Read%20more%20about%20scripting%20in%C2%A0Plainly%20here.)
### How does the Unlimited plan work?
The Unlimited plan provides unlimited video rendering minutes per month and unlimited cloud storage. However, at the base Unlimited plan, you can only render 2 videos at the same time. If you need more concurrent renders, you can easily upgrade your plan to accommodate additional rendering capacity.
### Do I pay extra for each team member?
No, you can invite as many team members as you want, and you won’t be charged anything extra.
### What are dedicated rendering nodes?
This is a dedicated rendering node that’s not shared with any other user. You will still use our shared rendering infrastructure, but your renders will be processed first on your dedicated node.
### Still have a question?
[Contact our support team!](https://www.plainlyvideos.com/contact) We’ll be happy to help you.
