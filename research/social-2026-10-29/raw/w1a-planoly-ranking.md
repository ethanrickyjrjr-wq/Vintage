# How Instagram Ranking Works: Everything You Need to Know

> Understanding Instagram's ranking system is crucial for creators looking to maximize their reach and engagement. Here's what you need to know.

By Codi Foster · Published 2025-01-31 · Updated 2025-02-10

Source: https://www.planoly.com/blog/how-instagram-ranking-works

![How Instagram Ranking Works: Everything You Need to Know](https://planoly-prod.imgix.net/website/blog/assets/c5add87c5750f96ab2050c2373df38a794d13c30e5f3edee950b64b5dde3d73d.png)

Head of Instagram, Adam Mosseri, has been breaking down how ranking works on the platform in a [short video series.](https://www.instagram.com/p/DFK_lARJBVC/) Understanding Instagram’s ranking system is crucial for creators looking to maximize their reach and engagement. Here’s what you need to know:

### **Two Types of Ranking**

Instagram categorizes content reach into two main types:

1. **Connected Reach** – Content shown to accounts that already follow you.
2. **Recommendations (Unconnected Reach)** – Content surfaced to accounts that don’t follow you yet.

### **The Three Key Metrics for Ranking**

Regardless of whether your content is reaching your followers or new audiences, Instagram’s ranking algorithm prioritizes three essential engagement metrics:

- **View Rate** – How long someone watches your video.
- **Likes** – How much engagement your content receives (more crucial for connected reach).
- **Sends** – How likely users are to send your video to others (more crucial for unconnected reach).

The **most important metric for both types of ranking is average watch time**—the longer people stay engaged, the better your content will perform.

### **Boosting Your Reach with Trial Reels**

If you’re worried about over-posting or fatiguing your followers, Instagram’s [Trial Reels](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels?locale=en_US) feature allows you to bypass your connected audience and target new viewers directly, making it a powerful tool for growth.

### **How to Get Your Content Recommended**

For your content to be eligible for Instagram’s recommendation system, it must meet these five criteria:

1. No watermarks (avoid reposting TikTok content with visible logos)
2. Add audio (especially for photos and carousel posts)
3. Videos must be **3 minutes or less**
4. Ensure your content is **original**
5. Maintain **good account status** (Check by going to Profile > Account Status)

### **How Instagram Ranks Stories**

Unlike feed posts and Reels, Stories are primarily designed to reach your existing followers. They are ranked based on:

- How likely people are to **like or engage** with your story
- How likely someone is to **reply or share** your story

Stories are best used to engage your most passionate audience with behind-the-scenes content, timely updates, and direct interactions.
