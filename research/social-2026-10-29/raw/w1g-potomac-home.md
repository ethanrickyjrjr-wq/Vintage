## Our Story & Stewardship
The Potomac Collective was born out of a deep love for heirloom design and a desire to thoughtfully rehome fine furnishings from one generation to the next. As a family business, we blend expertise in design, history, and hospitality — offering clients not just beautiful pieces, but meaningful transitions. Our work is grounded in care, discretion, and preservation, ensuring each item continues its story in a home that truly cherishes it.
[ Learn More About Us ](https://www.potomaccollective.com/about)
  *   *   *   *   *   *   *   *   *   *   *   *   *   *   *   *   * 

At the Potomac Collective, we offer more than beautiful pieces — we provide thoughtful transitions and personal guidance. Whether you’re looking to rehome heirloom furnishings or [source something timeless](https://www.potomaccollective.com/sourcingrequest) and storied for your space, our services are rooted in trust, discretion, and care. Every client, every piece, and every project is approached with the same commitment to preservation and beauty.
