## 54: Reselling Tips and Tricks – and Live Sales – with Hilary Myers
“I love showing people my adventures and encouraging them to be passionate about traveling and thrifting.”
Hilary Myers is from Ontario, Canada and runs an online vintage business on Instagram called @whiteblushhome. She is passionate about décor, thrifting, and finding that epic piece! Hilary loves traveling and sourcing, finding pieces for her home and for resale. She is always reading books about glass and vintage mid-century décor, and loves shinning a spotlight on Canadian thrifting and antiques. 
**SHOW HIGHLIGHTS & TAKEAWAYS:**  

  * The thrifting scene in Canada.  

  * Tips for selling vintage home décor on Etsy and Instagram.
  * The difference between boho and mid-century collectibles.
  * Combining different home décor styles.
  * Her tips for creating social reels that keep your audience interested.
  * Her live sales on Instagram.
  * How she gained a large social following over the last year.
