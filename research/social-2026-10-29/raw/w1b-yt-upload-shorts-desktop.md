# Upload YouTube Shorts
[YouTube Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070) is a way for anyone to turn an idea into a chance to connect with new audiences anywhere in the world. Using YouTube’s Shorts creation tools, you can upload a short-form vertical video as a Short.

## Upload Shorts
To upload a Short from a computer:
  1. Sign in to [YouTube Studio](https://studio.youtube.com/).
  2. In the top-right corner, click **CREATE** ![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/hu-bHEKwJTWiRE9gp-DPEXaLyg-EOasyWJDPdlWn8gmyoWC-7dTOTnM9CC8GkmZBK5Z4=w48-h48) ![and then](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/_KZw6iFLPOiYyWMg4YA4SL-ec-Eitj91jD29eyhArvOWBOlmDYcgi9Dj973hpW0oDEA=w48-h48) **Upload videos** ![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/RFqxEd98QuIJRHAF3U8FNxuitsXFa4CrJO3MqZvoD11huuKlkNOL48mPZ-M5ed1FmQ=w48-h48).
  3. Select a short-video file: 
     * Up to 3 minutes.
     * With a square or vertical [aspect ratio](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6375112).


You can choose up to 15 short videos at a time, but be sure to click **EDIT DRAFT** to complete your video details before publishing. [Learn more about video settings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57407#details).
Looking for pointers before uploading your first Short? Check out [Shorts upload tips](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12921536).
**Note:** The default video privacy setting for creators aged 13–17 is **private**. If you’re 18 or older, your default video privacy setting is set to **public**.
If you upload a short video you’ve created elsewhere, make sure that any copyright-protected material you’ve used is approved for your use on YouTube. Using copyright-protected material could lead to you getting a [Content ID claim](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6013276). Also if a copyright owner, artist’s label, or distributor sends us a valid and complete copyright takedown notice against your short video, it may be removed, and you could get a [copyright strike](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2814000).
