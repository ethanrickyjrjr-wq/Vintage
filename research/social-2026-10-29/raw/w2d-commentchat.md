## How CommentChat Works
![](https://try.commentsold.com/wp-content/plugins/a3-lazy-load/assets/images/lazy_placeholder.gif)
#### Custom Trigger Words
Your followers don’t need to say exactly what you want to hear. Set your own trigger words—”shop,” “link,” “order,” “details,” “DM,” whatever signals buying intent. CommentChat listens for them and replies instantly with your message. No two online businesses are alike; your automation shouldn’t be either.
#### Your Custom Messages
The automation works in  _your_ voice. Write the comment reply and the DM message once, customize them for different posts or seasons, and let CommentChat handle the heavy lifting. Say what matters to your brand—personality and all.
![](https://try.commentsold.com/wp-content/plugins/a3-lazy-load/assets/images/lazy_placeholder.gif)
![](https://try.commentsold.com/wp-content/plugins/a3-lazy-load/assets/images/lazy_placeholder.gif)
#### Direct Them Where It Counts
Every auto-sent DM includes a custom link—to your shop, a specific product, a waitlist, a booking page, or wherever the buyer needs to go. You control the destination. They get straight there.

## Frequently Asked Questions
Won't my followers think I'm spamming them? 
No. CommentChat replies  _only_ to people who comment with your trigger words—they’ve already shown buying intent. The reply appears as if you wrote it, because your voice is in the message. Meta’s own comment-automation tools show this isn’t flagged as spam when used respectfully.
Can I customize the replies and DM messages? 
Completely. You write both the comment reply and the DM. Change them per post, per season, or per product if you want. It’s all in your words, your tone, your brand.
Does CommentChat work on every platform? 
CommentChat works on Instagram and Facebook right now. TikTok support is coming soon. We’re expanding based on where our sellers are building community.
What happens to comments that don't match my trigger words? 
CommentChat only auto-replies to comments with your trigger words. Every other comment stays in your inbox for you to handle if you want to. You’re always in control.
