##  By Appointment 
### A More Personal Way to Shop
We're returning to a more intentional rhythm at Vestige. For now, the shop will be available by private shopping appointment, allowing you the time and space to explore the collection at your own pace.
Appointments are available Monday–Saturday, 12PM–7PM, and Sunday, 10AM–3PM, and can be booked through the **Private Shopping** section of our website.
While our regular walk-in hours take a pause, this isn't a goodbye to gathering. We're making room for what's next, with more events and opportunities to shop with us in person outside of appointments beginning next month.
Thank you for continuing to make space for this little shop and everything we're building. We look forward to welcoming you in, one visit at a time.
