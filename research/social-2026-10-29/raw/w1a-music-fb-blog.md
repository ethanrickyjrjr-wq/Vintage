# Adding Music to Your Reels
Captivate your audience by adding music to your reels to make them more entertaining.
![](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=1649280108571852&version=1753895524)
Music is a great way to complement a reel, creating an even more engaging way to connect with your community.
**Options for including music in reels**
You have several options for how to include music in reels , including using music that is in the public domain, available in Meta’s Sound Collection or securing licensed music directly from rights holders. Here are some details on each of these for you to consider:
  * **Using music in the**[** _public domain_**](https://www.facebook.com/help/697921770376427). . While it may not have as wide of a selection of popular music, it is a free and relatively frictionless path to injecting music into your videos.
  * **Using Meta’s**[** _Sound Collection_**](https://www.facebook.com/sound/collection/). This is an audio library of sound effects and royalty-free music that you can download for your videos. In the collection, you'll find over 14,000 music tracks from established composers and songwriters and over 1,500 sound effects. You can use Sound Collection to add free music and sounds to the videos that you create and upload to Facebook and Instagram. You can add, mix and edit your downloaded tracks into the videos you create.
  * **Using music approved via**[** _Rights Manager_**](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/2015218438745640), Meta’s copyright management tool helps rights holders manage and protect their content. If you want to utilize music not in the public domain or in Sound Collection, you should secure the appropriate licenses directly from the applicable rights holders for the applicable territories. In this process, you should ask the rights holders to place all Pages and accounts you will be posting and streaming on the allow list in their Rights Manager accounts to avoid potential disruptions to your video(s). Please note that rights holders make the ultimate decision on whether to add your Pages and accounts to their allow list.
