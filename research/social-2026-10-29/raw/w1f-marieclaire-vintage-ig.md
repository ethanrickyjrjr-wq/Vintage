After more than a year of pretty much never leaving the house, I’ve developed a  _slight_ [online shopping addiction](https://www.marieclaire.com/fashion/g1898/best-online-shopping/) (this is how I feel joy, OK?!), which has led me to discover the 21st century [vintage holy land](https://www.marieclaire.com/fashion/g26859070/best-online-thrift-stores/): Instagram. Highly-curated vintage Instagram accounts, many created as a result of the COVID-19 pandemic, have paved a way for vintage lovers to shop small and sustainable businesses without having to spend hours searching on Google or risking health and safety protocols shopping IRL. Plus, weekly drops of new items released on the accounts are like scoring a concert ticket to your favorite band knowing that you nabbed a one-of-a-kind trinket. To help spread the joy, I've rounded up some of the best vintage decor accounts, below, so you can find the perfect home accessory without leaving your couch. You're welcome in advance!
## Rosemary Home
> [A post shared by Rosemary Home (@shoprosemaryhome)](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_1214477157585289259&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fp%2FCMx17XYjOQo%2F&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com)
New York City-based [Rosemary Home](https://www.shoprosemaryhome.com/) is a curation of vintage glamour. With a collection of homewares that will, at the very least, brighten up your IG feed, this account will convince you to buy [new earrings](https://www.marieclaire.com/fashion/best-jewelry-brands/) just to stow them away in these opulent catchalls.
**Price range: as low as $36**
[SHOP IT](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_7123471922316617493&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fshoprosemaryhome&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com)
## Things PDX
> [A post shared by THINGS (@things.pdx)](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_1418605751250070478&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fp%2FCM5KSAQBPyP%2F&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com)
Krystyna Ninh Wood founded Portland-based [Things PDX](https://thingspdx.com/) and recently quit her job to pursue her business full time. The shop has the perfect quirky-to-kitschy ratio: glass bok choy, kitchenwares, and the occasional lip lamp. Find me here most Fridays anxiously awaiting the brand's next drop.
**Price range: as low as $20**
[SHOP IT](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_1217286855759173012&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fthings.pdx&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com)
## Abigail Bell Vintage
> [A post shared by Abigail Bell Vintage (@abigailbellvintage)](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_1308622792271868518&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fp%2FCFKezaIDHeT%2F&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com)
Created by Abby Campbell, [Abigail Bell Vintage](https://abigailbellvintage.com/) is the height of interior inspiration for the 2023 cool-girl. The light pink tones and '80s beach vibes will convince you to purchase a mid-century mushroom lamp ASAP. Run, don't walk to score a coveted item from this self-proclaimed "Brooklyn halfway house for vintage, secondhand, and contemporary pieces."
**Price range: from $95 to $795**
[SHOP IT](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_4438091254571248978&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fabigailbellvintage%2F&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com)
## Dream Wares
> [A post shared by LA-based vintage + pre-loved (@dream.wares)](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_4830241724230126183&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fp%2FCMihBmzpTxP%2F&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com)
[Dream Wares](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_1219154514146912237&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fdream.wares&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com) is a Los Angeles-based, BIPOC-owned shop that  _will_ transport you to a vintage wonderland. This dreamscape focuses on sustainability through upcycled goods and a dash of vintage fashion accessories. Warm up your fingers and try to be the fastest to secure a piece from its next drop.
**Price range: as low as $12**
[SHOP IT](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_7015229681407863708&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fdream.wares&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com)
## Ella Jane Vintage
> [A post shared by Ella Jane Vintage (@ellajanevintage)](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_1424977236136032966&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fp%2FCLVTvAHg49Y%2F&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com)
[Ella Jane Vintage](https://www.ellajanevintage.com/) sells the essentials: "glass, ash, and stash." Founded by best friends Shannon Wissler and Shay Bond, the brand has the perfect collection of gauntlets and glasswares to impress any party guest. Bonus: They ship worldwide!
**Price range: $16 and up**
[SHOP IT](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_8187128812903363475&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fellajanevintage%2F&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com)
## Freska Living
> [A post shared by Freska Living (@freska.living)](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_1920735682636046787&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fp%2FCMYxMExge8_%2F&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com)
If you miss the feeling of stepping into an antique store, [Freska Living](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_5092544397728899481&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Ffreska.living%2F&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com) is a must-follow. Los Angeles-based and BIPOC-owned, this shop has the perfect combination of funky-meets-fun homewares, posters, and decor. 
**Price range: as low as $20**
[SHOP IT](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_3016170603599493068&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Ffreska.living%2F&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com)
## House of Knick Knacks
> [A post shared by House Of Knick Knacks (@houseofknickknacks)](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_1210140755981928283&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fp%2FCMpx-VzJFsr%2F&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com)
[House of Knick Knacks](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_7063481831718045348&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fhouseofknickknacks&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com) began in 2020 in hopes of bringing happiness back into our homes, specializing in the standout pieces you'll want on your dining room table. A vintage cocktail glass, vase, or bedside carafe just might be the pick-me-up you needed.

[SHOP IT](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_2045398071110520082&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fhouseofknickknacks&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com)
## Vintage Diet
> [A post shared by VINTAGE DIET (@vntgediet)](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_7349325757736312679&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fp%2FCLkZMd6gp4t%2F&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com)
Ivy Ngyuen had a goal of inspiring followers to shop more sustainably, so she founded [Vintage Diet](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_4247547601080405873&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fvntgediet&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com) in 2020. While relatively new to the online vintage community, Vintage Diet has all of the makings of your next favorite trinket gift shop.
**Price range: as low as $12**
[SHOP IT](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_9221566007728554239&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fvntgediet&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com)
## Shalei vs. Wild Vintage

[Shalei vs. Wild Vintage](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_1216891807039750266&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fshaleivswildvintage%2F&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com) is also new to the vintage Instagram community, and most definitely a brand to keep on your radar. Curated by Shalei Gall, the Portland-based company is your one-stop shop for mid-century modern home and glassware. The photography and dreamy setups will warm up your feed and make you reconsider your current set of coupe glasses.
**Price range: as low as $25**
[SHOP IT](https://go.redirectingat.com?id=92X1662120&xcust=marieclaireus_us_1050041447770583235&xs=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fshaleivswildvintage%2F&sref=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.marieclaire.com)
