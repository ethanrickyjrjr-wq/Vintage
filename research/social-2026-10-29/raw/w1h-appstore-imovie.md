I’ve been editing my videos with iMovie for years. It’s such a great editing software. It truly has helped me kickstart my filmmaking from a young age. I used to use it to make videos with my barbies, and here I am now still using it many many years later. It’s pretty expensive to get pro software, but it’s been totally fine sticking with iMovie. When I first got iMovie I was obsessed with making trailers. They’re so fun, and teach a lot about filmmaking. My only complaint is the glitches on the mobile version. They’ve corrected a lot of these glitches over the years, but it can be some what glitchy. This may be because I have thousands of videos and pictures on my phone. Also I wish you could move the text where you want it to be on your frame, and some other font options would be nice. But overall this is a fantastic editing software. Highly recommend, especially to anyone looking to start editing or making videos.

someonerereEE
So overall I think iMovie is a pretty good editing software. I love how it's one of the only editing apps I've downloaded that don't bombard you with "IMOVIE PLUS" or "GET IMOVIE PREMIUM FOR BLAH BLAH BLAH" idk but it's clearly not money hungry lol. Other apps load you with ads every time you press a button. My only problems are... 1. Lack of freedom. There aren't many things you can do in iMovie besides mess with volume, duration, filters, etc. There are definitely other editing apps that I like better (like KineMaster and CapCut) because of their variety. 2. Low quality exportation. You can't choose the quality that it exports in. I'd say it's around 480P or 720P, while other apps (that I mentioned before) allow you not only 4K 2,160P quality max, but they allow you to choose (both quality and fps). 3. No overlay. You can only have 1 clip playing per section, and you can't add any overlays/other clips. I love to be able to do this and most apps provide it. Number 2 is the biggest problem for me because I actually end up just screen recording my videos instead of downloading them. This doubles my editing process. I think it's just inconvenient, especially when there are apps that provide better quality and more freedom/variety. Other than that, I really like this app! Being completely honest though, I don't use it. I definitely prefer other apps ase they are more convenient and efficient. However, I would recommend it to anyone new to editing who needs a nice, simple editing software! This was my first editing software ever, and I absolutely enjoyed it before I found other softwares!
