# Introducing Meta One plans for creators on Instagram
![](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=703721394001347&version=1793286030) Sep 15, 2026
For the past few months we’ve been testing new professional tools for creators on Instagram. Starting today, we’re gradually rolling out Meta One creator plans globally as part of the new [Meta One subscription service](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-meta-one-subscription-service-more-features-ai) available across our apps.
  

Instagram is free, and it always will be. Subscriptions let us build features that are specialized for specific groups or too costly to bring to everyone. A Meta One subscription adds more professional tools right where a creator’s audience and content already live so you can focus more on creating and spend less time on the busywork.
  

There are four Meta One plans for creators on Instagram: Essential, Advanced, Expert and Max. Each one unlocks more professional tools to establish your presence, plan and publish your content, and gain deeper insight into what’s working. These also include our single product plans designed to give people extra expression, connection, and messaging features — [Instagram Plus](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/introducing-instagram-plus), [Facebook Plus](https://www.facebook.com/help/1437311881513288?cms_platform=iphone-app&helpref=platform_switcher), [WhatsApp Plus](https://www.whatsapp.com/whatsapp-plus) — and expanded Meta AI usage.
![Instagram post with a native link added through a Meta One creator plan](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=1961711818549656&version=1789169627&transcode_extension=webp)
## What’s in each plan
  

### Essential — $14.99/mo
  

Establish your presence and build confidence with new audiences. This plan includes:
  

  * **Link page** – a sharable link page that makes it easier for people to find your website, social channels, and other key destinations directly from your Instagram profile. Share up to 20 links, add images and custom text, and get a unique URL to feature your link page outside of Instagram.
  * **Verified badge** – once you’re verified, get the verified badge so new audiences feel more confident engaging with you.
  * **Impersonation protection** – once you’re verified, we proactively monitor for profiles that pretend to be you.
  * **Includes all of the single product plans** – Instagram Plus, Facebook Plus and WhatsApp Plus, along with expanded Meta AI usage, including image and video generation.

  

Unlike in Meta Verified, verification is not required to take advantage of the professional tools unlocked across apps with a Meta One subscription. Only the verified badge and impersonation protection require verification.
![Instagram story being scheduled a month ahead with advanced content scheduling](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=1747813589812508&version=1789169627&transcode_extension=webp)
### Advanced — $49.99/mo
  

Everything in Essential, plus even more professional-grade tools:
  

  * **Links in posts and reels** – your links no longer have to live in your bio or Instagram DMs. Share native links directly in your posts and reels so people can go straight to your website, shop, or latest drop, right from your content. Add links to 4 posts and 4 reels per month.
  * **Advanced content scheduling** – plan your content with features designed to post at the right moments for your audience. Schedule your stories to publish up to a month in advance. Schedule posts and reels up to a year in advance, up from 30 days. See suggested times to schedule posts and stories based on when your followers are most active. Bulk upload posts and reels on instagram.com, and preview your scheduled content on your grid.
  * **Competitive insights** – learn from other public accounts in your space by comparing your performance to theirs in a single dashboard, so you can fine-tune your content strategy.
  * **Extended insights history** – See data about your content, audience and more beyond 90 days and easily download those insights into a CSV file.
  * **Shared access** – give people you trust access to your account to help with creating, posting, and scheduling content – allowing you to collaborate with others without sharing your password. Share access with 4 people.
  * **Support from a real person** – reach a real person through Meta AI support assistant when you need help with your Instagram and associated Threads account. Get one human support case per month.
  * **Appear ahead in search results** with optimized search, making it easier for people to find your profile.

  

### Expert — $149/mo and Max — $499/mo
  

Scale and optimize – these plans include everything in Advanced, plus our highest levels of feature access for links in posts and reels, shared access, Meta AI usage, and support. Additionally, starting at the Expert plan you can request a call with a real person for support.
  

Plans are priced per profile, so each plan covers one account – if you create on both Instagram and Facebook, you’d pick a plan for each. Learn more about Meta One plans [here](https://www.meta.com/help/subscriptions/960854640235758/?srsltid=AfmBOooMS5BhormjgUsZNp9kaL0Hv50KOrQpsuIYSU28IvPamydcD1hn).
  

_Plans, benefits, pricing and availability may vary by region, by app, and by account. To see the specific features available to your account, start the subscription process._
![Shared access settings letting a trusted teammate post without a password](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=1394944302705006&version=1789169627&transcode_extension=webp)
## What’s next
  

We’re just getting started – soon we’ll bring Edits Plus to Meta One, a new product plan that offers more cloud storage to sync and access projects across devices, plus additional usage of the upcoming assistant, which helps you analyze your Instagram insights and brainstorm new ideas.
  

We’re bringing the best of Meta Verified into Meta One. For current Meta Verified subscribers, nothing changes right now - we are working on facilitating migration to Meta One and will notify users when as soon as available.
