Draft posts, schedule them, and check what's coming up right in Claude, ChatGPT, or the other tools you're already in all day.

## How it works
It takes about a minute to set up, and you only do it once.
  1. 1
### Connect your tools
Head to Settings → Integrations in Buffer, pick your tool, and connect. No code required (promise!)
  2. 2
### Ask your AI to do the work
Just ask. "Draft a LinkedIn post about our product update and schedule it for Thursday morning." Your assistant sends it straight to Buffer.
  3. 3
### Review and publish from Buffer
Everything lands in your Buffer queue. Review it, tweak it if you want to, then let Buffer handle the publishing.


## What you can do
  * ### Draft posts in conversation
Bye bye, copy-pasting between endless tabs. Prompt your AI assistant to draft your posts and send it straight to Buffer.
  * ### Schedule from a prompt
"Schedule this for tomorrow at 9am on LinkedIn and Threads." Your assistant handles the timing and the channels.
  * ### Check what's coming up
Ask what's in your queue this week. Get a summary without opening Buffer.
  * ### Capture ideas on the fly
Had an idea mid-conversation? Tell your assistant to save it as a Buffer idea for later.
  * ### Manage across channels
Your assistant can see all your connected channels and post to whichever ones you choose, all from one conversation.
  * ### See what's working
Ask your assistant to review your recent posts and spot the patterns. Find out what to post more of.

## FAQs
### What is MCP?
MCP (Model Context Protocol) is the standard that lets AI tools like Claude and ChatGPT connect to apps like Buffer. You don't need to understand how it works. Just connect your tool and start using it.
### Do I need to know how to code?
No. Most integrations connect with a single click. If your tool needs an API key, Buffer shows you exactly where to find it and where to paste it.
### Which AI tools work with Buffer?
Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Raycast, Perplexity, Notion, Zapier, n8n, Manus, and Antigravity. We're adding more regularly.
### Is this free?
Yes, MCP access is available on every Buffer plan, even Free — no catch.
### Will my AI assistant post without my permission?
Nope — you're always the last step. Posts created through your AI assistant land in your Buffer queue as drafts or scheduled posts. You can review and edit everything before it goes live.
### What can my AI assistant actually do in Buffer?
Create posts, schedule posts, manage your queue, save ideas, and check your connected channels and upcoming content. More capabilities are being added as the API grows.
### I use Buffer for my business. Is this secure?
Yes. Each connection uses your personal API key or OAuth sign-in, and you control exactly which tools have access. You can revoke access at any time from Settings → API in Buffer.
### Where can I learn more about the technical details?
If you're a developer or want to go deeper, visit the [developer documentation](https://developers.buffer.com/guides/integrations/mcp.html) or the [API landing page](https://buffer.com/api).
