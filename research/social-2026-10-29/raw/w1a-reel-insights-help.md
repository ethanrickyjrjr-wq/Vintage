## Before you begin
  * You need to have a public (personal or professional) account on Instagram to view insights.
  * Insights for reels are available for reels posted on and after May 14, 2021. Insights are available to view for up to two years after the reel's original publish date.


## Available insights for reels
  * **Views:** The number of times your reel starts to play or replay.
  * **Watch time:** The total amount of time your reel was played, including any time spent replaying the reel.
  * **Viewers:** The number of unique accounts that have seen your reel on screen at least once, whether or not your reel was played. Viewers is different from views, which may include multiple views of your reel by the same accounts. This metric is estimated and in-development.
  * **Average watch time:** The average amount of time spent playing your reel. This is calculated by the watch time divided by the number of initial views. An initial view is when your reel starts to play for the first time in a reel session.
  * **Follows:** This is how many accounts started following you as a result of viewing your reel.


## View insights on your reels
To view insights on your reels:
  1. Go to the Reels tab on your Instagram profile.
  2. Click on the reel you'd like to view metrics for.
  3. Click **View insights**.
