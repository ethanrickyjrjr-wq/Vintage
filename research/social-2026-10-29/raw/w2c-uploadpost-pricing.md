##  Frequently Asked Questions 
Everything you need to know about posting, safety and pricing — before you sign up. 
Do scheduled posts perform as well as manual ones?
Yes! Scheduled posts perform just as well as manual ones when your content is relevant and high quality. Modern feeds are increasingly interest-based, so what matters most is the value of your post—not whether you clicked Publish or scheduled it. Scheduling helps you stay consistent and hit optimal times without sacrificing reach.
Is UploadPost safe to use with my accounts?
Absolutely. UploadPost uses official, approved API methods and OAuth flows for platforms like TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook—your uploads go through verified, policy-compliant channels. We recommend mixing scheduled posts with natural activity (logging in, engaging with your audience) for best results.
How does UploadPost handle uploads under the hood? Do you use rotating IPs?
We don't use rotating IPs, scraping, or workarounds. UploadPost integrates directly with the official, verified APIs of platforms like TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook using secure OAuth. Our app is verified with these platforms and adheres to their reviews and audits. All uploads go through platform-owned, documented endpoints, respecting rate limits and policies. This compliant approach maximizes reliability and protects your accounts versus bot-like methods.
Does Upload-Post store my personal data?
No, Upload-Post does not store any personal data. All information is processed securely and privately to ensure your privacy and data protection.
Can I add the same social media account to multiple Upload-Post accounts?
No, for security and privacy reasons, a social media account can only be linked to one Upload-Post account at a time. Once you connect a social media account to an email, it cannot be linked to another Upload-Post account.
How much time can I save using Upload-Post?
Users report saving up to 90% of the time spent on manually uploading videos across multiple platforms, allowing for more efficient content management.
Is Upload-Post compatible with all social media platforms?
Upload-Post supports 22 platforms: TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Threads, Pinterest, Reddit, Bluesky, Google Business, Discord, Telegram, Slack, Mastodon, Nostr, Lemmy, Dev.to, Hashnode, WordPress, Whop and Listmonk. We keep adding new ones — the full list lives on our Platforms page.
Can I run paid ads with Upload-Post?
Yes — Upload-Post supports ChatGPT Ads, so you can advertise inside ChatGPT with the same API key you publish with. Connect your OpenAI Ads account once, then create campaigns, ad groups and ads and pull impressions, clicks and spend. Ad spend runs on your own OpenAI billing; Upload-Post never charges for it. Everything is created paused, so nothing starts spending until you activate it.
Can I customize how my videos are posted?
Yes, Upload-Post offers extensive customization options, including adding captions, hashtags, scheduling posts, and selecting specific platforms for each upload.
How does Upload-Post adapt my content to each platform's requirements?
Each social media platform has specific technical requirements that can be very strict from resolution and aspect ratios to file sizes, codecs, and formats. Upload-Post automatically transforms your videos and photos under the hood to match the exact specs of TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, and other platforms. This means you don't need to worry about encoding settings, compression ratios, or format compatibility if your file isn't compliant, we'll convert it so uploads succeed.
Do I need a subscription to use Upload-Post?
We offer a free tier with 10 uploads per month and premium plans with increased upload limits and advanced features. You can try our service with the free plan, no credit card required.
How does Upload-Post handle sensitive information?
All sensitive information is processed securely and encrypted. We do not store or transmit your personal data, ensuring compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations.
Can my SaaS platform, agency, or tool integrate Upload-Post for my own users?
Absolutely! We designed Upload-Post with this in mind. You can integrate our API into your existing platform, allowing your customers to publish content directly to their social media accounts without leaving your service. This is a great way to add value for your users and streamline their workflows. Check out our API documentation for more details on integration.
