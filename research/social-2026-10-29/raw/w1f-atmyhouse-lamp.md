# Make Your  
Own Lamp Workshop
![Make Your Own Lamp Workshop](https://cdn.ma.to/prod/f7bf458f-580d-40e0-bebf-8648443be2e0-original.webp)
When
Monday, 10 August
6-9PM
Add to CalendarAdd to Calendar
Price
$85
About
A hands-on workshop led by Emilie Yonker and Kieran Betz. You will learn the basics of lamp wiring and transform an unexpected object into a one-of-a-kind light. A curated selection of objects is provided, but you are also welcome to bring an item from home. You'll head home with a fully functioning custom lamp and light bulb included. Note: This session is listed as sold out.
