# 5 ways to elevate your content with seasonal trends
August 27, 2025
![Trends-seasonal-moments-blog-desktop](https://images.ctfassets.net/jis2uwrnn1pu/5iXNgBqdEfzPxlMRGO5ZX6/feb1048bc51669bc75e6b93d6db06f25/Trends-seasonal-moments-blog-image_desktop.png?fm=webp&q=85&w=1680&h=1680)
Savvy creators know that timing is everything. And on Pinterest, that means following the seasons. With millions of searches starting months before big events, a celebration planned every three seconds and over 14.6 million celebration boards created last year, it’s clear: seasonal moments consistently drive discovery on the platform.¹
People use Pinterest to plan ahead and by syncing your content to these moments, you put yourself at the heart of their planning and inspiration. 
### Leverage seasonal trends
**1. Spot trends in real-time**
Explore the [_Pinterest Trends tool_](https://trends.pinterest.com/) to see what your audience cares about right now. Track trending topics, spot rising searches and catch seasonal moments early. Filter by age, interest or location to fine-tune your strategy and launch content as interest grows—so you’re not just keeping up, but leading the conversation.
_Pro tip: Use the search bar to see popular keyword suggestions as you type, and check Ads Manager for categories and keywords to further sharpen your focus._
**2. Identify content opportunities**
Successful creators don’t just create on-trend content, they also anticipate what people are looking for. Try the Trends tool to find fast-growing topics of interest, then scan the search results. If you see strong interest but mostly older or repeat Pins, that could be your chance to stand out with fresh content.
Check “Related Trends” for rising topics with little competition. Filling these gaps can help set you apart, and because trends last longer on Pinterest,² your content could shine longer.
**3. Target and time strategically**
Use Pinterest’s interest and keyword targeting to connect with the right audience at the right time. To maximize engagement, aim to launch your content 4-6 weeks before big moments. 
Pinterest Predicts, our [_annual trend report_](https://business.pinterest.com/pinterest-predicts/), and upcoming [_seasonal moments_](https://create.pinterest.com/creators/trends) are great resources to align your ideas. Whether you’re sharing a summer recipe or fall fashion, planning ahead puts your Pins front and center when trends peak.
**4. Keep your Pins in the spotlight**
Make your content easy to discover with best practices. This will help you reach more people and stay relevant even as trends shift.
  * Creative: Use visuals and color palettes that reflect current trends.
  * Copy: Add trending keywords to Pin titles, descriptions and board names.
  * Curation: Group Pins into boards on trending topics for more relevance and visibility. 


**5. Try collages for any occasion**
Collages are a dynamic way to showcase seasonal ideas and inspire your audience. Mix products and inspiration images into one vibrant format. Collages make your ideas easy to browse and help your content stand out as people plan their next big moment.
Think: gift guides, party essentials or holiday themes. Make the most of every season. By aligning your content with seasonal trends, you’ll stay ahead of what people are searching for and planning. It’s not just about tapping into trends—it’s also about becoming part of your audience’s favorite moments.
