## What is the story behind Blue Eyed Yonder?
Vintage Event Rentals 
Blue Eyed Yonder specializes in one-of-a-kind, vintage rentals to supply photographers, brides, event planners and designers with a resource for hard-to-find vintage props and decor. Browse our storehouse of vintage items to find the perfect pieces for your next event. You can view all available vintage rental inventory on this site. Select which pieces you’re interested in, submit your wish list for a quote and we’ll get back to you in two shakes of a lamb’s tail.
Prop Styling 
You’ve fallen in love with lots of vintage items, but now what? Sometimes you need just a little help pulling your vision together. We love brainstorming unique ways to incorporate vintage elements into any event. As a part of our prop styling service, BEY can help you with establishing theme, selecting colors, pairing vintage items, finding just the right accessories, you name it. Interested in adding a dash of hand-made charm? We can do that, too! Please contact us for pricing information.
Delivery & Set-Up 
As part of your rental, Blue Eyed Yonder offers delivery and set-up services. We deliver to Atlanta and the surrounding metropolitan area. Have an event that’s a little bit farther than that? Contact us, we’ll happily deliver to most of the Southeast when orders meet our minimum “long haul” delivery amount.
Consultation 
Sometimes it helps to meet in person to discuss vintage items for your next event. Let’s grab a cup of joe and put all of your ideas on paper. We’re also available for phone consultations. Contact us to schedule a time to chat.
