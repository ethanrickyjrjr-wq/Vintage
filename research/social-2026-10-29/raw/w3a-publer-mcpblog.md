##  [](https://publer.com/blog/publer-mcp/#so-what-exactly-is-publer-mcp)**So, what exactly is Publer MCP?**
[MCP](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/docs/2026-07-28/getting-started/intro) stands for Model Context Protocol. But you don’t need to understand the technology behind it to use it.
Think of Publer MCP as the secure connection between Publer and AI assistants like ChatGPT and Claude.
It gives your AI access to the social media context (your accounts, posts, calendar, analytics) and tools available in your Publer workspace, so it can go beyond generic advice and basic content generation and actually help you get work done.
[cky_video_placeholder_title]
##  [](https://publer.com/blog/publer-mcp/#your-social-media-is-now-part-of-the-conversation)**Your social media is now part of the conversation**
AI already knows a lot about social media. It can write captions, brainstorm ideas, answer questions, and help you build a strategy based on general knowledge.
But it doesn’t know YOUR social media. It doesn’t know what you have scheduled next week, which posts are performing best, how your accounts are growing, or what your competitors are doing, unless you send screenshots or copy and paste information to your AI. 
With Publer MCP, that context is already part of the conversation. Instead of explaining your strategy from scratch or jumping between Publer and your AI assistant, you can work with your Publer data directly in your favorite AI assistant.
So instead of asking:
_“Give me some ideas for Instagram.”_
You can ask:
_“Look at our Instagram performance from the last three months. What type of content is working best, and what should we create more of?”_
The first gives you advice based on what AI knows about social media. The other gives you advice based on what AI knows about your social media.
But Publer MCP doesn’t just give your AI more context. It also gives it the tools to work with that context.

##  [](https://publer.com/blog/publer-mcp/#what-can-you-do-with-publer-mcp)**What can you do with Publer MCP?**
Publer MCP lets your AI assistant use Publer’s calendar, scheduling tools, analytics, competitor data, and more. It can connect information across your workspace, answer questions using that context, and take action through Publer. Here are some examples:  

![](https://i0.wp.com/blog.publer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/sc8.png?resize=1500%2C1436&ssl=1)
###  [](https://publer.com/blog/publer-mcp/#know-and-plan-around-your-content-calendar)**Know and plan around your content calendar**
Your AI assistant can see what’s already planned in your Publer calendar, so you can ask what’s going out today, what’s coming up next week, where there are gaps, or plan new content around what’s already scheduled.
![](https://i0.wp.com/blog.publer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/sc9.png?resize=1500%2C1436&ssl=1)
###  [](https://publer.com/blog/publer-mcp/#create-content-in-your-tone-of-voice)**Create content in your tone of voice**
Turn an idea into ready-to-publish content that sounds like you. Your AI assistant can use the tone of voice you’ve already created in Publer to generate and draft posts, then adapt them for different social networks and accounts.
![](https://i0.wp.com/blog.publer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/sc11-2.png?resize=1500%2C1436&ssl=1)
###  [](https://publer.com/blog/publer-mcp/#schedule-posts-directly-from-the-conversation)**Schedule posts directly from the conversation**
Your AI assistant can use Publer’s scheduling capabilities to turn ideas or drafts into scheduled content. Once you’re happy with a post, tell it when you want it to go live, or let it help you decide based on your schedule or the best posting times.
![](https://i0.wp.com/blog.publer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/sc10.png?resize=1500%2C1436&ssl=1)
###  [](https://publer.com/blog/publer-mcp/#explore-and-analyze-your-performance)**Explore and analyze your performance**
Publer provides the data. Your AI assistant helps you make sense of it.
Ask about your analytics, what’s working and what’s not, compare different periods or accounts, identify trends, dig into individual posts, or explore Publer’s competitor data to understand how your performance stacks up. The more detailed your questions, the better the answer.
![](https://i0.wp.com/blog.publer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/sc12-1.png?resize=1500%2C1436&ssl=1)
###  [](https://publer.com/blog/publer-mcp/#turn-your-data-into-strategy)**Turn your data into strategy**
This is where AI can go beyond Publer’s individual features.
Because it can work across your calendar, content, analytics, and competitor insights at once, it can connect the dots between them. It can summarize what it finds, identify opportunities, make recommendations, and build an entire social media strategy around your actual context.
Instead of analyzing one chart at a time, you can ask it to figure out what’s happening, why it’s happening, and what you should do next.

Your AI assistant can turn Publer’s data into detailed reports with the metrics, comparisons, summaries, visualizations, insights, and recommendations you need.
Ask for a quick monthly recap for yourself, a detailed analysis for your team, or a polished report you can share with a client.
###  [](https://publer.com/blog/publer-mcp/#let-ai-take-the-work-from-here)**Let AI take the work from here**
You don’t have to be involved in every individual step. Your AI assistant can work more like an agent: give it a goal, let it explore the relevant context, decide what needs to be done, and use Publer’s tools to carry out the work.
You decide how involved you want to be. Ask it a question and use it as an assistant. Ask it to analyze your performance and recommend your next move and use it as a consultant. Or give it a goal and let it handle the workflow like a social media manager.
All from the same conversation.

##  [](https://publer.com/blog/publer-mcp/#how-to-connect-publer-mcp)**How to connect Publer MCP**
Getting started only takes a few steps:
  1. **Add Publer to your AI assistant**  
Copy [this URL](https://mcp.publer.com) below and paste it into your AI assistant’s connector settings.
  2. **Choose OAuth to connect**  
Select **OAuth** as the authentication method.
  3. **Sign in and allow access**  
Sign in to your Publer account if prompted, then click **Allow** to complete the connection.
  4. **Start talking social media!**  
Ask about your accounts, analyze performance, create content or tell your AI what to do.

Connecting AI to your social media shouldn’t mean giving up control. Publer acts as the secure layer between your AI assistant and your social media accounts, built on the same platform trusted by 500K+ users worldwide to manage their social media.
