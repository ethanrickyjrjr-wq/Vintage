## What you pay, no surprises
When you sell on Whatnot, commission and fees are deducted from each purchase before your earnings are available for   
payout.
### Commission
The commission rate is Whatnot's share of the sale. Unlike other places, we only charge on your item's final sale price, not the total order value.**Starts at 8%, goes as low as 3%**
### Payment Processing Fee
The payment processing fee covers the cost of processing buyer payments through our payment provider. This is based on the total order value.**2.9% on total order value + $0.30 per order**

## Frequently Asked Questions
Need more help? Read more in the [help center](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/articles/4847069165965-Whatnot-seller-fees).
### 
**How is my tier determined?**
Your tier is set by your total sales across all categories over a four-week period. Every sale counts toward your tier, even though the commission rate may vary by category.
### 
**How do the 4-week periods work?**
Your total sales progress and tier resets every four weeks at 12am Monday. When a four-week period ends, your new tier (and new rates) kicks in automatically for the next four-week period.
### 
**What counts as a sale, and how are my total sales calculated?**
Your total sales are the combined sale price of all your completed orders across every category, in both live shows and marketplace. Private shows and local pickup orders don't count. Canceled or refunded orders are subtracted from your total.
### 
**What if I sell in multiple categories?**
Selling across multiple categories will always count towards your total sales. The tier you unlock applies across all categories, although the rate may vary.
### 
**Am I still eligible for other promotions and discounts?**
Yes. During a promotion or discount, you will receive the best available rate by default.
