**The anatomy of a successful ad**
ImagesVideosCopyColorAccessibility
Plan for a vertical canvas
Most people use Pinterest on mobile devices, so aim for a vertical 2:3 aspect ratio. You can easily repurpose square assets using borders or vertical imagery.
Make your logo pop
Your logo or brand name should be clearly visible on every ad. This can help reinforce brand recognition—just be sure it doesn’t overtake the ad. 
Put your products front and center
Make your products the star, and show people using them in realistic settings. Stay clear of abstract images or lifestyle imagery that doesn’t show your brand.
Stack your story
Tell a complete story by putting visuals in the middle, then stacking text overlay with key messaging at the top and extra details at the bottom.

**Frequently asked questions** 


How do I develop an effective creative strategy for Pinterest ads?

    
Your creative strategy might change depending on your goals, but some tips work across formats and objectives. Make sure you’re using vertical formats, since a lot of people use Pinterest on their phones. You should also include concise messaging, clear branding and specific CTAs on every creative asset.  
  
If you want campaign ideas, check out annual reports like [_Pinterest Predicts_](https://business.pinterest.com/pinterest-predicts/) and [_the Pinterest Palette_](https://business.pinterest.com/blog/2025-pinterest-palette-this-years-trending-colors), which are based on people’s searches and interests on Pinterest. 


What types of visuals perform best on Pinterest?

    
The best Pinterest ads feel authentic and relatable. Make sure you’re representing your brand’s specific look and feel, rather than using obscure images or lifestyle photos. If you’re selling products, it’s important to include them in the ad. Depending on your goals, [_try using different ad formats for even better results_](https://business.pinterest.com/blog/lower-funnel-creative-best-practices). 


What are the right specs for Pinterest ads? 

    
Pinterest offers a variety of ad formats, so specs can vary. Use the specs guide above to make sure your assets will upload and display properly. 


What are the best ad formats for different campaign types?

    
Your ad format impacts the kind of storytelling you do, so think about your goals before you begin. [_Explore the full list of ad formats here_](https://business.pinterest.com/advertise) to start considering your options. 


How often should I refresh my ad creative on Pinterest?

    
There isn’t a formula, but a good rule of thumb is to monitor key performance metrics like engagement. This can help you identify when to try new or different content. You might also want to freshen up creative for seasonal moments like holidays or timely events to help make your creative more relevant. 


Can I test different creative elements on my Pinterest ads?

    
Yes, testing different creative elements is a great way to see what’s resonating with people on Pinterest. You can experiment by using multi-objective ads to compare results, or use A/B testing to gather helpful insights.
