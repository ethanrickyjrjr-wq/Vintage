# How to Share to Social Media from Your Shop 
Social media is a powerful [marketing tool](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360035903014?segment=selling) that can help you promote your products, reach potential new customers, and entice existing customers to buy again. 
Tips for using social media as a marketing tool:
  * Post regularly so followers come to expect and look forward to your posts. Create a schedule so you avoid large breaks between posts.
  * Respond to comments to make your customers part of the conversation.
  * Share a mix of posts that promote your products, showcase your process and inspiration, and provide value to your followers.
  * Tailor your posts to each channel to avoid overwhelming your followers with similar content.


## **How to create a social media post on Etsy**
From the **Social media** section in your shop, you can quickly promote your shop and share important updates to sites like Facebook, Instagram, and X (formerly Twitter).
There are six types of posts you can create through Etsy:
  * Latest listings you’ve added (up to 25 of your most recent listings) 
  * Recent milestones successes, like your shop’s anniversary
  * Current or upcoming sales and coupons 
  * 5-star reviews 
  * Items you’ve favorited 


To create a social media post on [Etsy.com:](https://www.etsy.com)
  1. Go to **[Shop Manager](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/dashboard).**
  2. Choose **Marketing**.
  3. Choose **[Social Media](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/social-media).**
  4. Choose **Social accounts** to connect an account.
  5. When you’re ready to create a post, select **Create post**.
  6. Select the type of post you want to create.
  7. Choose **Next**.
  8. Add images and a caption.
  9. Select the networks you want to share your post with.
  10. Choose **Post and continue**.
  11. Choose **Done**


To create a social media post on the Etsy Seller app: 
  1. Select **More** on the navigation bar.
  2. Under **Marketing** , select **Social media**. 
  3. Select which listing you want to share, which are filtered by **Newest** , **Most favorites** , **Least views** , and **Most views**. 
  4. Select **Share listing**. 
  5. Choose one image or video from your listing to share. 
  6. Select **Next**. 
  7. Select **Share**. This will save your listing link to your device's clipboard. 
  8. Choose which social media account you want to share your listing on. 
  9. Finish posting your listing. Remember to include the listing link you copied earlier. 


Buyers can buy the items that are tagged in the photos by clicking on the orange price tag or clicking through the listing image on the right of the photo.
