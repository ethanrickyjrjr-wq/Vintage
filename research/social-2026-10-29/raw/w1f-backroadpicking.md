# How I Got Started Selling Vintage – From a $1 Box Lot to a Full-Time Business
June 3, 2025
Share  Share 
Link
Close share Copy link
Everyone always asks me: _“How did you get into selling vintage?”_   
And honestly… it all started with a $1 box at a local auction. It was the late 1990s. I was at a neighbor’s estate auction—the second one I’d ever attended. I didn’t go in with the plan to resell anything. I was there for shelves and canning jars. But as the crowd thinned and the items stopped selling, I started picking up box lots for a dollar or two—mostly just for personal use. When I got everything home, it was clear we had way more than we needed. So I did what most people wouldn’t even think of at the time: I listed a few of the items on eBay. One of those items was an advertising pencil shaped like a bullet, dating back to the 1940s. Turns out, that was a hot collector’s item—and it sold fast. That was the moment I got hooked.  
  
**The Spark That Lit the  
** Fire From that one sale, I started sourcing more intentionally. I was off to garage sales, thrift stores, and more auctions, hunting for pieces of the past that others overlooked. We turned our garage into a mini warehouse. I figured out how to pack and ship fragile vintage glassware, how to write listings that got attention, and how to keep track of inventory in a home-based business setup. Back then, online selling was still in its early days. People were wary of buying on the internet. There were no credit card processors—just PayPal—and building a website felt like dreaming too big. But I still bought the domain. And I never stopped selling.   
  
**Turning a Side Hustle into a Full-Time Life**   
Over the years, that little side hustle grew into something much more. I built an eCommerce business and expanded onto platforms like Amazon and Etsy. I even went to work for a software company helping other online sellers succeed—while continuing to run my own resale business on the side. Fast forward to 2023: I left the corporate world and officially joined my husband in running BackroadPicking full-time. Since then, we’ve grown across multiple platforms including:   
🛒 eBay   
🎨 Etsy   
🌐 Our Website (Shopify-hosted)   
📦 Amazon   
📺 Whatnot (Live selling)   
👗 Poshmark   
🏬 A booth in a local antique mall   
  
Today, we’re doing more than just selling—we’re storytelling. Every vintage item has a history, and so do we.
