Some shipping features are not available to all eligible sellers.
In order to get paid, you must [mark your item as shipped](https://www.facebook.com/help/2292899320946832/?helpref=faq_content) and enter a tracking number if you are using your own label. You have 7 calendar days to ship your order and mark it as shipped. Our system will automatically cancel the order if it hasn’t been marked as shipped, regardless of any agreement made with the buyer.
**Note:** If you’re using a prepaid label, you don’t need to mark your item as shipped.
## How taxes work
For purchases made with the shipping and checkout option, Facebook collects taxes at checkout. Keep in mind that we only collect tax in marketplace facilitator states, and we don't handle taxes for local pickup. Learn [when you need to provide your tax identification number](https://www.facebook.com/help/704722346920952/?ref=faq&helpref=faq_content). If your sales reach a certain limit and you haven't provided your tax identification number, transfers to your bank account will be on hold until you enter this information.
