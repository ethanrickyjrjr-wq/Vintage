## Questions
#####  What do I get for free?
10 credits when you sign up, valid for 30 days.
#####  Should I pay as I go or choose a plan?
Pay as you go costs 20¢ a minute and suits occasional videos. Plans cost 15.6¢ to 5¢ a minute and suit regular rendering.
#####  What happens when I run out of minutes?
Rendering pauses. Top up any time from $10. On a monthly plan, top-ups cost your plan's rate.
#####  Do unused minutes carry over?
Plan minutes roll over for 3 months. Pay-as-you-go minutes last 12 months.
#####  How are minutes counted?
By the second, rounded down. A 30-second video uses half a minute.
#####  What are credits?
Credits are what you buy. On pay as you go, one credit renders one minute. Plans render more per credit: 1.25 minutes on Starter, 1.5 on Pro, 2 on Scale and 4 on Volume. Credits also pay for AI generation.
#####  What does AI generation cost?
Each model has its own price in credits, the same on every plan. A Nano Banana 2 image at 1K costs 0.5 credits. Every model's rate is listed in the dashboard and through the models endpoint.
#####  How is the white-label video editor licensed?
The editor is source-available under a PolyForm Shield License. The npm package uses FFmpeg for exports and works with the Edit API for rendering and automation.
#####  I'm on an older plan. Does anything change?
No. Your price, credits and terms stay the same.
