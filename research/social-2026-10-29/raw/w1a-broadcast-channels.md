# Everything to know about channels
Use channels as a quick, casual way to connect directly with your followers. 
**Share** authentic content and behind-the-scenes moments through versatile formats like text, video and voice - you can even invite another creator to chat live in your channel!
**Engage** your fans through polls and question prompts to crowdsource feedback or ideas.
**Build community** by connecting your engaged fans in a shared real-time space.

##### CHANNEL TIPS
## Getting started with channels
![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/rsrc.php/v4/y4/r/-PAXP-deijE.gif)
### **Share voice notes**
Say “good morning” or sing a tune; voice notes are an easy way to add a personal touch
![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/rsrc.php/v4/y4/r/-PAXP-deijE.gif)
### **Share your channel**
Once you create a channel, share to your stories and pin to your profile to make it easy for all your followers to join.
![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/rsrc.php/v4/y4/r/-PAXP-deijE.gif)
### **Personalize your channel**
Give your channel a descriptive title, add a welcome message, and set your intention for the channel. 
![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/rsrc.php/v4/y4/r/-PAXP-deijE.gif)
### **Collaborate with others**
Collaborators can message and moderate, but they can’t modify any channel settings.
![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/rsrc.php/v4/y4/r/-PAXP-deijE.gif)
### **Use interactive features**
Connect in real time through interactive features like polls, replies and prompts.
![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/rsrc.php/v4/y4/r/-PAXP-deijE.gif)
### **Direct line to different audiences**
You can create more than one channel to talk to different groups within your follower audience or cover new topics. Use insights to see what content resonates with your different audiences.
