##  Frequently Asked Questions 
Have questions? We have answers. 
### What makes Mixpost different from other schedulers?
You own it.
Mixpost Lite is open source, and every paid license installs the full source code on your own server. Inspect it, customize it for your own use, and keep full control of your data and accounts.  
  

Pricing that doesn't scale against you.
Self-hosted licenses are a one-time payment. Unlimited accounts, workspaces, and users. No per-seat fees.  
  

You choose how to run it.
Start on Mixpost Cloud in minutes, run the free open-source Lite version, or self-host the full version with a Pro license. 
### Which social media platforms does Mixpost support?

All platforms support scheduling, analytics, and the full feature set. You can connect unlimited accounts per platform and manage as many clients as you need. 
### Can I use Mixpost for free?
Yes. Mixpost Lite is completely free and open source, available on [GitHub](https://github.com/inovector/mixpost). For the full feature set, upgrade to a Pro or Enterprise license. 
### Is Mixpost multi-tenant?
Yes. Pro and Enterprise support multi-tenancy through workspaces: isolated environments, each with its own accounts, posts, media, and team permissions. Create unlimited workspaces from a single installation to keep every brand or client fully separated. Enterprise adds subscription billing, so you can run it as your own SaaS. 
### Will I have access to the source code?
Yes. Paid licenses are source-available: the full source code is installed on your server, and you can add custom features for your own business use. You may not resell or redistribute the code, and building a SaaS requires an Enterprise license, not Pro. Mixpost Lite, the free edition, is open source. 
### Is Mixpost secured?
Yes! Passwords are hashed with bcrypt, and sensitive data like third-party account credentials and access tokens are encrypted at rest. Mixpost also supports two-factor authentication (2FA) for user accounts. 
### Does Mixpost have an MCP server?
Yes. Mixpost ships an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server, so AI assistants like Claude and Cursor can draft, schedule, and publish posts just by chatting, no code required. Because Mixpost is self-hostable, your accounts and tokens stay private on your own server. Learn more on our [Mixpost MCP server](https://mixpost.app/mcp) page. 
### What do I need to run Mixpost on my own server?
Mixpost runs on a standard PHP stack: PHP 8.3+, MySQL, Redis, and a web server like Nginx or Apache. Install it manually, or use the Docker option for a one-command deployment that bundles everything. See the full requirements in our [documentation](https://docs.mixpost.app). 
### Which package is best for me, Pro or Enterprise?
Pro: for internal use
Use Mixpost with your own team or agency, with advanced scheduling, collaboration, and management.  
  

Enterprise: to generate revenue
Adds billing and subscription management, so you can sell access to your customers and run a SaaS. 
### Will I receive future updates?
Paid licenses (Pro and Enterprise) include 1 year of free updates. After that, Mixpost keeps working on the version you already have, and you can renew if you want to keep receiving updates. Lite gets free updates forever. See our [releases page](https://mixpost.app/releases/pro) for our update history. 
### What happens after I purchase? How do I get my license and install Mixpost?
Here is what happens next:  
  
- **Instant access:** you get a confirmation email with your customer account.  
- **Dashboard:** manage licenses and invoices at mixpost.app.  
- **Install:** use Docker (one command, recommended) or install manually via Composer.  
- **Activate:** enter your license key during install and it activates automatically.  
  
Full guides are in our [documentation](https://docs.mixpost.app). Prefer not to install it yourself? Our support team can help via the chat widget. 
### What if Mixpost isn't right for me? Do you offer a refund?
Yes. Every paid license, both Pro and Enterprise, comes with a 14-day money-back guarantee. If Mixpost isn't the right fit, contact our support team using the chat widget within 14 days of your purchase and we'll refund you in full.

No problem. Message us in the chat widget and our support team will help you out.
