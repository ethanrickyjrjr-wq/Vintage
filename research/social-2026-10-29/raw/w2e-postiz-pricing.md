## Frequently asked questions
![Frequently asked questions underline](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/questions-underline.6c40bad1.svg)
### Can I use Postiz for free?
![arrow Can I use Postiz for free?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
Postiz has a 7-day free trial on every plan, and paid plans start at $29 per month.
### Which AI agents are included on every plan?
![arrow Which AI agents are included on every plan?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
Every Postiz plan includes the hosted MCP server, the Postiz CLI, the public API and webhooks. Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor, Gemini, OpenClaw, Grok Bot, Hermes Agent and any other agent that speaks MCP or runs shell commands connect at no extra charge. There is no separate agent tier: the surfaces your agents use ship with the Standard plan.
### How much does Postiz cost?
![arrow How much does Postiz cost?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
Postiz has four plans: Standard at $29 per month, Team at $39, Pro at $49 and Ultimate at $99. On annual billing the same plans cost $23, $31, $39 and $79 per month, which saves about 20%. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial, and you can change or cancel the plan at any time.
### Can I get a refund for Postiz?
![arrow Can I get a refund for Postiz?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
We refund up to one month of fees per account, as long as your last charge was within the past 120 days. Annual plans are not refundable, and any refund beyond that is at our discretion. To request one, email support@postiz.com or use the [contact page](https://postiz.com/contact). The full policy is in our [Terms of Service](https://postiz.com/terms-of-service).
### What social media platforms does Postiz support?
![arrow What social media platforms does Postiz support?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
Postiz supports all major platforms including X (Twitter), LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, Pinterest, Reddit, Discord, Bluesky, Mastodon, and more. We're constantly adding new platforms based on user demand.
### What is the Smart Agent and how does it work?
![arrow What is the Smart Agent and how does it work?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
The Smart Agent is an AI-powered assistant that helps you manage your social media through natural conversation. You can ask it to schedule posts, generate content, create images, and even analyze your performance—all by simply chatting with it.
### What is AI video clipping and how are the minutes counted?
![arrow What is AI video clipping and how are the minutes counted?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
AI video clipping turns a long YouTube video into short vertical clips with burned-in captions. Postiz picks the best parts, saves every clip to your media library, and can create a draft post for each clip on the channels you choose.  
  
Every plan includes clipping minutes per month: one minute is used for every minute of the source video, no matter how many clips come out of it. If a clipping fails without producing a clip, the minutes are given back.
### How does cross-posting work?
![arrow How does cross-posting work?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
Cross-posting lets you publish the same content to multiple social media channels at once. You can customize the message for each platform if needed, or post the same content everywhere with one click. Perfect for maintaining a consistent presence across all your channels.
### What are Internal Plugs and Global Plugs?
![arrow What are Internal Plugs and Global Plugs?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
Internal Plugs let you automatically repost or comment on your posts using other connected accounts to boost initial engagement.  
  
Global Plugs trigger automatic actions based on engagement thresholds—for example, auto-repost when a post reaches 100 likes, or add a promotional comment when engagement is high.
### How do repeated posts work?
![arrow How do repeated posts work?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
Repeated posts allow you to automatically republish evergreen content at set intervals (daily, weekly, or custom). Great for promoting ongoing offers, quotes, or content that stays relevant over time without manually rescheduling.
### What is RSS auto-posting?
![arrow What is RSS auto-posting?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
RSS auto-posting monitors your RSS feeds (blog, podcast, news) and automatically creates social media posts when new content is published. You can even use AI to generate descriptions and images for each post.
### What are Sets and how do they help with posting?
![arrow What are Sets and how do they help with posting?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
Sets are pre-defined groups of channels with custom message templates. If you frequently post to the same combination of accounts (e.g., your personal X + company LinkedIn + Facebook page), you can save it as a Set for one-click posting.
### How do Signatures work?
![arrow How do Signatures work?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
Signatures are text snippets (like hashtags, links, or promotional messages) that automatically get added to your posts. You can set a default signature that applies to all posts, or add them manually when creating content.
### What analytics does Postiz provide?
![arrow What analytics does Postiz provide?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
Postiz provides comprehensive analytics including impressions, likes, comments, shares, bookmarks, and engagement rates across all connected platforms. You can track performance over time and see which content resonates best with your audience.
### Can I schedule comments and threads?
![arrow Can I schedule comments and threads?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
Yes! You can schedule follow-up comments on your posts to boost engagement. On platforms like X and Threads, you can create entire threads. On LinkedIn and Facebook, scheduled comments appear as replies to your main post.
### What is the delay feature between posts?
![arrow What is the delay feature between posts?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
The delay feature lets you set time gaps between your posts and comments for a more natural posting rhythm. Instead of everything publishing at once, you can space them out by minutes or hours.
### How do webhooks work in Postiz?
![arrow How do webhooks work in Postiz?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
Webhooks send real-time notifications to your own systems when posts are published. This lets you integrate Postiz with other tools—for example, updating a spreadsheet, triggering a Slack notification, or syncing with your CRM.
### Can I organize channels by clients or brands?
![arrow Can I organize channels by clients or brands?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
Yes! The Customer Groups feature lets you organize your connected channels by client, brand, or any grouping you prefer. This makes it easy to manage multiple clients or separate personal and business accounts.
### Can I self-host Postiz myself?
![arrow Can I self-host Postiz myself?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
Yes. Self-hosting is a secondary option; the hosted platform is where the agent features ship first. See the [docs for the self-host guide](https://docs.postiz.com).
### When using the self-hosted service, where will my app be deployed?
![arrow When using the self-hosted service, where will my app be deployed?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
We can deploy Postiz on any server you own, for example AWS, Azure, GCP, Railway, Hetzner or Coolify. Contact us on Discord to talk through your setup.
### I want the self-hosted service. Why do I need to pay for app approval?
![arrow I want the self-hosted service. Why do I need to pay for app approval?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
You do not have to pay for app approval. Getting your own social media apps approved by each network can take weeks and several rounds of review, so we offer it as an optional paid service for teams that would rather not do it themselves.
### Why are there different prices for social media app approval?
![arrow Why are there different prices for social media app approval?](https://postiz.com/_next/static/media/chevron-up.ae661b29.svg)
Some approvals only need a single submission. Facebook, Instagram, Threads and YouTube are more involved: they usually take more than a month, with several rounds of changes to the submission, privacy policy and terms of service, so they cost more.
