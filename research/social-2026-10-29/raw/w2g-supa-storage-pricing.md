Pricing
You are charged for the total size of all assets in your buckets. If storage usage is higher than expected or you see a storage size quota error, see [Storage size quota errors](https://supabase.com/kb/troubleshooting/storage-unexpectedly-high-usage-or-exceed_storage_size_quota-errors).
$0.00002919 per GB-Hr ($0.0213 per GB per month). You are only charged for usage exceeding your subscription plan's quota.  
| Plan  | Quota in GB  | Over-Usage per GB  | Quota in GB-Hrs  | Over-Usage per GB-Hr  |  
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |  
| Free  | 1  | -  | 744  | -  |  
| Pro  | 100  | $0.0213  | 74,400  | $0.00002919  |  
| Team  | 100  | $0.0213  | 74,400  | $0.00002919  |  
| Enterprise  | Custom  | Custom  | Custom  | Custom  |  
For a detailed explanation of how charges are calculated, refer to [Manage Storage size usage](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/manage-your-usage/storage-size).
If you use [Storage Image Transformations](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/storage/serving/image-transformations), additional charges apply.
