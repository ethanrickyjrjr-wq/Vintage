# Getting Started on Threads: A New Way to Engage Your Community in Conversation
  

Threads was built by the Instagram team for sharing with text.
![](https://scontent-mia3-2.xx.fbcdn.net/v/t39.8562-6/421386649_937312910659835_2339492052439610081_n.jpg?_nc_cat=105&ccb=1-7&_nc_sid=f537c7&_nc_ohc=P2iAe2dezi4Q7kNvwED3BhN&_nc_oc=AdpoAOkPXvDcw30SR_lxiY22TwEdKLudgtuyBbM2-fENYe9oMzxMyzlrsfaG9QnlfdI&_nc_zt=14&_nc_ht=scontent-mia3-2.xx&_nc_gid=nhV-DA-uQCgw5pTv7OQDMw&_nc_ss=7b289&oh=00_AQQMwKwK6QUstzKDHmvUVkJHN8Lf3qDtII02YJf2rzksOA&oe=6AE95932)
In July 2023, Mark Zuckerberg announced the launch of [Threads](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fabout.fb.com%2Fnews%2F2023%2F07%2Fintroducing-threads-new-app-text-sharing%2F&h=AUCzM6LWEayU9sN2-wMREya9jGVLs-ZYPbEQtKiwM5hAHOiWGF5BSrAvINO328KvtJld6KCSt7X6c6nPTapqDhYS-o_PBJg4yJsys77S9V9DpgG_miIr5YUZ-27agEAu1rbm4gCT3cEGKA), an app built by the Instagram team for text-based sharing. The vision for Threads is to take what Instagram does best, connecting people via photos and videos, and expand that to text, to create a positive space for public conversation.
  

Threads is quickly growing and we are working to integrate feedback, build new features, and introduce fun and exciting ways to connect. Some governments and nonprofits have already begun to use Threads to inspire, educate, and build their community.
  

Threads is available in over 100 countries. You can install the app on [iOS](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fapps.apple.com%2Fus%2Fapp%2Fthreads-an-instagram-app%2Fid6446901002&h=AUDD2N8mflMhPzLdiOOJIh2V0BcFxXNfffXKyxoO04dmyoBmm1t9u8swBsHM5-Dvkn5wX-4WqIN8TLxiNrlowUeFBSNPd5TuTnMn4LJt9eet4COcA_I3zTjssqr3CLfvoO4JiME-D4Yfbw) or [Android](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fplay.google.com%2Fstore%2Fapps%2Fdetails%3Fid%3Dcom.instagram.barcelona%26hl%3Den_US%26gl%3DUS%26pli%3D1&h=AUDPHuFrN5Zd2VcPQU4sXoS9GhDkCTcO4UHmGcMv5iHqg93kIGE-L-YvKCRtviyE1Tlm6a81GKmaERUk2ja5zRx0GAoudcC5p0iMBwKHzLazod1gjAMD2K3PqV2qDJTAVV-p9NchyFAEGA) to start exploring how it can help you build your community online.
  

**Get started with your Instagram login**
  

![](https://scontent-mia5-1.xx.fbcdn.net/v/t39.8562-6/422596679_710136337852802_8228920083700870890_n.jpg?_nc_cat=102&ccb=1-7&_nc_sid=f537c7&_nc_ohc=TQkU8CRZQpgQ7kNvwG8NZoM&_nc_oc=AdocuNSnkFIeQEIBo2nsChN0dEkzJtbf0R-HrQ5uaW3dxT-9HW5bicOXxttxbEh8h5o&_nc_zt=14&_nc_ht=scontent-mia5-1.xx&_nc_gid=nhV-DA-uQCgw5pTv7OQDMw&_nc_ss=7b289&oh=00_AQR3k0B87QZGQOhUZu-mylZVFEjAvm_UAdVJnv7uIslylA&oe=6AE97BED)
  

It is easy to get started with [Threads](https://www.threads.net/): simply use your Instagram account to login. [Click here](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F1747515265645443%2F%3Fhelpref%3Duf_permalink%26parent_cms_id%3D179980294969821&h=AUDXJho5nD4FBsLtQCK6qjFxDBKnjCV28uXuuYnOyqa9ZrjSnIa-creJeWmZ-qKOZ9zMOzJbuFP-hyD3kNSjAfHzarDn0wkS0G6XJp8bZfo-tRjj7e-PadfO1Fe5g3BxHYtXO6PGLObfZA) for step-by-step instructions for setting up your profile. Your Instagram username and verification will carry over, so there is no need to worry about reserving your handle. When you create your profile, you will have the option to customize your profile specifically for Threads. You can choose to follow the same accounts you do on Instagram, and find more people who care about the same things you do.
  

![](https://scontent-mia3-1.xx.fbcdn.net/v/t39.8562-6/10000000_311207501447442_7007320961458451552_n.gif?_nc_cat=111&ccb=1-7&_nc_sid=f537c7&_nc_ohc=RrIy_CKKxeEQ7kNvwF93qUs&_nc_oc=AdoF32Wy-U8S7dTbb4w42oPRpsXQbh97u1wgXcbvmHNkAwwJhSXYSPy_RxEd1HIkaoc&_nc_zt=14&_nc_ht=scontent-mia3-1.xx&_nc_gid=nhV-DA-uQCgw5pTv7OQDMw&_nc_ss=7b289&oh=00_AQQ-FOedCXzXWqWP2KJVvyXsYE-XWmGtNtFMTh99vR6igw&oe=6AE978C1)
  

  * Your feed on Threads includes threads from people you follow, and recommended content from new creators you have not discovered yet.
  * To customize your feed, use the search feature to discover new accounts to follow. [Click here](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F2456211561209945%2F%3Fhelpref%3Duf_permalink%26parent_cms_id%3D179980294969821&h=AUCIauxoUXAL_YBqzy0nhAnr1Rkcs2HhfjWa7tFswgBlAu5oXLJHXmobBV2AIZ72ki3mdo_y-RRGJ0y1rQHiyYmUJkfeSiWr5tX2acErz5LHFnHIRaMjZjsLly-NVm_cj1UwLC2EUkloQw) for instructions on how to search on Threads.
  * You can also switch to “Following” to see a chronological feed of threads from only people you are following.

  

When you first join Threads, consider making a post announcing your arrival. You can let your existing Instagram followers know you joined Threads by sharing this post to your Instagram Story or Feed.
  

**Posting on Threads**
  

![](https://scontent-mia5-1.xx.fbcdn.net/v/t39.8562-6/422473917_7033425310025915_1102206412836166763_n.png?_nc_cat=104&ccb=1-7&_nc_sid=f537c7&_nc_ohc=k5FBSG-bKdMQ7kNvwFJW-RO&_nc_oc=Adoy_Ri7m-tG0cySlKTVn03L43AqcNhSLJ4sN8mV6F0shwdT_pbNic8vfbYakwi8xfw&_nc_zt=14&_nc_ht=scontent-mia5-1.xx&_nc_gid=nhV-DA-uQCgw5pTv7OQDMw&_nc_ss=7b289&oh=00_AQQFxaraWzCqZoBGFOe5eH34kxMGzCa0FyiSEEM60LK1kw&oe=6AE94EED)
  

Posts on Threads can be up to 500 characters long. You can attach links, photos, videos (up to 5 minutes in length), and carousels (a combination of up to 10 total photos and videos) to your threads. You can also engage your followers using polls, GIFs, and voice threads. [Click here](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F1217144552251333%2F%3Fhelpref%3Duf_permalink%26parent_cms_id%3D179980294969821&h=AUAm99L5myK1E9XH9kahxCwYYBZ6BZ37k0cX7uuUcxsFubKiF1BcYzq8SojKORNuRkKVDJP1bKVBE0c8BBn3FJ6-f9BArI78rTASmi7-CvkHNHiyXt0cvxK6QZUxIhbT0Tbfh646jaCSjA) for instructions on how to share your first thread.
  

![](https://scontent-mia5-1.xx.fbcdn.net/v/t39.8562-6/422697692_763746265616727_5441866692317348919_n.png?_nc_cat=104&ccb=1-7&_nc_sid=f537c7&_nc_ohc=zh5mnNaa6dQQ7kNvwECwEPq&_nc_oc=AdqfR5kqrA28DdGp0lV0zCXD4EkpS2zqwzgh9Y9t9AMjfW4Kaj1bUPu0eg0HRrd8qOw&_nc_zt=14&_nc_ht=scontent-mia5-1.xx&_nc_gid=nhV-DA-uQCgw5pTv7OQDMw&_nc_ss=7b289&oh=00_AQRsxvqwYkSC3rEsJVycGbRKVWhbmV7hxDOKZ5s82VcmZg&oe=6AE95E43)
  

Threads includes core accessibility features available on Instagram, including screen reader support and AI-generated image descriptions.
  

  * You can easily share a thread to your Instagram story, via Instagram DMs, or share your post as a link on any other platform you choose.
  * After you share a thread, you have up to 5 minutes to make changes including fixing typos or adding missing photos.

  

**Start A Conversation on Threads**
  

_Threads is all about conversation:_ to get your followers engaged, create threads that spark a discussion and invite replies. The easiest way to start a conversation is to pose a question. Consider being direct and asking your followers what they want to hear from you on Threads. You can start a conversation, ask for advice, or share a poll.
  

![](https://scontent-mia3-1.xx.fbcdn.net/v/t39.8562-6/422681970_1047842136317854_5690111780447986242_n.png?_nc_cat=110&ccb=1-7&_nc_sid=f537c7&_nc_ohc=taASMzIk2r8Q7kNvwHDfaFx&_nc_oc=AdpoIbb-VIje4575UXR8qAyGa091dA6zqkHJC2W1Sl4Vu6E97K-3U3dgaQzIVAFXmy8&_nc_zt=14&_nc_ht=scontent-mia3-1.xx&_nc_gid=nhV-DA-uQCgw5pTv7OQDMw&_nc_ss=7b289&oh=00_AQQtRiQ8FaG0aiBk2Qa0YhDqRB7Jm-dn9XRBqNXXMwWcIA&oe=6AE955DA)
  

_Let your community inform your content strategy_ : Make sure your content is relevant and relatable to your followers. You can also interact with other people’s threads by liking or replying to them.
  

_Find your community on Threads_ : Like and reply to threads posted by your followers, public figures, and other organizations to get your name out there and increase discoverability. You can also expand on a conversation, or add your own take, by reposting or quoting a thread.
  

_Let people know what you are thinking:_ consider commenting on cultural moments and current events, or create buzz by sharing a big announcement. When posting, consider adding a topic tag (#) to join a wider conversation around the ideas, moments, and trends that are being discussed on Threads
  

**Tune Out the Noise***
  

Threads has tools to enable positive, productive conversations. You can control who can mention you or reply to you within Threads. Like on Instagram, you can add hidden words to filter out replies to your threads that contain specific words. You can unfollow, block, restrict or report a profile on Threads by tapping the three-dot menu, and any accounts you have blocked on Instagram will automatically be blocked on Threads.
