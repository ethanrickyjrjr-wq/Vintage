## About
Chic Patina is a wedding rental company located in West Palm Beach, Florida, that serves the Miami region. They offer couples antique furniture, decor, and accessories for their wedding events to enhance the beauty of their special day. Couples will enjoy the variety of furniture they have and their many services.  
  
**Services Offered**  
Chic Patina offers a showroom of their rentals so couples can view and choose the pieces they feel fits perfectly with their vision. This company is also happy to build couples furniture, as well as restore, repaint, and/or repurpose furniture. Chic Patina will also deliver and set up rentals, which include the following:  

  * Aisle markers  

  * Aisle runners  

  * Centerpieces  

  * Chair covers  

  * Chairs  

  * Decor  

  * Drapery  

  * Glassware  

  * Lights  

  * Lounge furniture  

  * Signage  

  * Tent accessories  

  * Trinkets  

  * Vases
