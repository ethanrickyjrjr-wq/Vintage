  * ### Terrible Photo Backup App
09/27/2025
LunarLemoon
The primary purpose of a backup is to restore data from it. However, in practice, Immich is horrible at restoring photos to your phone. Consider this scenario: you purchase a new iPhone and use your own Immich server to back up your old iPhone’s photos. You want some of those photos on your new iPhone, so you open the Immich app, connect it to your new phone, and press the download button on a few photos to create a wallpaper on your new iPhone. Surprisingly, the app is coded so poorly that it doesn’t exclude the photos you restored or downloaded from Immich to your new phone. As a result, you end up with duplicates on the Immich server when the auto-backup begins from your new iPhone. It only excludes those photos once it backs them up again.I conducted some research and discovered that someone had submitted a feature request on the Immich GitHub to “Prevent re-upload of downloaded photos during app backup task.” The maintainer, bo0tzz, responded to them by suggesting that you should exclude the download album from your backup selection. However, this solution is invalid. Once you exclude the download album, you won’t be able to backup any photos saved from Safari or any other app. The app is stupidly designed to only exclude photos it backs up rather than also excluding the restored photos you download from it.

# Terrible Photo Backup App
09/27/2025
LunarLemoon
The primary purpose of a backup is to restore data from it. However, in practice, Immich is horrible at restoring photos to your phone. Consider this scenario: you purchase a new iPhone and use your own Immich server to back up your old iPhone’s photos. You want some of those photos on your new iPhone, so you open the Immich app, connect it to your new phone, and press the download button on a few photos to create a wallpaper on your new iPhone. Surprisingly, the app is coded so poorly that it doesn’t exclude the photos you restored or downloaded from Immich to your new phone. As a result, you end up with duplicates on the Immich server when the auto-backup begins from your new iPhone. It only excludes those photos once it backs them up again.I conducted some research and discovered that someone had submitted a feature request on the Immich GitHub to “Prevent re-upload of downloaded photos during app backup task.” The maintainer, bo0tzz, responded to them by suggesting that you should exclude the download album from your backup selection. However, this solution is invalid. Once you exclude the download album, you won’t be able to backup any photos saved from Safari or any other app. The app is stupidly designed to only exclude photos it backs up rather than also excluding the restored photos you download from it.
