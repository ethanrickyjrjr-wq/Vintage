  * ### Not worth paying for.
Jul 7
Sacredcellf
Before I do a formal review on the App Store, I would like to describe my experience using Captions. First and foremost, I want to clarify that this was a paid subscription. The biggest issue that I had using this app was how the credits that were given to me were applied to correct AI inaccuracies. There are many times when I use the descriptive AI function where I’d give a specific command and then AI would execute the command and when the edits were applied, they were significantly different than what I’d described. So in essence, a lot of my credits were not primarily used on editing. They were used to correct the inaccuracies that AI had applied to my projects. I also did not like the experience as a new user because it does take time to understand the app, and to understand how the edits look when you’re creating content. So when content was being created, and I was being charged for each edit, I didn’t like the fact that when I did not like the edit, I still had to pay for that using my own credit. I think that the app should give people an experimental time where we get familiar with all of the functions and then at some point, we begin being charged for the AI credits and things that we do. But the AI prompt section where we are to give descriptive information to me was a little fraudulent. And that is the reason why I am canceling this app and canceling my paid subscription
