If you have a public Instagram account, you can plan ahead by scheduling reels, photos and carousel posts from the Instagram app. Scheduling posts can help you save time and stay organized when you create and schedule your posts. 
## Before you begin
  * You must have a [public account](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F517073653436611%2F%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUASy27zmfR4Vi4_QhN2uovu35Qf9dSm59h20Ulbb8UlvI08MzdfD2gaXcyiiWQdcaamOzZ1Y85hdZnXIpokyXZtvUL_aht-xOyET27N-wjZpx7Z7xWaTLzPx-l-NwmYQ7xrPmO55GHvBw) on Instagram.
  * You can schedule up to 25 posts a day, up to 30 days in advance.
  * Your scheduled content will retain the same privacy and audience settings that were configured at the time of scheduling.


## Schedule a post or reel on Instagram
  1. Tap ![](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=890637207121394&version=1774019797)**Create** in the top left or swipe right anywhere in Feed. Then, tap**Post** or **Reel**.
  2. Select a photo or video, then tap Next.
  3. Add a caption, effects, or filters. 
  4. Tap ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=edits-3dots-shared&density=1)**More options**.
  5. Turn on **Schedule this post** or **Schedule this reel** , then choose a date and time. 
  6. Tap **Done**.
  7. Tap **Back**.
  8. Tap **Schedule** to schedule the post or Reel.


**Note:** Features like product tagging, collaborative posts, sponsored posts and fundraisers aren’t compatible with scheduled content at this time.
## Manage scheduled posts and Reels on Instagram
There are 2 ways to manage scheduled content: from your profile, or when you create a new post or reel. 
To manage scheduled content from your profile: 
  1. Tap **Profile** or your profile picture in the bottom right to go to your profile.
  2. Tap **Menu** in the top right. 
  3. Below **Your insights and tools** , tap **Scheduled content**. 
  4. Tap **Options** next to the post or reel you want to edit. 
  5. Select **Delete** ,**Edit post** , **Reschedule** or **Share now**.


To manage scheduled content when you create a new post or Reel:
  1. Tap ![](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=890637207121394&version=1774019797)**Create** in the top left or swipe right anywhere in Feed. Then, tap **Post** or **Reel**.
  2. Tap **Scheduled** to see your scheduled content.
  3. Select the post or reel you want to edit, then tap **Options**.
  4. Select **Delete** , **Edit post** , **Reschedule** or **Share now**.
