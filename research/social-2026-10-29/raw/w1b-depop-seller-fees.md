#  Seller fees and charges 
### **Selling on Depop:**
Outlined below are the fees that apply when selling on Depop. These fees apply automatically to sales made through the BUY button.  
|   | **Fee Amount**  | **Fee Basis**  |  
| --- | --- | --- |  
| **Sellers in the United Kingdom (UK), United States (US) and Australia (AUS)** |  
| No Depop Selling fees will be charged to sellers based in the UK, US, or AUS. |  
| **UK payment processing fee (Depop Payments)**  | 2.9% + £0.30   | The payment processing fee for Depop Payments (via Stripe) is charged on the item sale price plus shipping costs and any applicable taxes.  |  
| **US payment processing fee (Depop Payments)**  | 3.3% + $0.45  | The payment processing fee for Depop Payments (via Stripe) is charged on the item sale price plus shipping costs and any applicable taxes.  |  
| **AUS payment processing fee (Depop Payments)**  | 2.6% + $0.30 AUD  | The payment processing fee for Depop Payments (via Stripe) is charged on the item sale price plus shipping costs and any applicable taxes.  |  
| **Sellers outside of the UK, US and AUS** |  
| **Depop Selling fees**  | 10% on all sales  | The selling fee is charged on the item sale price (excluding taxes) and, if you haven’t used a Depop Shipping label, the shipping cost.   
The selling fee applies to sellers outside of the UK, US, or AUS and selling in a currency other than GBP, USD, or AUD.  |  
| **Payment Processing fee for sellers outside of the UK, US and AUS (PayPal)**  | Sellers outside of the United Kingdom, United States and Australia will pay a payment processing fee via PayPal. PayPal’s fee may vary depending on your location and PayPal account setup (check your PayPal account for more information).  |  
| **Sellers in the United Kingdom (UK) and United States (US)^:** |  
| **Boosting fee (for Boosted Listings)**  | 12%  | The boosting fee is charged on the item sale price (excluding taxes) and, if you haven’t used a Depop Shipping label, the shipping cost. Learn more [here](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/10110299060753).  |  
| ^new listings from 23 March 2026 |  
| **Sellers outside of the UK and US:** |  
| **Boosting fee (for Boosted Listings)**  | 8%  | The boosting fee is charged on the item sale price (excluding taxes) and, if you haven’t used a Depop Shipping label, the shipping cost. Learn more [here](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/10110299060753).  |  
### **When are the fees deducted?**
If you are selling using Depop Payments (via Stripe), all fees are deducted before your sale proceeds are paid to your bank account. 
If you sell via PayPal, sale proceeds are sent to your PayPal account. The Selling fee and PayPal’s payments processing fee are then deducted as separate charges from your PayPal account.
### **Are the fees refundable?**
If your sale is refunded, all fees (the selling fee, payments processing fees and boosting fees) will be reversed too. 
This happens automatically for transactions using Depop Payments. If you’re outside the UK, US, or AUS and using PayPal, you can get a refund for the Depop selling fees by [raising a ticket](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/requests/new) with our Support team and attaching a screenshot of your refund and confirmation of your @username.
### **What about bundles?**
If you sold a bundle on Depop and are based outside of the UK or US, Depop selling fees will apply for each item you sold. These will show separately, so make sure you review how many items you sold. The payment processing fees will apply once per bundle transaction. 
###    
**How can I check my fees?**
**Sold with Depop Payments?**
  * Go to **My Depop** , then tap **My Account** in the top-right corner
  * Tap **Depop Balance** , then scroll to **Latest Transactions** to view your activity


  
**Sold through PayPal?**
Log in to PayPal using the website, click **Activity,** and review any recent Depop sales and fee payments
