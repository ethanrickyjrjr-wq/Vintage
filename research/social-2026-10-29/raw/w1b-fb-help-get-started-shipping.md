Some shipping features are not available to all eligible sellers.
This article is about items sold by individual sellers with shipping and checkout. Learn about the different [ ways to buy and sell](https://www.facebook.com/help/329122794534612?helpref=faq_content) on Marketplace.
If this is your first time selling something with shipping on Marketplace, you'll need to set up your account to make sure you can get paid for your sales.
## Offer shipping for your items on Marketplace
  1. From your Feed, click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=comet-marketplace-desktop&density=1)**Marketplace** in the left menu. If you don't see ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=comet-marketplace-desktop&density=1)**Marketplace** , click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=comet-arrowthinseemore-desktop&density=1)**See more**.
  2. Click **Create new listing** , then click **Item for sale**. Enter the details for an item you'd like to sell.
  3. Click **Next** , then click **Set up shipping**. If you don't see **Set up shipping** , you may not be eligible right now. See more information below.
  4. Select **Shipping** , then enter the shipping details and follow the on-screen instructions to publish your listing.


**Note:** All sellers who set up shipping and checkout agree to follow our [Seller Performance and Accountability Policies](https://www.facebook.com/legal/merchant_policies).
