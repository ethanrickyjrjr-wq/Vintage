## Frequently Asked Questions  

What are activated contacts & how do I activate them?
Any campaign, segmentation, data enrichment, or automation contributes to "activating” a contact. Manual actions like sending a DM manually are not considered activations.
Once a contact is activated, you can run any number of automations on them for the month. The quota is set per contact, not per interaction.
Every month, the quota is reset. For the full list of what counts and what does not, see [contact activation](https://www.inro.social/glossary/contact-activation).
What happens when I reach my maximum of contacts?
It depends on your plan. The Pro monthly plan has no cap: activations are metered, and your price follows how many contacts you activate that month.
The Free plan (100 activated contacts a month), the 14-day Pro trial (1,000) and the Annual plan (10,000 a month) are capped. Once you reach the cap, your automations and campaigns stop reaching new contacts until the count resets or you upgrade. For what counts as an activation, see [contact activation](https://www.inro.social/glossary/contact-activation).
Can I share my Inrō account with other teams members?
In the Free Plan, sharing an account is limited to one user only. However, the Pro Plan allows you to add up to 10 team members, enabling collaboration on campaigns and automations. This means more hands on deck, faster project completion, and better team coordination.  
  
With the Managed Account plan, you have complete control over team members. You can include an unlimited number of team members, each with role-based access control (RBAC), ensuring that everyone has the appropriate permissions for their tasks.
Can I downgrade plans?
With Inrō, you don't need to adjust your plan manually. The system automatically scales up or down based on your activity level and the number of contacts engaged, so you only pay for the value you get.
