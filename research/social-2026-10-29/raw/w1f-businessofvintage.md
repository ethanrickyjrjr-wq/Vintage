# About
[ View Our Services ](https://businessofvintage.com/services)
Sloane Middleton Mann is the founder behind Business of Vintage, **the only marketing agency designed _exclusively_ for the unique world of vintage retail.**
With _nearly a decade_ of experience in furniture & vintage eCommerce, plus an MBA from Northwestern’s Kellogg School of Management ( #1 business school for marketers), Sloane’s career achievements include: 
  * Advised 1,200+ vintage & antique dealers on eCommerce strategy
  * Coordinated press & partnerships with industry leaders like Architectural Digest and MoMA
  * Built online presences for multiple brands from scratch
  * Host of [Business of Vintage Podcast](https://businessofvintage.com/podcast), where top vintage sellers share what’s working and what’s not in their shops
