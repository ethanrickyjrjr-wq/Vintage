# _Chaney Widmer, Founder_
I’m the gal behind Mix & Match Design Company. Since 2015, I’ve been coming alongside my clients to create the home they’ve been dreaming about. My style is a mix of coastal, transitional, and modern, and it’s always warm and inviting. I can’t wait to work with you on your home.

# VIRTUAL DESIGN CONSULTATIONS
[ Learn more & Book ](https://mixandmatchdesign.com/virtual-consultation)
Need some one-on-one design help? Virtual consultations are available to help you make decisions, pull it all together, and move forward with confidence.
Bring Chaney your room, your renovation plans, your Pinterest boards, your furniture options and all those open browser tabs. We'll work through them together so you can stop second-guessing and start moving forward.
