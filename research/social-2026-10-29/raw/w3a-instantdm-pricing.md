## Pricing FAQ
Still deciding? Start free and upgrade only when you need more capacity.
[Create your free account ](https://app.instantdm.com/signup)
What does “unlimited automation” mean?
There is no monthly automation cap on the paid single-account plans. Legend Pro and Trendsetter have hourly throughput limits; Trendsetter Pro removes the InstantDM hourly limit. Meta platform rules still apply to every plan.
Can I start without a credit card?
Yes. The Free plan does not require a credit card. Paid plans also include a 7-day free trial.
When should I choose a multi-account plan?
Choose Multi Starter or Multi Pro when you need to manage separate Instagram or Facebook accounts for multiple brands, clients, or team workspaces from one subscription.
Which plan includes API and webhook access?
API, webhook, and MCP access are included with Trendsetter, Trendsetter Pro, Multi Starter, and Multi Pro.
Can I change plans later?
Yes. You can move to a different plan as your automation volume, feature needs, or number of managed accounts changes.
