To create a Threads profile, you’ll need to sign in with your Instagram or Facebook account. You can create one profile on Threads for each Instagram and Facebook account you have.
## Create a profile
Tap an option below to learn how to create a profile.

## Keep in mind
  * Profiles you follow and profiles following you on Threads won’t affect who you’re following or your followers on Instagram or Facebook.
  * You can edit your profile on Threads at any time to update your bio, links and profile picture. To update other things like your personal information, edit your profile on the [Instagram](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F1136464441271820%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUAx8EgM_fTSGP9dbPws5HlLC2COERTNRKRbeRULlv_71ZG3KSDmFz6t-_vA_HbtRUnOplVb4VnxrxSf-Dfftn7wsNheOnCo6yPFlOAaFu-jUmHJ9MCvwVL_QesMOBTgPudgErBREPSFtg) or [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/help/1017657581651994?helpref=faq_content) account used to create your Threads profile.
  * If the Instagram account you used to create your Threads profile is verified, you can only change your profile picture on [Instagram](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F557544397610546%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUAx8EgM_fTSGP9dbPws5HlLC2COERTNRKRbeRULlv_71ZG3KSDmFz6t-_vA_HbtRUnOplVb4VnxrxSf-Dfftn7wsNheOnCo6yPFlOAaFu-jUmHJ9MCvwVL_QesMOBTgPudgErBREPSFtg). A verification badge will also be shown on your Threads profile.
  * If you create a Threads profile with a Facebook account that is verified, your verified badge won’t carry over to Threads.
  * If you would like to link your identity between your Threads profile and your profile on another server, like Mastodon, you can add a link to your other profile from another server in your Threads bio to create a rel="me" link. Learn how to [edit your profile](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F759628745637994%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUAx8EgM_fTSGP9dbPws5HlLC2COERTNRKRbeRULlv_71ZG3KSDmFz6t-_vA_HbtRUnOplVb4VnxrxSf-Dfftn7wsNheOnCo6yPFlOAaFu-jUmHJ9MCvwVL_QesMOBTgPudgErBREPSFtg) on Threads.
