You can boost Instagram reels to turn them into ads to help your brand reach new audiences and drive engagement. Boosted reels appear in the Reels tab, Explore, Feed and Stories.
You can also learn how to [boost an Instagram post](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/2090822074310288?helpref=faq_content) or [boost an Instagram Story](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/2281642782069520?helpref=faq_content).
## Before you begin
If you haven’t boosted on Instagram before, you’ll be prompted to choose an ad account to connect to. You can choose an existing Facebook ad account, or keep your Facebook and Instagram accounts separate.
Before you boost an Instagram reel, check its eligibility before posting. To be eligible for boosting:
  * Reels must be 90 seconds or less and have a full-screen (9:16) vertical format.
  * Reels can’t use licensed music. Instead, we recommend using royalty-free music, like what's available in our [Sound Collection,](https://www.facebook.com/sound/collection) or original audio.
  * Your ad shouldn't use Instagram Reels published before October 15th, 2021.
  * Your reels media can’t contain face or camera effects, GIFs, product tags, interactive stickers or camera filters from a third party.
  * Reels that have already been shared to Facebook can’t be boosted.


## Boost an Instagram Reel
**Note:** When you boost a reel on the Instagram iOS app, you’ll need to pay with available funds. To save money and [avoid the Apple service fee](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F898715828241123%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUDEUdLoR_OH8gAjbKpQ8-OqaRqTE79l1NpBebZ9_xqOdiG0f7sBzlYTxTxvRWA5NBxDp6U8PjO6AqG6dMXXLfAcE6y3b9e1kuxqWw5ucYaLw_GpCL5qx1D7gWqwqCTE-NSU1sYTynXBKw) when boosting your content, use [instagram.com](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=http%3A%2F%2Finstagram.com%2F&h=AUDEUdLoR_OH8gAjbKpQ8-OqaRqTE79l1NpBebZ9_xqOdiG0f7sBzlYTxTxvRWA5NBxDp6U8PjO6AqG6dMXXLfAcE6y3b9e1kuxqWw5ucYaLw_GpCL5qx1D7gWqwqCTE-NSU1sYTynXBKw) instead of the Instagram iOS app.
To boost an Instagram reel from the Instagram app:
  1. Go to your profile.
  2. Tap the reel you'd like to boost.
  3. Below the reel, tap **Boost**.
  4. Fill in the details of your ad by selecting a **Goal**(what results you'd like from this ad), **Audience** (who you want to reach), **Budget** (how much you want to spend daily) and **Duration** (how long you want your ad to run). Tap **Next** once you've completed these details.
  5. To complete your ad, tap **Boost reel** under **Review your ad**.


To boost an Instagram reel from [instagram.com](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=http%3A%2F%2Finstagram.com%2F&h=AUDEUdLoR_OH8gAjbKpQ8-OqaRqTE79l1NpBebZ9_xqOdiG0f7sBzlYTxTxvRWA5NBxDp6U8PjO6AqG6dMXXLfAcE6y3b9e1kuxqWw5ucYaLw_GpCL5qx1D7gWqwqCTE-NSU1sYTynXBKw):
  1. Click **Profile,** or select your profile picture to go to your profile.
  2. Select the reel you’d like to boost, then select **Boost**.
  3. Fill in the details of your ad by selecting a **Goal** (what results you'd like from this ad), **Audience** (who you want to reach), **Budget** (how much you want to spend daily) and **Duration** (how long you want your ad to run).
  4. To complete your ad, select **Boost reel**.


Once you select **Boost post** , your ad will be submitted for review to make sure it meets our [ad policies](https://www.facebook.com/policies/ads). You'll receive a notification after you submit an ad for review, when it's being reviewed, when it's approved or disapproved and when the ad ends. Your ad will begin running after it's been reviewed and approved.
