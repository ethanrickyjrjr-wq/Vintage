# Business verification
Updated: Oct 14, 2026
Business Verification confirms your organization as a registered legal business entity. Choose this path if you have your business documentation (such as incorporation certificates or business licenses) readily available. If you’re an independent developer or a small studio without formal business registration, [Admin verification](https://developers.meta.com/vr/resources/publish-organization-verification-admin/) is likely the better option.
For an overview of both verification paths, see [Verify your organization](https://developers.meta.com/vr/resources/publish-organization-verification/).
## Verify your business
  1. Open your browser and navigate to the [Developer Dashboard⁠](https://developers.meta.com/vr/manage/).
  2. From the left-side navigation, click **Team** , and then click **Org verification**.
  3. Scroll down and click **Start verification** under **Business verification**.
  4. Read and follow the instructions in the **Verify your business** modal. When you’re ready to continue, click **Get started**.
  5. Choose the country where your business is located, and then click **Next**.
  6. Add the required business details including name, address, and phone number, as well as any optional details you’d like to provide like website and tax ID. When you’re finished, click **Next**.
  7. Select the correct business from the list of businesses generated from public records, or select **My business isn’t listed** if you don’t see your organization on the list.
  8. Review your business details and click **Next**.
  9. **Select a contact method** and choose how you want to receive your verification code, either through email, phone call, or text message (SMS).
  10. Enter your verification code.
  11. Click **Submit**.


Once you submit your verification, you’ll receive a notification when the review is complete. Most verifications are processed within 48 hours, though some may take longer.
## Accepted document types
When completing business verification, you may need to upload documentation that confirms your business identity. The following document types are generally accepted:
  * Business registration or incorporation certificate
  * Business license
  * Tax registration certificate
  * Articles of incorporation or organization
  * Partnership agreement (for partnerships)
  * Utility bill or bank statement showing the business name and address (as supplementary documentation)


## File format requirements
  * Accepted formats: PDF, PNG, JPG, or JPEG
  * Maximum file size: 10 MB per document
  * Documents must be legible with all text readable
  * Documents must not be expired


## Country-specific requirements
Document requirements vary by country. In some regions, you may need to provide additional documentation such as:
  * **European Union** : VAT registration certificate or chamber of commerce registration
  * **United States** : EIN confirmation letter (IRS Letter 147C or CP 575) or state business registration
  * **United Kingdom** : Companies House registration or HMRC documentation
  * **India** : GST registration certificate, certificate of incorporation, or PAN card
  * **Brazil** : CNPJ registration


If your country is not listed above, provide the most relevant official business registration document issued by your government.
## Common rejection reasons
If your business verification is rejected, check for the following common issues:
  * **Document mismatch** : The business name on your uploaded document does not match the business name on your developer account.
  * **Expired documents** : The document you uploaded has expired. Provide a current, valid document.
  * **Illegible uploads** : The document image is blurry, cropped, or too low resolution to read. Upload a clear, full-page scan or photo.
  * **Unsupported document type** : The uploaded document is not an official business registration document (for example, a business card or marketing material).
  * **Incomplete information** : The document does not show all required information (business name, registration number, or address).


If your verification is rejected, you can resubmit with corrected documentation from the **Org verification** page in the Developer Dashboard.
