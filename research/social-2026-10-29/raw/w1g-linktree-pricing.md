## Frequently asked questions
What is a Linktree and why do I need one?
Linktree gives you one powerful link to share everything you do, offer, and care about — all in one place. Whether you’re a creator, brand, business, or just starting out, Linktree helps you easily connect your followers, fans and customers to your content, products, services, and socials in seconds.  
  
You can add your unique URL to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, or even your email signature and print materials using a QR code to easily send people to your Linktree.  
  
With over 70 million users, Linktree is the most trusted link in bio platform.
Can I customise my Linktree?
Please! Customize everything! Fonts, colors, layouts, backgrounds, you name it. Create your Linktree as a true reflection of your personal or business brand.  
  
You’ll find beautiful layouts and templates to get you started straight away, and always optimized for your success.
Can I make money with a Linktree?
Yes! It’s easier than ever to earn income with Linktree’s suite of monetisation tools.  
  
Once you’ve set up your Linktree you’ll have access to:  
  
**Sell Digital Products** like Online Courses, Digital Downloads (think guides, eBooks, music files, lead magnets and endless ideas), paid access into Private Communities and paid communities (currently US only) directly via Linktree, no website or extra tools needed!  
  
**Earn Affiliate Commission** by adding your own affiliate links, or add from our curated affiliate marketplace. Currently US only.  
  
**Sales Funnel** — Use Linktree to build a simple, effective sales funnel by combining email collection, sponsored links, and product links.
What tools are included with Linktree to grow and engage my audience?
Linktree is far beyond a link in bio, with us you get an entire toolkit to build and grow your brand, business and audience, including:  
  
**Automating your Instagram** replies to comments and triggering DM’s so you never miss a chance to connect or convert a fan.  
  
**Plan and schedule your social media** content across all leading networks.  
  
**AI-powered** idea generator and caption writer to keep your social media on track.  
  
**Collect leads directly** into your email CRM with a growing library of partner integrations.  
  
**Unlock detailed analytics** _and way more._
Is Linktree safe to use on all social media platforms?
Absolutely, yes. Linktree is trusted by Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and more — and is even used in their official profiles. The Linktr.ee domain is safe, recognizable, and built for social, giving your audience confidence to click.
Does Linktree charge transaction fees?
Yes, and we’re proud of how competitive they are.  
  
At Linktree, we believe creators should keep more of what they earn. That’s why our transaction fees are among the lowest in the industry. For example, we currently only charge USD$0.25 per payout and constantly looking for ways to pass on more profit to our creators.  
  
While many platforms take a significant cut, we’re committed to market-leading transparency and flexibility.
Do I need a website to use Linktree?
Not at all! In fact, we’re so confident in our product we can’t wait to see how you could use Linktree as your website replacement, giving you everything you need to share, sell, and grow — without hiring a developer or spending hours (and spending money!) updating a traditional website.
