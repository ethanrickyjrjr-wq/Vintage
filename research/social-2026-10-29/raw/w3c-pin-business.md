Convert your personal account into a business account
You can convert your personal account to a business account. Business accounts are public on Pinterest, so if your personal account is set to private, it must be switched to public in order to convert to a business account. Your Pins and followers will remain the same. You can switch back to a personal account at any time.
If your personal account is linked to another Pinterest account, you’ll need to unlink your account before you can convert it to a business account.
