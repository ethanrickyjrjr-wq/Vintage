#  Selling on Depop 
You’ve sorted through your closet and decided what you want to sell. So what now?
Here’s how to turn your clothes into cash!
## **Listing your items**
To increase your chances of selling:
  * Include a clear item description, accurate brand names and photos that accurately represent your item
  * Describe and include photos of any faults
  * Double-check your items are authentic, and include photos of any labels and tags
  * Always use original photos — your listing will be removed if you use stock images


## **What to do once you’ve sold**
We’ll notify you by email and in the app once you sell.
To see all your sold items, head to **My Depop** and click the **Sold** tab. On web, receipts can be found in the **Selling Hub** under **Sold.**
Then it’s up to you to package up your item and send it on to your buyer — always ship as soon as you can to increase your chances of receiving a 5-star review!
For more info on shipping your items, check out our guides:
  * [How to ship - US](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/16523051318033-How-to-ship-US)
  * [How to ship - UK](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/16523025929617-How-to-ship-UK)
  * [How to ship - Worldwide](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001771988-How-to-ship-Worldwide)


## **Getting paid**
You’ll get paid as soon as your sale is delivered to the buyer, but exact timings can vary depending on a few factors, including the payment method you signed up to sell with.
For more info on getting paid, check out our guides:
  * [How do I get paid? - US](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/9057092334865-How-do-I-get-paid-US)
  * [How do I get paid? - UK](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791147-How-do-I-get-paid-UK)
  * [How do I get paid? - AU](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/29758489111185-How-do-I-get-paid-AU)


## **Reviews**
Reviews are a key part of Depop and help to inform and build trust for both buyers and sellers.
We recommend leaving a review for your buyer once the purchase is complete. This not only helps build trust with other buyers, but also encourages your buyer to leave you a review in return.
## **How to stay safe when selling**
Here’s how to stay protected:
  * Only sell your items directly through the Depop app or [Depop.com](http://depop.com)
  * Only list items that comply with our [Terms of Service](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001773148-Terms-of-Service)
  * Use Depop Shipping, or if you ship your own way, always choose a tracked shipping method so you and the buyer are protected if something goes wrong
  * Never follow links outside of Depop unless you trust them
  * Never give out your personal information to another user, or include it in your bio or listing descriptions. Depop will never ask you to confirm your personal details to complete a sale.


If you receive a [suspicious message from another user](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360048480773-I-ve-received-a-suspicious-message-from-another-user-or-a-suspicious-email-claiming-to-be-Depop), report any issues or concerns to Depop Support.
