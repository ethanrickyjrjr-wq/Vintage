Who is it for? Canva Pro is for individual creators and solo business owners. Only one person can hold a Canva Pro subscription.
How much does it cost? This subscription incurs a flat monthly fee of US$12/month per person billed yearly (US$144/year), or US$18 month-to-month. Prices shown are for the US; for your region, see [canva.com/pricing⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.com/pricing). You can try Canva Pro for free with a 30-day trial.
This plan makes professional design easy for individuals and small business owners. Packed with powerful design features, unlimited premium content, a Brand Kit and AI-powered tools, [Canva Pro⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.com/pro/) is perfect for a one-person powerhouse looking to save time.
Here’s how:
Everything in the free plan, plus:
  * Unlimited premium content: Craft high-quality content across all your channels with unlimited access to [100+ million photos, templates, graphics, and videos⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.com/pro/premium-content/). 
  * Build and scale your brand: Easily apply your brand to any design with up to 5 [Brand Kits⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.com/pro/brand-kit/) and generate on-brand copy with [Canva AI⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.com/canva-ai/).
  * Boost your productivity with AI: Work smarter with [AI-powered design tools⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.com/magic/) that instantly expand, resize, and remove backgrounds from your images in a click.


Working solo? Magic Video makes editing social videos feel effortless and all watermark-free. Just drop in your clips, add a quick prompt, and AI does the rest.

## Frequently Asked Questions
### 1. What is the difference between Canva Pro, Canva Business, and Canva Enterprise?
Canva Pro is a single-user plan for individual creators. Canva Business adds brand management, team collaboration and marketing tools for small teams. Canva Enterprise adds multi-team governance, advanced security and custom integrations for large organizations with 100 or more employees.
### 2. Who is Canva Pro designed for?
Canva Pro is designed for individual creators and solo business owners. It includes unlimited premium content, Brand Kit access and AI-powered design tools, but only one person can hold the subscription.
### 3. Who is Canva Business designed for?
Canva Business is designed for small businesses working solo or in small teams. It combines Canva Pro's features with Canva Grow ad insights, up to 100 Brand Kits, 500GB of storage, and real-time collaboration and approval workflows.
### 4. Who is Canva Enterprise designed for?
Canva Enterprise is designed for large organizations with multiple teams, typically 100 or more employees, that need cross-functional access across marketing, sales, HR and communications, along with enterprise-grade security and governance.
### 5. What security features does Canva Enterprise include that Pro and Business don't?
Canva Enterprise includes SSO, SCIM, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II certifications, advanced admin controls, audit logs, data residency options and AI governance controls, none of which are available on Canva Pro or Canva Business.
### 6. Does Canva Enterprise integrate with other business tools?
Yes. Canva Enterprise supports API access, two-way DAM sync with Bynder, Widen and Brandfolder, native Salesforce and HubSpot apps, and connections to marketing automation platforms like Marketo and Eloqua.
### 7. How many Brand Kits can each Canva plan create? 
Canva Business supports up to 100 Brand Kits. Canva Enterprise supports up to 1,000 Brand Kits with multi-level approvals and centralized asset management across departments.
### 8. How much does Canva Enterprise cost?
Canva Enterprise uses custom pricing based on an organization's size and needs. Teams should contact Canva Sales at canva.com/contact-sales for a quote, while Canva Pro and Canva Business use flat monthly and per-person pricing.
Also, canva.com/contact-sales applies for companies that are a Canva Business fit yet have larger scale or more complex buying needs—such as procurement, invoicing, or a higher number of licences.
### 9. Can a small business use Canva Enterprise instead of Canva Business?
It's possible, but Canva Business is built for small to medium teams that need brand consistency and collaboration without complex procurement or security requirements. Canva Enterprise suits larger, multi-team organizations with dedicated IT and security needs.
### 10. Does Canva Enterprise reduce production costs and time?
Canva Enterprise customers have reported measurable gains. Salesforce, for example, reduced its cost per design by 66% and increased design production by 63% after adopting Canva Enterprise, using Brand Kit, approvals and Magic Switch resizing. Source: canva.com/case-studies/salesforce/.
### 11. How do I move from Canva Business to Canva Enterprise?
Contact Canva Sales at canva.com/contact-sales to discuss your organization's size and needs. Canva's enterprise onboarding includes a dedicated customer success manager and professional services for a tailored implementation.
### 12. How much does Canva cost per month?
Canva Pro costs US$12/month per person billed yearly, or US$18 month-to-month. Canva Business costs US$20.83/month per person billed yearly, or US$25 month-to-month. Canva Enterprise is custom-priced per organization. All are US pricing; see canva.com/pricing for your region.
### 13. Is Canva Premium the same as Canva Pro?
Yes. "Canva Premium" isn't an official plan name, but it usually refers to Canva Pro, the single-user paid plan. Larger organizations looking for a premium plan for many teams should compare Canva Business and Canva Enterprise.
### 14. What happened to Canva for Teams?
Canva for Teams became Canva Business. The plan is priced per person (US$20.83/month billed yearly) and adds brand controls, approval workflows and team collaboration on top of everything in Canva Pro.
### 15. Why pay for Canva Pro when Canva Free is so good?
Short answer: Canva Pro isn’t expensive, it’s bundled. At US$144 a year (that’s $12 a month billed annually), it replaces three or four tools most creators already pay for separately.
Canva Free is, honestly, one of the best freemium products in the world. Millions of people design beautifully without ever paying, and that’s by design. You get real templates, full editing and 5GB of storage, free forever. For plenty of people, that genuinely is enough.
Pro is for when design becomes part of how you work, not a one-off. The upgrade buys you:
  * The full premium library: 141M+ photos, videos, graphics and audio, plus 3.6M+ templates (vs ~1.6M on Free)
  * 5 Brand Kits with your logos, fonts and colours, so everything stays on-brand automatically (vs 1 Brand kit and 3 colours only on Free)
  * The real time-savers: Magic Resize, Background Remover, one-click transparent and SVG exports, and the full AI tool suite
  * 100GB of cloud storage (vs 5GB) and built-in social scheduling


### 16. How can I get Canva at a lower price?
Billing yearly cuts Canva Pro from US$18 to US$12/month, a 33% saving; and 16% savings for Canva Business.
### 17. Do team members have to pay for Canva?
On Canva Business, each member needs a paid seat, priced per person. People on the Free plan can still collaborate on designs shared with them. However, Free users will only have access to features available on their plan, so some premium elements or tools may not be editable for them.
### 18. Does Canva Business have unlimited AI?
Every plan includes a monthly AI allowance that resets each month, and higher plans get more: Canva Free gets up to 20 Standard or Premium AI uses; Canva Pro includes about 10x more AI(including Premium or Ultra) than Canva Free, and Canva Business and Enterprise include about 20x more. If you need extra, Pro and Business offer an AI Pass add-on and top-ups. The exact number of uses depends on which AI tools you use (Standard, Premium or Ultra).
