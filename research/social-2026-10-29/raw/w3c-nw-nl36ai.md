## Features
  * **Professional LED Soft Lighting-** With 36 high brightness LEDs (18 warm light LEDs and 18 white light LEDs), a 120° beam angle, a maximum illuminance of 800 lux at 0.5m, this small portable camera light kit with a soft diffusion panel provides key lighting for streamer live streaming, podcast videography, makeup artists selfies, taking studio photos, photoshoots, filming, games, content creator essential and influencer must have for YouTube TikTok videos, vlogs, portrait, product photography
  * **Adjustable Brightness & Bi-Color Temperature-** 10 adjustable brightness levels (10%~100%), a high CRI of 97+, and adjustable color temperature of 3200K (warm white), 4100K (mixed color) or 5600K (cool white). The NEEWER LED light provides you with accurate color replication, and a wide range of brightness adjustment
  * **8 Scene Modes-** There are 8 scene modes to meet different photography needs, including Lightning 1, Lightning 2, Lightning 3, Flash, Flash-Conference, Flash-Paparazzi, TV, and SOS. It is suitable for use as a short-distance fill light and is most effective when used within 2m
  * **Built-In Lithium Battery & Type-C Port- **Built-in 2000mAh rechargeable lithium battery - fully charged within 2.6 hours. After a full charge, the continuous lighting time is up to 2.6 hours (at 100% brightness). It also features an USB Type-C port for convenient charging
  * **Multi-Combination & Easy to Carry-** Measuring 3.1" x 2.6" x 1.18" / 7.87 x 6.6 x 0.3cm, the mini LED light is easy to carry around. A cold shoe mount adapter with 1/4" female thread on the bottom allows the lighting prop to be mounted on cameras or other equipment or studio gears including tripods, compatible with Sony Canon Nikon Fujifilm DSLR cameras. 3 cold shoe mounts on both sides and the top allow you to attach microphones, fill lights, and other photography accessories
