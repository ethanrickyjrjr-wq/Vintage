Update on 6/10/2026 at 9am PT
## Your Algorithm Is Now Available Across All of Instagram
Your Algorithm is now live across all of Instagram, including Feed, Reels, and Explore. This expansion gives people more ways to take control of their experience globally.
### How it works
You can access Your Algorithm by navigating to Reels or Explore and tapping the icon in the upper right corner (the two lines with hearts). Your Algorithm is one unified system — so when you tell us what topics you want to see more or less of, your recommendations update everywhere: across Feed, Reels, and Explore. No need to adjust each one separately.
• **See what's shaping your Algorithm** – View an AI-generated summary of the topics influencing the posts and recommendations you see.
• **Adjust your interests** – Tell us what you want to see more or less of, or add new topics entirely.
• **See what’s being recommended** – When a post in your feed was recommended based on one of your Your Algorithm interests, you'll see a label like "From Running" or "From Street Style" right on the post — you can click on the label to see more related content or to update your preferences as your tastes evolve.

## Your Algorithm Expands to Explore
In December we announced ‘Your Algorithm,’ a new way to see and control the topics driving your Reels recommendations. Starting today, ‘Your Algorithm’ extends beyond Reels to the Explore surface.
Just like on Reels, you can view the topics Instagram thinks you’re into and easily adjust them to see more or less of what you want. You can…
**1. See your top interests:** View a summary of the topics Instagram thinks you care about most, right at the top.
**2. Tune your preferences:** Type in the topics you want to see more or less of, and your Reels and Explore content will reflect your choices. It’s all one system, so changes you make in Reels or Explore carry across both. One algorithm, reflected across surfaces.
**3. Share your algorithm:** If you’d like, you can share your interests to your story, so friends and followers can see what you’re into.
**4. Deep dive on topics of interest:** On Explore, you can add or remove interests directly from the topic pills at the top of the page, making it easy to dive deeper into your interests.
To get started, look for the icon in the upper right corner of Explore or Reels (two lines with hearts) and tap it to open Your Algorithm.

Posted on December 10, 2025
Today, we’re excited to announce a new way to control your Instagram experience, starting with the Reels tab. Your interests change – and now using AI, your algorithm can change with you.
Instagram has always been a place to dive deep into your interests and connect with friends. As your interests evolve over time, we want to give you more meaningful ways to control what you see on Instagram, starting with Reels. Using AI, you can now more easily view and personalize the topics that shape your Reels, making recommendations feel even more tailored to you.
## See and Control your Reels Algorithm
When you’re watching Reels, look for the icon in the upper right corner (two lines with hearts). Tap it to open Your Algorithm. You can see what topics we think you’re interested in, tell us which topics you want to see more or less of, and your recommendations will adapt.
**1. See your top interests:** View a summary of the topics Instagram thinks you care about most, right at the top.
**2. Tune your preferences:** Type in the topics you want to see more or less of, and your Reels will reflect your choices.
**3. Share your algorithm:** If you’d like, you can share your interests to your Story, so friends and followers can see what you’re into.
You can also access Your Algorithm by navigating to Content preferences and tapping on Your algorithm within Settings and activity.
Instagram is leading the way in giving you more control, so your Reels feel relevant and made for you. We’re starting with the Reels tab, and are planning to bring this ability to Explore, and more places in the app soon.
![](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=1177047857736404&version=1781032399&transcode_extension=webp)
This new feature is available starting today in the US and rolling out globally in English soon. We’re still early in this journey, and will keep refining and improving the experience over time.
