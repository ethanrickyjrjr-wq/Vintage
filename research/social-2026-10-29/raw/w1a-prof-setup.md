You can change your personal Instagram account to a business or creator account.
**Business accounts** are best for retailers, local businesses, brands, organizations and service providers. **Creator accounts** are best for public figures, content producers, artists and influencers. Setting up a professional business or creator account gives you access to features to help you grow your account, monetize, create ads, understand your growth, and manage your messages.
You can switch back to a personal account, or change your professional account type anytime. **Note:** If your personal account is private, switching to a professional account will make it public. All pending follow requests will be automatically accepted when you go public. Learn more [about professional accounts on Instagram](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F138925576505882%2F%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUDobpsTIT_QOkyk7k6kHT_Dr2ZkdArabHLRCydaAhImJaTWvROPeZZU3UdhM8vowX66Cj-zndWnpkCskTJ7g3MLLjYaFm1LGIeONu2pGeXJNZJAJIj2kHTnTiz16IyFPk9ntkqLhNXRDQ).
## Before you begin
  * To set up a professional account, you’ll need to [create an Instagram account](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F155940534568753%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUDobpsTIT_QOkyk7k6kHT_Dr2ZkdArabHLRCydaAhImJaTWvROPeZZU3UdhM8vowX66Cj-zndWnpkCskTJ7g3MLLjYaFm1LGIeONu2pGeXJNZJAJIj2kHTnTiz16IyFPk9ntkqLhNXRDQ) or use an existing Instagram account.
  * You can have [up to 10 Instagram accounts logged in at once](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F1696686240613595%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUDobpsTIT_QOkyk7k6kHT_Dr2ZkdArabHLRCydaAhImJaTWvROPeZZU3UdhM8vowX66Cj-zndWnpkCskTJ7g3MLLjYaFm1LGIeONu2pGeXJNZJAJIj2kHTnTiz16IyFPk9ntkqLhNXRDQ).


## Set up a professional account on Instagram
To set up your professional account from the Instagram app:
  1. Tap ![](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=859348803194577&version=1792520827)**Profile** or your profile picture in the bottom right to go to your profile.
  2. Tap ![](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=1587888968861065&version=1792520827)**More** to go to your **Settings and activity**. 
  3. Below **For professionals** , tap **Account type and tools**. 
  4. Select **Switch to professional account.**
  5. Select your category. You can choose a category that best describes what you do.  
**Note:** The category you choose does not determine whether you’ll set up a business or creator account.
  6. Tap **Switch to professional account**.
  7. To set up a creator account, choose **Creator**.   
To set up a business account, choose **Business**.
  8. Tap **Next**.
  9. If you’re setting up a creator account, complete the optional steps to set up your profile. To skip this step, tap **X** on the top right corner to return to your profile.


You now have access to the [professional dashboard](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F257516379077270%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUDobpsTIT_QOkyk7k6kHT_Dr2ZkdArabHLRCydaAhImJaTWvROPeZZU3UdhM8vowX66Cj-zndWnpkCskTJ7g3MLLjYaFm1LGIeONu2pGeXJNZJAJIj2kHTnTiz16IyFPk9ntkqLhNXRDQ) to see your insights, start creating ads, and view your eligibility to use monetization tools on Instagram.
Continue to follow the steps below to finish setting up your business or creator account:
  1. Add contact details for your business and tap **Next**.   
To skip this step, tap **Don’t use my contact info**.
  2. To connect to an existing Facebook Page, tap **Login to Facebook**. This step is optional and will make it easier to share Instagram posts on Facebook and use connected experiences such as ads and shopping tools.  
To skip this step, tap **Skip**.
  3. To display or hide business information on your profile, go to your profile and tap **Edit profile**. Go to **Profile display** below **Public business information** to choose whether you want to hide or display your category label and contact info. Then, tap **Done**.


To set up a professional account on [instagram.com](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2F&h=AUDobpsTIT_QOkyk7k6kHT_Dr2ZkdArabHLRCydaAhImJaTWvROPeZZU3UdhM8vowX66Cj-zndWnpkCskTJ7g3MLLjYaFm1LGIeONu2pGeXJNZJAJIj2kHTnTiz16IyFPk9ntkqLhNXRDQ): 
  1. Click ![](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=1587888968861065&version=1792520827)**More** , then select **Settings**.
  2. Below **For professionals** , select **Account type and tools**.
  3. Click **Switch to professional account**.
  4. To set up a creator account, choose **Creator**.   
To set up a business account, choose **Business**.
  5. Click **Next**.
  6. Select your category. You can choose a category that best describes what you do.  
**Note:** The category you choose does not determine whether you’ll set up a business or creator account.
  7. Click **Done**. You’ll need to confirm that you want to switch to a professional account by clicking **Continue**.


You now have access to the [professional dashboard](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F257516379077270%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUDobpsTIT_QOkyk7k6kHT_Dr2ZkdArabHLRCydaAhImJaTWvROPeZZU3UdhM8vowX66Cj-zndWnpkCskTJ7g3MLLjYaFm1LGIeONu2pGeXJNZJAJIj2kHTnTiz16IyFPk9ntkqLhNXRDQ) to see your insights or create and manage ads.
Continue to follow the steps below to finish setting up your business or creator account:
  1. Add contact details for your business and click **Save**.   
To skip this step, select **Don’t use my contact info**.
  2. Click **Done** to return to your profile.


You can choose what information is displayed on your profile at any time. To display or hide business information on your profile:
  1. Click ![](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=1587888968861065&version=1792520827)**More** , then select **Settings**.
  2. In the left-hand menu below **For professionals** , click **Professional account**.
  3. Click the checkbox next to **Display category label** to hide or display it.
  4. Enter any business contact info you want displayed in your profile below **Public business information**.
  5. Click the checkbox next to **Display contact info**.
  6. Click **Submit**.


## Switch between account types on Instagram
You can switch between account types at any time.
To switch your account type from the Instagram app:
  1. Tap ![](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=859348803194577&version=1792520827)**Profile** or your profile picture in the bottom right to go to your profile.
  2. Tap ![](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=1587888968861065&version=1792520827)**More** to go to your **Settings and activity**.
  3. Tap **Account type and tools** , then tap **Account type**.
  4. Tap **Switch account type**.
  5. Tap to choose the type of account you want to switch to.
  6. Tap **Switch**.


To switch your account type on [instagram.com](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2F&h=AUDobpsTIT_QOkyk7k6kHT_Dr2ZkdArabHLRCydaAhImJaTWvROPeZZU3UdhM8vowX66Cj-zndWnpkCskTJ7g3MLLjYaFm1LGIeONu2pGeXJNZJAJIj2kHTnTiz16IyFPk9ntkqLhNXRDQ): 
  1. Click ![](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=1587888968861065&version=1792520827)**More** , then select **Settings**.
  2. If you have a creator account, select **Creator tools and controls**. If you have a business account, select **Business tools and controls**.
  3. Click **Switch account type**.
  4. Choose the type of account you want to switch to, then click **Switch**.


**Note:** If you switch from a professional account to a personal account, you’ll no longer have access to ads and may lose access to select tools. Any Facebook Page connected to your professional account will be disconnected.
