About this vendor
With a decade of experience offering wedding rentals in South Florida, Mi Vintage strives to provide high quality pieces at affordable prices along with the best customer service our clients deserve! We specialize in farmhouse, boho, modern-vintage, eclectic and glam décor. Our ever-growing collection ranges from handcrafted farm tables to curated lounges and unique heirlooms you won't find anywhere else. We are proud to say that not only are we a Hispanic female owned company, but we love supporting other #bossbabes we meet along the way. We can't wait to help you get the party started! We are located in Miami Dade County, home of the cafecito and pastelitos! We service weddings and events from Miami to West Palm Beach and the Florida Keys area. If you have an event outside of our service area, send us an email anyways, we love traveling and may be able to accommodate delivery for your special celebration! Our rental collection includes: Farm tables Lounge furniture Bars + Bar backs Chairs Backdrops Arbors Carts Furnishings
