## Frequently asked questions
  * ### What are media hours and AI credits?
![](https://static-cdn.descript.com/descript-website/images/chevron-down-light.svg)
Media hours track uploaded media files and media recorded in Descript, regardless of whether they're transcribed (though you still have the option to transcribe them). AI credits track your usage of AI features like Underlord, Studio Sound, Green Screen, Eye Contact, and AI-generated media and avatars.
  * ### I’m interested in setting up an Enterprise plan for my business. Where can I learn more?
![](https://static-cdn.descript.com/descript-website/images/chevron-down-light.svg)
[Contact sales](https://www.descript.com/contact) to learn more about our Enterprise plans.
  * ### Do I need to enter a credit card to try Descript?
![](https://static-cdn.descript.com/descript-website/images/chevron-down-light.svg)
No, Descript's Free plan includes everything you need to record, edit and mix audio and video content. 
  * ### How are subscription payments processed?
![](https://static-cdn.descript.com/descript-website/images/chevron-down-light.svg)
Descript Hobbyist, Creator, and Business plan subscriptions are processed automatically to the credit card you provide, according to your selected billing cycle. Users requiring custom invoicing options (such as ACH transfer) can reach out to our [Enterprise team](https://www.descript.com/enterprise) to learn more.
  * ### Do you offer special rates for students, educators, and non-profits?
![](https://static-cdn.descript.com/descript-website/images/chevron-down-light.svg)
Yes, fill out [this form](https://descript.notion.site/e677e85ecdec4c87ba2f3a5644895e9f)
  * ### How does Descript protect my privacy?
![](https://static-cdn.descript.com/descript-website/images/chevron-down-light.svg)
Your Project Information is confidential, even from Descript. We are SOC 2 Type II compliant. Read more about [Descript Security](https://www.descript.com/security).
  * ### How do I cancel my subscription?
![](https://static-cdn.descript.com/descript-website/images/chevron-down-light.svg)
You can easily cancel your subscription from your [Descript subscription page](https://web.descript.com/subscription). You can also delete your account and permanently wipe your data from our servers.
