# Winter Holidays Data and Trends
The latest insights on consumer spending for the holiday season
NRF’s holiday surveys gauge consumer sentiment and shopping trends during the holiday season, including Thanksgiving, Black Friday, Small Business Saturday, Cyber Monday, Super Saturday and Christmas. Check out NRF's [Winter Holidays FAQs](https://nrf.com/research-insights/holiday-data-and-trends/winter-holidays/winter-holiday-faqs) and our Winter Holidays historical highlights for more information on the latest holiday season and years past. 
_Stay tuned for more 2026 holiday insights throughout the winter holiday season._
## 2026 Winter Holiday Spending Highlights
  * NRF predicts retail sales in November and December will grow 4.2% over 2025, equating to total spending of $1.013 trillion
  * 43% of holiday shoppers plan to start before November
  * 56% of holiday shoppers say the state of the economy will affect their holiday spending plans this year
  * 47% of holiday shoppers say they would like to receive gift cards this holiday season followed by clothing (44%), books and other media (25%) and personal care or beauty items (24%)
