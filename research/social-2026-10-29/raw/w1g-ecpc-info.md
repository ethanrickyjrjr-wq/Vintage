**East Coast Prop Co.** is a prop rental service based in Atlanta, GA serving the needs of commercial prop stylists and content creators. We focus on contemporary and on-trend vintage props as well as photoshoot basics such as glassware, tableware, faux plants, books, tech, cleared art and more.
We aim to share resources to promote sustainability within our industry. Our inventory continues to grow with your contributions via donations, selling or sub-renting.
We are strictly for the styling industry, so you must register before you rent.
