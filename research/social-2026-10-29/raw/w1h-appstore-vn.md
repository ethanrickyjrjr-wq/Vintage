  * ### Good app, but some suggestions
10/31/2024
🦈🦑🦖
I came to this app from using CapCut for a while now, and I think it’s a decently good app. It’s mostly free, and you don’t have to pay for a lot of the different stickers and things like that. Just one issue: I mainly use video editing software for making edits with fancy transitions, altering the music in the background, and a bunch of other techniques, and I found that this app doesn’t exactly have a lot of options regarding that type of work. A few recommendations would be to add more ways to edit the audio, like filters and different ways you could change the sound, as well as transitions for the stickers themselves instead of just an abrupt cut of them off the screen. It would also be nice to have more ways to change the videos or pictures in a project, like for example, the way that CapCut allows you to add little bursts of fire or starbursts to introduce a new clip. The transitions between the clips right now are okay, but if you could make them in a way where you can move them around the project as if it were a sticker, that would be helpful. Also, I’d really like to have a drawing tool of some sort, I keep having to switch between apps just to get a drawing tool, and it’s really annoying. A drawing tool would improve this app drastically. There’s another thing, more animated stickers and animated frames. I feel that more moving parts to add to projects would really make this app great.
