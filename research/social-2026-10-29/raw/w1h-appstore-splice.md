  * ### my favorite video app by far
06/14/2019
Thegirly
I’ve had this app for at least five years and it has never let me down. It’s very easy to experiment with, and has a down to earth format that can do some pretty great things considering the other apps in this genre. The audio settings, whether that be little sound effects or songs in numerous different genres, are all amazing. I can simply drag and slide to whatever time I want, and can crop sounds and even extend them if I want, as well. There is even an option to layer these and do a voice over if wanted—a very generous format for a video-maker’s creativity. I love the various video effects: Ken Burns motion, text effects, video speed, cropping, filters, and much more. Landscape or Portrait settings, transitions, are easy to use as well. All in all, I use this app to make 30 second videos to 5:00 videos. It’s good for all of it. I appreciate that it doesn’t rub it’s watermark all over the video. Splice gives you the option to turn on or off the watermark at the end of the video. It differentiates itself from other video apps in this way—and it inspires me to tell others about this app because they’ll ask what I used. I wish they would put more of the effects they already have though. I have never come across an instance that I needed any other effects, especially with the new update that the app has. The “split” option on the video editing is soooo nice. letting me crop videos but also leaving the aftermath of the video intact incase I wanted to do something with that part as well.

  * ### Great features especially to animate photos except for music
04/17/2020
07Raven
I especially liked using the Ken Burns effect on still photos which worked much, much better - is far more versatile - and easier than its counterpart in iMovie. It’s relatively also easy to splice film clips together, too. Biggest complaint with upgrades made since summer 2019 is the loss of some of the holiday music (I had a hard time doing holiday video last Christmas) and other suitable music to use, and the loss of the feature which let you splice the free music together easily with fade-out options available at beginning and end. Also, most music supplied is too short (less than a minute) - my videos typically are 1-1/2 to 2-3/4 minutes in length. I now rarely use Splice’s music and have to import video clips I do here into iMovie or some other program to finish. Other complaint is not having special features with how text or titles displays such as the ability to have letters dropping down, or fading in and out, etc., like text features available on iMovie. However, I do like the colored text/type choices which can make text much easier to read on bright backgrounds. I also like the ease in selecting aspect ratio although it is limited to only 3 choices. Overall this is a nice, fairly easy to use editing package. I use it the most for animating static photos so they zoom in or out, move from side to side or even - on rare occasion - rotate, which is especially cool if done right.
