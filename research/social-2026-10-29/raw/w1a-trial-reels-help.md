Trial reels on Instagram let you experiment with new ideas and get early feedback on whether your content resonates by allowing you to share reels with accounts that don’t follow you. You can choose to share a trial reel with your followers at any time.
## Eligibility criteria
Before you can begin to use trial reels, you’ll need a public Instagram account. Depending on the type of account you have, you’ll need to have a minimum number of followers:
  * If you have a **professional (business or creator) account** , you must have at least 200 followers. 
  * If you have a **personal account** , you must have at least 1,000 followers.


**Note:** If your public account is ineligible to appear in recommendations, you will also lose access to trial reels. Your account will regain access to trial reels once it’s eligible to appear in recommendations again and meets all other above eligibility criteria. To see if your account has any limits to its reach, go to [Account Status](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F338481628002750%2F%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUB_A_44nnVMtVRB_FmOwnNQKVEli8cTUUik6cNPAwwflnuju2NvTGesiqEmEBzVa0NKC25DJ3vLlesMbI88I4ZnLWhW8P_tZ_oW4Vqyrx-3cVUO_TbZH0DEqnlev5Is4B98BLi6YWLeWA) in your settings. 
## Share trial reels on Instagram
To share your trial reel on Instagram:
  1. Follow the steps to [record a reel.](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F2720958398006062%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUB_A_44nnVMtVRB_FmOwnNQKVEli8cTUUik6cNPAwwflnuju2NvTGesiqEmEBzVa0NKC25DJ3vLlesMbI88I4ZnLWhW8P_tZ_oW4Vqyrx-3cVUO_TbZH0DEqnlev5Is4B98BLi6YWLeWA)
**Note:** Make sure to create a new reel. Your trial reel may get limited reach if Instagram detects that you’ve previously shared the same content.
  2. Before you share your reel, tap **Trial**.
  3. Tap **Next** , then tap **Share**.


After you share your trial reel, your followers won’t see it in feed or Reels. Trial reels don’t appear in your profile unless you decide to share them to everyone.
**Note:** Some followers may still see your reel in other places. For example, someone might see your trial reel if it’s shared with them in a direct message, on a page that shows reels with the same audio, or in the results of search engines.
You’ll see insights on your trial reel within 24 hours after sharing so you can understand how it is performing.
## Share trial reels to everyone
After you share a trial reel, you can learn more about its performance by viewing insights like **Views** , **Likes** , **Comments** and **Shares**. If your reel is performing well, you can share it with everyone to increase its reach and connect with your followers. 
**Note:** Trial reels may take longer to get views because they’re being shown to people who don’t follow you. Trial reels are evaluated independently in Instagram’s ranking and don’t impact the performance or ranking of your standard reels content. 
To share your trial reel with everyone:
  1. Go to your Instagram profile, then tap ![](https://lookaside.fbsbx.com/elementpath/media/?media_id=1572139717201279&version=1793206480) to see your reels. 
  2. Tap the reel you want to share. 
  3. Tap **View insights**.
  4. Tap **Share to everyone**.


Your reel will now be displayed on the main grid and reels tab of your profile, and may be seen by your followers in their feed and Reels tab.
You can also share trial reels to your followers automatically. When you turn on **Share to everyone automatically** , your reel will automatically be shared if it’s performing well soon after you publish it, based on signals like views and engagement.
**Note:** This setting will be automatically applied to all trial reels you share in the future unless you change it.
To share your trial reels automatically: 
  1. Follow the steps above to record a trial reel.
  2. Below **Trial** , tap **Trial settings**.
  3. Turn on **Share to everyone automatically**.
  4. Tap **Got it** , then tap **Share**.
