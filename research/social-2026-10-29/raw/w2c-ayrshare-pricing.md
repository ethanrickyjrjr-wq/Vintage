## Ayrshare Pricing, Explained Plainly
### 
### Does one customer with multiple social networks count as one profile in Ayrshare pricing?
Yes. Under Ayrshare pricing, a single social profile can include multiple social networks. One customer who connects LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, and X still counts as one profile for billing purposes.
### 
### How does Ayrshare pricing work for multi-location businesses or franchise customers?
Independent posting accounts count as separate profiles under Ayrshare pricing. A franchise with 10 locations managing their own local social media accounts counts as 10 profiles. One corporate account posting from a central brand counts as 1 profile. The rule is simple: if it posts independently, it's its own profile.
### 
### Does Ayrshare charge per user, seat, or team member?
No. Ayrshare pricing is not seat-based. You're not charged for engineers, product managers, support staff, internal users, or dashboard logins. Ayrshare API pricing is based only on active social profiles connected to the platform. Premium is a single-user plan; additional team member logins are available on Launch and above.
### 
### How does Ayrshare pricing compare to other social media APIs and tools?
Most social media management tools charge per user or seat, and many social media APIs charge per connected account or per post. Ayrshare charges per social profile, so one customer with several networks connected counts once. See how these pricing models compare in our guide to [Ayrshare alternatives](https://www.ayrshare.com/compare/).
### 
### Does Ayrshare charge per API call or per post?
No. Every plan includes unlimited API calls and unlimited posts. Ayrshare pricing is based only on the number of active social profiles, so you never pay more for posting more often.
### 
### Can I remove churned, inactive, or test profiles to lower my bill?
Yes. Use the [Delete a User Profile](https://www.ayrshare.com/docs/apis/profiles/delete-profile) API endpoint to remove any profile you no longer need, such as a churned customer or a test account. Deleted profiles stop counting toward your bill. Billing is based on the peak number of active profiles in each monthly cycle, so you only pay for profiles that were actually active.
### 
### Does Ayrshare offer volume discounts for high-profile counts?
Yes. Volume discounts are built into every Ayrshare Business Plan. Pricing drops from $7.99 per profile to $1.99 per profile (annual billing) as you scale beyond 500 profiles. Enterprise agreements can reduce effective Ayrshare pricing to approximately $1 per profile per month at larger volumes.
### 
### Is there a minimum commitment for Ayrshare pricing plans?
Yes. Ayrshare Business Plans require a minimum of 10 or 30 social profiles depending on the plan tier. Enterprise plans include higher minimum commitments in exchange for deeper discounts and additional features, including Max Pack and X/Twitter real-time webhooks.
### 
### Does Ayrshare offer a free trial?
Yes. The Ayrshare Launch plan includes a 28-day free trial. The trial includes full Business Plan access, including Max Pack and premium features, so you can evaluate the Ayrshare Social Media API before choosing a paid plan.
### 
### How do I estimate Ayrshare API pricing for my product?
To estimate Ayrshare API pricing, calculate (1) how many customers, brands, or locations you expect to support and (2) how many of those customers will actively connect social profiles. Then use the pricing calculator above or contact our team for a custom estimate.
