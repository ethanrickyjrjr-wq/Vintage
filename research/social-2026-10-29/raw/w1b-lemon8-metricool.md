Put Instagram, Pinterest, Canva and a splash of [RedNote](https://metricool.com/how-to-use-rednote-app/) in a blender, hit the button, and enjoy your freshly squeezed [**Lemon8**](https://www.lemon8-app.com/). If there’s one word that defines TikTok’s lifestyle-focused sister app, it’s _aesthetics ✨._ Since its U.S. launch in 2023, this photo and video-sharing app has had its ups and downs but managed to build some momentum among creators in the beauty, fashion, wellness, and travel niches. And now with the [TikTok sale](https://metricool.com/tiktok-sale/) done, its future seems stable in the U.S.
## What is Lemon8? 
Think of Lemon8 as the polished, curated alternative to TikTok’s fast-paced video content. While TikTok thrives on spontaneous, raw videos, Lemon8 rewards thoughtful, aesthetically pleasing content that could be straight out of Vanity Fair.
Quick Stats for Social Media Managers:
Launched in Japan (2020), expanded to U.S. and U.K. (February 2023)
**Age requirement:** 13+
**Content formats:** Photo carousels, videos up to 60 seconds, magazine-style posts
**Primary niches:** Beauty, fashion, food, wellness, travel, home, pets
The app first launched in Japan in 2020 and expanded to the U.S. and U.K. in February 2023. It quickly climbed the app store charts, then saw its growth slow after the initial hype. 
To give Lemon8 a second push, ByteDance rolled out a deeper integration with TikTok in late 2024, allowing users to log into Lemon8 using their TikTok account and easily repurpose content between the two platforms.
## TikTok Integration and U.S. Ownership 2026
That TikTok integration? It was just one of two major developments reshaping Lemon8’s future in the U.S. 
The other is the recent change in TikTok’s ownership structure. As TikTok’s sister app, Lemon8 was part of the deal and is now [operated](https://www.lemon8-app.com/legal/supplemental-terms-of-use?region=us) in the United States by [TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC](https://usdsjv.tiktok.com/). 
### 1. Unified TikTok Login (Late 2024)
In late 2024, ByteDance [integrated](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/enhancing-photo-sharing-for-tiktok-creators-with-lemon-8) Lemon8 with TikTok, allowing creators to use one account across both platforms. Sound familiar? It’s the same playbook Meta used when launching Threads alongside Instagram and it worked quite well for gaining new users. 
What this means for your content strategy:
  * Use your existing TikTok credentials to access Lemon8 (no separate account setup required)
  * Repurpose content between platforms without the friction of switching accounts
  * Tap into TikTok’s user base to supercharge Lemon8 discovery
  * Manage both platforms from a single unified login system


### 2. New U.S. Ownership Structure (Early 2026)
And here’s some details you need to know around the new owners. Operations now run through TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC, a majority U.S.-owned entity. ByteDance? They’re just minority shareholders now with no operational control over the U.S. versions of Lemon8, TikTok, or CapCut.
As a result, the platform is more stable now. You can pitch Lemon8 to clients and executives without worrying about another shutdown drama.
## Lemon8 Features: Platform Comparison 
The aesthetics aren’t an accident – Lemon8 positions itself as a lifestyle community app for users who love exploring and sharing content on _fashion, wellness, and travel_. You can either scroll through your **For You** page or dive into dedicated category feeds:
  * Fashion
  * Beauty
  * Food
  * Wellness
  * Travel
  * Home 
  * Pets

![lemon8](https://metricool.com/lemon-8/)
Lemon8 lets users **tag clothing items and add labels** showing where items are from. Many of these tags come from influencers partnering with major brands, making it perfect for shoppable content.
At first glance, Lemon8 feels familiar (which might explain why it’s still finding its unique voice). Here’s how it borrows from platforms you already know:
  * **Content Discovery** : _Just like TikTok_ , Lemon8 features “For You” and “Following” feeds, which makes it easy to browse trending content or stay updated on posts from beloved creators.
  * **Visual Aesthetics** : _Just like Pinterest_ , the platform is all about high-quality, influencer-style imagery with a grid layout, ideal for users who enjoy visually pleasing content. 
  * **Photo Editing Tools** : _Just like Instagram_ – with built-in _Canva-like_ editing features –, Lemon8 lets users edit their photos using collages, filters, effects, stickers, and text templates.


**Pro Tip for Social Media Managers:** If your [Instagram carousel posts](https://metricool.com/instagram-carousels/) outperform your Reels, Lemon8 might be your sweet spot.

  * Choose from: Beauty, Fashion, Food, Travel, Wellness, Pets

  * Use consistent branding across TikTok and Lemon8.

  * Include your website or link-in-bio URL

### Content Strategy: How to Post on Lemon8
Unlike TikTok’s spontaneous in-app camera, Lemon8 requires pre-created content. This makes it ideal for repurposing your already-polished assets.
**Upload Process:**
  1. **Tap the yellow + icon** at the bottom of the screen
  2. **Select content from your camera roll** (up to 3 photos/videos)
     * Videos: Maximum 60 seconds
     * Photos: High-resolution recommended for grid display
  3. **Edit with built-in tools**
     * Choose templates that match your category
     * Add text overlays, stickers, and tags
     * Apply filters for cohesive aesthetic
  4. **Optimize metadata**
     * Headline: Clear, benefit-driven (functions like a title)
     * Caption: Use “Caption ideas” feature for inspiration
     * Hashtags: Category-specific tags (research trending tags in your niche)
     * Location: Add for local discovery
  5. **Publish and track performance**
     * Monitor likes, saves/shares in Notifications (bell icon)
     * Track follower growth
     * Engage with comments promptly

## Can You Make Money on Lemon8?
While Lemon8 doesn’t offer built-in monetization tools like [TikTok](https://metricool.com/monetize-tiktok/)’s Creator Fund or [YouTube](https://metricool.com/make-money-on-youtube/)’s Partner Program yet, there are still solid ways to make money on the platform. Since it focuses on lifestyle content and high-quality visuals, creators can leverage their audience to generate income in several ways:
  * **Brand Partnerships** : Many influencers collaborate with brands to promote products in sponsored posts. Since Lemon8 is highly visual, brands in fashion, beauty, and wellness are particularly interested in working with creators on the platform.
  * **Driving Traffic to Your Website** : If you have a blog, online store, or another digital platform, you can use Lemon8 to attract visitors by sharing engaging content and including links in your bio.


[How to use SmartLinks](https://metricool.com/socialmedia-smartlinks/)
  * **Affiliate Marketing** : By sharing product recommendations with affiliate links, you can earn commissions when followers make purchases through your unique links. Many brands and platforms, such as Amazon Associates or RewardStyle, offer affiliate programs.


  * **Promoting Your Own Products** : Whether you sell digital products, physical goods, or services, Lemon8 can serve as a visually appealing showcase for your offerings, helping you connect with potential buyers.


Because the platform is still smaller than TikTok or Instagram, you may find it easier to stand out in some niches, but it can be harder to reach TikTok-level scale or earnings. Lemon8 works best as a support channel for your broader ecosystem, not your only monetization engine.

## Is Lemon8 Getting Banned?
No, it’s not. In early 2026, TikTok’s U.S. operations were placed under a new, majority U.S.–owned joint venture. This company runs the U.S. operations of other ByteDance apps now, including Lemon8 and CapCut. ByteDance now holds only a minority, non‑controlling stake in this structure.
## Is Lemon8 Worth It in 2026?
Given everything that’s happened, is Lemon8 worth investing time in?
That depends on your goals and niche, but here’s a simple framework to help you decide:  
| **You Should Test Lemon8 If…**  | **Keep Lemon8 Secondary or Experimental If…**  |  
| --- | --- |  
| You create **lifestyle content** (beauty, fashion, wellness, home, food, travel)  | Your niche isn’t highly **visual or lifestyle-driven**  |  
| You already produce **polished photos and carousels** (e.g., for Instagram or Pinterest)  | You focus on **short-form video** (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) and have limited bandwidth  |  
| You want to experiment on a platform with **high visual standards** but **lower competition**  | You rely heavily on **platform-native monetization**  |  
| Your team has **design skills** or regularly uses tools like **Canva**  | Your **brand voice** is more casual/authentic than polished  |  
| You’re looking for a **first-mover advantage** on a newer platform  | You create mainly **B2B or technical content**  |  
Lemon8 in 2026 is less about “Will it disappear because of a ban?” and more about “Does this niche, aesthetic-first platform fit my brand and content pipeline?”
It’s best treated as a complementary channel alongside TikTok, Instagram, and Pinterest – a place to repurpose your strongest visual stories and test new creative angles, without overcommitting or making it your only growth pillar.
## How to Delete Your Lemon8 Account
Tested and no fit for your strategy? Here’s how to cleanly exit:
  1. Go to the Lemon8**settings on your profile.**
  2. Select “Account” and then **“Delete Account”.**
  3. **Enter your password and confirm**.
  4. Read the pop‑up message about what deletion means.
  5. Tap **“Delete”**. 


Account deletion works similarly to TikTok: it isn’t instant. Your account is scheduled for deletion, but you have a grace period during which you can change your mind. To reactivate, log in again with your registered email and password and confirm in the dialog box that you want to restore the account.
