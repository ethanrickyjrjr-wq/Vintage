#### _All Seven Seasons Available Now_
Since premiering in July 2020, [Decorating the Set: From Hollywood to Your Home](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/decorating-the-set-from-hollywood-to-your-home-with/id1519062454) has been popular in the United States and around the world, having appeared on Apple’s Top 250 Podcast Charts in the categories of TV & Film and Design in multiple podcast markets and featuring listeners in over 30 countries, including the United States, Canda, Australia, New Zealand, the United Kingdom, the Czech Republic, Denmark, Hungary, Israel, Macedonia, Norway, Romania, Portugal, France, Germany, South Africa, Ireland, India, Pakistan, the Netherlands, Kenya, and Japan. 
> “From films and TV shows to your own backdrop, Beth navigates the wild world of making spaces come to life. She’s helped me and other listeners work out thorny decorating problems to make vivid our places. Wonderful listen.”
— 5-Star Review • Bay W.
Becoming “the decorator by your side,” each episode is filled with advice to help listeners create the perfect spaces for their homes. Beth effortlessly brings tips and tricks derived from her long career in television and film right into the homes of her listeners. There is also an interactive component to the podcast in which Beth answers questions from listeners, submitted through her active Instagram account. 
The podcast is available on all platforms, including [Apple](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/decorating-the-set-from-holly-wood-to-your-home-with/id1519062454), [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/show/1MuRfoDQ8ijClfFwpoqSOP), [Amazon ](https://music.amazon.com/podcasts/4b86479d-2d64-49c3-9300-49ff2322778c/decorating-the-set-from-holly-wood-to-your-home-with-beth-kushnick)and at[ Pod Clubhouse’s website](https://podclubhouse.com/2023/02/decorating-the-set-israeli-set-decorator-ohad-ostro/). All feedback, press, interview, and sponsor inquiries should be directed to press@podclubhouse.com
> “Beth walks among the giants of the set design world, and it’s incredible to have the opportunity to get the inside scoop on her process, and to see interiors through her eyes. Part Hollywood design tell-all, part practical how-to, and 100% inspirational, it all comes together to lend we listeners some of Beth’s genius to use in our own design projects, however humble or grand. It feels like we’re getting a million dollar experience, all for free.”
— 5-Star Review • Alex N.
