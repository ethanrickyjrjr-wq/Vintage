## Frequently asked questions
#### What is Beacons and how does it help creators?
Beacons is a creator’s AI-powered business platform designed to help you build, grow, and monetize your creator business with the help of your AI teammate, Beam. It offers tools for digital sales, marketing, brand partnerships, and performance insights.
#### How much does Beacons cost?
Beacons has a $0/month plan that is free forever, with no upfront costs and no monthly subscription costs.  
‍  
Beacons also offers subscription plans for creators ready to scale their business with more advanced marketing and content features such as:  
• Custom domains  
• 0% transaction fees on digital product and affiliate sales  
• More daily AI credits  
• Unlimited email sends  
  
Paid monthly subscription plans start at $10/month, or $8.33/month when billed annually.
#### How quickly can I see results?
Immediately! Beacons has a $0/month plan that is free forever, with no upfront costs and no monthly subscription costs, so creators can get started growing and monetizing their business at any stage.
#### Can I customize my Beacons page?
Yes, you can customize everything on your Beacons page! Beacons offers tons of customization options for creators, including fonts, colors, layouts, backgrounds, link styles, headers, and lots more.  
  
On Beacons, you’ll find beautifully designed templates and layouts that allow you to customize your page to your heart’s content right away. You can even let Beam, your AI teammate on Beacons, design your page based on any text prompt with just one click. You can tailor your page to your exact vibe and truly own your brand.
#### What analytics does Beacons offer?
Beacons is much more than a Link in Bio—it’s an AI-powered business tool that helps creators optimize their marketing funnel. On Beacons, you can see what your audience is clicking on, where they’re coming from, how your traffic is converting to clicks and sales, how your posts are contributing to your social media growth, and lots more.  
  
You can chat with Beam, your AI teammate on Beacons, to understand deeper performance insights. Ask Beam questions like “Why did my follower count increase more than usual last week?” and “Which one of my posts is driving the most engagement, and how can I replicate that?” Beam is your teammate to help with all areas of content analysis and growth strategy.
#### What creator tools does Beacons include to grow and monetize?
Beacons is an all-in-one platform of creator tools, with everything a creator needs to grow and monetize in one place.  
  
The creator tools include a Link in Bio, website builder, automatically-updating media kit to get brand deals, digital storefront to sell digital products, affiliate marketplace, email marketing tool, auto-DM Smart Reply, and detailed traffic analytics.  
  
Beacons is powered by Beam, an AI teammate designed specifically to help creators grow by giving you custom insights and advice on next steps.
#### Does Beacons charge a platform commission on sales?
Beacons offers different options for creators depending on what stage of their journey they are in:  
• We offer 0% commission on all sales as part of the Creator Plus and Creator Max plans  
• We charge a 9% commission on all sales as part of the Free and Creator plans, which allows creators to start selling and earning before investing in a more powerful plan.  
  
We believe it should be straightforward for creators to start earning and growing, which is why we’ve designed our pricing to reflect this. The fees deducted as a small percentage of every sale on the Free and Creator plans mean that we only make money when you do.
#### Is Beacons safe to use on all social media platforms?
Yes, it absolutely is. Beacons was designed to be used on social media, across all of your platforms, and is trusted by Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, X, and more.
#### How do I make money on Beacons?
There are tons of ways to make money on Beacons! In fact, Beacons brings together all of the ways to monetize as a creator in one easy-to-use platform.  
  
One Beacons account gives you access to:
• **Brand partnership opportunities** via tools like an automatically updating media kit to pitch yourself and exclusive Beacons offers from brand partners, like gifting and posting bonuses
• **Digital product sales** like digital downloads, guides, eBooks, music files, templates, courses, and more, with instant cash-outs directly to your Stripe or PayPal accounts

• **Marketing tools** that amplify all of your sales efforts, like email marketing (including embedded email collection forms) and Smart Reply (Instagram auto-DM), to drive more traffic from all of your channels to your links and products
• **Link in Bio and Website** to host all of your links and products to ecommerce stores (like Shopify and Spring)  
  
You can do this all through Beacons, without any extra tools needed!
