You must be at least 13 years old to create an Instagram profile.
## Ways to create an account
There are two ways to create an Instagram account. You can create a new account from the Instagram app or Instagram.com. If you already have an existing Facebook or Instagram account, you can use your existing account to create a new Instagram account. Both accounts will be added to the same [_Accounts Center_](https://www.facebook.com/help/943858526073065?helpref=faq_content) and have access to connected experiences.

## Security & saving your login info
If you created your account recently, people on Instagram may see that you have a new account. Learn about keeping your [account secure](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F369001149843369%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUDNw31h155wiacUrnk-YCf8zq3gExb1Jjl2wNrKGKpiovO3FUgGbXcr4A05J4GfBOgvDHRapKv4DtsHCr3IsTMjlbKET5mWndhnIGM2g2qNnZnjf6XRNGU5fCAw3hubb9KbnFQBRlYeLQ).
You may be asked to save your login info. If you choose to save your login information, you won’t need to enter it the next time you log back into your account.
