### The Palm Beach Show – AKA the Palm Beach Jewelry, Art & Antique Show  
An Exhibition of Spectacular Breadth, Depth and Scope
Known as the most luxurious and sophisticated showcase of its kind in the United States, the **24th Annual Palm Beach Show returns February 11-16, 2027** to the **Palm Beach County Convention Center** over Presidents’ Day Weekend.
The Palm Beach Show remains the area’s only high-end showcase offering items spanning every genre, juxtaposing many periods and movements. Each year the show reinforces its all-inclusive nature as well as reaffirms our prominent presence in the Palm Beach market as the most prestigious cultural event of the season.
Space is limited for exhibitors interested in participating amongst the world’s most sought-after dealers, featuring stunning collections at one of the most popular and important art, antique & jewelry shows in the country.

The Palm Beach Show Group is the leading full-service show production company in the nation, specializing in world-class fine art, antique and jewelry shows. Providing an unparalleled experience for exhibitors and attendees at each and every show, the Palm Beach Show Group currently produces the industries’ most anticipated events and is known for their diverse roster of top international dealers, exceptional merchandise and elegant décor.
