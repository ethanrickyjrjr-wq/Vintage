# Adding alt text to your images
1min read
#### Share
Currently, it is possible to add alt text to static images scheduled to X/Twitter, Mastodon, Threads, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Facebook, Instagram, and Bluesky through Buffer.
:::info Looking for the mobile version of the [Adding alt text to your images](https://support.buffer.com/en-us/articles/adding-alt-text-to-your-images-on-the-buffer-mobile-app-7FQhwo8Oaw) article?
:::
### Generating alt text
To add alt text to your image, click on the image you uploaded within your Buffer composer. This will open the image in a larger view, and there will be a text box at the bottom where you can add your alternative text description.
While we recommend using fewer than 200 characters to align with [accessibility best practices](https://www.accessibility.com/blog/what-are-best-practices-for-creating-alt-text), the character counts for alt text in Buffer are as follows:
  * Pinterest - 500 characters
  * LinkedIn - 300 characters
  * X/Twitter, Mastodon, Threads, Facebook, Instagram - 1,000 characters
  * Bluesky - 2,000 characters


![Alt text button on image uploaded to the Buffer dashboard](https://support.buffer.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=1b16ec38-3cd4-4bb9-83dc-b111d0a19ce0)
Once you're done, click _**Save.**_
:::info **Good to Knows**
  * It is only possible to view alt text on Instagram with a screen reader.
  * Currently within Buffer, it is not possible to add alt text to:
  * GIFS
  * Google Business Profile posts
  * TikTok posts


:::
We are committed to improving in the area of accessibility, and you can find our full [Accessibility Statement here.](https://buffer.com/accessibility)
### Generating alt text with AI
The option to generate alt text with AI is currently in Beta. [Learn more about how to test our Beta features here.](https://support.buffer.com/en-us/articles/how-to-test-buffers-beta-features-EqubsYGbuR)
If you'd like a head start on writing alt text, click **Generate with AI** below the description box. Buffer will read the image and suggest a short, accessibility-friendly description (up to about 300 characters) you can review and edit before saving. You can click the button again at any time to get a different suggestion.
![Buffer alt text composer with arrows pointing to description area and Generate with AI option](https://support.buffer.com/api/attachments.redirect?id=32e672e7-3e63-4d70-bb31-76b07c6e610f)
:::info AI alt text generation is currently rolling out to the **post composer** only. It's not available in Ideas yet.
:::
