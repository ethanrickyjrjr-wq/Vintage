**Easy and reliable transfer solution to automatically backup new photos & videos in the background from your iPhone / iPad to predefined targets.**

## Encrypt Photos & Videos 
Encrypt photos & videos before transfer and store them encrypted on NAS and file-based cloud services. Browse and view encrypted photos & videos directly in PhotoSync.
Rclone and OpenSSL are supported, enabling easy workflow integration and seamless decryption on PC and Mac.
**Note:** 3rd party services require a user account for the cloud / photo service + Internet connection

**Three transfer options are available:**

**Wi-Fi Camera Support**
Manually download photos, videos and RAWs from Canon EOS, Nikon, Sony, Lumix, Fujifilm & Olympus Wi-Fi cameras to the photo library on your iPhone / iPad. 
Instant backup / wireless tethering: Automatically transfer photos, videos & RAWs to the photo library on your iPhone / iPad immediately after pressing the shutter button. Additionally, you can transfer files to any supported remote target (computer, NAS or cloud service). 
![USB Camera Support \(iOS 14.2+\)](https://www.photosync-app.com/files/content/images/premium/wificameras/icon_usb.png)
**USB Camera Support (iOS 14.2+)**
Manually download photos, videos and RAWs from cameras connected over USB to the photo library on your iPhone / iPad or directly to remote targets (computer, NAS or cloud service). 
Instant backup / tethered shooting over USB is supported for many Canon EOS, Nikon, Sony and Fujifilm cameras. PhotoSync supports both PTP / MTP and Mass-Storage Mode of external cameras. If both are available on your camera, choose PTP / MTP for better performance and more features. 
![FTP Server Support](https://www.photosync-app.com/files/content/images/premium/wificameras/icon_ftp.png)
**FTP Server Support**
Integrated FTP server support for Pro Cameras from Canon, Nikon, Sony and Fujifilm. 
**Highlights**
Photo booth support: Instant photo display on a TV or projector right after recording over AirPlay / HDMI.
Easy deletion (also large numbers) of photos & videos from your camera directly in PhotoSync.
**Note:** Tethering and photo booth support subject to camera model.

## Wireless SD-Cards
**Wireless SD-Cards**
Take pictures, videos & RAW photos on your camera equipped with a Toshiba Flashair™ or Transcend Wi-Fi SD Card and download the full resolution files directly to the Camera Roll or user-defined albums on your iPhone / iPad.
View the photos & videos stored on your camera in full detail without having to first import them.
