Facebook Pages are for businesses, brands, organizations and public figures to share updates and connect with people. A Page is different from:
  * [Pro mode](https://www.facebook.com/help/2978174759158711/?helpref=faq_content) (a setting that gives a personal profile creator tools), which you can turn on for your Facebook profile.
  * [Groups](https://www.facebook.com/help/337881706729661?helpref=faq_content), which allow you to communicate shared interests with a group of people.


If you want to create a Facebook Page for your business, brand or public figure, follow these steps to create and customize it.
Anyone with a Facebook account can create a Page or help manage one, as long as they have [access to the Page](https://www.facebook.com/help/289207354498410?helpref=faq_content). When someone follows a Page on Facebook, they can start seeing updates from that Page in their Feed.
## Create a Facebook Page
To create a Facebook Page on a computer:
  1. Log into Facebook, then click **Pages** on the left. 
  2. Click **Create page**. 
  3. Select **Public page** , then click **Next**.
  4. Click **Get started**. 
  5. Add your Page name and category.
  6. Add an optional bio and click **Create Page**.
  7. Add information, such as contact info, location and hours, and click **Next**.
  8. Add profile and cover photos, edit the action button and click **Next**.
  9. Connect your WhatsApp to your Page, or click **Skip**.
  10. Invite friends to connect with your Page, and click **Next**.
  11. Select your notification settings and click **Done**. 


## Customize your Facebook Page
Once you've created your Page, you can customize your Page by:
  * Adding [basic information](https://www.facebook.com/help/160672070698623/?helpref=faq_content).
  * Adding a [profile picture](https://www.facebook.com/help/284445998278828/?helpref=faq_content) or [cover photo](https://www.facebook.com/help/333543230019115?helpref=faq_content).
  * Adding an [action button](https://www.facebook.com/help/977869848936797/?helpref=faq_content).
  * [Managing people’s access to the Page](https://www.facebook.com/help/187316341316631?helpref=faq_content).
  * [Connecting your Page and WhatsApp account](https://www.facebook.com/help/2783732558314697/?helpref=faq_content).


If you’re aiming to monetize your Page, [check if it’s eligible in Meta Business Suite](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/536194053479553?helpref=faq_content). Learn what to do if [you're having trouble creating a Page](https://www.facebook.com/help/224490150894941?helpref=faq_content) or [troubleshoot common issues](https://www.facebook.com/help/722403865122892?helpref=faq_content) if something isn’t working correctly on Facebook.
