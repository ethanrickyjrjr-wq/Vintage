# Create a YouTube channel
You can watch, like videos, and subscribe to channels with a [Google Account](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/69961). To upload videos, comment, or make playlists, **you need a YouTube channel**. Without a channel, you won't have a public presence on YouTube.
[How to Create a YouTube Channel & Customize It (Creator Basics)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aKydtOXW8mI)
## Create a personal channel
Follow these instructions to create a channel that only you can manage.
  1. Sign in to YouTube on a computer or the mobile site.
  2. Click your profile picture ![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/NB5qyD2bwPLSxRz3L4RkFWHtTntWnKPJ5-jUmi5tToCc3-230ToGVw1WbpGWolgh2eT4=w48-h48) ![and then](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/_KZw6iFLPOiYyWMg4YA4SL-ec-Eitj91jD29eyhArvOWBOlmDYcgi9Dj973hpW0oDEA=w48-h48) Settings ![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/4RU_NopOgstXUTLud4JYjpliQhZFf_KTeblVSckNkfGPrx9Mr_iZYv3Gwqbf1-x5suHj=w48-h48).
  3. Go to the **Account** section and select **Add or manage channel(s)**.
  4. Click **Create a channel** ![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/0sOmEncYy8G_f4cbA4cX1SN_NZd6OmiqXQGub8r_EjoiA8o4coILxw5ZM1bxSg53xBDq=w48-h48).
  5. Choose a profile picture, type in your name and Handle.
  6. Click **Create channel**.


## Create a channel with a business or other name
Follow these instructions to create a channel that can have more than one manager or owner.
You can connect your channel to a Brand Account if you want to use a different name on YouTube than your Google Account. Learn more [about Brand Accounts](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/7001996).
  1. Sign in to YouTube on a computer or the mobile site.
  2. Go to [your channel list](https://www.youtube.com/attribution_link?m=ext-hc-ytm-pe-1&u=%2Fchannel_switcher).
  3. Choose to create a new channel or use an existing Brand Account: 
     * To create a channel, click **Create a new channel**.
     * To create a YouTube channel for a Brand Account that you already manage, choose a Brand Account from the list. If the Brand Account already has a channel, you can't create a new one. When you select the Brand Account from the list, you’ll be switched over to that channel.
  4. Choose a profile picture, name, and Handle.
  5. Click **Create channel**. This creates a new Brand Account.
  6. Learn how to [add channel owners and managers](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/4628007).


**Note:** If you use YouTube with a [supervised experience](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10314940), you may not be able to create a channel.
