# How to use the Commercial Music Library
Last updated: July 2026 
The TikTok Commercial Music Library (CML) is a global music library of 1 million songs that helps make it easier for businesses to find music to soundtrack their content on TikTok. This library includes all styles, genres, and regions, with songs sourced from independent and emerging artists, as well as top-tier music houses, free for businesses.
  

## How to access the Commercial Music Library
When you add sound to your video on the TikTok app**,** you may first be shown all sounds or just sounds from the Commercial Music Library, depending on what type of account type you use. Learn [about the types of TikTok accounts](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-and-tiktok-business-account-types). 
  1. If you use a Business Account, when you start a new video and click on **Add Sound** , you will only see sounds from the Commercial Music Library ("Commercial Sounds"). 
  2. If you use a personal account, when you start a new video and click on **Add Sound** , you will see all sounds. To choose from the Commercial Music Library, tap the **Sounds** button and select **Commercial Sounds** from the dropdown.

  

## How to use the Commercial Music Library
  1. Select the region where your campaign will run in order to find music available in that region.
  2. Select from all music or playlist. **Note** : Up to 10,000 Commercial Sounds can be displayed at a time. You can use different filters to search for specific music collections.
  3. Select filters. You can filter by usable placements, themes, genre, mood, and duration. You can also search for music using the search box.


Once the Commercial Sounds have been selected, you can use them for organic or paid content.
