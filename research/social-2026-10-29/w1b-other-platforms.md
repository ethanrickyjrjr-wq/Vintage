## TL;DR

1. **Nothing here changes a fixed constraint.** Two things tighten one: Facebook Marketplace-by-pickup and Facebook Groups run on message/comment claims, and Whatnot lives run on bidding. Those are flagged, not recommended. (I)
2. **Ranking beyond Instagram: 1 Pinterest, 2 YouTube Shorts, 3 Facebook Page via native cross-post, 4 TikTok (defer), then Threads mirror, Marketplace (shipped checkout only), Nextdoor (service only). Skip Whatnot, Poshmark, Depop, Lemon8, Facebook Groups.** (I)
3. **Pinterest is the only platform here whose native action is a click to your page**, which is the shop's one allowed call to action. It has two gates: one real URL per piece (items are `#item-<id>` hash routes today) and no pins to sample listings with illustrative prices. Until both clear, pins go to room, story and hire pages only. (I; gating fact from round-d-synthesis)
4. **YouTube has the best verified vintage-lamp evidence, but it is a reseller audience.** Crazy Lamp Lady 309K subs; her 72 s Short drew 23,394 views; IvyDogVintage (4,170 subs) has a 49,465-view video. Titles are "what sold on Etsy" and "buying to resell". (V, yt-dlp [S6])
5. **The same sellers show one primary channel plus mirrors**: Crazy Lamp Lady 309K YouTube, 11.6K Threads, 124 Pinterest. (V [S6][S18][S24])
6. **Meta says captions with "no links" perform best** on Facebook, which supports the "reply LINK with the link" pattern. Meta also says: avoid visible third-party watermarks, and "You can share the same content across any of your Pages or profiles." (V [S11])
7. **Facebook Marketplace fee: 10%, minimum $0.80, on sale plus shipping plus tax, payment-provider fees extra.** Shipped checkout fits the no-DM-claim rule; local pickup does not. (V [S7])
8. **TikTok Business accounts may use only the Commercial Music Library** (1 million tracks), and content that is "unoriginal or reused material without anything new" is ineligible for the For You feed. One public vintage-fairy-lamp TikTok reached 1.9M views. (V [S19][S20][S36])
9. **Etsy has a native Social media tool** (Shop Manager > Marketing) that posts to Facebook, Instagram and X; Offsite Ads charge 15% (12% from $10K) on attributed sales and advertise on Google, Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest. The "4% credit for your own traffic" claim was not found on Etsy's own Marketing pages. (V [S30][S32])
10. **Marginal effort for the recommended set is about 1.75 to 2.0 hours a week over the Instagram batch** (I; assumptions in section 6).

## 2. Findings

### 2.1 Pinterest
- 640M global MAU, up 11% yoy; US and Canada 106M (Q2 2026). V [S14]. ">50% Gen Z" appeared in a search summary only: US.
- "Visual search engine" and keyword-in-board rule: d1 V (not re-read). Scheduling 30 days ahead, 10 queued: d1 V.
- Specs below are from the **ad** spec page: 2:3 (1000 x 1500), title 100 chars (first 40 show), description 800 chars "used by our algorithm to determine relevance", video 4 s to 15 min. Organic use is I. V [S15]
- Spam rule: no "repetitive, deceptive, or irrelevant" content made for money; linked sites must be "original" and add "unique value". V [S16]. A Pin to a sample listing with an illustrative price is a deceptive destination (I).
- Downloaded video Pins carry a username watermark (Pinterest's own). V [S17]
- Pre-owned Product Pins are not allowed: d1 V. Organic = plain Pins.
- "Pins live for months" was **not read** this session. Do not rely on it.
- Evidence of sellers: resellers mostly ignore it. Crazy Lamp Lady 124 followers; Reinvented Delaware 7.6K; marketplaces are huge (1stDibs 941.4K, Etsy 11.2M). V [S18]. No seller performance data found: gap.

### 2.2 YouTube
- Shorts: up to 3 minutes, square or vertical, up to 15 files per upload; max 1080p. V [S1][S2]
- Scheduling in Studio: date, time and time zone; video stays private until then; a Community Guidelines strike blocks scheduled publishing. No cap stated on the page. V [S3]
- Views count every start/replay since 2025-03-31; "Engaged views" kept separately. V [S2]
- Music: Shorts Audio Library; YouTube Audio Library tracks "won't receive a Content ID claim"; Shorts over one minute with an active copyright claim are blocked. V [S4]
- Reuse rules are **monetization** rules (channel-level): reused content, and "mass-produced, generic, repetitive" template content, are ineligible for the Partner Program, with AI-template content named. The page states no distribution penalty, and we are not pursuing monetization. V [S5]. Risk for an automated pipeline: do not mass-template (I).
- Reseller-audience caveat: see section 3.

### 2.3 Facebook
**Page and Reels**
- Originality: filmed/produced by the Page owner is original; duplicates get "reduced distribution"; minor edits (borders, captions, speed) are low-value; "avoid using visible third party watermarks and content that is visibly recycled from other apps"; "captions with no links, minimal use of capital letters, and no more than five hashtags perform best". V [S11][S12] (S12 dated 2026-03-13)
- Native Instagram-to-Facebook cross-post ("uploading only once"; toggles for stories, posts, reels). V [S13]
- Small vintage-lamp Pages: Vintage Shades 4,505 likes; Nostalgic Bulbs 753; Antique brass 624; One Of A Kind Lamps 54 followers. US [S37]

**Marketplace**
- Fee: US Marketplace 10%, minimum $0.80, on "total sale price, any shipping fees and Applicable Taxes"; payment-provider fees are separate; $20 per chargeback. V [S7]
- Ship within 7 calendar days or the order auto-cancels; Facebook collects tax in facilitator states; payouts are held past a sales limit until a tax ID (SSN/ITIN) is entered. V [S8]
- Purchase Protection covers "arrived damaged or different than described" for checkout orders; **local pickups and Messenger deals are excluded**. V [S9]
- "No selling fee on local pickup": US (search summary quoting Meta).
- Package size/weight limits for shipped items: not read (gap).
- Fit (I): checkout-only listing = the listing is the claim, so no DM claims. Pickup = DM haggling, a street address, no protection, and "not an open store" pressure. Marketplace is not in the decided fan-out (Etsy > Crosslist > eBay/Shopify per CLAUDE.md), so every sale means a **manual delist elsewhere**, which is the double-sell risk the constraints exist to stop.

**Groups**
- Buy/sell groups fall under Meta's Commerce Policies; admins choose the features; each group has its own rules, unreadable without login. V [S10]
- Named groups (practitioner list updated 2026-10-08): "All Things Vintage and Retro" (mid-century decor to glassware), "Thrift Store & Antique Finds" (open buy/sell/trade), "Ooh La La! 1963-1990s Vintage Hive Mind BST". UP [S37]. SWFL results: an online-yard-sale group and free/curb-alert groups that ban sales without admin approval. US [S37]
- Verdict (I): Groups sell by "sold/PU/dibs" comments. Skip as a channel. Read-only for sourcing and identification threads.

### 2.4 TikTok
- Not eligible for For You: "unoriginal or reused material without anything new"; people can still find a video through search or the account page. V [S19]
- Business Accounts: "cannot use the general music library for commercial usage", including organic posts; use the Commercial Music Library (1 million tracks). V [S20]. Means no trending sounds (I).
- Ownership: US joint venture finalized 2026-01-22 per press; TikTok runs a USDS JV site. UP [S21]
- Vintage evidence: `@feathers_and_twigs` "Vintage fairy lamps #tiaraglass #vintageglassware" (26 s, 2022-12-30): 1.9M views, 300.5K likes, 3.5K comments, 23.7K reposts; follower count not returned. V [S36]. One video, almost four years old: an outlier, not a base rate (I).
- Other snippets: Tori Thrift Finds (@toreye.thrifts) 21 likes on a swag-lamp post; The Coop Antiques (Oviedo FL) 4 to 108 likes per post. US [S37]
- No TikTok Shop lane: d1 V.

## 6. Recommendation

**Effort budget, marginal to the Instagram batch (I).** Assumes one weekly shoot already yields 3 to 5 reels and stills; Claude drafts edits and captions. Includes replies (canned "LINK" answers).

| Item | h/wk |
|---|---|
| Pinterest: 3 Pins, crop, title/description, schedule | 1.0 |
| YouTube Shorts: 3 uploads, title/description, schedule, replies | 0.5 to 0.75 |
| Facebook Page: cross-post toggle, replies | 0.25 |
| **Recommended set** | **1.75 to 2.0** |
| + TikTok | +0.75 |
| + Marketplace (shipped) | +0.5 |

**(a) Free and native only.** Pinterest + YouTube Shorts + Facebook cross-post, native schedulers. Human: shoot; approve every caption; pick TikTok/YouTube music in-app; reply to comments; mark Gone and delist on every sale; confirm the page each Pin points to is real.
**(b) One paid tool.** Not needed for platform presence: weekly batches (5 posts, 3 Shorts, 3 Pins) fit the free caps (d1). Price of any paid scheduler not read in this lane; see w2c.
**(c) Claude + Codex pipeline.** Claude drafts Pin titles (100 chars) and descriptions (800), Shorts titles, per-platform captions and a sold-state swap; local ffmpeg crops 9:16 to 2:3. Human in approve-everything mode: every post. In approve-by-exception mode: music pick, any post naming a price, and delisting elsewhere. Publish APIs are not researched here (w2c); `wiki/social-comment-ai.md` says TikTok developer signup needs a company-domain email and legal entity, and Meta own-page posting needs no App Review.

**What to skip and why**
- Whatnot: bidding/claim live format, Florida regulated-auction question unchecked, and a 2+ hour live is also the largest time cost.
- Poshmark: 20% fee, built-in offers, brand collision on "Holliewood".
- Depop: fashion-led marketplace, eBay-owned since 2026-07-30, offers.
- Lemon8: no vintage evidence, depends on TikTok.
- Facebook Groups and pickup Marketplace: claim-by-comment culture.
- YouTube long-form: 4 to 8 hours per video for a reseller audience (I).
