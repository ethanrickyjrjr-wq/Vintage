Lane w1g, written 2026-10-29. Raw captures: `research/social-2026-10-29/raw/w1g-*.md` (live pages read this session). Prior-lane captures are cited by file: `research/fabulous-from-online/c1..c5` (read 2026-10-28) and `research/round-d/d1,d4,d6,d7`.

## TL;DR

1. **Every service seller I could read routes inquiries to its own site.** Mix & Match's bio reads "Need a designer's eye? Virtual consultations" with an arrow to the link `[SS]` (instagram.com/mixandmatchdesigncompany); the site then runs /virtual-consultation to "Book a consultation" `[V]`. Whether DMs also convert cannot be read through the Instagram wall; designers in the panel below say some inquiries arrive on Instagram.
2. **Referrals lead, and Instagram is the portfolio and warm-up.** Business of Home's 10-designer panel (Aug 13, 2021, n=10, anecdote): one designer gets half her new business from referrals and half from Instagram; another says Instagram inquirers "almost always" are already fans `[PR]`. A UK guide (Whito, updated Aug 28, 2026) calls Instagram "top-of-funnel awareness, not lead generation" `[PR]`. I found no survey with lead-source percentages.
3. **Two of the three small designers I could compare have more Pinterest than Instagram followers.** Mix & Match: Pinterest 14.4k `[V]` vs Instagram 12K `[SS]`. Of Houses and Trees: 3.1k `[V]` vs 885 `[SS]`. Noeka is the reverse: Pinterest 40 `[V]`, Instagram 4.7K `[SS]`.
4. **Two lead patterns: the free consult, and the paid micro-product.** Of Houses and Trees puts "CLICK TO BOOK A FREE ONLINE DESIGN CONSULTATION!" on its pages `[V]`; Mix & Match sells 30/60/90 minutes at $175/$300/$425 `[P c1]`. Hollie's free Screen Test follows the first, so cap its length `[I]`.
5. **The written Hire Hollie prices sit inside every public band I found**: $95 one find, $295 corner, $750 room, $150 an hour or $1,200 a day in person `[P c1,c3]`. Two items need review: the Casting Call is under the $150 sourcing floor at two vintage shops `[P c2]`, and nobody publishes a rush fee, so +25% and +50% have no market check `[P c1,c3]`.
6. **Constraint conflict:** the live Hire page says Full Production is "10% off each room after the first" `[V]` (site/hire.html line 121); the shop rule is "no discounts". Flagged, not changed (section 1).
7. **The ch. 481 documents disagree**: BRIEF says "style, never interior design"; the repo design doc and statute text do not restrict "interior designer" `[V]`. I keep "style" and draw no legal conclusion (sections 1, 7).
8. **Screen Test as content needs three separate yeses**: recording the call (934.03(2)(d)), publishing a face (540.08), house details. Use a staged call with a consenting volunteer until a lawyer reads it (section 7).
9. **Google Business Profile wants a service area, not a radius**: up to 20 areas by city or ZIP, about 2 hours of driving, address removed if nobody is served there `[V]`. With d6's finding that online-only shops are ineligible, it fits only once the Cameo (in-person visits) is live.
10. **A $0-a-month booking stack is enough at launch** (plus Stripe's 2.9% + 30c per payment): Cal.com Free, Stripe Payment Links, Tally Free. A CRM (Dubsado from $335 a year, HoneyBook from $29 a month billed yearly) wins only past about four paying projects a month `[I]`.

| Topic | Source | What accounts get wrong or skip | Practical line |

## 9. Recommendation for Holliewood & Vine

**(a) Free and native only.** Instagram Professional account with a bio line that names the product ("Free video Screen Test") and one link to site/hire.html. Pinterest business account with keyworded boards. Cal.com Free for the Screen Test, Tally Free for photo intake, Stripe Payment Links for the fee, Kit Free for the drop list.
