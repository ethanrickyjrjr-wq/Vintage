# w1d: audience, demand and the Nov 2026 to Oct 2027 content calendar

Lane w1d, accessed 2026-10-29. Raw captures: `research/social-2026-10-29/raw/w1d-*` (29 pages, 9 Google Trends series as JSON, 2 pull scripts). Already known and not redone: d1 (accounts, free scheduler caps, TikTok and Pinterest rules), d5 (search and guides), d6 (Lee County quarters, Vintage Market Days, River District, Naples by Design, Lee 6.5% and Collier 6% tax). Instagram's own timing statements are w1a's.

Tags: `verified` read live this session. `vendor claim` the vendor's own marketing. `study (n=, date)` stated sample. `unverified (search snippet)` not read on a page. `inferred` my reading. `computed` calendar arithmetic. Times are Eastern (SWFL).

## TL;DR

1. **Search demand for this lane's objects is modestly seasonal, so the calendar should be moment-led, not search-led.** US weekly Google Trends, four full years (Nov 2021 to Oct 2025): vintage lamp and brass lamp run winter-high and spring-low (January index 1.11 and 1.18, May 0.92 and 0.89; Jan-Feb beat Apr-Jun in all four years, by 12-32%); pink Depression glass 0.86-1.11; disco ball peaks in December (1.29). `verified` (own pull, rescaled and repeated for the lamp terms, section 1.4), with a caveat on an unexplained 2026 jump.
2. **Pinterest Predicts 2026 names this shop's look twice:** "Neo Deco" (antique bar cart +100%, brass aesthetic +35%, pendant lamp +40%, per trade press) and "Opera Aesthetic" (dark drapery, red roses, masquerade decor +40%). Released Dec 9, 2025; the 2027 edition is expected in December 2026. `vendor claim`; the release date is `verified`.
3. **1stDibs designers (n=468, Jul-Aug 2025, vendor-commissioned):** burgundy rose from 7% to 21%; 36% of items sourced were vintage or antique; but interest in the **1970s declined** while 1920s-50s grew. Burgundy helps the brand; the 1970s line is a risk for 60s-80s stock. `study`, `verified` (press-release text).
4. **Pinterest wants seasonal content live 4-6 weeks ahead ("if the moment peaks in June, launch in April") and says holiday activity spikes in September.** The native queue holds 30 days and 10 Pins, so a Pin can be queued only within 30 days of its go-live date. **Consequence:** the shop has no Pinterest, Instagram, TikTok or Facebook accounts yet, so a brand-new Pinterest account opened in November cannot be on time for December gifting by Pinterest's own rule; the first moment it can hit with a proper lead is Valentine's (Pins live Dec 20-Jan 3). `vendor claim`; caps `verified` (d1); the consequence is `inferred`.
5. **Holiday timing is early:** 43% of holiday shoppers plan to start before November (NRF page, 2026, n not stated); NRF forecasts Nov-Dec sales up 4.2% to $1.013 trillion. Etsy's 10-K says Q4 is its seasonal peak. `verified`.
6. **USPS 2026 send-by dates, lower 48 (verified):** Ground Advantage and First-Class Thu Dec 17, Priority Fri Dec 18, Express Sat Dec 19. A third-party guide shows Dec 19 and Dec 21 for the last two; the USPS page wins.
7. **Timing studies conflict.** Sprout Social (about 2 billion engagements, 307,000 profiles, Nov 2025 to Feb 2026): Pinterest Tue-Thu 10a-1p, Sunday worst overall. GenSumo (173,000 pins): Pinterest Sunday best, evenings 8-10 pm, home decor Saturday morning. Both are tool vendors. Treat them as hypotheses to test, with the weekly drop slot held fixed. `vendor claim`.
8. **No calendar row is usable until the accounts exist, and product-link rows also need real stock.** The calendar's gate column says which (A = accounts only, S = accounts plus stock); November is a setup month.
9. **No first-party data on who buys vintage decor by age or gender.** Etsy's 10-K, 1stDibs and ThredUp give scale, not that split. Gap.

**Two-slot test (free, native Insights):** hold the drop slot fixed. For 8 weeks alternate non-drop posts between slot A (midweek midday) and slot B (Sunday evening or Saturday morning), same format, same caption style, at least 8 posts per slot. Compare saves plus shares per reach (Instagram) and saves per impression (Pinterest). With samples this small, switch only if one slot wins by a wide margin for 3 weeks running. Sprout's window is Nov-Feb only (SWFL's peak season); the pattern may shift in summer.
