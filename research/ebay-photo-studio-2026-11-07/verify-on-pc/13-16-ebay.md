## 4. Seller consensus (lane 16)
- **Mannequin vs flat lay: split, no data.** "the flat lay items are outselling the ones displayed on a mannequin" (32785890, undated, closed). Against: "Laying out clothing on a clean backdrop on the floor or bed can cause odd angles (think straight leg jeans looking like massive flare leg jeans)" (Listing Clothes thread, undated).
- **White: advised, not required.** "I use mostly white in my backgrounds because I like consistency" vs "I do not ever use a white background, always look like there is not enough light" (156571, undated). A seller claim, unverified: "Google used to prefer it... that requirement was dropped."
- **Photo count: no thread says anything.** The only count thread (#5) is a shell. Others: "Sellers who list often, bad photos or not, are naturally going to sell more because they're putting more out there." (34960307).
- **Search placement: no photo factor in threads.** "titles, item specifics and accuracy is what it rewards" (34960307). eBay's own page: "Do not add graphics to images such as badges, logos, copyright notices or watermarks as they can affect your listing's placement in search results" (4148).
- **eBay's remover on garments: mixed to poor.** Sellers move to Photoroom or Adobe; "I give my square photo a professional white background... the app sees makes it taller" (34679136, undated).

## 5. Surprises the decision doc misses
- The two threads the lane ranked as key evidence (flat-lay sell-through #1, six-photo/search #5) cannot be read at the cited URLs.
- The mannequin sentence exists only on eBay's export hub, a regional site that itself says terminology may differ.
- The VAR article's alteration is offsite and watermark cleanup; its example left the eBay page unaltered. The lane's "check the live listing after publishing" would not catch it.
- Magical Listing in the app is "for private sellers"; business sellers are "Stay tuned". That matters if the shop account is a business account.
- The User Agreement's listing clause points to the "Images, videos and text policy" (id=4240), not the picture policy. We hold a 2026-10-27 capture (`research/cutouts/raw/01-ebay-images-text-policy.md`): "truthful, accurate".
- eBay's "4.5% more likely to sell" study defines better photo quality only as "500 or greater pixels on the longest side, do not have added text or graphics, and are uploaded to eBay picture service". White backgrounds and models are not part of it.
- The export hub says "The item should take up 80-90% of the frame." Compare that with the script's padding.
