## Recommendations for Ricky

1. Buy one adjustable, pinnable form with removable arms and an adjustable height pole for the daily photo work, and one cream linen vintage-look form as the room piece that also appears in shots. Do not rely on a fixed vintage form for pinning: old ones may be unpinnable and often have narrow hips and wide shoulders.
2. For sizes 6 to 10, a fixed size 6 plus batting beats a size 10 plus nothing, per the Fabulous Fit and blog snippets. Verify the Dritz Small and Medium charts before ordering.
3. Keep a kit: binder clips for back-taking, tissue for sleeves and shoulders, transparent tape, pins, a bra for the bust, black cloth under sheers. Etsy mentions tissue, tape and pins; the rest is seller-blog advice.
4. The pipeline lanes (ghost mannequin, background removal) should expect to remove a light-colored form: a white linen form on a white background is hard to cut out, so a darker form or a black undercloth may separate better (UNVERIFIED reasoning, ties to the 04 lane).
