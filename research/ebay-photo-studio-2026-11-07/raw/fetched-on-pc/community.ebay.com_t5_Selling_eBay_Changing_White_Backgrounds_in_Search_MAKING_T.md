##### eBay Changing White Backgrounds in Search! MAKING THEM GRAY!
**eBay has a new "feature" whereby they change a pure white background to gray in the search (ON PC).**
I have always worked hard to ensure my backgrounds were pure white so they would seamlessly blend in with a websites white background. This would give the appearance that my item was floating on the page..
This is how professionals do their photos on all other websites like Etsy, Amazon, Spotify, etc.
I'm not sure when they did this but they are now ALTERING my Gallery Photos in Search so the item backgrounds are more Gray!! This only is occurring on a desktop PC as far I as I can tell.
The Search on the android app looks like how the PC should with the background of the page and item merging together.
