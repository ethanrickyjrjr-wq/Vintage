##### Do the quality of photos really matter (or matter enough based on time vs return)
Iv'e been pondering this subject for a long time. I sell mostly clothing, and when I review other sellers numbers and sell through rates, the quality of photos seems to matter less and less. Most sellers are now doing flat lay photos on gray or off white carpet. The pictures are often quite poor, but good enough to give a general idea of what your'e buying. Many of these sellers still get top dollar for their items, with a good sell through rate. It appears to me, that Ebay values greatly people who list lots of items daily and does not give any bonus points for good photos. I may give this method a try, even though it goes against my nature. After all we are all here to make money. What are your thoughts.
