## FAQ
What Is a Photo Light Box or Shooting Tent Used For?
A photo light box (or shooting tent) is an all-in-one portable mini studio with built-in diffusers and LED lighting. It surrounds small-to-medium products with seamless, shadow-free light, allowing e-commerce sellers, jewelry makers, and collectors to capture clean, professional product photos for Amazon, eBay, Shopify, and social media in seconds.
What Size Light Box Do You Need for Your Products?
Always choose a light box slightly larger than your largest product to leave room for lighting diffusion:  
10"–12" (25–30 cm): Jewelry, coins, rings, watches, and small cosmetics.  
16"–20" (40–50 cm): Shoes, handbags, mugs, small electronics, and toys.  
24"–32"+ (60–80 cm+): Large backpacks, boots, small appliances, and folded apparel.
Light Box vs. Fabric Shooting Tent: Which One Should You Choose?
Light Boxes with Built-in LEDs: Feature integrated, dimmable LED light strips and rigid fold-out panels. They offer quick setup, bright illumination, and adjustable color temperatures without needing extra external lights.  
Fabric Shooting Tents: Made of thick translucent fabric without built-in lights. They require external studio lights or speedlites placed outside the tent to shine through, providing ultimate creative lighting control for professional studio photographers.
How Do You Avoid Glare and Harsh Reflections on Metallic or Glass Objects?
Shiny objects like jewelry, watches, and glassware act like mirrors. To eliminate glare:  
-Cover the built-in LED strips with the included light diffuser cloth.  
-Use the top-down shooting hatch (shooting through a small slot rather than wide-open front) to block ambient room reflections.  
-Angle your product slightly away from direct light points.
How Do You Get a Pure Seamless White Background in a Light Box?
Achieving a seamless pure white background requires a combination of background material and lighting:  
-Use the included wrinkle-free PVC backdrop (which wipes clean easily and won't crease like paper).  
-Increase the LED dimmer brightness so the white backdrop becomes bright and evenly lit without hot spots.  
-Slightly bump up your camera or phone's exposure value (+EV) to ensure whites look bright white rather than dull gray.
