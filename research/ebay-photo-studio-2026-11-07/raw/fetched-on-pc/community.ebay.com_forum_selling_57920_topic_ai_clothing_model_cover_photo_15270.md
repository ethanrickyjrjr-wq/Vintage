##### A.I. clothing model cover photo
I am a pre-owned clothing seller. I take all my own photos and include them in my listings. I do not use stock photos.
Lately I have been using a cover listing photo of an A.I. model rendering wearing the front view of the item I am listing.
The A.I. models I use are supplied by a well know online photo editing business.
Since they are, I believe them to be royalty free.
Are there any known issues with the eBay platform selling policy the prevents using an A.I model wearing a listed item?

Even if the images generated are free, I'd use it with caution. Those AI tools can alter the clothing in subtle ways, and things like the way the clothes fit/hang/drape on the AI model versus a real person may not be accurate (which can be very important when it comes to some womens clothing.) They may increase the risk of INADs. There are different model body types (regular size, plus size etc.) but they still may not be accurate as to how they will look on the same real life body type. Granted, the issue is still there with catalog photos etc., but with AI there is possibility for error or unexpected variance.
I'd use a photo of the item itself as the first photo, not the AI model one. It will also show you are not a drop shipper using a stock image. Put real pictures of the item, then pictures of details and flaws, and then the AI model one last, if you choose to use it.
