Easily remove photo and video backgrounds with one click.
Background remover is included with paid plans. See [Understand your AI usage](https://www.canva.com/help/ai-access/) to learn more about AI access and usage limits.
You can access [Background Remover or Add Background tools](https://www.canva.com/help/ai-tools-pages/) without creating or opening a design.
**Does Background Remover add an AI label to your design?** Background Remover uses AI, but it's considered an editing tool. It doesn't add Canva's AI-generated content metadata to your design. Using it on its own shouldn't cause your design to be labelled "AI content" when you post to platforms like Instagram or TikTok.

## Remove photo backgrounds in a folder
You can remove the backgrounds from all supported photos in a folder at once, or select multiple photos in a folder to remove their backgrounds in bulk.
### Remove backgrounds of all images in a folder
DesktopDesktop
MobileMobile
  1. From the homepage, go to **Projects**.
  2. Hover over the folder you want to edit, then select (More).
  3. Select **Remove background**.


You’ll see the progress as Canva removes each photo’s background. Once complete, Canva creates a new folder named **"[folder name] - Edited"**. Your original photos and folder stay unchanged.
### Remove backgrounds of selected images inside a folder
DesktopDesktop
MobileMobile
  1. From the homepage, go to **Projects**.
  2. Open a folder.
  3. Hover over the images you want to edit, then tick the checkbox.
  4. From the toolbar at the bottom of the screen, select **BG Remover**.


You’ll see the progress as Canva removes each photo’s background. Once complete, the edited photos are added to the same folder.
### **Limitations**
  * **Paid plans only:** Background Remover is available with paid plans. See your plan options on our [_pricing page_](https://www.canva.com/pricing/).
  * **Up to 50 images at a time:** Folders with more than 50 images can’t use Background Remover. The option appears grayed out.
  * **One folder at a time:** You can only remove backgrounds from one folder at a time.
  * **Uploaded images only:** Only uploaded images will be processed. Canva library photos and designs added to a folder will be skipped.


## The Remove background option is grayed out for a folder.
### What can cause this
The folder contains more than 50 images.
### What to do
  * Remove some images from the folder until it contains no more than 50 images.
  * Select another folder that contains 50 images or fewer.


## Some photos are missing from the new remove background folder.
### What can cause this
  * Canva couldn’t detect a clear subject in some uploaded images.
  * Some items in the original folder are Canva library photos or designs. Only uploaded images can be processed.


### What to do
  * Check the progress for images marked **“An error has occurred.”** Images that Canva couldn’t process remain in the original folder.
  * Check whether the missing items are Canva library photos or designs. These are skipped during bulk background removal.
  * Check the new **"[folder name] - Edited"** folder for the images Canva successfully processed.


## Remove photo backgrounds in Canva Sheets
Easily clean up images in your sheet by removing backgrounds with just a few clicks.
DesktopDesktop
MobileMobile
  1. **Select the cells** with the image you want to edit.
  2. Click **Actions** in the floating toolbar.
  3. Choose **Background Remover** to remove the background automatically.
  4. Wait for the tool to process the image.
  5. Click **Apply** to save the changes.


**Background Remover** appears in the toolbar when one or more image cells are selected.
### Remove photo backgrounds in Canva AI
Ask [Canva AI](https://www.canva.com/help/using-canva-ai/) on the homepage to remove your photo’s background. This is available on paid plans only.
DesktopDesktop
MobileMobile
  1. From the homepage sidebar, select **Canva AI**.
  2. Select the **plus** **icon**.
  3. Select **Upload** to choose a photo from your device. You can also choose a photo from your [connected cloud storage app](https://www.canva.com/help/import-to-canva-ai/).
  4. Enter a prompt such as _“Remove background”_ or _“Delete the background”_.
  5. Press **Enter**(Windows), **return** (Mac) on your keyboard, or **Submit** to send.
  6. Wait for Canva AI to reply with the result in the chat. 


Once the result is available, you can do the following: 
  * Select the photo then the **Use Canva editor** button to open it in a design and make more edits. You can also use the erase and restore brushes in the editor to refine the result.
  * Select the download icon to save the photo to your device. 
  * Select the menu icon to copy, rate, or report the result. 


Canva AI doesn’t support [_video background removal_](https://www.canva.com/help/background-remover/#remove-video-backgrounds/) yet, but you can still do it in the editor. See Remove video backgrounds section below. 
### Remove photo backgrounds in Ask Canva
While in the editor, select Ask Canva and request to remove the background in a photo.
DesktopDesktop
MobileMobile
  1. Select an image. 
  2. From the floating toolbar, select **Ask Canva**. You’ll be redirected to the Canva AI panel.
  3. Enter a prompt such as _“Remove background”_ or _“Delete the background”_.
  4. Press **Enter**(Windows), **return** (Mac) on your keyboard, or the arrow icon in the chat to send.
  5. Wait for Canva AI to apply the result directly to your image.


### Use the erase and restore brushes 
After removing the background, use the Pixel Eraser's erase and restore brushes to fine-tune your result.
When you're in this mode, zooming and undoing or redoing brush strokes won't work.
DesktopDesktop
MobileMobile
  1. Reselect **BG remover** from the editor.
  2. Select **Erase** or **Restore** brush. You can use the **Brush size** slider to adjust your brush size.
  3. Drag the brush over the parts of the photo you want to erase or restore. 
  4. To see the original version while editing, select **Show original image.**
  5. Click the **Undo** or **Redo** icons to **Undo** or **Redo** a brush stroke.


For best results, try zooming in and moving around the image when editing. Learn more about [_mouse and trackpad gestures for brush tools_](https://www.canva.com/help/brush-gestures/). 
You can also remove backgrounds on your uploaded photos from **Projects**. Learn more about [_uploading and editing photos in bulk_](https://www.canva.com/help/image-editor/?query=Remove%20photo%20and%20video%20backgrounds).
Currently, Background Remover only works for photos **under 9MB** in size. It's not available for Vector images. 
Any uploaded photo with a resolution of more than 10MP will also be downscaled to 10MP after removing its background.
If the background remover isn’t loading for you, check your internet connection and try again.

## FAQs
### Does Background Remover use AI?
Yes, Background Remover uses AI technology to detect and remove backgrounds automatically. It's considered an everyday editing tool, and not a content generator.
### Will using Background Remover get my post flagged as AI-generated on social media?
It shouldn't. Canva only adds AI-generated content metadata when a feature creates entirely new content or significantly changes it, like Magic Media or Magic Edit. Background Remover doesn't do either of these, so it doesn't add that metadata to your design.
### My design was flagged as AI even though I only used Background Remover. What should I do?
[_Contact Canva Support_](https://www.canva.com/help/contact-us/) and share your design link and where you posted it. We're actively looking into reports like this.

Drag the handle up to expand the timeline, or down to reduce its height. Use timeline controls Playhead : The vertical line that shows your current position in the timeline. Drag it to preview or move through your video. Time ruler : Displays video timing in seconds and minutes along the top of the timeline. Track area : The space where you place and arrange video clips, audio, and elements. Play controls : Play, pause, and preview your video while editing. Plus button: Add videos, photos, backgrounds, or a blank page to the main track of your video. Add Audio button : Use this to browse and add music or sound effects. How do tracks work? Tracks help organize your video so each part plays at the right time and in the right order. Main track : This is the thick track in the timeline with the plus button beside it. Use this for your main videos, photos or background clips. Clips in this track play one after another with no gaps. Upper track : Use this for text, graphics, or other visuals that appear on top of your video. You can also drag anything from the main track into upper tracks to layer your media on the timeline (overlays). Clips here can overlap and play at the same time. Audio tracks : This is a separate section for music, sound effects, and voiceovers. Layering rule : Clips in higher tracks will appear on top of clips in lower tracks when your video plays, just like in the Layers panel. Why does my old video design look different? Older video designs created before October 2025 still use the page-based structure. These aren't automatically converted to the new timeline to preserve your work. All new video designs use the updated timeline with multi-track editing. What happened to the (+) button in the timeline? The (+) button now adds media directly to the timeline instead of creating new pages. Can I go back to the old editor? The new timeline is now the standard for all new video projects. The timeline gives you more control and matches standard video editing workflows. Existing designs keep their original structure and can be duplicated and edited if you wish to continue using the old timeline.
