## 2. Adobe Photoshop, best for advanced editing control

## FAQ
**What is ghost mannequin photography?** Ghost mannequin photography (also called invisible mannequin photography) is a technique where clothing is photographed on a mannequin, and then the mannequin is digitally removed. The result shows the garment as if it is floating in 3D space, displaying its shape and fit without any visible support. AI tools now automate this process, replacing hours of manual Photoshop work.
**Can I create ghost mannequin photos without a model or mannequin?** Yes. Most AI ghost mannequin tools accept flat-lay photos, hanger shots, or even photos of worn garments. The AI generates the 3D ghost mannequin effect without requiring a physical mannequin at all, and our guide to [shooting ghost mannequin photos with no form](https://www.wearview.co/blog/ghost-mannequin-photos-without-a-model) covers the stuffing and hanger tricks that get you a usable source photo. WearView's [ghost mannequin product photography](https://www.wearview.co/ghost-mannequin) feature works with any garment input type.
**What is the best free AI ghost mannequin tool?** Musely.ai offers a free ghost mannequin tool with no sign-up required, but with limited daily processing and lower quality. For better results with free credits, WearView provides free credits on signup that you can use for ghost mannequin generation along with other AI fashion photography features.
**How much does AI ghost mannequin photography cost?** Costs range widely. Free options like Musely.ai exist but with quality trade-offs. Subscription plans start from $14/month (Photta) to $99/month (WearView Advanced). Hybrid AI + human services like Pixelz charge from about $0.95 per image, plus a $0.50 invisible mannequin add-on. For most e-commerce sellers, budget $29-49/month for a good balance of quality and volume.
**Is AI ghost mannequin photography good enough for Shopify and Amazon?** Yes. Modern AI tools produce results that meet or exceed the image quality requirements for Shopify, Amazon, eBay, and Etsy. The output resolution and garment detail preservation from tools like WearView, Photta, and PixFocal are marketplace-ready without additional editing.
**What is the difference between ghost mannequin and invisible mannequin?** They are the same technique. "Ghost mannequin" and "invisible mannequin" both refer to removing the mannequin from product photos to create a 3D floating garment effect. Some tools also call this "hollow man" photography. The AI approach is identical regardless of terminology.
**How long does AI ghost mannequin processing take?** Pure AI tools like WearView deliver results in under 15 seconds. Most AI-only platforms complete processing within 30-60 seconds. Hybrid services like Pixelz that include human review take 1-3 hours for enterprise and next-morning for standard tiers.
**Can I use ghost mannequin AI for all clothing types?** Ghost mannequin AI works best with structured garments like shirts, jackets, blazers, dresses, and pants. Results vary with very thin fabrics, heavily draped items, or accessories. Most tools handle standard apparel categories well, including t-shirts, blouses, hoodies, and outerwear.
**What is the ghost mannequin technique in traditional photography?** The traditional ghost mannequin technique requires photographing the garment on a mannequin (front and back), then manually stitching the images together in Photoshop while removing the mannequin layer by layer.
