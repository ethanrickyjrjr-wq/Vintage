##### Ebay's Background Removing Tool Greatly Improved
Maybe I am just late to the party but I don't remember seeing any reference to this subject. I had given up using Ebay's background removing tool long ago as it simply did not work well the vast majority of the time. But fairly recently they made some sort of major improvement and now it is working wonders most, not all, but most of the time. I just went through all my holiday listings and will, as time permits, revisit most of my photos over the coming weeks.

Improved to what end?
As noted in another thread, eBay is adding a uniform gray background to seller images that have already had the background removed. In other words, turning a pure white background to gray for no good reason.
Why have a tool at all if that's the case? There are better tools than eBay's remover. Sellers who take the time to remove their backgrounds before listing end up with gray anyway.
