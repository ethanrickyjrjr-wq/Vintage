By "doubling the amount of white background," do you mean eBay is converting rectangular images to squares? If so, then I say that's good.
While on regular website search you may see rectangular images, on mobile search you will not. Those rectangular images get cut off and cropped into a square so you'd lose half the pants. The same applies to certain areas of the website view (saved search feed, for one).
The tool is doing you a favor by adding white space to create a square. You're doing yourself a disservice to crop it back into a rectangle.

I do not see puckering at the seems. I do see the edges of the pants have a slight waviness, if that's what you're referring to. But you can see where that came from by comparing it to your original image. For example, the fabric on inseam of the pants in your original image does have that waviness and that's what the tool is reading.
The fabric on the outer seam of the pants in your original image does not have that waviness, however the background fabric is crinkled which causes a wavy shadow along the edge and that's what the background remover tool is reading. If you used a flat surface instead of a wrinkly sheet, it may resolve the issue you described as "puckering."
The fact that you're taking screenshots and re-cropping is not helping. That's degrading the resolution (a lot) and exasperating the wavy edges.
