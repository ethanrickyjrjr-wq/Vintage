Remove an image background in one simple step using quick action.

[ Open Adobe Express ](https://express.adobe.com/tools/remove-background?promoid=9J8K491Z&mv=other)
Open a design in the Adobe Express editor.
Select the image in your design that you want to remove the background from.
In the left Image panel, select Remove Background. To restore the background, select the image and select Remove Background again.
Note
The tool automatically identifies your subject and removes the background to create a clean cutout. It may also remove fine details or low-contrast areas. Review the cutout and restore anything important before finalizing your image.
Select Download to save the image to your device.
Tip
To download an image with a transparent background, click outside the canvas, then select Background in the left panel and select No fill. When downloading the file, make sure to choose Transparent PNG as the file format.
