Ebay does not require a white background but the big internet marketplace does.
Many sellers who do not use a white background suffer fewer views because the thumbnails with a busy background do not clearly show buyers what they are selling and they do not click through from the search.
If you are getting lots of impressions and few views do some experimentation to determine whether your images are creating this situation.
I looked at your listings and you do not have any problems with any of the backgrounds you are using.
I find the parallax problems on some of your listings less than professional, but they are better than a lot of seller's images. Same with the underexposed backgrounds on some.
