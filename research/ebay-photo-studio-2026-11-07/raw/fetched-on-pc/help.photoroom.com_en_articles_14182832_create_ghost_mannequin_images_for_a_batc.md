# Use AI tools to generate images in Batch (Web App)
How to use the various AI tools to add AI-generated images to your Batch of images. Web App.
C
Written by Craig Wright
Updated this week
Table of contents
[Steps (Web App)](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/16001503-use-ai-tools-to-generate-images-in-batch-web-app#h_d5e9ea6199)[Related content](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/16001503-use-ai-tools-to-generate-images-in-batch-web-app#h_5a5b2ba4fc)
In Batch, the **AI tools** work in a similar way to the standalone AI tools. The important difference is that you can use them to **generate multiple images at once**. For example, you could use:
  * Ironing to remove wrinkles from clothing images
  * AI Fashion Models to show models wearing your clothing products
  * Recolor to fine-tune or change the color in your images
  * Product Staging to show your products in realistic scenes.

# Steps (Web App)
The following steps show you how to access the AI tools when working in the Batch Editor (Web App). The video shows AI Fashion Models as an example.
**Note:** The video has been edited to show faster AI generation and export times.
  1. [Create a batch of images.](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/14170189-create-a-batch-of-images-web-app)
  2. Select **AI tools** in the sidebar.  
​  
Alternatively, you can select a batch image and choose an AI tool from its pill menu.   
​
[![AI tools being selected from the image tile's pill menu](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h69nsblw/2712713215/9b6a10e015535b103426170f7652/ai-tools-in-batch-pill-web.png?expires=1794096000&signature=fd04e801e7f083f70ce9b69e9aa7614c04e27d9af7d876f54cff3e7f92f31e42&req=dicmFM5%2FnoNeXPMW1HO4zRxKWD6tfWTU4RSRAxjMcxjJda%2FVekZvrXmqYANf%0AwSYV%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h69nsblw/2712713215/9b6a10e015535b103426170f7652/ai-tools-in-batch-pill-web.png?expires=1794096000&signature=fd04e801e7f083f70ce9b69e9aa7614c04e27d9af7d876f54cff3e7f92f31e42&req=dicmFM5%2FnoNeXPMW1HO4zRxKWD6tfWTU4RSRAxjMcxjJda%2FVekZvrXmqYANf%0AwSYV%0A)
  3. Select an AI tool (for example, Ghost Mannequin or Product Staging).  
​  
​**Note:** **Product Fixer** is not included in the AI tools, but you can access it from an image's pill menu. For details, see [Fix product details on batch images (Web App)](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/15690725-fix-product-details-on-batch-images-web-app).  
​
  4. Select the images you want to use as the source images.
  5. Use the AI tool settings to define what you want in the generated image.   
​  
For details on each of the settings, refer to the "How to use ..." articles for each of the AI tools (see the **Related content** section below).  
​
  6. Select **Generate**.  
​  
Photoroom uses the selected AI tool to generate new images. The new images are based on the source images and your chosen settings. They are added to your batch.  
​
  7. [Optional] Use the other Batch Editor options to make further changes, such as adding text, graphics, or shapes.
  8. [Download your batch images](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/14178972-download-batch-images-web-app).
