## Recommendations for Ricky

1. Start in the cheapest tier. Two softboxes at 45 degrees plus a backdrop 5 to 6 feet or more behind the form matches the one setup the sources agree on for clean edges. With a small space, use a wide paper roll curved down to the floor and keep the form a step or two forward so the shadow does not climb the backdrop.
2. Prefer 5,500 to 5,700K bulbs, CRI 90+ where listed, and the same bulbs in every light, so colour is consistent across a batch.
3. The cheap tier gives one light problem: nothing lights the backdrop. If it reads grey, add the third light (Neewer 660) aimed only at the backdrop.
4. For cut-outs, keep the form away from the paper and keep the garment edge distinct. For white or cream garments, use a pale grey or coloured backdrop rather than white, per the eBay light-for-dark guidance, and let the rembg or Birefnet step whiten it. This is not confirmed by a source, so test it on the example photos before buying paper.
5. Keep a soft shadow: shoot with the form on the floor sweep, then keep or re-add the shadow as a separate layer during processing.
6. Buy a 53" paper roll first (about $45). Skip vinyl until paper proves too fragile; no vinyl price was found.
