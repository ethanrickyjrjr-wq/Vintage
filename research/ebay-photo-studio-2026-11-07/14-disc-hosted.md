## Findings

### Photoroom
- SEARCH_SNIPPET, https://www.photoroom.com/api/remove-background : title "Background Removal API: $0.02 per Image". CARRIED from cutouts/02: same page said "for $0.02 per image", accepts PNG, JPEG, WEBP or HEIC (so HEIC is accepted on the API).
- SEARCH_SNIPPET (third parties, conflicting): Basic API plan listed as $20/month plus $0.02 per image; Image Editing API (AI backgrounds, shadows, relighting) $0.10 per image on a Plus plan at $100/month; Enterprise custom. Default rate limit 60 images per minute per official page, though a Photoroom blog claims 500. A third-party page claims a 1,000-image minimum, which contradicts pay-as-you-go; treat as unreliable.
- SEARCH_SNIPPET, https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/12523455-free-accounts-and-commercial-use : the summary quotes "You cannot use a free account for commercial purposes." and that with a paid subscription "you can use content generated by the Photoroom AI for commercial purposes", with the burden on the user to hold the legal right. This matches the CARRIED terms quote that Free Accounts are for "personal, non commercial purposes". Consequence: Ricky needs a paid plan, not free, for eBay listings.
- SEARCH_SNIPPET, https://docs.photoroom.com/image-editing-api-plus-plan/ghost-mannequin and https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/12353253-how-to-use-ghost-mannequin : Ghost Mannequin exists in iOS, Android, Web App (including a batch article, 14182832) and Shopify; uses AI credits per generation; accepts uploaded photos; API default output 1K (1024x1024), "2K and 4K limited to Enterprise plans". The API marketing page is about removing mannequins and hangers and says model-to-ghost-mannequin at scale needs sales. The Android help article notes the AI interprets the garment, so output can differ from the real garment. This matters for resale: a generated garment can misstate color, trim or flaws, which is an eBay accuracy risk.
- SEARCH_SNIPPET: AI Fashion Models is said (one third-party review) to be on every paid plan. Web app batch caps vary by source (500 per month Pro, 1,500 Max in one; 250 per batch in another). Free plan export caps conflict (100 vs 250 per month, watermark). Do not budget on these.
- SEARCH_SNIPPET, https://www.photoroom.com/customer-stories/depop : Depop integrated Photoroom background removal and shadows into its listing flow; "Photoroom is free for Depop sellers"; dated April 24, 2026; vendor-written.

### Pixelcut
- SEARCH_SNIPPET, https://www.pixelcut.ai/api/background-remover : "5 Credits per image = $0.05 per image". Consumer plans per third parties: Pro about $10/month ($8 yearly), Business $30/month ($24 yearly), 1,000 and 2,000 batch exports; commercial license on Pro and Business, not listed for free. One review says Pixelcut is now called Pixa (UNVERIFIED). HEIC: not found.

### remove.bg
- CARRIED (VERIFIED earlier from https://www.remove.bg/pricing): "background removal is moving to Canva. The standalone website will no longer be available from 1 December 2026"; "Credits expire December 1, 2026"; "Go to Leonardo.ai for API Pricing"; $3 for 3 credits PAYG, $9/month for 40 credits.
- SEARCH_SNIPPET, https://www.remove.bg/faq : "We're now migrating remove.bg's background removal functionality into the Canva platform". Nero blog says the site closes 1 Dec 2026 9:00 AM CET and API users go to Leonardo.Ai. Do not build on it; use up any bought credits before 1 Dec.

### Canva background remover
- SEARCH_SNIPPET, https://www.canva.com/help/background-remover/ : third parties say it requires Canva Pro, Teams, Education or Nonprofits, and free users may hit an AI credit limit. Price, bulk and commercial terms not found. This is where remove.bg users are being sent, so worth a fetch.

### Adobe Express, Photoshop Express
- SEARCH_SNIPPET, https://helpx.adobe.com/lv/express/web/create-and-edit-images/edit-images/remove-background.html : transparent PNG needs Background, "No fill", file format Transparent PNG; warns it "may also remove fine details or low-contrast areas". CARRIED: upload "JPEG (JPG), PNG, or WEBP and up to 50MB" (HEIC not listed). A 2026 article claims the Express remover moved to Firefly (UNVERIFIED), and that batch uses generative credits. Older Adobe community thread says Express has no batch; Photoshop desktop batch via File > Automate > Batch. Free-plan limits (one source: 5 MB uploads) and commercial terms not confirmed. Photoshop Express (mobile) was not found in any result.

### Claid.ai
- SEARCH_SNIPPET, https://claid.ai/blog/article/remove-bg-api-migration and https://claid.ai/api-pricing : background removal "costs 2 API credits per image", resize and crop in the same request free; trial 50 web plus 50 API credits (about 25 cutouts by API). Web plans: 1 credit per cutout; Essential $15/month for 500 credits (monthly) or $9 (yearly) per different sources; Pro $35 for 2,000 credits. API credits beyond trial bought through sales per one source. Claid markets a remove.bg migration path. HEIC and commercial terms: not found.

### Pebblely
- SEARCH_SNIPPET (vendor comparison checked 19 Oct 2026): no free plan; Lite $9/month for 30 images, Basic $19 for 200, Pro $39 for 500. Older listings claim a free 40 images. Paid plans carry commercial license per one source. It generates scenes around products; no ghost mannequin or garment-on-form feature found. Weak fit for clothing.

### Pixian.ai
- SEARCH_SNIPPET, https://pixian.ai/pricing : API is paid by credit pack, priced by megapixels: "0.25 MPx image is 0.05 credits and a 25 MPx image is 1 credit", free to integrate and test. Third-party estimate $0.0024 (2 MP) to $0.0166 (25 MP) at 10,000 images a month; older $0.02 figure from August 2025. Dollar per credit not retrieved. Likely the cheapest hosted API if a script is wanted; output transparent PNG (UNVERIFIED), HEIC unknown.

### Clipdrop
- SEARCH_SNIPPET: Clipdrop is reported to be under Jasper, not Stability (June 2026 review, one source). One check says clipdrop.co/apis/pricing redirects to a contact form (Sept 2026); other sources give 100 free dev credits and packs from $9 or $50 per 100 credits. Conflicting, no reliable price. Web free tier about 20 removals per 24 hours at limited resolution. Skip unless a quote is easy.

### Cutout.pro, Slazzer, Erase.bg, Fotor
- Cutout.pro SEARCH_SNIPPET: Basic $5/month for 40 credits, Pro $16.90/month for 200; about $0.084 to $0.13 per image; free preview low resolution (500x500); API exists.
- Slazzer SEARCH_SNIPPET: about $11 to $13.20/month for 100 credits up to about $518 to $622 for 10,000 (roughly $0.05 to $0.06 each); credits valid 2 years per one source; API access.
- Erase.bg SEARCH_SNIPPET: no reliable price; bulk goes through Pixelbin.io per one guide.
- Fotor SEARCH_SNIPPET: consumer app, no API price found, Pro priced in rupees on one page.
- Commercial-use terms and HEIC support for all four: not found.

### Built-in and platform tools
- iPhone Photos SEARCH_SNIPPET: press and hold subject to lift it (iOS 16 or later), free, offline. Files app Quick Action "Remove Background" can run on several images and saves PNG per one guide (https://www.howtogeek.com/how-to-quickly-remove-the-background-from-images-on-iphone-or-mac/). Fine for tests, hard-edged on fringe and glass (UNVERIFIED), a cutout of a garment on a mannequin will also remove the mannequin only if the model classes it as background (UNVERIFIED).
- Windows: sources conflict whether Paint or Photos does it; a Photos tutorial describes a background brush with a Remove button (https://www.elevenforum.com/t/remove-background-of-image-in-photo-viewer-app-in-windows-11.19741/). Manual, one image at a time.
- Google Photos on iPhone: search found only Magic Eraser (a dated article says Google One), no subject cutout. UNVERIFIED.
- eBay app SEARCH_SNIPPET, https://innovation.ebayinc.com/stories/ebay-uses-computer-vision-to-enable-sellers-to-create-cleaner-images/ : computer vision removes the background and replaces it with white; first photo often cleaned automatically when separability is near 100%; seller can touch up. https://www.valueaddedresource.net/ebay-sellers-lose-background-removal-in-app/ reports the tool once turned the item white and the background black and was disabled, and that manual brush and eraser were removed on desktop. A June 2024 PetaPixel piece says eBay added AI-generated backdrops on iOS (US, UK, Germany). Current 2026 behavior, free or not, and whether it is app-only: not confirmed. Output is white JPEG, not transparent.
- Vinted: no native background removal found; third-party apps only (Shopfront, SnapEdit, VendyStudio etc.). Depop: Photoroom built in (above).

## Recommendations for Ricky
1. Do not pay any hosted tool before the local pipeline (rembg with birefnet, per cutouts/02) is tried; only Photoroom has a verified $0.02/image rate and verified "no pixel change" claim in the earlier run.
2. If a hosted fallback is wanted for hard items, use the Photoroom API at a paid level, never the free account. Test Ghost Mannequin on 3 garments and compare to the real item before using it, because the AI redraws the garment and eBay buyers return items that do not match.
3. Avoid remove.bg entirely (ends 1 Dec 2026). Skip Clipdrop, Fotor, Erase.bg (no usable price).
4. Pixian.ai is the one to price-check as a cheap API alternative; Claid for resize plus cutout in one call.
5. The eBay app white-out is free insurance for a quick listing but gives white JPEG with failures reported; keep own cutouts as the master.
6. iPhone lift-subject is a good zero-cost spot check on the phone.
