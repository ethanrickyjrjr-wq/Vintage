2. SEARCH_SNIPPET. The only real data on mannequin vs model vs flat lay is an eBay Research Labs paper, "When relevance is not Enough: Promoting Visual Attractiveness for Fashion E-commerce" (arXiv 1406.3561). It compares Mannequin, Person and Flat displays. A secondary summary says human-model display "significantly boosts clicks, watches, and final purchases". Snippet tables put the flat share of clicked items near 41% and mannequin near 34%; these numbers are unchecked and the snippet was cut off. This favours person over mannequin over flat, but is from 2014, on mostly new apparel, not vintage. Sources: https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1406.3561 and https://www.arxiv.org/pdf/1406.3561
3. SEARCH_SNIPPET. A Journal of Research in Interactive Marketing paper (169 and 156 participants) says mannequin pictures suit category/browse pages (product evaluation) and human-model pictures suit the product page. Source: https://www.emerald.com/jrim/article-abstract/17/3/470/250559/Can-you-imagine-yourself-wearing-this-product
4. SEARCH_SNIPPET. Blondes in Heaven (a vintage shop's guide): use a dress form or model to show how a piece hangs; "Flat-lay photography can hide drape problems, fit issues, or shoulder distortion." Also: pair photos with measurements because vintage sizing is unreliable, and avoid heavy retouching that erases texture and patina. Source: https://blondesinheaven.com/blogs/vintage-fashion-guide/how-to-photograph-vintage-clothing
5. SEARCH_SNIPPET. ProductScene (Etsy): at least one model or dress form shot per listing; flat lays as supporting images; claims shops using only flat lays "consistently see lower conversion rates". This is the author's assertion with no data. Source: https://productscene.com/product-photography/clothing-product-photos-for-etsy

## House style recommendation for Hollie (judgment, built on the above, not proven)

- Photo 1 (eBay main, and crosslist cover): garment on the vintage form or mannequin, white background, whole garment in frame with even margins, same crop ratio (square, 1600 px or more) for every listing. Clean-first suits eBay search thumbnails and the cutout pipeline.
- Photo 2: one in-room shot of the same piece on the form against the burgundy wall with a lamp or neon edge. This is the Etsy and Shopify cover and carries the brand. Keep it second on eBay so the white-background rule is never at risk.
- Photos 3 to 4: back view, and a flat or hanging view for pieces where drape on the form misleads.
- Photos 5 to 8: label and maker tag, fabric close-up, closures and hardware, every flaw close-up with the same light (flaw shots build trust on vintage).
- Final photo: measurements card (pit-to-pit, shoulder, length, waist, sleeve) shot flat with a tape visible, same card layout every time. Also put the numbers in the description text.
- Count: 8 to 12 per listing on eBay, well under the 24 limit. Do not retouch texture away; whiten the background only.
- Use flat lay only for denim, tees, scarves and accessories, where the form adds little.
