## Why the huge price spread?
The gap between $40 and $800 for the same pink gooseberry listing comes down to form (a rare chip-and-dip or promotional piece beats a common bowl), completeness (full nesting sets bring 2 to 3x the sum of singles, and original lids matter), condition (dishwasher fading and utensil marks cut value hard), and the stamp (all-caps PYREX is vintage Corning; lowercase pyrex is modern with little collector value).
