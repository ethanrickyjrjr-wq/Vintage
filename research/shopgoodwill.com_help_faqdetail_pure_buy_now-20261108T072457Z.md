##  [](/help "Back to help") What is a pure Buy Now item? 
Pure Buy Now items are items that are available for immediate purchase. The item listings remain active on the site until a payment is received from a buyer. Pure Buy Now Items do not end until the item is paid for, or until the listing reaches its ending date and time. Because of this, **_Pure Buy Now items cannot be combined for payment or shipping with any other items._**
  

To purchase a Pure Buy Now item, click on the Buy It Now icon in the item listing and complete checkout.
  

If you click on the Buy It Now icon but do not complete your payment, the item listing will remain active on the site and will be available for purchase by another buyer.
  

To remove an unpaid pure Buy Now Item from your Open Orders, click on the Delete icon in the Action column to the right of the item listing in your Open Orders page.
