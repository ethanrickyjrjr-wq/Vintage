## Leave the Packing to the Pros
Let the certified packing experts at your local The UPS Store® package your items safely and securely. 
![](https://www.theupsstore.com/image%20library/theupsstore/general-content/gc1/gc1_packing-services_1.jpg)
From fragile and high-value items like [artwork](https://www.theupsstore.com/pack-ship/artwork/index) or [electronics](https://www.theupsstore.com/pack-ship/electronics/index), to large or oddly shaped items like [golf clubs](https://www.theupsstore.com/pack-ship/golf-clubs/index) or [sports equipment](https://www.theupsstore.com/pack-ship/sports-equipment), we’ll be happy to help pack your shipment so that everything arrives in one piece.
In fact, we guarantee it, as part of our Pack & Ship Guarantee*.
### Our Pack & Ship Promise®
As long as your items are packed by a participating The UPS Store, using materials purchased at that location, and shipped via UPS®, your shipment will be covered by our Pack & Ship Guarantee*.
Should anything happen to your package in transit, you’ll be reimbursed for:
  * The item’s value (subject to the lesser of actual value, replacement or repair cost)
  * The full retail price you paid for packaging materials and services
  * Your cost for shipping (excluding declared value charges or costs for insurance as applicable)


*For complete details, visit our [Pack & Ship Guarantee](https://www.theupsstore.com/pack-ship/pack-ship-guarantee) page.
