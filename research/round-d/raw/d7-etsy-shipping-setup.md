# How to Set Up Shipping Information 
Streamline your shipping process and entice buyers with competitive shipping rates using Etsy’s shipping tools.
  * Save your shipping settings across multiple listings using shipping profiles. 
  * Offer discounted shipping when buyers purchase multiple items.
  * Offer buyers the option to pay more for faster shipping.


You can also use free shipping to entice buyers to complete a purchase.[ Learn more about offering free shipping.](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000343368?segment=selling)
Starting July 9, orders shipping into the US will need to include duties and import fees upfront to keep your shop eligible for [Etsy Purchase Protection](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/5850122619287?segment=selling). [Learn more and explore resources to help you manage cross-border selling more easily](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1355662653395).

## How do I add shipping rates?
If you don't want to use calculated shipping, add shipping rates when creating or editing a listing. Be sure to set a rate for each country you'd like to ship items. Many buyers only see Etsy listings that ship to their country.
To add shipping rates on Etsy.com: 
  1. Go to your [**Shipping settings**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/shipping-profiles?ref=seller-platform-mcnav).
  2. Under **Shipping profiles** , select **Create profile**.
  3. In the **Shipping prices** section, choose **I’ll enter fixed prices manually**. 
  4. Enter the **Ship from country** and **Origin Zip code** you’re shipping from.
  5. Choose the item’s **Processing time** - how long it will take you to prepare and ship the order.
  6. Choose the **Shipping service**. 
  7. For shipping within your country, under **What you’ll charge** , choose **Free shipping** or **Fixed price** if you don’t want to include shipping costs in the item price.
     * If you choose **Fixed price** , enter how much you’ll charge for **One item** or **Additional item**. The additional item rate will be applied if a buyer purchases more than one of this item or another item within your shop, and this listing’s shipping is less or equal to the other item.
  8. If you’d like to sell the item outside your country, set a rate for **Everywhere Else** or choose **Add another location** to set your own rates for specific locations.
     * **Everywhere Else** will cover anywhere else you don’t choose your own rate for. You can remove it by selecting the trash can icon if you only want to sell to specific countries.
  9. Choose **Add a shipping upgrade** to offer buyers the option to pay more for faster shipping.
  10. Choose **Save as shipping profile** to reuse this information on multiple listings.
  11. Select **Apply**. 


To add shipping rates in the Etsy Seller app: 
  1. From the **Home** menu, choose **More**. 
  2. Under **Shop settings** , choose **Shipping**. 
  3. Under **Shipping profiles** select the **+** button. 
  4. In the **Shipping prices** section, choose **I’ll enter fixed prices manually**. 
  5. Enter the **Ship from country** and **Origin Zip code** you’re shipping from.
  6. Choose the item’s **Processing time** - how long it will take you to prepare and ship the order.
  7. Choose the **Shipping service** you want to offer.
  8. For shipping within your country, under **What you’ll charge** , choose **Free shipping** or **Fixed price** if you don’t want to include shipping costs in the item price.
    1. If you choose **Fixed price** , enter how much you’ll charge for **One item** or **Additional item**. The additional item rate will be applied if a buyer purchases more than one of this item or another item within your shop, and this listing’s shipping is less or equal to the other item.
  9. If you’d like to sell the item outside your country, set a rate for **Everywhere Else** or choose **Add another location** to set your own rates for specific locations.
    1. **Everywhere Else** will cover anywhere else you don’t choose your own rate for. You can remove it by selecting the trash can icon if you only want to sell to specific countries.
  10. Choose **Add a shipping upgrade** to offer buyers the option to pay more for faster shipping.
  11. Enter a **Profile name**. 
  12. Choose **Save profile** to reuse this information on multiple listings.


## Can I opt out of selling to specific countries? 
You can use shipping profiles to prevent your products from being sold in specific countries. Buyers in those countries will be unable to purchase listings connected to those shipping profiles. 
To select which countries you sell to on Etsy.com: 
  1. Go to **Shop Manager** and select**Settings**.
  2. Select [**Shipping settings**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/shipping-profiles?ref=seller-platform-mcnav).
  3. Under **Shipping profiles & processing**, scroll to **Shipping profiles**.
  4. Select the pencil icon to edit an existing calculated shipping profile. 
  5. In the **What countries will you ship to?** section, you can choose which countries you want to sell to. 
  6. After completing all remaining fields, select **Save profile**.


To select which countries you sell to on the Etsy Seller app: 
  1. From the **Home** menu, select **More**. 
  2. Under **Shop settings** , select **Shipping**. 
  3. Select the **…** button of the calculated shipping profile you want to change and select **Edit**. 
  4. In the **What countries will you ship to?** section, you can choose which countries you want to sell to. 
  5. After completing all remaining fields, select **Save profile**.


## How do I create a shipping profile?
To reuse the same shipping settings on multiple items, apply a shipping profile. If you edit a shipping profile, it will update on every listing you’ve applied it to.
Save a shipping profile after adding shipping rates to a listing, or use the [Shipping profiles](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/shipping-profiles) page to create and manage your profiles.
To add a shipping profile to a listing on Etsy.com: 
  1. Select **Shop Manager**.
  2. Select [Listings](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/listings).
  3. Select the checkbox of the listing that needs a shipping profile.
  4. Select **Editing options**.
  5. Select **Change shipping profiles** from the dropdown menu.
  6. In the next window, select the **Select profile…** dropdown menu and choose a shipping profile.
  7. Select **Apply** to save your changes.


A listing can only be connected to one shipping profile.
To add a shipping profile to a listing on the Etsy Seller app: 
  1. From the **Home** menu, choose **Listings**. 
  2. Select the listing you want to add a shipping profile to. 
  3. Select the **Listing** tab. 
  4. Scroll to the **Shipping details** section and select the pencil icon. 
  5. Select **Use shipping profile instead**. 
  6. Select the shipping profile you want to use from the list. You can also choose **New shipping profile** to create one. 
  7. Choose **Save**. 


## **How much should I charge for shipping?**
US sellers can use the[ Etsy Shipping Calculator](https://www.etsy.com/shipping/us/price-your-postage) to determine rates with UPS, USPS or FedEx.
US or Canada-based sellers can use[ calculated shipping](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115013946647?segment=selling) if you ship with USPS or Canada Post. Enter the weight and dimensions of the item and select the shipping services you want to offer. When a buyer enters their address and chooses a service, Etsy will calculate the shipping and add it to the cost of the order.
If you live in another country or don’t want to use calculated shipping, you can add your own shipping rates to your listings.
  * You determine your own shipping rates, but the[ Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/22925492768) can help guide you.  

  * Get tips and advice from other sellers in the [Shipping](https://community.etsy.com/forum/shipping-306/) section of the Etsy Forums.


### **What if I sell very bulky or heavy items?**
If you sell very heavy items, like furniture, consider mentioning in your listing descriptions that shipping prices vary. Ask buyers to contact you before checking out for a shipping quote. Then, create a custom listing for your buyer. [Learn how to create a custom listing.](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015663107?segment=selling)
## **How do I offer combined shipping?**
When you set fixed shipping rates, you can offer discounted shipping for multiple items when you add a rate for **One item** and an **Additional item**.
  * **One item price** : The cost of shipping the item alone.  

  * **Additional item price** : The cost of shipping this item when added to an additional item in your shop. This could be either a different listing in your shop or more than one of the same item.


To calculate the shipping rate, Etsy takes the listing with the most expensive **One item** price and then adds each **Additional item** price.
  
For example:  
|   | **One item**  | **Additional item**  |  
| --- | --- | --- |  
| Item A  | $2.00  | $0.50  |  
| Item B  | $1.00  | $0.75  |  
  * If a buyer purchases **Item A and Item B** , the total shipping is $2.75. We’ll take **Item A’s One item** price of $2.00, since it’s most expensive, and **Item B’s Additional item** price of $0.75.
  * If **three Item A** are purchased, the total shipping would be $3.00. We’ll take the **One item** cost of $2.00 and add two **Additional item** costs of $0.50 each.


### If you offer calculating shipping
It isn't possible to set discounted rates for listings with [calculated shipping](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115013946647?segment=selling) because the precise cost of shipping multiple items is calculated for you.
If some of your listings have fixed rates and others have calculated shipping, we first add the calculated shipping on those listings in the order as normal. For the fixed rate listings, we’ll add your **Additional item** rates.
## How do I add shipping upgrades?
You can offer your buyers shipping upgrades so they can opt for faster shipping at checkout. 
If you’re in the US or Canada and offer calculated shipping, shipping upgrades are available based on which shipping services you’ve chosen to offer in your calculated shipping settings.
Otherwise you can enable shipping upgrades as part of setting fixed rates.
To enable shipping upgrades from Etsy.com: 
  1. Go to your **Shop Manager**.
  2. Select **Settings**.
  3. Choose [Shipping settings](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/shipping-profiles).
  4. Select the **Rates & upgrades** tab.
  5. Choose **Enabled** under **Shipping upgrades**.


After enabling shipping upgrades, you can add them to specific listings or shipping profiles.
To add shipping upgrades to a listing:
  1. Sign in to Etsy.com and open **Shop Manager**.
  2. Choose **Listings**.
  3. Select [**Add a listing**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/listings/create) or open one of your listings.
  4. Under **Shipping** , next to **Shipping option** , select **Select profile** or **Create option**.
     * If you’re in the US or Canada, select **I’ll enter fixed prices manually** from the dropdown menu in the **Shipping prices** section.
  5. Scroll to **Shipping upgrades** and select **Add a shipping upgrade**.
  6. Fill out the details. 
  7. Select **Save and Apply** to save your changes.


To enable shipping upgrades from the Etsy Seller app: 
  1. From the **Home** menu, select **More**. 
  2. Scroll down to **Shop settings** and select **Shipping**. 
  3. From the top navigation menu, scroll over to **Rates & upgrades**. 
  4. Under **Shipping upgrades** , select **Enabled**. 


### How are buyers charged for shipping upgrades?
The upgrade price is added to the initial item cost you set.
When adding shipping upgrades, you can enter either a **Domestic** upgrade, an **International** upgrade, or both.
In an order for multiple items, if a shipping upgrade is available on one item, it will be available for the buyer to select. The cost will apply to every item in the order, even for items whose shipping profiles don't offer the upgrade.
Your location and the shipping address for the buyer determine the cost of shipping for you. The shipping price the buyer sees is based on the settings in your shipping profile. 
On Etsy, you can offer fixed-price shipping, or calculated shipping if you purchase USPS or Canada Post shipping labels through Etsy.

Here are the solutions to some common issues with shipping prices, costs, and profiles:
**Why is my calculated shipping cost wrong?**
**The order contains multiple items**
There are a few reasons that an order with multiple items may have incorrect shipping costs:
  * The combined dimensions of the items in the order may not fit into your [chosen package preferences](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000344628?segment=selling). Calculated shipping splits the order into multiple packages unless all items can fit into one package, which can increase shipping cost. You can reduce item dimensions so that the correct number fits into your available packages, or find a package that works for this order.
  * The items may have different shipping origin postal codes. Check the [shipping profiles](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115014115187?segment=selling) of all listings in the order and [update any with an incorrect origin postal code](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115013946647?segment=selling).
  * The order may combine items with calculated shipping and fixed-cost shipping. In this scenario, we determine the shipping cost for all calculated items, and add the “Additional item” costs for all fixed-cost items, regardless of what the costs are. To fix this, you can either use calculated shipping for all listings, or change the “Additional item” price for your fix-cost listings.


**The order only contains one item**
**There are a few reasons that an order with only one item may have incorrect shipping costs:**
  * You may want to change your shipping rate type. If you use USPS calculated shipping, you have the option to charge the Etsy Shipping Label rate or the USPS retail rate. If you use Etsy’s labels, you likely want to use the Etsy Shipping Label rate. If you go in person to the post office, you’ll likely want USPS retail rates. You can edit your shipping rate type from the [Rates & upgrades tab](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/shipping/settings).
  * You may have an old origin postal code set for the shipping profile for this item. Check and update the origin postal code in the listing’s [calculated shipping profile](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115013946647?segment=selling).


**Why is my fixed shipping cost wrong?**
**The order has multiple items**
If the shipping price for an order with multiple items is too high or too low, it may be because of the shipping price for an additional item. 
On a [fixed-cost shipping profile](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115014115187?segment=selling), you can set the price of shipping an additional item that uses that same profile. If you'd like to offer a discount for shipping additional items, you'll want to include an "Additional item" shipping cost that's lower than the item's "One item" shipping cost.
If an item(s) in this order has a different shipping profile or no profile, the highest "One item" shipping cost of all the items is charged, then the "Additional item" cost for each additional item in the order is added to that.
To check that all listings use the same shipping profile:
  1. From your [**Listings** page](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/listings), select **More filters**. 
  2. Select the shipping profile you want to see, or listing that don't have a profile associated with them ("No Shipping Profile"). 


**The order has a shipping upgrade**
Fixed-cost shipping upgrades add to the base shipping cost, rather than replace it. For example, a base shipping cost of $5 with an upgrade cost of $10 will charge the buyer $15 total for shipping.
**How do I set different shipping costs for variations of a listing?**
[Variations](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015664047?segment=selling) don’t have separate shipping costs. If you have a listing that varies greatly in size or weight, the best way to reflect accurate shipping costs is to list each variation in a [different listing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015628707?segment=selling).
**Why can’t I set up calculated shipping?**
There are a few reasons you may not be able to use calculated shipping:
  * Currently, only sellers who use USPS or Canada Post to send orders can use calculated shipping. If you’re in a different country, can’t use these carriers, or ship your items as letter mail or freight, you’ll need to [set fixed-cost shipping rates](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115014115187?segment=selling). 
  * Calculated shipping is not available yet on the Android version of Sell on Etsy. If you use the Sell on Etsy app on an Android phone or tablet, you’ll need to use Etsy.com to set up or edit calculated shipping settings.
  * Calculated shipping can’t be used on private, custom listings. 


If none of these apply, select **I still need help** and Etsy Support can look into your issue.
I received an order from a location I don’t ship to 
If you received an order from a location you don’t ship to you have a few options. You can choose to: 
  * [Cancel the order](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015587347?segment=selling)
  * Request that the buyer pay additional postage through a [custom listing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015663107?segment=selling)
  * Ship at your expense


**Why did I receive an order from a location I don’t ship to?**
This can mean that an international shipping destination was enabled by mistake. If a listing or shipping profile shows that it ships to the destination “Everywhere Else”, the listing ships worldwide. 
You can remove “Everywhere else” by clicking the trash can icon on its right side in the listing’s **Shipping** section or on the [shipping profile](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/shipping-profiles). 
This issue is often related to some listings not using a shipping profile. You can check for listings without shipping profiles from your Listings:
  1. Go to your [**Listings**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/listings) page. 
  2. In the **Shipping profiles** filter, select **No Shipping Profile**. 
  3. Select the checkbox on these listings.
  4. Select **Editing options**.
  5. Select **Change shipping profile**.
  6. Choose the shipping profile you want to apply to these listings.
  7. Select **Apply**.


I ship from multiple locations 
Each listing can have only one shipping origin. If you fulfill orders using multiple different distribution centers, your options are separate listings, or [separate shops](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360017604474?segment=selling).
Why is my ship-by date or processing time incorrect? 
There are a few reasons why your processing time or ship-by date might seem incorrect:
  * Processing times are in business days (Monday through Friday). Orders on Saturday and Sunday are considered Friday orders, in terms of their processing times.
  * Processing times can appear incorrect due to differences in the seller’s, buyer’s, and Etsy’s time zones. 


If your shipping deadline is off by a day, these reasons are the most likely causes. If it’s more than a day, it may be a technical issue. Select **I still need help** and include an example order number to investigate further.
