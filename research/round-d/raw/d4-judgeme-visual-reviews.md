## Questions about photo and video reviews
[Collect reviews for free](https://apps.shopify.com/judgeme?utm_source=photo-and-video-reviews&utm_medium=website)
What are photo, video and visual reviews?
Photo reviews are customer product reviews that include photos. Video reviews include a customer’s video showing or describing the product. Visual reviews is the umbrella term for both. Judge.me lets Shopify stores collect and display them alongside star ratings and written feedback.
How do I collect photo and video reviews on Shopify?
Install Judge.me and set up automatic review request emails. Customers can add photos or videos when they leave a review. If a customer has already left a text-only review, the Awesome plan lets you send a media reminder inviting them to add photos or videos.
Are photo and video reviews free with Judge.me?
Yes. Judge.me’s Forever Free plan includes unlimited photo and video reviews and core displays. The Awesome plan costs $15 USD per month and adds more design options and media reminder emails. [Compare all plan features](https://judge.me/pricing).
How can I display photo and video reviews in my store?
Use a gallery to showcase customer photos, a carousel to browse photo or video reviews, or a product-page widget that combines media, ratings and written feedback. Judge.me offers ready-made [review widgets](https://judge.me/app/widgets) for different placements. Add the standalone grid and carousel blocks through the Shopify theme editor on an Online Store 2.0 theme.
Can I show only reviews with photos or videos?
Yes. Judge.me supports displays focused on reviews with photos or videos, so you can put visual reviews in the spotlight. Available filters depend on the widget you choose.
Does Judge.me need extensive customisation to look good?
No. Judge.me offers ready-made visual review layouts that you can use with your customer reviews. Built-in design options let you match the display to your brand without starting from scratch. Additional layouts and styling options are available on the Awesome plan.
Do I need to code to make my reviews match my store?
No coding is needed to use Judge.me’s ready-made layouts and built-in design options. Choose a layout and personalise it in the settings. More bespoke changes may need custom code.
Can photo and video reviews show a verified badge?
Yes. Judge.me can show a verified badge on eligible reviews, including photo and video reviews. Adding a photo or video does not make a review verified on its own.
Will my photo and video reviews work on mobile?
Yes. Judge.me’s review layouts adapt to smaller screens so shoppers can browse customer photos, watch video reviews and read feedback on their phones.
