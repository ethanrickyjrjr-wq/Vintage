# Submit to Participate
Do you have videos or photos of a wildfire? Waterspout or something that makes people say "WOW!"? Give us a call at (239) 435-3458 to submit your breaking news. 
To be considered for publishing, please provide a title, a description of the photo or video, and the name of the person who took the photo or video.
Go to [**participate.naplesnews.com**](http://participate.naplesnews.com/) to begin uploading your photos and videos.
