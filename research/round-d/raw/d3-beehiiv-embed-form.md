Embedded subscribe forms (also called a subscribe widget or newsletter signup form) let you add a beehiiv newsletter signup to any external website you own — a blog, storefront, portfolio, or any other site. These forms collect subscriber details like email, name, and birthday, and automatically add new signups to your beehiiv audience. Embedded subscribe forms are fully customizable and can be embedded across external websites to help grow your newsletter beyond your publication site.
As of April 30, 2026, the subscribe form builder was updated with new embed layout options, trigger settings, and a draft/publish workflow. All new forms use the updated builder, and most existing forms have been migrated to it as well. If you have a form that still opens in the previous builder, see the FAQ for how to handle it.
This article covers how embedded subscribe forms work alongside other beehiiv features, how to create and customize a form, and choosing an embed layout and trigger. The FAQ at the end covers common questions including how to manage forms created before the April 2026 update.

## [Quick tips to get you started](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/12977090590487-creating-an-embedded-subscribe-form#h_01JWXTX17E1T9MWQ0XJYAEC1R7-quick-tips-to-get-you-started)
  * **Embedded subscribe forms are for external sites only.** Embedded subscribe forms are designed to be placed on outside websites — like a personal site, blog, or storefront. They're separate from your beehiiv Subscribe page and from [subscribe forms](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/33831754065047-how-to-add-subscribe-forms-to-your-website) built in the Website Builder, which connect to a [signup flow](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/33701445445271-adding-signup-flows-to-your-website-subscribe-forms).
  * **Build audience segments.** You can craft [audience segments](https://beehiiv.com/support/article/9366426644503) based on which embedded form a subscriber used, helping you target specific groups more effectively.
  * **Trigger automations when someone subscribes.** Use the [Email submitted trigger](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/13080928484887) to start an [automation workflow](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/18743686099991-types-of-workflows-how-to-create-automations-for-your-subscribers) whenever someone completes a subscribe form.
  * **Duplicate existing forms before editing.** If you have forms embedded on external sites that you'd like to update, duplicate the existing form first and edit the copy. This keeps your live embed working while you make changes. When you're ready, grab the new embed code and update your site. See the FAQ for more detail.

## [How to create a subscribe form](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/12977090590487-creating-an-embedded-subscribe-form#h_01KQHWBNTKKEEBA40PRQQ847G5-how-to-create-a-subscribe-form)
  1. From the left panel, go to **Subscribers > **[**Subscribe forms**](https://app.beehiiv.com/subscribe_forms). Click **Create new form.**  
  

![Image](https://beehiivhelp.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/40155505540375)
  2. The form builder will open with the **Content** tab active and the **Header** section expanded.  
  

![Image](https://beehiivhelp.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/40155505542551)

### [Name your form](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/12977090590487-creating-an-embedded-subscribe-form#h_01KQHWBNTT17Z3S4EFYE0DZM01-name-your-form)
  1. Click the **pencil icon** next to the name at the top of the canvas to name your form. In the modal that appears, enter a clear, descriptive name, this is for your reference only and won't be visible to subscribers. Click **Save**.  
  

![Image](https://beehiivhelp.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/40155505545495)

### [Using the form builder](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/12977090590487-creating-an-embedded-subscribe-form#h_01KQHWBNTWEA95HFMZJHGVJ859-using-the-form-builder)
Key controls across the top of the builder:
  * **Live preview on the canvas** : The canvas displays a live preview of your form. Changes made in the right panel are reflected immediately.
  * **Undo/Redo** : Use these icons above the canvas to reverse or reapply recent changes.
  * **Save changes / Get embed code** : While editing, the button in the top-right corner reads **Save changes**. Once your changes are saved, it updates to **Get embed code**.
  * **Exit builder** : Click the arrow in the upper-left corner to return to your Subscribe forms list.

### [Setting up your content](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/12977090590487-creating-an-embedded-subscribe-form#h_01KQHWBNTYBZQ58NMQZ7QP8S9Y-setting-up-your-content)
  1. On the **Content** tab, configure the **Header** section:


  * **Title** : Displays your publication name by default. You can update the text or toggle it off.
  * **Subtitle** : If you've added a one-line description in your [publication settings](https://app.beehiiv.com/settings/publication/general), it will appear here by default. You can toggle it off or edit the text to customize it.  



  1. Click the arrow next to **Form** to expand the fields and button controls.  
  

![Image](https://beehiivhelp.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/40155505546135)
  
**Form options include:**


  * **Fields** : The fields on subscribe forms are pulled from the [custom fields](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/7712894720023-using-custom-fields-with-your-subscribers) you've created in your account. The **Email** field is required and appears by default. To collect additional data, click **Add Field** and select from your existing custom fields. You can designate fields as required and add as many fields as you'd like.
  * **Label** : To display a name for a field on the form, toggle on **Label**. You can edit the label text without changing the underlying custom field in your account. Otherwise, Label is disabled when there is only one field.
  * **Button** : Button text is required and defaults to _Subscribe_. You can customize it. Set the message that displays while the form is submitting under **While submitting** , this field is also required.
  * **Use beehiiv Consent Text:** Toggle on to include beehiiv's standard consent text which links to both our [Terms of Service/Use](https://www.beehiiv.com/tou) and [Privacy Policy](https://www.beehiiv.com/privacy).
  * **ToS and Privacy Policy** : Toggle either on to include your own links beneath the form.


**Expert Tip:** The **Slim** layout does not support Terms of Service toggles or additional fields. If you need those elements, use the **Regular** layout instead.
### [Choosing an embed layout and trigger](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/12977090590487-creating-an-embedded-subscribe-form#h_01KQHWBNV5ZPADYVA0GY4C4PA2-choosing-an-embed-layout-and-trigger)
  1. Click on the **Style** tab at the top of the right panel. The **Embed** section will be expanded by default. Here you can configure how and when your form appears on the page.  
  
**Layout** : Click the **Layout** field to open the **Embed layout** selector. Choose a layout type, **Regular** or **Slim** , and an embed type.


  * **Regular** gives you a full form with an optional header, title, subtitle, and additional fields.
  * **Slim** gives you a compact single-row layout with just an email field and submit button.  
  

![Image](https://beehiivhelp.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/40155505546903)
  
**Select an embed type:**


  * **Inline:** Renders directly where you paste the embed code, in a footer, sidebar, article body, or dedicated signup section. Works well with both Regular and Slim layouts.
  * **Slide left** : Slides in from the bottom-left corner of the page.
  * **Slide right** : Slides in from the bottom-right corner of the page.
  * **Popup** : A modal that appears centered over the page.
  * **Sticky top** : A persistent banner fixed to the top of the viewport. Stays visible as the visitor scrolls. Slim works especially well here.
  * **Sticky bottom** : A persistent banner fixed to the bottom of the viewport. Stays visible as the visitor scrolls. Slim works especially well here.


**Trigger** : For any embed type other than Inline, you can configure the trigger when the form appears.   
  

![Image](https://beehiivhelp.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/40155505547415)
  
Click the **Trigger** field to choose from the following options:
  * **Immediately:** The form appears as soon as the page loads.
  * **After time delay:** The form appears after a set number of seconds on the page.
  * **After scrolling:** The form appears after a visitor scrolls to a set percentage of the page.
  * **When an element is visible:** The form appears when a specific page element comes into view.
  * **On click or DOM event:** The form appears when a visitor clicks a specific element or a custom DOM event fires.
  * **Exit intent:** The form appears when the visitor's cursor shows intent to leave the page or desktop.


Under **Frequency** , set how often the form can reappear to the same visitor:
  * **Every visit** : The form appears every time the visitor returns to the page.
  * **After X days** : The form reappears after a set number of days when the visitor returns.

### [Styling your form](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/12977090590487-creating-an-embedded-subscribe-form#h_01KQHWBNVEW4HBRSG54YFJ533A-styling-your-form)
  1. Still on the **Style** tab, click to close the **Embed** section, then click to open the **Background** section to customize the visual appearance of your form. Styling is organized into three sections: **Background** , **Header** , and **Form**.  
  
**
![Image](https://beehiivhelp.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/40155505548311)
**


**Background settings:**
  * **Color** : Click the color swatch to open background options. Use the **Color** , **Gradient** , or **Image** tabs to set the background. Only one option can be applied at a time. Access recently used and preset colors from your theme.
  * **Transparent background:** Toggle on to give the form a transparent background.
  * **Border color, style, and thickness** : Set the color, style (Solid, Dashed, or Dotted), and thickness of the form's border.
  * **Shadow** : Choose a shadow size for the form border, or select None.
  * **Width** : Set the form width, choose from Fit Content, Fixed, Relative, or Fill. Select **Fill** to create a full-width form.
  * **Height** : Set the form height, choose from Fit Content or Fixed.
  * **Direction** : Set the form layout to vertical or horizontal.
  * **X-Align / Y-Align** : Align form content horizontally and vertically.
  * **Padding, Gap, Corner radius** : Adjust spacing and corner rounding in pixels.  



  1. Click to close the **Background** section, then click to open the **Header** section. The **Header** styling section is divided into **Base** , **Title** , and **Subtitle**.  
  

![Image](https://beehiivhelp.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/40155484909591)


**Header settings:**
  * **Base** : Set background color, border color, border style, shadow, thickness, sizing, alignment, padding, gap, and corner radius for the header container.
  * **Title and Subtitle** : For each, set font family, font weight, font size, line height, font color, alignment, and width.


**Expert Tip:** If you've toggled off the Title and Subtitle on the Content tab, you can skip the Header styling section entirely.
  1. Click to close the **Header** section, then click to open the **Form** section. The **Form** styling section is divided into **Base** , **Fields** , **Button** , and **Terms**.  
  

![Image](https://beehiivhelp.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/40155505549847)


**Form settings:**
  * **Base** : Set background color, border, direction, form width, field and button height, padding, gap, and corner radius.
  * **Fields** : Set font family, weight, size, input text, placeholder text color, background color, border, and corner radius for form fields. Under **Label styling** — only applies when **Label** is toggled on for a field: set font family, weight, size, and label color. Under **Radio/checkbox styling** — applies to True/False and List (Multi-Select) custom fields: set accent color and background color. Tech Note: If **Label** is not toggled on for a custom field, the label styling options will not apply.
  * **Button** : Set font family, weight, size, input text, placeholder text color, background color, border, and corner radius for the submit button.
  * **Terms** : Set font family, font size, and font color for the ToS and Privacy Policy label text. Set accent color and background color for the consent checkbox. The Terms styling section only applies when ToS, Privacy Policy, or beehiiv Consent Text is enabled in your [publication's security settings](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/33544946516375-security-settings-for-your-website).

### [Configuring post-submission settings (redirecting or delivering a file after signup) ](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/12977090590487-creating-an-embedded-subscribe-form#h_01KQHWBNVTD6FGWDGJZ2ZS12YB-configuring-post-submission-settings-redirecting-or-delivering-a-file-after-signup)
If you're trying to deliver a file or product after someone subscribes, use the redirect URL to point to an external thank-you or download page, or set up a beehiiv automation using the **Email submitted** trigger to deliver it directly. See[ Using free digital products as lead magnets](https://beehiivhelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/39024852575767) for the beehiiv-native approach.
  1. Click on the **Settings** tab in the right panel to control what happens after someone submits the form.  
  

![Image](https://beehiivhelp.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/40155484911383)


  * **Show a success message** : Enabled by default with an example message. Enter or adjust the confirmation text that appears after submission (up to 80 characters).
  * **Redirect to an external website** : Switch to this option to redirect subscribers to a URL after they sign up. Use the full URL including https:// at the beginning.
  * **Remove emails from the redirect URL** : Enable this to substitute the subscriber's email in the redirect URL with their subscription ID.
  * **Require double opt-in** : Enable this to require all new subscribers through this form to confirm their email address via a double opt-in email. This setting applies only to this form — it will override your publication-wide opt-in setting only if [double opt-in is disabled](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/13081072798743-how-to-enable-double-opt-in-and-smart-nudge) at the publication level.

### [Saving and publishing your form](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/12977090590487-creating-an-embedded-subscribe-form#h_01KQHWBNVXP14Z9G1KEKQST7VT-saving-and-publishing-your-form)
  1. Once you're happy with your form, click the arrow next to **Save changes** in the top-right corner. The button will update to **Get embed code** once your changes are saved.  
  

![Image](https://beehiivhelp.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/40155505552023)
  
**Select from:**


  * **Save & get embed code:** Saves your form and opens the embed code modal. Your live embed keeps showing the previously published version until you explicitly publish, so you can save and iterate without affecting what visitors see on your site.
  * **Save changes:** Saves your current changes without opening the embed code modal. When you're ready to push your changes live, click the dropdown **arrow** next to **Get embed code** and select **Publish**.

### [Getting and using the embed code](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/12977090590487-creating-an-embedded-subscribe-form#h_01KQHWBNVZQNW28BW1Q94TDEV9-getting-and-using-the-embed-code)
  1. After clicking **Save & get embed code**, a modal will appear with your form's embed code ready to copy and paste as a single script tag.  
  

![Image](https://beehiivhelp.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/40155484912663)


  * For **Inline** forms, copy and paste the embed code in the body of the page where you want the form to appear.
  * For **Popup, Slide left, Slide right, Sticky top** , and **Sticky bottom** forms, you can copy and paste the script anywhere on the page HTML, in a header, footer, or any HTML block. The script handles placement based on your embed layout settings.  
  
For advanced setups, copy the **Attribution tracking** script to forward UTM parameters to beehiiv. Paste this script just before the closing <body> tag of the page where your form will appear.  
  
Click **Close** when you're done.


  1. To return to your list of forms, click the **arrow** in the top-left corner of the canvas. Once you publish a new version, the script automatically serves the latest version, so your changes appear on your site without needing to replace the embed code.

### [Embedding tips](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/12977090590487-creating-an-embedded-subscribe-form#h_01KQHWBNW257ET1Z9Q2SMF6H8R-embedding-tips)
  * **Always preview your site** after embedding to ensure the form displays and functions as expected.
  * **Check the form on both mobile and desktop** to confirm it performs across devices.
  * If you're unsure where to paste the embed code, check your platform's documentation or use their support resources.
  * **For mobile-heavy audiences, consider the Regular layout over Slim.** Slim's single-row layout can feel cramped on small screens; Regular stacks fields vertically and adapts better.

## [Duplicating a form across publications](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/12977090590487-creating-an-embedded-subscribe-form#h_01KVTJY483MT1B0M9N4R7B149S-duplicating-a-form-across-publications)
**Note:** This feature is available on Enterprise plans only.
If your account includes multiple publications, you can duplicate an existing subscribe form into one or more of them at once. This saves you from rebuilding the same form manually in each publication.
  1. From the left panel, go to **Subscribers > **[**Subscribe forms**](https://app.beehiiv.com/subscribe_forms).  
  

![Image](https://beehiivhelp.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/41430248521239)
  2. Click the **3 dots menu** to the right of the form you want to copy.  
  

![Image](https://beehiivhelp.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/41430217755671)
  3. Select **Duplicate to publications**.  
  

![Image](https://beehiivhelp.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/41430248524183)
  4. In the modal that appears, click **Select publications** and choose which publications you want to duplicate the form into. You can select multiple publications at once.  
  

![Image](https://beehiivhelp.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/41430248525207)
  5. Click **Duplicate to publications** to confirm.  
  

![Image](https://beehiivhelp.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/41430217758487)


The form is duplicated into each selected publication. Results are shown per publication, so if one destination fails you'll see which one and the others will still complete.
### [What carries over](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/12977090590487-creating-an-embedded-subscribe-form#h_01KVTJY48CB10B5TSRX4JH5B3T-what-carries-over)
The duplicated form includes the source form's layout, styling, content, and post-submission settings.
**Custom fields** : Custom fields on the form are matched to fields in the destination publication by name. If a matching field doesn't exist in the destination, it will be created there automatically. The modal displays an info banner reminding you of this before you proceed.

## [Troubleshooting embedded forms](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/12977090590487-creating-an-embedded-subscribe-form#h_01KQWRTT1A76STJSY5W1H7CBTF-troubleshooting-embedded-forms)
If your embedded form isn't displaying, isn't accepting submissions, or has stopped working after a recent change, try these steps before reaching out to support.
### [The form isn't appearing on the site](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/12977090590487-creating-an-embedded-subscribe-form#h_01KYJ85VM81NFBCZSBMMXAXDZV-the-form-isnt-appearing-on-the-site)
  * **Check that changes have been saved and published on your external website** : Many platforms require you to save or publish after adding custom code before it becomes visible to visitors.
  * **Verify the embed code was copied completely** : Return to the form builder, click **Get embed code** (step 12 above), and confirm the entire code block was pasted into your site correctly.
  * **Check the website platform's embed restrictions** : Some site builders restrict where custom code or JavaScript can be placed. Consult your platform's documentation for guidance on adding third-party embeds.

### [The form is displaying but submissions aren't working](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/12977090590487-creating-an-embedded-subscribe-form#h_01KYJ85VMAA4R833KAQZASPBGT-the-form-is-displaying-but-submissions-arent-working)
  * **Disable browser extensions temporarily** : Ad blockers and privacy tools can interfere with form submission. Test the form in an incognito or private browser window with extensions disabled.
  * **Confirm there are no redirect conflicts** : If a redirect URL has been configured in the form settings (step 10 above) and the form isn't submitting, temporarily remove the redirect, save the form, and test again to isolate the issue.
  * **Test with a fresh email address** : If the email being tested is already subscribed to the publication, the form may appear unresponsive. Use an email address that isn't currently in the audience.
  * **Check for low-contrast label or button text** : If field labels or the submit button's text aren't visible, it's usually a color contrast issue between the text and its background. On the **Style** tab, under **Form > Fields > Label styling** (only available when **Label** is toggled on for that field), confirm the label color contrasts with the form background. Under **Form > Button**, confirm the button's text color contrasts with its background color. Save your changes and publish to see the update live.
  * **Form-level double opt-in override:** Even if double opt-in is off for your publication, an individual form can require it. Go to **Subscribers > Subscribe forms**, open the form, click the **Settings** tab, and check **Require double opt-in**. If it's on and you don't want it, toggle it off and save.
  * **Form stuck on ‘Subscribing…’** : Wait a few seconds before resubmitting, since a brief delay is sometimes normal. If it's still stuck, disable browser extensions (especially ad blockers and privacy tools), then try again in an incognito window or a different browser.

### [The form stopped working suddenly](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/12977090590487-creating-an-embedded-subscribe-form#h_01KQWRTT1ET95N12F0THVXXKB8-the-form-stopped-working-suddenly)
If an embedded form that previously worked is now failing, this may be related to the April 30, 2026 builder update, please see the FAQ below for how existing forms were affected.   
Otherwise:
  * Check the [beehiiv status page](https://www.beehiivstatus.com/) for any reported service interruptions.
  * Navigate to **Subscribers > **[**Subscribe forms**](https://app.beehiiv.com/subscribe_forms) to confirm the form still exists in the account and hasn't been deleted.


**Expert Tip:** To confirm whether the issue is specific to your site's environment, test your embed code on a simple HTML page. If the form works there, the problem is likely tied to your website's configuration rather than the form itself.
If you've tried these steps and the issue persists, please select **Ask beehiiv** in the left panel of your account and ask the beehiiv Agent to help you submit a support ticket, or submit one directly from the [Help page](https://app.beehiiv.com/help).
### [The popup or slide form is appearing multiple times](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/12977090590487-creating-an-embedded-subscribe-form#h_01KST9R8C4SPBH6FSNT5K73A6A-the-popup-or-slide-form-is-appearing-multiple-times)
If a Popup, Slide left, Slide right, Sticky top, or Sticky bottom form is appearing more than once on the page, or requires closing more than once, the embed script has likely been added to the page more than once. Because the script handles placement automatically, adding it multiple times causes multiple instances of the form to load.
To fix this, review your site's HTML and confirm the script tag appears only once per page. It can be placed anywhere in the page HTML — header, footer, or an HTML block — but should not be duplicated.
### [New subscribers seem delayed or missing from the list](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/12977090590487-creating-an-embedded-subscribe-form#h_01KYJ86A8EHKGZ75HP12CJGJ5G-new-subscribers-seem-delayed-or-missing-from-the-list)
Ask the subscriber whether they saw a success message or received a confirmation email. If they did, the subscriber likely completed the signup and just needs a page refresh, since the subscriber list doesn't auto-update.

## [Frequently asked questions about embedded subscribe forms](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/12977090590487-creating-an-embedded-subscribe-form#h_01JWXTX17FSKA0MG9KF2WYE6BJ-frequently-asked-questions-about-embedded-subscribe-forms)### [Existing forms and migration](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/12977090590487-creating-an-embedded-subscribe-form#h_01KQHWXHGBN11ZKMBH6N68VET7-existing-forms-and-migration)What happened to my existing forms after the April 2026 update?
Most existing forms were automatically migrated to the updated builder and will open with the new experience when you edit them. A small number of forms that have not been edited in a long time may still open in the previous builder. In either case, your live embed is unaffected, what changed is the editing experience, not what's rendering on your site.
I want to update an existing form that's already embedded on my site. What's the safest way to do that?
Duplicate the existing form first, then edit the copy. This keeps your live embed running without interruption while you make changes. When you're ready, click **Get embed code** on the new form, replace the embed code on your external site, and the updated form will go live. You can leave the original in place and unpublish or delete it once you've confirmed the new one is working.
**Tech Note:** Editing and saving an existing form generates a new embed code. If you edit the original directly without updating the embed code on your site, your external site will continue to show the previously published version until you replace the code.
Can I have multiple embed layouts for the same form?
Each form has a single embed layout. To run the same signup experience as both an inline form and a popup, duplicate the form and change the embed layout on the copy. Both forms will add subscribers to the same beehiiv audience.
Does changing the embed layout of an existing form affect my live site?
Yes. Because a form has a single embed layout, changing it will affect how the form appears on every page it's currently embedded on. If you want to try a different layout without disrupting your live embed, duplicate the form and change the layout on the copy instead.

The embed code is a single script tag that loads your subscribe form. It handles placement automatically based on your embed layout settings; inline forms render where you paste the code, while popup, slide, and sticky forms are positioned by the script regardless of where it sits on the page.
Where does my form name appear in beehiiv?

Why isn't my Terms of Service or Privacy Policy showing on the form?
If either field is left blank or the URL isn't properly formatted, it won't appear on your form. Make sure you include the full URL, starting with https:// — for example, https://yourwebsite.com.
How do I delete an embedded subscribe form?

**Reminder:** If you delete a form that was used to collect subscribers, any data tied to that form, including how a subscriber joined, will be removed from their profile in beehiiv. Proceed with caution when deleting active or previously used forms.
How does beehiiv protect against bots and spam submissions?
Embedded subscribe forms use the same bot protection system as the rest of the beehiiv platform. If a submission is flagged as suspicious, the subscriber will be prompted to complete a verification challenge inline within the form before it submits. All communication with beehiiv's servers is encrypted via HTTPS to ensure subscriber data is transmitted securely.

Embedded subscribe forms do not route subscribers through multi-step signup flows. You can configure a single redirect URL on successful submission. To use a multi-step signup flow, [build your subscribe form](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/33831754065047) in the Website Builder instead.
Can I configure a redirect after someone subscribes through an embedded form?
Yes. In the **Settings** tab of the form builder (step 10 above), switch the post-submission behavior to **Redirect to an external website** and enter the full URL you want subscribers sent to after they sign up. Include https:// at the start of the URL.
A few things to keep in mind:
  * **The redirect opens in the parent page's browser tab.** Some platforms restrict how iframes can navigate the parent page using sandbox or security policies. If your redirect isn't firing after submission, check your platform's documentation for iframe navigation restrictions, or contact that platform's support team.
  * **Embedded forms support one redirect URL.** They do not route subscribers through multi-step signup flows. To use a signup flow with recommendations, surveys, or additional steps, [build your subscribe form in the Website Builder](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/33831754065047-how-to-add-subscribe-forms-to-your-website) instead.
  * **Using a legacy site?** You can combine a redirect URL with the [Recommendations widget for a legacy website](https://beehiiv.com/support/article/21215417091991) to give subscribers a two-step post-signup experience.
