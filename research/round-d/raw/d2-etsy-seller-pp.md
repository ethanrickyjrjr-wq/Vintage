# What is Etsy's Purchase Protection for Sellers? 
If an order is eligible for [Etsy Purchase Protection](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/5850122619287?segment=selling), Etsy will cover up to $250 of a refund (or the converted equivalent in your local currency), and any remaining amount will be charged to the seller. You won’t need to issue the refund yourself. Etsy Purchase Protection may apply in the following situations:
  * A buyer never received their package (for example, if it was lost in transit)
  * Their item arrived 7+ days after its [estimated delivery date](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360001922768?segment=selling) window provided at checkout
  * Their item arrived damaged (for the first instance per calendar year)
  * Their item matches the listing description and photos, but a buyer claims it does not


To make sure your orders are eligible for Etsy Purchase Protection, you’ll need to follow the eligibility criteria outlined in our [Etsy Purchase Protection for Sellers](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/seller-protection-policy/34509585385) and remain in[ good standing with Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/seller-protection-policy/34509585385#order), and meet our [customer service standards](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360036207794?segment=selling). 
For cases opened prior to May 7, 2026, refer to the [Etsy Purchase Protection policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/purchase-protection-program-for-sellers/1478632171992) in effect at that time.
## What isn’t eligible for Etsy’s Purchase Protection program? 
The following situations will make an order ineligible for Purchase Protection: 
  * The order does not meet one or more of the [eligibility requirements for Etsy Purchase Protection](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/seller-protection-policy/34509585385).
  * The item doesn’t match the listing description. We strongly recommend using accurate photos and descriptions when listing an item.
  * You obtain insurance from a third-party source, or have carrier and/or payment processor coverage. We ask that you first submit a claim to your primary source of coverage instead. 
  * Your shop is not in good standing for violating [Etsy policies](https://www.etsy.com/legal/sellers/).


Sellers are responsible for refunding costs for orders that fall outside of these eligibility requirements.
See our article of [best shipping practices ](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360023131333-How-To-Reliably-Ship-Your-Orders?segment=selling)to ensure you’re following the proper steps for your order to arrive undamaged. 
Buyers are responsible for paying any customs duties, import duties, or other charges imposed by their country's government, payable on delivery. Items that arrive late or are not received due to buyer delay or refusal to pay charges owed upon delivery, do not qualify under Etsy's Purchase Protection Program. In this instance, sellers are not responsible for refunds but may offer them at their discretion.
## How do I qualify for Etsy Purchase Protection? 
Etsy Purchase Protection will automatically cover qualifying orders with no setup required from the seller. 
The Etsy Purchase Protection process only begins after:
  1. A buyer reaches out to you about an order through a Help request
  2. The buyer opens a case about the order


Once a buyer has taken both of these actions, Etsy will review the order and make a resolution. If the order is eligible for Purchase Protection, Etsy will refund the buyer, and you’ll keep your earnings from the sale.
Our [guide to case resolution](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360016126873-How-to-Resolve-a-Case-or-Help-Request-from-a-Buyer?segment=selling#h_2) gives an in-depth breakdown on how the case system works, and the steps that should be taken to manage buyer issues. 
Orders are eligible for Etsy’s Purchase Protection program if: 
  * The order was shipped within your [stated processing time](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015588087-How-to-Set-Processing-Times-and-Ship-By-Dates?segment=selling)
  * [Valid tracking](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015774228-How-to-Add-Tracking-and-Complete-an-Order-?segment=selling) was provided and/or it was mailed out with a [shipping label purchased through Etsy](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360001967188?segment=selling)
  * Your listing has an [estimated delivery date](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360020601674-What-Is-an-Estimated-Delivery-Date-?segment=selling), which includes transit times and, where applicable, an origin zip code
  * The item received by the buyer [matches the photos and description](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015628707-How-to-List-an-Item-in-Your-Etsy-Shop?segment=selling) on your listing
  * You meet Etsy’s [customer service standards](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360036207794?segment=selling)
  * You responded to the buyer’s Help with Order message within 48 hours
  * It is the first instance in the calendar year of an item arriving damaged
  * Duties and import fees are included upfront if the order is shipping into the US


If an order meets these requirements, Etsy will cover up to $250 (or the converted equivalent in your local currency) of the refund amount, even if the order total exceeds $250. Sellers are responsible for refunding costs for orders that don’t qualify for Etsy Purchase Protection.
In addition to these requirements, you must ship orders on time and provide tracking when available. Etsy may also use carrier tracking scans, in addition to sellers marking the order as shipped and uploading valid tracking, to confirm that an order was shipped on time.
To help protect the full order value, consider [purchasing shipping insurance](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360001988847?segment=selling) for orders over $250.
In some situations, a specialist may need to reach out for more information about the order. It’s important that you respond to requests for information in the [case log](https://www.etsy.com/your/cases/shop/open) within the time frame given to show both the buyer and Etsy that you are working towards a solution.
Etsy Purchase Protection continues to evolve to better support sellers. Your feedback helps shape how the program improves over time. [Share your thoughts in our survey.](https://survey.alchemer.com/s3/8697830/Etsy-Feedback-8163333496)
If you still have questions about a **closed case** for an item that **arrived damaged** or **different than described** , you can reach out to us to dispute the outcome.
### **How it works**
  1. Share what happened on the next page. The more specific you are, the better we’ll be able to help.
  2. A trained specialist will review your case and outcome to make sure everything aligns with Etsy’s policies and Etsy’s Purchase Protection program. 
  3. You’ll get a final decision via email within 1-3 business days.
