# Shipping with USPS
If you're shipping from a fulfillment location in the United States, then you can [buy shipping labels](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/fulfilling-orders/shipping-labels/buying-labels/buying-labels) from USPS. You can ship your packages domestically and internationally.
The shipping rates that you're charged are based on Shopify's account with USPS.
#### Note
Not all packaging types are eligible to receive a USPS tracking number. For example, package tracking isn't supported for the First Class Large Envelope (flat) package type. To learn more, [compare the services that USPS offers for each packaging type](https://www.usps.com/business/business-shipping.htm).
#### Note
Using USPS through Shopify is subject to [the USPS terms of service](https://pe.usps.com/text/dmm300/dmm300_landing.htm), [international mail manual](https://pe.usps.com/text/imm/welcome.htm), and [PUB 52](https://pe.usps.com/text/pub52/welcome.htm).

## Domestic shipping with USPS
#### Note
First Class Mail labels can't be voided and are non-refundable. Make sure that First Class Mail service is correct for your shipment before purchasing.
You can ship domestically within the United States with USPS using the following mail types:  
Description of USPS shipping service types and delivery timelines  
| Shipping service  | Delivery timeline  | Description  |  
| --- | --- | --- |  
| [Ground Advantage](https://www.usps.com/ship/ground-advantage.htm)  | 2-5 business days  | An economical and reliable way to ship packages within the United States.  |  
| [Priority Mail](https://www.usps.com/ship/priority-mail.htm)  | 1-3 business days  | Ship orders within the United States.  |  
| [Priority Mail Express](https://www.usps.com/ship/priority-mail-express.htm)  | 1-2 business days  | The fastest way to ship orders within the United States and a money-back guarantee.  |  
| [First-Class Mail](https://www.usps.com/ship/first-class-mail.htm)  | 1-5 business days  | An easy way to ship large envelopes.  |  
| [Media Mail](https://www.usps.com/ship/mail-shipping-services.htm)  | 2-10 business days  | A cost effective way to send media, such as books, music, and other media. For more information, refer to [USPS approved list for media mail.](https://pe.usps.com/text/DMM300/273.htm)  |  
#### Note
You can create and purchase return labels with USPS directly from the Shopify admin. Only domestic returns within the United States are available. Refer to [_Buying return labels_](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/fulfilling-orders/shipping-labels/buying-labels/return-labels) to learn more.
### Package requirements for USPS domestic shipments
The following are the maximum values that USPS allows for domestic shipments:
  * maximum weight is 70 lb (31.75 kg)
  * maximum girth and length combined is 108 inches (274 cm)


For more information about package requirements, refer to the [USPS website](https://faq.usps.com/s/article/Parcel-Size-Weight-Fee-Standards).
### USPS domestic prohibited goods and hazardous materials
Shipping labels do not support the shipping of hazardous materials (HAZMAT) or USPS prohibited goods. As the shipper, it's your responsibility to understand and comply with all USPS regulations and guidelines. Although USPS allows some HAZMAT to be shipped following their rules and regulations, shipments containing these items are prohibited through Shopify and must be shipped through USPS directly.
For more information on domestic prohibited goods and HAZMAT, refer to the [USPS Domestic Shipping Prohibitions, Restrictions, & HAZMAT page](https://www.usps.com/ship/shipping-restrictions.htm) and the USPS [Publication 52](https://pe.usps.com/text/pub52/welcome.htm) - Hazardous, Restricted, and Perishable Mail.
## International shipping with USPS
You can ship internationally with USPS using the following mail types:  
Description of USPS International shipping service types and delivery timelines  
| Shipping service  | Delivery timeline  | Description  |  
| --- | --- | --- |  
| [First-Class Package International Service](https://pe.usps.com/text/imm/immc2_021.htm)  | Varies  | An economical way to ship small packages internationally.  |  
| [Priority Mail International](https://www.usps.com/international/priority-mail-international.htm)  | 6-10 business days  | Ship orders internationally to 190 countries.  |  
| [Priority Mail Express International](https://www.usps.com/international/priority-mail-express-international.htm)  | 3-5 business days  | The fastest way to ship orders internationally to 190 countries and a money-back guarantee to specific destinations.  |  
### Package requirements for USPS international shipments
The maximum values that USPS allows for international shipments vary per destination country. To find the maximum package values, go to the [USPS Index of Countries and Localities](https://pe.usps.com/text/Imm/immctry.htm?_gl=1*13qeceu*_ga*MTQ1MzgwMjAyMS4xNjUyOTU3MTY2*_ga_3NXP3C8S9V*MTY1Mjk1NzE2NS4xLjEuMTY1Mjk1NzMyMS4w) page, and then select your shipment's destination country.
### USPS international prohibited goods and hazardous materials
Hazardous materials (HAZMAT) or USPS prohibited goods aren't supported. As the shipper, it's your responsibility to understand and comply with all regulations and guidelines for the USPS, US, and the destination country you are shipping to. While USPS allows some HAZMAT to be shipped following their rules and regulations, shipments containing these items are prohibited and must be shipped through USPS directly.
For more information on international prohibited goods and HAZMAT, refer to the [USPS International Shipping Prohibitions & Restrictions page](https://www.usps.com/international/shipping-restrictions.htm).
## Getting your products to USPS
You can drop off your products at a [USPS drop-off location](https://tools.usps.com/find-location.htm) or schedule a pickup.
### Schedule a pickup for your USPS shipment
Pickup times are based on the time zone of the pickup address. Any pickup times are estimates and depend on traffic, weather, and pickup address.
Pickups from USPS are free unless you want to specify a pickup time. Free pickups can be scheduled at any time during regular mail hours. It costs $24 USD to select the time of day for the pickup. Each pickup needs be scheduled separately through USPS.
For any assistance with scheduling a USPS pickup, [contact USPS](https://faq.usps.com/s) or call 1-800-ASK-USPS (1-800-275-8777).
#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to [**Orders**](https://admin.shopify.com/orders).
  2. To schedule a pickup, do either of the following:
     * To schedule a pickup immediately after you purchase a shipping label, in the **Package pickup** section, click **Schedule USPS pickup**.
     * To schedule a pickup for an order that was fulfilled with USPS, open the order, click the ![Horizontal menu](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/MenuHorizontalIcon.svg) icon, and then click **Schedule pickup**.
     * Schedule a pickup directly from [USPS's website](https://tools.usps.com/schedule-pickup-steps.htm).
  3. Complete USPS's pickup form.


You can view your scheduled pickups from the [**Orders**](https://admin.shopify.com/orders) page in your Shopify admin.
If a driver doesn't arrive for your scheduled pickup, then call USPS at 1-800-ASK-USPS (1-800-275-8777) with your pickup confirmation number. You can find your pickup confirmation number in the [order's Timeline](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/managing-order-details#view-an-orders-timeline).
### Cancel a USPS pickup
Do one of the following to cancel a USPS pickup:
  * Visit [USPS's pickup scheduler](https://tools.usps.com/schedule-pickup-steps.htm), and then click **Modify an Existing Pickup**.
  * Contact USPS at 1-800-ASK-USPS (1-800-275-8777).


## Signature Confirmation with USPS
You can set your shipments to require a signature upon delivery by adding **Signature Confirmation** when you [buy a shipping label](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/fulfilling-orders/shipping-labels/buying-labels) for your order.
Shipments that use Priority Express Mail include Signature Confirmation by default. You can also pay $2.60 USD to add **Signature Confirmation** to the following USPS mail types:
  * Ground Advantage
  * Priority Mail
  * Parcel Select Ground
  * Media Mail


You can learn more about Signature Confirmation at [USPS extra services](https://www.usps.com/ship/insurance-extra-services.htm) page.
## Shipping date with USPS
You can set a shipping date for your order. The date that you choose will print on the shipping label.
#### Caution
If you specify a shipping date, then the post office can refuse your package if you bring your package on a different date.

## USPS Cubic Pricing
USPS offers discounted rates for packages, envelopes, and soft-packs on Priority Mail and Ground Advantage shipping services. If you typically send small and heavy products, such as weights or coffee beans, then consider using Cubic Pricing.
Review the following table to learn more about maximum shipment size values for USPS Cubic Pricing:  
Description of USPS cubic pricing per shipping service  
| Shipping service  | Dimensions and weight  |  
| --- | --- |  
| [USPS Priority Mail](https://www.usps.com/ship/priority-mail.htm)  | 
  * Maximum weight is 20 pounds.
  * Maximum volume is 0.5 cubic feet.
  * Maximum length, width or height for packages is 18 inches, and for envelopes or soft-packs is 36 inches.

 |  
| [USPS Ground Advantage](https://www.usps.com/ship/ground-advantage.htm)  | 
  * Maximum weight is 20 pounds.
  * Maximum volume is 1 cubic feet.
  * Maximum length, width or height for packages is 18 inches, and for envelopes or soft-packs is 36 inches.

 |  
#### Note
Rolls and tubes don't qualify for cubic pricing.
### Calculating USPS Cubic pricing
USPS offers separate Cubic Pricing for packages, soft-packs, and padded envelopes. You can add small boxes to an envelope as long as the envelope keeps its flat shape. Expandable envelopes don't qualify for the envelope pricing and need to use cubic pricing for packages.
Review the following table to learn how to calculate the volumetric size of your shipment:  
Calculations and examples of USPS Cubic pricing per shipment type  
| Shipment type  | Formula  | Example  |  
| --- | --- | --- |  
| Package  | Length (in inches) x Width (in inches) x Height (in inches) / 1728 = cubic feet  | 14" x 8" x 5" / 1728 = 0.4 cubic feet  |  
| Envelope or soft-pack  | Length (in inches) + Width (in inches) = Total package length  | 14 + 8 = 22  |  
## USPS shipping manifests
If you have multiple shipments to give to USPS, then you can create a shipping manifest that links all your shipments together in a single barcode. USPS also refers to these shipping manifests as _Shipment Confirmation Acceptance Notice forms_ , or SCAN forms.
Creating a manifest means that when USPS comes to collect your shipments, or when you drop off your shipments, USPS can scan the single barcode on your manifest to accept your shipments, instead of scanning each shipment individually.
Each shipping label in the manifest must have the same shipping origin address and the same **Shipping date**.
### Considerations for creating USPS shipping manifests
Review the following considerations when creating shipping manifests:
  * Each shipping label in the manifest must have the same shipping origin address and the same **Shipping date**.
  * Shipping dates for international labels on a manifest need to match the date that the labels were purchased.
  * If a label has been added to a shipping manifest, then it can't be voided for about 15 minutes. After this time has passed, the label can be successfully voided.
  * If you void a shipping label that was added to a manifest, then the manifest is no longer valid. You need to have USPS scan the other shipments in that manifest individually. If you use a manifest that has voided labels on it, then you'll be charged for the voided labels.
  * To add shipping labels to the same day's manifest, you need to purchase them by 9:00 pm local time at the location from where the items are shipped.
  * You can't add labels purchased after the cutoff time to the same day's manifest. If you're buying labels after the cutoff time, then you can postmark them to the next day and add them to the next day's manifest.


### Create a USPS manifest
You can use the [Shipping Manifests app](https://shipping-manifests.shopifyapps.com/login) to create a manifest directly in the app, or from the **Orders** page.
When you create a manifest, labels that meet the following conditions are added:
  * tracking is included in the label
  * the **Shipping date** is the same day that you’re creating the manifest
  * the label hasn’t been added to another manifest


To avoid issues with multiple manifests, it's best to create your manifest at the end of the day before you drop off your shipments, or USPS comes to collect your shipments.
#### Steps:
  1. Create a new manifest using one of the following options:
     * From the app:
       1. From your Shopify admin, go to **Apps** > [**Shipping Manifests**](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/shipping-manifests).
       2. Click **Create manifest**.
     * From the Orders page:
       1. From your Shopify admin, go to **Orders**.
       2. Click **More actions** > **Create manifests**.
  2. Select the fulfillment location. All available shipping labels from the fulfillment location will be added to the manifest.
  3. Click **Create manifest**.
  4. Click **Print manifest**.


### Reprint a USPS manifest
You can reprint a manifest up to 6 months after you created the manifest.
#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to **Apps** > [**Shipping Manifests**](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/shipping-manifests).
  2. Click the manifest.
  3. Click **Print manifest**.


## Getting support for USPS shipments
To get help with your USPS shipment, such as rerouting a package or submitting a claim, you can contact Shopify Support or USPS directly depending on your issue.
### Included insurance coverage for USPS shipments
Shipping insurance typically covers damaged and lost items, and lost shipments. The amount that your shipment is insured for depends on the shipping service that you're using.
The following shipping services have liability coverage:
  * Ground Advantage (up to $100 USD)
  * Priority Mail (up to $100 USD)
  * Priority Mail Express (up to $100 USD)
  * Priority Mail International (up to $200 USD)
  * Priority Mail Express International (up to $200 USD)


You can [add more shipping insurance](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/fulfilling-orders/shipping-labels/shipping-insurance) when you buy a shipping label.
### Submitting a shipping claim for a USPS shipment
If you're using USPS through Shopify and your USPS shipment is lost or damaged, then you need to submit the claim to USPS online within 7 to 60 days of shipping your package. If you purchase additional shipping insurance when you purchase a shipping label in Shopify, then you can [submit your Shipsurance insurance claim](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/fulfilling-orders/shipping-labels/shipping-carriers/shipping-claims) in the Shopify admin.
In certain cases of lost or delayed shipments, USPS offers refunds without the need for an insurance claim. For more information on domestic shipments, refer to the [USPS's domestic refunds page](https://www.usps.com/help/refunds.htm). For more information on international shipments, refer to the [USPS's international refunds page](https://www.usps.com/help/international-refunds.htm).
If your claim is approved, then you receive a check mailed to the address that is in the USPS online account.
### Intercepting USPS shipments
USPS can stop the shipment or redirect your package that isn't out for delivery or already delivered.
You can check if your package can be intercepted by entering the USPS tracking number at the [Package Intercept](https://retail-pi.usps.com/retailpi/actions/index.action) page. If the package can be intercepted, then refer to the [USPS interception instructions](https://www.usps.com/manage/package-intercept.htm) for submitting a request to change your shipment.
