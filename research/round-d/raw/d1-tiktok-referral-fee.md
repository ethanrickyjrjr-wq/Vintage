# TikTok Shop Referral Fee Updates - 2024
05/08/2025
Applies to:United States
![](https://lf16-cdn-tos.tiktokcdn-us.com/obj/static-tx/edenx/deploy/academy_ssr/static/svg/IconAiSummaryCircle.91111565.svg)![](https://lf16-cdn-tos.tiktokcdn-us.com/obj/static-tx/edenx/deploy/academy_ssr/static/svg/IconAiFeature.701b0004.svg)
## AI Summary
**Referral fees increased to 6% per qualified order from April 1, 2024**. For refunds/returns, TikTok refunds the referral fee minus a **Refund Administration Fee** of **20% of the referral fee** , and from **May 15, 2025** this fee is **capped at $5 per SKU**. **No refund admin fee** applies to “buy now refund later” creator sample refunds or eligible pre-shipment auto-cancellations. Settlement is made after delivery with no active refund/return request, subject to the **$1** payout threshold.

**Updates to Referral Fees in 2024** TikTok Shop charges a fee from sellers on all qualified transactions. This policy document defines such a fee as a "referral" fee which encompasses all TikTok Shop fees, with the exception of shipping and tax fees.  
**Starting April 1, 2024,** TikTok Shop will increase the referral fee on all qualified transactions **to 6% per order**.   
All new sellers enrolled in promotional plans will continue on their respective plans until the conclusion of the promotional period.  
These changes are designed to allow us to better serve customers while ensuring that we continue to provide great value.  
For more details on Referral Fees at the category level, please check out:

  * _**Qualified Orders**_ : Orders that have been completed are eligible orders. The baseline GMV used for the calculation will be referred to as Buyer Paid + Platform Discount - Tax.
  * _**Referral Fee**_ : The fee that TikTok Shop charges and collects on all completed orders.

**Refunds and Returns**
Customers can request a **cancel/return/refund request** through the Orders section within the TikTok App. The request can only be made if the order falls within the return period. If a refund, return, or cancellation occurs after the customer successfully places an order, TikTok Shop will refund the referral fee amount for the product(s), minus the appropriate "Refund Administration Fee," which is**20%** of the referral fee.
Starting from May 15, 2025, TikTok Shop updated its Refund Administration Fee rules, as follows:  

  1. For each refund, the maximum "Refund Administration Fee" for each SKU type is capped at **$5**.

  1. Refunds for Creator Sample Orders: For "Buy now refund later" creator sample orders, no Refund Administration Fee will be charged when processing refunds.
  2. If a refund is initiated by the buyer before the order is shipped and meets the [Auto-canceling criteria](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=3995852763301633&from=policy&role=1&identity=1&anchor_link=EB8600D0 "https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=3995852763301633&from=policy&role=1&identity=1&anchor_link=EB8600D0"), no "Refund Administration Fee" will be charged.

**Settlement****Settlement Period Rules** TikTok Shop will settle the order amount with sellersafter the **order is delivered to the customer and if no processing refund or return request for that order has been made.** Payments will be automatically made to the seller's bank account after the Settlement Period Rules and threshold for payment are met, which is $1 USD. Further information on Settlement guidelines can be found in Seller Center.
