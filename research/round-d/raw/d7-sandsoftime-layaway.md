#  Layaway Payment Plan 
**PAYMENT PLAN**
Sands of Time offer a very flexible layaway payment plan for all objects offered for sale on our website. Here's how it works:
  * **Deposit:** A non-refundable deposit of 15% of the total price is required upon ordering. 
  * **Checkout:** Order through our website and select the "layaway" option at checkout. We will then send you a PayPal request to make the initial 15% payment.
  * **Flexibility:** You are not restricted to a set monthly payment; pay what you wish when you wish, however your balance must be paid within six months of the order date or you risk forfeiture.
  * **Title** : Property title remains with Sands of Time Antiquities, Inc. until payment is made in full.
  * **Shipment** : Once payment is complete, we will ship your order by the next business day. 


Cancellation of your layaway purchase will incur a 15% restocking fee. If you exchange or trade up an item purchased on layaway, a 10% restocking fee will be charged on the original purchase. If you would like to take advantage of our layaway system or would like to discuss an alternative option that better suits your budget, please contact us via e-mail or call 1-202-342-0518.
