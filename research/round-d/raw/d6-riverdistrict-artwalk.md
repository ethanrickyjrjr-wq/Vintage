# art…**walk** …applicaton
Calling all artists!
![](https://www.myriverdistrict.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/at-walk-image.jpg)
The RDA welcomes Artist Vendors to participate in selling and/or demonstrating their works specific months of the year during Art Walk. Artist Vendors will be invited to participate once their application is accepted. Please read the list of guidelines for Art Walk before applying.
[APPLY NOW](https://www.myriverdistrict.com/art-walk-applicaton/#apply)
##  **art walk** requirements
**APPLICATION:**
  * Artists are required to complete an application, being as descriptive as possible regarding their artwork.
  * If approved, the artist will be contacted directly.
  * Applications must be received at least 5 days prior to the event.


**FEE** :
  * The cost of participation is **$50** for a**10×10 space**.
  * Artists are responsible for bringing all materials needed to sell and display their work (tables, chairs, tents.)
  * Artists are responsible for any business dealings, including but not limited to reporting of city/state sales tax, licenses, etc.


**SPACES:**
  * We reserve the right to assign spaces, primarily to ensure that an artist is not located near a store or vendor with similar items.
  * Spaces cannot be shared without our prior knowledge/permission.
  * If your space is occupied upon arrival, typically with a car, PLEASE CONTACT US and we will figure it out.


**INSTRUCTIONS:**
  * DETAILED instructions will be sent a few days prior to the event.
  * Please read and follow all instructions. Any questions, please ask.
  * IF INSTRUCTIONS ARE NOT FOLLOWED:  
We reserve the right to ask you to leave and/or not offer a future opportunity to attend. No refund.


**ITEMS NOT ALLOWED:**
  * There are certain items we cannot allow to be sold at the event. The items are (but not limited to)beaded bracelets, gemstones/crystals, soaps, food/beverage items…etc.
  * If we deem any item to be inappropriate for our event, it must be removed.
  * If items are not removed, you may be asked to leave. No refund.


**SET-UP & BREAKDOWN**: 
  * The artist is responsible for set-up and breakdown on the night of the event.
  * Again, detailed information regarding times and rules will be sent before each event.


**ELECTRICITY** : 
  * Electricity is NOT guaranteed**.**
  * Our power source comes from the city-operated light poles and is often unreliable.
  * We have no jurisdiction over them and cannot be held accountable if the electricity isn’t properly operating during the event.
  * We HIGHLY encourage you to bringsolar-powered or battery-operated lights**.**


**WEATHER** :
  * SWFL weather can be unpredictable at best, but we will make every effort to proceed with an event. 
  * Because rain and gusty wind can occur suddenly, we ask artists to bring whatever is needed to protect themselves and their artwork.
  * The RDA is not responsible for any damage that may occur.
  * Should we decide to cancel an event prior to set-up, your fee will be refunded.


**CONDUCT, ETC:**
  * All artists are expected to act in a professional manner.
  * RDA staff reserves the right to ask an artist/vendor to leave the event for any misconduct. No refund.
  * Any artist/vendor that is asked to leave an event due to misconduct will not be allowed at future events.
  * Misconduct is determined solely by RDA.


Thank you for your interest in Art Walk, we look forward to having you at our events!
