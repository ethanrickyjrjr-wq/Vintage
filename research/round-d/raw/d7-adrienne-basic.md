# E-Design Basic Package
$599.00 CAD
(Plus applicable taxes)

**This basic package is great for the room you****'****ve started but just doesn****'****t feel right. Maybe you have a few pieces that you need to work with but would like to add more. I****'****ll evaluate your current room and make recommendations about how to take it to the next level.**  
**What is a E-Design?**  
E-design is the interior design of a space, done entirely online.
  * It's a way to work with me if you live in another city.
  * If you live locally, it is a great way to get a design that you can DIY.
  * I create the design and you purchase the products and install them.


**How Does It Work?**
  1. Select the E Design package that's right for you budget and needs
  2. Get access to the Digital Design Studio
  3. Fill out Design Questionnaire
  4. Share photos and measurements of the room to be redesigned
  5. Receive an electronic copy of you completed design.


**What does this package include?**  
This package includes one of each of the following
  * **A concept board** -This will help you visualize the feeling we're creating in the room
  * **A Style board** - This is the board with the items we have sourced for your room, they could include paint selections, furniture, art, accessories and window coverings
  * **A floor plan** showing furniture placement
  * **A shopping list** with links to each product you will be purchasing
  * **1 set of revisions** if there are items you don't care for.


**FAQ****'****S**  
**WHAT IS E-DESIGN?**  
I am offering a digital design service so all the work is done online. I’ll never need to  
visit your home so that means whether you live in Victoria or St. John’s I can design a room for you.  
**HOW CAN YOU DESIGN A ROOM WITHOUT VISITING THE SPACE?**  
Once you decide to work with me I will allow you access to my Digital Design Studio.  
This is where we communicate and collaborate. You will be given complete instructions about how to share your photos and room information with me. That’s all I need to create your dream room.  
**HOW WILL YOU BE ABLE TO FIGURE OUT MY STYLE?**  
If you’re not clear on your style at the beginning we’ll figure it out together. I know exactly what to ask and what to look for. Sometimes it’s as simple as understanding what your fashion preferences are that unlock a design style.  
**HOW CAN YOU GUARANTEE THE FURNITURE WILL FIT?**  
The floor plan that I present is created to scale based on the room measurements you provide for me. All of the furniture pieces are selected based on what will fit into the space. I won’t choose anything without considering size first and foremost.  
**WHAT IS INCLUDED IN THE PACKAGE?**  
The Deluxe Package includes a Floor Plan, Concept Board, Style Board, 1 Elevation and a full Shopping List with links to the products you’ve approved.  
**HOW LONG WILL IT TAKE TO RECEIVE MY DESIGN?**  
Once you login to my studio you will be asked to upload your information (a hand drawn floor plan and images of the space) along with inspiration images of rooms you like. Then you’ll complete the questionnaire and we’re ready to start.  
From that point, the design will be delivered to you in 3 weeks.  
**DO I NEED TO DO ANYTHING ONCE YOU BEGIN WORK?**  
You are responsible for providing feedback after you receive the Concept Board within 3 days. If that period passes without a reply your project will be put on hold. You can contact us when you’re ready to restart the process.  
**HOW MANY ROOMS CAN I INCLUDE?**  
Each package purchased is for 1 room. If you have an open concept space, each separate area will be considered a room ie open living and dining room are 2 packages.  
**CAN I MAKE CHANGES TO THE DESIGN?**  
Your package includes 1 round of revisions. If you require additional changes you will be charged an hourly fee.  
**CAN I GET A REFUND IF I DON’T LIKE THE DESIGN?**  
Due to the nature of e-design work no refunds are available once the work has begun.  
**WHO IS RESPONSIBLE FOR ORDERING AND RECEIVING ITEMS?**  
You are responsible for placing the orders and managing the delivery process.  
**CAN I CONTACT YOU ONCE THE PROJECT IS COMPLETE**?  
If you have extra questions regarding you design. I have a separate package called "Ask Adrienne" its perfect for the few extra questions you may have. You will see it linked below under "Related Packages"
