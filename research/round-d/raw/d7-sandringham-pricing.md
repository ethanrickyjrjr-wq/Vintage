# Design Services
So you have landed here because you are possibly interested in persuing E-design Service for your decorating needs? If so, please feel free to browse any of our Design Service options and let us know if we can help you. Additionally, if you have any questions, trepidations about the process, or would like to chat a little more about how this works and how to tackle your design project, please don’t hesitate to [drop us a line](https://www.sandringhaminteriors.com/contact), we would love to hear from you!

**Designer on Call Consulting**  
$175 (1 hr min call or In-home visit) 
Call or meet with me regarding any of your design challenges, questions and/or decorating ideas, and I’ll be happy to guide you. Send me the furniture or decor items you are considering and I will give you my honest advice and if it will work in your space or provide an alternative if requested.
You could ask questions like: 
  * What light fixture should I buy for my dining room? 
  * What color should I paint my kitchen cabinets? 
  * What tile should I use for my bathroom floor? 
  * What artwork should I buy for my wall? 
  * How do I arrange furniture in my bedroom (I will provide a floor plan for this) 


The Designer on Call service does NOT include any concept boards, digital renderings or floor plans. It is solely for advice, consulting, and suggestions for your home. If you’d like to add a concept board, that can be done for an additional fee.
