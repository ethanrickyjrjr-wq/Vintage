# 5 Hours of Design Time
$1000.00 USD
# **How does it work?**
How you can use a 5-hour session:  
1 or multiple video calls totaling 5 hours over a 12-month period of time. 
  * Going shopping? Take me with you to your local store after an initial video or messaging and I'll help you make your design selections right there in the store, all from your cell phone via video or the chat.
  * Great use of time if you have a few questions quick validation questions in different areas in your home. 
  * Have you been searching for the right piece but need a designer eye to find the right item to complete your look? Typically we can find, collaborate and order about 3-5 different product solutions during a 5-hour period. 
  * Use your time to commission floor plan room layouts. Typically 2-3 layout options for 1 room can be derived during this time. 


  
*This is not enough time for a concept board containing a fully sourced room or render.   
Use over the course of 12 months or all in 1 day. Flexible collaboration cumulative time to use at your pace.
