Welcome to Palm Beach Regency, our little slice of vintage lovers’ paradise. We’re the dot on the map where Hollywood Regency flair meets Old Florida charm. We’re obsessed with our signature style, and we hope you feel it the moment you walk through our doors.
Vintage lovers flock to Palm Beach Regency for our timeless furniture and décor. Especially when on the hunt for faux bamboo, Chinese Chippendale, rattan, wicker, lucite, pagodas, ginger jars, shells, and every detail in between that captures our carefree, classic, coastal chic style.
For over 15 years, we've operated a 5,000-square-foot Warehouse in Lake Park, Florida, along with an online shop shipping across the country and around the world.
![](https://palmbeachregency.com/cdn/shop/files/267A1960.jpg?v=1789492039&width=1468)
There are two ways to shop our iconic vintage finds in person**.** Our Showroom is in West Palm Beach's emerging SoSo Design District, on South Dixie Highway just south of Southern Boulevard. Beyond our fabulous vintage furniture and smaller accessories, we also carry new home décor like fun gift finds, entertaining and tabletop, coffee table books and much more. 
Whether you're starting a full decorating project or just need one special piece to refresh or complete a room, we're here to help you brainstorm, dream, and shop. All while having fun along the way!
At the end of the day, it’s all about the thrill of the hunt. You never know what you'll find! We source nearly 100 new pieces of vintage every week, so stop by often. Over scheduled? Follow us on social media for daily new arrivals, events and more.
Happy Hunting!
