#  9 Antiques Accounts To Follow On Instagram  

If you want to add more character to your home, your best bet is usually to shop vintage. Need some fresh inspiration for tracking down the best antique homeware, furniture and accessories? These are the Instagram accounts to follow now…  

By Georgina Blaskey
/ 
[ Save To My Favourites ](https://sheerluxe.com/user/login?destination=/home/accessories-furniture/9-antiques-accounts-to-follow-on-instagram)
View Image Credits
Images: @ALBIE_AND_PEARL; THEANTIKSTORE_; @VINESTREETVINTAGE 
[ ![](https://media.sheerluxe.com/TKwkuN5CM3HjPkP-0l1p8kXtcj4=/800x0/smart/https%3A%2F%2Fsheerluxe.com%2Fsites%2Fsheerluxe%2Ffiles%2Farticles%2F2022%2F10%2Falbie-pearl.png) ](https://www.instagram.com/albie_and_pearl/?hl=en)
## Albie & Pearl 
Established by Marie Pontefract, who has a background in fashion, Albie & Pearl offers curated collection of vintage homeware. From Art Deco cut crystal glassware to Hollywood Regency lighting and Victorian ceramics, the selection reflects Marie’s expert eye for design. There’s also a design consultancy and a bespoke sourcing service available.
Follow [_@Albie_And_Pearl_](https://www.instagram.com/albie_and_pearl/)
[ ![](https://media.sheerluxe.com/k98n2dNDJ3T3w7JHBbtdTaIor64=/800x0/smart/https%3A%2F%2Fsheerluxe.com%2Fsites%2Fsheerluxe%2Ffiles%2Farticles%2F2022%2F10%2Falbie-pearl_0.png) ](https://www.instagram.com/albie_and_pearl/?hl=en)
## Antik 
Antik founder Georgia travels around the UK and Europe building her exceptional homeware collection. Her range of glassware, trays and platters will elevate your tablescapes, and there are plenty of ceramic candle holders to add personality to your setting. Antik also supports a small, independent supply chain of market sellers and charities, so you can think of it as money well spent.
Follow [_@TheAntikStore__](https://www.instagram.com/theantikstore_/)
[ ![](https://media.sheerluxe.com/XkDaaDkg4VWF4VMHUxXP9Aoxn24=/1920x0/smart/filters:no_upscale\(\)/https%3A%2F%2Fsheerluxe.com%2Fsites%2Fsheerluxe%2Ffiles%2Farticles%2F2022%2F10%2Fantik1.png) ](https://www.instagram.com/theantikstore_/)
## Cotswold Antique Barn 
We challenge you to look at this account and not find something you love. The lighting range is worth keeping an eye on, but there are plenty of larger items on offer – plus, the team can source items on request.
Follow [_@CotswoldAntiqueBarn_](https://www.instagram.com/cotswoldantiquebarn/?igshid=YmMyMTA2M2Y%3D)
[ ![](https://media.sheerluxe.com/sOWiEGk018-6aAKC4VrILIgPfj0=/800x0/smart/https%3A%2F%2Fsheerluxe.com%2Fsites%2Fsheerluxe%2Ffiles%2Farticles%2F2022%2F10%2Fcotswolds.png) ](https://www.instagram.com/cotswoldantiquebarn/?igshid=YmMyMTA2M2Y%3D)
[ ![](https://media.sheerluxe.com/bpvkf5gsnHrEFhJ7SYwiWeqqTOg=/800x0/smart/https%3A%2F%2Fsheerluxe.com%2Fsites%2Fsheerluxe%2Ffiles%2Farticles%2F2022%2F10%2Fcotswolds-antique-barn.png) ](https://www.instagram.com/cotswoldantiquebarn/?igshid=YmMyMTA2M2Y%3D)
## Dimity Home 
Dimity Home founders Kelly and Elodie are based in Shropshire and their mission is to find beautiful and useful things for your home and garden. Passionate about local artisans, country house style, gardening and homeware, the pair hand-pick antiques, vintage and artisan finds.
Follow [_@DimityHome_](https://www.instagram.com/dimityhome/)
[ ![](https://media.sheerluxe.com/mLAFCwvVbTpjq16BxrllcCKLhOA=/1920x0/smart/filters:no_upscale\(\)/https%3A%2F%2Fsheerluxe.com%2Fsites%2Fsheerluxe%2Ffiles%2Farticles%2F2022%2F10%2Fdimity-home_0.png) ](https://www.instagram.com/dimityhome/)
[ ![](https://media.sheerluxe.com/6hnUSRDNC0zLyGjX2pLrlmfhuJo=/800x0/smart/https%3A%2F%2Fsheerluxe.com%2Fsites%2Fsheerluxe%2Ffiles%2Farticles%2F2022%2F10%2Fkept-london_0.png) ](https://www.instagram.com/keptlondon/)
## Kept London 
Born out of lockdown, Kept London sells a mixture of vintage and antique decorative items, from small pieces of furniture to paintings, lamps to brassware. The interior images shared by founder Hen are supposed to inspire a few styling ideas and allow customers to envisage how a period piece could work in their home.
Follow [_@KeptLondon_](https://www.instagram.com/keptlondon/)
[ ![](https://media.sheerluxe.com/WTMU_iNMgLdztlMkLmhalRtl02Q=/800x0/smart/https%3A%2F%2Fsheerluxe.com%2Fsites%2Fsheerluxe%2Ffiles%2Farticles%2F2022%2F10%2Fkept-london.jpg) ](https://www.instagram.com/keptlondon/?hl=en)
## Lineage London 
Dorcas Jamieson styles and photographs a carefully curated collection of vintage and antique treasures for sale via Lineage London. A third-generation collector, Dorcas spent her childhood visiting antiques fairs with her mother and grandmother. Her expertise lies in finding elegant white ceramics which will add a pretty but sculptural quality to any display.
Follow [_@Lineage_London_](https://www.instagram.com/lineage_london/?hl=en)
[ ![](https://media.sheerluxe.com/TfxmFMthF7ejw5mlNJOuZS_lEMg=/1920x0/smart/filters:no_upscale\(\)/https%3A%2F%2Fsheerluxe.com%2Fsites%2Fsheerluxe%2Ffiles%2Farticles%2F2022%2F10%2Flineage-london_0.png) ](https://www.instagram.com/lineage_london/?hl=en)
## Louise Spencer Decorative Antiques 
Sourcing mainly from Sweden, France, Italy and the UK, Louise Spencer finds unusual furniture with an international feel. From elegant chaise longue and pretty upholstered chairs to solid chests and rustic tables, the range is extensive and inspirationally shot.
Follow [_@LouSpencerDecorativeAntiques_](https://www.instagram.com/louspencerdecorativeantiques/?hl=en)
[ ![](https://media.sheerluxe.com/-FuSFe_FLn9RNlnJkB5FHQbM0l4=/800x0/smart/https%3A%2F%2Fsheerluxe.com%2Fsites%2Fsheerluxe%2Ffiles%2Farticles%2F2022%2F10%2Flouise-spencer-decorative-antiques.png) ](https://www.instagram.com/louspencerdecorativeantiques/?hl=en)
[ ![](https://media.sheerluxe.com/r75p5Nl7Wo819NLcew9oLN6M7uo=/800x0/smart/https%3A%2F%2Fsheerluxe.com%2Fsites%2Fsheerluxe%2Ffiles%2Farticles%2F2022%2F10%2Flouise-spencer-1.png) ](https://www.instagram.com/louspencerdecorativeantiques/?hl=en)
## Vine Street Vintage 
Self-described as ‘eclectic, eccentric, country house and rock’n’roll’, Vine Street Vintage puts a glamorous spin on second-hand homeware. Expect lots of brass and gold finishes, marble and velvet. 
Follow [_@VineStreetVintage_](https://www.instagram.com/vinestreetvintage/)
[ ![](https://media.sheerluxe.com/ARQykH7xqTgrAHVgwNMkIZ_HPxY=/1920x0/smart/filters:no_upscale\(\)/https%3A%2F%2Fsheerluxe.com%2Fsites%2Fsheerluxe%2Ffiles%2Farticles%2F2022%2F10%2Fvine-street-vintage_0.png) ](https://www.instagram.com/vinestreetvintage/)
[ ![](https://media.sheerluxe.com/RgQvSzpGRnjn89qbf_03l2Mbw5Y=/800x0/smart/https%3A%2F%2Fsheerluxe.com%2Fsites%2Fsheerluxe%2Ffiles%2Farticles%2F2022%2F10%2Fscene-chloe_0.png) ](https://www.instagram.com/scenebychloe/?hl=en)
## Scene By Chloe 
Launched in 2020 when Chloe left her job as a luxury fashion buyer to pursue a lifelong dream of becoming an antiques dealer, Scene By Chloe is an eclectic antiques emporium. Here, you can shop a playful mix of unique design objects including vintage furniture, lighting and art. If you’re into retro or mid-century style, this is the account to follow.
Follow [_@SceneByChloe_](https://www.instagram.com/scenebychloe/?hl=en)
