#  Statistics 
Local tourism research and data provide valuable insight needed to help you make the best marketing decisions possible. View monthly and annual visitor profile reports and review the performance dashboard. A 20-year recap report provides a long-term view of the local tourism industry climate. This section also gives a numerical picture of tourism's powerful impact on the area, and includes information on how local bed tax funds are allocated.
