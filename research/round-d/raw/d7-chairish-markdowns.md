#  Marking an Item Down 
This article describes how sellers can mark down the price of their items, and the benefits of doing so.
This article contains the following sections:
  * [Markdown Benefits](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44170940096657-Marking-an-Item-Down#h_01KQB2YQBDDHH5DWATZGZ7VP9K)
  * [Marking Down an Item](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44170940096657-Marking-an-Item-Down#h_01KQB2XV3DGBXZFWEYGT0WS1M6)


## Markdown Benefits
* * *
Marking down your price is a great way to keep your item fresh and at the top of buyers' minds! Marking your item down will notify anyone who has added the item to their favorites, and will bump your listing to the top of its category and the "Sale" page for extra exposure! Please note that there is a markdown minimum of 10%.
Need help pricing your item? Check out our [Pink Book!](https://www.chairish.com/pinkbook)
## Marking Down an Item
* * *
You can mark down a listing by navigating to your My Listings page and clicking the **Edit** link to the right of the applicable item. Once you have opened the listing, navigate to the Logistics portion of the [listing form](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44177598146065), and change your **Asking Price**. Be sure to click the **Submit Changes** button at the top of bottom of the screen to submit the update.
![markdown3.png](https://support.chairish.com/hc/article_attachments/44254873264913)
  * Please note, the minimum markdown is 10% of the current Asking Price.
  * Additionally, markdowns are permanent. Once an item is marked down, the price cannot be raised. For assistance, please reach out to production@chairish.com.


You can also mark down your item by navigating to your [My Listings](https://www.chairish.com/product/list) page and clicking the **Markdown** link to the right of the applicable item. 
![markdown1.png](https://support.chairish.com/hc/article_attachments/44254873262737)
A pop-up window will open where you can enter your **New Price** and then click the **Submit Markdown** button to save the change. 
Be sure to enter the new price, not the markdown amount! For example, if you'd like to mark your $100 listing down to $90, enter $90 in the pop-up, not $10.
