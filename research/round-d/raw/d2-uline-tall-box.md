**DIMENSIONS:**
  * Outside: 12 3/8 x 12 3/8 x 52 5/8" (+/- 1/8")
  * All FedEx and all UPS Dimensional Weight: 55 lbs


**MATERIAL:**
  * C flute


**FEATURES:**
  * All boxes open along the length (first dimension).
  * Seams are glued.


**SPECIFICATIONS:**
  * Meets ASTM D5118


**TERMINOLOGY:**
  * 200# Test vs 32 ECT (Edge Crush Test):
    * 200# Test:
      * 60% stronger bursting strength.
      * Recommended by UPS for shipping up to 40 lbs.
    * 32 ECT:
      * Recommended by UPS for shipping up to 30 lbs.
  * Pound Test Refers to Bursting Strength:
    * 200# test boxes withstand 200 lbs/sq.in. of side wall pressure before bursting.
  * ECT Refers to Stacking Strength:
    * 32 ECT boxes withstand 32 lbs/linear inch of stacking weight before crushing.
    * An alternative for lightweight shipments.
