# Product prices
You can add prices to your products to balance between making a profit and meeting your target market's expectations. You can also add additional product pricing details, such as unit pricing, compare-at pricing, and cost per item to provide more information to your customers and track your business operating costs and profits. Setting the right prices can help you stay competitive while maintaining healthy profit margins.
