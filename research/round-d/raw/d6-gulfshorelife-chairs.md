# Chairs for Charity
* * *
![chairs for charity logo](https://gulfshorelife.com/downloads/48689/download/chairs%20for%20charity%20gulfshore%20life.jpg)
* * *
Join us for the **2nd Annual Chairs for Charity** , an inspiring evening of design, creativity and community, hosted at Wilson Lighting.
* * *
The evening will showcase a collection of one-of-a-kind chairs creatively reimagined by leading local design firms, including Bay Design, Clive Daniel Home, California Closets, Stock Development and Wilson Lighting. Each chair will be transformed into a unique work of art, highlighting the imagination and craftsmanship of Southwest Florida’s design community.
* * *
Guests will enjoy delicious and innovative bites by Paradise Hospitality, while DJ Evrsol sets the soundtrack for an unforgettable evening.
* * *
Come experience these extraordinary chair creations, mingle with the designers and fellow design enthusiasts, and celebrate the power of design to make a difference in our community.
