This document outlines the requirements for selling **Collectibles** on TikTok Shop. Collectibles are also subject to our [**Restricted Products Policy**](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=3238037484275457 "https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=3238037484275457"). ## **Collectibles**
A collectible is a unique or rare item with personal, historical, or cultural value.   
When listing collectibles, the seller is required to:  

  * Include proof of authenticity.
  * Clearly describe the item’s condition.
  * Provide accurate and detailed information.

TikTok Shop permits the sale of the following products when sellers meet the platform's documentation requirements:  

  * Trading Cards
  * Sports Memorabilia
  * Entertainment Collectibles
  * Comics, Manga, and Magazines
  * Coins and Money Collectibles
  * Antique Collectibles

For Breaks with collectible products, please refer to the [**Gambling Policy**](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=2903157654996737&course_type=1#breaks "https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=2903157654996737&course_type=1#breaks").

## **Coins and Money Collectibles**
Coins and money collectibles include coins and paper money offered as collectible items rather than for active monetary use. **Only eligible sellers and creators may offer these products on TikTok Shop.**
> "Circulating" refers to coins or money currently used as legal tender in everyday transactions.
Examples of Coins and Money Collectibles include, but are not limited to:
  * Graded, circulating US coins and money
  * Ungraded, non-circulating US coins and money
  * Bullion (graded or ungraded)
  * Non-circulating foreign coins and money (graded or ungraded)
  * **Publications** such as price guides and hobby magazines
  * **Supplies** such as coin holders, cleaning products, and collection kits


### **Prohibited Coins and Money Collectibles**
  * Coins and money that are ungraded and circulating
  * Foreign coins and money that are circulating
  * Coins and money listed for active monetary use rather than as collectible items (including raw or uncertified items)
  * Foreign money, tokens, medals, or bullion bars/rounds intended for monetary use or investment purposes
  * Fantasy/novelty notes intended to resemble real money
  * Counterfeit, replica, or misrepresented items

For more information, see [**Prohibited Products Policy**](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=1399532709988097#monetary-products-and-financial-services "https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=1399532709988097#monetary-products-and-financial-services").  

### **Requirements to Sell Graded Coins and Money Collectibles**
Sellers listing graded coins or money must meet the following requirements:  

  1. Items must be graded and authenticated by a recognized grading provider (see [**Recognized Grading and Authentication Providers**](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=8818411388094215#coins-bullions "https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=8818411388094215#coins-bullions") for full list of providers).
  2. Listings must accurately state whether the item is circulating or non-circulating.
  3. The grading service, numeric grade, denomination, and certification number must match the slab (the protective case for a graded item).
  4. Clear images of the slab must be provided, including the certification number.
  5. Any material facts affecting collectibility must be disclosed (for example, toning, damage, environmental exposure, or cleaning).
  6. Listings must clearly state that collectible items are not guaranteed investments and must not guarantee appreciation, profits, or financial returns.


###  **Requirements to Sell****Ungraded Coins and Money Collectibles**
Sellers listing ungraded coins, money, or bullion must meet the following requirements:  

  1. Items must be clearly identified as ungraded or raw.
  2. Listings must accurately state that the item is non-circulating. Ungraded circulating coins and money are prohibited.
  3. Listings must include photos of the actual item for sale. Stock photos are not allowed.
  4. Any material facts affecting collectibility must be disclosed (for example, toning, damage, environmental exposure, or cleaning).
  5. Listings must not include a numeric grade anywhere in the listing or represent the ungraded item as professionally graded or certified.
  6. Listings must clearly state that collectible items are not guaranteed investments and must not guarantee appreciation, profits, or financial returns.


## **Antique Collectibles (Collectible Condition)**
Antique Collectibles include items with historical, cultural, or decorative value produced in earlier time periods. **Only eligible sellers may offer Antique Collectibles on TikTok Shop.** Examples of Antique Collectibles include, but are not limited to:
  * Furniture, musical instruments, and sewing items (pre-1930)
  * Decorative arts (for example, silver, tapestries, rugs, carpets)
  * Books, manuscripts, maps, and globes
  * Cultural, maritime, scientific, and medical artifacts
  * Architectural, garden, mercantile, and trade items
  * Restoration and care supplies for antiques


### **Prohibited Products**
TikTok Shop prohibits the following:
  * Artifacts, archaeological items, or protected cultural heritage items that are restricted under applicable cultural property or heritage protection laws
  * "Antique-style" items made with modern craftsmanship
  * Forged or falsified items, including misrepresented provenance, age, or cultural origin


### **Requirements for Antique Collectibles**
Sellers listing Antique Collectibles must:
  1. Provide real images of the item
  2. Accurately describe age, materials, and condition
  3. Clearly distinguish modern reproductions from genuine antiques
  4. Ensure compliance with all applicable laws, regulations, and requirements for the products they sell


## **Breaks**
A "break" is when a sealed pack, box, or case of items is opened—typically during a livestream—and the contents are distributed to paying customers.Offering breaks on TikTok Shop is limited to **eligible****sellers**. You must also maintain a **Shop Performance Score (SPS) ≥ 2.5**. Only the following products are currently allowed:
  * **Trading Cards**
  * **Memorabilia**


### **Break Types You Can Offer**
The following break types are allowed on TikTok Shop:
  * **Personal Breaks**
    * Customers purchase an entire pack, box, or case, which the seller then opens live on their behalf.
  * **Pick Your Team/Type Breaks**
    * Customers select the type of item they want, such as a team, player, division, or energy. They then claim all items matching their selection after the break is conducted live.
  * **"Pull-Til-You-Win" Breaks**
    * Customers select a specific item they want, usually a rare or high-value item like an autograph or trading card. You'll then open sealed products live until the item is pulled.
    * If the item target is not pulled within the guaranteed minimum number of items, you must continue opening sealed items until it is opened.
    * All opened items are shipped to the customer.
    * Listings for "pull-til-you-win"breaks must clearly include the minimum pack guarantee in the title or variant.
  * **Case Breaks**
    * The seller opens an entire factory-sealed case containing multiple boxes.
    * Spots are sold in advance and all hits are revealed live, then mailed to the corresponding buyers.

All break types must be sealed and unmodified prior to the break.
> Break livestreams are only allowed within the US.
### **Prohibited Break Types**
The following are non-exhaustive examples of prohibited Card Breaks on TikTok Shop:  

  * **Random Team Break** :
    * Buyers purchase a spot and are randomly assigned a unit (team, player, division, letter/serial number, or draft position). Each buyer receives every card matching their assigned unit pulled during the break.
    * _**Exceptions:**_
      * This is allowed if you are using a manufacturer-sealed surprise set.
      * Random Team Breaks are permitted exclusively through TikTok Shop's Surprise Set feature (limited to eligible sellers). All unit assignments must be generated by the platform's official randomization tool; no external tools, manual draws, or off-platform methods are permitted.
      * No secondary randomization or raffles for unsold slots are allowed.
      * Random Team Breaks are limited to trading cards only. The entire break must be conducted live on camera in a single session.
  * **Draft-Style Break** :
    * After the boxes are opened, participants select the revealed cards in a serpentine draft order (reversing the selection order every even round) that was randomized beforehand. This continues until the whole break is claimed.
  * **Bounties Break** :
    * The seller sets a live "bounty" for hitting a specific card or serial-number. If the chase card appears, its owner takes the bounty, otherwise it rolls over and grows for the next break.
  * **Pull Games Break** :
    * Instead of sealed packs, buyers purchase individual "pulls" from a face-down stack, mystery bag or wall. Each pull is revealed on-air and can range from common cards to high-value chasers.
  * **Points Program Break** :
    * Spots earn loyalty points with the seller's shop. These points accumulate across streams and can be cashed in for discounted or free break entries.
  * **Train Game** :
    * Viewers try to keep a "train" alive by buying consecutive spots before a short time expires. Reaching the required length unlocks extra packs, giveaways or chat-wide bonuses.
  * **King of the Hill:**
    * Throughout the break, the lowest (or highest) serial-numbered card pulled sits "on the hill". The holder at the end wins an added prize or sealed product.
  * **HP Battle** :
    * Common in Pokemon streams where each entrant opens a pack and only the single card with the highest (or sometimes lowest) printed Hit-Points number counts. That player scoops extra packs or even the whole box.

Gambling activities are prohibited on TikTok Shop. This includes any form of randomized distribution where customers don't select a specific type of product. ### **Prohibited Marketing Claims for Breaks**
  1. **Floor and Ceiling Value Claims**

Sellers must not reference or imply specific item values, including:
  * Minimum values (for example, "floor of $50", "nothing less than...", "minimum value guaranteed", "there are no duds in this set")
  * Maximum values (for example, "ceiling of $500", "worth up to...", "potential value of...")
  * Average or expected values (for example, "average value $200", "typical outcome is...")
  * Value ranges (for example, "$50–$500 range", "between X and Y")


  1. **Scarcity and Opportunity Framing**

Sellers must not present break items as rare opportunities, prizes, or investments, including:
  * Extremely rare or once-in-a-lifetime opportunities (for example, "you will never see this again", "rarest item available", "one-of-a-kind chance", "golden ticket")
  * Prize-like acquisitions (for example, "win the prize", "grand prize item", "jackpot card")
  * Investment opportunities (for example, "investment piece", "will appreciate", "hold value")
  * Limited-availability claims that exaggerate rarity or exclusivity


### **Requirements for Breaks**
These requirements apply to **break listings** , specifically those sold through **live auctions or streaming events**. Each listing must meet the following requirements to be eligible for sale.
  1. **Product Category**

Choosing the right category ensures your listing appears in search and filters. Break products must be from the same category. Select the most accurate category for your break:
  * **Collectibles > Trading Cards and Accessories**
    * Sports Trading Cards > Sports Card Breaks > Trading Card Breaks Only
    * Sports Trading Cards > Sports Card Breaks > Mixed Breaks
    * Non-Sports Trading Cards > Non-Sports Card Breaks
  * **Collectibles > Memorabilia Breaks**
    * Sports Collectibles > Memorabilia Breaks
    * Entertainment > Breaks


  1. **Product Title**

Titles must reflect the nature of the break. You can keep titles general—especially for live selling—or include more detail when appropriate.
  * **Allowed Examples:**
    * _Live Auction Sports Trading Cards_
    * _Cards on the Screen – Mystery Pack Break_
    * _Pick Your Energy – Shrouded Fable Elite Trainer Box (ETB) CASE BREAK (x90 Packs) (for memorabilia breaks)_

You are not required to reference specific players, teams, or products in the title, but you may do so if relevant.
  1. **Product Description**

Your product description must give buyers enough information to understand what they are purchasing. The level of detail can vary depending on the break type, but the following are required:
  * **You must include:**
    * The card or memorabilia type or category
      *  _Examples: sports, non-sport, pre-owned, mystery pack, repack_
    * A general explanation of how the break works
      * _Example: "Participants will receive cards pulled live based on selected energy type."_
      * _Example: "Mystery repack cards will be revealed live and distributed to buyers post-break."_
  * **Optional, but encouraged:**
    * Mention general contents if known (teams, players, themes, styles, certification info)
      * _Example: "This autographed bat is certified by Beckett, Fanatics, or JSA."_
      * _Example: "Cards may include rookies and legends from major sports leagues."_

Detailed itemization of every card or memorabilia piece is **not required** , especially for live breaks.
  1. **Product Images**

Your images should help buyers identify the break. However, **you are not required to show every individual card or item**.
  * **You may include:**
    * Company or brand logos
    * Images of sealed products or break formats
    * Themed graphics that represent the type of break
  * **You do not need to include:**
    * Photos of each individual card or item shown during the break
    * Full inventory breakdowns


  1. **Break Fulfillment Requirements**

All break purchases must result in a physical product being shipped to the customer.
  * Every customer must receive at least one product for every break purchase.
  * Products shipped must come from the same category as the break.
    * Example: In a baseball trading card break, each buyer must receive at least one baseball trading card.
    * Example: In a helmet memorabilia break, each buyer must receive at least one helmet.
