#  CGB Extends the Effective Date of the TCPA's Consent Revocation Rule
  * Full Title:
Consumer and Governmental Affairs Bureau Extends the Effective Date of the Telephone Consumer Protection Act's Consent Revocation Rule
  * Document Type:
Order
  * Bureau(s):
Consumer and Governmental Affairs
  * Description 
By this Order, the Commission extends a waiver of section 64.1200(a)(10) relating to revocation of consent until January 31, 2027

DA-26-12
  * Docket No:
02-278
