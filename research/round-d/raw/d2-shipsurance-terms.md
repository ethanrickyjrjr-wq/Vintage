##  Complete Coverage Rules
### Definitions
Throughout this page the following definitions apply:
  1. **Carrier** means the company that is transporting the **parcel** or **freight** covered by this program.   

  2. **Freight** means packages or shipments transported through commercial freight carriers. Banded boxes and pallets of boxes are considered one single package.  

  3. **Insured** means the Shipper(s), individual(s) or companies who have elected to purchase insurance through this program.  

  4. **Parcel** means packages or shipments transported through parcel carriers. 
  5. **Shipsurance** means the agent, Shipsurance Insurance Services.  

  6. **Total Insured Value** means the total value of the item(s) declared for insurance coverage and can include shipping fees. Includes the carrier provided coverage, if any.   

  7. **We** , **us** and **our** means Voyager Indemnity Insurance Company.  

  8. **Theft** means a parcel or freight that is stolen outside of a residence or business where it has been commercially delivered, with intent to deprive the rightful owner of the contents.  



### Coverage Details
Coverage provided is for all risks of physical loss or damage from an external cause (subject to exclusions) while in transit, and physical loss or damage from theft upon delivery. Coverage is on a package-by-package basis. Inbound shipments are not covered. 
**Coverage is for loss, damage, or shortage to the original merchandise shipped plus the original shipping cost (if declared), only.  
Insured shipments must be under commercial sales invoice in order to be covered. **
You acknowledge that the insurance coverage is provided by Voyager Indemnity Insurance. Voyager is a **[non-admitted insurer](https://s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/download.shipsurance.com/docs/nonadmitted.pdf)**.   
  
If you are shipping using the United States Postal Service, make sure to follow the USPS International Mail Manual rules located here: **<http://pe.usps.com/text/imm/welcome.htm>**. If you ship a prohibited item, there is NO COVERAGE. 
In order for a package to be covered, the shipping carrier must have verified acceptance of your package for mailing. This is found on the respective tracking websites of the shipping carriers. If a shipping label is printed but not scanned by the carrier as accepted it is deemed as never been mailed and there is no coverage.
### Limits and Covered Carriers
This insurance covers up $5,000.00 per package for shipments sent via DHL, FedEx, LaserShip, OnTrac, UPS, and the USPS service. It covers up to $1,000.00 per package for shipments sent via DHL eCommerce, FedEx Ground Economy, and UPS Ground Saver. Maximum coverage per conveyance is $100,000.00.
