# Step 1: Set up your sign-up form
To set up your full page/embedded sign-up form:
  1. Go to [**Marketing > Forms**](https://app.brevo.com/contact/forms/subscription).
  2. From the **Sign-up** tab, click **Create sign-up form**.
  3. Select the **Full page/embedded** card.
  4. Give an internal name to your form.
  5. Click **Create form**.
  6. Activate the **Enable GDPR fields** option to automatically include the **GDPR field** and **GDPR Declaration** blocks in your form. These blocks are important to ensure that your email and SMS marketing practices comply with the GDPR.  
[![mceclip0 \(3\).png](https://help.brevo.com/hc/article_attachments/21013387647506)](https://help.brevo.com/hc/article_attachments/21013387647506)  

  7. Click **Next**.


# Step 2: Design your sign-up form
In the form builder, you will find a pre-made form that can be customized to fit your needs.
On the left side of the builder, you will find two tabs: the **Build** tab where you can find blocks and fields and the **Form design** tab where you can find design elements:
[Build tab](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208771869-Create-a-sign-up-form-in-Brevo#tab-content-120)[Form design tab](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208771869-Create-a-sign-up-form-in-Brevo#tab-content-121)
From the **Build** tab, you'll find the blocks and fields you can drag and drop to design your sign-up form. 
We'll walk you through each one below, but feel free to pick and choose the ones you want—you don't need to use them all in your form (except for the **GDPR field** , **GDPR Declaration** , and **Captcha** , which we highly recommend you include).

Select the list(s) where you want your new subscribers to be **automatically added after they submit your form**. You can either select an existing list or create a new one.

# Step 4: Manage your subscription confirmation settings
❗️ Important
Double and single confirmations are**only available if you have added an email address field to your sign-up form**. Otherwise, no confirmation will be sent. 
## Select your subscription confirmation option
You have three options to manage your subscription confirmations:
[[Recommended] Double confirmation](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208771869-Create-a-sign-up-form-in-Brevo#tab-content-252)[Simple confirmation](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208771869-Create-a-sign-up-form-in-Brevo#tab-content-253)[No confirmation](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208771869-Create-a-sign-up-form-in-Brevo#tab-content-254)
With the **Double confirmation** option, your subscribers will go through a double opt-in process. This means that they will receive an **email containing a confirmation link (double opt-in email)** after submitting your sign-up form. They have to click this link to confirm their subscription and be added to your contact database in Brevo. Otherwise, they won’t be added nor subscribed. 
The double opt-in process is useful to verify that the email address is valid and **recommended for GDPR compliance**. 
➡️ To learn more, check our dedicated article [Double opt-in (DOI): What it is and how to track user sign-ups](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208733449). 
## Add a double confirmation to your form
  1. At the **Settings** step, select **Double confirmation email**. 
  2. Click the **Select a template** dropdown and select one of the following options:[![select_template.png](https://help.brevo.com/hc/article_attachments/21013387623058)](https://help.brevo.com/hc/article_attachments/21013387623058)  

    1. Select the**Default Template Double opt-in confirmation**. You can modify the default template by going to **[Marketing> Templates > Email](https://app.brevo.com/templates/listing/email)** and searching for "**Default Template Double opt-in confirmation** " in the search bar. 
    2. Or select a **custom template** you have already created.   

❗️ Important
If you want to create your own custom template, make sure you follow the procedure in our dedicated article [Create a custom double opt-in (DOI) confirmation template for Brevo sign-up forms](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360019540880) to**include the mandatory field**. Otherwise, your custom template **won't be displayed in the dropdown**. For additional proof of consent, we also recommend adding the **consent text** from your GDPR field to the double opt-in confirmation template.
  3. (Optional) Activate and configure any additional confirmation pages and final confirmation email you want to add to your double confirmation process. Expand the following accordions to learn more about the available options for additional confirmation pages and email:  

Confirmation page after submitting the form
Enable this option to redirect your subscribers to a specific page, such as a confirmation page or your website homepage, **after they submit the form**. You can use our default confirmation page or create a custom one and link it. 
Confirmation page after clicking the validation link in the email
Enable this option to redirect subscribers to a specific page, such as a confirmation page or your website homepage, **after they click the subscription confirmation link** in the double opt-in email received after submitting your form. You can use our default confirmation page or create a custom one and link it. 
Final Confirmation Email
Enable this option for subscribers to receive a final confirmation email **after they complete the double opt-in process**. The default template for the final confirmation email is named **Default template - Final Confirmation** , and you can modify by going to [**Marketing > Templates > Email**](https://app.brevo.com/templates/listing/email) and searching for "**Default template - Final Confirmation** " in the search bar. 
💡 Good to know
The default confirmation pages **cannot be customized** but will adapt to the language of your subscriber's browser. For more customization, you can create and use your own **custom pages**.


With the **Simple confirmation** option, your contacts receive a confirmation email and are added to your contact database in Brevo right after submitting your sign-up form. 
## Add a simple confirmation to your form
  1. At the **Settings** step, select **Simple confirmation email**. 
  2. Click the **Select a template** dropdown menu and select one of the following options:  

    1. Select the**Default Template Simple confirmation**. You can modify the default template by going to **[Marketing> Templates > Email](https://app.brevo.com/templates/listing/email)** and searching for "**Default Template Simple confirmation** " in the search bar. 
    2. Or select a **custom template** you have already created.  
[![forms_simple-confirmation_en-us.jpg](https://help.brevo.com/hc/article_attachments/21013387700114)](https://help.brevo.com/hc/article_attachments/21013387700114)
  3. (Optional) Enable the **Confirmation page after submitting the form** option to redirect your subscribers to a specific page, such as a confirmation page or your website homepage, after they submit the form. You can use our default confirmation page or create a custom one and link it. 


With the **No confirmation** option, your subscribers are added to your contact database in Brevo right after submitting your sign-up form. They don't receive any email confirming their subscription. 
## Add no confirmation to your form
  1. At the **Settings** step, select **No confirmation email**. 
  2. (Optional) Enable the **Confirmation page after submitting the form** option to redirect your subscribers to a specific page, such as a confirmation page or your website homepage, after they submit the form. You can use our default confirmation page or create a custom one and link it.  

💡 Good to know
If you don't enable this option, a **Success message** will be displayed at the top of your sign-up form without changing the page. You can modify its content at the [**Messages** step](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208771869-Create-a-sign-up-form-in-Brevo#h_01HG84568853TKS0412J4K1CMV). 


## (Optional) Configure the advanced settings of your sign-up form
To configure the advanced settings of your sign-up form, click **Show advanced settings**. To see what advanced settings are available, expand the following accordions:
Block sign-ups
✅ Plan availability
The **Block sign-ups** option is only available on the **Standard** , **Professional** , and**Enterprise** plans. For more information, check our [pricing page](https://www.brevo.com/pricing/).
Activate **Block sign-ups with an address from a free service provider** to **reject freemail domains**. Free email addresses make it challenging to identify a contact's business or intent. 
Activate**Block sign-ups with a disposable email address** to **reject temporary email addresses** created on disposable email address services, such as Yopmail, MyTrashMail, etc. Disposable email addresses tend to bounce after some time and end up negatively impacting your deliverability. 
Completed form behavior option
Activate this option to **hide your sign-up****form after it has been submitted**. If you don't activate this option, your form will still appear in input mode to your subscribers even after they have filled and submitted it successfully. 
💡 Good to know
Even if you activate this option, your form will **reappear** if visitors reload the page. 
# Step 5: Configure the success and error messages
Configure the success and error messages displayed when your subscribers submit your sign-up form:

# ❓ FAQs
Expand the accordions below to find answers to common questions about sign-up forms.
Why are contacts not appearing in my list after they submitted the form?
If you selected the **Double confirmation** option, contacts are only added to your list after they click the confirmation link in the email they receive. Make sure they have completed that step. 
If you are not using double confirmation and contacts still don't appear, check that: 
  * The list selected in [Step 3](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208771869-Create-a-sign-up-form-in-Brevo#h_01HG8FGHWT8ZKV852D1PKDA90T) is correct. 
  * The form was saved by clicking **Done** at the end of the setup. 
  * The form submission was successful (the subscriber should see a success message or be redirected to a confirmation page). 


➡️ To learn more, check our dedicated article [Troubleshooting issues with your forms](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360019846960). 
Can I customize the sign-up form URL?
No, the URL generated by Brevo for your sign-up form is not customizable. If you need a shorter or branded URL, you can use a third-party URL shortener service and redirect it to your form's URL. 
My form works in Brevo but not on my website. What should I do?
The most common causes for an embed form not working on a website are: 
  * The embed code on your website is outdated. Re-generate the code in Brevo and replace the old code on your website. 
  * Your CAPTCHA is not configured correctly. Check our dedicated article [Add a CAPTCHA to your sign-up form](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360019551939). 
  * Your Brevo account is suspended or restricted.


➡️ To learn more, check our dedicated article [Troubleshooting issues with your forms](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360019846960). 
How do I receive an email notification when someone fills out my form?
Brevo does not send automatic email notifications when a contact submits your form. To receive a notification, you need to set up an automation workflow triggered by a form submission event. 
➡️ To learn more, check our dedicated article [Notify your team by email when a contact submits a form](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/27278282993682). 
My success message edits are not saving. What is happening?
After editing the success or error messages in [Step 5](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208771869-Create-a-sign-up-form-in-Brevo#h_01HG84568853TKS0412J4K1CMV), make sure you click **Next** to move to the next step or before closing the editor. Changes that are not explicitly saved will not be applied to the live form. 
How do I add a First Name field (or other fields) to my form?
All form fields are based on contact attributes. To add a First Name field: 
  1. In the form editor, drag an **Attribute** block into your form. 
  2. In the block settings, select the **FIRSTNAME** attribute from the dropdown. 


If you don't see the attribute you need, you may need to create it first. To manage your attributes, click the account dropdown and select [**Settings > Contacts > Contact attributes**](https://app.brevo.com/objects/settings/contact). 
➡️ To learn more, check our dedicated article [Create and manage custom contact attributes](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/10617359589906).
