## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is Broadly?
Broadly is an AI-powered customer engagement platform that helps local businesses attract more customers, generate online reviews, manage their reputation, communicate with customers, and book more jobs. It brings together AI-powered tools such as AI Receptionist, review management, messaging, website chat, appointment scheduling, and marketing automation to help businesses save time and grow.
### How does Broadly help businesses get more Google reviews?
Broadly makes it easy to request customer reviews by sending automated text messages or emails after a completed job or appointment. By encouraging satisfied customers to leave reviews on Google, businesses can strengthen their online reputation, build customer trust, and improve their visibility in local search results.
### Can Broadly improve local SEO?
Yes. Broadly helps businesses strengthen their local SEO by encouraging authentic customer reviews, supporting Google Business Profile engagement, improving online reputation, and increasing customer interactions. While no platform can guarantee search rankings, these activities contribute to better local search visibility and help businesses attract more local customers.
### What is Broadly AI Receptionist?
Broadly AI Receptionist is an AI-powered virtual assistant that helps businesses answer incoming customer calls, capture lead information, respond to common questions, and reduce missed opportunities. It helps businesses provide fast and consistent customer service, even outside normal business hours.
### Does Broadly integrate with Google Business Profile?
Yes. Broadly works with Google Business Profile to help businesses collect customer reviews, monitor feedback, respond to reviews, and strengthen their local online presence. Managing customer interactions in one place makes it easier to maintain an active and trusted business profile.
### What types of businesses use Broadly?
Broadly is built for local service businesses of all sizes, including HVAC companies, plumbers, electricians, roofers, landscapers, contractors, auto repair shops, dental practices, healthcare providers, legal firms, and other businesses that rely on customer reviews, local search visibility, and strong customer relationships.
