Send emails built for commerce
Create email and SMS campaigns with ready-made templates, edit emails quickly with Sidekick, and get insights all in one place with pricing that scales with your sends.

Create beautifully branded emails in minutes with a drag-and-drop editor and fully customizable templates that let your brand shine.

Use automations with Shopify Messaging for even more engagement. Choose from automation templates, edit the email, turn on the automation, and watch your business grow.
### Manage campaigns

## SMS Marketing
### Connect and convert with SMS marketing
Send SMS marketing messages with timely offers and updates that encourage higher open rates. Create campaigns quickly using expert-built templates, pay only for what you send, and track how they perform alongside email metrics.
[Learn more](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/shopify-email/sms)
SMS marketing is now available in select countries.
## How Shopify Messaging pricing works
### Start for free, then pay for what you use
Every month, you can send up to 10,000 manual or automated emails for free. After that, only pay for what you use at $1 USD per 1,000 additional emails sent (or $0.001 USD per additional email).
### Commitment-free pricing
Shopify Messaging is available on all Shopify plans. There are no monthly commitments, and you'll have access to all Shopify Messaging features—including SMS.
### Unlock better pricing as you grow
After your first 300,000 emails per month, you'll automatically pay lower rates on every additional email.   
[Learn more about volume pricing](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/shopify-email/shopify-email-cost).
