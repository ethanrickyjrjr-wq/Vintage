THE PROFESSIONAL ASSOCIATION FOR INTERIOR DESIGNERS
The American Society of Interior Designers (ASID) advances the interior design profession and communicates the impact of design on the human experience.
[LEARN MORE](https://flwc.asid.org/about)
[![](https://flwc.asid.org/img/cache/lib24watch/Lib24watchFiles/477/lib24watch_file/0-f95be01e58fe8a4bc20db3d2ac9b15eb)](https://flwc.asid.org/chapter-sponsorship)
As a sponsor of your local ASID chapter, your company will engage with members, participate in their events, host meetings, and sponsor activities. Additionally, you will have the opportunity to increase visibility through additional marketing, branding, special access, and deeper engagement with ASID members.
