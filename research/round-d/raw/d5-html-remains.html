A pair of vintage table lamps made of ascending ball and waist turnings in an age mellowed brass finish set on dark lacquered wood bases with tall columns painted in an ivory color supporting the lamping. By the Chicago, Illinois maker Stiffel.

    • Overall: 51-1/2" h. x 9" w. x 8" d.
    • Height to socket: 46-1/2"
    • Base: 2-1/2" h. x 8" w. x 8" d.

    Application:

    Lamping:

    • 2 Edison sockets, maximum 25 watts per socket
