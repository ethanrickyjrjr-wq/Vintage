Available on the Free plan
If you sell the same products on Etsy and Shopify, the reviews you've earned on Etsy don't have to stay there. Connect your Etsy shop to Judge.me to automatically pull those reviews into Shopify and keep them syncing as new ones come in.

# How it works
Here’s how syncing reviews from your Etsy store works:
  1. **You connect your Etsy store.** You connect your Etsy store and initiate the sync.
  2. **You choose how to match products.** You can choose to match your active Etsy listings and Shopify products in 2 ways:
     * **Automatically** : Match your active Etsy listings to Shopify products by SKU. Reviews sync as product reviews.
     * **Manually** : For each Etsy listing, choose whether to match it to a Shopify product (reviews sync as product reviews), sync its reviews as store reviews, or skip it.
  3. **We match products, then sync reviews.**
     * First, we fetch your active Etsy listings and match them to your Shopify products using your chosen matching method (automatically by SKU, or manually).
     * Then we sync reviews from matched Etsy listings. Reviews with written content from active Etsy listings are synced into Judge.me as verified reviews.
     * New Etsy reviews are pulled in automatically every 7 days. You can also trigger a manual sync once every 24 hours.
  4. **You display the reviews.** Use any Judge.me widget to show your synced Etsy reviews on your Shopify storefront.


* * *
# What we sync from Etsy
All reviews with written content from your active Etsy listings can be synced to Judge.me. For reviews that are synced from Etsy:
  * We sync the following attributes from each review:
    1. Star rating
    2. Review text
    3. Review images (if any)
    4. Reviewer name
    5. The date the review was posted on Etsy
  * Etsy shop replies to reviews aren't synced.
  * All synced reviews are marked as verified in Judge.me.


The following reviews are not synced from Etsy:
  * Reviews from inactive, sold-out, draft, or expired Etsy listings
  * Rating-only reviews

# How it works with other features
## 1. Google Shopping
Google requires that product reviews originate from the store they're submitted under. As a result, reviews synced from Etsy are not included in the product ratings feed for [Google Shopping](https://judge.me/help/en/articles/13845576-syncing-product-reviews-to-google-shopping) — even though they are marked as verified.
## 2. Meta, TikTok Shop, and Shop app
Product reviews synced from Etsy are included in your syndications to:
  * [Meta](https://judge.me/help/en/articles/8362521-showing-reviews-in-facebook-and-instagram-shops-with-meta-syndication)
  * [TikTok Shop](https://judge.me/help/en/articles/10305619-syncing-verified-product-reviews-from-judge-me-to-tiktok-shop)
  * [Shop app](https://judge.me/help/en/articles/8362827-syncing-reviews-with-shop-app)


## 3. Review management
You can moderate Etsy-synced reviews in Judge.me like any other review — publish, hide, archive, feature, or pin them. They also follow your autopublish settings.
You can also reply publicly to these reviews, but the reply stays in Judge.me and isn't sent back to Etsy.
However, you can't edit, move, delete, [send private replies](https://judge.me/help/en/articles/8375131-replying-to-reviews), or [request a review update](https://judge.me/help/en/articles/8368457-asking-customers-to-update-their-reviews) for reviews synced from Etsy.
* * *
# Considerations
  * This integration only syncs reviews from Etsy to Judge.me, not the other way around.
  * Once a review is synced to Judge.me, later changes to the original review on Etsy don't propagate. If a buyer edits or delete their review on Etsy, the synced version in Judge.me stays as it was.
  * Once a review is synced, it stays attached to the Shopify product it was originally matched to. Updating SKUs, replacing Shopify products, or otherwise changing product matching afterward only affects future syncs — we won't re-match already-synced reviews, and we won't re-sync them to the newly matched products.

# Match Etsy listings to Shopify products
Once you've connected your Etsy store, you can match your Etsy listings to your Shopify products in two ways:
  * **Automatically by SKUs:** Best when you use SKUs and your Etsy SKUs match your Shopify SKUs. We'll map every listing for you, no manual work needed.
  * **Manually:** Best when you don't use SKUs, when your Etsy and Shopify SKUs don't match, or when you want different rules per listing


You can change your match method at any time.
## Automatic product matching
With the automatic product matching method, we use SKUs to automatically map your Etsy listings to your Shopify products.
After you've selected **Automatically match products by SKUs codes** and click **Sync reviews** , here's how SKU matching works:
  1. **We'll first fetch your Etsy listings.** Each listing comes with one or more SKUs (one per variant, if the listing has variants).
  2. **We look for matching SKUs in Shopify.** For each of those SKUs, we search your Shopify store for a variant with the exact same SKU.
  3. **We link the listings to their first match.**
     * Etsy sends us listing-level data, not variant-level — we know which Etsy listing a review belongs to, but not which specific variant. So we match at the listing level: as long as one Etsy variant SKU matches a Shopify variant SKU, the whole Etsy listing is matched.
     * If the same SKU exists on more than one Shopify variant — whether in different products or in different variants of the same product — we'll still match the Etsy listing, but to whichever variant we find first.
     * If no Etsy variant SKU matches any Shopify variant SKU, the listing stays unmatched.
  4. **We match new and updated listings.**
     * On each sync, we re-run the matching steps above for new Etsy listings plus any existing listings updated on Etsy since the last sync. 
     * We detect updates from Etsy's **last updated** time, so changes you make in Shopify won't pull an Etsy listing back in.


## Manual product matching
With the manual product matching method, you'll manually choose how each Etsy listing maps to your Shopify products before reviews are synced.
After you've selected **Match products manually** and click **Sync reviews** , here's how manual matching works: 
  1. **We fetch your Etsy listings.** We'll first fetch your Etsy listings to prepare for manual matching. While the fetch is in progress, you'll see this status: "_We're fetching products from Etsy to prepare for manual product matching. You'll receive an email when it's complete_."

# Reauthorize to sync reviewer names
**This section is for stores that connected Etsy before reviewer name syncing was added.**
  * If you see the **Upgrade your Etsy syndication** banner in your Etsy syndication settings, follow the steps below to reauthorize your connection.
  * If you're setting up Etsy syndication for the first time, see [Sync reviews from Etsy](https://judge.me/help/en/articles/10713924-syncing-reviews-from-etsy#h_874a10ff02) instead.


## Why you should reauthorize 
Previously, Etsy reviews synced into Judge.me showed the reviewer as "Etsy customer." 
We've now updated the integration to sync the actual reviewer name shown on Etsy. Reauthorizing is optional, but recommended.
## Things to know before reauthorizing
  * Reauthorization keeps your sync schedule and previously synced reviews. However, when you reconnect, the **Sync reviews** step defaults back to **automatic (SKU) matching** , even if you were using manual matching before. If you don't explicitly select **Match products manually** again, your existing manual matches are at risk of being lost. To keep them safe, make sure to select **Match products manually** in the Sync reviews pop-up after reconnecting — not the automatic SKU option.
  * **Only new reviews are affected.** Reviews synced before reauthorization keep their "Etsy customer" labels — reviewer name syncing only applies to new reviews going forward.

**Etsy API review import is stuck in "Import in progress"**
If your Etsy sync (or Etsy API import) is stuck in the **Import in progress. This might take a few minutes** status, you can ignore the error and trigger a manual sync instead.
[![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/z09y8g87/1920409626/04aae4456ed35778160ee0772da8/image.png?expires=1793279700&signature=1ab8bfeced26c3245778fef79e18bb9b10273b8d76e6136ac15e82da4f9c4a66&req=dSklFs1%2BlIddX%2FMW1HO4zR%2FNgVTxH%2Bn%2BGzmWf7VR8Q8tlwTEbb1oYxjMUQ0s%0AA7MPER1aNHjo6%2Bo1rJk%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/z09y8g87/1920409626/04aae4456ed35778160ee0772da8/image.png?expires=1793279700&signature=1ab8bfeced26c3245778fef79e18bb9b10273b8d76e6136ac15e82da4f9c4a66&req=dSklFs1%2BlIddX%2FMW1HO4zR%2FNgVTxH%2Bn%2BGzmWf7VR8Q8tlwTEbb1oYxjMUQ0s%0AA7MPER1aNHjo6%2Bo1rJk%3D%0A)
To trigger a manual sync:
  1. From your Judge.me admin, go to **[Settings](https://app.judge.me/settings/integration_settings?app=etsy)**[ > **Integrations > Etsy**](https://app.judge.me/settings/integration_settings?app=etsy).
  2. In the **Etsy syndication** section, click the **Sync reviews manually** button.
