#  Share Your Signup Form 
[ Copy Article URL ](https://mailchimp.com/help/share-your-signup-form/ "Copy URL to clipboard.")
Copied 
After you customize the hosted signup form for your audience, you're ready to share with potential subscribers. A simple way to promote your audience is to share your signup form to social media and other communication channels. All Mailchimp forms are mobile responsive, so potential subscribers can sign up from any device.
In this article, you'll learn how to copy and paste your signup form URL to share on social media, your website, or anywhere else you can add a link.
##  Before you start 
Here are some things to know before you begin this process.
  * The built-in sharing options send potential subscribers to the [Basic subscribe form](https://mailchimp.com/help/create-a-hosted-signup-form/). You can also [host your own custom signup form](https://mailchimp.com/help/host-your-own-signup-forms/).
  * Each signup form URL is randomly generated for your specific audience and can't be customized.


##  Find Share Options 
To find options to share your signup form, follow these steps.
  1. Click **Forms** , then click **Forms settings**.
  2. In the **System forms** table, you'll see a link icon that you can click to copy the link to share your basic subscribe form.


##  Share in an email 
To share a signup form in an email, you'll add the *|LIST:SUBSCRIBE|* [merge tag](https://mailchimp.com/help/getting-started-with-merge-tags/) to your content in place of a URL. When you send your email, we'll convert the merge tag to a [signup form link](https://mailchimp.com/help/include-a-signup-form-link-in-a-campaign/). Any time someone receives a [forwarded](https://mailchimp.com/help/add-the-forward-to-a-friend-link/) email or views your email in their browser, they'll be able to sign up to your email marketing.
##  Embed a form on your website 
For websites hosted on another platform, we provide HTML code for a signup form that can be customized and embedded on your website. Design your form, then copy and paste the code into your website's HTML where you want the signup form to appear. To learn more, check out [Add an Embedded Form to Your Website](https://mailchimp.com/help/add-a-signup-form-to-your-website/).
##  Next steps 
Mailchimp has many signup form options. To learn more about signup forms on websites and landing pages, check out [About Signup Forms Options](https://mailchimp.com/help/about-signup-form-options/).
