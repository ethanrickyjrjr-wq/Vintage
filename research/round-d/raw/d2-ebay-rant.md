##### Sellers who DON'T properly package fragile items for shipping that arrive broken RANT!
I'm a buyer. I buy items that are hard to find, discontinued, retired - that I'm unable to find anywhere else.
I have been purchasing increasingly hard to find Franklin Mint items - Particularly from the Treasures Of Tutankhamun collection. In late 2019 I found a full collection of 18 individual Retired Egyption statues on Ebay for $450 + $75 S&H - I had been looking for this complete collection for a couple of YEARS so I was very excited to be finally getting it. 
Cut to 5 days later the package arrives and I could already hear the broken glass inside...Open the box and EVERY SINGLE STATUE and Glass dome was completely shattered. This seller didn't wrap each statue individually, she put the statues with glass domes in a box with shredded newspaper and somehow expected it to arrive from California to Pennsylvania with no problem. I was disappointed, kinda **bleep**, and baffled at how someone could put so little care or effort into packaging a $500 sale... I submitted a return request with a bunch of photos and her response blew me away, she said and I quote " I went out of my way to make sure they were protected enough for shipping " WHAT? NO, NO YOU DIDN'T!! She actually believed she did a good job packaging these statues...it boggles my mind, and I still haven't found another complete set since then, so it was a terrible experience. I also cut my hand open, and almost had to go to the hospital. How else was I going to take pictures if I didn't take everything out of the box?
I usually stay away from ordering anything fragil - I've had such bad luck with Ebay sellers that I just don't usually bother anymore...
I waited a year and a half before I tried again...
I ordered 2 limited Edition, retired since the 1990's Egyption Vases. Paid $200 + S&H and they arrived totally shattered! Once again I'm baffled at how little effort went into protecting these 2 vases (retired, limited edition, numbered, from a company that is no longer in business) I could once again hear the shattered pieces inside the box before I even opened it. Inside, there was some bubble wrap, but shockingly both vases were put into the box in a plastic bag and misc bubble wrap, scrunched up paper - but the vases were not individually wrapped or protected from banging into each other... it's sad, disappointing, and I can't understand how this keeps happening.
I will of course be refunded. The problem becomes bigger...I find myself NOT ordering things now out of fear they will just arrive broken. I just came across an almost complete set of those statues again, and I don't think I will order them because I don't have confidence they will arrive undamaged.
I could write the seller and ask for them to wrap each statue with care, but I shouldn't have to do that.
Here's what I want to make CRYSTAL CLEAR - I'm sure there are buyers who try to scam sellers, as well as sellers who try to scam buyers... Sellers MUST remember that MOST people are NOT trying to scam anybody. I just wanted the items I ordered to arrive undamaged! That's it! Apparently spending more doesn't equal better care. 
I'm now at a crossroads... I'm not sure I want to order anymore of these kind of items from Ebay. And who cares, right? Nobody will miss 1 person's sales...but I'm not the only one! 
Sellers should have to watch packaging and shipping tutorials before they can sell items on Ebay. There needs to be a standard! Too many people just do whatever they want - ship items and believe they did a great job when in fact the only time the job was great is when the item arrives undamaged!
And it does have long terms consequences when you consider I still haven't found that entire collection again... It's just mind boggling, and disappointing! 
I said this was a rant in the title!!! 
Thanks for reading.
