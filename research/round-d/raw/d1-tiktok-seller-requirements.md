# Requirements to register as a seller on TikTok Shop
Last updated: August 2025 
All information provided during registration is kept strictly confidential and secure. We do not share your details outside of our secured platform, adhering to strict privacy policies to protect your data.
To register as a seller:
  * You'll need a valid phone number or email address not already used for another TikTok Shop. If you're already using a phone number or email address for a TikTok account, learn how to troubleshoot TikTok Shop registration.
  * You'll need a strong password that includes a combination of numbers, letters, and special characters. The password should be between 6-20 characters long.
  * Politicians are [prohibited](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/growing-your-audience/government-politician-and-political-party-accounts) from becoming TikTok Shop sellers.
  * You must reside in the country you choose for your shop.
