Rewarding customers with coupons encourages more reviews and increases repeat purchases. With Judge.me, you can automatically generate and send coupon codes to customers who leave a review - helping boost engagement and loyalty.

# How it works
Here’s what happens after you enable coupons:
  1. Your customer leaves a review.
  2. We wait **30 minutes** to allow the customer some time to upload any media (photo/video) and to confirm their review (if it was submitted on the web).
  3. After 30 minutes, we check if the customer and their review qualify for a reward based on your settings. Reviews don’t need to be published to qualify.
  4. If they meet all requirements, we automatically generate a unique coupon code for them. The coupon is sent to the customer via email.
  5. The customer can then use the coupon on a qualified purchase in your store.

# Considerations
  * Incentivized reviews are automatically tagged with the `<is_incentivized_review>` attribute in the Product Ratings feed for Google Shopping.
  * To comply with Shopify’s Product Review Policy, the option to generate coupons based on review ratings (e.g., only send coupons for 4- or 5-star reviews) was removed.

# Coupon generating conditions
## 1. Coupon details
To adjust the description and prefix of your coupon:
  * **If you're part of the[Early user program](https://judge.me/help/en/articles/8389897-judge-me-early-user-program):**
    1. From your Judge.me admin, go to **[Settings](https://app.judge.me/settings/rewards/coupons)**[ > **Coupons**](https://app.judge.me/settings/rewards/coupons).
    2. In the **Coupon settings** section, select **Automatically by Judge.me under** "How to generate coupons".
    3. In the **Coupon code** field, enter a prefix (optional). We'll add a unique identifier for each new coupon code (_default example_ , `THANKYOU-ABC123`).

### Different discount depending on media upload

       * "With a video"
    6. If you enable more than one option, the discount will work in tiers:
       * Text-only reviews receive the discount set for "Any".
       * Reviews with a photo receive the discount set for "With photo".
       * Reviews with a video receive the discount set for "With a video".

The coupon email template supports two ways to display the coupon code:
  * `{{ block_coupon_code }}` — Renders a pre-styled coupon code card with fixed labels. The card styling and text (e.g. "Your coupon code") cannot be customized. This is the default for new-version templates.
  * `{{ coupon_code }}` — Outputs just the plain coupon code text (e.g. `THANKYOU-ABC123`), with no styling or labels. Use this if you want full control over the text and layout around the coupon code.


To customize the text around your coupon code, replace `{{ block_coupon_code }}` with `{{ coupon_code }}` in your coupon email template, then use the editor's formatting tools (bold, italic, underline, links, images) to style the text around it. ## 2. In the Write a review flow
Once you enable Judge.me-generated coupons, the coupon promo can automatically appear in supported Write a review flows. This helps customers see the reward before, during, and after they submit a review.
The coupon promo can appear in these flows:
  * **Modal widget form** , such as the write-a-review modal opened from your storefront widgets
  * **Standalone review page**
  * **QR code and shareable review link flows**
  * **Shopify Customer Accounts** , if you’ve enabled Judge.me in [Customer Accounts](https://judge.me/help/en/articles/10174105-customer-accounts-widgets)


**Note:** Coupon promos appear on the new modal-based review form only. The older fold-down review form doesn't support this.
### How the coupon promo appears
The coupon promo appears as a banner in the review form. The new banner uses a dashed border and a horizontal layout, with the discount amount on the left and the promo message on the right.
The message changes based on your coupon settings. For example:
  * If customers can receive a coupon for any review, they will see:  
​**Write a review and get a coupon for 10% off your next purchase**
  * If customers need to add a photo or video to receive the coupon, they will see:  
​**Write a review and add a photo or video to get a coupon for 10% off your next purchase**
  * If customers need to add a video to receive the coupon, they will see:  
​**Write a review and add a video to get a coupon for 10% off your next purchase**


If you offer different coupon values depending on the review type, the intro screen shows the highest available reward with **up to** messaging.
For example: 
**`Write a review and get a coupon for up to 15% off your next purchase`**
### Coupon promo on the media upload step
If your coupon settings reward customers for adding a photo or video, the coupon promo also appears on the media upload step.
For example:
  * **Add a photo or video and get a coupon for 10% off your next purchase**
  * **Add a video and get a coupon for 15% off your next purchase**


If both photo and video rewards are enabled with different values, customers see separate tier-specific banners.
  * On desktop, the banners appear side by side.
  * On mobile, the banners appear stacked.


### Coupon confirmation after review submission
After a customer submits a review and qualifies for a coupon, the success screen confirms that the coupon was earned.
Customers will see:
**`You’ve got a coupon for your next purchase at [store name]!`**  
​**`You’ll receive your coupon email within the hour.`**
The coupon code is not shown directly on the review form. Customers receive the coupon by email.
### QR codes and shareable review links
When customers open a review form from a QR code or shareable review link, the coupon promo appears in the same Write a review flow.
If the link opens an all-products review page, the coupon promo appears after the customer chooses a product to review.
### Shopify Customer Accounts
When customers submit reviews through Judge.me in Shopify Customer Accounts, they can also see coupon promo messaging if they’re eligible for a coupon.
The Customer Accounts banner uses text-only messaging, but the wording follows the same coupon logic used in the other Write a review flows.
### When the coupon promo is hidden
The coupon promo won’t appear if:
  * Coupon rewards are disabled.
  * The customer isn’t eligible to receive a coupon based on your coupon settings.
  * The customer already received a coupon for this review.
  * The review flow uses the older fold-down review form.


For external Shopify-native coupons, the promo uses the word **reward** instead of showing a specific discount amount.

# Troubleshoot
* * *
**Why wasn’t a coupon generated for this review?**
If a customer submitted a review, but you see that no coupon was generated for them, first check when the review was submitted.
If it’s been **less than 30 minutes** , the coupon may still be processing. We have a 30-minute delay before generating coupons.
If more than 30 minutes have passed since the review was submitted, check if the customer and their review qualify the requirements in your settings.
* * *
**My customer said their coupon is not working**
If a customer submitted a review and received a coupon code, but reports that the coupon doesn’t work at checkout, it's usually due to one of the following reasons:
  * Coupon validity:
    * The coupon has expired.
  * Coupon usage requirements:
    * The order doesn’t meet the minimum requirements (e.g. order value or quantity).
    * The coupon is restricted to a specific customer (e.g. only usable by the reviewer).
    * The coupon was already used once, and your settings limit one-time use.
    * The customer is trying to use it with another discount that’s not allowed.


To check why a customer can't use a coupon:
**Step 1: Find the discount name**
First, identify which Shopify discount the customer’s coupon belongs to.
  1. [See how to find the coupon code in Judge.me](https://judge.me/help/en/articles/8379747-offering-judge-me-generated-coupon-codes-for-reviews#h_ab7cf6d9d4).
  2. Copy the **Corresponding coupon on Shopify** (the second of the 2 codes, which starts with `Judge.me Coupons`).


**Step 2: Check the coupon validity**
To see whether the discount is still active:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to **[Discounts](https://admin.shopify.com/discounts)**.
  2. Find the discount you copied from Step 1.
  3. See if its status shows **Active** or **Expired**.


If the discount status is **Expired** : This means the discount — and all coupon codes generated from it — has expired. 
You can re-activate the discount so the customer can use their code again. However, reactivating a discount will reactivate all coupon codes under that discount, not just the one for this customer.
To reactivate a discount:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to **[Discounts](https://admin.shopify.com/discounts)**.
  2. Find and open the discount you want to reactivate.
  3. On the top-right corner, open the **More actions** dropdown.
  4. Select **Reactivate**.


If the discount status is **Active** : continue to Step 3 to check the coupon usage requirements.
**Step 3: Check the coupon usage** **requirements**
If the discount status is Active, review its usage rules:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to **Discounts**.
  2. Find and open the discount you copied from Step 1.
  3. On the right menu, check the **Details** section.
  4. Confirm whether the customer and their order meet all requirements (minimum order value, customer eligibility, usage limits, discount combinations, etc.).


If needed, you can manually adjust the settings of that discount in Shopify to let the customer user it. Keep in mind that any changes you make will apply to all coupon codes under that discount, not just one specific customer.
* * *
**A customer is using multiple discounts in their order**
Judge.me coupons are generated per qualified review. For example, if a customer submits 2 qualified reviews, they will receive 2 separate coupons. By default, they may be able to apply both coupons in the same order.
While it’s not possible to change the “one coupon per review” generation rule, you can reduce the chance of multiple coupons being used together by:
  1. Disabling the "Order discounts" combination option
  2. Selecting a "Minimum order value" or "Minimum quantity of items" as one of your coupon usage requirements.


* * *
**Can I use external or static discount codes for tiered rewards?**
  
No. Tiered discount feature is available only with **Judge.me-generated coupon codes**. If you use **your own discount codes** (one static code or dynamic codes you upload), Judge.me only **sends the code** — the **discount is whatever you already set in Shopify** for that code. You can't set tiered amounts in Judge.me for those static or dynamic codes.
