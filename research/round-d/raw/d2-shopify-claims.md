# Submitting shipping claims
If your shipment with shipping labels is missing, late, or damaged, and your shipment has carrier coverage or [shipping insurance](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/fulfilling-orders/shipping-labels/shipping-carriers/shipping-claims#shipping-insurance), then you can start the process of submitting a claim for your shipment in the Shopify admin. The process varies, depending on the carrier that you used for shipping and whether your shipment has additional insurance through Shipsurance.

## Shipping insurance with Shipsurance
You need to meet the following requirements to be eligible to purchase shipping insurance through Shipsurance:
  * Your shipment needs to be shipped from the United States.
  * You need to buy a shipping label in Shopify.
  * Your products need to comply with Shipsurance’s Terms of Service.


If you meet the following additional requirements, then up to $200 USD of shipping insurance is included on each eligible label at no cost:
  * Shopify Payments must be activated on your store.
  * Your store must be on the Grow, Advanced, or Plus plan.


## Submit a claim with Shipsurance
If you have [shipping insurance with Shipsurance](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/fulfilling-orders/shipping-labels/shipping-carriers/shipping-claims#shipping-insurance), then you can fill out a form to submit a claim directly through your Shopify admin.
#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to [**Orders**](https://admin.shopify.com/orders).
  2. Click the order that you want to submit a shipping claim for.
  3. In the fulfillment card for the order, click ![Horizontal menu](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/MenuHorizontalIcon.svg).
  4. Click **File shipping claim**.
  5. Select **Shipsurance** from the list of available options.
  6. Click **Next**.
  7. Complete the claims form and make sure to include the following information:
     * the claim amount
     * payout details
     * contact information
  8. Select **I have read and understand the disclaimer above** , and then click **File claim**.


## Submit a claim directly with the shipping carrier
If you ship using one of the following carriers, then your claim isn't tracked or stored by Shopify:
  * [Shipping with USPS](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/fulfilling-orders/shipping-labels/shipping-carriers/usps#shipping-claim)
  * [Shipping with Canada Post](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/fulfilling-orders/shipping-labels/shipping-carriers/canada-post#shipping-claim)
  * [Shipping with Purolator](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/fulfilling-orders/shipping-labels/shipping-carriers/purolator#shipping-claim) (lost and damage claims only)
  * [Shipping with FedEx](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/fulfilling-orders/shipping-labels/shipping-carriers/fedex#shipping-claim)
  * [Third-party carrier calculated rates with Fedex](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/setup/shipping-rates/third-party-carrier-calculated-shipping/fedex)
  * [Third-party carrier calculated rates with UPS](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/setup/shipping-rates/third-party-carrier-calculated-shipping/ups)


#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to [**Orders**](https://admin.shopify.com/orders).
  2. Click the order that you want to submit a shipping claim for.
  3. In the fulfillment card for the order, click ![Horizontal menu](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/MenuHorizontalIcon.svg).
  4. Click **File shipping claim**.
  5. After you click **File shipping claim** , you're provided with a link to submit a claim directly with the carrier.

### Shipping claims process for carriers supported by Shopify Support
The following details outline the process for obtaining and using your reference ticket ID:
  * You submit a claim to Shopify Support.
  * An automated email response is sent to acknowledge your request, but this email doesn't contain a reference ticket ID.
  * Shopify Support replies to your email, which includes a reference ticket ID. The timeline of the order that the claim is about updates to include this reference ticket ID.
  * You can reply directly to the email from Shopify Support to continue resolving the request.


### Submit a claim to Shopify Support
Before filing a claim, review the following consideration:
  * Claims can only be filed from your order page through desktop or a mobile browser. Claim filing isn't supported via the Shopify app.
  * The option to file late claims is only available for eligible mail services.


#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to [**Orders**](https://admin.shopify.com/orders).
  2. Click the order that you want to submit a shipping claim for.
  3. In the fulfillment card for the order, click ![Horizontal menu](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/MenuHorizontalIcon.svg).
  4. Click **File shipping claim**.
  5. Select the shipping carrier and the type of claim from the list of available options.
  6. Click **Next**.
  7. Complete the claims form and make sure to include the following information:
     * the reason for filing a claim
     * the product's description and category
     * photos of the damage to the products, the shipment packaging, and the shipping label attached to the packaging with the tracking number clearly visible
     * the claim amount
  8. In the **Summary** section, review the information, and then click **File claim**.


After you submit the claim, the fulfillment card indicates that a shipping claim has been made.
