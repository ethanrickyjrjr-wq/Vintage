# Manage your services on your Business Profile
Businesses in certain categories, like service businesses, might have the option to showcase their offerings directly on their Business Profile. [Learn how to manage your business category](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7249669).
When local customers search Google for an offering you provide, that service may be highlighted on your profile. On the Google Maps app, customers can view your full menu under "Services."
## How the services editor works
When adding services, you can choose from suggested options tailored to your business type. For example, a plumbing business might get options such as "Install faucet" or "Repair toilet." You can also create and add custom services options.
If your business offers different types of services, you can group services into categories and include additional details, like descriptions and prices.
**Important:** Custom services that violate policy will be automatically rejected. Avoid including profanity, gibberish, personal information, prices, or phone numbers in custom service names.

##  Add your services
  1. [Go to your Business Profile](https://business.google.com).
  2. Select **Edit services**.
  3. Select the services you want to add.
  4. Select **Save**.


## Add or edit details about your services
  1. [Go to your Business Profile](https://business.google.com).
  2. Select **Edit services**.
  3. Select the service you want to edit.
  4. You can add info such as price and description. 
     * To remove the service, select **Delete service**.
  5. Select **Save**.


## Add additional services
  1. [Go to your Business Profile](https://business.google.com).
  2. Select **Edit services**![and then](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/3_l97rr0GvhSP2XV5OoCkV2ZDTIisAOczrSdzNCBxhIKWrjXjHucxNwocghoUa39gw=h36) **Add more services**.
  3. Select the services you want to add. 
     * If your service isn’t listed and you want to create your own service, select **Add custom service**.
  4. Select **Save**.


## Add service categories
  1. [Go to your Business Profile](https://business.google.com).
  2. Select **Edit services**![and then](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/3_l97rr0GvhSP2XV5OoCkV2ZDTIisAOczrSdzNCBxhIKWrjXjHucxNwocghoUa39gw=h36) **Add a new business category**.
  3. Enter the business category and select from the available options. 
     * To add services you offer in that category, select the services you offer or add a custom service.
  4. Select **Save**.
