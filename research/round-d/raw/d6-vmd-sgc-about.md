# About
![](https://www.vintagemarketdays.com/site_images/franchise/78/37458-640.png)
### Danny & Meri Stoute
Bienvenu!! Most of you know us as the couple with the cajun accents and southern hospitality! We were vendors with Vintage Market Days® for approximately two years with our Cajun Food Truck - Allons Mangér. Being a part of this family is the reason we are bringing our southern hospitality to Vintage Market Days®. We have a large blended family with five children and six grandchildren between us. As a couple we have ventured into several businesses together and now we are the proud owners of Vintage Market Days® of South Gulf Coast Florida. We love "bringing the vintage experience" to the Southwest Florida area.
We can't wait to see you at our next market!
