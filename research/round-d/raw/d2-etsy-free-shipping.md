# How to Offer Free Shipping 
To better meet buyers’ expectations when it comes to shipping prices, Etsy has three flexible options that you can use to offer free shipping: free shipping guarantees, our smart pricing tool, and shipping profiles. 
You can also learn [strategies for offering free shipping](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/the-ultimate-guide-to-shipping/541298773986) in our Seller Handbook.

## **How to set up free shipping**
Pricing your items to include the shipping cost can help offset the cost of offering free shipping.
You can choose to offer free shipping to buyers only located in your country, or to all buyers around the world. 
### What does offering free shipping cost me as a seller?
When you offer free shipping in your shop, you’ll still need to pay the shipping carrier to deliver your orders. When you add the cost to ship to the item price, the listing will still show free shipping, and you won't lose profits.
For domestic orders 
Imagine that you sell a $35 item, and you currently charge a $5 flat rate to ship it. By adjusting the item price to $40, you recover your shipping cost without charging a separate shipping fee.  
|   |  Current pricing  |  Pricing with free shipping  |  
| --- | --- | --- |  
|  Item list price  |  $35  |  $40  |  
|  Shipping price  |  $5  |  $0 (Free!)  |  
|  Total price for the buyer  |  $40  |  $40  |  
For international orders 
You can update your international shipping costs to account for any shipping costs that you included in your item prices. If you lower your shipping prices for international shipping, your international customers don’t have to pay more than they did before you adjusted your item prices to recover your shipping costs.
For example, you sell a $35 item, and you currently charge a $5 flat rate to ship it domestically, and $10 to ship it internationally. You can change the item price to $40 to recover the cost of domestic shipping, and charge a $5 flat rate to ship internationally.  
|   |  Current international pricing  |  International pricing with free domestic shipping  |  
| --- | --- | --- |  
|  Item list price  |  $35  |  $40  |  
|  International shipping price  |  $10  |  $5  |  
|  Total price for an international buyer  |  $45  |  $45  |  
To update your international shipping prices:
On Etsy.com: 
  1. In **Shop Manager** , choose **Settings**.
  2. Select [Shipping settings](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/shipping-profiles).
  3. Choose **Edit** next to a shipping profile. 
  4. Subtract the number you are charging for domestic shipping from your international shipping cost. 
  5. Enter this number as your price and choose **Save profile**.


On the Etsy Seller app: 
  1. Tap **More**. 
  2. Tap **Shipping**. 
  3. Choose **Edit** in the three-dot menu next to a shipping profile. 
  4. Subtract the number you are charging for domestic shipping from your international shipping cost. 
  5. Enter this number as your price and choose **Save profile**.


Sellers outside the US can [set up a different listing price for domestic orders and international orders](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/4403156582039?segment=selling). This means you can price your items to include different shipping costs for international orders.
## **How to set up a free shipping guarantee**
A free shipping guarantee automatically applies free shipping for US buyers when they purchase the following from your shop: 
  * An individual item $35 USD and up
  * An order with a total cost of $35 USD and up


Once set up, the free shipping guarantee is also applied to new listings that are over $35 USD, no matter what your shop currency is set to. Your shipping prices for buyers in other countries will not change from what is set in your shipping profiles and item listings. The free shipping guarantee applies to both domestic and international sellers shipping orders to US buyers. 
If a buyer chooses to purchase shipping upgrades, they will be charged for those even if their order qualifies for free shipping. 
To set up a free shipping guarantee:
On Etsy.com: 
  1. Go to your **Shop Manager**. 
  2. Choose **Settings**
  3. Choose **Shipping settings**
  4. Go to the [Free shipping guarantee](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/shipping/free-shipping-guarantee) tab and choose **Get started**. 
  5. Choose **Next.**
  6. Choose **Set free shipping now**.


On the Etsy Seller app:
  1. Tap **More**. 
  2. Tap **Shipping**. 
  3. Choose **Next.**
  4. Choose **Set free shipping now**.


## **How to use the smart pricing tool when you offer a free shipping guarantee**
To recover your shipping costs, use the smart pricing tool to bulk edit your listing prices for items costing over $35. These changes will remain in place even if you turn off your free shipping guarantee.
​​You’ll have access to the smart pricing tool if:
  * Your shop is located in the US
  * You don’t already offer free shipping on your items $35 or more
  * You have set up a [free shipping guarantee](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360024198553-How-to-Offer-Free-Shipping?segment=selling#h_01G575JX0X6PT84HT9XTZ7DC65)


How to adjust item prices with the smart pricing tool 
  1. [Set up a free shipping guarantee for your shop](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360024198553-How-to-Offer-Free-Shipping?segment=selling#h_01G575JX0X6PT84HT9XTZ7DC65).
  2. In the[ Free shipping guarantee](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/shipping/free-shipping-guarantee) tab, select **Start repricing**. 
  3. Select **Build in shipping prices** , then choose **Review and edit**.
  4. Select **Edit item prices**.
  5. If you want to edit individual item prices, select the price under that item and enter the new price you want to charge.
  6. If you want to edit multiple listing prices at once, you can choose to:  
- **Decrease amount by:** Decrease the new list price for selected items by this amount.  
- **Increase amount by:** Increase the new list price for selected items by this amount.  
- **Set new amount:** Set the new list price for selected items by this amount.
  7. Enter the amount in the text box.
  8. Select **Apply**.
  9. Select **Publish**. 
  10. Select **Publish** again. It may take a few minutes for the new prices to show in your shop.


The new prices will be the same for all Etsy shoppers, including shoppers outside of the US.
### **How are built-in shipping prices calculated?**
The built-in shipping price change would increase your item price by the average amount US buyers would pay at checkout on Etsy, rounded up to the nearest dollar. You’ll still need to pay the cost of shipping orders.
### **How does the free shipping tool adjust my shipping profiles?**
The shipping prices in your shipping profiles will be adjusted to account for any price adjustments you made to your items. This means that most buyers will pay around the same total price they did before these adjustments.
Shipping adjustments
**US shipping prices:** US standard shipping prices will be adjusted to 0.00 for any profiles with items with prices you adjusted. 
**Shipping prices outside the US:** If you choose to build in shipping prices for your items with[ fixed price shipping](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360024198993?segment=selling#h_29558f7c-a56f-4642-ade5-32fa41599d91), your shipping prices to other countries will be reduced by the same amount as your standard US shipping price.
**Profiles with items $35 USD and over, and below $35 USD:** If you had a[ shipping profile](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115014115187?segment=selling) that had both items over and under $35 USD, all items $35 USD and over are moved to a new shipping profile. The new profile has the same name as the old profile, followed by **(Reduced shipping)**.
**Shipping profiles with variations:** The price of your variations will change by the same amount that your base listing price changes. For example: If you increase the price of a silver ring by $5 and have a gold variation of that ring, the price of the gold variation will also increase $5.
## **How to recover shipping costs**
There are several ways you can recover shipping costs depending on if you use fixed or calculated shipping.
For sellers who use fixed shipping prices 
Choose either:
  * **Build in shipping prices to recover shipping costs** : Adjust your item prices to include the shipping costs you've specified for each listing. This helps you recover the cost of shipping for items with free shipping.
  * **Keep your current prices** : Keep your current item prices. 


Then select **Review & edit** to review your item prices. You can edit the amount your item prices will be adjusted by to recover shipping costs as you see fit. The amount that you adjust your prices by to recover costs is up to you. 
For US and Canada sellers who use calculated shipping 
Sellers who use calculated shipping can choose one of the following options to recover shipping costs:
  * **Closest buyer:** Increase your item prices by the amount it costs to ship to buyers in your postal code.
  * **Average buyer distance:** Increase your item prices by the amount it costs to ship the median distance of your US orders in the last year. If you don't have enough orders for us to determine this distance, we use the median distance for similar items.
  * **Farthest buyer:** Increase your item prices by the amount it costs to ship to buyers the farthest from you.
  * **Keep your current prices:** Keep your current item prices. 


Then click **Review & edit** to review your item prices, adjusting them as you see fit. 
## **How to use shipping profiles to offer free shipping**
If you set up a [free shipping guarantee](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360024198553-How-to-Offer-Free-Shipping?segment=selling#h_01G575JX0X6PT84HT9XTZ7DC65) for your shop, all orders $35 or more shipping to the US will have free shipping for the buyer. 
If you want to offer free shipping to buyers outside of the US, or you want to offer free shipping for all orders from your shop, you can use free shipping profiles.
[Learn more about shipping profiles.](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115014115187?segment=selling)
To create or edit a free shipping profile on Etsy.com: 
  1. Go to [**Shop Manager**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/dashboard) , then **Settings**. 
  2. Go to **Shipping settings**.
  3. Select the pencil icon next to a shipping profile that you want to change to free shipping. You can also choose **Add profile**. If you’re adding a new shipping profile, complete the [required information](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115014115187?segment=selling).
  4. If you want to add free shipping for specific countries, make sure to select **I’ll enter fixed prices manually** under **Shipping prices.** Then, use the **Add a location** dropdown under **Fixed shipping prices**.
  5. Select **Free shipping** from the **What you’ll charge** dropdown.
  6. Select **Save profile**.


To create or edit a free shipping profile on the Etsy Seller app: 
  1. From the **Home** menu, tap **More**. 
  2. Under **Shop settings** , tap **Shipping**. 
  3. Select the **…** button next to the shipping profile you want to edit, then choose **Edit**. You can also tap the **+** button to create a new profile. 
  4. Select **Free shipping** from the **What you’ll charge** dropdown.
  5. Select **Save profile**.


To update the shipping profiles on your listings on Etsy.com: 
  1. Go to [**Shop Manager**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/dashboard) , then [**Listings**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/listings).
  2. If you want to edit all your listings, select the checkbox at the top left. If you want to edit some listing, select the checkboxes under those listings.
  3. Select **Editing options**.
  4. Select **Change shipping profile**.
  5. Select your free shipping profile.
  6. Select **Apply**.

How to make free shipping work for your shop 
The simplest way to make free shipping work for your shop is to [include the cost of shipping in the item price](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360024198933?segment=selling). When you make this decision, remember that how you determine and set your prices is your decision.
By including shipping costs in your item prices, buyers know what they need to pay for your items up-front, and you don’t lose money on orders with free shipping. [Learn more about how it can benefit your business to offer free shipping](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360024564734?segment=selling).
[Learn more about offering free shipping](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000343368?segment=selling).
How to set up a free shipping guarantee 
To better meet US buyers’ expectations when it comes to shipping price, you can set up a free shipping guarantee for your shop. A free shipping guarantee applies to orders $35 and over that ship to the US.
[Set up a free shipping guarantee](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/shipping/free-shipping-guarantee)
Why wasn’t shipping free on a US order? 
Even if you offer a free shipping guarantee, some orders with a US shipping address may not qualify for free shipping because of other account settings. Buyers must also:
  * Set their [region on Etsy](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015306687?segment=selling) to the US and
  * Set their [currency preference](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015520608?segment=selling) on Etsy to USD


[Read the terms of the free shipping guarantee](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/free-shipping-terms/587583372646).
Why did a buyer receive a shipping upgrade for free? 
A free shipping offer covers the lowest-cost available mail class. If an order exceeds the weight or dimensions of a low-cost mail class, a mail service that can accomodate the order is used. For example, if an order exceeds the allowed dimensions for USPS’ First-Class mail, USPS Priority mail is offered free. 
To prevent this, check if you can update the dimensions of an item to fit within your preferred mail class. Remember that item dimensions used for shipping must be accurate, or you may incur a shipping label adjustment fee from the carrier.
Can I set up different item prices for international free shipping? 
If you’re a seller outside the US and India, you can set up a different listing price for orders shipping within your country and orders shipping outside your country.
When you set up domestic & global pricing for items with free shipping, make sure to add domestic & global pricing to all the other listings linked to your free shipping profile. [Learn how to add domestic & global pricing to your listings](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/4403156582039?segment=selling).
