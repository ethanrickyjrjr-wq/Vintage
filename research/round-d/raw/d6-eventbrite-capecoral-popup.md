# Pop up shops Events and Things to do in Cape Coral, FL
Pop up shops events and things to do in Cape Coral, FL highlight local maker culture, rotating artisan goods, and creative community markets. Independent creators frequently display handcrafted wares, specialty foods, and apparel in casual open-air settings. Browsing these temporary vendor setups provides a direct connection to regional makers and unique small-batch items.
  * South Cape acts as a primary hub for pedestrian-friendly retail gatherings and street-level vendor displays during community street events.
  * The Cape Coral Farmers Market offers a weekly destination for local food producers, craft makers, and specialty merchants showcasing goods in an open-air format.
  * Surfside Farmers Market provides a recurring seasonal setting featuring regional creators, agricultural vendors, and handmade apparel displays.
