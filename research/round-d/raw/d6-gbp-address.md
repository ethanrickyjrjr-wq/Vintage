# Manage your business address
To help customers find your business on Maps and Search, add your business address on your Business Profile. You can also pin your location on the map to show where your business is.
The address on your Business Profile is public. If you don't serve customers at your business address, such as for a service-area business, you can choose to not show your address and list your service area instead. [Learn how to manage your service area](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481).
[Manage your business](https://business.google.com/?skipLandingPage=true&gmbsrc=ww-ww-et-gs-z-gmb-v-z-h~bhc-core-u|mybb)
## Understand address guidelines
To verify your business, enter a business address that Google can find. Make sure your address follows these guidelines. [Learn more about guidelines for representing your business on Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177/#address).
[Troubleshooting Claiming Your Business Profile](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3dMB25FczI4)
  * Enter a complete street address. 
    * **Example:** 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA, 94043.
  * Include building, suite, and floor numbers and other unit info.
  * Only include info that’s part of your official address. In places where the official address isn’t accurate, you can add nearby cross-streets or landmarks. 
    * Avoid showing your address like "Corner of Main St. and Broadway Ave." or "Across from Central Park."
  * If your address doesn't have a street number or if the system can't find a correct address, you can pin your location on the map.


## Add or edit your address
  1. [Go to your Business Profile](https://business.google.com).
  2. Select **Edit profile**![and then](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/3_l97rr0GvhSP2XV5OoCkV2ZDTIisAOczrSdzNCBxhIKWrjXjHucxNwocghoUa39gw=h36) **Location**.
  3. Next to “Business location,” select Edit ![Edit pencil](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/YdpYLwumnJmx_u1Rf_yYxCbUsEcuV5VdfZLJzWUVn0xob_N_7Zrz-I9CSXl-Q0VMfOEJ=h36).
  4. Enter your address details. 
     * If we can't find your address, you'll get a warning message. Re-enter parts of the address that are highlighted in red.
     * If we still can’t find your address, select **Adjust** to set a pin on the map to show where your business is.
  5. Select **Save**.


**Tips:**
  * Changes to your profile usually take about 10 minutes to show up**.** In some cases, it can take up to 30 days. [Learn more about pending review edits](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038311#pending).
  * If your business is located in another business, like a store inside a shopping mall, you can [request to have this info shown in your profile](https://support.google.com/business/gethelp).
  * Based on your business category and location, your profile may show a location description. This helps customers find your business by a landmark, location, or a nearby business. You can't edit your location description on Search or Maps.


## Add or edit your address on the Google Maps app
  1. On your mobile device, open the Google Maps app ![Maps](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/yBT9NcPCOum61BXUrmlfBecOpNufMGBDbh3rFQfIQu1Rs23tjRnhNdEou6yHAurnyw=h36).
  2. At the bottom right, tap **Business**.
  3. Select **Edit profile**![and then](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/3_l97rr0GvhSP2XV5OoCkV2ZDTIisAOczrSdzNCBxhIKWrjXjHucxNwocghoUa39gw=h36) **Location**.
  4. Next to “Business location,” select Edit ![Edit pencil](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/YdpYLwumnJmx_u1Rf_yYxCbUsEcuV5VdfZLJzWUVn0xob_N_7Zrz-I9CSXl-Q0VMfOEJ=h36).
  5. Enter your address details.
  6. Select **Save**.


## Add or edit a pin for your business
If your address doesn't have a street number or if the system can’t find your business at the address you entered, you can pin your business directly on the map.
To adjust your pin location:
  1. [Go to your Business Profile](https://business.google.com).
  2. Select **Edit profile** ![and then](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/3_l97rr0GvhSP2XV5OoCkV2ZDTIisAOczrSdzNCBxhIKWrjXjHucxNwocghoUa39gw=h36) **Location**.
  3. Next to “Business location,” select Edit ![Edit pencil](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/YdpYLwumnJmx_u1Rf_yYxCbUsEcuV5VdfZLJzWUVn0xob_N_7Zrz-I9CSXl-Q0VMfOEJ=h36).
  4. To adjust your pin location, select **Adjust**.
  5. Move the map so the pin points to your business location.
  6. Select **Done** ![and then](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/3_l97rr0GvhSP2XV5OoCkV2ZDTIisAOczrSdzNCBxhIKWrjXjHucxNwocghoUa39gw=h36) **Save**.


## Remove your address
**Important:**
  * You should only choose to not show your address if your business is a service-area business.
  * If you hide your business address, your Business Profile will only show your service area. If you don't enter a service area, a local area is chosen for you. You can edit it later. [Learn how to manage your service area](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481).

## Manage Street View imagery for your business
If Street View is available for your business, you can review it in Google Maps or Search. In Maps, select **Street View & 360°** in your profile’s photo section. On Search, select the photo with the "See outside" label. To update your business’s Street View, you can either:
  * Edit the map pin for your business to associate it with a more accurate Street View image. [Learn how to add or edit a pin for your business](https://support.google.com/business/answer/2853879?hl=en#pin).
  * Add your own Street View imagery.


**Tip:** To find how your change affected the imagery, check your updated Street View in Google Maps. [Learn how to use Street View in Google Maps](https://support.google.com/maps/answer/3093484).
### Add new Street View imagery
  * With Street View Studio, you can create and publish Photo Spheres to Google Maps. [Learn how to upload 360 videos to Street View Studio](https://support.google.com/maps/answer/7546470).
  * You can hire a professional photographer to capture an indoor Street View. You can then add your own Street View imagery to show off the inside of your location to customers. [Learn how to create and publish your own Street View imagery](https://www.google.com/streetview/contribute/).
  * To help customers recognize your business on Google, you can add your own photos to your Business Profile. [Learn how to manage photos or videos for your Business Profile](https://support.google.com/business/answer/6103862).


### Report outdated Satellite or Street View imagery
**Important:** Google can't commit to a specific timeline to review and update imagery.
To report outdated imagery, [fill out this form](https://support.google.com/maps/contact/report_outdated_imagery). Your feedback helps us to determine where imagery updates are most important.
## Report incorrect driving directions to your business
Accurate driving directions help customers find and arrive at your business. If driving directions to your business in Google Maps are incorrect, report them. [Learn how to report wrong directions in Google Maps](https://support.google.com/maps/answer/6194894).
