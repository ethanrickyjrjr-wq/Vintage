#### Category
  * **Vintage Home and Goods**  
90% of items presented are antique, vintage, retro, re-purposed, and/or up-cycled. (Market, new, wholesale or vintage inspired items for the home only must be limited to 10% or less of entire exhibit)
  * **Clothing and Accessories**  
100% of goods presented for sale are clothing and/or accessories to be worn or used in conjunction with items being worn by men, women, and children. 100% of goods are on-trend, vintage inspired, vintage, retro, re-purposed, up-cycled, and/or hand crafted.
  * **Jewelry**  
100% of goods presented for sale is jewelry or made from jewelry. 100% of goods are antique, vintage, retro, re-purposed, up-cycled, and/or hand-crafted.
  * **Consumable Yummies - Specialty Food**  
Meals prepared sold and consumed on site. 100% of goods presented are consumable, prepackaged and/or prepared on site. 100% of goods are on-trend, gourmet, hand-made, unique, organic, and/or upscale foods, snacks and/or drinks. Displays and presentation must be eye pleasing and unique.
  * **Consumable Yummies - Specialty Snack and Drinks**  
Snacks or drinks prepared, sold and consumed on site. 100% of goods presented are consumable, prepackaged and/or prepared on site. 100% of goods are on-trend, gourmet, hand-made, unique, organic, and/or upscale foods, snacks and/or drinks. Displays and presentation must be eye pleasing and unique.
  * **Consumable Yummies - Make and Take**  
Snacks, ingredients, or meals that are samples, prepackaged and consumed at a later time. 100% of goods presented are consumable, prepackaged and/or prepared on site. 100% of goods are on-trend, gourmet, hand-made, unique, organic, and/or upscale foods, snacks and/or drinks. Displays and presentation must be eye pleasing and unique.
  * **Art**  
100% of goods are identified as original works and have been mass produced by hand or machinery. Items must be clearly marked and displayed as original works.
  * **Children**  
100% of goods presented is for children. Items specifically created for children include but not limited to clothing, accessories, toys and/or educational materials that are hand-crafted, custom and/or original works, hand-made, vintage and/or vintage-inspired.
  * **Signs**  
90% of goods for sale are signs that are original and/or custom works, antique, vintage, retro, re-purposed, up-cycled, handmade and/or hand crafted. (Vintage home and good, market, new, wholesale or vintage inspired items for the home only must be limited to 10% or less of entire exhibit)
  * **Garden**  
100% of goods presented is to be used in or around the garden or home. 90% of goods consist of seasonal plantings, antique, vintage, re-purposed and or up-cycled items, including but not limited to seasonal plantings, containers, furniture, decorative items and/or architectural salvage pieces. (Market, new, mass produced or vintage inspired items for the home only if exhibited must be limited to 10% or less of entire exhibit)
  * **Specialty**  
100% of goods presented are unique to vendor and cannot be sold or displayed by other vendors in event. 100% of Specialty items are to be original works. Specialty goods and/or services include but not limited to: bath & body, bedding, paper goods, books, photography, party planning, and/or home staging.
  * **Accessories**  
100% of goods presented are for the body, to use or wear, and are items used in a secondary manner to contribute to an individual's style or well-being. Items may include: purses, crystals, fashion hats, caps, and bags.
  * **Health & Beauty**  
100% of goods presented for sale are high quality, handmade alternatives to mass-marketed bath, body, and skincare products.
  * **Scents & Fragrances**  
100% of goods presented for sale are tailored, handmade scents solutions for the home and upscale lifestyle.
  * **Textiles**  
90% of goods presented are created by weaving, knitting, sewing, and/or felting various materials such as cotton, wool, nylon, or any other suitable material. 100% of goods presented are on-trend, vintage, vintage-inspired, retro, repurposed, upcycled, and/or handcrafted items suitable for the home and/or lifestyle categories. (Vintage home and goods, market, new, wholesale, or vintage-inspired items for the home only must be limited to 10% or less of the entire exhibit).


Vintage Styles (Choose all that apply) (Required)
Antique
Any work of art, piece of furniture, decorative object, clothing, or the like, created or produced more than 100 years prior to purchase.
True Vintage
Any work of art, piece of furniture, decorative object, clothing or the like created or produced more than 50 years prior to purchase.
New Vintage
Any work of art, piece of furniture, decorative object, clothing or the like created or produced more than 20 years prior to purchase.
Retro
Any work of art, piece of furniture, decorative object, clothing or the like, created or produced more than 10 years prior to purchase. These items can look out of style for the current time period but have potential of making a comeback.
Re-purpose
Using an item or material for a purpose other than what it was originally created or produced. An original item or material given a new purpose for use.
Up-cycled
Any work of art, piece of furniture, decorative object, clothing or the like created or produced using ordinary objects to make something extraordinary.
Hand Crafted or Hand Made
Any work of art, piece of furniture, decorative object, clothing, or the like, created or produced by hand labor.
Original Work
Any item or writing that is a result of independent effort and not created, received, or copied from or based on others work. Original works can be mass produced by an individual or manufacturer.
Custom Work
Any item or good made to suit or in accordance to customer request.
Market, New, and/or wholesale Items
Goods purchased in large quantities from a manufacturer with the intent to sell at a higher price. These items are vintage inspired or on trend, not unique or original to the vendor selling the items at a VMD market.
Vintage Inspired
Any work of art, piece of furniture, decorative object, clothing, or the like, that is mass produced and resembles or is influenced by vintage design.
On Trend
Any clothing or accessory item having characteristics which conform to current fashion trend, current or in high demand.
Food Vendor
Any consumable food or beverage that does not fit one of the other options.
