#  Lee County Bed Tax Information 
The Lee County Visitor & Convention Bureau (VCB) is funded by a five percent (5%) tourist tax collected on accommodations rented for six months or less. The funding is allocated in the following manner:
![Bed Tax Allocation Pie Chart](https://www.visitfortmyers.com/sites/default/files/styles/webp/public/2023-11/Bed%20Tax%20Allocation%20Pie%20Chart%20PNG.png.webp?itok=lI3AasXi)
**53.6%** - advertising and promotion (VCB operations and Lee County Sports Development operations)
**26.4%** - beach and shoreline improvements and maintenance
**20.0%** - stadium debt service (includes debt payments for the Lee County Sports Complex and Jetblue Park, as well as major maintenance for both facilities)
