We offer standard sizes like the classic 4" x 6" for quick, budget-friendly campaigns, along with square and oversized formats if you want your postcard to stand out from the rest of the mail. And if you’re running a direct mail campaign, try our [EDDM® postcards](https://www.vistaprint.com/marketing-materials/every-door-direct-mail-postcards) for USPS-compliant cards, so you can seamlessly reach every address on a route without needing a mailing list.
Once you've settled on a shape and size, the right paper and finish can help your postcards go the extra mile. Glossy paper enhances photo-heavy designs and includes a UV coating that helps protect your colors in the mail, while matte paper has a smooth, non-shiny finish that's easy to write on, perfect for personal notes or appointment reminders. And if you want something that feels as premium as it looks, soft touch paper adds a smooth, tactile coating that makes your postcards memorable long after they're opened.
When it comes time to design, our templates and built-in guidelines make setup simple, walking you through safe zones and bleed margins so your text and images print exactly where you want them. And don’t forget that while you’re designing your postcards, you can add a QR code with your own personalized URL, giving you an easy way to track engagement and measure results from your campaign. And if your timeline is tight, you can explore our express or 2-day rush delivery options for postcards that fit your schedule. So whatever your vision, custom postcards get it done, and we’re [here](https://www.vistaprint.com/customer-care/help-center/) to help every step of the way.

Frequently asked questions
### How can I use custom postcards?
Postcards are one of our most versatile products. Businesses have traditionally sent customers (or potential customers) postcards in the mail, and this is a great way to reach people in a way that’s convenient for them and cost-effective for you. However, there are a variety of other ways you can use custom postcards. If you have an office or retail location, you can use them as a handout. You can bring them to events as takeaways, or even add a coupon to your design and pass them out on the street like flyers. They also work well as [packaging inserts](https://www.vistaprint.com/marketing-materials/packaging-inserts) when you’re shipping off products to customers, including thank you messages and instructions. With a wide range of sizes and designs, there’s no shortage of options for you.
### Can I design my own postcards? 
Yes, there are a couple of ways to design your personalized postcard:
  * Customize one of our professionally designed postcard templates. You can narrow your search based on specific businesses and uses.
  * Start from scratch by picking one of the more basic designs from our library of templates. In the design studio, you can customize the shapes, text fields and colors for a completely unique design.
  * Choose the upload option to work with a print-ready postcard design.


### Can I use photos on custom postcards?
Absolutely. Using photos on postcards is a great way to engage recipients, whether you’re showcasing a new house on the market, advertising a restaurant opening or announcing your annual sale. We recommend choosing a glossy finish to help your photos pop.
To add photos to postcards, select the image icon in our design studio or choose a template with pre-designated photo placements (you’ll be able to replace them with your own). Just be sure your photos have an image resolution of at least 300 dots per inch (DPI) and are saved in one of the following formats:  
.jpg, .jpeg, .jfif, .bmp, .png, .gif, .heic, .svg, .pdf, .psd, .ai, .eps, .ait, .ppt, .pptx, .tif, .tiff
### What postcard paper stocks are available? What’s the difference between Matte, Glossy and Soft Touch finishes?
We offer several paper stocks and finishes to match your project. You can check out the ‘Compare papers & finishes’ module to see images and explanations of all our materials, but here’s a short overview of some of our most popular stocks:
  * Glossy paper enhances photo vibrancy, making it a great choice for visuals like real estate listings or event photos.
  * Matte paper has a non-shiny finish that's easier to write on, perfect for appointment reminders or personal notes.
  * Soft touch paper has a smooth, velvety coating that gives your postcards a premium, tactile feel, ideal for luxury branding or client gifts.
  * We also offer recycled and uncoated options if you're looking for something more natural.


### Can designs and branding be brought over from my other marketing materials?
If you’re signed into your VistaPrint account, you’ll have access to previously uploaded design elements like photos or logo files while working in our design studio.
###  What custom postcard sizes are available?
The standard size for a custom postcard is 4″ x 6″, but we also offer 4″ x 4″, 4.2″ x 5.5″, 5″ x 7″, 5.5″ x 8.5″, 6″ x 6″, 6″ x 9″ and 6″ x 11″ postcards.
### How do VistaPrint's Postcard Mailing Services work? 
Both help you streamline your direct mail campaigns, but the way we go about it differs:
  1. Our [Direct Mail Postcards](https://www.vistaprint.com/marketing-materials/mailing-services-postcards) option is end-to-end. You can upload your own mailing list, purchase one from us or access a previously downloaded list if you’ve mailed with us before. After we print your run of postcards, we’ll address and send them out according to the info you provide – it’s all included in your order.
  2. With VistaPrint’s [EDDM® postcards](https://www.vistaprint.com/marketing-materials/every-door-direct-mail-postcards), you get access to EDDM®-compliant postcard sizes and templates – removing the guesswork while you’re designing. We’ll print them out and send them to you ready to mail. You’ll take them to the post office to choose a mailing area and pay for postage.


### What are the design file requirements to ensure accurate postcard printing?
To keep your design sharp and properly aligned, we recommend working in CMYK color mode at a resolution of at least 300 DPI. Be sure to keep any important text or images within the safe zone, away from the edges, and include a 0.125-inch bleed around your design so nothing gets cut off during trimming. Our downloadable templates are a good place to start if you’re uploading a print-ready design.
### How fast can I get my custom postcards delivered? 
We offer expedited postcard shipping if you need your postcards sooner, including [2-day delivery](https://www.vistaprint.com/marketing-materials/2-day-postcards/templates) with select postcard options. Just check the shipping calculator while you’re designing to confirm shipping options and estimates in your area.
### Will my postcards get smudged or damaged in the mail? 
We do everything we can to make sure all of our paper and printing options are durable and smudge-resistant. That said, if you plan to send out your postcards through the mail, we can’t guarantee they won’t be damaged or smudged during the mailing process.
