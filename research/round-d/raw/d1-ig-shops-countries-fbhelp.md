Some features related to Shops and checkout on Facebook and Instagram are no longer supported. Learn more about [recent changes](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1314349509894768?helpref=faq_content).
To use Shops on Facebook and Instagram and associated features, including tagging products on Instagram, your business must meet our [Commerce Eligibility Requirements](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/2347002662267537?helpref=faq_content) and be located in a supported country.
**Note** : If you travel temporarily outside of a supported country, you may also lose the ability to [tag products on Instagram](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F1135563777270082&h=AT20NXhSbfv7pd1yAXxCqM4mK5ZwMAcgha9Cm2f72BW84-GqA2C5OMaWSZZt77RxSf5Sco9OjlY1Ll0k5s7d9PeXNbbzMy3xWJOvGx5eUD9s5bOl_-2_o7h5N7qkusVc4FTLJXg). You can [contact support in Commerce Manager](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/2476867112525530?helpref=faq_content) for help with your shop or other account issues.
The following countries are eligible to use the full experience for [Shops on Facebook and Instagram](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/268860861184453?helpref=faq_content) and [Shops ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/786101482615028?helpref=faq_content):  
| **North America**  | 
  * United States (excluding US Territories: American Samoa, Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands, Guam, Puerto Rico and US Virgin Islands)

 |

| **Europe**  |

**Note** : The location of a shop is determined by the location of the commerce account it is associated with. The location of the commerce account may be based on the country that was selected when the shop was set up on Commerce Manager or Shopify, the location of the account’s creator, or other factors.
