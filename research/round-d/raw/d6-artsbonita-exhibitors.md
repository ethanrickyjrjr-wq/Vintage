## Exhibitors Rules & Requirements
##### Exhibitor Rules & Requirements
  * **Final Authority** : The Bonita Springs National Art Festival Committee reserves the right to make the final interpretation of all rules and guidelines pertaining to the event.
  * **Artwork Standards** : The Committee reserves the right to reject any artwork it deems unsuitable for a fine art festival, including but not limited to works that do not meet professional standards of quality, originality, or craftsmanship.
  * **Political Content Prohibited** : Artwork depicting or promoting any political messages, symbols, slogans, or affiliations is strictly prohibited. The Festival is a nonpartisan event, and any work found in violation of this policy will be subject to removal.
  * **Compliance Requirement** : Exhibitors are required to follow all guidelines and recommendations provided by Arts Bonita and the Festival Committee throughout the event. This includes adherence to setup, display, and conduct standards.
  * **Enforcement and Penalties** : Any artist who fails to comply with Festival rules or directives may be removed from the event. No refunds will be issued in the event of ejection due to rule violations.


##### General Rules
  1. Exhibit Hours are from 10 AM to 4 PM Saturday and Sunday. Artists must exhibit during all scheduled festival hours. No rain dates.
  2. ARTWORK must be original in design and executed by the applicant. Each artist must accompany his own work and may display and sell only in the category in which his work has been accepted. No commercial dealers, agents, kits, castings from commercial molds or mass-produced items. No Buy-Sell.
  3. Commercial Status: The rules committee will make final interpretation of commercial status. Artists violating the rules of this Festival will become ineligible for admission to future festivals.
  4. JEWELRY CATEGORY only artists accepted in the jewelry category may display and sell jewelry.
