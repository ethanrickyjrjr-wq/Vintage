# Vendor Info
Here you will find all necessary information on how to become a vendor at the Pine Ridge Road Farmers Market. 
**VENDOR APPLICATION**
  * Go to our **[Vendor Application](https://www.naplesmarketplace.net/vendors/application/)** page to open and and download the form. 
  * Now you can download and fill out the **[Vendor Application](https://www.naplesmarketplace.net/vendors/application/)** on your phone. Email your completed application to naplesmarketplace@gmail.com.


**REQUIREMENTS**
  * Vendors and/or vendors’ representatives MUST speak English!
  * Vendors are responsible for acquiring all necessary licenses and permits to operate and conduct business in Florida/Collier County.


**PRICES FOR THE 2026-2027 SEASON**
  * $40.00 for a single space / per market day
  * A 4-week minimum deposit ($160.00 for a single space) is required up front with Vendor Application.
  * [**Contact**](https://www.naplesmarketplace.net/contact) the Market Manager for payment options.
  * All deposits are non-refundable.
  * We offer monthly terms only.
  * Vendor fees must be paid in advance.
  * Next month’s payments are due on the last market day of the current month.
  * All fees must be paid in full. No partial payments will be accepted.
  * There is NO refunds or absentee credit for missed days.
  * A current-rate processing fee will be added to all credit card charges.
  * Invoices MUST be paid within 24 hours!


**SEASON UPDATES**
  * We are now accepting new vendors for the 2026-2027 season. [**Contact**](https://www.naplesmarketplace.net/contact/) the Market Manager with inquiries. Text or email only!
  * Vendors are no longer allowed to bring their pets to the market. No exceptions!
  * We are NOT accepting any more jewelry vendors at the Pine Ridge Road Farmers Market at this time.
