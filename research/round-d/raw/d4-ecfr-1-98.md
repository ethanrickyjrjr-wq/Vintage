###### eCFR Content
The _Code of Federal Regulations_ (CFR) is the official legal print publication containing the codification of the general and permanent rules published in the _Federal Register_ by the departments and agencies of the Federal Government. The Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (eCFR) is a continuously updated online version of the CFR. It is not an official legal edition of the CFR.
[Learn more](https://www.ecfr.gov/reader-aids/understanding-the-ecfr/what-is-the-ecfr) about the eCFR, its status, and the editorial process.
#### § 1.98 Adjustment of civil monetary penalty amounts.
This section makes inflation adjustments in the dollar amounts of civil monetary penalties provided by law within the Commission's jurisdiction. The following maximum civil penalty amounts apply only to penalties assessed after January 17, 2025, including those penalties whose associated violation predated January 17, 2025.
(a) Section 7A(g)(1) of the Clayton Act, [15 U.S.C. 18a(g)(1)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/18a)—$53,088;
(b) Section 11(_l_) of the Clayton Act, [15 U.S.C. 21](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/21)(_l_)—$28,205;
(c) Section 5(_l_) of the FTC Act, [15 U.S.C. 45](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/45)(_l_)—$53,088;
(d) Section 5(m)(1)(A) of the FTC Act, [15 U.S.C. 45(m)(1)(A)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/45)—$53,088;
(e) Section 5(m)(1)(B) of the FTC Act, [15 U.S.C. 45(m)(1)(B)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/45)—$53,088;
(f) Section 10 of the FTC Act, [15 U.S.C. 50](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/50)—$698;
(g) Section 5 of the Webb-Pomerene (Export Trade) Act, [15 U.S.C. 65](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/65)—$698;
(h) Section 6(b) of the Wool Products Labeling Act, [15 U.S.C. 68d(b)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/68d)—$698;
(i) Section 3(e) of the Fur Products Labeling Act, [15 U.S.C. 69a(e)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/69a)—$698;
(j) Section 8(d)(2) of the Fur Products Labeling Act, [15 U.S.C. 69f(d)(2)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/69f)—$698;
(k) Section 333(a) of the Energy Policy and Conservation Act, [42 U.S.C. 6303(a)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/42/6303)—$575;
(l) Sections 525(a) and (b) of the Energy Policy and Conservation Act, [42 U.S.C. 6395(a)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/42/6395) and [(b)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/42/6395), respectively—$28,205 and $53,088, respectively;
(m) Section 621(a)(2) of the Fair Credit Reporting Act, [15 U.S.C. 1681s(a)(2)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/1681s)—$4,983;
(n) Section 1115(a) of the Medicare Prescription Drug Improvement and Modernization Act of 2003, [Public Law 108-173](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/plaw/108/public/173), as amended by [Public Law 115-263](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/plaw/115/public/263), [21 U.S.C. 355 note](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/21/355)—$18,768;
(o) Section 814(a) of the Energy Independence and Security Act of 2007, [42 U.S.C. 17304](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/42/17304)—$1,510,803; and
(p) Civil monetary penalties authorized by reference to the Federal Trade Commission Act under any other provision of law within the jurisdiction of the Commission—refer to the amounts set forth in [paragraphs (c)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1.98#p-1.98\(c\)), [(d)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1.98#p-1.98\(d\)), [(e)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1.98#p-1.98\(e\)) and [(f)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/section-1.98#p-1.98\(f\)) of this section, as applicable.
