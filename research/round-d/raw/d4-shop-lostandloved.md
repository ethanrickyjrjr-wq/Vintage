## Why We Love Vintage
We love searchig for and finding tiny treasures full of character, and if you are like us, once you start buying Vintage - you won't be able to stop! The great thing about buying second hand or preloved items whether it be clothing or homeware, is that it allows items to stay in circulation that little bit longer. 
We realise that consumers are more aware than ever about the production and longevity of the items they are buying, and here at Lost & Loved we aim to supply beautiful products that will continue to stand the test of time. (Our motto is 'We love to Re-Love').
We hope you find your vintage treasure here, but if there is something specific you are looking for, drop us an email and we will get our hunting hats on!
