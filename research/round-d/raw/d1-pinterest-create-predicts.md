# 3 ways to use Pinterest Predicts™ to level up your 2026 content
December 9, 2025

Figuring out what content your audience will love doesn’t have to be a guessing game. Allow us to reintroduce Pinterest Predicts™, our annual not-yet-trending report that shares emerging trends for the year ahead. Backed by data analysis from billions of keyword and visual searches, these trends aren’t just more powerful—lasting nearly 2x longer than trends originating elsewhere and growing bigger1,2—they’re more reliable, too. Over the last six years, 88% of the predictions we’ve made have come true.2 So skip any old crystal ball, and lean into intel that can help you get ahead, and stay ahead, in 2026 and beyond.
### **Give your content a head start**
Our trends come from real keyword searches on Pinterest, which means they connect with real audiences like yours. And as these trends grow, they're not only more searched, they’re also more saved, clicked and shopped. Incorporating topics that are growing in popularity can help you plan and create content that’s more likely to reach your audience, and encourage them to engage and take action. From design, to planning, to SEO, are a few impactful ways the trend insights can inform your content.  
**  
1. Get creative  
** Pinterest is a visual search engine, so your visuals are the first thing people see. Use this year’s trends to help guide new content or imagery for your content. Or, for the simplest way to get started, audit your assets to find areas of overlap, then use your existing or refreshed content. If you want to try a subtle approach, borrow inspo from the general aesthetic or color palette. Or go all out and create your take on a trend that speaks to you. For example, if you’ve got favorite products that are total “Cool Blue” dreams, now’s the time to give them the spotlight. Or maybe you’ve got a signature eccentric makeup look that embodies “Glitchy Glam.” Time to let that shine.  
**  
2. Consider your keywords  
** Keywords add context to your content, which helps people discover your Pins and boards. Choosing the right ones can make sure you’re showing up early and often. Check out the rising keywords and topics for each trend, and consider adding them to your titles and descriptions. For instance, if you cover weddings, you could work in a rising “Opera Aesthetic” term like “wedding ceiling draping,” or a “Cool Blue”-led term like “ice blue wedding dress,” where relevant. Or if home decor is your wheelhouse, you could hone in on “Afrohemian Decor” by finding opportunities to add “adire fabric” and “bamboo beaded curtains” to the mix.  
  
**3. Find those seasonal moments  
** Pinterest Predicts trends will grow all year, but looking out for opportunities to highlight them ahead of time can help give your content an added boost. Think of seasons, holidays, milestones and events that could tie your content to an emerging trend. Then use these pairings to plan your content weeks or even months in advance. For example, if food’s your focus, you could share a cozy “cabbage alfredo” or “golumpki soup” recipe for cooler weather. Or a fashion brand could plan to feature “brown linen shirts” as temps warm up and “high collar jackets” as they cool down.
