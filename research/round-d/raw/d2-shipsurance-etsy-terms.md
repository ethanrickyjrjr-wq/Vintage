##  Complete Coverage Rules
### Definitions
Throughout this page the following definitions apply:
  1. Carrier means the company that is transporting the parcel or freight covered by this program.  

  2. Freight means packages or shipments transported through commercial freight Carriers.  

  3. Insured means the Shipper(s), individual(s) or companies who have elected to purchase insurance through this program.   

  4. Parcel means packages or shipments transported through parcel carriers.
  5. SHIPSURANCE means the agent, Shipsurance Insurance Services. 
  6. Total Insured Value means the total value of the item(s) declared for insurance coverage. Includes the Carrier provided coverage, if any.  

  7. We, us and our means Voyager Indemnity Insurance Company.  



### Coverage Details
Coverage provided is for all risks of physical loss or damage from an external cause (subject to exclusions) while in transit. Coverage is on a package-by-package basis. Inbound shipments are not covered. 
**Coverage is for sales made on Etsy.com, only, and covers loss, damage, or shortage to the original merchandise shipped plus the original shipping cost (if declared), only.**
You acknowledge that the insurance coverage is provided by Voyager Indemnity Insurance. Voyager is a _[non-admitted insurer](https://s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/download.shipsurance.com/docs/nonadmitted.pdf)_. You may access Voyager Indemnity Insurance Company's Privacy Notice here: _<https://www.assurant.com/privacypolicy/insurance-solutions>_   
  
If you are shipping using the United States Postal Service, make sure to follow the USPS International Mail Manual rules located here: <http://pe.usps.com/text/imm/welcome.htm>. If you ship a prohibited item, there is NO COVERAGE.   
  
If you are shipping using Federal Express (FedEx), make sure you are not shipping any prohibited items. The FedEx list can be found here: <https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/international-prohibited-items.html>. If you ship a prohibited item, there is NO COVERAGE. 
In order for a package to be covered, the shipping carrier must have verified acceptance of your package for mailing. This is found on the respective tracking websites of the shipping carriers. If a shipping label is printed but not scanned by the carrier as accepted it is deemed as never been mailed and there is no coverage.
**Dollar Zero Coverage**  
Coverage for shipments sent via a covered shipping carrier is from dollar zero of declared value. 
  * Claims do not need to be filed with the shipping carrier for their default liability coverage.
  * Premium is collected for the full declared value of the shipment.

####  [ General Claims Questions ](https://www.shipsurance.com/etsy/terms#collapseThree)
**DO I FILE A CLAIM WITH THE SHIPPING CARRIER, FIRST?**  

If you insured a package with Shipsurance, file the claim directly with Shipsurance. Do not file a claim with the shipping carrier as Shipsurance coverage through this program starts from the first dollar.
  
**HOW DO I FILE A CLAIM?**  

Claims are filed from within the Etsy website.  
  
- Sign in to Etsy.com and go to Shop Manager.  
- Go to Orders & Shipping.  
- Select the order with the shipping label you insured.  
- Select the … (ellipsis) icon to the right.  
- Select File Insurance Claim.   
  
During the claims process, a claim statement/affidavit signed by the recipient (or intended recipient) will be required. This is a form document or website link provided by Shipsurance that allows the recipient (or intended recipient) to indicate loss or damage. 
  
**HOW DO I CHECK THE STATUS OF MY CLAIMS?**  

You can check the status of an existing claim [here ](https://app.shipsurance.com/claimstatus/?cid=etsy)
