## Why Design With Havenly?
### Clients
Havenly brings the clients to you, so you can spend your time on designing - not on marketing!
### Flexibility
You decide how many clients you want and where you work from - home office, coffee shop, on the beach?!
### Earnings
Designers earn a fee per project and are also eligible for commission and performance incentives!
### Exposure
Designing with Havenly can lead to features in top-tier design & lifestyle publications and high-profile projects.
## What Happens Next?
### Step 1: Tell Us More
Complete our entrance form. Answer a few simple questions and show us your work! 
### Step 2: Show us your skills 
If we think you're a great fit for the platform you'll be asked to complete a case study to show off your skills! 
### Step 3: Learn About Out Platform
Upon approval of your case study, you'll enter our enrollment program and will be on your way to working with Havenly clients!
