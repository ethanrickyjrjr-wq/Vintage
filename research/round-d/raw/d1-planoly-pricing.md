## Frequently Asked Questions
Does Planoly offer a free trial?![](https://www.planoly.com/media/pricing/Arrow-Down.svg?dpl=dpl_wj54EpABdNrPrYcnHiSACZADNYvU)
Yes! All paid plans include a 14-day free trial so you can explore everything before committing.
Is there a free plan?![](https://www.planoly.com/media/pricing/Arrow-Down.svg?dpl=dpl_wj54EpABdNrPrYcnHiSACZADNYvU)
Yes, our free plan is available on the Planoly mobile app and includes 10 uploads per month.
Can I switch plans after I sign up?![](https://www.planoly.com/media/pricing/Arrow-Down.svg?dpl=dpl_wj54EpABdNrPrYcnHiSACZADNYvU)
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your account settings.
Do you offer annual billing?![](https://www.planoly.com/media/pricing/Arrow-Down.svg?dpl=dpl_wj54EpABdNrPrYcnHiSACZADNYvU)
Yes, and you'll save when you do. Annual plans are billed upfront at a discounted rate.
Can I add additional users or social sets to the Starter plan?![](https://www.planoly.com/media/pricing/Arrow-Down.svg?dpl=dpl_wj54EpABdNrPrYcnHiSACZADNYvU)
Our Starter plan includes one user and one social set. To add additional users or social sets, upgrade to the Growth or Pro plan.
What is a social set?![](https://www.planoly.com/media/pricing/Arrow-Down.svg?dpl=dpl_wj54EpABdNrPrYcnHiSACZADNYvU)
You can link up to one of each social channel, including Instagram, Facebook, X (formerly Twitter), Pinterest, LinkedIn, YouTube, Threads and TikTok.
How much does it cost to add an additional user?![](https://www.planoly.com/media/pricing/Arrow-Down.svg?dpl=dpl_wj54EpABdNrPrYcnHiSACZADNYvU)
For Growth plans, additional users are $5 per month. For Pro plans, additional users are $3 per month.
How much does it cost to add an additional social set?![](https://www.planoly.com/media/pricing/Arrow-Down.svg?dpl=dpl_wj54EpABdNrPrYcnHiSACZADNYvU)
For Growth plans, additional social sets are $10 per month. For Pro plans, additional social sets are $8 per month.
Can I cancel anytime?![](https://www.planoly.com/media/pricing/Arrow-Down.svg?dpl=dpl_wj54EpABdNrPrYcnHiSACZADNYvU)
Yes! You can cancel or downgrade your plan at any time. Visit our [help page](https://www.planoly.com/help) to learn more.
