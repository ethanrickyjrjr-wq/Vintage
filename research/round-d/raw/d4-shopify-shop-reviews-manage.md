# Manage your Shop product reviews
Reviews are visible in the Shop channel to staff members who have the [**Customers** permission](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/users/roles/permissions/store-permissions).
Each of your [products that display in Shop](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/manage-shop-store/products-and-collections) are eligible for review. You can't opt out of receiving product reviews if you sell products on Shop. If you don't want to sell products on Shop, then you can [manage your shopping settings in the Shop channel](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/setup#sell-with-shop).
If you believe a review violates the [Shop Product Review Policy](https://shop.com/product-review-policies), [Shop Terms of Service](https://shop.com/terms-of-service), or otherwise violates the law, then you can [report a review](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/product-reviews/manage#report-product-reviews). This includes reviews with images or videos.

## Customize review request notification intervals
After an order is delivered, you can send a review request any time between 1 to 180 days after delivery. You can customize the time period from when a customer receives their order to when they get a notification requesting a review.
#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to **Sales channels** > [**Shop**](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/shop-72).
  2. Click **Settings**.
  3. In the **Shop reviews** section, in the **Once an order is delivered customers will get a notification after** field, enter the number of days that you want to send a notification after.


## Reply to Shop product reviews
You can reply to a Shop product review in your Shopify admin. Your reply may be screened before being published to make sure that it follows the [Shop product review merchant guidelines](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/product-reviews/merchant-guidelines).
After your reply is published, it displays in Shop underneath the review that you replied to, and the reviewer receives a push notification.
#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to **Sales channels** > [**Shop**](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/shop-72).
  2. Click **Reviews** , and then click the review that you want to reply to.
  3. In the **Reply to this review** field, type your reply up to 1,000 characters, and then click **Send reply**.


#### Note
If a review that you replied to is edited or deleted by the reviewer, then your reply is unpublished. However, you can reply to the new version of that review, if applicable.
### Edit or delete a reply
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to **Sales channels** > [**Shop**](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/shop-72).
  2. Click **Reviews** , and then click the review that you want to edit or delete.
  3. Do one of the following:


  * Click **Edit reply** to edit your reply, and then click **Edit reply** to publish your changes.
  * Click **Delete reply** to delete your reply, and then click **Delete reply** to confirm deletion.


## Report a product review
Shop takes certain proactive and reactive steps to help make sure that reviews that violate the [Shop Product Reviews Policies](https://shop.com/product-review-policies) aren't published, or that they're removed.
If you believe that a review violates Shop’s policies, then you can report a review. Reasons for reporting reviews include the following examples:  
Report a Shop product review  
| Reason for reporting  | Description  |  
| --- | --- |  
| Unrelated  | The review isn’t about the product the customer reviewed, or your store.  |  
| Spam  | The review contains ads, links, or promotional content.  |  
| Offensive  | The review contains inappropriate, sexually explicit, or violent content.  |  
| Bullying or harassment  | The review attacks an individual or group of people.  |  
| Hate speech  | The review contains harmful content based on an individual or group identity.  |  
| Fraud or scam  | The review contains allegations about improper store or customer behavior.  |  
| Personal information  | The review contains information that could identify the reviewer such as email, phone number, or credit card details.  |  
| Illegal activities or regulated goods  | The review references items that go against [Shop Merchant Guidelines](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/eligibility).  |  
| Intellectual property infringement  | The review violates intellectual property laws.  |  
| Conflict of interest  | The review is from someone affiliated with your store, or a competing store.  |  
If any of the above reasons apply, then you can use the following steps to report a review.
#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to **Sales channels** > [**Shop**](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/shop-72).
  2. Click **Reviews** , and then click the review that you want to report.
  3. Click the ![Horizontal menu](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/MenuHorizontalIcon.svg) icon, top right of the review, and then click **Report**.
  4. Select a reason for reporting from the drop-down list, and then click **Report**.


After you report a review, it can take up to 5 business days for the report to be investigated. After you report a review, there is no further communication from Shop regarding the outcome of the investigation. If the review is found to violate Shop's policies, then it's removed from both the Shop app and the Shop sales channel, and no longer displays in your Shop admin. If the review doesn't violate Shop's policies, then it remains published.
## Collect reviews on purchases made outside of Shop
By default, customers can review products that they purchase both through the Shop app, and on your online store. You can activate or deactivate reviews for purchases made on your online store.
#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to **Sales channels** > [**Shop**](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/shop-72).
  2. Click **Settings**.
  3. In the **Shop reviews** section, select or deselect the **Reviews on purchases outside of Shop** checkbox.


## Shop review automations with Shopify Flow
You can use the **Shop Review Published** trigger to create automations in [Shopify Flow](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-flow) that run whenever a Shop review is collected and published. The trigger can be found in Flow after you have the Shop channel installed by searching for **Shop** under **Installed App Triggers**.
The _Shop Review Published_ trigger provides the workflow with review data such as the body, rating, product variant ID, line item id, display name of the reviewer, and the timestamp for when the review was submitted. It also provides the workflow with the Shopify product, order, and shop data.
You can use the **Shop Review Published** trigger to create useful workflows such as having a staff member notified when a review was published in Shop. To find and install example templates from the Flow template browser, filter **Apps** by **Shop**.
You can install Shopify Flow from the [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/flow). Learn more about [Shopify Flow](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-flow).
