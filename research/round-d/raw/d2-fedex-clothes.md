Know before you ship 
Prep to ship clothes: Here’s what you’ll need
  * Wardrobe box or other sturdy corrugated box
  * Hangers (for wardrobe boxes with metal rods)
  * Acid-free paper or muslin cloth (for delicate items)
  * Plastic garment bag or polybag (as appropriate)
  * Packing tape at least 2" wide
  * Packing materials, such as packing peanuts, bubble packaging, or tissue paper

##  How to ship with a wardrobe box 
Whether you’re sending a student off to college or moving to another state, a wardrobe box with a hanging rod is one of the most convenient ways to quickly transfer a closet of clothes, including high-value items like formal attire.
**1. Assemble the wardrobe box.**
  * Use the H taping method and apply at least three strips of packing tape (at least 2" wide) to enclose the bottom of the box.
  * Hang the rod inside the box according to the manufacturer’s instructions.


**2. Prepare your items.**
  * Hang the clothes on hangers.
  * Button shirts and zip jackets.
  * For extra protection, wrap items like blouses in a plastic bag.


**3. Hang your items inside the box.**
  * Put the bottom of your hanging clothes into the box first, adjust them, then hang the hangers on the rod. Don’t hang the hangers first and force your clothes in after.
  * If you have room, you can place other articles in the bottom of the box, such as hats, purses, or shoes. These items may travel best in boxes to avoid distortion or soiling clothing.


**4. Properly seal and label your box.**
  * Use the H taping method and apply at least three strips of packing tape (at least 2" wide) to the top of the box. Tape all seams and flaps.
  * Place your shipping label on the top of the box.

##  How to ship casual clothes 
If you're shipping a small item or two, you may want to use a [poly mailer](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/packing/supplies/near-me.html#mailers). For multiple pieces or large bundles exceeding 5 inches in height, we recommend placing the items in a [corrugate box](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/packing/supplies/boxes.html). Use the steps below to help pack items efficiently, save on shipping costs and reduce wrinkling or damage.
**1. Choose the right box.**
  * Choose a box that’s the right size for your items. If it’s too big, you’ll need to purchase extra packing materials to keep the items from shifting inside the box, and you’ll pay more to ship it. On the other hand, a box that is too small can cause excessive wrinkling, or the seams can burst and potentially expose your item.
  * Use the H taping method and apply at least three strips of packing tape (at least 2" wide) to enclose the bottom of the box.


**2. Prepare your items.**
  * If you’re shipping a shirt, hoodie, dress, or jacket, place it face down on a clean surface and fold the sleeves backwards. Then fold the length of it in three.
  * If you’re shipping pants or jeans, fold them from the knees to the pockets, then fold in half once more. Smooth as you go.
  * Consider enclosing your items in a plastic bag or a polybag to add an extra layer of protection.


**3. Place your items inside the box.**
  * If you are shipping multiple items, stack them or place them in the box so that they make efficient use of the box space.
  * Use packing materials, such as bubble packaging or packing peanuts, to limit internal shifting during transit and minimize wrinkling.


**4. Properly seal and label your box.**
  * Use the H taping method and apply at least three strips of packing tape (at least 2" wide) to the top of the box. Tape all seams and flaps.
  * Place your shipping label on the top of the box. Avoid placing labels over closure seams or tape.

##  How to ship high-value clothing like wedding dresses and formal wear 
Valuable clothing like wedding dresses, tuxedos, suits, prom dresses, and expensive accessories like beaded veils or gloves need special prep and attention. Use these steps to help keep your formal attire looking sharp.
**1. Choose the right box.**
  * Choose a box that’s the right size for your garment. A box that is too large will require inner packing material to fill void space and minimize shifting. Excessively large boxes can also be subject to dimensional weight pricing versus actual weight pricing. A box that is too small can cause excessive wrinkling or if overstuffed, can potentially burst open in transit resulting in exposure or loss.
  * Use a sturdy corrugated box to protect delicate items.
  * Use the H taping method and apply at least three strips of packing tape (at least 2" wide) to enclose the bottom of the box.


**2. Prepare your garment.**
  * To protect delicate fabrics and keep embellishments or beading intact, cover delicate areas with acid-free tissue paper or muslin cloth. Place the tissue paper or cloth between each layer of fabric and fully wrap the garment. For an extra layer of protection, you can encase the garment in a clear plastic bag. Doing so will add an additional layer of protection against damage from snags, soil, or water.
  * Fold your garment according to its design. If you’re packing a dress with a train, fold the left and right sides toward the center of the dress and roll up carefully without bunching or crushing. Fold the dress from bodice to hem.


**3. Place the item inside the box.**
  * Pack the box with packing materials, such as packing peanuts and bubble packaging, to keep your garment from shifting inside the box during transit.


**4. Properly seal and label your box.**
  * Use the H taping method and apply at least three strips of packing tape (at least 2" wide) to the top of the box. Tape all seams and flaps.
  * Place your shipping label on the top of the box. Avoid placing labels over closure seams or tape.

###  Frequently asked questions about how to ship clothes 
What’s the cheapest way to ship clothes?
Shipping costs depend on the weight and size of the box and your delivery needs and timeline. If you’re a small-business owner who ships items that weigh less than 50 lbs. frequently, you might find flat-rate shipping to be most cost-effective. FedEx Ground is another economical shipping option. [Learn more about our shipping options](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/how-to-ship-clothes.html#shippingservice) or [get a rate](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/how-to-ship-clothes.html#shippingservice).
How much does it cost to ship a hoodie or shirt?
Generally, hoodies are small and lightweight, so they’re easy to ship. How much it costs to ship them really depends on how fast you need to ship it and where you need to ship it. Take a look at our [packaging and packing supplies](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/packing/supplies/boxes.html) and [service options](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/how-to-ship-clothes.html#shippingservice) to see what fits your timeline and budget or [get a rate](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/online/rating.html).  

I bought a clothing item online, and I want to return it. How do I ship it back to the retailer if I don’t have the original packaging?
You can purchase [packaging and packing supplies](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/packing/supplies.html) from a nearby [FedEx Office location](https://local.fedex.com/en-us). Learn more about how easy it is to [make a return](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/returns.html).
What’s the best way to ship clothes to another state?
The best way to ship clothes when you’re moving depends on what you’re shipping and how fast you need to get it there. Learn more about shipping [casual clothes](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/how-to-ship-clothes.html#howtoshipcasualclothes) and how to ship using a [wardrobe box](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/how-to-ship-clothes.html#howtoshipwardrobe). You can also [review our shipping service options](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/how-to-ship-clothes.html#shippingservice) to help you determine what works best for your budget and timeline.  
Packaging pointers:
  * Place your shipping label on top of the box to increase the chances it will be transported in the preferred orientation. We cannot ensure compliance with markings such as up arrows or “This End Up.”
  * Choose the right-sized box to avoid paying more than necessary for the box, packing materials, and shipping costs.
  * Choose heavy-duty packaging to help protect delicate items from vigorous shipping conditions.
  * Pack clothing items in a plastic bag, polybag, or garment bag to help prevent them from potentially getting dirty or wet.
  * If moving, write info on the box to let you know who the clothes belong to, or which room they go to.
  * Be cautious when using previously used boxes. They might be weak or contain dirt or insects.
  * If shipping a secondhand item to a customer, such as a wedding dress or casual attire, consider the unboxing experience. Fold your item to help prevent wrinkling and soiling. You might even use decorative tissue paper or include a kind note.
  * If shipping to a consumer, consider using reusable packaging as part of your returns strategy. You’ll help your customer and the environment.


Does FedEx ship wedding dresses?
Yes. It’s a good idea to use one of our [heavy-duty double-walled boxes](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/packing/supplies/boxes.html) or other sturdy box to protect it in transit. There are also certain [steps you can take when packing it](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/how-to-ship-clothes.html#howtoshipformal) to help make sure the special dress is ready for the special day.
Should I leave my clothes on the hanger if I ship them in a standard box?
If you are [shipping your clothes in a standard box](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/how-to-ship-clothes.html#howtoshipcasualclothes) (instead of a [wardrobe box](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/how-to-ship-clothes.html#howtoshipwardrobe)), you should fold your clothes without the hangers. Wire hangers can potentially puncture the box, rip your clothes, and take up unnecessary space in the box.
Can I use FedEx to ship clothes for an online business or marketplace?
Absolutely. [Learn more about e-commerce](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/small-business/ecommerce.html) and get info on how you can use our services and tools to help you grow your online business.
