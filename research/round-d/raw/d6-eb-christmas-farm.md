Overview
Get into the holiday spirit at Buckingham Farms with a festive Christmas on the Farm celebration.
Saturday, December 5, 2026Vendor Setup: 8:00 AM  
Public Hours: 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM  
Live Music: 11:00 AM – 2:00 PM (Two 90-minute sets)  
  
Location  
Buckingham Farms  
12931 Orange River Blvd Fort Myers, FL 33905  
  
Get into the holiday spirit at Buckingham Farms with a festive Christmas on the Farm celebration. Guests can enjoy a magical Sip & Shop experience — sipping wine while shopping from local vendors for unique gifts, holiday décor, and seasonal treasures, all set against the beautiful backdrop of the farm.
