## How it Works
A Personal Approach to Sourcing
1. TELL ME: Contact me and tell me what you're searching for. Helpful information includes style, approximate size, preferred age or origin, color/material, budget, timeframe, and inspiration photographs.
​
2. WE DISCUSS THE SEARCH: I'll review your request and determine which sourcing option is appropriate.
​
3. RECEIVE YOUR SOURCING PACKET: For paid sourcing projects, I'll personally send you the appropriate sourcing information, terms, and project quote.
​
4. I BEGIN THE SEARCH: Once your quote is accepted and the required sourcing fee or retainer has been paid, I'll begin dedicated sourcing.
​
5. YOU REVIEW: When I locate a potential piece, I'll provide available photographs, measurements, condition information, relevant details, and your purchase price.
​
6. YOU DECIDE: You are never required to purchase a piece simply because I located it.
​
7. APPROVE & PAY: If you'd like the piece, you approve it and pay the required merchandise amount before I purchase it on your behalf.
​
8. INSPECTION & DELIVERY: When appropriate, the piece will first come to R&R Relics for inspection and documentation before final shipping or delivery to you.
Your Sourcing Fee Is Separate From Your Merchandise
Sourcing fees pay for my professional time and search services. They are not deposits toward merchandise.
  
When I locate a piece you would like to purchase, you'll receive the client purchase price and any applicable shipping, freight, delivery, insurance, taxes, customs/import charges, or other approved expenses.
  
No merchandise will be purchased on your behalf until you approve the piece and the required merchandise payment has been received.

### The R&R Relics Promise
Personally Sourced. Carefully Researched. Honestly Represented.
​
At Rescued and Reimagined Relics, LLC, I believe the story behind a piece matters just as much as its beauty.
​
I personally select antique and vintage pieces with an emphasis on distinctive European finds, working with trusted dealers, collectors, markets, and sourcing relationships in the United States, France, England, and beyond.
​
I take the time to research the pieces I offer and to share the information available to me as accurately and transparently as possible.

Antiques carry history, age, imperfections, and sometimes unanswered questions. I believe those details should be represented honestly - not embellished simply to make a sale.
