## TL;DR

1. **The niche sells on a fixed weekly drop plus comment-or-DM claims.** Stated drop slots in bios: Mill Street Vintage "New collections every Wednesday 2pm CT"; Select Vintage NYC "new drops every WED 7 PM EST ... on Instagram live"; Curated by Lucy "Shop Live Sales Every Monday 6pm EST"; Ca Marche Shop "Tues Market Live Sale 2pm mst"; Gaby's Old Treasures "New collections weekly". Palm Beach Regency posts "daily new arrivals". These are stated cadences from bios, not measured ones. `unverified (search snippet)` except where the site was read.
2. **Claiming by comment or DM is the norm, and the repo has already put DM-to-buy on its skip list** (double-sell risk on one-of-one stock, `docs/site-ideas-and-consignment-synthesis.md`). The skeleton below replaces it with the one answer that survives three channels: the listing page. The best structural answers to "is this still available?" are Palm Beach Regency's FAQ ("It's very difficult for us to hold an item as it could sell at any time"), Mill Street's AVAILABLE / ARCHIVE / LAST CHANCE nav, and The Brass Lotus's "Sold Art Gallery". `verified`.
3. **TikTok Shop has no clear lane for this stock.** Its pre-owned policy lists only authenticated luxury bags, footwear, apparel, fine jewelry and watches (apparel, jewelry and watches invite-only); "Antique Collectibles" (pre-1930 furniture, decorative arts) are for "eligible sellers" and "Antique-style" items are prohibited. 1960s-80s brass lamps and pink glass fit none of these. `verified` for the text, `inferred` for the conclusion. Use TikTok as a content channel only.
4. **Instagram/Facebook Shops are blocked by the site, not by the stock.** Used goods are allowed (only used cosmetics are banned), but Shops use website checkout since Sept 2025, need a domain with items "available for direct purchase", and our site says "Checkout coming soon". Services (Hire Hollie) cannot be catalog items. Needs real checkout first.
5. **Pinterest is a search engine, but pre-owned products are not allowed as Product Pins** (only in Shopping Ads). Organic Pinterest for this shop means plain Pins that link to pages, and keyword-named boards. `verified` for the text, `inferred` for the workaround.
6. **Free schedulers all fit a weekly batch.** Buffer free: 3 channels, 10 scheduled posts per channel. Publer free: 3 accounts, 10 scheduled per account. Metricool free: 20 posts a month. Pinterest native: 30 days ahead, 10 queued. TikTok Studio: 10 days ahead. Later and Hootsuite have trials only. `verified`.
7. **A practitioner source says product photos alone do not grow an account** ("Do Not Post Your Product Photography"). The author runs a vintage marketing agency, so treat as opinion. The 4-week skeleton leans on the room, the neon, Hollie's hands and the mirror ball, with the drop as the anchor.
8. **Gaps:** no TikTok-native or Pinterest vintage account was read live; the glassware account (Barfly Vintage BK, 179 followers) rests on one snippet; no before-and-after-room post was found in the sample; Facebook Groups and Marketplace were not covered.
