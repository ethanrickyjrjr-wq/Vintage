Please note that Craigslist has stopped allowing external images as well as most html tags in their "for sale" listings. Your template will still have a full link to your sale and your sale photo gallery.
[Preview your Craigslist template](https://estatesales.org/export/2470523?cl)
  1. Go to [Craigslist](http://www.craigslist.org) and find your city.
  2. Under **For Sale** , click on the **Garage Sale** category.
  3. Click on the **Post** link in the upper right corner of the page.
  4. Choose the Garage Sale category and then select your area. _Craigslist doesn't have an Estate Sale category and this is the closest match._
  5. Add the title of your sale and the location.
  6. Add your email address and keep it anonymous to prevent spam.
  7. Copy & Paste the html code below into the **Posting Description** text box.  
<p><h3>Estate Sale</h3>In-person estate sale</p><p><strong>DATES & TIMES</strong></p><ul><li><strong>Sat, Nov 14, 2026</strong> 9:00AM - 3:00AM</li><li><strong>Sat, Nov 14, 2026</strong> 9:00AM - 3:00AM</li><li><strong>Sat, Nov 14, 2026</strong> 9:00AM - 3:00AM</li></ul><p><strong>ADDRESS</strong><br /><p>2104 SE 25th Ln<br>Cape Coral, FL 33904</p></p><p><strong>ABOUT</strong></p>Come discover a wonderful collection of vintage treasures and unique finds including Waterford crystal, fine china, jadeite, carnival glass, artwork, beanie babies, vases, angels, holiday decor, vintage collectibles and so much more. You'll also find a lot of home decorations, collectables, and one of a kind treasures.<p><strong>VIEW THE COMPLETE LISTING HERE:</strong></p><p>https://estatesales.org/estate-sales/fl/cape-coral/33904/estate-sale-2470523</p>
  8. Click Continue to add photos and publish your sale. You will receive a confirmation email from Craigslist to verify the sale. Activate your sale by clicking the link in your email and your done!
  9. Craigslist allows you to re-post your ad every 48 hours. Re-post any sooner and your ad may be subject to removal.
