## Mixed Vintage Clothing (mens & womens) by the pound
* * *
#### Product Description
This is a wholesale offering of mixed mens and womens vintage clothing, sold by the pound. Included are all styles, sizes, eras, designs, fabrics.. think of it like a grab bag of vintage clothing. This mix may include any of the clothing items we sell. We do spend time picking this for you but our taste may not be the same as yours.  

Modern may be included because some of our mixes are graded for content, not age. Specifically: Disney, Harly Davidson, college sweatshirts and jackets, sports sweatshirts and jackets, Levis jeans and jackets, hawaiian shirts, silk clothing, tie dye tshirts, bajas, Champion clothing. If this is a problem, please do not order this mix.
Examples may include (but are not limited to):
** WOMENS: dresses from the 1940s through the early 2000s- both day and party styles; skirts, blouses, sweaters, hippie tops, sweatshirts, cotton blazers, jackets, faux, fur, trench/overcoats, sequined dresses/tops, silky bombers, shorts, cutoffs, acidwash denim, denim jackets, jeans, and more.
** MENS: any style of button-up shirts, westerns, tshirts, jeans, sweaters, vests, sweatshirts, sports jerseys, trench/overcoats, military, shorts, windbreakers, bajas, denim jackets, pants, blazers, camo pants, light/mid/heavyweight jackets, and more.
Condition: Grade 1, 2, and 3  

_For "by the pound" orders we literally grab armfuls of the clothing until we meet the weight you order. We do not look at each and every piece, so your order may contain ANY variety of the clothing listed above (including any style, any colour, any era, any size, etc....). If you know you'll get upset if you received something damaged or "not cool/ cute", please do not order via this method. * People come in all sizes and this mix may contain any adult size.  
_
Clothing averages 0-3 pieces per pound. Some heavier items (jackets) weigh more than 1 lb, and others weigh less (blouses).  

_You should absolutely expect to steam/clean for small stains. May contain damaged items. We try very hard to not send anything trashed, but we always ship extra just in case we miss something._
