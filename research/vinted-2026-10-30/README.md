# Vinted as a sales channel (2026-10-30)

Question: can Holliewood and Vine crosslist to eBay and Vinted? Raw pages are beside this note, each with its source URL at the top.

## Findings

- **Vinted US terms exclude business use.** A User "uses our Services for your personal benefit only and not for any professional purposes" (`vinted-us-terms.md`).
- **No US business account.** The Vinted Pro page limits sign-up to sole traders and non-profits registered in France (`vinted-pro.md`). Crosslist's blog states Vinted Pro is not available in the US (`crosslist-does-vinted-allow-resellers.md`).
- **Crosslisting tools are barred.** The terms ban "any kind of external software tools" unless authorised by Vinted (`vinted-us-terms.md`). Crosslist says enforcement stepped up in September 2026 and that no consumer crosslister has been authorised.
- **Crosslist closed Vinted to new accounts** on 2026-09-23; existing users keep it (`crosslist-discontinuing-vinted.md`, `crosslist-autodelist.md`).
- **Vendoo does not list Vinted** among its 11 marketplaces (`vendoo-marketplaces.md`).
- **List Perfectly posts to and delists from Vinted but does not detect sales there** (`listperfectly-pricing.md`). Its own Vinted guide is not saved here.
- **FLIPSAIL claims cloud auto-delist on Vinted**, $30/month or free on 2 marketplaces up to 100 listings (`flipsail-vinted.md`). Vendor claim only; not tested.
- **Stock fit:** lamps, mirrors, vases, wall decor, paintings, sculptures, tableware and textiles are allowed (`vinted-home-category.md`). Adult furniture such as sofas, tables and chairs is restricted (`vinted-catalog-rules.md`).

## Discrepancy

Crosslist disagrees with itself on bans. `crosslist-discontinuing-vinted.md` says suspensions were temporary, 24 hours, with no permanent bans seen. `crosslist-does-vinted-allow-resellers.md` says one suspected violation can bring a temporary restriction or potentially a permanent ban. Vinted's own terms were checked for the rules, not for how often it enforces them, so the real ban rate is unknown.

## Vendor numbers for the eBay and Etsy route

Vendoo Starter is $14.99/month with a 14-day trial; sale detection and auto-delist cover eBay and Etsy, not Shopify (`vendoo-pricing.md`). Crosslist Gold is $39.99/month and covers eBay, Etsy and Shopify in the cloud (`crosslist-autodelist.md`).
