### **Vendoo Pricing FAQs**
**How does the Vendoo free trial work?**  

Vendoo’s free trial lets you use all premium features for 14 days free of charge. You can start with any plan. Cancel before your trial ends to avoid being charged.  

**Do I need a credit card to start?**  

Yes, a valid payment method is required to activate your trial, but you won’t be charged if you cancel before the trial ends.  

**What happens when my trial ends?**  

Your subscription will automatically begin on day 14. If you choose not to continue, simply cancel before your trial ends to avoid payment.  

**Are there any hidden fees on my account?**  

No, there are no hidden fees. The subscription cost is precisely what you’ll be charged each month. Additionally, Vendoo applies state sales tax where required, which varies by state. If applicable, the appropriate sales tax will be added to your subscription**.**  

**Do you offer any discounted plans?**  

Yes! Vendoo's yearly plans offer the biggest savings. With a yearly plan you can save 2 months free.  

**What payment methods does Vendoo accept?**  

Vendoo currently accepts all major credit cards.  

**Can I upgrade/downgrade my plan at any time?**  

If you are in a monthly plan, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan between each billing cycle. Annual plans cannot be modified until the end of the cycle.  

**What is Vendoo’s refund policy?**
At Vendoo, we're committed to giving you the best possible service. Our refund policy is designed to be fair and transparent, and every paid plan starts with a free trial so you can decide whether Vendoo is right for you before paying anything.  
  
**Free Trial**  
Every paid plan includes a 14-day free trial with full access to that plan's features, including unlimited listings. You won't be charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel at any time before then at no cost. We'll email you before your trial ends as a reminder, so you have time to cancel if you choose not to continue.  
  
**Refund Eligibility**  
‍Once your trial ends and you're billed, the charge is non-refundable. This applies to both monthly and annual plans, so please check which billing cycle you select at checkout. We don't grant refunds for personal reasons, a change of mind, or unused time left in your billing period.  
  
We may issue a refund or account credit, case by case, if Vendoo fails to provide the service you paid for. This includes persistent software errors that prevent you from listing your items as intended, or being charged after you canceled before your trial ended.  
  
If you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of your current trial or billing period. After that, you'll lose access to your plan's features, and you can reactivate at any time.  
  
Questions? Concerns? Please don't hesitate to contact our Customer Support team. We're here to help!
