# Catalog Rules
We seek to ensure that Vinted is a friendly and safe place to trade second-hand items. Therefore, members are required to comply with the below Catalogue rules when they upload their items.
Please note that these rules are a part of our[ Terms & Conditions](https://www.vinted.com/terms_and_conditions). Upon creating an account on Vinted, you are agreeing to these rules and our Terms & Conditions.
**ALLOWED ITEMS**
**You can sell on Vinted:**
  * Women’s, men’s and children’s clothing, footwear and accessories.
  * Children’s toys, furniture and childcare equipment.
  * Home and kitchenware, such as small kitchen appliances, cookware and bakeware, kitchen tools, tableware, household care, textiles, home accessories, office supplies, celebration and holiday items, tools, DIY, outdoor and garden equipment, and pet care items.
  * Electronics such as cameras, computers, laptops, 3D printers and scanners, tablets and e-readers, mobile phone and communication devices, audio, headphones and Hi-Fi, television and home cinema equipment, video games and consoles, wearables, non-professional beauty and personal care electronics, smart home devices, and electronics accessories such as adapters, cables and chargers.
  * Entertainment such as books, magazines, music and video media.
  * Hobbies and collectables, such as board games, puzzles, trading cards, tabletop and miniature gaming, stamps, memorabilia, postcards, art and craft supplies, coins and banknotes, musical instruments and gear, collectables storage items, and gaming accessories. 
  * Sports equipment related to activities such as cycling, fitness, running and yoga, outdoor, water, team, or racquet sports, golf, equestrian, skateboarding, boxing and mixed martial arts, casual sports and games, such as boules and darts, and winter sports. 
  * New cosmetics, beauty and care products, beauty devices and gadgets.


A small number of unique handmade items is allowed, if this activity is occasional and they comply with the catalog rules. If an item that you trade came with instructions of use, manuals or safety warnings, ensure that you include these when you send the item to the buyer. If your electronics came with accessories and you are not selling the accessories with the item ensure to make it clear in the description and have in mind this may influence the item price. Make sure that the electronics you list are safe to be used.
We reserve the right to redetermine what items are and aren’t allowed to be listed on Vinted. If we discover that certain items violate our terms or pose any risk to our members, we may remove those items even if they’re not listed below.

**GENERAL RULES**
**1. General**
  * You cannot list items that exceed [these](https://www.vinted.com/help/65) carriers’ shipping limits
  * You need to upload each of your items separately, under their corresponding category, except if it’s a bundle (and brand, if applicable).
  * You should only have one listing per item.
  * All items should have an [appropriate item condition assigned to them.](https://www.vinted.com/help/50) In the item description, you should also describe any defect, alteration, missing part of the item. 
  * You can choose up to 3 materials when uploading an item. A detailed description should also include information about additional materials (besides the main 3 already selected when uploading).
  * Bundle listings (sets) must state that it is a bundle or set in the listings’ title and/or description. Each bundle listing must have the total price for all the items it includes. The items included in the bundle cannot be listed separately. For electronics, only up to 2 devices with a battery are allowed in a bundle. As for the brand field, choose one of your item's brands. 
  * Listings and profile descriptions must not contain references or links to external websites or online platforms. Sharing links or references that lead to illegal content, adult content, hateful speech or any type of behavior that violates our Catalog Rules is strictly forbidden and can lead to account ban. As an exception, although we do not recommend it for privacy reasons, you may share a link to your social media profile, provided that it is not linked to commercial activities or content that goes against our Catalog rules.
  * It’s forbidden to upload an item that is not available or already sold. Items must not be uploaded for the purpose of searching for the item. It is also not allowed to list something that is not an actual item (e.g. for trolling purposes). Find out [why your listing was hidden or deleted](https://www.vinted.com/help/62).
  * You cannot offer any kind of service. This includes but is not limited to:
    * Offering items to rent 
    * Offering items made to order
    * Offering item customization
    * Offering financial, educational, or beauty related services 
    * Offering subscription based or digital services
  * The maximum item value allowed on our platform is $1000 (USD).
  * All listings and other posts related to auctioning or bidding activities are not allowed. When your item’s price is unusually low/high and doesn’t seem to match the real item’s value, the listing may be considered as an auctioned one. Find out [how to set a reasonable price for your item](https://www.vinted.com/help/376).
  * ​​​​For any allowed socket plug electrical product and any battery powered products that transmit heat they must not present material damage such as frayed or loose cables.
  * All personal data should be cleared and personal accounts should be disconnected from electronic devices. All devices should be reset to their original factory settings, should be fully turned off, and if applicable deregistered (e.g. drones) prior to being shipped. Remember to remove any personal belongings from the item that you list, like your SIM card or similar items.


**2. Requirements for the item’s photos**
The pictures used in your listing shall meet the following requirements:
  * They have to represent the item as it is - no image editing is allowed. We also recommend showing any defects, scratches, wear, etc. 
  * They must be sharp and clear. Check out our [photo tips](https://www.vinted.com/help/48).
  * They have to be taken by you with the purpose of using the photos on Vinted.
  * They cannot be replaced with stock photos, photos from ads and watermarked images.
  * The first photo should clearly represent the whole item. You’re free to choose how you want to showcase your item in your additional photos. Note: You can upload up to 20 photos per listing.
  * They must showcase your item’s authenticity. Check [out our tips on this](https://www.vinted.com/help/601).
  * For products which are subject to age ratings (like adult video games) they need to display the necessary age rating label.


**3. IP policy**
  * It is strictly forbidden to upload counterfeit items to our platform. Counterfeit items usually copy or imitate the trademark (e.g. name of the brand, name of its product and/or logo) of a company without its permission. They may also copy the specific notable features of that company’s product (e.g. the shape of a perfume bottle, the design and specific pattern of a bag). Note: Sale of counterfeit items could expose sellers to civil and criminal proceedings.
  * Members can only upload items which they are sure are authentic. They cannot add a disclaimer to their listing saying they are not sure whether or not the item is authentic.
  * Members must also upload [pictures showcasing](https://www.vinted.com/help/601-quelles-photos-dois-je-mettre-en-ligne-pour-mes-articles-de-marque) as clearly as possible their item’s authenticity. 
  * Using other brands which are not related to your item in the brand category, title/description or as a hashtag in your listing is not allowed.
  * The use of words such as "inspired by" followed by a brand is also generally prohibited on our platform.
  * It is strictly prohibited to upload and sell items which are reproduced without IP rights holder’s consent (e.g. copied books, any kind of sport, diet plans created for individual purposes, copied video games into blank CD or in any other kind of medium).


**4.**Commercial selling policy****
Standard Vinted accounts are for private, non-commercial use only. As a standard seller, you can't use Vinted to operate a reselling business (even if you own a legally registered business), or act as a professional sole trader.
**Prohibited commercial activity**
Additionally, we don’t allow the following commercial activities on our platform:
  * **Offering services** – including rental, repair, customization, or made-to-order services.
  * **Intentionally misleading buyers** – misleading buyers about an item's condition, origin, brand, or authenticity, including:
    * Using photos that you didn’t take, aren’t of the actual item, or don't accurately represent the item you’re selling.
    * Falsely listing new items from a commercial stock inventory as pre-owned or personal property to circumvent our rules.
  * **Selling new, handmade items** – while standard sellers can list a small number of items that were handmade for personal use, we don’t allow items that have been designed or handcrafted specifically to sell.


**Why do we have this policy?**
Vinted is primarily intended for peer-to-peer, second-hand selling. Although we allow registered [Vinted Pro members](https://www.vinted.com/help/918) to operate as professional sellers in some markets, we restrict commercial activity that doesn’t comply with regulation and that isn’t aligned with our overall mission. 
**How we enforce this policy**
If we receive a report or detect activity that suggests a seller isn’t complying with our Catalog Rules, we reserve the right to issue account restrictions, in line with Section 7 of our [Terms and Conditions](https://www.vinted.com/terms_and_conditions). Repeated or serious violations may result in a permanent account ban. 
You can find more detailed guidance on this policy in our [Help Center](https://www.vinted.com/help/1120). 
**5. Penalties**
When members disregard our Catalogue rules, we may take the following measures:
  * If a reported listing needs some amendments, we make it invisible in the member’s wardrobe and we send a message to the member informing them about the actions they need to take within 24 hours. If the member doesn’t take these actions, their item remains hidden.
  * If a reported listing includes a prohibited item, we remove the listing from the member’s wardrobe and we send them a message informing about the reason for such removal.
  * If a member uploads repeatedly prohibited items, items of a seemingly commercial nature and all other items which breach our Terms & Conditions and/or Catalogue rules, we can suspend their account (e.g. for a period of 7 days) or even block it definitively depending on the severity of the violation. We will send a message to the member informing them about this. For more information, please consult our [Terms & Conditions](https://www.vinted.com/terms_and_conditions).


We reserve the right to change the above-stated rules at our sole discretion without having to notify you thereof in advance. If you have any questions regarding the above-stated rules - [contact our team](https://www.vinted.com/help) and we’ll be happy to assist you.
