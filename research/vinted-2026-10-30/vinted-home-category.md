Only allowed if new and unused
Some items can only be sold if they’re new, unused, and in their original packaging. This is typically for safety or hygiene reasons.
For example:
  * **Drink dispenser accessories** : water filter inserts and cartridges
  * **Cooking accessories** : barbecue grills & grates, drip trays, smoker chips
  * **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)** and DIY equipment: paint brushes & rollers, sandpaper, saw blades, drill bits, safety goggles & gloves
  * **Cartridge razors and electric razors**


Items not allowed
Some items can’t be sold on Vinted because of our [Catalog Rules](https://www.vinted.com/catalog-rules). We have to remove or delete listings if they’re not allowed or if they don’t match a catalog category.
Here are some examples of items that are not allowed:
  * **Devices with batteries over 100 watt-hours (Wh) — certain portable home and garden appliances or power tools might exceed this limit**
  * **All sharp knives with a pointed tip, regardless of size or intended use** , including bread, steak, and butcher knives
  * **Sharp outdoor tools** : chainsaws, axes, saws with a handle and elongated blade that resemble a knife
  * **Straight razors and razor blades** (single or double-edged)
  * **Certain bedding items** : mattresses for adults, inflatable mattresses, mattress pads and toppers, duvets
  * **Certain flooring items** : large carpets, linoleum
  * **Certain large outdoor equipment** : wheelbarrows, outdoor playground sets, play towers, outdoor kennels and houses for pets, frame pools, beach umbrellas
  * **Plants** : live plants, seeds, aquatic plants
  * **Single-use items** : disposable tableware, cutlery and cups (paper or plastic), confetti, balloons, inflatable decorations
  * **HVAC systems**(Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning): fixed & non-portable units, wall-mounted air conditioners
  * **Fetish-related items** or items promoting sex services
  * **Certain pet care items** : pet food, vitamins & supplements, litter, wood shavings, electronic grooming tools, electronic aquarium and terrarium equipment, pet cameras and monitors, veterinary medicinal products, cosmetics for pets
  * **Other items that don’t match a****catalog****category** : freestanding floor clocks, trash cans, buckets, advent calendars, street signs and public signage, sparkling candles


Making a listing **that offers a service isn’t allowed** either, since it’s not selling a physical item. Examples of offering services include:
  * Renting out items to other members
  * Home decoration and design services
  * Gardening and landscaping services
