Vinted charges US sellers nothing. The buyer pays a Buyer Protection fee of 5% + $0.70 plus shipping, so a $40 sale pays you the full $40. Vinted's US push began in January 2026, which makes it the newest serious option for US clothing resellers, and the simplest way to try it is to copy listings you already have on another app.

## What you keep on Vinted vs your other stores  
| On a $40 sale  | You keep  |  
| --- | --- |  
| Vinted  | $40.00 (buyer pays the protection fee)  |  
| Depop  | $38.23  |  
| Mercari  | $36.00  |  
| eBay (most clothing)  | $34.16  |  
| Poshmark  | $32.00  |  
Item price only, standard US fees, September 2026. Depop without Boost.
A Vinted buyer paying $40 for your item actually pays $42.70 plus shipping, so a Vinted price a few dollars under your Poshmark price still nets you more. Check any price in the [Vinted fee calculator](https://www.flipsail.io/fee-calculator/vinted).
## What sells on Vinted in the US
  * Everyday and mall brands: Zara, H&M, Gap, Old Navy, Target labels, Nike and Adidas basics. On Poshmark these compete with thousands of listings; on Vinted the zero fee makes low prices worth it.
  * Kids' clothing and bundles. Parents buy in lots, and Vinted's bundle discounts suit them.
  * Lower-priced items, since a $12 top loses $2.95 on Poshmark and keeps every dollar on Vinted.
  * High-end designer and pieces over a few hundred dollars suit it less. Poshmark's and eBay's authentication programs and brand-first buyers do better there.


## Moving a listing to Vinted, step by step
Start with the photos. Vinted allows up to 20, more than Poshmark (16) or Depop (usually 4), so bring them all, cover first. Vinted buyers scroll a grid, and a clean, bright cover on a plain background matters most.
Write the title the way a buyer searches: brand, item, size, color ("Levi's 501 jeans W30 L32 light wash"). Drop Poshmark shorthand like NWT, EUC and "bundle to save", and Depop hashtags. Put measurements and flaws in the description.
Vinted search filters heavily on category, size, brand and condition, so fill each field instead of relying on the text. Map "New With Tags" to new with tags, and most "excellent used condition" items to very good.
Price at about your Poshmark payout. An item listed at $40 on Poshmark (which pays you $32) can go on Vinted at $32 to $35 and net the same or more. Vinted buyers send offers and ask for bundle deals, so leave a little room.
For shipping, Vinted gives you a prepaid USPS label and you ship within 5 business days of the sale. The buyer pays shipping, as on Poshmark.

## Frequently Asked Questions
Can I sell the same item on Vinted and Poshmark?
Yes. Neither prohibits listing an item elsewhere. Take the other copy down as soon as one sells, because cancelling a sold order counts against you on both.
Does Vinted charge US sellers fees?
No. US sellers keep the full item price. The buyer pays a Buyer Protection fee of 5% + $0.70 and the shipping. As of September 2026.
Can I import my Poshmark closet to Vinted?
Vinted has no importer. You can copy listings by hand, or use a crosslisting tool that supports Vinted to copy them for you.
How many photos can I add on Vinted?
Up to 20 per listing, more than Poshmark (16) or Depop (usually 4).
