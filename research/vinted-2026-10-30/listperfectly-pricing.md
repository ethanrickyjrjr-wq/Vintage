## Inventory Management
## Delist / Relist
Delist and relist your active listings with the click of a button to stay ahead of the game. 
## Mark Sold & End Listings
Use the Mark Sold feature to update a listing’s status to "Sold" in your catalog.  
  
You can also end the remaining active listings for that item on other platforms at the same time to avoid overselling. 
## Link Existing Listings
You'll see a "Link" option next to any marketplace item with an Item ID that isn't yet in your List Perfectly catalog.  
  
This allows you to quickly connect those listings to your catalog for better tracking and management — making it easy to get started without duplicating work. 
## Update Listings
Use the “Update” button to quickly edit active listings across all linked marketplaces at once.  
  
Choose to update all listing details or apply specific changes — including price, quantity, title, images, and description. 
## Custom Marketplaces
Do you sell on a non-supported platform or locally? Utilize Custom Marketplaces to manage inventory and gain insights from those platforms. 
## Google Lens Pricing Tool
From a single click utilize the Google Lens pricing tool to take your image and run it through Google Lens to gain insights. 
## eBay Pricing Lookup
From a single click from your listing lookup comps on eBay. 
## Auto Sales Detection
Automatically detect sales that occur on connected marketplaces. *Shopify is not currently supported. 
## Auto Delist
Automatically delist items from other marketplaces when you mark them as sold in List Perfectly.  
  
Helps prevent overselling by keeping your listings in sync. 
## SKU Generator
Automatically generate unique SKUs for your listings based on your preferences.  
  
Helps you organize, track, and reference inventory more efficiently across your catalog. 
## Inventory Labels
Generate and print inventory labels with QR codes for your listed items.  
  
Scan QR codes to quickly pull up listings in List Perfectly, making inventory tracking, shipping, and relisting faster and more accurate. 
## Issue Finder
Automatically scan your listings for missing or incorrect information that could affect performance on marketplaces.  
  
Quickly fix issues like missing images, prices, titles, or other required fields — so your listings are ready to post or crosspost successfully. 
## Pick List
Generate a pick list of items that need to be pulled for orders marked as sold in List Perfectly.  
  
View product details, custom SKUs, locations, and quantities to speed up shipping and reduce errors. 
## Stale Listings
When your items go stale, buyers are less likely to see them. But with Stale Listings in List Perfectly, you can:  
🚨 Get alerts when a listing is going stale.   
🔄 Update, relist, or remove it before it loses visibility.   
📈 Keep your catalog active and your sales moving!
