Frequently asked questions
Is Vinted Pro free?
Yes, Vinted Pro is entirely free. With Vinted Pro you can list as many second-hand items as you want at no cost.
Can I have both a standard and a Pro account?
Yes. You can keep your current account, and create a separate Pro account alongside it.  
  
Simply log out, then re-enter this page. When signing up, make sure to use a different email address and phone number. Here’s more on how [registering for Vinted Pro](https://www.vinted.fr/help/906) works.
What policies must I follow as a Pro seller?
You will have to agree to [Vinted Pro terms of use](https://www.vinted.fr/pro-terms-of-use), and [Vinted Pro terms of sale](https://www.vinted.fr/pro-terms-of-sale). Note that they will require you to allow the buyer to return an item within 14 days of receiving it.  
  
In addition, you may provide your own additional policies, which can complement our standard terms of sale with additional points on item returns, refunds, and more.
What details will be visible on my profile? 
Your email address will be visible on your profile page. If you include your phone number and/or business address in your own additional policies, these details will be visible on your profile too.  
  
Also, your business address, SIRET number, and contact details will be visible to buyers after they purchase one of your items.
What can I sell using Vinted Pro? 
You can sell second-hand items in these categories:  

  * Women’s, men’s and children’s clothing, footwear and accessories
  * Children’s toys, furniture and childcare equipment
  * Tech accessories (e.g. headphones, smart watches, phone cases)
  * Homeware (e.g. textiles, tableware, home accessories)
  * Books

You can sell as many second-hand items as you want. For more details, see our [Catalogue Rules](https://www.vinted.fr/catalog-rules).
