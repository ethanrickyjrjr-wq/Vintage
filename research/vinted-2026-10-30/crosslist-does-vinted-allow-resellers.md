# Does Vinted Allow Resellers? Understanding the New Rules
[![Rupali Jeganathan Headshot](https://framerusercontent.com/images/fVLWRk1w7zjYZB9g0CfthabVg.webp?width=60&height=60) Rupali Jeganathan](https://crosslist.com/authors/rupali-jeganathan)

![Does Vinted Allow Resellers - Banner](https://framerusercontent.com/images/VAYVY7GDfPhX8ELl6Hk58Bm8reA.webp?width=1600&height=460)
As all resellers who use Vinted know, the hottest question right now is this: **does Vinted allow resellers?** Well… yes, and no. 
**Yes, if you have a Vinted Pro account. No, if you’re reselling for profit on a Standard account or using cross listing apps.**
**And if you’re in the US, the answer is simply no, because Vinted Pro isn’t available there.**
Here’s the 101 for those of you who don’t know what’s happening. 
Vinted has recently started cracking down on a lot of commercial resellers, especially the ones who use “automated activity” such as cross listing apps or bots to delist and relist.
In this guide, we explain all of Vinted’s current rules in plain terms.
We will tell you why “safe Vinted automations” isn’t a thing, and give you the lowdown behind Crosslist’s decision to stop supporting Vinted for new users.
## Key takeaways:
  * Vinted has gotten more aggressive about cracking down on commercial resellers and sellers who use automation tools, including cross listing apps. This is why Crosslist has decided to no longer support Vinted for new users.
  * Standard, private accounts can’t be used to resell commercially; you need a Vinted Pro account for that. However, Pro is available only in certain European countries for now.
  * Vinted’s terms ban the use of any unauthorized third-party apps or bots by resellers. This includes all cross listing apps. Since September 2026, they have been actively enforcing this policy.
  * The platform has not publicly authorized any consumer cross listing apps. So, when you use one to list to Vinted or delist and relist your listings, you’re at risk of getting banned.


## Does Vinted allow resellers?
If you’re a US seller, the short answer is no. Vinted Pro isn’t available in the US, so there’s no way to resell for profit on Vinted there.
Everywhere else, whether Vinted allows resellers or not depends on the type of Vinted account you have.
Vinted has two types of accounts: a Standard (or private) account, and a Pro account.
Standard accounts are for personal use, so, on these accounts, only private individuals are allowed to resell their own products. 
For example, reselling your clothes that no longer fit. 
You cannot operate as a reselling business (i.e. take part in commercial reselling) unless you have a Vinted Pro account. 
This means **absolutely no commercial reselling on Standard accounts**. In fact, reselling for profit on private accounts is exactly what Vinted’s rules are designed to stop.
### Vinted Pro
![Vinted Pro](https://framerusercontent.com/images/cxisO5ekJmzYexB9NKSIuym05k.webp?width=1600&height=761)
If you want to resell for profit on Vinted, your only option is to sign up for a Vinted Pro account. 
[To become a Pro seller](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/906), you need to be a sole trader, a charity, or a registered company. 
Most importantly, though, your company must be incorporated in one of the countries where Vinted Pro is supported. 
This includes, but isn’t limited to:
  * The UK
  * The Netherlands
  * Italy
  * France
  * Spain
  * Portugal
  * Luxembourg
  * Belgium


Note that **Vinted Pro is not yet available in the US, so US-based resellers cannot use Vinted to sell for profit at this time**. 
Vinted adds new countries over time, so check their help page for the latest list.
If you’re eligible to be a Vinted Pro seller, you can:
  * Sign up for a new Pro account
  * Convert your existing private account into a Pro account (but only after all of your current orders are closed and your Vinted balance has been withdrawn to your bank account), or
  * Have separate Standard and Pro accounts (as long as you use different email addresses and phone numbers for each)


When you sign up to Vinted Pro, you need to give the platform details about your business, such as VAT number, business address, tax identification number, and so on. 
You're also required to allow buyers up to 14 days to return the products.
You’ll get a shiny new ‘Pro’ badge for your profile. Buyers will also be able to see the following details about you and your business:
  * Seller policy
  * Company name
  * Company number
  * Contact details
  * Business address
  * Trade registrar


But, if you’re only a sole trader, you have the option of deciding which of the details your buyers can see. 
**📜 Note:** Based on your selling activity and eligibility, Vinted itself may sometimes ask you to switch to Pro. If you don't make the switch, the platform may block your access. 
## What is “commercial selling” according to Vinted?
[According to Vinted’s definition](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1120), commercial selling refers to buying items and reselling them to make a profit. 
This includes:
  * Selling as a business (or on behalf of one in exchange for payment)
  * Selling to make regular income or profit (typically identified by large volume transactions and/or the amount of revenue you earn)
  * Selling large quantities of new items (both with and without their tags attached)
  * Selling large quantities of similar items in different colors or sizes
  * Selling large quantities of identical items from the same brands or categories
  * Dropshipping multiple quantities of items at a low resale value


**📜 Note:** While Vinted does let you list and resell as many of your own products as you want on private accounts, a high listing volume may still draw attention.
## Vinted’s rules on bots, automations, and third-party tools
![Vinted T&C](https://framerusercontent.com/images/rUoLeGgM44FQgTHBCcpxftnCyM.webp?width=1600&height=237)
[**Vinted’s terms and conditions**](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms_and_conditions)**clearly state that you cannot use any type of third-party software or tools with your Vinted profile unless Vinted explicitly authorizes them**. 
This means that you cannot use any external tool to publish your listings for you, promote your items for you, add items to your favorites, and so on. 
So, the type of prohibited tools include:
  * Cross listing tools
  * Automation bots
  * Scraping programs
  * Relisting bots
  * Crawling programs
  * Spiders


Delisting and relisting the same items multiple times without making any meaningful changes to your listings are also a no-go. 
You also cannot make duplicate listings, or relist several items in bulk at the same time. All of these put your account at risk.
Since September 2026, Vinted has been enforcing stricter rules against automated activity. Several sellers have already received 24-hour account restrictions for suspected automated listing.
Vinted has become much more aggressive in detecting and restricting accounts suspected of using bots or automation, including through AI-powered detection systems.
Unlike before, even a single suspected violation can now result in a temporary restriction or potentially a permanent ban.
## Are cross listing apps allowed on Vinted?
**No, cross listing apps of any kind are not allowed on Vinted**. 
Vinted’s terms make it very clear that it doesn’t allow unauthorized third-party apps or tools of any kind. And Vinted hasn’t authorized any cross listing app.
The only official integration allowed by Vinted is the [Vinted Pro Integrations](https://pro-docs.svc.vinted.com/#overview), which is the platform’s own API for a select few Pro sellers. 
The API helps high-volume Pro resellers automate inventory, orders, and shipping.
Unfortunately, regular Pro sellers do not have access to this API, and cross listing apps cannot request the authorization on their behalf.
### The assumptions that most resellers rely on
Up until now, you’ve probably used your private Vinted account to resell for profit without running into any issues. 
This is largely because of a few assumptions that all of us in the reselling community relied upon, namely:
  * “If it’s the sellers hitting publish on the listing, it won’t be detected as automated activity.” Vinted has placed a blanket ban on all unauthorized third-party tools with no exceptions for a final manual click. So, even if you use a form-filling cross listing tool where you hit the final publish button, it still counts as automation.
  * “Spacing out actions to look human will keep us safe.” Nope. Making automation harder to detect doesn't mean it's allowed or okay. Moreover, Vinted uses AI to detect suspicious activity, so there's no guarantee that your account won't be flagged.
  * “There are lots of users doing this, and very few of them get banned, so it will be okay.” Vinted has tightened its enforcement since September 2026.


**Bottom line:** What worked before likely won't fly now. And no matter what any third-party tool claims, Vinted’s rules still apply to them. 
So, no matter which cross listing app or automation bot you use, you're putting your account at risk. 
## What happens if Vinted flags your account?
If Vinted notices a lot of unusual bot-like activity on your account, it may ask you to solve CAPTCHA checks to prove you’re human. 
Going against Vinted’s rules about creating duplicate listings can get your [listings hidden or even removed](https://www.vinted.com/help/62-why-your-listing-was-hidden-or-deleted?access_channel=hc_topics). 
If Vinted made a mistake, you can appeal the decision as long as you’ve not broken any of the platform’s rules.
**Additionally, when Vinted flags you for using external, unauthorized tools, you may receive a temporary 24-hour ban.**
During this enforcement period, you won’t be able to edit your listings or publish new listings.
Selling items for commercial use from a Standard Vinted account may get you [banned for 7 to 30 days](https://www.vinted.com/help/15/92-my-account-was-banned-i-want-to-appeal-the-decision). 
In such cases, you’ll automatically get unbanned after a maximum period of 30 days.
**Repeated breaches when selling items for commercial use may cause you to get permanently banned from Vinted. We haven't seen permanent bans as a result of the temporary 24-hour bans though, even if you accumulate a lot of these.**
Before kicking you out of the platform, though, Vinted may ask you to switch to a Pro account (if available in your country) if you’re selling a lot of new items with tags, or undertaking a large volume of orders. 
Not making the switch when you’re selling commercially can result in a ban.
**📜 Note:** If you do get banned by Vinted, do not try to circumvent this by creating a new account. It only makes things worse as they hand out hardware bans and will simply ban your new account as well.

**The main reason:** Vinted’s terms no longer support the use of unauthorized third-party tools, and that covers every cross listing app, Crosslist included. 
If you signed up for Crosslist before September 2026, you can still use Vinted through Crosslist. 
We decided to keep Vinted available to existing users because we didn't want to disrupt the workflows of sellers who have come to rely on it
However, given Vinted's stricter enforcement, it's important to understand that continued use carries a risk of account restrictions or bans, and that users are responsible for accepting this risk.
Just keep in mind that Vinted’s rules on third-party tools still apply, so Vinted may flag, restrict, or ban accounts it detects using them. 
If you choose to keep cross listing to Vinted, make sure you’re selling from a Vinted Pro account, avoid bulk actions, and weigh the risk to your account before you decide.
We've seen that having a Pro account significantly reduces the risk of getting the 24-hour suspensions for automated activity. That being said, if you interpret the terms strictly, even Pro sellers cannot use external software.
Some cross listing tools still boast a Vinted integration. But, the truth is that Vinted’s terms apply to them as well. It’s not just Crosslist that’s being affected. 
So, if you’re thinking of using any of those apps for Vinted, be very aware of the risks attached to your account.
## How to keep selling safely on Vinted
Vinted’s tighter enforcements might mean that you may have to take a few extra steps, but it doesn’t mean that you have to stop selling on Vinted. 
Here’s what you can do to follow all the rules and keep selling safely:
  * **If you’re reselling as a business** , sign up for a Vinted Pro account. 
  * **Don’t use any cross listing apps.** Instead, create listings manually. 
  * **Take pictures of the products yourself** instead of using stock images or AI-generated images. And write the listing descriptions yourself. 
  * **Avoid duplicate listings.** If you're relisting, don't do it in bulk, and always make some meaningful changes to the listing details before delisting and relisting.

## FAQs
### Can you get banned on Vinted for reselling?
Vinted only allows commercial reselling on Pro accounts. On Standard or private accounts, you can only resell your own items that you no longer use. 
If you use a private account to resell for profit, you run the risk of a restriction or a ban. 
### Is Vinted Pro available in the US?
No, Vinted Pro is not available in the US. Currently, a Pro account is only available in certain European countries.
### Does Vinted allow bots or auto-relisting tools?
No, Vinted does not allow users to use any third-party bots or auto-relisting tools. This applies to both Standard and Pro accounts.
Use one, and you may get banned. We have seen fewer bans for Pro accounts.
### Can I relist items on Vinted?
Yes, you can relist items on Vinted, but only if you do so manually, and only after you have delisted the existing listing. This prevents duplicates, which aren't allowed on Vinted. 
To prevent your account from getting flagged for suspicious activity, don't relist in bulk, and always make some sort of change to the listing details before relisting.
### Is there a safe Vinted cross listing app?
No, Vinted’s ban on automation tools applies to all cross listing apps equally. So, no matter how safe a cross listing tool claims to be, it's not the truth.

If you use a private account to resell for profit, you run the risk of a restriction or a ban. 
### Is Vinted Pro available in the US?
No, Vinted Pro is not available in the US. Currently, a Pro account is only available in certain European countries.
### Does Vinted allow bots or auto-relisting tools?
No, Vinted does not allow users to use any third-party bots or auto-relisting tools. This applies to both Standard and Pro accounts.
Use one, and you may get banned. We have seen fewer bans for Pro accounts.
### Can I relist items on Vinted?
Yes, you can relist items on Vinted, but only if you do so manually, and only after you have delisted the existing listing. This prevents duplicates, which aren't allowed on Vinted. 
To prevent your account from getting flagged for suspicious activity, don't relist in bulk, and always make some sort of change to the listing details before relisting.
### Is there a safe Vinted cross listing app?
No, Vinted’s ban on automation tools applies to all cross listing apps equally. So, no matter how safe a cross listing tool claims to be, it's not the truth.
