# Why We're Discontinuing Vinted for New Users (Existing Users Stay)
[![Gilles Couvreur Headshot](https://framerusercontent.com/images/SeH5cASjCqDxi2rGiGRABAeym54.webp?width=60&height=60) Gilles Couvreur](https://crosslist.com/authors/gilles-couvreur)

I want to personally explain a difficult decision we've made: Crosslist has stopped offering Vinted to new customers. 
I know Vinted matters to many of your businesses, so here's why we made this call, and what it means for you.
## Key takeaways
  * **New users:** Vinted is no longer available on Crosslist. New accounts can't connect to Vinted or import from it.
  * **Existing Vinted users:** nothing changes. Your access and settings stay as they are, and you don't need to do anything.
  * **Why:** Vinted's rules work against resellers, and it has stepped up detection of automated activity across all tools.
  * **What's next:** cloud-based autodelist and new marketplaces, starting with TikTok Shop.
  * **Where to sell instead:** you can still cross list to 11+ other marketplaces with Crosslist.


## Why we're stepping back from Vinted
The truth is that Vinted doesn't want resellers on its platform. 
Its rules prohibit buying items with the intention of reselling them, which Vinted treats as "[commercial selling](https://crosslist.com/blog/does-vinted-allow-resellers)". Accounts flagged for it can be permanently suspended.
Many resellers use tools like Crosslist to manage their listings, so Vinted has also increased its detection of automated activity. 
As a result, some users of Crosslist and similar tools have received temporary 24-hour suspensions. 
These suspensions affect all automated software, without exception. We haven't seen any permanent bans for automated activity.
Even so, we've decided to close Vinted to new customers while continuing to maintain it for existing users for as long as we reasonably can.
## What this means for you
### If you're new to Crosslist
Vinted isn't available when you sign up. New accounts can't connect a Vinted account, which also means you can't import listings from Vinted. 
Every other marketplace on Crosslist works as usual (see below).
### If you already use Vinted through Crosslist
For most of you, nothing changes. Around 90% of our Vinted users post completely normally, with no warnings or suspensions, and you can carry on exactly as you do today.
If you're in the smaller group seeing 24-hour "automated activity" suspensions, we've added a new option: **posting as drafts**. Crosslist prepares each listing as a draft, and you review and publish it manually inside Vinted. 
This lowers the amount of automation involved. So far we haven't seen suspensions from it, though we can't guarantee that will stay the case.
To turn it on:
  1. Make sure you're on the latest version of the Crosslist extension. If you're not sure, [reinstall it here](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/crosslist/knfhdmkccnbhbgpahakkcmoddgikegjl).
  2. Switch on **posting as drafts** in your [settings](https://app.crosslist.com/account/settings/marketplaces). It's available on desktop and in the mobile app.


If your account is affected, posting as drafts is unfortunately the new reality. 
No tool can currently get around these suspensions, and many others dropped Vinted long before we did.
You can also list on Vinted manually and then import those listings into Crosslist to cross list them to other marketplaces. 
Importing doesn't trigger automated activity suspensions.
## What's next for Crosslist
Stepping back from Vinted doesn't mean we're slowing down. It frees our team to make Crosslist better on the marketplaces you sell on most:
  * **Cloud-based autodelist** for Facebook Marketplace, Depop, Poshmark and Mercari. Facebook Marketplace and Depop are already in beta. Because these integrations run in the cloud, autodelist keeps working even when your computer is offline.
  * **New marketplaces** , starting with TikTok Shop in the US and UK, with more to follow.


We gave the Vinted integration our best shot, and we'll keep it running for existing users for as long as it remains viable. 
But we want to put our energy where we can give you a reliable, long-term experience.
Thank you for your understanding and for continuing to trust Crosslist. If you have any questions, reach out to our support team through the chat bubble in the Crosslist app.

Vinted is one marketplace. Crosslist connects you to 11 more, so you can keep reaching buyers wherever they shop.
