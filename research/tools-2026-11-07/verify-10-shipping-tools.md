# Verify lane 10: shipping label tools, freight, insurance and gear

Date: 2026-11-07. Author: research subagent (adversarial price and claim checker).
Question: do the prices, free tiers, platform claims and availability claims recorded by the first lane-10 agent hold up against the vendors' own pages read today?
Legend: V = vendor page read today; U = unverified (snippet, memory, or the page would not load).
Verdicts: confirmed = the exact figure or claim is on the page read today; refuted = the page says something different; unreadable = the page would not load after one alternative, or the claim is not stated on any page that loaded.

Method: every page was read on 2026-11-07 with the session fetch helper (headless Chromium through the proxy, curl fallback). Long pages were grepped for price lines and then read around the hits. eBay's help center returned its "Security Measure" captcha; the eBay Seller Center pages were used as the one alternative. Munbyn's product page URL was not known, so the vendor's own site search was read. No subagents, no WebSearch, no environment or credential tools. Twenty-six URLs fetched, twenty checks across eighteen tools: all eight PICKS plus the highest-fit TOOLS (Shippo, Veeqo, ShippingEasy, Rollo, ShipStation, Stamps.com, Lugg, Easyship, eBay ShipCover).

## 0. Bottom line

- Every PICK price holds. Pirate Ship is free with no card; InsureShield is $1.00 per $100. Etsy's Shipsurance is $0.80 per $100 domestic and $1.35 international. Shopify Basic is $29/mo yearly or $39 monthly. USPS Shoe Box and Medium Cube boxes are $0.00. Munbyn's RW403B is $79.99 and the IPS03 scale $39.99, both sale prices against struck list prices.
- The eBay International Shipping size caps (48 in, 84 in length plus girth, 44 lb) could not be proved today. The help article is captcha-walled and the Seller Center page does not state them. Keep them as U.
- Lugg: the "no Southwest Florida" conclusion holds, but the first agent's details were wrong. Florida shows 26 cities, not three, all in the Miami, Fort Lauderdale and West Palm Beach metro. The dollar figures on today's page run $68 to $305, not $55 to $335.
- Rollo Ship's limit after 200 free labels is not "unclear". The page says 5 cents each after that, down to 1 cent at VIP.
- ShipStation's rumoured "Free Plan with chatbot support" is on no page read today. Chatbot-only support is a Stamps.com Basic feature; the first agent probably mixed them up.
- Veeqo's own FAQ says it is owned by Amazon (acquired 2021). Nothing else read today carried a shutdown or acquisition notice.
- Shippo's $17/mo Pro price is the annual-billing figure ($205/year); the monthly-billed price is not shown as text. Standalone address validation on Starter is priced at 9 cents, so "Shippo validates addresses free" is not supported by the pricing page.
