# Gaps review: lane 08, marketing tools and extra selling channels

Date: 2026-11-07. Author: research subagent (completeness critic for lane 08).
Question: What is missing from the lane 08 list (marketing tools and additional selling channels that bring buyers to one-of-one vintage home decor), what does each cost, which are free, and is anything on the list defunct, acquired or renamed?
Legend: V = vendor page read today (2026-11-07); U = unverified (page would not load, was bot-walled, or the number is not on the page).

Method note: WebSearch's shared budget for this turn was already used up when this agent started, so no search engine was queried. Every page below was fetched directly at a vendor, help-center, app-store or event URL through the session's headless Chromium fetcher (`fetch.sh`), with a link-lister to find fee pages inside help centers. No roundups or Reddit were read. Leads came from the lane report's own gaps (Vintage Market Days unread, Linkpop and Kaiyo absent), from vendor cross-references (Shopify's own marketing overview names Collabs; the Shopify App Store search for "microsoft channel"; TalkShopLive's help center), and from category knowledge checked against today's pages. 65 URLs were tried; 43 returned readable vendor text.

## 0. Bottom line

- Two live-selling platforms are missing and both are cheaper than Whatnot's 8%: Palmstreet ($0 monthly, "Selling Fee: 6.9% across LIVE, Purge, Invoice, and Shop", application required, plant-centric but lists pottery and ceramics and art and prints) and TalkShopLive ("TSL Platform Fee: 10.25% + $0.30 per item sold", no listing fee, connects a Shopify store, simulcasts to Facebook, Instagram and YouTube). Palmstreet fits glassware, ceramics and small art; TalkShopLive's catalog is books, music and autographed goods, so it only fits the signed memorabilia. V.
- The lane's Vintage Market Days row (U, "Southwest Florida" URL) is stale. The market is now listed as "South Gulf Coast Florida": January 29 to 31, 2027, Lee Civic Center, 11831 Bayshore Rd, Fort Myers. It is juried, the vendor profile is free, and "home decor is accepted into the market but in limited quantities." Booth fees are only inside the vendor login. This is the one local event with a confirmed Fort Myers date. V.
- Three free Shopify-side channels the lane skipped: Microsoft Channel (free listings on the Bing Shopping tab, same zero-work idea as Google free listings, 2.8 stars from 338 reviews), Shopify Collabs (free affiliate and creator gifting, "+ 2.9% commission processing fee on automatic payments may apply"; a reviewer on 2026-10-30 warns Open Access is on by default) and Shopify Marketplace Connect (formerly Codisto; "First 50 marketplace-synced orders/mo free, 1% fee per additional synced-order, capped at $99/month"; eBay, Amazon, Walmart, Target Plus, no Etsy). None is needed before the Shopify store exists. V.
- Craigslist is missing from the local list and has a real fee for Hollie: "All for sale by-dealer categories in the US—$5" per post. By-owner posts are free, but a shop posting weekly is a dealer. V.
- Email tools with better free tiers than the four on the list: beehiiv Free is $0 forever for "Up to 2,500" subscribers with "Unlimited email sends" (newsletter-first, no Shopify integration shown, beehiiv branding stays); Brevo Free sends "up to 300 emails per day" with no contact cap stated; MailerLite Free is 250 subscribers and 2,500 monthly emails (five times Klaviyo's 500 sends). Flodesk now has a $0 plan with "No limit" on active subscribers but limited sending, and its old flat unlimited price is gone. For the Etsy-only period before Shopify Messaging exists, beehiiv or Brevo is the free pick. V.
- Consignment and mass-market channels checked and mostly not worth it: EBTH is full-service auction consignment ("3 million+ registered bidders", "proceeds within 30 days") but its commission is not public (support center returned 401); Walmart Marketplace Resold charges "Home, Kitchen, Decor & Garden 15%" with "zero setup, monthly, or hidden fees" but its buyers shop on price; AuctionNinja is estate-sale software ($19.19/mo plus $250 signup plus 2.35% or $75 per sale); Bonanza says "Posting items for sale is free" but every fee page returned 403. V except the fee numbers marked U.
- Hire Hollie lead sources the lane did not cover: Yelp for Business ("It's free to be on Yelp"), Thumbtack ("no charge to join", pay per lead, lead prices not public) and Houzz Pro ($199/mo, far over budget, a Free Plan exists). Yelp free page is the only one to do now. V.
- Defunct or renamed: Linkpop (Shopify's link-in-bio) is gone, linkpop.com redirects to shopify.com and its help page redirects to the general marketing overview; Shopify Marketplace Connect was Codisto; Vintage Market Days' SWFL market URL is dead and the event is now South Gulf Coast Florida. Kaiyo, Michaels MakerPlace, Popshop Live and Kit (formerly ConvertKit) were all walled or empty today, so their status is U. Nothing in the lane's JSON list is defunct; Shopify Email to Shopify Messaging was already recorded by the lane.

## 2. Notes per tool

Palmstreet (V). Owned by Plant Identification Inc. The fee page lists "Monthly Fee: $0", a 6.9% selling fee on "both the product price and the shipping cost", unlimited lives, and processing passed through at Stripe's 2.9% + $0.30 (6% on pay-in-4, 3.49% + $0.49 on PayPal). The Buyer Referral program (from 2025-11-18) drops the selling fee to zero on buyers Hollie brings with her own link, up to $3 million in sales. A "Trendy LIVE" with 100 to 300 bookmarks earns a 0.33% to 1% reduction. A "Palmstreet Foundation Program" wants exclusivity ("Facebook and Instagram excluded"), so do not sign it while Whatnot or eBay Live are in play. Sellers apply; Palmstreet collects sales tax; 1099-K comes through Stripe or PayPal. The sell page names "pottery & ceramics, art & prints, jewelry, and more", and the FAQ does not require plant-only stock, but the audience and the examples are plants. Platform: iOS and Android app plus a web Seller Hub that prints labels.

TalkShopLive (V). Pricing article updated 2026-08-19: no listing fee, a 10.25% + $0.30 platform fee per item that is added on top of the seller's "Master Price" to form the retail price. Example on the page: to net $10.00, set retail at $11.31. Seller setup needs a channel name and a US debit card or bank account; a business registration lets TSL collect sales tax. Shopify products can be connected and resynced. Shows simulcast to Facebook, Instagram and YouTube with shoppable overlays, which is the real value for a one-person shop already posting on Instagram. The home page catalog is books, music, electronics, beauty and autographed goods, so vintage decor would be a stranger there.

Vintage Market Days (V). The franchise page says it is "a juried event accepting vendors based on variety, originality, and quality of goods sold" and that "Vintage inspired clothing and home decor is accepted into the market but in limited quantities." Applications need photos "showcasing the vendors' ability to merchandise their goods." The markets list shows three Florida markets: Jacksonville, South Gulf Coast Florida and Tampa. South Gulf Coast Florida is Lee Civic Center, Fort Myers, January 29 to 31, 2027 (Friday early buying 10 to 4, Saturday 10 to 4, Sunday 10 to 3). The lane's "southwest-florida" URL returns "Not Found" on both the bare and www hosts. Booth fees sit behind the vendor login.

Craigslist (V). The fee page is explicit: by-dealer for-sale posts in the US cost $5, Visa, MasterCard and American Express accepted. Nothing on the page prices by-owner furniture, so those stay free. There is no sale commission and no shipping system; it is pickup and cash or app payment, with the usual safety caveats.

Microsoft Channel (V). Shopify App Store, developer Microsoft, "Free to install. Additional charges may apply." The charges are Product Ads; free listings on the Bing Shopping tab and Microsoft Start Shopping need no spend. Rating 2.8 from 338 reviews is the lowest of any Shopify app in this lane, so expect feed errors. Still free reach, and the catalog is the same feed Google uses.

Shopify Collabs (V). Made by Shopify, launched 2021-04-02, 4.2 stars from 450 reviews, free, with a "2.9% commission processing fee on automatic payments." Works with Instagram, YouTube, Klaviyo, Yotpo and Flow. The 2026-10-30 review says Open Access is enabled by default so "Fraudsters can easily hijack your store's orders and claim commissions"; a 2026-10-05 review notes there is no API. For Hollie this is a gifting and tracking tool for one or two decor creators, not an affiliate program.

Shopify Marketplace Connect (V). Made by Shopify, formerly Codisto, 4.1 stars from 2,135 reviews. Pricing: free to install, first 50 marketplace-synced orders a month free, then 1% per synced order capped at $99 a month. Marketplaces: Amazon, eBay, Walmart, Target Plus. No Etsy. A reviewer says it is "a fine start point" below about 200 items. It is the free way to put the Shopify catalog on eBay later, so compare it against Crosslist Gold's $39.99 when the Shopify store exists.

beehiiv (V). Free forever to 2,500 subscribers with unlimited sends; includes landing pages, a website, recommendation network and API. Lite is $49 a month on yearly billing and Pro $95 a month on yearly billing; monthly figures are not in the text layer. Branding removal is Pro. No Shopify or Etsy integration is listed on the pricing page. A newsletter about new finds and the "do it for the plot" room suits it; abandoned-cart automation does not exist here.

Brevo (V). Free forever, 300 emails a day after account approval, no card. Starter is $9 a month monthly or $8.08 a month yearly, "From 5,000 emails per month", and the "Sent with Brevo" footer comes off only on paid plans. Shopify is named in the integrations list. Pay-as-you-go credits "do not expire" for a shop that emails rarely. Platform: web.

MailerLite (V). Free $0 a month, 250 subscribers, 2,500 monthly emails, knowledge-base support, free automations, landing page, signup forms and one digital product or booking. Comfort is $12 monthly or $10.80 a month yearly for 500 subscribers and 5,000 emails; Power is $25 monthly or $22.50 a month yearly with unlimited emails. 14-day trial of paid features, no card. Platform: web.

Flodesk (V with a gap). The pricing page now has a Free plan at $0 a month with forms, landing pages, templates, segmentation and "Email sending", and the comparison table gives Free "No limit" on active subscribers. Lite adds "Unlimited email sends" and "Max of 25,000 subscribers"; the slider shows paired prices per tier (25 and 19 at 1,000 subscribers, 32 and 29 at 2,500, 52 and 48 at 5,000) and the text layer does not say which is monthly and which is annual, though "Annual (1 month free)" is the toggle label. The FAQ asks "Does Flodesk still offer unlimited pricing?", so the old flat unlimited plan is gone. The free plan's send cap is not on the page (U).

EBTH (V process, U commission). Sell page: consult, consign, sell, get paid; "Our dedicated team handles everything: sorting, expert authentication, and photography."; "You receive your proceeds within 30 days."; a "Business Partnerships" path for "collectors and dealers to pickers and flippers." The intake form asks for a property zip code and up to 10 photos. The FAQ page is buyer-side only. support.ebth.com returned 401 on both URLs tried, so the commission rate is unverified today. © 2026 High Road Holdings, LLC.

Yelp for Business (V). "It's free to be on Yelp"; claim or add the page, respond to reviews, add photos. Yelp Ads are separate. Yelp Host (restaurant AI) is noise. For Hire Hollie, a Yelp page is a free review surface alongside Google Business Profile.

Thumbtack (V model, U price). Free to join, no subscription; pros set a lead budget and pay per lead; lead prices are shown only inside the account. Fit depends on whether "interior styling" leads exist in Fort Myers and Naples, which the page does not show.

Houzz Pro (V). Pro $199 a month for one user, a $399 a month tier, and ads "Starting at $499/mo". A Free Plan and a 30-day trial exist. Over budget by four times and aimed at contractors and full-time designers.

Walmart Marketplace Resold (V). The pricing page lists category referral fees and "zero setup, monthly, or hidden fees". Decor is 15%; furniture is 15% to $200 then 10%; collectibles 8% "for approved sellers". The Resold page cites a Walmart study (April 2026, n=126): "66% of surveyed Walmart customers say that lower price is the #1 reason they buy Resold items." Application and item setup are built for catalog goods with identifiers, not one-of-one vintage.

AuctionNinja (V). Three tiers on the sell page. The auction tier is $19.19 a month plus a $250 one-time signup, then 2.35% or $75 per sale, whichever is greater. Estate-sale listings are $29 each on the free tier or $39 a month unlimited. This is software for companies that clear houses; it is not a buyer channel for single pieces.

Alliance for the Arts GreenMarket (V, dated). The event text says the market runs every Saturday 9 to 1 on the 10-acre Fort Myers campus and that "Non-food vendors pay $30 per Saturday, or $100 per month." The page is a 2018 event entry, so confirm the current rate by phone (239-939-2787, on the page).

Bonanza (partial). The FAQ confirms free listing with pictures and points to a pricing page that returned HTTP 403 on two URL variants; the support subdomain also returned 403. The FAQ's own figures are from 2016 and 2018. Mark fees U and do not plan on it.

Linkpop (defunct, V). Requesting https://linkpop.com/ lands on https://www.shopify.com/ with the Shopify home page title. The former help URL https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/linkpop now renders the general "Marketing and promotions" overview, which lists Collabs, Audiences, Shop Campaigns and SEO tools and never mentions Linkpop. Any roundup still recommending Linkpop as Shopify's free link-in-bio is stale; Linktree Free on the lane list stands.

## 3. Recommendation for Hollie

Top picks, free first:
- Apply for a vendor profile at Vintage Market Days (free) today and ask for the South Gulf Coast Florida booth rate; January 29 to 31, 2027 at Lee Civic Center is the one confirmed local vintage crowd. Shoot content there.
- Apply to Palmstreet as a live seller (free, 6.9%). Use it for glassware, barware, figurines and small framed art in 20-to-40-piece lives; keep furniture off live. Decline the Foundation Program exclusivity.
- Claim the free Yelp for Business page for Hire Hollie beside the Google Business Profile already on the list.
- For the newsletter before Shopify exists, pick beehiiv Free (2,500 subscribers, unlimited sends) if it is a story letter, or Brevo Free (300 emails a day, Shopify integration later) if it should become shop email. Both are $0 forever.
- Craigslist by-dealer for furniture pickup at $5 a post, only for pieces too big to ship.

Install on day one of Shopify (all free): Microsoft Channel for free Bing listings; Shopify Collabs with Open Access turned off, used only to gift creators; Shopify Marketplace Connect to test eBay from Shopify against Crosslist Gold.

Pay only once it earns it: nothing in this gap list needs money before a sale. TalkShopLive (10.25% + $0.30) is worth one test show only for signed memorabilia. EBTH only for a piece over about $500 once the commission is known.

Skip: Walmart Resold (price shoppers, 15%), AuctionNinja (estate-sale software, $75 minimum per sale), Houzz Pro ($199 a month), Flodesk paid tiers (Shopify Messaging is free), Bonanza (fees unreadable, 2018 traffic claims), GreenMarket (grocery crowd).
