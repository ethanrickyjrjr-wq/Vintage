# Lane 08: Marketing tools and extra selling channels for Holliewood and Vine

Date 2026-11-07. Author: research subagent. Question: Which marketing tools and additional selling channels bring buyers to one-of-one vintage home decor, what do they cost, and which are free? Legend: V = page read today; U = unverified (snippet, roundup, memory, or the page would not load).

Method note. Pages were read with the session's headless-Chromium fetcher. WebSearch was used only to find URLs and ran out of budget partway through, so the back half of this sweep is direct fetches of vendor URLs. Every price below is quoted from a vendor page read today unless marked U. Where a vendor's page would not load (bot wall, login wall, 401/403/502, or a JavaScript-only page that returned no text), the row says so and is U. No third-party roundup numbers are used as prices.

## 0. Bottom line

- The cheapest buyer-acquisition move is already inside Etsy: Share & Save refunds 4% of order total on sales from Hollie's own shared link, and 6.5% from October 20 to January 4. Every bio link, email and Pinterest pin should carry the `yourshopname.etsy.com` trackable link. Free. (V)
- Etsy Offsite Ads cost nothing unless an ad click leads to a sale, then 15% of the order (shop under $10,000 lifetime-365-day revenue), capped at $100 per order. Under $10,000 Hollie can opt out. Leave it on at launch and read the attribution before deciding. (V)
- Email: Shopify Messaging (the renamed Shopify Email) gives 10,000 emails a month free, then $1 per 1,000. That beats Klaviyo free (250 profiles, 500 emails), Omnisend free (250 contacts, 500 emails) and Mailchimp free (250 contacts, 500 sends) for a Shopify store. No reason to pay for email in year one. (V)
- Free product distribution once Shopify is live: Shop channel (automatic if eligible), Google Merchant Center free listings through the free Google & YouTube app, Pinterest product pins through the free Pinterest app, and Facebook Shop plus Instagram Shopping through the free Meta channel. All "free to install". (V)
- Reviews: Judge.me is free forever with unlimited reviews; its paid plan is a flat $15 a month. The "Shopify Reviews" standalone app does not exist on the App Store today; Shop app reviews live inside the Shop channel. (V)
- Extra marketplaces that fit one-of-one decor: Chairish (free to list, 30% commission once 10 listings are live, Chairish curates and arranges shipping), Mercari (10% of item plus buyer-paid shipping, no listing fee) for sub-$200 glassware and figurines, Whatnot live shows (8% commission at the Standard tier plus 2.9% + $0.30 processing on the total order). 1stDibs is vetted professionals only with two references; fees are not published. Ruby Lane, AptDeco, Depop and TikTok Shop pages were walled today. (V/U mixed, see table)
- Local Southwest Florida: Facebook Marketplace fee page would not load (U), Nextdoor Business Page and posts are free (V), Fleamasters (Fort Myers) and Flamingo Island (Bonita Springs) rent booth space by the day and book online, downtown Fort Myers runs monthly Art and Music Walks (V). Google Business Profile is free but Google requires a location "able to receive customers during its stated hours", so a home-based online shop is a poor fit unless Hollie offers in-person appointments. (V)
- Skip for now: Beacons free (9% seller fee on its store), Stan Store (price not visible, built for digital products), Linktree paid tiers (free tier is enough), eBay Live (beta, Collectibles and Luxury only), 1stDibs (not eligible yet).

## 2. Notes per tool

Etsy Share & Save (V). Enroll once in Shop Manager > Marketing > Share & Save. Only links in the `yourshopname.etsy.com` format count; a custom domain must forward to that format. Links put in Etsy Messages or listing descriptions do not count. Attribution is last click within 30 days, so an Offsite Ad click after Hollie's link steals the sale and charges 15% instead of refunding 4%. The 6.5% holiday rate runs October 20 to January 4 per the page. Promo-code links and, in the Seller app, shop-section links are also trackable.

Etsy Offsite Ads (V). Auto-enrolled. Opt-out is allowed only while the shop has never hit $10,000 in any 365 days; above that it is mandatory for life at 12%. One click can attribute several orders inside 30 days, each charged. Fee is on the order amount before the fee. The Advertising & Marketing Policy is linked from the page but was not read.

Etsy Ads (V). Click-priced auction; Etsy does not expose a bid. Maximum daily budget starts at $25 and is recalculated weekly. Below a $25 budget all listings run "Efficient spending". Charges post to the Payment account the next day. The $1 minimum daily budget appears in search snippets but was not on the page read, so it is U.

eBay Promoted Listings (V). Two strategies. General is cost-per-sale: paid only when the item sells after an ad click. Priority is cost-per-click with targeting and a daily budget. The actual ad-rate floor and attribution window were not on the Seller Center page read, so they are U. eBay's base final value fee is 13.6% plus $0.40 per order in most categories, with 250 free insertions a month. eBay's terms ban scrapers; Crosslist is an approved integration path, not a scraper.

eBay Live (V). Beta. Open only to Collectibles and Luxury. Not an option for decor today.

Shopify Messaging (V). Renamed from Shopify Email. The help center redirect confirms it covers email, SMS and WhatsApp and that "Pricing is calculated based on how many messages you send". App listing: first 10,000 emails a month free, then $1 per 1,000. SMS is billed separately. Requires Shopify Network Intelligence to be enabled in privacy settings. Reviews on the listing praise it as cheaper than Mailchimp for small stores.

Klaviyo (V). Free: 250 active profiles, 500 emails a month, email support for the first 60 days only. $20 a month for 500 profiles and 5,000 emails. A $1 first-month promo was showing today. Strong automations, but pay only if the list outgrows Shopify Messaging's features.

Omnisend (V). Free: 250 contacts, 500 emails and 500 web push a month, unlimited time. Standard showed $11.20 a month today with a 30% promo (list $16). SMS from $0.007. The FAQ says free covers workflows, segmentation, A/B tests, pop-ups and landing pages.

Mailchimp (V for free tier, U for paid). Free plan is 250 contacts and 500 sends a month with a 250 daily cap; exceeding 250 contacts pauses sending. The marketing pricing page rendered "0/month" placeholders, so paid prices are U. Smallest free tier of the three.

Shop channel (V). Automatic listing when eligible; eligibility page body did not render beyond "comply with ... the Shopify Payments Terms of Service". Shop is available where Shopify Payments is. Shop Campaigns charges only on conversion at a target acquisition cost Hollie sets. Shop collects and files US sales tax on Shop orders (page title "Sales tax in Shop").

Google & YouTube app and Merchant Center free listings (V). Free to install; connects Merchant Center, Ads, Analytics. Free listings are on by default for new Merchant Center accounts and show in Search, Images, Lens, YouTube, Gemini, the Shopping tab and Business Profile. Only product pages are eligible, and Google does not guarantee display. Paid Performance Max is optional; skip at first.

Pinterest (V). Business account is free. The Shopify app is free, syncs the catalog daily and installs the Pinterest Tag. One reviewer complained the app "keeps trying to push some free webinar". Etsy's own Pinterest integration page was not found today (U); Etsy listing pages carry a Pinterest save button, which is enough to pin manually with the Share & Save link.

Facebook & Instagram by Meta (V). Free channel; eligibility rules at Meta. Since August 26, 2025 Meta no longer collects sales tax as a marketplace facilitator, so Hollie's Shopify tax settings must cover Meta orders. Instagram Shopping eligibility page returned only navigation (U on details).

TikTok Shop (U). Seller Center returned 401 on the fee article and empty pages elsewhere. Third-party snippets disagree on the referral fee (6% vs 8%) and on whether vintage/secondhand sits in a lower band. Nothing here is verified. Do not price around TikTok Shop until the policy page is read in a logged-in browser.

Whatnot (V). Help article dated November 1, 2026. Commission tiers reset every 28 days on total sales. US Standard tier ($0 to $14,999 in a four-week period) is 8.00% for Fashion, Other Collectibles and Other; Coins 4.00%. Commission is on item price only. Payment processing is "2.9% + $0.30 per transaction" on "the total order value paid by the buyer, including the item price, buyer-paid shipping, and taxes". No signup or monthly fee. The platform is phone-native: "Start a show from your phone". Terms around refunds and category approval were not read. For Hollie: a monthly "barware and glass" show could clear small items; lamps and chairs are not live-sale goods.

Poshmark (V fees). Legal center notice: 20% on sales over $15, $2.95 on sales $15 and under, Buyer Protection Fee removed, effective October 24, 2024. Prepaid labels. Posh Shows exist ("Join Posh LIVE" on the App Store listing); hosting eligibility text could not be read (U). An App Store reviewer says "there are some restrictions on who's able to host a show". Use only for the fashion pieces.

Depop (U). 403 from this IP on three URLs. Fashion only; skip.

Chairish (V). Default Consignor plan 40%. Reaching 10 live listings auto-upgrades to Professional at 30% with free membership; dropping below 10 starts a 6-month grace period. Premium is $49 a month or $539 a year for 25% on vintage. Plus ($99 a month) and Elite ($149 a month) are for high-volume sellers only. Chairish approves items and "touch up photos", organizes shipping or allows local pickup, pays "70% or more of the sale price via PayPal" after a 48-hour return window. Commission excludes shipping and tax. Best fit of the extra marketplaces for furniture and lighting. Florida ch. 538 holds are Hollie's problem, not Chairish's.

1stDibs (V process, U amounts). Professional sellers only, two references, in-house vetting that may include site visits and social audits. Plans trade a monthly subscription against a commission rate; no numbers published. Not now.

AptDeco (U). Bot wall. Historically Northeast pickup markets; Florida coverage unknown.

Pamono (U). Vendor Application exists; terms unpublished on the pages read. Trade program for buyers. European focus.

Ruby Lane (U). reCAPTCHA wall. Third-party reports of a $45 to $54 monthly maintenance fee plus a 9.9% service fee are unverified. The audience (antique glass, figurines) fits, the fixed fee does not fit a shop with a few dozen items.

Mercari (V). Free listing. 10% on item plus buyer-paid shipping, no processing fee, effective January 6, 2025. Buyer pays 3.6% Buyer Protection. Good for sub-$200 items and a fast second channel from the phone.

Facebook Marketplace (U). Help pages are behind login or 404. Local pickup listings are widely reported free, but not verified today. No public API; Meta's commerce eligibility applies to Marketplace as a "commerce surface". Any crosslister posting to Marketplace does so through browser automation at Hollie's risk.

Nextdoor (V business, U For Sale). Free Business Page and free Business Posts in the neighborhood feed; paid Nextdoor Ads exist. The For Sale & Free help article returned 502 twice.

OfferUp (U). The fee article is not linked from the help center categories read. Paid promotions sold in 1-day and 3-day blocks and a "Promote Plus" subscription; prices are only shown in-app. The widely quoted 12.9% shipping fee with a $1.99 minimum is unverified today.

Judge.me (V). Free forever: unlimited product and store reviews, photo and video reviews, rich snippets, unlimited review requests. Awesome $15 a month adds the custom email editor, automatic reminders, Google Shopping review feed and full widget customization. Cancelling drops back to free without losing reviews.

Shop app reviews (V). Part of the Shop channel; syncs with the store's reviews app. The App Store URL apps.shopify.com/shopify-reviews redirected to a search page, so no standalone official app exists today.

Linktree (V). Free tier is enough. Starter $7 a month billed annually or $9 monthly. Pro $14 / $17. Premium $30 / $35. 7-day trials on Pro and Premium.

Beacons (V). Free forever but "9% seller fees" on its own store. $10 monthly or $8.33 on annual removes the custom-domain limit; 0% seller fees only at $25 a month on annual ($30 monthly). Not needed when Etsy and Shopify are the stores.
