# Lane 10. Shipping tools, label apps, freight and insurance for Holliewood and Vine

Date 2026-11-07. Author: research subagent. Question: Which shipping tools, label apps and services make shipping lamps, art, glass and small furniture cheaper and easier for one person in Florida, and what do they cost? Legend: V = vendor page read today; U = unverified (snippet, memory, or page would not load).

Method note: WebFetch was broken, so pages were read with a headless Chromium fetcher through the session proxy. The shared WebSearch budget ran out after five searches, so most URLs were typed from the vendor's known site structure. About 70 URLs were fetched; 48 returned real content. Every price below is quoted from a page read on 2026-11-07. eBay help pages, Uline, FedEx, 1stDibs and USPS Business Rate Card pages were bot-walled or JS shells and are marked U.

## 0. Bottom line

- Pirate Ship stays free and now has a native Etsy integration as well as eBay and Shopify: "No monthly fees—we're paid by the carriers, not by you!" It sells USPS (including Priority Mail Cubic and Ground Advantage Cubic for heavy small glass) and UPS (Ground, Ground Saver). No FedEx. Install nothing; sign up on the web (V).
- For Etsy orders, compare Etsy's own labels first. Etsy sells USPS, UPS and FedEx labels with no membership fee, auto-fills the buyer address, and its Shipsurance add-on is $0.80 per $100 domestic, cheaper than Pirate Ship's InsureShield at $1.00 per $100. Buying labels on Etsy also "helps you qualify for Etsy Purchase Protection" (V). The Etsy Seller app on iPhone can buy UPS labels (V).
- Insurance plan: carrier-included $100 (USPS Ground Advantage, Priority Mail, UPS; UPS Ground Saver only $50) for cheap items; for anything over $100, buy third-party cover at label time: Shipsurance on Etsy ($0.80/$100) or InsureShield on Pirate Ship ($1.00/$100, UPS up to $10,000, USPS up to $5,000). InsureShield replaces, not stacks, the carrier's $100, and claims need proof of value (V).
- eBay Labels is free with USPS, FedEx and UPS, prints by QR code at the counter with no printer, and eBay International Shipping costs the seller nothing extra: ship to a US hub and eBay covers loss after that. Size cap 48 in long, 84 in length plus girth, 44 lb billable, so most lamps and large art will not qualify (V).
- Shopify Shipping is included on every plan ("Up to 87% off" on Basic through Plus; UPS "save up to 82%"), but the free $200 insurance per label only comes with Grow ($79/mo yearly) or higher plus Shopify Payments. Do not upgrade for that; use Pirate Ship or Etsy insurance instead (V).
- Large pieces: Chairish-Managed Shipping (UPS parcel under 150 lb; In-Home delivery "start at $149", buyer pays, Chairish insures) is the easiest furniture route, as a channel. uShip is the quote marketplace for one-off chairs and big art; fees were not published on the pages read (U). Locally, GoShare lists Fort Myers, Cape Coral and Naples; Lugg does not serve Southwest Florida; Dolly is now Taskrabbit Delivery and Taskrabbit lists Fort Myers and Naples (V).
- The UPS Store will pack art, glass, lamps and furniture with a Pack & Ship Guarantee that reimburses item value, packing and shipping, but only if they pack it with their materials and it ships UPS. Prices are set per store and are not online (V). Use it for the few high-value chandeliers and framed pieces.
- Hardware: Munbyn RW403B thermal printer $79.99 and Munbyn IPS01 scale $35.99 (or IPS03 440 lb $39.99 for furniture). Rollo wireless is $279.99, Brother QL-1110NWB is $334.99; both are overkill (V). Free USPS Priority Mail boxes are $0.00 at the Postal Store (V).

## 2. Notes per tool

### Pirate Ship (V)
- Home page: "Save up to 89% off USPS® & UPS® rates with our free shipping software" and "No monthly fees, payment fees, or hidden costs". Web-based; "Share your labels through a Private Share link".
- Rates page lists USPS Ground Advantage (weight and Cubic, auto rate-shopped), Priority Mail (Cubic, weight, Flat Rate), Priority Mail Express, Media Mail, UPS Ground, Ground Saver, 3 Day Select, 2nd Day Air, Next Day Air, UPS international, Simple Export Rate. No FedEx anywhere on the rates page.
- Integrations pages exist for Etsy ("Connect your Etsy shop to Pirate Ship to import your orders... sync the tracking number back to Etsy"), eBay ("access discount shipping services that eBay doesn't offer, like Priority Mail Cubic Softpack") and Shopify (free app, no batch limits). Support search also lists BigCartel, BigCommerce, Cratejoy, Discogs, Ecwid and others.
- Simple Export Rate (international): "4 POUNDS AND UNDER Max Length: 22 inches, Length + Height + Width: 36 inches, Max Value: $400"; "OVER 4 POUNDS ... Max Volume: 2 cubic feet, Max Weight: 20 pounds, Max Value: $2,500". No carrier coverage included on Simple Export Rate or Media Mail.
- Insurance article (updated September 14, 2026): USPS Ground Advantage, Priority Mail, Cubic and Flat Rate include $100; "Most UPS services include $100 of carrier liability... The only exception is UPS Ground Saver ($50 of carrier liability)". InsureShield: "$1.00 for every $100 of declared value" domestic and international; "Round up to the nearest hundred-dollar amount"; limits "$10,000 per UPS shipment and $5,000 per USPS shipment", international $2,500. "Purchasing InsureShield coverage on Pirate Ship nullifies any underlying carrier protection". Postage cost is reimbursed with a payout; the premium is not.
- Collectibles article: "Proof of value is required for insurance claims"; evidence can be a paid commercial invoice (an Etsy or eBay sale qualifies as "Collectibles sold under a paid, commercial invoice"), dated appraisals, recent comparable sales. Take photos of packing inside and out before shipping.
- InsureShield terms: excluded goods include stamps, cash, bullion, securities, live plants and animals; glass, ceramics, art and lamps are not on the exclusion list read. "Notice of Co-Insurance Penalty": under-declare and the payout is prorated. Disputes under $15,000 go to desk arbitration.
- Gotcha: the "pricing", "usps", "ups", "integrations" and "shipping-calculator" URLs all 404; rates live at /rates and integrations at /integrations/<store>.

### Etsy shipping labels (V)
- USPS labels: US sellers, domestic and international. "you may receive discounted volume shipping rates." Includes $100 insurance on Ground Advantage, Priority Mail and Priority Mail Express; Media Mail none; Priority Mail International and Express International $200. Cubic pricing available on Priority Mail (up to 0.5 cu ft) and Ground Advantage (up to 1 cu ft), sides under 18 in, under 20 lb. USPS non-standard fee: "For specific package types that require special handling, USPS charges an additional $4 fee", including "Cylindrical rolls" and "Packaging or cans made of wood or metal". Relevant for rolled prints and wooden crates.
- The page carries this notice: "Starting April 26, USPS is implementing a temporary 8% rate increase for select shipping services, including Priority Mail, Priority Mail Express, USPS Ground Advantage, and Parcel Select. The increase is expected to last through January 17, 2027."
- USPS pickup can be scheduled from the Completed orders tab; 70 lb limit per package. Unused labels refundable within 30 days.
- UPS labels: "To buy UPS shipping labels from Etsy, you must be a seller based in the US. At this time, labels can only be purchased for domestic addresses." Buyable "directly from Etsy.com or the Etsy Seller app". $100 liability included. UPS Ground Saver: max 70 lb, 130 in length plus girth, no side over 60 in, 1 lb minimum billable. Drop at UPS Access Points or Drop Boxes.
- FedEx labels: "All FedEx labels purchased through Etsy come with up to $100 of liability insurance."
- Shipsurance on Etsy: up to $5,000; "$0.80 per $100 of order value" domestic and APO; "$1.35 per $100 of coverage" international. Available on USPS, FedEx, UPS and Global Postal labels. Insure postage plus item value.
- Shipping calculator page claim: "You won't find better USPS domestic rates from any other third-party label provider*" (asterisk: based on published rates and reseller purchases in the last 30 days). It also says "Buying your labels on Etsy helps you qualify for Etsy Purchase Protection". Worth testing against Pirate Ship on a few real packages; Pirate Ship claims "BELOW USPS Commercial Pricing".
- Gotcha: the Seller Handbook (etsy.com) returned 403 to the fetcher; help.etsy.com loaded fine.

## 3. Recommendation for Hollie

Free first, today:
1. Create a Pirate Ship account (web, no card) and connect Etsy, eBay and later Shopify. Use it for every package that is not an Etsy order, and as the rate check for Etsy orders. Buy InsureShield at $1 per $100 on anything worth more than $100; declare the full value; photograph the packing.
2. For Etsy orders, open the label flow in Etsy first. Compare its USPS or UPS price with Pirate Ship. If close, buy on Etsy: Shipsurance there is $0.80 per $100 and the label counts toward Etsy Purchase Protection. Use the Etsy Seller app on the iPhone for UPS labels.
3. For eBay orders, use eBay Labels (free, USPS/FedEx/UPS, QR code at the counter). Leave eBay International Shipping on for small items; it will not accept most lamps or framed art because of the 48 in and 44 lb caps.
4. Order free USPS Priority Mail boxes (shoe box, medium cube) for figurines and glass that ship Priority Mail. Weigh everything after packing.
5. Buy a Munbyn RW403B printer ($79.99) and an IPS03 440 lb scale ($39.99). Skip Rollo and Brother.
6. For an accent chair or large framed piece sold locally, quote GoShare in the app (Fort Myers and Naples are listed). For a high-value chandelier or glass art going far, get a packing quote from the nearest UPS Store and ship UPS from the store so the Pack & Ship Guarantee applies.
7. Keep Shippo Free (30 labels a month) or Veeqo Free in reserve only if FedEx or DHL Express is ever cheaper for a specific box; do not run three label tools day to day.

Pay only once it earns it:
- Shopify Shipping is included in Basic ($29/mo yearly) when the store launches; do not move to Grow ($79/mo) for the $200 free insurance.
- Shippo Pro ($17/mo) or ShipStation Starter ($14.99/mo) only if volume passes about 30 labels a month and multi-carrier batching saves real time. ShipStation is the only one with LTL freight quotes inside.
- uShip or Craters & Freighters only per item, quoted, and priced into the listing before sale.

Skip and why:
- Stamps.com (Basic caps at 10 labels; Professional costs more than alternatives). Easyship (built for international volume). EasyPost (developer API; revisit for the dashboard's insurance at 1%). Smarty (address API not needed). Lugg (not in Southwest Florida). Shiply (UK-centric). Direct Shipsurance, U-PIC and InsureShield accounts (volume products; the same cover is cheaper inside Etsy and Pirate Ship). Brother QL-1110NWB and Rollo Wireless (price).

Channel note for the synthesis: Chairish-Managed Shipping (buyer pays from $11 parcel or $149 In-Home, Chairish insures) solves furniture shipping better than any tool here; it belongs in the channels decision, not the tools stack.
