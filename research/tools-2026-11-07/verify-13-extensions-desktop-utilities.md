## 2. What the first agent got wrong or left out

1. Crosslist: a fourth tier, Diamond at $44.99 per month, was omitted. The "20 free listings" line is a 3-day money-back guarantee on a paid first purchase (card required), not a free tier; annual-plan refunds lose a $9.99 processing fee. Gold is still $39.99 per month, matching 2026-10-26.
2. EverBee: Growth $24.99/month and Business $69/month are the annual-billing rates ($299 and $828 charged once a year, under the page's "Annual Save 36%" toggle). The month-to-month Growth price is not in the page text; the struck-through "$468" per year implies about $39 a month.
3. iCloud for Windows: the "Windows 11 recommended, Microsoft Store" facts are on support.apple.com/en-us/103232, not on 108994 (which has no requirements). "Windows 10 for older versions" and "app free" are not on any Apple page read today.
4. PowerToys: the install page never says "free". The evidence is the MIT licence on the GitHub repo. The Microsoft Store listing would not render without JavaScript.
5. Terapeak: the Terapeak help page never says "free". The Seller Hub page does: "It's free to use". The claim stands, but the source was the wrong page.
6. Win+V clipboard: the 4 MB per item figure is right, but the page also caps history at 25 entries and clears it on every restart except pinned items. Both matter if Ricky pastes condition notes all day; Espanso or Ditto covers that gap.
7. Keywords Everywhere: the "$80 for 200,000 credits" top-up figure is not on the subscriptions page. Unverified.
8. LocalSend: Apple's listing says only "In-App Purchases Yes". The "donation" description comes from a user review and the Donate link on localsend.org.
9. LanguageTool: the quarterly plan ($49.90 per quarter, $16.63 per month) was omitted. Every other LanguageTool figure matched exactly.
10. Phone Link: still unverified for iPhone photos. The requirements page's iOS tab did not render.

Everything else checked (Bitwarden, eRank plans and extension, TextExpander, Grammarly, 1Password, iCloud+ US prices, Espanso, Google Lens, Snipping Tool, Microsoft 365 Basic) matched the vendor page word for word today. No shutdown or acquisition notices were seen on any page read.
