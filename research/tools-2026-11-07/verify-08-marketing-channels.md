# Verification of lane 08: marketing tools and extra selling channels

Date 2026-11-07. Author: research subagent (adversarial price and claim checker).
Question: Do the prices, free tiers, platform claims and availability claims recorded by the lane 08 agent match the vendors' own pages today?
Legend: V = vendor page read today (2026-11-07) with the session fetch script; U = unverified (snippet, memory, or the page would not load).
Method: every PICK plus the highest-fit TOOLS were re-opened on the vendor's own pricing, plans, fee or help page. Verdict "confirmed" means the exact figure or claim is on the page read today; "refuted" means the page says something different (correction given); "unreadable" means the page would not load or did not render the claim after one alternative. Quotes are verbatim and under 25 words. 39 page fetches were made. WebSearch was unavailable for most of this run (the shared per-turn budget was spent), so no new URLs were discovered; every URL below was either in the lane file or a vendor URL already known.

## 0. Bottom line

- The lane 08 agent's eight PICKS all hold. Etsy Share & Save (4%, 6.5% from October 20 to January 4), Shopify Messaging (10,000 free emails a month then $1 per 1,000), Pinterest (free business account, free Shopify app), Google & YouTube app (free to install, free listings "at no cost"), Shop channel (free; Shop Campaigns "charged only when a customer converts"), Judge.me ($0 and $15 per month), QRCode Monkey (free, commercial use, never expires) and Chairish (30% at 10+ listings, free membership) are all on the vendors' pages today.
- Three corrections. Omnisend's "$11.20/mo" Standard price is a pay-3-months-upfront promo ("Total of: $48.00 $33.60 today -30%"), so the real monthly list price is $16; the agent labeled it "(monthly)". Chairish has an iPhone and iPad listing app, not web only, and the agent left out the Plus plan ($99 / Month, 22%) and Elite plan ($149 / Month, 20% tiered). Buffer's "$5 /month" is the yearly-billed price ("$60 billed yearly"), not a monthly price.
- Two downgrades from V to U. Poshmark's fee_policy URL renders only the Terms of Service tab today; the "20% / $2.95" figures were not on the page and the support site returned 502. Whatnot's "US Standard tier ($0 to $14,999 per four weeks)" label sits behind a market selector that does not render; the 8% ceiling, the 2.9% + $0.30 processing fee and "No monthly costs" are confirmed.
- Two gaps filled. Mailchimp paid prices render today: Essentials $13 /month, Standard $20 /month, Premium $350 /month. Google Business Profile is confirmed free on Google's own page.
- Still unreadable after one alternative: Ruby Lane (both /info/fees and /sell return "Oops! Something Went Wrong."), Facebook Marketplace fees (the agent's URL is the commerce eligibility page with no fee text; /help/marketplace is "Page not found").
- Nothing checked today is shut down, acquired or renamed since the lane report, except that Shopify's own marketing page for Shopify Email now carries the title "Shopify Messaging", which supports the agent's "formerly Shopify Email" note.
- Omissions worth adding to the lane report: Judge.me now advertises "Judge.me Email is here! Get 15,000 emails/mo free"; the Shop channel "requires Shopify Network Intelligence to be enabled in your privacy settings"; Klaviyo runs a "$1" first-month promo; Chairish pays out "via PayPal" after a 48-hour return window; Mercari now charges buyers a 3.6% Buyer Protection fee on top of the seller's 10%.

## 2. What the first agent got wrong or left out

1. Omnisend Standard: "$11.20/mo ... (monthly)" is wrong. That price needs 3 months paid upfront ("Total of: $48.00 $33.60 today -30%"). The monthly list price is $16/mo. Free tier figures are right.
2. Chairish platform: not web only. The consign page offers an iPhone and iPad app for listing. The plan list also skipped Plus ($99 / Month or $1,089 / Year, 22%) and Elite ($149 / Month or $1,639 / Year, 20% tiered), and the 1-to-9-listing plan is called "Consignor". The support URL used (115006460407) redirects to article 44165603442321. Payouts go "via PayPal" after a 48-hour return window; Hollie needs a PayPal account.
3. Buffer: "$5 /month per channel" is the yearly-billed price ("$60 billed yearly"). Monthly billing costs more.
4. Poshmark: marked V, but the fee_policy URL renders only the Terms of Service tab today and the support site returned 502. The 20% and $2.95 figures should be U until read.
5. Whatnot: the "US Standard tier ($0 to $14,999 per four weeks)" label is not visible on the help page today (market tables are behind a selector). The 8% ceiling, 2.9% + $0.30 processing and "No monthly costs" are confirmed on whatnot.com/sell. The help page notes the new tiered structure "took effect September 21, 2026 for existing sellers" and rates "vary by category".
6. Mailchimp paid prices were recorded as unreadable; they render today at mailchimp.com/pricing/marketing/: Essentials $13 /month, Standard $20 /month, Premium $350 /month (or $297.50 per month for 12 months). Mailchimp stays the smallest free tier (250 contacts, 500 sends, 250 a day), so the "low" fit stands.
7. Minor omissions: Judge.me's new free email product ("15,000 emails/mo free"); the Shop channel's Shopify Network Intelligence requirement; Klaviyo's $1 first-month promo; Mercari's 3.6% buyer-side Buyer Protection fee; a Google & YouTube app review reporting items shown out of stock for 12 days after install; the Pinterest app review complaining about webinar pushes.
8. Facebook Marketplace and Ruby Lane were already U in the lane file and stay U; no Meta or Ruby Lane page read today states fees.
