# Gaps review: lane 02, AI listing writers and Etsy/eBay SEO tools

Date: 2026-11-07. Author: research subagent (completeness critic for lane 02).
Question: What is missing from the lane 02 tool list (AI writers and keyword/SEO tools for Etsy, eBay and Shopify listings of one-of-one vintage pieces), and which listed tools are defunct, acquired or renamed?
Legend: V = vendor page read today (2026-11-07); U = unverified (page would not load, price not in the text layer, or no vendor page found).

Method note: WebSearch's shared budget for this turn was already used up when this agent started, so every page below was fetched directly at a vendor, app-store, help-center or GitHub URL through the session's headless Chromium fetcher. No search engine was queried. Roundup pages were not read; leads came from the lane report's own "not opened" list (Insight Agent, EtsyHunt, Lysterly, EtsyGenerator, Frooition, 3Dsellers), from app-store and GitHub category pages, and from vendor pages that cross-reference each other (Copysmith lists Describely and Rytr; OneShop's footer names Hammoq, PrimeLister, ListingJoy, ResellKit).

## 0. Bottom line

- The lane missed three free Etsy-side writers or keyword tools: EHunt (the renamed EtsyHunt, free tier with 5 keyword searches a day), Insight Factory (15 free credits, no card) and EtsyGenerator (a free, no-login "restored build" running on OpenRouter free models). None beats the free Etsy Marketplace Insights plus a free chatbot already on the list, but EHunt is the one worth a free account. V.
- The lane missed the free eBay title tools: 3Dsellers eBay Title Builder, ZIK eBay Title Builder and title-builder.com. All three pull live eBay keyword and competitor-title data at no cost. Keyword Tool Dominator adds eBay autocomplete with a free tier and one-time pricing from $9. For eBay titles of vintage lamps and glassware these are the cheap complement to Terapeak. V.
- Paid Etsy AI writers now exist as a category: Lysterly ($19.99/mo for 30 generations) and Vela ($29.95 per shop per month, AI listing optimization, one-week trial). Both are over the free-first bar and under $50. Neither offers a free plan. V.
- Crosslisters other than Vendoo, Nifty and Crosslist also bundle AI writers: List Perfectly Simple is $29 Monthly with 25 AI-generated listings a month; OneShop is $45/month; PrimeLister's price page is a 404. The crosslister choice stays in lane 01 (Crosslist Gold); this only prices the writer inside each. V except PrimeLister (U).
- Free general assistants the lane skipped: Microsoft Copilot is a free Windows app already on Ricky's PC; Copilot Pro no longer exists as a product (its store URL now lands on Microsoft 365 Premium, $19.99/month or $199.99/year). LM Studio (Free $0) and Ollama ($0, local) run open models offline on Windows; Ollama already runs on the Fedora box. These duplicate ChatGPT Free, Claude Free and Gemini Free for drafting; they add privacy, not quality. V.
- Shopify SEO apps for the later site: SEOWILL (formerly SEOAnt) has a free plan with 30 AI content credits; Yoast SEO is from $19/month with a 14-day trial. Not needed until the Shopify store exists. V.
- Defunct or renamed: Reeva.ai is now an industrial engineering-data company (the lane's "no reseller product found" is confirmed); Roketfy pivoted to Trendyol review analytics and has no self-serve price; EtsyHunt is now EHunt; Copysmith is the parent of Describely and Rytr; SEOAnt is now SEOWILL; Copilot Pro became Microsoft 365 Premium; Rytr pivoted to a relationship and communication tool. Flyp could not be checked (403 wall). V except Flyp (U).
- Etsy's help center shows no new seller-side AI listing writer beyond the Search Visibility title tool already on the list. "How Etsy AI Assistants Work" covers buyer and support chat assistants only. eBay's own AI listing tool is confirmed only through the eBay App Store listing text; the eBay help and press URLs tried today were 404. V (App Store) / U (eBay help).

- Refund: "Request a refund within 24 hours of your first purchase". Dropshipping menu item signals the audience.
- Fit: good free complement to Etsy Marketplace Insights for keyword ideas on lamps, barware and glass. Not a writer on the free tier.
