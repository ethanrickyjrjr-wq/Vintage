# Gaps review, lane 07: Southwest Florida sourcing venues with online tools

Date 2026-11-07. Author: research subagent (completeness critic). Question: what did the first lane-07 agent miss among Southwest Florida sourcing venues and the online tools (alerts, online bidding, photo previews, schedules) that go with them, and is anything on its list defunct, acquired or renamed? Legend: V = page read today (2026-11-07) with the fetcher; U = unverified (snippet, memory, or the page would not load).

Method note. WebSearch's shared budget for this turn was already used up when this agent started, so no search engine was used. Discovery came from aggregator listing pages read today (EstateSales.net company directories for Fort Myers / Cape Coral, Naples, Punta Gorda and Sarasota / Bradenton; HiBid radius lists around Sarasota and Naples; AuctionNinja's Florida list) and from vendor pages fetched by known URL. Tools already on the lane list were not re-verified, with one exception: MaxSold's buyer's premium, which the first agent left unknown, was read from MaxSold's own help desk today.

## 0. Bottom line

- The lane list has no general garage-sale aggregator. YardSaleSearch.com showed 41 sales around Fort Myers today with photos and an "Alert me" button, and gsalr.com sends free daily emails with keyword highlighting. Both are free. Both are V.
- A third estate-sale aggregator, EstateSale.com, is missing. It offers free email notifications by zip radius and keyword. Its Florida pages would not render, so the SWFL sale count is U.
- Invaluable was in the seeds but never made the list. It is a free aggregator with an iOS and Android app and inbox alerts; Freedom Auction Company in Sarasota lists its mid-century design auction there (and on LiveAuctioneers, AuctionZip and HiBid).
- Local companies with their own alert sign-ups are missing: Allegro Estate Sales (Fort Myers, text or email), Can't Take It With You (Sarasota to Fort Myers, online auctions, email list), Deming Estate Sales (Bradenton, email alerts), Venice Estate Sales & Gallery (Subscribe link, two shops open daily), Crimson Estate Services (live auctions, West and Southwest Florida).
- New in 2025 and 2026 on EstateSales.net: Grasons of Sarasota-Venice (member since 2026, the first Grasons franchise in the region), King's Road Auctions (Naples, 2025, HiBid page empty today), Blackwell Auction Group (2026, Florida licensed), Florida Estate Sales & Antiquities (2025), Bayshore Estate Sales (2026, site down today).
- Local marketplaces with saved-search alerts are missing: OfferUp (free, "search alerts") and Nextdoor's For Sale & Free (free). Both are apps on Hollie's iPhone, both are manual and both forbid scraping; use them as alert feeds only.
- Goodwill Manasota is a separate Goodwill with Bargain Barn outlets and a deals newsletter, and its territory includes "portions of Lee". Worth one trip north.
- Defunct or renamed: Sarasota Architectural Salvage's flagship store is permanently closed; Letgo is folded into OfferUp; the listed Gulf Coast Auction House (Bonita Springs) now also runs gcauctionhouse.com as "Gulf Coast Auctions & Estates"; EstateSales.org and gsalr.com share one owner (Treasure Listings LLC), so they are not two independent nets.
- MaxSold's buyer's premium is 18% (read today from its help desk). Its main site returns 403 to the fetcher, so whether it runs SWFL sales is still U.

## 1b. Defunct, acquired or renamed (evidence read today)

- **Sarasota Architectural Salvage (Sarasota).** Not on the lane list, but the obvious lamp-and-decor salvage source anyone would suggest. Its site says "our flagship store at 1093 Central Avenue in Sarasota, which is now permanently closed" and "we are currently in transition as we renovate our new home". Remaining stock is a Google Photos album. V. https://www.sarasotasalvage.com/

- **Gulf Coast Auction House (Bonita Springs), on the lane list via its HiBid subdomain.** Now also runs its own site at gcauctionhouse.com ("© 2026 Gulf Coast Auction House", "Auctioneer License #AU5688") and appears on EstateSales.net as "Gulf Coast Auctions & Estates", "Member Since 2026". Same company, new domain and trade name; the site adds "Buy It Now" and "Storage unit discoveries, Local pickup auctions". V. https://www.gcauctionhouse.com/
- **EstateSales.org (on the lane list) and gsalr.com share one owner.** gsalr.com's footer: "Partner Sites gsalr.ca EstateSales.org Snaplist.com", "© 2007-2026 Treasure Listings, LLC". Count them as one net, not two. V. https://gsalr.com/
- **Grasons.** No Grasons franchise in Lee or Collier. A new "Grasons Of Sarasota-Venice, FL" is "Member Since 2026" on EstateSales.net and has its own page. Launched, not defunct. V. https://www.estatesales.net/companies/FL/Sarasota-Bradenton

- **King's Road Auctions (Naples, EstateSales.net member since 2025).** HiBid page is live but empty today: "(Please check back soon as upcoming auction information is posted regularly.)" Possibly dormant; join its "Email List" and wait. V. https://kingsroadauctions.hibid.com/
- **Burchard Galleries (on the lane list).** Its Nov 14, 2026 Discovery Auction on HiBid is "Live Onsite Only Auction", "No Internet Bidding", absentee bids by phone or email only. Online bidding is not a given for every Burchard sale. V. https://hibid.com/florida/auctions?zip=34236&miles=40

- **Gulf Coast Humane Society (Fort Myers) thrift store.** No thrift page exists on the site today (guessed URL 404, home page lists only adoption and clinics). If a roundup names a GCHS thrift shop, treat it as stale. V. https://gulfcoasthumanesociety.org/thrift-store/

## 2. Notes per tool

**YardSaleSearch.com.** The Fort Myers page lists sales with photo counts (one sale had 74 photos), a radius filter from 1 to 100 miles, a date range, keyword search and sale-type filters including "Estate Sale". The alert is per area, free, after registering. It is a listing site, not a marketplace; nothing to buy online. No terms problem for a shopper.

**gsalr.com.** The footer says "Partner Sites gsalr.ca EstateSales.org Snaplist.com" and the copyright is "© 2007-2026 Treasure Listings, LLC". So EstateSales.org on the lane list and gsalr.com are one company; do not count them as independent coverage. Email digest: "Add keywords to highlight sales with items you are looking for". The city page pattern I guessed returned 404; set the alert from the home page by city.

**EstateSale.com.** Different owner from EstateSales.net (Vintage Software LLC) and EstateSales.org (Treasure Listings). Search "within 25 to 500 miles of zip code with keywords". Florida and Fort Myers URLs returned empty pages to the fetcher, so I could not count SWFL sales. Allegro's Linktree carries an "EstateSale.com Profile Page", which shows at least one Fort Myers company lists there.

**Invaluable.** Header shows "Saved" and "Notifications" for logged-in users. The vendor's help and FAQ URLs I tried returned 404, so the alert mechanics (keyword alerts) and buyer's premium rules are U beyond the home-page quote. Premiums are set per house; Freedom Auction Company's premium was not on its page.

**OfferUp.** The App Store listing says "OfferUp and Letgo have come together as one online marketplace", so Letgo is gone. OfferUp Support search returned "Get notified about items you want" with "search alerts and similar item alerts". Promote Plus at $19.99 is a seller boost, not needed for buying. No public API; do not scrape.

**Nextdoor.** Free, "over 100 million verified neighbors", "free and for sale items", "Find local events like garage sales". Verified-address network, so it only shows Hollie's own neighborhood radius. The help center (help.nextdoor.com) returned 502 through the proxy.

**Freedom Auction Company.** Address "7202 21 Street East, Sarasota, FL 34243". Upcoming: poster auction Nov 27, 2026 online only; "MID-CENTURY MODERN DESIGN DECEMBER 11TH, 2026"; circus collections Feb 17, 2027. Also "FREEDOMCO ON WHATNOT WEEKLY SHOWS" covering estate, antiques, collectibles. Bidding is through LiveAuctioneers, Invaluable, AuctionZip or HiBid, each with its own premium.

**Can't Take It With You.** Phone numbers are Long Island (631) and the company also runs Long Island sales; Florida coverage is "Sarasota to Fort Myers". Email list on the home page. Online auction platform not named.

**Allegro Estate Sales & Services.** Fort Myers (239) number on EstateSales.net. The Linktree has "Current Estate Sale" and the text/email notice link. Linktree joined "September 2022".

**Grasons of Sarasota-Venice.** National Grasons locations page is a map with no text list; no Fort Myers or Naples franchise appeared anywhere. The Sarasota-Venice page has "SEE OUR LISTINGS" but no alert sign-up of its own; the national home page has an email box for "Insights and Tips", not sale alerts.

**Goodwill Manasota.** Separate from Goodwill Southwest Florida (goodwillswfl.org). Location types listed: "Bargain Barns/Outlets", "Bookstores", "Community Resource Centers". Store addresses sit behind a JavaScript locator I could not read. Instagram feed on the home page mentions a weekly "color sale".

**StorageAuctions.com.** Owned by Storable (self-storage software), "©2025 Storable". "Over 100,000 storage auctions since 2014". Deposits and clean-out timelines are set by each facility. Florida list pages failed to load (chunk error and an empty page).

**AuctionNinja.** Good platform, wrong geography today. The Florida list shows two sales, both on the Atlantic side. Buyer FAQ URLs returned 404. Worth a monthly glance only.

**Bid13.** The FAQ titles confirm a "buyer's fee" and a "cleaning deposit" and $10 bid increments, but the answer text did not render. Lockerfox (on the list) publishes 15%; Bid13 did not.

**EBTH.** Pickup locations shown today were Cincinnati and Columbus only. Shipping is quoted per item; some items need a custom shipping estimate by email. For cheap decor the freight kills the margin.

**MaxSold (re-verification only).** maxsold.com and www.maxsold.com returned HTTP 403 to the fetcher; the Google Play package id I guessed was 404. The help desk loaded: "At MaxSold, the buyer's premium is 18%" and "our 18% buyer's premium is also subject to tax". Whether MaxSold runs SWFL sales is still U.

**Thrift checks that came up empty.** Gulf Coast Humane Society (Fort Myers) has no thrift page (the guessed URL was 404 and the home page has no store). Patty Baker Humane Society Naples (hsnaples.org redirects there) shows no thrift store on its home page. The Shelter for Abused Women & Children (Naples) does have "The Shelter Options Shoppe" in its menu, but the page URL I guessed was 404, so hours and address are U.

## 3. Recommendation for Hollie

Free first, today:
1. Register on YardSaleSearch.com and press "Alert me about new yard sales in this area!" for Fort Myers, radius 30 miles. Tick Estate Sale, Moving Sale and Multi-family.
2. Subscribe to gsalr.com's daily email for Fort Myers and Cape Coral and add keywords: lamp, chandelier, crystal, decanter, barware, depression glass, figurine, mannequin, dress form, turntable, projector, signed.
3. Sign up for EstateSale.com free email notifications at zip 33901, 50 miles, same keywords.
4. Create a free Invaluable account, follow Freedom Auction Company and Burchard Galleries, and save a search for the same keywords; install the app.
5. In the OfferUp and Nextdoor apps, set search alerts for the same keyword list within 25 miles.
6. Join the alert lists at Allegro (text or email), Can't Take It With You and Deming; subscribe at Venice Estate Sales & Gallery.

Pay only when it earns it: nothing here needs money. gsalr's $4.99 ad-free is optional. Invaluable, LiveAuctioneers and HiBid premiums are per lot; set a ceiling (hammer plus premium plus tax) before bidding.

Skip: EBTH (Ohio pickup, freight), Bid13 and StorageAuctions.com unless a Lee or Collier unit appears (storage units are a gamble, not a stock plan), AuctionNinja until a SWFL seller joins.
