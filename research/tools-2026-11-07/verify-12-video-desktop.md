Date 2026-11-07. Author: research subagent (adversarial checker). Question: Do the prices, free tiers, platform claims and availability recorded by the first lane-12 agent match the vendors' own pages today? Legend: V = page read today; U = unverified (page would not load, or the claim is not on any page read today).

Method: 29 page fetches today through the session's headless-Chromium fetcher (fetch.sh, curl fallback). Every quote below is copied from the page text as it rendered today. WebFetch and crawl4ai were not used. No new tools were added. Pages that failed got one alternative URL each (help center, app store listing or homepage), then were marked unreadable.

Verdicts: confirmed = the exact figure or claim is on the page read today; refuted = the page says something different (correction given); unreadable = the page or the relevant part could not be read after one alternative attempt.

## 2. What the first agent got wrong or left out

1. Epidemic Sound: wrong on "price cards did not render" and loose on the plan name. The cards rendered today: Creator $9.99/mo (billed $119.88 yearly), Pro $16.99/mo (billed $203.88 yearly), Pro Plus $39.99/mo (billed $479.88 yearly). The tier for "personal video content" is called Creator, not Personal. The tier described for "commercial use online", with "Licensing for digital ads" and "client work", is Pro. For a shop's promotional video the honest comparison price is $16.99/mo billed yearly, which is over the $50-a-month-total budget once Crosslist is counted.
2. CapCut: the figures (Pro USD 19.99/month, Ultra USD 79/month) are right, but two cautions were left out. The page has a Monthly/Yearly toggle and does not label which the shown figures are. It also says "Displayed pricing is for reference only. Sign in to see final pricing, taxes, and any applicable offers for your account." A Teams tier at USD 12.49/month per seat (2 seats minimum) was omitted.
3. Filmora: the figures match, but US$53.99 and US$79.99 are sale prices against struck list prices of US$59.99 and US$99.99. The one-time Perpetual price is behind a tab that did not render. The add-on "Creative Assets" trial auto-renews at US$19.99/mo and is "Software Not Included"; a careless checkout adds it.
4. DaVinci Resolve: "no watermark stated" is accurate only in that the page does not mention watermarks at all. The free download is simply labelled "Free". Studio's $236 is a "SAVE 20%" sale price against $295.
5. OBS: the homepage does not name a licence. The GPL claim rests on the GitHub repo, which says "GPL-2.0 license ... (or any later version)". The first agent cited both pages, so this is a sourcing note, not an error.
6. Clipchamp Premium: the Microsoft compare page confirms $99.99/year and $9.99/month for Microsoft 365 Personal, but that page's text never mentions Clipchamp. The link between Premium and Microsoft 365 is on the Clipchamp pricing page. Both pages are needed to support the claim; the first agent cited both.
7. Submagic, Kapwing, Loom, Veed, Canva: the recorded figures are right but are the yearly-billing (or yearly-toggle) rates. Monthly figures still did not render on any of them today. Submagic's "save $84 / $192 / $336" implies about $19, $39 and $69 month to month; Kapwing's "SAVE 33%" implies about $24 for Pro monthly. Those are arithmetic, not quotes.
8. Descript: the platform claim (Windows 11 and newer, macOS 14) was not re-checked; the download page was not fetched today. Pricing and the free tier matched exactly.
9. Adobe Express, Microsoft Designer, Uppbeat: all three stay unreadable. adobe.com returns 403, designer.microsoft.com renders no text, uppbeat.io returns 429 to this fetcher. Adobe Express's "free with In-App Purchases" and the existence of a Premium plan are confirmed from Google Play; the Premium price is not.

Everything else checked (Clipchamp free tier, Subtitle Edit, CapCut Desktop Windows 7+, CuePrompter, Photopea, Pexels, Veed Creator, Canva, Descript pricing, Kapwing, Opus Clip, Loom, Shotcut, Kdenlive, Audacity) matched the vendor page word for word today.
