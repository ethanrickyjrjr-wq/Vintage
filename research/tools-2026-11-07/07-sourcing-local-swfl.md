# Lane 07: Sourcing vintage stock in Southwest Florida, and the online tools attached to each venue

Date 2026-11-07. Author: research subagent. Question: Where in Southwest Florida (Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Naples, Bonita Springs, Estero, Punta Gorda, Sarasota) can Hollie buy vintage stock cheaply, and which of those sources have online tools (email alerts, online bidding, photo previews, schedules) she can use from the computer? Legend: V = page read today; U = unverified (snippet, memory, or the page would not load).

Method note. WebFetch is broken in this session, so every page was read with the headless-Chromium fetch script over the session proxy. Six WebSearch calls were used for leads before the shared search budget ran out; after that, every URL was guessed or taken from a page already read. No Reddit, no Facebook, no scraping. Quotes are verbatim from pages read today unless marked U.

## 0. Bottom line

- Two free aggregators cover the estate sale side and both have real email alerts. EstateSales.net ("Free E-mail notifications: get notified via E-mail about upcoming sales near you!") and EstateSales.org ("Email me sales within" 5 to 100 miles, with a day-of-week picker). Sign up for both today; they are the daily inbox. V.
- Early November is thin in Collier County. Today the Naples page on EstateSales.net showed 43 results at its 100-mile default, but the in-person sales were in Lehigh Acres, Punta Gorda, Rotonda West, Bradenton and the Miami side; nothing in-person inside Naples or Bonita. The Lee County action right now is online: Caring Transitions of Cape Coral has five CTBids auctions ending Nov 8 to Nov 16 and KCM Galleries (Cape Coral) runs framed-art auctions every Sunday and Wednesday. V.
- Online bidding with local pickup is the cheapest way to buy whole-house contents from the desk: CTBids (free account, "Bidding starts at $1", Premier is "$99/year" and not needed), HiBid (free, "Join Our Mailing List", "Manage Notifications", "Watch List"), and Gulf Coast Auction House in Bonita Springs on HiBid ("18% Buyer's Premium | 3% Discount for Cash Payments", previews Fri 10 to 6 and Sat 12 to 3). V.
- Charity thrift is the cheapest floor stock and three chains publish real schedules and lists: Goodwill SWFL (free VIP Perks, 5% back, 50% color tag each week, $1.99 leftovers Sunday and Monday, Outlet Center at 5100 Tice St), Habitat ReStores (North Fort Myers and Bonita Springs, Mon to Sat 10 to 5; East Naples, Mon to Sat 9 to 6:30, with an email list for "fresh inventory, sales"), and Hope Chest (four stores, Mon to Fri 9 to 5, Sat 9 to 4, newsletter). V.
- Flea markets run Friday to Sunday year round: Fleamasters (Fri to Sun 9 to 5), Flamingo Island (Fri, Sat, Sun 8 to 4) and Ortiz (Sat 8 to 4, Sun 6 to 4). None has an alert; Fleamasters and Flamingo post on Facebook and Flamingo has a newsletter box. V.
- Storage auctions are live for Naples on StorageTreasures (21 auctions today, free account, unit photos, "framed art" and "lamps" appear as categories) but they are gambles on sealed contents; good for a one-off, not a weekly habit. Lockerfox charges 15% of the winning bid with a $10 minimum. V.
- Watch out for stale directories: AllCoast Auction's domain now redirects to an Indonesian gambling site, and Crimson Auctions, Busted Gavel, Encore and Caring Transitions Cape Coral's own sites would not load. Buy through the platform pages (CTBids, HiBid, EstateSales.net), not the company domains. V for the redirect, U for the others.
- Not verified today: Salvation Army family store addresses, St. Matthew's House store addresses and hours, MaxSold (403 wall), Facebook group names, church rummage seasons, and the snowbird sell-off calendar. See section 4.
