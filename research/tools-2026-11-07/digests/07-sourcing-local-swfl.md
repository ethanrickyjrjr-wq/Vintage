# Lane 07: Sourcing local SWFL (estate sales, auctions, thrift, flea markets)

Question: where in Southwest Florida (Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Naples, Bonita Springs, Estero, Punta Gorda, Sarasota) can Hollie buy vintage stock cheaply, and which venues have online tools (email alerts, online bidding, photo previews, schedules) she can run from the desk?

Date 2026-11-07. V = vendor page read by an agent that day; U = the fetcher could not read it. Source files: lane, verify, gaps (full names under Sources). Where verify disagrees with the lane, verify wins.

## Bottom line

- Estate-sale alerts are free and real. EstateSales.net ("Get Notified of Estate Sales For Free!") and EstateSales.org (radius 5 to 100 miles, weekday picker, online auction item emails) confirmed. Gaps adds EstateSale.com, a third aggregator under a different owner, with "FREE EMAIL NOTIFICATIONS". EstateSales.org and gsalr.com share one owner (Treasure Listings LLC), so they are one net.
- The lane had no garage-sale layer. Gaps found YardSaleSearch.com (41 sales around Fort Myers today, photos, "Alert me") and gsalr.com (free daily email with keyword highlights), plus OfferUp search alerts and Nextdoor For Sale & Free. All free, all manual, none scrapable.
- Online bidding with local pickup is the desk route to whole-house contents. CTBids: free account, "Bidding Starts at $1", Premier "$99/year" not needed. HiBid: watch list, notifications, mailing list; no fee shown, but no page says "free". Gulf Coast Auction House, Bonita Springs: "18% Buyer's Premium | 3% Discount for Cash Payments", previews Fri 10 to 6 and Sat 12 to 3. Verify corrected two counts: CTBids' own Cape Coral page shows four live auctions, not five, and EstateSales.org's six include one in-person sale.
- KCM Galleries is not premium-free. The lane's "NO BUYERS PREMIUM" was an EstateSales.net listing title. KCM's How to Bid page says bidders "pay any stated buyer's premium", set per auction. MaxSold's premium is 18% (gaps, help desk); SWFL activity still U.
- Charity thrift is the cheapest floor stock and the schedules hold. Goodwill SWFL VIP Perks: free, 5% back, a 50% color-tag discount each week from Sunday, "$1.99 the following Sunday & Monday" leftovers. Habitat ReStores: North Fort Myers and Bonita Springs Mon to Sat 10 to 5; East Naples Mon to Sat 9 to 6:30 with an inventory email. Hope Chest: four stores, Mon to Fri 9 to 5, Sat 9 to 4, newsletter. McGregor ReStore is "PERMANENTLY CLOSED".
- Flea markets: Fleamasters (Fri to Sun 9 to 5) and Flamingo Island (Fri to Sun 8 to 4) run Friday to Sunday. Ortiz does not: "Saturday 8AM-4PM Sunday 6AM-4PM", "Closed Monday-Friday to public". No site states free entry; Ortiz says "Free parking."
- Storage auctions are a gamble, not a habit. StorageTreasures is free; verify found the Pro prices (ProBidder $10.00 / Month, ProBidder Max $20.00 / Month, ProBusiness $30.00 / Month). Lockerfox charges 15% of the winning bid, $10.00 minimum. Early November was thin in Collier: no in-person estate sale inside Naples or Bonita in the snapshot; the action was Cape Coral online auctions. Re-check in January.

## Picks

Use now (free):
- EstateSales.net alerts at ZIP 33901 and 34102, 50 miles, two days ahead; EstateSales.org form, all seven days, "Online Auction Items" ticked; EstateSale.com notices at 33901.
- YardSaleSearch.com "Alert me" for Fort Myers; gsalr.com daily email with keywords (lamp, chandelier, barware, figurine, dress form); Craigslist garage-sale calendar Thursday night; OfferUp and Nextdoor search alerts within 25 miles.
- CTBids free account; HiBid account with Gulf Coast Auction House on the watch list; Invaluable account following Freedom Auction Company.
- Goodwill VIP Perks, Habitat Lee ReStore Rewards app, Habitat Collier email, Hope Chest newsletter, Allegro text or email list, Can't Take It With You list, Dovetails by-request list.

Pay when it earns it:
- CTBids Premier, "$99/year": when she wins several lots a month and pickup slots keep selling out.
- StorageTreasures ProBidder, "$10.00 / Month": only after one free unit buy has paid for itself.
- gsalr "Go Ad Free for $4.99" (one-time): only if she reads the daily email daily and the ads slow her down.

Skip:
- Lockerfox (15% fee, $10.00 minimum, 72-hour clear-out) and storage units as a routine.
- EBTH (Ohio pickup), AuctionNinja (no SWFL sellers), Bid13 and StorageAuctions.com (no Florida list read).
- Sarasota Estate Auction and Helmuth Stone as buying venues; catalogs for comps only.
- Fort Myers Auction unless its "Get Auction Notices" email shows a household estate.
- Any directory-listed company domain that redirects (AllCoast).

## Contradictions with earlier decisions

None. Nothing here argues against Crosslist Gold, rembg BiRefNet, Buffer Free, Canva Free, Instagram Edits, Claude Haiku drafts, Supabase, Pushover or fixed Etsy shipping classes. The sourcing feed holds alert emails and what Hollie types, not scraped listings.
