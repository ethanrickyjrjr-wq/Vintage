# Lane 11. Customer service automation: messaging, bookings, link pages and small workflows

Question: which tools automate buyer messages, replies, bookings, link pages and small workflows for a one-person vintage shop, and what do they cost today (2026-11-07)?

Legend: V = vendor page read 2026-11-07; U = unverified. "lane only" = verified by the lane sweep, not re-checked by the verify pass. Source column names the file (lane, verify, gaps) and the URL.

## Bottom line

- Etsy ships the message automation Hollie needs, free: temporary auto-replies ("from an hour to a maximum of five days"), weekly auto-replies, quick replies and a US-only writing assistant. Auto-replies "count as a reply towards your Star Seller messaging score" ("reply to 95% of first messages in a thread within 24 hours"). They are set on Etsy.com only, not in the Etsy Seller app. Verify confirmed every claim (V).
- Shopify's free layer covers the Shopify side once the store exists: Inbox ("Pricing Free"), Flow (free on Basic; Send HTTP Request needs Grow or above), Email ("up to 10,000 manual or automated emails for free") and pickup in store. The only cost is Basic at "$39 USD/mo" monthly or "$29 USD/mo" yearly (V).
- Bookings: Cal.com Free stays the pick, "no usage limits" and "Accept Stripe & PayPal payments" on free (V). Gaps adds three free backups that also take payment on free: zcal ("Fee on payments 3%"), TidyCal ("$29 once", "1% platform fee on Free and Lifetime") and YouCanBookMe ("30 bookings per month"). Calendly Free has no payments. For a Shopify-product booking, Appointo Free has unlimited bookings, but verify adds that Free shows Appointo branding.
- Small workflows: Zapier Free is "100 tasks per month", but Shopify is a Premium app and "Etsy has not yet built an integration on Zapier"; the 15-minute polling claim is U. n8n self-hosted Community edition is free "indefinitely" for "your own internal business purposes"; verify corrects Cloud Starter to "$20 /mo, billed annually", not 20€. Gaps adds Activepieces Free ("1,000 credits a month", "Unlimited flows"). Make stayed walled (U).
- Instagram DM automation was the lane's biggest hole. Instagram has built-in FAQ "automated responses" for free (V, feature). ManyChat says "Start Free" on Google Play but its price page is a 403 (U). Chatfuel has no free plan.
- Canned replies: Espanso is free (GPL-3) on the PC. Gaps adds iPhone Text Replacement (built in, iCloud sync) and Text Blaze Free in Chrome. TextExpander Free caps at "Active Snippets 5".
- Link page and forms: Linktree Free now; Carrd Pro Standard at "$19 / year" beats Linktree Starter ("$7 /mo Billed annually") once a custom domain matters. Beacons' "9% seller fees" apply on Free and Creator (verify). Tally gives "unlimited forms and submissions, completely free" with "Collect payments", so Typeform and the unverified Google Forms are unnecessary. Judge.me Free now adds "15,000 emails/mo free".

## Picks

Use now (free):
- Etsy Messages: weekly auto-reply for away hours, 8 to 12 quick replies, writing assistant on.
- Cal.com Free: one Stripe-paid event type for consults, one free Screen Test type. zcal, TidyCal or YouCanBookMe as backups.
- Espanso on the PC plus iPhone Text Replacement, same reply library.
- Linktree Free in the Instagram bio; Instagram's built-in FAQ automated responses.
- Tally for the Hire Hollie intake form, deposit via "Collect payments".
- Google Voice personal number for the shop phone; no scripted texts.
- Once Shopify is live: Inbox, Flow, Email, pickup in store, Judge.me Free.
- n8n Community (Fedora box) or Activepieces Free only if the dashboard needs glue Supabase does not cover.

Pay when it earns it:
- Appointo Pro "$14 / month": bookings must be Shopify products with reminders and calendar sync.
- Carrd Pro Standard "$19 / year": the bio page should sit on Hollie's own domain.
- Cal.com Teams "$12per user/month" yearly: branding removal only.
- ManyChat (price U): "link please" comments on drops outrun manual replies.
- Zapier Professional "Starting from $19.99 /month": a Shopify-to-outside automation Flow and n8n cannot do.
- Linktree Premium "$30 /mo Billed annually": Instagram auto-replies, after ManyChat's price is read.
- Quo Starter or Twilio: a business texting line, or the dashboard texts buyers.

Skip:
- Gorgias, Freshdesk, Chatfuel, paid Tidio, Help Scout, Crisp: team tools or no free plan.
- Sesami, BTA, Cowlendar, Tipo: Appointo Free has unlimited bookings.
- Acuity, Calendly Free, Koalendar, Zoho Bookings: no payments on free, or no free plan.
- Square Appointments: processing fees, unless Square is wanted for in-person card sales.
- TextExpander Free, Typeform, Jotform, Fillout, Pipedream, Stan Store, Make (U), ChatGPT Plus: a free pick already covers each.
- Beacons for selling: 9% fee on Free and Creator.
- Any browser bot on eBay or Facebook Marketplace: eBay's User Agreement bans "LLM-driven bots" without permission; the Marketplace API is partner-only.

## Contradictions with earlier decisions

- None direct. Two boundary notes and one open check.
- Claude Haiku drafts: Etsy's US writing assistant drafts buyer replies free inside Messages, and Shopify Inbox has a free AI agent. Haiku stays for listing drafts (handoff T6b), not buyer replies.
- Pushover: confirmed at "$4.99 USD one-time purchase on each platform". One constraint: Shopify Flow on Basic cannot Send HTTP Request, so Pushover alerts from Shopify events need the Shopify API, n8n or Activepieces, not Flow.
- Crosslist Gold (possible tension, not confirmed): eBay's User Agreement bans "automated means (including ... LLM-driven bots ...) to access our Services for any purpose, except with the prior express permission of eBay". None of the three files say how Crosslist reaches eBay. Confirm it posts through the eBay API.
- Buffer Free, Canva Free, Instagram Edits, rembg BiRefNet, Supabase, fixed Etsy shipping classes: nothing in these files argues against them.
