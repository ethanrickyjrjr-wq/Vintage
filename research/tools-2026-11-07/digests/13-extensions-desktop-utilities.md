# Lane 13: Browser extensions and desktop utilities that save a reseller time

Digest date 2026-11-07. Question: which Chrome or Edge extensions and small Windows utilities save a one-person vintage reseller time (comps, Etsy research, image identification, writing, text expansion, clipboard, screenshots and OCR, resizing, passwords, iPhone photos to a Windows PC)? Every Chrome extension also runs in Edge (V).

## Bottom line

- Comps: skip every eBay "sold comps" extension. eBay Product research (Terapeak) in Seller Hub is free for all sellers and covers "the last 3 years of eBay sales data" (verify: "free" is on the Seller Hub page, not the Terapeak page). The extensions are tiny or dropship-built and brush against eBay's scraper clause.
- Etsy research: the eRank extension with a free account (5 keyword lookups a day, 1 shop, 50 listings; updated Oct 23 2026). EverBee's free tier is thinner (10 keywords). Verify correction: EverBee's "$24.99 / month" and "$69 / month" are annual-billing rates charged as $299 and $828 a year; the monthly price is unreadable. Keywords Everywhere is general SEO on paid credits. Skip both.
- Identification: Google Lens is built into Chrome (right-click, "Search this tab with Google Lens"). Gaps find: Search by Image (open source, 300,000 users) puts Google, Bing, Yandex, Baidu and TinEye in one right-click menu. TinEye alone finds copies, not similar objects, and is "free for non-commercial use" only.
- Writing and typing: LanguageTool is free with no account. Gaps find: Harper, a free open-source checker that runs entirely in the browser (updated Nov 7 2026). Grammarly is not needed (its company is now Superhuman). Espanso (free, GPL-3) for desktop boilerplate; Text Blaze Free if snippets must fill listing forms. Win+H voice typing drafts descriptions free.
- Windows built-ins: PowerToys (Image Resizer, Text Extractor, PowerRename, Quick Accent, plus Advanced Paste, which the lane missed), Snipping Tool with local OCR, Win+V clipboard. Verify correction: Win+V holds 25 entries and clears on restart except pinned items; Ditto covers that. Gaps find: Caesium (free, GPL-3) batch-compresses a shoot with quality control, which PowerToys cannot.
- Passwords: Bitwarden Free ("Unlimited devices, unlimited passwords"). Gaps find: Apple's iCloud Passwords extension rides on iCloud for Windows if passwords already live on the iPhone. 1Password has no free tier.
- iPhone photos to PC: iCloud for Windows is the documented full-resolution path (Apple: Windows 11 or later; 5 GB free; 200 GB "$2.99" billed monthly). Gaps find: Windows needs Microsoft's free HEIF Image Extension to open HEIC originals, or set the camera to Most Compatible; HEVC video is "$0.99". LocalSend for same-day batches. Phone Link's iPhone photo support is unreadable (U). Snapdrop, Intel Unison and Edge Drop are gone.

## Picks

Use now (free): PowerToys with Advanced Paste on; Snipping Tool; Win+V; Bitwarden Free (every marketplace, Supabase and Anthropic key from day one); LanguageTool or Harper, one of them; eRank extension with a free account; Google Lens plus Search by Image; Terapeak in Seller Hub; iCloud for Windows with iCloud Photos on; HEIF Image Extension; LocalSend; USB cable as fallback; Caesium; Win+H voice typing; Espanso once ten listings of repeated phrases exist.

Pay when it earns it: iCloud+ 200 GB at "$2.99" billed monthly, when the free 5 GB fills (fast). eRank Basic "$5.99/mo" when 5 lookups a day block a listing push. Bitwarden Premium "$19.80" billed annually when she wants TOTP codes in the vault. HEVC Video Extensions "$0.99" the first time an iPhone video will not play. Text Blaze Pro "$2.99 USD per month, billed yearly" when the free snippet cap bites. TextExpander Pro "$60" billed annually only if snippets must sync to the iPhone. Microsoft 365 Basic "$19.99 /year" only as an alternative to iCloud+.

Skip: eBay comps extensions (Terapeak is free and official); EverBee and Keywords Everywhere (volume-seller research); TinEye alone (copies, non-commercial); Grammarly (redundant); 1Password (no free tier); Phone Link for photos (unverified); Google Photos (no Windows sync client); Dropbox (2 GB is one shoot); Snapdrop, Intel Unison, Edge Drop, Magical (gone); iMazing, CopyTrans, Send Anywhere (nothing free lacks); IrfanView and XnView without a licence.

## Contradictions with earlier decisions

- Crosslist Gold (trial): the verify file refutes the "trial" framing. Crosslist's "20 free listings" is "a 3-day money-back guarantee on your first purchase, as long as you've created fewer than 20 listings" ("whichever comes first"); a card is required and "For annual plans, a small $9.99 processing fee applies". Gold is still "$39.99 per month" (unchanged since 2026-10-26) and a Diamond tier at "$44.99 per month" exists. Plan on paying $39.99 up front and testing inside 3 days or 20 listings, not on a free trial. The extension is actively maintained (v3.12.7, Nov 6 2026), which supports the vendor choice.
- rembg BiRefNet, Buffer Free, Canva Free, Instagram Edits, Claude Haiku drafts, Supabase, Pushover, fixed Etsy shipping classes: none. Harper and LanguageTool check text; they do not replace Haiku drafting. The Photoroom extension listing would not render (U).
