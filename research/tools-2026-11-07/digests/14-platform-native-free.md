# Lane 14: Platform-native free tools (Etsy, eBay, Shopify)

Question: which built-in, free tools inside Etsy, eBay and Shopify already do the job for a one-person vintage shop, and what do the platforms themselves cost today (checked 2026-11-07)?

## Bottom line

- Etsy's own stack covers almost the whole job for free: Seller app, Share & Save, discounts and offers, Stats, Search Visibility, Marketplace Insights (15 free keyword searches a week), two silent videos per listing, Purchase Protection to $250, bulk edit, five helpers, plus two the gaps pass added: USPS and FedEx labels with $100 insurance, and Star Seller. Fees: $0.20 per listing, 6.5% of the total order amount including shipping, 3% + 0.25 USD processing; verify confirmed every figure. One unknown: a "one-time, non-refundable fee for opening your shop", amount not printed anywhere read.
- Share & Save is the day-one switch: "an instant refund of 4% off the eligible order total", 6.5% back from October 20 to January 4. Links in Etsy Messages or listing descriptions do not count; an Offsite Ad last click overrides. Decide Offsite Ads before the first sale: 15% per attributed order, capped at $100 USD, optional under $10,000, mandatory for life above it.
- eBay runs free with no Store: 250 listings a month then $0.35, 13.6% final value fee, per-order fee $0.30 up to $10 and $0.40 above (verify corrected the lane's flat $0.40). Seller Hub, Product Research, Best Offer, Offers to Buyers, eBay International Shipping, eBay Labels with ShipCover and freight rate tables are free. Verify refuted the lane's $1,500 Store break-even: the Basic Store ($21.95 yearly billing) pays off at about $2,440 a month of Antiques and Art sales, or on any single piece over $2,500.
- Shopify Basic is the only recurring cost: $29 USD/mo paid yearly, $39 USD/mo monthly, after "3 days free, then $1/month for 3 months". Included: Sidekick, 10,000 free emails a month, Flow, Inbox, Forms, Search & Discovery, Shopify Tax free to $100,000 USD lifetime sales, POS basics, agentic storefronts. The Starter comparison is dropped: Starter is not on shopify.com/pricing (verify).
- Sales tax needs no tool on the marketplaces: Etsy and eBay collect and remit at no charge. eBay's state list names Florida; Etsy's was collapsed (U). Shopify Tax covers the own store.
- Shopify Marketplace Connect (formerly Codisto) is a free Shopify-to-eBay bridge (50 synced orders a month free, then 1% capped at $99/month) but takes no new Etsy connections, so it does not replace Crosslist.
- Defunct or moved: Linkpop, eBay Collective, Shopify Starter (off the pricing page), Lite (legacy name). Etsy Pattern is alive at $15.00 USD per month after a 30-day trial.

## Picks

Use now (free)
- Etsy: Share & Save link in every bio and pinned comment; list from the Seller app, one unedited photo per listing; Make an Offer (web) plus favorites and abandoned-cart targeted offers; two videos per listing; Marketplace Insights and Search Visibility instead of a paid keyword tool; every label bought inside Etsy, calculated shipping where a USPS box fits; Star Seller rules from day one.
- eBay: plain account, no Store; Seller Hub, Product Research for comps, Best Offer on every listing, Offers to Buyers, 24 photos and a video, eBay Labels with ShipCover above $100, freight rate tables for chairs; leave eBay International Shipping on.
- Let both marketplaces collect sales tax; leave Etsy's AI-assistant purchasing and Shopify agentic storefronts on.

Pay when it earns it
- Shopify Basic, $29 USD/mo paid yearly: when the own site is ready to take checkout.
- eBay Basic Store, $21.95 yearly billing: when eBay Antiques and Art sales pass about $2,440 a month, or a piece over $2,500 is listed.
- eBay Promoted Listings General, fixed 3% to 5% on slow pieces: once Above Standard; never dynamic without a cap.
- Shopify Grow, $79 USD/mo paid yearly: only for the free $200 shipping insurance, Flow's Send HTTP Request (Pushover, Supabase) or staff accounts.
- Shopify Marketplace Connect: free under 50 synced orders a month; the Shopify-to-eBay bridge once the store is live.
- Etsy Pattern, $15.00 USD per month: only if the Shopify store is delayed.

Skip
- Etsy Plus, $10 USD per month: 15 listing credits, $5 USD ads credit, restock requests useless at quantity 1.
- Etsy Ads beyond $1 to $3 a day tests: 15-day wait; quantity-1 listings vanish before the ad learns.
- eBay Promoted Offsite: pay per click, monthly cap 30.4 times the daily budget, no sale guarantee.
- Shopify Agentic $0/mo plan as a site substitute: no storefront, direct checkout off by default.
- Shopify Starter, Collabs, eBay Live, Linkpop: unverified, irrelevant, beta, gone.

## Contradictions with earlier decisions

None refuted outright. Five findings tune a decision:
- Crosslist Gold: Shopify Marketplace Connect is a free native crosslister to eBay, Amazon, Walmart and Target Plus, but it runs Shopify-to-eBay, opposite to the Etsy-first fan-out, and "New connections to Etsy can't be made at this time" (gaps). A free eBay leg later, not a replacement.
- rembg BiRefNet: "Etsy requires at least one unedited photo for most items"; the first image need not be it (lane, verify). Cutouts stay allowed in every other slot. Sidekick and the eBay app offer free background removal, fallbacks rather than reasons to drop rembg.
- Claude Haiku drafts: Etsy is "testing a new form that suggests titles, tags, attributes" with a limited number of sellers, the eBay app fills item details from a photo, Sidekick drafts descriptions at no additional cost (lane). Free but uncontrolled; Haiku stays the controlled path.
- Pushover and Supabase: Shopify Flow is free on Basic, but Send HTTP Request needs Grow ($79 USD/mo yearly), so Flow cannot call Pushover or Supabase from Basic (lane). Alerts from Shopify events need Grow or a webhook of our own.
- Fixed Etsy shipping classes: "US listings with domestic shipping prices lower than $6 will be prioritized in search" (lane, verify); calculated shipping rejects furniture (gaps). Keep fixed classes, set the smallest under $6 or free, price chairs by hand.
- Buffer Free, Canva Free, Instagram Edits: nothing in these files touches them.
