# Lane 08: Marketing channels (marketing tools and extra selling channels)

Question: which marketing tools and extra selling channels bring buyers to one-of-one vintage home decor, what do they cost, and which are free? V = vendor page read 2026-11-07; U = page or figure unreadable. Where the verify file disagrees with the lane, the verify figure is used.

## Bottom line

- Cheapest buyer acquisition is inside Etsy. Share & Save refunds "4% off the eligible order total" on sales from Hollie's own `yourshopname.etsy.com` link, and "6.5% back" from October 20 to January 4. Verify confirmed. Links in Etsy Messages or listing descriptions do not count; an Offsite Ad last click overrides the credit.
- Offsite Ads cost nothing until an attributed sale, then 15% (12% at $10,000 or more), capped at $100 per order. Opt-out is allowed under $10,000. Leave it on for 60 days, then read the attribution.
- Email: Shopify Messaging (was Shopify Email) gives 10,000 emails a month free, then $1 per 1,000. Klaviyo, Omnisend and Mailchimp free tiers stop at 250 contacts. Verify corrected Omnisend: "$11.20/mo" needs 3 months upfront; list is $16/mo. Mailchimp paid prices now read ($13, $20, $350 a month). Before Shopify, Brevo Free sends "up to 300 emails per day". Judge.me now adds "15,000 emails/mo free".
- Once Shopify is live, six channels need no spend: Shop channel, Google & YouTube free listings, Microsoft Channel (gaps find), Pinterest app, Facebook & Instagram by Meta (Hollie handles sales tax since August 26, 2025) and Judge.me Free. Shopify Marketplace Connect (formerly Codisto) syncs Shopify to eBay free for 50 orders a month, but has no Etsy.
- Extra marketplaces: Chairish (30% at 10+ listings, free membership, arranges shipping, pays via PayPal, web plus iPhone and iPad app), Mercari (10% of item plus buyer-paid shipping; buyers pay 3.6% on top), Whatnot (≤8% plus 2.9% + $0.30 on the total order) and the gaps find Palmstreet (6.9% plus 2.9% + $0.3, plant-centric, cheapest live fee). Poshmark's 20% and $2.95 were not readable today (U).
- Local: the lane's Vintage Market Days URL is dead. The market is now "South Gulf Coast Florida", January 29 to 31, 2027, Lee Civic Center, Fort Myers, free juried vendor profile. Craigslist is $5 per by-dealer post. Nextdoor and Yelp pages are free. Google Business Profile fits Hire Hollie as a service-area business only.
- Still unreadable: Facebook Marketplace fees, Ruby Lane fees, TikTok Shop fees and vintage policy, Whatnot's US tier table, EBTH commission.

## Picks

**Use now (free).** Share & Save link in every bio, pin and email. Offsite Ads left on. Pinterest business account today. Linktree Free. QRCode Monkey static code for the disco-ball tag. Nextdoor Business Page. Yelp page and a service-area Google Business Profile for Hire Hollie only. Vintage Market Days vendor profile. Mercari for pieces under $200. Facebook Marketplace by hand for local pickup. Chairish once 10 pieces over about $300 can be live. At Shopify launch: Shopify Messaging, Shop channel, Google & YouTube, Microsoft Channel, Pinterest app, Meta channel after tax settings, Judge.me Free, Marketplace Connect.

**Pay when it earns it.** Etsy Ads: small daily budget on the five best-photographed listings for two weeks; keep only if attributed revenue is at least 5x spend. eBay Promoted General when eBay opens. Shop Campaigns after the first 20 Shopify orders. Judge.me Awesome ($15 per month) when reviews should show in Google Shopping. Chairish Premium ($49 / Month) if Chairish sales average more than $1,000 a month. Whatnot or Palmstreet: one show when 20 to 40 small pieces are in stock. Craigslist $5 for a piece too big to ship. One flea-market day as a content day.

**Skip.** Klaviyo, Omnisend, Mailchimp (tiny free tiers; Shopify Messaging is free). Linktree paid tiers (free is enough). Shopify Collabs in v1 (Open Access on by default). Everything in the Dropped line, for the reasons given there.

## Contradictions with earlier decisions

- Crosslist Gold (trial), partial. Shopify Marketplace Connect is "Free to install", "First 50 marketplace-synced orders/mo free", then 1% per synced order "capped at $99/month", and lists on eBay, Amazon, Walmart and Target Plus. No Etsy, and Phase 1 lists on Etsy first, so it does not replace Crosslist. It does argue against paying for Crosslist once Shopify exists if Etsy is handled another way. Gaps file: "compare it against Crosslist Gold's $39.99 when the Shopify store exists."
- Buffer Free, none. Verify confirms "Connect up to 3 channels" and "10 scheduled posts per channel" on Free. Its correction is to the paid tier: "$5 /month" is "$60 billed yearly"; the monthly price did not render (U).
- Fixed Etsy shipping classes, none, with a note. Mercari's 10% and Palmstreet's 6.9% also apply to buyer-paid shipping, and Chairish organizes shipping itself, so an Etsy class price copied to those channels loses the fee share.
- Canva Free (QR generator confirmed free), rembg BiRefNet, Instagram Edits, Claude Haiku drafts, Supabase, Pushover: nothing argues against them.
