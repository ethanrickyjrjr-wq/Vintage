# Lane 02: AI listing writers and Etsy/eBay SEO tools

Question answered: which AI writers and keyword tools produce better titles, tags and descriptions for Etsy, eBay and Shopify listings of one-of-one vintage pieces, and what do they cost?

Files: lane report (L), verify file (Vf), gaps file (G); all three exist. Vf re-read 23 pages and refuted no price. Its caveats are applied below. G tools were not re-checked by Vf, so their V or U is the gaps agent's own.

## Bottom line

- First-party data is the best free keyword source. Etsy Marketplace Insights: "Sellers can explore up to 15 keyword searches per week for free." Etsy Stats shows the terms that brought shoppers in. eBay Product research (Terapeak) gives three years of sold data to all Seller Hub sellers; the page names no fee but never says "free" (Vf). All V.
- Two third-party Etsy tools have real free tiers: eRank Free ("5 keyword lookups/day", "1 AI Listing Helper use/day") and Alura Free ("5 daily keyword & product searches", "AI listing optimization (about 20)", one-time). G adds EHunt, the renamed EtsyHunt: "Keyword search 5 times/day" free. V.
- For eBay titles G found 3Dsellers' free eBay Title Builder ("Keyword scores and competitor titles are pulled from eBay live") and Keyword Tool Dominator's eBay autocomplete at "$9 USD One payment · 30 Days access". V.
- The cheapest purpose-built writer is already in the stack. Crosslist AI add-on: "+$4.99/month", "Consumes 1 credit per request", "1000 AI credits per month" on Gold. Gold is still "$39.99 per month" (Vf). V.
- Free chatbots cover drafting until the Haiku dashboard exists: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, plus Microsoft Copilot's free Windows app (G). OpenAI: "We plan to begin testing ads in the free tier and ChatGPT Go in the US soon" (Vf). Haiku 5.5 input is "$0.10 / MTok for prompts up to 100,000 tokens", then "$0.50 / MTok" (Vf). V.
- Vf corrections in the table: Vendoo's monthly-billed prices are on the page ("$14.99 Per month, billed monthly", $29.99, $59.99); EverBee's $24.99 is "billed annually at $299", monthly unquoted; the Etsy Search Visibility page says "title recommendations", never "AI"; Pinterest Trends gates only own-audience features behind a business account.
- Paid Etsy-only writers exist (Lysterly "$19.99/mo ... Up to 30 Monthly Generations"; Vela "$29.95 / Per shop Per month"). No free plans. Neither beats a free chatbot with a saved prompt at a few one-offs a week.

## Picks

Use now (free):
- Etsy Marketplace Insights, Etsy Stats and the Search Visibility title tool.
- eRank Free and EHunt Free for Etsy keywords; Alura Free for a second opinion on rewrites.
- eBay Product research in Seller Hub for sold-title vocabulary; 3Dsellers' free eBay Title Builder for every eBay title; eBay's in-app AI description, reviewed line by line.
- One free chatbot (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot) with the saved prompt: title under 15 words, item first, 13 tags of 20 characters or fewer, honest description with measurements and condition.
- Google Trends and Pinterest Trends for timing. Insight Factory's 15 free credits for one tag-harvest session.

Pay when it earns it (trigger):
- Crosslist AI add-on, "+$4.99/month": when the Gold trial proves out and listing pace justifies 1 credit per item.
- eRank Basic "$5.99/mo" or Koalanda "$5.99 / month Billed every year as $71.88 USD": when 5 free lookups a day run out.
- Etsy Plus "$10 USD per month": when 15 Marketplace Insights searches a week is a real limit.
- Keyword Tool Dominator Plus "$9 USD One payment · 30 Days access": when a batch of eBay listings is ready and Terapeak titles are not enough.
- Lysterly Starter "$19.99/mo": only if chatbot drafts keep needing rewrites before the Haiku dashboard; cancel when it ships.
- SEOWILL Pro "$29.99 / month": once the Shopify store exists and the free 30 credits run short.

Skip (reason):
- Marmalead, Sale Samurai: no free tier; free tools cover keywords.
- EverBee: sales estimates for products to copy; one-of-one stock gains nothing; $24.99 is the annual rate.
- Vela: per-shop bulk editor.
- Vendoo, List Perfectly, OneShop, PrimeLister, 3Dsellers paid: crosslisters; lane 01 chose Crosslist Gold.
- ZIK, Nifty: dropship or Poshmark automation.
- Copy.ai, Jasper, Hypotenuse, Hammoq, Describely: team, enterprise or catalog pricing.
- Keywords Everywhere, Google Keyword Planner, Ubersuggest: Google only.
- EtsyGenerator, title-builder.com: hobby or possibly unmaintained builds.
- LM Studio, Ollama, Microsoft 365 Premium: privacy or Office, not listing quality.

## Contradictions with earlier decisions

- Crosslist Gold (trial): no file argues to drop it. Vf confirms "Gold $39.99 per month". Price context only: Vendoo Growth bundles AI Listing Enhancement at "$24.99 Per month, billed yearly" or "$29.99 Per month, billed monthly" (Vf), and List Perfectly Simple is "$29 Monthly" with "AI Generated Listings 25 / mo." (G), both under Gold plus add-on ($39.99 + $4.99). Both files leave the crosslister choice to lane 01.
- Claude Haiku drafts: no file argues against. Vf adds the over-100k-token rate ("$0.50 / MTok") and says prompts will stay far under it. G says of LM Studio and Ollama "do not re-plan it". Overlap worth knowing: the Crosslist AI add-on does the same photo-to-draft job for +$4.99/month, so once the dashboard ships one of the two is redundant.
- rembg BiRefNet, Buffer Free, Canva Free, Instagram Edits, Supabase, Pushover, fixed Etsy shipping classes: nothing in these files touches them. None.
