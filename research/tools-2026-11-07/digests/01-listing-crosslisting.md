# Lane 01: Listing and crosslisting

Question: which tools make creating a listing and posting it to Etsy, eBay and Shopify faster for one person, and what do they cost (2026-11-07)? The verify file wins over the lane. V = vendor page read; U = not readable.

## Bottom line

- Prices the repo leans on hold. Crosslist Gold is "$39.99 per month", AI is "+$4.99/month", and the "trial" is "20 free listings with our 3-day money-back guarantee" ("whichever comes first"), not a free trial. Autodelisting is Gold and Diamond only. Yearly tab (verify): Gold "$32.99 per month billed yearly".
- Cheaper crosslisters cover all three channels with sale detection on the entry plan. Vendoo Starter: "$12.49 Per month, billed yearly" or "$14.99 Per month, billed monthly" (verify), but auto-delist is "for Poshmark, eBay, Mercari, Depop, Whatnot, and Etsy", not Shopify. Voolist Starter: "$19.99 / month Billed monthly", auto-delist on every plan. FLUF Starter: "$9 /month", "Sales checked every 90 min".
- Best gaps finds. Secnd: "Free $0 CAD/mo Free forever" with "Inventory sync + automatic delisting", Etsy and Shopify API notices, eBay only in examples, 17 ratings. Swiftlisted: "Starter $6 USD/mo $72 USD billed yearly", 30-day trial with no card, eBay and Shopify by API, Etsy through a Chrome extension on a PC that must be on, 0 ratings. VendorSnap: Pro "$6.67/month Billed yearly at $79.99", "eBay and Etsy list through their official APIs" from the phone, no Shopify.
- Free built-ins improved. eBay app: "Create a listing in a snap with the help of AI"; Seller Hub: "Multiple listings from photos". Etsy Seller app lists from the phone; no bulk upload ("you can copy listings"). Shopify Sidekick writes descriptions; "availability can vary by plan, device, and language".
- Stock sync is a separate layer once the store exists. QuickSync: "Free: $0, 100-variant stock sync for eligible shops" (Shopify and Etsy). Trunk ("From $35/month") is the only real-time Shopify, Etsy and eBay quantity sync read; it creates no listings.
- Verify refuted two lane rows. PrimeLister is public at /pricing/cross-listing: "$49.99 Per Month" or "$399.99 Per Year", the dearest monthly plan here. Zipsale Free is "10 items per month", not 30, and "Currently, Zipsale is made to be used in the UK". Drop it.
- Terms: eBay bans "any robot, spider, scraper ... or other automated means" without permission, so prefer API posting (Crosslist is "API-first"; Voolist, Nifty, SellerAider and Secnd print Etsy API notices). Facebook Marketplace is always extension-driven. inkFrog redirects to Wix; Reeva cannot be found.

## Picks

Use now (free):
- Etsy Seller app for photos and first drafts; Etsy web for bulk edits and copy-listing on near-identical pieces.
- eBay app AI listing from photos; Seller Hub "Multiple listings from photos" on the PC.
- Shopify Sidekick for product descriptions once the store exists.
- Secnd Free, tested on two cheap items: end one on eBay, watch the Etsy and Shopify copies.
- Swiftlisted 30-day trial (no card), run against Secnd on the same items.
- VendorSnap Free, then the 7-day Pro trial, for phone listing to Etsy and eBay.
- QuickSync's free 100-variant Shopify-to-Etsy stock sync when the store exists.

Pay when it earns it:
- Swiftlisted Starter ("$6 USD/mo $72 USD billed yearly") if it wins the trial.
- Vendoo Starter ("$12.49 Per month, billed yearly" or "$14.99 Per month, billed monthly") once hand-listing across three sites hurts; Shopify sales need a manual mark-sold.
- Voolist Starter ("$19.99 / month Billed monthly") if Shopify auto-delist or monthly billing matters more.
- FLUF Starter ("$9 /month") if Vendoo's app or price disappoints.
- Crosslist Gold ("$39.99 per month", AI "+$4.99/month") only after Vendoo, FLUF and Swiftlisted fail; its window is 3 days.
- Shuttle Growth ("$19 / month") or shopUpz UNLIMITED ("$18 / month") if the shop is not eligible for QuickSync's free tier.
- Trunk ("From $35/month") only after a crosslister misses a sale on a one-of-one item.
- Sellbrite Pro 100 ("$19 / month") only if Shopify is the master catalog and the free 2-hour sync caused a double sale.

Skip (reason): PrimeLister ("$49.99 Per Month"); List Perfectly (sale detection $69, auto delist $99+); Nifty, Posh Sidekick, Flip Press, EzList (no Shopify); SellerAider (auto-delist needs the $29.99 Pro); OneShop (no marketplace list); Hero (eBay and Facebook only); SellRaze (marketplaces unnamed); ExportYourStore (2-hour sync, yearly); SixBit (Shopify at $99.99); stallkit (Etsy-only); Marketplace Connect (no Etsy).

## Contradictions with earlier decisions

- Crosslist Gold (trial). Three findings argue against it as the first paid step. It is not a free trial: "20 free listings with our 3-day money-back guarantee", "whichever comes first"; annual refunds carry "a small $9.99 processing fee". Autodelisting is Gold-only and AI is "+$4.99/month", so Gold with AI is $44.98; only PrimeLister ($49.99) costs more. Five tools cover all three channels with sale detection on the entry plan for less: Secnd "Free $0 CAD/mo", Swiftlisted "$6 USD/mo ... billed yearly", FLUF "$9 /month", Vendoo "$12.49 Per month, billed yearly" (no Shopify auto-delist), Voolist "$19.99 / month Billed monthly". Softening fact: Gold is "$32.99 per month billed yearly".
- Claude Haiku drafts. Not refuted, but partly duplicated: eBay's app and Seller Hub write listings from photos free, Shopify Sidekick writes descriptions, and every pick bundles an AI writer (Voolist "AI listings a month 25", FLUF "50 / mo", Swiftlisted 150, Secnd 5 on Free). Etsy has no AI writer, so a Haiku draft keeps its job there.
- rembg BiRefNet, Buffer Free, Canva Free, Instagram Edits, Supabase, Pushover, fixed Etsy shipping classes: none. Etsy bulk edit covers "Shipping profiles", which supports the fixed classes.
