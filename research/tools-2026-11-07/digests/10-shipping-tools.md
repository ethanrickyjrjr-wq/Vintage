# Lane 10. Shipping tools, label apps, freight and insurance

Question answered: which shipping tools, label apps and services make shipping lamps, art, glass and small furniture cheaper and easier for one person in Southwest Florida, and what do they cost? Pages read 2026-11-07. V = vendor page read that day; U = not readable or not stated on any page that loaded. Where the verify file disagrees with the lane, the verify file wins.

## Bottom line

- Pirate Ship is the primary label tool: "No monthly fees"; "we're paid by the carriers, not by you!"; "No credit card required". Native Etsy, eBay and Shopify integrations. USPS (including Cubic) and UPS; no FedEx. InsureShield is "$1.00 for every $100 of declared value" and nullifies the carrier's included $100. All confirmed by the verify file.
- Etsy orders: buy the label inside Etsy. No membership fee, the label "helps you qualify for Etsy Purchase Protection", and the Shipsurance add-on is "$0.80 per $100 of order value" domestic ("$1.35 per $100" international). eBay orders: eBay Labels, "no subscription fees", USPS, FedEx or UPS, QR code printed at the counter.
- Best gaps find: ShipSaver, Shipsurance's pay-as-you-go storefront. "No monthly fees (pay-as-you-go)"; UPS and FedEx "$0.75" per $100 up to $5,000; USPS "$0.65" up to $50 then "$1.00" per additional $100; "Integrates with Shopify, Etsy, eBay, and BigCommerce". It covers the eBay and Shopify orders Etsy's add-on cannot reach and undercuts InsureShield on UPS.
- Second gaps find: ShipSaving Starter, "Free/ month", "Up to 50 labels/month", USPS, UPS, FedEx and DHL Express, an iPhone app and QR codes: the FedEx backup Pirate Ship lacks. USPS Label Broker prints a QR-code label at the Post Office, so Hollie can ship before a printer arrives. USPS Web Tools and Addresses 3.0 are "free of charge", so Smarty ("$552 per year") is out.
- Hardware: Munbyn RW403B "$79.99" and the IPS03 440 lb scale "$39.99" are sale prices against struck list prices of "$159.99" and "$49.99"; Munbyn's phone and Mac claim is U. USPS Priority Mail Shoe Box and Medium Cube-Shaped Box are "$0.00".
- Verify corrections: Lugg still has nothing in Lee or Collier County, but Florida shows 26 cities and sample totals run $68 to $305, not $55 to $335. Rollo Ship after 200 free labels is "5¢ each, as low as 1¢ at VIP". ShipStation has no free plan; chatbot-only support is Stamps.com Basic. The eBay International Shipping caps (48 in, 84 in length plus girth, 44 lb) are U. Shippo's "$17/mo" is annual billing only; address validation is "9¢/validation" on Starter, not free.
- Shopify Basic ("$29 USD/mo" yearly, "$39 USD/mo" monthly) includes labels and "Up to 87% off"; the $200 free insurance needs Grow ("$79" yearly) plus Shopify Payments, so do not upgrade for it. Large pieces: GoShare locally (Fort Myers, Cape Coral and Naples listed), The UPS Store Pack & Ship Guarantee for a high-value chandelier, TSI or Freightquote per out-of-state piece. Chairish-Managed Shipping (buyer pays from "$11" parcel or "$149" In-Home) is a channel decision, not a tool.

## Picks

Use now (free): Pirate Ship as primary, InsureShield on non-Etsy items over $100. Etsy labels plus Shipsurance on Etsy orders. eBay Labels on eBay orders. ShipSaving Starter as the FedEx and DHL backup with a phone app. ShipSaver for eBay and Shopify orders over $100. USPS free Priority Mail boxes. USPS Label Broker until the printer arrives. GoShare for local runs. One-time hardware: Munbyn RW403B "$79.99" and IPS03 "$39.99" while the sale holds.

Pay when it earns it: Shopify Basic "$29 USD/mo" yearly (trigger: store launch). The UPS Store Pack & Ship (trigger: a high-value chandelier or glass art going far). TSI, Freightquote or FreightCenter (trigger: first out-of-state chair or oversized canvas; quote before listing). Route "$0 /month" and FedEx Ship Manager (trigger: Shopify live; FedEx account for the latter). Boxify "From $19/month" (trigger: flat-rate shipping on lamps and chairs loses money). ShipStation Starter "$14.99 /month" (trigger: LTL booking inside one tool past 50 shipments a month). ShipStation Shipping API free plan or EasyPost (trigger: the dashboard's label code).

Skip: Stamps.com (10 labels on Basic). Shippo (30 free labels, below ShipSaving's 50; "$17/mo" is annual only). Veeqo (Etsy absent; Amazon-owned). Descartes XPS Ship (duplicates ShipSaving). eBay ShipCover (rates unreadable; pairs paid partially). Everything in the Dropped line, for the reason given there.

## Contradictions with earlier decisions

None. Nothing in the three files argues against Crosslist Gold, rembg BiRefNet, Buffer Free, Canva Free, Instagram Edits, Claude Haiku drafts, Supabase or Pushover. Two facts bear on the fixed Etsy shipping classes without refuting them. Etsy's USPS label page: "Starting April 26, USPS is implementing a temporary 8% rate increase for select shipping services, including Priority Mail, Priority Mail Express, USPS Ground Advantage, and Parcel Select. The increase is expected to last through January 17, 2027." Class prices set now carry that 8%; re-check them in January 2027. Same page: "USPS charges an additional $4 fee" for "Cylindrical rolls" and "Packaging or cans made of wood or metal"; Etsy's UPS page: "UPS may adjust the final cost of your label after delivery" when measurements differ. Rolled prints and crated pieces need their own class or a buffer. Adjacent, for the Shopify cost line only: "Over 50 synced marketplace orders 1% up to $99/mo".
