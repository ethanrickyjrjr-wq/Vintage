# 05-identification: Identification and authentication tools

Question: which tools identify a vintage piece (maker, era, pattern, material) and check authenticity, and what do they cost? Digest date 2026-11-07. V = vendor page read by an agent; U = the fetchers could not read it. "lane", "verify" and "gaps" are the three source files listed at the end.

## Bottom line

- The free backbone holds. The verify agent re-read Google Lens, Bing Visual Search, Kovels' marks guide pages, thepotteries.org, porcelainmarksandmore.com, silvercollection.it, 20thcenturyglass.com, the VFG Label Resource, the FTC RN database and Collectors Weekly. All free. Lens gives the first guess; marks and labels are the proof.
- Correction to the lane: Kovels is not free-only. kovels.com/subscriptions redirects to an offers page: "Kovels All Access Online $59.99 per year" and "Kovels All Access + Print 79.99 per year". Kovels and Antique Trader have merged under Active Interest Media. The free guide pages and the free Kovels Komments newsletter are unchanged.
- Correction to the lane: WorthPoint's prices are on Apple's own App Store listing, not only a third-party tracker: MARKS Premium Monthly $19.99, MARKS Premium Annual $99.99, Price Guide Monthly $26.99, Price Guide + MARKS Monthly $37.99. worthpoint.com/pricing still returns 403. The app rates 2.1 from 117 ratings with login lockout complaints.
- Best gaps find: pay-per-item human appraisal, a category the lane missed. Appraisily identifies for free and sells a working value for $9. Mearto charges $25 an item. ValueMyStuff charges $28 a credit. For the chandelier, a painting or an unmarked lamp this is the cheapest expert opinion.
- Second gaps find: art signature databases. FindArtInfo has a free account, a $4.95 day pass, $17.95 a month and 450,000+ signatures. Art Signature Dictionary is 7 days free, then $12/month.
- Elvis photo correction: it carries two signatures (Scotty Moore, D.J. Fontana), so PSA/DNA's "2 Signatures" column applies, $20 to $500 priced off the premier signer, not the single-signed $10 tier. Premium Letter upgrade $15 under $50. A declared value of $1,000 or more adds $40 and up. About 30 business days. Certifying the print itself is a separate Original Photograph order, $50 for $999 or less.
- Skip the AI "antique identifier" apps: weekly billing from $4.99 to $9.99 a week, zero to a few ratings, reviews saying "Lens is better and faster". The new Antiqly for Chrome (version 1.0.0, October 19, 2026, 4 users) is $7.99 per week or $39.99 per year with no trial. Claude, Gemini and Copilot free tiers are fine for turning a photo into search vocabulary; never cite them as attribution. TinEye is "free for non-commercial use" only, and a shop is commercial.

## Picks

Use now (free): Google Lens on the iPhone and in Chrome; Bing Visual Search on the PC; Kovels marks guide pages and the Kovels Komments newsletter; a "Marks" bookmark folder with thepotteries.org, porcelainmarksandmore.com, silvercollection.it, 20thcenturyglass.com and The Marks Project; VFG Label Resource plus rn.ftc.gov for every garment; Antiquers.com and Collectors Weekly accounts for the pieces nothing else names; Appraisily's free identification; a free FindArtInfo account for the framed art; the Eames, Stiffel and Frederick Cooper label guides; Claude, Gemini or Copilot free tiers for vocabulary only.

Pay when it earns it: Appraisily $9 when an asking price depends on a value. Mearto $25 or ValueMyStuff $28 for one piece whose price depends on the maker (the chandelier, the Gehry-attributed chair, a painting); one sale covers it. FindArtInfo $4.95 day pass, checking every signed piece in one sitting. Art Signature Dictionary $12/month only if FindArtInfo fails; cancel inside the 7 free days. Kovels All Access Online $59.99 per year once the shop lists weekly; trial first. WorthPoint MARKS Premium $19.99 monthly after the free trial, when unidentified marks block listings weekly and the web price is confirmed. PSA/DNA for the Elvis photo: the 2 Signatures tier plus $15 for the Premium Letter if under $50; call JSA's Florida office as a price check. Real Authentication $30 plus $20 Identification plus $10 Age only if a designer piece appears.

Skip: Antiqly for Chrome and every AI identifier app (weekly billing, few or no free scans, accuracy and cancellation complaints; Lens is free). Entrupy (over budget, wrong stock). LegitGrails, Legit App and Legit Check unless a designer label appears. TinEye (free tier excludes a shop). Lenso.ai, Yandex and Pinterest Lens (copies and look-alikes). Marks4Antiques (unreadable, no trial). Ollama locally (no GPU). Authenticate First (stale).

## Contradictions with earlier decisions

None. Nearest caveat, not a contradiction: the lane's rule "never cite an AI answer as attribution in a listing", backed by the vendors (Anthropic: Claude "can occasionally produce responses that are incorrect or misleading"; Google: "Gemini Apps can make mistakes"). Claude Haiku drafts stay; a draft must not assert a maker or era that no mark, label or appraiser confirmed. Nothing in the three files touches the other listed decisions.
