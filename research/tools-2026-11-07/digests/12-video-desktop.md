# Lane 12-video-desktop: Desktop video, caption, repurposing and design tools (Windows)

Question answered: which Windows desktop and web tools make editing, captioning, repurposing and designing short videos and graphics easier for a one-person vintage shop, beyond the phone apps already chosen (Instagram Edits, Canva Free, Buffer)? Date 2026-11-07. V = vendor page read by one of the three agents. U = page would not load, or the figure is not in any page text.

## Bottom line

- A fully free Windows stack covers today's needs, all V: DaVinci Resolve (full edit), Clipchamp (1080p cuts, "Watermark-free video exports"), CapCut Desktop (vertical templates, one-click video Remove BG), Subtitle Edit or Buzz (offline Whisper captions), OBS Studio (recording and live selling), CuePrompter, Canva Free video editor, Audacity. Nothing in this lane needs money today.
- The gaps file changes the install list. Affinity by Canva is now "$0, free" on Windows: a full vector, photo and layout app that opens PSD, exports to Canva and replaces Photopea plus GIMP for framed panels and cutout touch-ups. LosslessCut trims iPhone clips without re-encoding. HandBrake shrinks finished files under the TikTok 500 MB cap. Windows 11 records the screen with Windows logo key + Shift + R.
- Music licences matter more than tool prices. Meta Sound Collection is "Free to use in any videos you create and share on Facebook and Instagram", nowhere else. Incompetech is CC BY 4.0 with a credit line. Coverr's own library is "free for commercial and personal projects". ElevenLabs Free "does not include a commercial license". Every CapCut terms page failed (404, 500, 502), so no CapCut library music in shop videos until Ricky reads them.
- Verify correction: Epidemic Sound's price cards did render. The tier is Creator, not Personal, at "$9.99 Per month, billed $119.88 yearly" for "personal video content". The tier for "commercial use online" is Pro at "$16.99 Per month, billed $203.88 yearly", over the $50-a-month total once Crosslist is counted. Skip for now.
- The AI clip-and-caption category (Opus Clip, Submagic, Kapwing and ten more in the gaps file) is built for long talking-head sources. Hollie's clips are 15 to 60 second product walk-throughs. Every free tier watermarks, caps at 720p, expires or is a trial. Veed Creator ("$12 per user / month billed yearly", "Unlimited Auto Subtitles") is the one to trial if captions become a daily chore.
- AI video generators do not fit a one-of-one shop: the buyer must see the real object. Sora was "discontinued on April 26, 2026". Pika Free has "Not included: Commercial license". TikTok and Meta label realistic AI content. Adobe Premiere Rush's store listings are 404; treat it as gone. Unscreen now redirects to "the Video Background Remover on Canva Pro".
- Figure cautions from verify: CapCut, Kapwing, Loom, Veed, Canva and Submagic show one side of a Monthly/Yearly toggle, and CapCut says "Displayed pricing is for reference only". DaVinci Studio's "$236" is a "SAVE 20%" sale price. Filmora's checkout adds "Creative Assets" at "US$ 19.99/mo" unless unticked.

## Picks

Use now (free): DaVinci Resolve, Clipchamp, CapCut Desktop (no library music), LosslessCut, HandBrake, Subtitle Edit or Buzz, OBS Studio, Windows 11 Snipping Tool, CuePrompter, Affinity by Canva, Photopea, Canva Free video editor, Audacity, Pexels and Coverr free items for b-roll, Meta Sound Collection for Reels, Incompetech with a credit line everywhere else, Kittl Free for retro lettering.

Pay when it earns it:
- DaVinci Resolve Studio, one-time "Was $295 Now $236": when text-based editing and magic mask would save hours a week.
- Veed Creator "$12 per user / month billed yearly": when a captioned video goes out daily and Subtitle Edit feels slow.
- Kittl Pro "$14 /mo, billed annually" or "$19" monthly: when the free templates run out and the vintage lettering is selling the brand.
- Bensound All Access "$9 /month (Billed $119 annually)": when credit lines on free music become a chore across platforms.
- ScreenPal Deluxe "$4.00/mo paid annually": when Hollie wants a hosted how-to library (care, styling, packing) with captions instead of YouTube.
- Microsoft 365 Personal "$99.99/year": only if Word and OneDrive are wanted anyway; Clipchamp 4K is a side benefit.
- Epidemic Sound Pro "$16.99 Per month, billed $203.88 yearly": only if cross-platform music is a daily need and the budget has room after Crosslist.

Skip:
- AI clippers and repurposers (Opus Clip, Submagic, Kapwing paid, and the gaps list): long-source tools; free tiers watermark or expire.
- Descript: only for long talking pieces; Free is 1 media hour and 720p.
- ElevenLabs and any AI voice: Free is non-commercial; Hollie's own voice is the brand.
- AI video generators (Runway, Pika, Veo, Luma, Suno, Pippit AI, Google Vids): generated content misrepresents a one-of-one item.
- Riverside, Loom Business, Camtasia, StreamYard paid, Streamlabs Ultra: OBS and Clipchamp cover these free.
- Epidemic Sound Creator: for "personal video content", not a shop. Auphonic free: adds a jingle.
- NVIDIA Broadcast unless the PC has an RTX 2060 or newer.

## Contradictions with earlier decisions

None. Three findings reinforce prior calls: Unscreen now lives behind Canva Pro and remove.bg was already reported to end 2026-12-01, so rembg BiRefNet stays the cutout tool. Affinity is free and exports to Canva, so Canva Free holds. Repurpose.io is redundant with Buffer Free and Crosslist.

## Check by hand

- CapCut Desktop: open the Terms of Service from the app footer or settings (capcut.com/terms-of-service returned 404). Are library music and sounds licensed for commercial or business use?
- https://www.capcut.com/pricing: click the Monthly/Yearly toggle. Is "USD 19.99 /month" the monthly or the yearly rate?
- https://www.veed.io/pricing: click "Pay monthly" and open the free-plan FAQ. Note the Creator monthly figure and the free plan's watermark and export limits.
- https://www.epidemicsound.com/pricing/: click "Monthly". Note Pro's month-to-month price.
- https://www.adobe.com/express/pricing: the Premium price (403 to the fetchers).
- https://uppbeat.io/pricing and https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/: is free music cleared for commercial use, and does it need credit (429 and 403)?
- https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/creativity-standards/: rules on AI-generated imagery or video in listings (403).
- On the PC: run dxdiag, Display tab. Is the GPU an RTX 2060 or newer? If yes, NVIDIA Broadcast is worth installing.
- https://www.affinity.studio/get-affinity: expand "Is Affinity really free?" and "Will Affinity by Canva get free updates and new features?" (answers were collapsed).
