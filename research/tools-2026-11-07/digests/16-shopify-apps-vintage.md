# Lane 16: Shopify apps for a one-of-one vintage shop

Question: which Shopify App Store apps and built-in features fit a shop where every piece is quantity one (sold items stay visible, make-an-offer, fixed shipping classes, local pickup and delivery, oversize shipping, product video and room views, shop-the-room bundles, reviews, Etsy sync), what do they cost, and what do recent reviews say? Digest date 2026-11-07.

## Bottom line

- The verify file confirmed all 16 price, free-tier and availability checks. Nothing in the lane's picks was wrong. Most of what Hollie needs is built in and costs nothing extra: pickup in store, local delivery (100-mile radius, 10 zones per location; works with Shop Pay but not Apple Pay, Google Pay, Amazon Pay or PayPal), 99 custom shipping profiles for fixed classes, 250 videos or 3D models per store on Basic (1 GB, 10 minutes each), sold products that stay published at zero inventory by default, and a Dimensions metafield that replaces size-chart apps.
- Etsy bridge: Shopify Marketplace Connect takes no new Etsy connections ("New connections to Etsy can't be made at this time"). Shuttle is the free bridge: 10 product, 10 order and 10 review transfers a month, one Etsy shop; its description says "no limits or quotas" but the Free plan is capped as quoted. Essential is "$12 / month or $120/year" for unlimited transfers; Growth "$19 / month" adds "Real-time stock sync (200 orders/mo)". shopUpz UNLIMITED "$18 / month" is the two-way quantity sync alternative. Caveat on every Etsy app, from QuickSync's listing: "Etsy restricts shipping addresses and tracking for US shops connected on or after 21 Oct 2024", so treat Etsy orders as fulfilled on Etsy.
- Reviews: Judge.me Forever Free carries "Unlimited product and store reviews" and "Sync with Shop App, Etsy, Amazon" (5.0 from 49,785). The gaps file calls Etsy review import "the biggest hole" and says no listed tool does it; that overlooks Judge.me's Etsy sync. Reputon Etsy Reviews ("$6.99 / month") is the fallback, not the first buy. Gaps also found AG Product Reviews (Air Reviews by Avada), fully "Free" with 4.9 from 3,208, and the Shop channel's own 1-to-5-star reviews after delivery.
- Offers: Magical Make an Offer Starter is "Free to install" with "1% on paid orders only. No sales, no fee." Offerly Starter is "Only 0.9% of paid orders via offers" or Pro "$20 / month" flat, cheaper once offer sales pass about $2,000 a month. Auction Today (gaps) adds reserve-price auctions, "FREE 1 Auction", "STARTER $9 / month 15 Auctions", with a Make an Offer button included.
- Sold archive: the free baseline needs no app. For sorting, the gaps file's MB Push Down Hide Out of Stock ("Free Plan ... Up to 50 products & 100 collections", "Tag sold-out items") beats the lane's PushLast ("$3.99 / month", no free plan, 18 reviews).
- Pickup slots: one8's Pickup : Delivery Date Picker ("Free 200 orders/month", launched Dec 22, 2025, 5.0 from 59) beats Pickeasy's free tier ("Upto 10 orders/month"), but Pickeasy (4.9 from 1,344) is the proven app if the young one disappoints. Zapiet at "$49.99 / month" is priced for bakeries.
- Oversize: SMART Shipping Rates & Rules Pro "$19.99 / month" claims "Dimension & volumetric weight rates" and "Works on Basic plan, no CCS needed". uShip ("Free") and ShipEasy White Glove & LTL ("Free to install", pay per shipment) are the only freight apps found, with 2 and 1 reviews. Intuitive Shipping "$70 / month" and ShipperHQ "$75 / month" are over budget.

## Picks

Use now (free): built-in pickup, local delivery, shipping profiles, product video, Dimensions metafield and Shop Pay Installments; Shop, Pinterest and Google & YouTube channels; Shopify Messaging, Inbox, Flow and Forms; Judge.me Forever Free (or AG Product Reviews, not both); Magical Make an Offer Starter; Shuttle Free; MB Push Down Free; Instafeed Starter; Vizup basic; Tiny SEO Free; Lookfy Free; one8 date picker Free if slots are wanted; Auction Today FREE to test one auction.

Pay when it earns it:
- Shuttle Essential "$12 / month or $120/year" when transfers pass 10 a month; Shuttle Growth "$19 / month" or shopUpz UNLIMITED "$18 / month" when Etsy and Shopify both sell live and stock must hit zero on both within minutes.
- Offerly Pro "$20 / month" when offer sales pass about $2,000 a month (cheaper than 1%).
- MB Push Down Basic "$4.99 / month or $47.88/year" past 50 live products; PushLast "$3.99 / month" is the alternative.
- Pickeasy Starter "$9.99 / month" when more than 10 scheduled orders a month and the young free pickers disappoint.
- Reputon Etsy Reviews "$6.99 / month" if Judge.me's Etsy sync does not show Hollie's Etsy reviews; FSL "$4.99 / month" if 18 reviews is enough trust.
- Auction Today STARTER "$9 / month" when more than one auction runs at once.
- SMART Shipping Rates Pro "$19.99 / month" only if 99 profiles cannot express an oversize class.
- Timesact CORE Flex "$5 / month" first, Reservo "$29 / month" later, if holds with a deposit become frequent.
- Picture It "$19 / month" for AR art previews on the Shopify store, if art is a top seller there (Hollie's own site already has a room editor).
- Reputon Customer Reviews App (Beginner "Free 50 emails / month"; Lite "$9.99 / month") for Google reviews once local buyers exist.

Skip (with reason):
- Zapiet Pickup + Delivery "$49.99 / month", Intuitive Shipping "$70 / month", ShipperHQ "$75 / month", Parcelify "$19.99 / month", BS Better Shipping "$29.99 / month", Boxify "$19 / month", Zapiet Rates by Zip Code "$14.99 / month", Advanced Shipping Rules Lite "$9 / month", ShipX Starter "$9.99 / month": priced for volume or duplicating free profiles and delivery zones.
- CedCommerce ("$9 / month" lists only 10 products; 2026 drift complaints), QuickSync Lite "$19 / month" (review-begging complaints), DPL "$19.99 / month": worse terms than Shuttle or shopUpz.
- LitCommerce "$29 / month or $278/year", Nembol "Basic $39 / month", Trunk "Essential $35 / month", Sellbrite (Free up to 30 orders, 3.9 rating, billed outside Shopify): crosslister decision, not this lane.

- Downpay "$29 / month" (value-capped), Molsoft Layaway "$17 / month" (zero reviews).
- Page builders: Shogun (free "No Page Publishing"; "$39 / month"), PageFly Builder "$24 / month", GemPages "$29 / month", EComposer "$25 / month": theme sections first.

## Contradictions with earlier decisions

None refuted. Two notes. Shuttle Free (10 transfers a month) covers the Etsy-to-Shopify leg that Crosslist Gold also fans out to; this is an overlap, not a conflict, since Crosslist still carries eBay. The QuickSync caveat ("Etsy restricts shipping addresses and tracking for US shops connected on or after 21 Oct 2024") supports fulfilling Etsy orders on Etsy, which matches the Phase 1 flow. The 99 shipping profiles finding supports fixed shipping classes. Nothing touches rembg BiRefNet, Buffer Free, Canva Free, Instagram Edits, Claude Haiku drafts, Supabase or Pushover.

## Check by hand

- https://apps.shopify.com/judgeme: confirm "Sync with Shop App, Etsy, Amazon" on Forever Free imports Hollie's Etsy shop reviews into the Shopify widget. Decides whether Reputon "$6.99 / month" is needed.

- https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/shopify-shipping (403 today): carrier weight and size limits for oversize pieces.
- Theme editor: the sold-out badge text, and whether a "sold" tag can drive a SOLD badge (Lably's out-of-stock rule condition unconfirmed).
- https://apps.shopify.com/flow: the inventory-quantity trigger that tags sold items; not verified.
- https://apps.shopify.com/instock-1: launch date "July 6, 2026" and whether reviews are still zero.
- https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shop-pay-installments, "Getting paid" section: payout timing.
- https://apps.shopify.com/etsy-reviews-importer: header says "Free plan available" but only "$5.99 / month" is shown.
