# Gaps for lane 11. Customer service automation: what the first list missed

Date 2026-11-07. Author: research subagent (completeness critic for lane 11-customer-service-automation).
Question: Which tools that automate buyer messages, replies, bookings, link pages and small workflows for a one-person vintage shop are missing from the lane 11 list, and what do they cost today?
Legend: V = vendor page read today (2026-11-07) with the fetch script; U = unverified (snippet, memory, or the page would not load). Prices are quoted as the vendor writes them, with the billing period where the page shows it.

Method note: the shared WebSearch budget was already used up before this agent's first query, so no search ran. Every lead below came from vendor URLs typed directly. The "appears in two independent 2026 roundups" test could not be run today; the tools chosen are the ones the first agent's seeds and section 4 gaps point at (Instagram DM automation, free scheduling tools beyond Calendly and Cal.com, Shopify booking apps with free plans, text expanders, open-source automation, free forms, free help desks, link pages, scripted SMS). 56 fetches were attempted; 42 returned a readable vendor page.

## 0. Bottom line

- The biggest hole is Instagram DM automation. The lane dropped ManyChat after a 403 and never looked at the category again. ManyChat's pricing page and homepage still return 403 today (U on price), but its Google Play listing loaded (V): "Start Free", works with "Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger", 3.7 stars from 6.07K reviews. Chatfuel loaded (V): Business S "US$18 per month Billed yearly, US$216/year", no free plan. Instagram itself has a free built-in option the lane missed: "Set Up Frequently Asked Questions on Chats" with "automated responses" (V, Instagram Help Center search result).
- Free scheduling tools with payments on the free tier, which the lane's "payment before any visit" rule needs: zcal Free (unlimited links, Stripe, "Fee on payments 3%"), TidyCal (Free, or "$29 once"; Stripe or PayPal, "1% platform fee on Free and Lifetime"), YouCanBookMe Free ("30 bookings per month", "Take payments with Stripe"). All V. Cal.com Free stays the pick; these are the backups the lane did not list. Koalendar Free and Zoho Bookings Forever Free are generous but put payments on paid plans (V). Google Calendar appointment schedules are free with "A personal Google Account" (V), good for the free Screen Test call, no payments.
- Shopify booking apps missed: Cowlendar (4.9 stars, 2,263 reviews) has a Free plan of "5 bookings/mo", then Starter "$13.99 / month"; Tipo Appointment Booking has a free plan, then "$9.90 / month or $99/year". Both V. Appointo Free (already on the list, unlimited bookings) still beats both on price.
- Text expansion: Text Blaze (Chrome extension plus Windows app) is "Free forever" with "Limited snippets", Pro "$2.99 USD per month, billed yearly" (V). Beeftext is a free MIT-licensed Windows expander (V). The iPhone already has Text Replacement built in and syncs through iCloud (V, Apple support). That is the zero-cost phone answer the lane left open.
- Small automations: Activepieces Free gives "1,000 credits a month", "Unlimited flows", open source and self-hostable (V); Automatisch is an AGPL-3.0 self-hosted Zapier alternative (V, GitHub); Pipedream is free only in development, production starts at "$99 per month" (V). Power Automate for desktop is "preinstalled" on Windows 11 (V, Microsoft Learn); the cloud plan is "$15.00 user/month, paid yearly" (V).
- Forms: Tally gives "unlimited forms and submissions, completely free" including "Collect payments" (V). Fillout Free is "Unlimited forms 1000 responses/mo" (V). Jotform Starter is "FREE" but "5 Forms 100 Monthly Submissions" with Jotform branding (V). Tally makes Typeform (on the list, 28 USD / mo) and Google Forms (unverified on the list) both unnecessary.
- Free help desks the lane missed: Chatwoot Hacker "$0 /agent/month Up to 2 agents" with "500/month" conversations, MIT-licensed self-host (V); Zoho Desk Free Edition "three user licenses", "US$0 Free Forever" (V); HubSpot Free "$0/mo Free for up to 2 users" (V). Freshdesk, which roundups still call free, shows no free plan today: Growth is "$19 /agent/month, billed annually" (V).
- Link pages: Carrd Free allows "up to three sites" with branding; Pro Standard is "$19 / year" with a custom domain (V). Bio.link has a free tier and one Pro plan at "$7.49 /mo" (V). Carrd at $19 a year undercuts Linktree Starter ($7 /mo billed annually on the list).
- Scripted SMS: Google Voice bans it (lane finding). The lawful path is Twilio at "$0.0083" per segment plus "registration onboarding fees" for US A2P 10DLC (V). Only needed if the dashboard ever texts buyers; Pushover and ntfy already cover Ricky's own alerts.
