[YOWIE](https://helloyowie.com/) is a boutique hotel, shop and cafe in Philadelphia with a focus on work by independent labels, artists, and emerging designers. The hotel's thirteen individually designed rooms, created by founder Shannon Maldonado, feature local artwork and items available for purchase or customization in the downstairs shop. Recently named one of  _Travel + Leisure_ 's [100 Best New Hotels in the World](https://www.travelandleisure.com/best-new-hotels-of-2024-8607510), YOWIE has also made a name for itself with its shop's interactive design workshops, pop-ups, creative direction, and community partnerships.
