## The store that turns donations into homes
Habitat ReStores are nonprofit thrift stores and donation centers that operate to contribute towards Habitat’s mission of building homes, communities, and hope. The ReStores accept donations and sell a constantly changing inventory of diverse, high-quality merchandise to the public at a fraction of the retail price while diverting reusable household items and building materials from area landfills.
When you purchase donated items from the ReStore, **100% of the profits go to Habitat for Humanity of Lee and Hendry Counties** to partner with local families to build, retain and improve safe and affordable homes.

## FREE ReStore residential pick-up
  * FREE donation pickup service provided by Habitat ReStore.
  * Pickups typically occur within 2 – 8 business days (Tues-Sat).
  * All items must be located on the ground level or have an elevator that the items will fit in.
  * Minimum size requirement of 2 – 3 large furniture/appliance items.


[SCHEDULE FREE RESIDENTIAL PICKUP](https://www.habitat4humanity.org/schedule-free-pickup)
## FREE ReStore business pick-up
  * FREE donation pickup service provided by Habitat ReStore.
  * Pickups typically occur within 2 – 8 business days (Tues-Sat).
  * For large items or bulk donations, submit a donation pickup request.
  * We can coordinate one-time or regularly scheduled pickups for your business.


[SCHEDULE FREE BUSINESS PICKUP](https://www.habitat4humanity.org/schedule-free-pickup)
### 3rd-party pick-up service
  * SERVICE FEE APPLIES.
  * Pickup provided by a 3RD PARTY HAULER.
  * Pickups as soon as 2 business days (Mon-Sat), subject to timeslot availability.
  * Can pick up from multiple floors.
  * Can disassemble furniture.
  * Items of ANY condition accepted.
  * Pick-up fee reflects the operating costs and is much smaller than a typical moving company or junk hauler fee.

## Our Impact
The impact of the ReStore goes beyond great deals. When you donate and shop at the ReStores, you’re starting a chain reaction of community investment that will make an impact now and for generations to come.
That’s because 100% of ReStore profits directly support Habitat for Humanity of Lee and Hendry Counties to build homes, allowing families to provide a safe and decent place to live through affordable homeownership. Every cent counts.
