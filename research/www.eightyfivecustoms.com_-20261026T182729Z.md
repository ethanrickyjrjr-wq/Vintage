## Every Sneaker Has A Story. I Help Write Their Next Chapter.
**For over six years, I’ve been at the forefront of my craft—upcycled sneaker leatherwork—pioneering creative ways to transform worn and damaged sneakers into handcrafted pieces with soul. Every creation is a labor of love, shaped by originality, craftsmanship, and a deep passion for sneaker culture, it’s history, and leathercraft.**
**This isn’t mass production. It’s art. Each piece is meticulously designed and stitched by hand, carrying the history of the sneaker it came from and the creativity that turns it into something entirely new. By giving these materials a second life, I’m not just preserving their story—I’m rewriting it.**
**When you invest in a piece from eightyfivecustoms, you’re not just purchasing a product; you’re owning a piece of sustainable artistry, built with care, vision, and authenticity.**
**Thank you for being part of the journey.**
