### Community Resource Centers
These centers help people develop skills, earn credentials or certificates, plan for a career, create a resume, polish interview skills, connect with local employers, and get finances in order through financial literacy.
[](https://goodwillswfl.org/services/community-resource-centers/)
### Housing Assistance
Goodwill currently provides housing for people with disabilities in Cape Coral, Clewiston, Fort Myers, LaBelle, Lehigh Acres, North Fort Myers, Port Charlotte, and Punta Gorda.
[](https://goodwillswfl.org/services/housing/)
### Disability Services
Goodwill has more than a century of experience at helping people with disabilities and other barriers to employment. Let us help you take the Next Step Forward!
[](https://goodwillswfl.org/services/disability-services/)
### Small Business Development
Assists job seekers to choose, obtain, and/or retain employment that matches their career goals. Offers 90 days of continuing support from an employment consultant once employment is secured.
[](https://goodwillswfl.org/services/small-business-development/)

### Helping People In Southwest Florida
Goodwill Industries of Southwest Florida’s mission is to provide programs and services for people with disabilities and other barriers to employment.
We provide job training/placement, affordable housing, youth programs, and other needed services in Charlotte, Collier, Hendry/Glades, and Lee counties. Our programs are funded partially through our 38 Southwest Florida Retail & Donation Centers.
![A person with Down syndrome using a laptop](https://goodwillswfl.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/serviceb.jpg)
![A wheelchair user reaching for a book in a library.](https://goodwillswfl.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/servicec.jpg)
Programs include: Certificate Computer Training, Community Support Services, Disability Opportunities, Small Business Training
