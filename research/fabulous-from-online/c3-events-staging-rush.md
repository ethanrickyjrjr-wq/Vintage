Geography legend: **SWFL** = Lee/Collier (Naples, Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Bonita Springs, Estero) by the brief's definition. **FL, not SWFL** = Florida vendor outside Lee/Collier (Sarasota counts here). **National** = no Florida base. ## TL;DR

1. **Almost nobody in SWFL posts prices, and the few who do are the ones to copy.** Of 15 SWFL vendor sites read, six post any prices of their own (Vandenhul, Naples Home Staging, The Party Girl Co., Trinity for plates, Family First for bounce houses, Fix Move Haul); ProMatcher's aggregate adds an hourly band. Vandenhul markets it: "Real starting ranges, not 'contact for a quote.'" A public price menu is a differentiator here. `verified`
2. **SWFL staging anchors:** consult $150 to $600 (Vandenhul), $75 credited to the balance (Party Girl); occupied staging $1,500 to $3,000; vacant $2,000 to $6,000+; furniture rental $500 to $600 per room per month (Vandenhul); Naples Home Staging charges 15% of the initial fee per month after month three and uses only new furniture. Stager labor is $110 to $130 an hour in Fort Myers/Cape Coral (ProMatcher, unsourced aggregate). `verified`
3. **Event styling is priced as flat packages with minimums, not hours.** Party Girl (Naples): balloon-install minimum $500 indoor, $800 outdoor, neon sign rental $75 to $150. Siesta Bach & Co (Sarasota, FL not SWFL): $750 per party for a 2-hour on-site decor setup. Bach Me (national): $495 to $990 packages "include delivery and installation", with strike ("Decor Clean Up") from $150 "depending on location". `verified`
4. **Vintage rental policy has converged:** 50% non-refundable retainer, balance 14 to 30 days out, full pay when booking is inside that window, client liable from delivery to pickup, "availability cannot be guaranteed until confirmed" for one-of-one pieces, free delivery inside a radius with a minimum order (Vintage Gathering: 40 miles, $100 minimum). `verified`
5. **In-person costs more than virtual in every source that compares them:** $100 virtual vs $120 general hourly (Well Built), $150/hr chat vs $300/hr on-site (Bee Setups), "a different package" (Morel). Day rates: Morel $1,250 for 6 hours + 2 office hours; AD says stylist days start at $750 to $1,000; Interior Design Community prices a day at 8x hourly. `verified` for the numbers; Well Built and Bee Setups are unsourced vendor blogs.
6. **No styling or decorating vendor posts a rush fee, or the amount of an after-hours surcharge.** Delivery-side vendors say after-hours costs "an additional fee" (Atlas, Party Time Naples, Discount Party, Uniquely Chic) and never print the amount. The only rush percentages are from freelance-industry pages (25% to 300%, benchmark 25%; 50% for 24h, 25% for 48h), so a rush multiplier for Hollie is `inferred` from adjacent trades. Publishing one would be new in this market.
7. **Travel is billed at about half the hourly rate, or by flat zone.** Half rate beyond 30 minutes (JNR Designs, NYC), transit at 50% to 60% of hourly (Interior Design Community), flat delivery zones of $40 to $90 (All Miami). Fix Move Haul (Naples) hauls furniture from $79 and flags gated communities as a coordination line. `verified`
8. **Gaps I could not close:** no SWFL tablescape or dinner-party styling service price (only rental per-piece: Trinity $2 dinner plate, $6 charger; Suzi, Destin $3); no per-bedroom turnkey Airbnb fee (Hudson's and Coastal Stage post none; Bee Setups' $500 / $1,200 / $2,500 per room e-design is a proxy only); no published "shop with a designer" hourly for home decor (closest: designer hourly $50 to $200 and a fashion personal-shopper range of $25 to $200).

## Recommendation for Holliewood & Vine

All numbers below are `inferred` from the verified evidence above, not published rates, and should be tested against Hollie's actual costs.

### 1. Rush multiplier
- **Two tiers, applied to the styling/service fee only (not to the sticker price of items for sale):** "Fast" (starting inside 7 days) **+25%**, "Now" (starting inside 48 hours) **+50%**, floor **$250**. Same-day only if the calendar is open, and then quoted at **+100%** or declined.
- Evidence: 25% is the freelance benchmark, 50% for 24h, 25% for 48h (Freelancermap); FreelanceTally calls its 25/50/100 table "illustrative, not market benchmarks", so these are a starting point. The $250 floor sits inside the $200 to $500 flat band Out of Office Pro gives for professional services; for decor it is unproven. Lisa Green (Fort Myers) gives the reason to print: rush means "expedited production or substituting what is available".
- Rental side: add a **Priority Delivery** option as a flat fee. Party Time Rentals (Naples) sells one and prints no price.

### 2. In-person rate structure
- Virtual first: video call, photos or email brief. **Virtual hour $100**; **in-person hour $150** (1.5x virtual; Bee Setups spans 2x, Well Built's average spread is 1.2x). Day = 8 hours = **$1,200**, which matches Morel's $1,250 for "6 working hours + 2 office hours", and Interior Design Community's "day = 8x hourly".
- **Consultation:** $75 virtual (Party Girl) and $150 in-person walkthrough, both **credited to the project** if booked (Party Girl, Vandenhul).
- **Styling Day (in person):** $1,200, includes 2 office hours for sourcing and follow-up (Morel); half-day (4 hr) **$650** (a small premium over 4 x $150). Prep and return days bill at the same rate (AD).
- **Install / strike:** priced as separate lines (Bach Me strike from $150; Vandenhul delivery as its own line). Pieces rented for an event: collection price (Vintage Gathering $325 to $495) plus the styling fee.
- **Post these publicly.** Almost nobody in SWFL does (finding 1).

### 3. Travel radius and fee
- Hollie's base is unknown, so set the radius after naming it. Structure: **free within 20 miles of base with a $300 minimum**; **20 to 40 miles** add **transit time at $75/hr** (50% of the in-person hour, portal to portal, billed after the first 30 minutes; IDC 50 to 60%, JNR half beyond 30 minutes); **beyond 40 miles** by quote (Vintage Gathering uses 40 miles as its edge and "calculated based on distance and logistics" beyond it). The 20-mile free band is my choice, not a source's.
- **Hauling:** pass through at cost, from $79 (Fix Move Haul, Naples), flat-quoted. Gated and HOA communities (Pelican Bay, Pelican Marsh, Pine Ridge, the Vineyards) are a named intake question.
- **After-hours:** standard hours Monday to Friday 9 to 5, Saturday 9 to 3 (Atlas's posted window); outside those (early, late, Sundays, holidays) **+$150 flat per visit**. Atlas, Party Time (Naples) and others print no amount, so this is a first-mover choice. Events happen on Saturdays: do not surcharge Saturday by default.

### 4. Minimums and terms
- **Minimum order $300** for any delivered/placed visit inside the free radius (Atlas $300 delivery minimum; 2 hours at the in-person rate). **Installations** (events, lounges): **$500 indoor, $800 outdoor** (Party Girl, Naples; theirs are balloon installs, used here as the nearest SWFL install minimum); outdoors also needs advance approval and a cover (Vintage Gathering).
- **Retainer 50% non-refundable at booking; balance due 14 days before; full payment inside 14 days** (Vintage Gathering, Party Girl, Discount). **Cancellation ladder:** retainer forfeited inside 30 days; 50% at 29 to 14 days; 100% inside 14 days (Party Girl).
- **Damage:** client responsible from delivery until pickup (Vintage Gathering, Party Time). Waiver is 5% at one vendor and 10% at two (X verified, Y need review: the number is vendor-set). Because pieces are one-of-one and irreplaceable, **10% damage waiver or a card on file** is the safer pick (`inferred`).
- **Availability:** "cannot be guaranteed until confirmed" and "may not be moved" (Vintage Gathering). Rental period 48 hours (Vintage Gathering) vs 24 hours (Party Time): pick 48.
- **Lead with** the top-block occasions (bar, table, party, bachelorette, lounge, photo set). Keep listing staging off the menu. State Hollie's base only after Hollie picks one.
