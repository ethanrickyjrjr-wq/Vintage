#  File Upload question 
**Include a File Upload question in your form to let respondents attach files to their answers.**
The File Upload question is a [Basic ](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/basic-plan-360060585211/)account and higher feature that allows respondents to upload a file from their computer or mobile device. You can then download the file via the [Results](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/explore-individual-responses-in-the-results-panel-360029253732/) panel.
The File Upload question supports all file types, with a maximum file size of 10MB. So, for example, you could ask your respondents to send you an MP3 file with their demo tape, a DOC file with their resume, or a photograph in JPEG format.
Respondents can also upload files from their mobile device. If they’re viewing the form on a phone or tablet, they can either upload a photo from their library or snap a picture with the camera and upload it.
## How to add a File Upload question to your form
**1.** Open your form and click **Add content**.
![Add_content.png](https://help.typeform.com/hc/article_attachments/36621256331668)
**2.** Select **File Upload**.
![File_upload.png](https://help.typeform.com/hc/article_attachments/36621302513300)
**3.** Now enter your question text and description. In this case, we’re asking the person to upload their CV, and we’re adding a sentence that explains the 10MB file restriction.
![Screenshot 2025-04-15 at 17.18.07.png](https://help.typeform.com/hc/article_attachments/36621256337940)
**4.** In the **Question** tab, you can make the File Upload [required](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/make-your-questions-required-360052416212/), meaning that people would have to upload something to submit the form. To enable this, click on the **Required** switch.
![FileUuload_required.png](https://help.typeform.com/hc/article_attachments/36621302519700)
**5.** You can recall information in your File Upload question to personalize it a bit. Type the **@** key on your keyboard, then select which answer you’d like to insert into the question. In this example, we’re using the person’s name.
![Screenshot 2025-04-15 at 17.21.10.png](https://help.typeform.com/hc/article_attachments/36621256340756)
**6.** If you’d like to allow people to upload multiple files to your form, add a [Question Group](https://help.typeform.com/hc/en-us/articles/360052316132) from the Questions menu, then add separate File Upload questions within this group, inserting text for each one.
![Screenshot 2025-04-15 at 17.22.40.png](https://help.typeform.com/hc/article_attachments/36621256345236)

## How to access and download files
There are three ways to do this:
**Download all files from your form**
**1.** The first is to log into your Typeform account, and head over to the **Results** panel and then click **Responses**.
![](https://help.typeform.com/hc/article_attachments/46254155321748)
**2.** Then choose which results you want to download using the check boxes. Alternatively, you can click **Download all responses**.
![File_upload_download.png](https://help.typeform.com/hc/article_attachments/36621302531476)
**3.** Next, this window appears, where you can choose to download your results in CSV or XLSX formats, or to grab your files directly. Check **The files your respondents uploaded** option to download them to your computer in a .zip file.
![Screenshot 2025-04-15 at 17.40.12.png](https://help.typeform.com/hc/article_attachments/36621302533524)
**4.** Double-click your .zip file to unpack it and check out what your respondents have given you.
**Download individual files in Typeform**
**1.** Open the **Responses** view from the Results panel in your form. Click on the **expand** icon next to any individual response to view its details. 
![](https://help.typeform.com/hc/article_attachments/46254155324564)
**2.** Click on the file link under the File Upload question to download it. 
![](https://help.typeform.com/hc/article_attachments/46254168762772)
######  ![HC_Note_new.png](https://help.typeform.com/hc/article_attachments/4402049823124)  
**Note**! If you [delete an individual response](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/what-happens-to-deleted-responses-typeforms-and-accounts-360040196472/) to a File Upload question, the corresponding file uploaded by the respondent will be deleted too.
**Download individual files from a direct link**
**1.** If you share submissions in [follow-up messages](https://help.typeform.com/hc/en-us/articles/13610123653396), or view your results in [Google Sheets](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/google-sheets-for-typeform-360029256192/), the File Upload question result will be a direct link to the file.
![File_Upload_6.png](https://help.typeform.com/hc/article_attachments/360075279012)
**2.** Click the link to access your file.
######  ![HC_Note_new.png](https://help.typeform.com/hc/article_attachments/4402049823124)  
**Note**! You do not need to be signed in to your Typeform account to download files from your results with these URLs. 
## Managing access to uploaded files
Learn how uploaded files can be accessed, when public links to files are created, and what to consider when sharing responses.
  * You can view and download uploaded files directly from the table view in the Responses tab.
    * You must be logged into your Typeform account to access and download files directly from the Responses tab.
  * If you use one of the following features, it'll create a public accessible link to the uploaded file, meaning anyone with the link can access uploaded files:
    * [Downloading a responses export file (CSV or XLSX)](https://help.typeform.com/hc/en-us/articles/360029253572).
    * Sending responses (or [partial responses](https://help.typeform.com/hc/en-us/articles/21102221958676)) in a [follow-up message](https://help.typeform.com/hc/en-us/articles/13610123653396).
    * Sending responses to an [integration](https://help.typeform.com/hc/en-us/categories/37272909352084-Integrations).
    * Sending responses to a [webhook](https://help.typeform.com/hc/en-us/articles/360029573471).
  * Using one or more of the features listed above is a convenient way to share responses, however, anyone can access the uploaded files potentially. Similar to tools like Google Docs, there is convenience to sharing the link publicly but with the tradeoff of privacy.
    * The generated links for uploaded files are long and difficult to guess, but it should still be treated as shareable access.
  * Before using one of the features listed above, consider:
    * How sensitive the content of the uploaded file is.
    * Whether the convenience of sharing responses outweighs the privacy tradeoff.


## How to handle files bigger than 10MB
If the respondent needs to upload a file that’s larger than 10MB to your form, you can ask them to upload the file to a repository service—like [Dropbox](https://www.dropbox.com/) or [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/)–and let them paste the link into a Website question. This will allow you to collect the links and download the files later.
With Logic you could go a stage further and:
  * Ask your respondents the size of the file they're about to upload.
  * Based on the answer they give, either direct them to a File Upload question where they can upload the file (if the answer is “under 10MB”) or to a Website question where they can paste the link to the file (as described above).


## How to add downloadable files to a form
We’ve explained what to do to allow respondents to upload files, but what if you want to let them download a file from a form? It’s possible to add a download link to your form in cases where you want to make documents or media files available to people.
Simply upload your file to a repository service such as Google Drive or Dropbox, then copy the link to this file, and paste it into your form (in a [Statement question](https://help.typeform.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051789692), for example).
Note that it’s not possible to add a clickable hyperlink, but you can use a URL shortener such as [Bitly](https://bitly.com/) or [Ow.ly](https://www.hootsuite.com/pages/owly) to make the link look a bit nicer.
![Screenshot 2025-04-15 at 17.37.57.png](https://help.typeform.com/hc/article_attachments/36621256360724)
######  ![HC_BobRoss_ilu_cropped.png](https://help.typeform.com/hc/article_attachments/20183148807316)  
**Tip**! Depending on your plan, there's a total limit of 4GB for files uploaded to your form. However, we don’t currently have a way for you to monitor how much space you're using. Therefore, we work on a fair usage policy. This means that although we say your limit is 4GB, your account won’t actually stop storing files once you reach that amount. To avoid getting too far over your account’s limit, we'd recommend downloading your files from time to time and storing them on a 3rd-party service. Both Dropbox and Google Drive offer free storage options, for example.
