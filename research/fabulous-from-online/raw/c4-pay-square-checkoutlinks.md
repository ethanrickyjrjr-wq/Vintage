# Create and share payment links
[In this article](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/6692-get-started-with-square-checkout-links)

Who is this article for?
Account owners or team members with payment link permissions. Set permissions in [Square Dashboard](https://squareup.com/dashboard/team/permissions).
## About payment links
Square Payment Links provide a simple way to accept online payments without the need for a dedicated eCommerce website. You simply create a payment link and share it directly with customers, or post it where they can easily find it and pay at their convenience.
With payment links, you can even offer a touch-free checkout experience through your Square Point of Sale. When you send a payment link from your point of sale, your customer will get a unique link on their phone they can use to pay without needing to enter PINs or install special apps, helping create a safer, contact-free environment when necessary.
## Before you begin 
  * You can create, share, and deactivate payment links from Square Dashboard and Square Point of Sale. From your Square Dashboard, you can create a payment link to **Collect a payment** , **Sell an item** , **Accept a donation** , or **Sell an event or class**. From your point of sale app, you can create a payment link to **Collect a payment** , **Sell an item** , or **Accept a donation**. You cannot **Sell an event or class** from your point of sale app at this time.
  * Depending on the payment link type, some payment link options—such as redirecting links to a website—can only be set up from Square Dashboard.
  * Payment links are available for both domestic and international sales. However, for international sales, taxes and shipping fee settings cannot be applied.
  * If you’d like to sell a larger online catalog, reach more customers internationally, set up more online fulfillment methods, or create a fully customizable online store, learn how to [set up your website](https://squareup.com/help/article/6852-get-started-with-square-online-store).


Payment links cannot be created or customized from Square Restaurants POS, Square Appointments POS, or Square Invoices apps at this time.
## Create a payment link
You can create payment links to take payments, sell items, sell events or classes, or accept donations. If using a payment link to collect donations, learn how to [set up donation goals with payment links](https://squareup.com/help/article/7184). 
##  [ Square Dashboard  ](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/6692-get-started-with-square-checkout-links)
  1. Sign in to Square Dashboard and go to **Payments & orders** (or **Payments & invoices** or **Payments**) > **Payment links** > [**Payment links**](https://app.squareup.com/dashboard/payment-links).
  2. Click **Create link**.
  3. Choose either **Collect a payment** , **Sell an item** , or **Sell an event or class** and click **Continue**.
  4. Enter a **Title** for your link that will be displayed to the customer, a **Frequency** (either a one-time or recurring payment) if applicable, and an optional **Description**. If you’re selling an item or event/class, select the item from your item library or create a new one and add a price.
  5. Add an optional image by selecting **Upload image**.
  6. Optionally toggle on **Custom fields** and add up to two custom fields to collect additional information from customers.
  7. Optionally toggle on **Tipping**.
  8. Optionally toggle on **Redirect to a website after checkout** and enter a website URL.
  9. Click **Save** to start sharing your link.


##  [ Square Point of Sale app  ](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/6692-get-started-with-square-checkout-links)
  1. Sign in to the Square app and tap **≡ More** > **Payment links**. If **Payment links** doesn’t appear, add it to your menu by tapping **≡ More** > **Add-ons** > **Payment links** > **Add for free**.
  2. Tap **+**.
  3. Choose a payment link type.
  4. Enter a name, amount, and description for your link and choose any applicable options.
  5. Tap **Save** to start sharing your link.


## Share a payment link
##  [ Square Dashboard  ](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/6692-get-started-with-square-checkout-links)
  1. Sign in to Square Dashboard and go to **Payments & orders** (or **Payments & invoices** or **Payments**) > **Payment links** > [**Payment links**](https://app.squareup.com/dashboard/payment-links).
  2. Next to the payment link you want to share, click **Share** and choose your share option.


You can share the payment link by copying the link, or via QR code, buy button, email campaign, social media, and more.
##  [ Square Point of Sale  ](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/6692-get-started-with-square-checkout-links)
  1. Sign in to the Square app and tap **≡ More** > **Payment links**.
  2. Tap the payment link you want to share, then tap **Share link** and choose your share option.


You can share the payment link by copying the link, or via text or QR code. You can also send payment links from the checkout screen of your point of sale by selecting **Charge** and tapping the **Send Payment Link** option. This will create a touch-free checkout experience so you don’t have to manage cash or cards.
## Deactivate a payment link
Payment links must be **Active** to be used, but you can deactivate or delete a payment link at any time. Once deleted or deactivated, the payment link will become unavailable to anyone you shared it with. This action cannot be undone.
##  [ Square Dashboard  ](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/6692-get-started-with-square-checkout-links)
  1. Sign in to Square Dashboard and go to **Payments & orders** (or **Payments & invoices** or **Payments**) > **Payment links** > [**Payment links**](https://app.squareup.com/dashboard/payment-links).
  2. Select the payment link you want to deactivate or delete.
  3. Click **Delete** or **Deactivate**.


##  [ Square Point of Sale app  ](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/6692-get-started-with-square-checkout-links)
  1. Sign in to the Square app and tap **≡ More** > **Payment links**.
  2. Select the payment link you want to deactivate or delete.
  3. Tap **Actions** > **Deactivate** or **Delete**.


## View payment links reporting
You can easily track the amount of sales generated from each payment link you've created to understand how each one is performing.
  1. Sign in to Square Dashboard and go to **Payments & orders** (or **Payments & invoices** or **Payments**) > **Payment links** > [Payment links](https://app.squareup.com/dashboard/payment-links).
  2. Review the sales per link in the **Total Sales** column.


You can also view all of your sales and then filter by payment links.
  1. Sign in to Square Dashboard and go to **Reports** > **Sales** > [Sales summary](https://app.squareup.com/dashboard/sales/reports/sales-summary).
  2. Click **Filter by** , then click **Source** > **Payment Links**.
