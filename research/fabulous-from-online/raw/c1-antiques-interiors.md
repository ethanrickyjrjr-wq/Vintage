◆ Our Standard ◆
## The Antiques & Interiors llc Standard
Every piece we offer is chosen for authenticity, condition, and design integrity — so your home gains depth, narrative, and lasting quality. 
### Provenance & Research
Documentation, period assessment, and careful vetting so you buy with clarity and confidence.
### Ethical Sourcing
Responsible acquisition networks and transparent history wherever possible.
### Restoration & Care
Trusted workshops for sympathetic conservation, upholstery, and finishing — only when it serves the piece.
### Private Viewings
Quiet, unhurried appointments in our gallery or on site for designers and collectors.
### Insured Logistics
White-glove packing and insured transport for fragile and high-value furnishings worldwide.
### Interior Design & Consultation
Dedicated studio support — full-time interior design and decoration with ongoing consultations from concept to installation.
