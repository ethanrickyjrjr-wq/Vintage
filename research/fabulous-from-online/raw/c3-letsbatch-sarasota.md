## About this Experience
**What you'll do:**
You hand over your vision and Siesta Bach & Co. handles everything else, arriving at your rental or home in Sarasota or Siesta Key to build a full custom bachelorette setup while you focus on celebrating.
  * **Theme consultation:** You share your color palette, vibe, and any must-have elements, and the team translates that into a cohesive design plan before setup day.
  * **Full installation:** The crew arrives, unpacks, and assembles every piece of the décor, from balloon arrangements and tablescapes to personalized signage and photo-ready backdrops.
  * **Custom touches:** Details like personalized banners, custom labels, and bride-specific pieces are built into the design so the setup reflects this specific group, not a generic template.
  * **On-location styling:** Everything is placed and styled with the actual space in mind, so the final look fits your venue rather than fighting it.
  * **Clean aesthetic finish:** Before the team leaves, the setup is checked and adjusted so what you walk into looks polished and photo-ready from every angle.


This is a two-hour on-site service priced at $750 per party. The result is a fully dressed space your group walks into ready to celebrate.
**Why you'll love it for your bachelorette party:**
  1. **No lifting required:** The bride's crew is there to have fun, not spend the morning wrestling with balloon arches, and this service takes that off the table entirely.
  2. **Designs that actually match:** Because every setup is built around a specific theme and palette, the décor looks intentional rather than pulled from three different stores.
  3. **Gulf Coast setting:** Siesta Key and Sarasota are already stunning backdrops, and décor designed to complement that environment photographs better than generic setups.
  4. F**amily-run attention to detail:** Rebecca and Felicia treat each booking as a standalone project, not a repeat of last weekend's setup, so the details get real thought.
  5. **Stress removal for the planner:** Whoever is organizing this trip gets one major task off their list, which tends to make everyone a little more relaxed going into the weekend.


**About this experience partner:**
Siesta Bach & Co. is a family-run bachelorette décor and planning company based in Sarasota, Florida, founded by aunt-and-niece duo Rebecca and Felicia. Rebecca brings over 20 years of experience in live events and wellness, and Felicia runs Siesta Glow Mobile Spray Tan, contributing her background in beauty and luxury service. Together they specialize in custom bachelorette theme installations delivered and set up at private homes and vacation rentals across Sarasota and Siesta Key.
**Additional information:**
  * Setup takes approximately two hours and is completed at your home or vacation rental.
  * Pricing starts at $750 per party; custom themes and add-ons may affect final cost.
    * Service area covers Sarasota and Siesta Key, Florida.
  * Reach out before booking to discuss your theme, guest count, and any specific décor requests.
