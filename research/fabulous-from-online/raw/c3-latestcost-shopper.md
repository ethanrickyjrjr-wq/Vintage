# Personal Shopper Pricing Guide 2026
February 14, 2026 
Prices for personal shopper services vary based on service level, region, and the scope of work. Typical cost ranges depend on whether the shopper offers hourly guidance or full wardrobe editing, with major drivers including geography, experience, and project size. This guide presents cost, price, and budgeting details in clear USD terms for U.S. buyers.  
| Item  | Low  | Average  | High  | Notes  |  
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |  
| Hourly rate  | $25  | $75  | $200  | In store or virtual sessions differ by market  |  
| One time wardrobe session  | $150  | $350  | $800  | Includes shopping time and outfit selections  |  
| Wardrobe overhaul package  | $500  | $1,200  | $3,000  | Full closet edit plus shopping list  |  
| Monthly subscription  | $40  | $120  | $350  | Ongoing access to looks and returns  |  
| Trip or event styling  | $100  | $300  | $900  | Includes fittings and look selection  |  
| Shopping budget (client funded)  | Varies  | Varies  | Varies  | Usually paid to retailers or as a debit card  |  
Assumptions: region, service level, and scope of work affect estimates. This table uses typical ranges for urban markets and midsize cities in the United States.
## Overview Of Costs
**Cost ranges reflect two main service models** a per hour guidance and a fixed package approach. In general, expect higher rates in major urban areas and for experienced stylists with extensive networks. Typical ranges for a first consult are smaller when combined with shopping time, while ongoing services accrue monthly fees. 
## Cost Breakdown
**Major cost components for personal shopper services** include time, staff, and access to inventory. The following table summarizes common cost elements and ranges with brief assumptions.  
| Component  | Low  | Average  | High  | Notes  |  
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |  
| Materials  | $0  | $0  | $0  | Some clients supply own budget  |  
| Labor  | $25  | $75  | $200  | Includes stylist time and assistants if any  |  
| Delivery/Returns  | $0  | $25  | $100  | Fees if items are shipped or exchanged  |  
| Permits, Taxes  | $0  | $0  | $50  | Sales tax where applicable  |  
| Overhead  | $10  | $35  | $100  | Office and travel costs proportioned to project  |  
| Contingency  | $0  | $25  | $150  | Unplanned fittings or item changes  |  
## Pricing Variables
**Key factors that shift pricing include region and service scope** linear by hours or flat for a complete overhaul. Regional differences can swing costs by 20 to 40 percent between coastal and inland markets. Service breadth matters a lot a single consult vs ongoing wardrobe management changes the total considerably. 
## Ways To Save
**Practical budgeting tips help control costs without sacrificing value** book a bundled session plan instead of ad hoc visits, request virtual styling to reduce travel, and provide a clear shopping list to cut down on time. Combining personal shopping with closet organization can yield better per hour value. 
## Regional Price Differences
**Prices vary across regions due to market density and living costs** compare three distinct markets to gauge how your area might deviate from national averages. Urban centers typically command higher hourly rates and larger inventories. Suburban markets often show mid tier pricing and quicker turnaround. Rural areas can be lower but may have limited options and longer lead times.
## Labor, Hours & Rates
**Labor dynamics shape total cost in meaningful ways** a two to four hour personal shopping session fits many wardrobes, while a full overhaul with fittings might extend to days or weeks. High demand periods may push rates upward. Clients should expect a clear forecast of total hours and the corresponding rate before work begins.
## Additional & Hidden Costs
**Hidden or optional charges can affect final pricing** look for shopping fees, restocking or return fees, expedited shipping, and any membership or subscription charges. Some services levy a consultation surcharge for complex needs or special events. Ask for a detailed written quote that itemizes each potential add on. 
## Real-World Pricing Examples
**Three scenario cards illustrate typical outcomes and values**
**Basic** scenario: one 2 hour in store session with a limited shopping list and 5 recommended outfits. Estimated labor $150, shopping time $60, taxes and fees $20. Total $230-$310 depending on region. Per unit estimate $25-$60 per outfit equivalent.
**Mid-Range** scenario: four hours total across two sessions includes a wardrobe edit and a shopping plan with 15 outfits. Estimated labor $280, shopping time $160, delivery or returns $40, overhead $25. Total $520-$620. Per outfit $30-$90 depending on pieces.
**Premium** scenario: a full wardrobe overhaul with 8 hours spread over multiple visits plus revisions and a dedicated shopper. Estimated labor $420, shopping time $320, accessories and alterations $200, shipping/returns $80, contingency $60. Total $1,100-$1,600. Per outfit $120-$250 for high end pieces.
