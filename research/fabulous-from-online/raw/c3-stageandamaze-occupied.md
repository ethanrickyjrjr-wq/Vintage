When sellers are still living in the home, staging starts with strategy. Our Occupied Home Staging service is designed to identify areas for improvement so your home shows better, photographs beautifully, and appeals to today’s buyers while working with your existing furnishings whenever possible.  
  
The process begins with a walkthrough to assess your furniture, décor, layout, flow, and overall presentation. From there, we provide clear recommendations on what to remove, store, rearrange, refresh, or supplement so each room feels intentional, balanced, and market-ready.  
  
Once the home is edited and prepped, we can bring in curated rental furniture, artwork, décor, bedding, rugs, and styling accents from our staging inventory to complete the look where needed.  
The goal is simple: help buyers focus on the home, not the distractions, so they can connect with the space and see its full potential.
