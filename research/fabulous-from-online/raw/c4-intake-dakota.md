If your onboarding process feels messy, inconsistent, clunky, or non-existent, it’s because you don’t have a streamlined way to gather and centralize client details.
When clients don’t know what information to share (or why it matters), you end up chasing information, clarifying basics, and reworking concepts later. That slows projects down, creates frustration, and leads to unnecessary (costly) revisions.
The **Full Service New Client Design Questionnaire** (intended to be used alongside a welcome guide) gives you a structured, professional way to collect the right information upfront so you can nail your design presentations and build client trust. 
This fillable Google Form guides clients through:
  * Their goals and priorities
  * Style preferences and inspiration
  * Functional needs and lifestyle considerations
  * Budget expectations and timeline
  * Decision-making roles and logistics
  * Existing conditions and constraints


Instead of piecing information together across emails, calls, and jotted-down notes, everything lives in one clean, organized intake form that clients can complete digitally on their own time. 
The result?
  * Fewer follow-up questions and missed details
  * Fewer revisions and more design approvals
  * A more intentional sourcing process that is in budget
  * A more elevated first impression of your business


This is the same style of intake system I’ve helped hundreds of designers implement inside high-end firms.
