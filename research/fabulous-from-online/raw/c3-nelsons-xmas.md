# Installation Information
![](https://nelsonschristmasdecorating.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/IMG_3174-2.jpeg)
**Professional Christmas Light Installers**
When you are ready for an estimate on a Christmas light installation for your home or business, please feel free to reach out to us. Please use our “**[request an estimate](https://nelsonschristmasdecorating.com/contact/)** ” link, or simply call us to arrange an appointment. All estimates are free of charge. Our professional Christmas light installers are ready to make your home sparkle for the holidays.
## Scheduling
Appointments for estimates are available during the holiday season, but are very limited. Please be advised that November and December is our peak season for installations, so appointments for lighting estimates should be made far in advance if possible. **Appointments for estimates between January 15th through October 15th are preferred by our company.** Materials such as commercial lighting, ornaments, garland, wreaths and bows should be ordered in advance. Sometimes supplies do run out during the installation season, and sometimes even before the decoration installation season begins.
## Lighting and Decoration Installation 
LED lights, garland, bows and electrical cables are commercial grade and installed by our trained professionals. Maintenance calls are available during the season if needed. Christmas decoration removal is scheduled in advance for early January.
## Residential Lighting Leasing 
All residential lighting is leased by the customer from our company, unless a sales agreement has been made in advance. **We do not install retail store lighting and decorations pre-owned by residential homeowners.**
## Commercial Lighting Leasing
Nelson’s Christmas Decorating specializes in holiday decoration leasing to homeowners associations, businesses, shopping malls, and hotels. We offer all-inclusive Christmas light installations that include design, installation, maintenance, removal and storage. We have a large selection of commercial-grade lighting and decorations with several options available to meet your needs.
## Storage
Nelson’s Christmas Decorating stores all holiday decorations for both residential and commercial customers unless prior storage arrangements have been made.
## Payments
A non-refundable 50% deposit is required upon acceptance of lighting proposal. The balance will be due on the day installation is complete. A penalty of 2% per month will be applied to all balances if not paid after 30 days of the invoice date.
Unpaid invoices will result in customer’s display being removed at contractor’s discretion and loss of all monies paid to contractor prior to installation.
