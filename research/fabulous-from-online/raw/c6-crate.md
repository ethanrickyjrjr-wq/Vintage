###  Are Crate & Barrel interior design services free?
Yes. Our interior design help is 100% free.

Yes. You can work with our interior stylists virtually or at a Crate & Barrel store.

The Design Desk at Crate & Barrel can help with interior design for any room, including living rooms, bedroom interior design, and kitchen interior design. We even design outdoor spaces including patios, decks, pools terraces and more.
