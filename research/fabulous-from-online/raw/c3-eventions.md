## EVENT DESIGN & PLANNING 
At _Eventions-Naples_ , we believe every celebration should be as unforgettable as the people it honors. From intimate baby showers and milestone birthdays to elopements and private soirées, we curate bespoke events with thoughtful design and meticulous planning. Our signature style is refined yet effortless—clean lines, modern elegance, and immersive details that leave a lasting impression.
Let us bring your vision to life while you enjoy every moment. With a deep understanding of event logistics and a passion for storytelling through design, our team ensures that no detail is overlooked and every element is flawlessly executed. From concept to clean-up, we handle it all—so you can be a guest at your own event.
At _Eventions-Naples_ , we’re more than planners—we’re creators of experiences. Whether you need custom invitations, striking backdrops, beautifully styled tablescapes, organic balloon installations, or one-of-a-kind favors, we craft personalized proposals tailored to your style, space, and story.
Your celebration deserves more than ordinary. Let us create an event that feels like you—elevated, elegant, and entirely unforgettable.  
Ready to begin? Send us an inquiry today. We can’t wait to hear your ideas and design something extraordinary together.

## KIDS EVENTS
**We takes kids parties to the next level!** We specialize in creating unforgettable kids’ events that are fun, stylish, and completely stress-free for parents. From the initial concept to the final cleanup, we handle every detail to bring your child’s dream party to life.
Whether you're planning a grand birthday celebration with 100 guests, a memorable Bar or Bat Mitzvah, or a cozy picnic party in the park with a catered lunch, we make every event magical.
Our expertly designed children's parties are more than just events—they're joyful experiences where families and friends come together to celebrate in a beautiful setting filled with laughter, entertainment, and lasting memories.
Let Eventions-Naples take your kids' party to the next level—so you can enjoy the moment while we handle the magic.
