# Settings for both popup and inline forms
When you [create a form](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/create), the following sections display on the **Create form** page for both popup and inline forms:
  * **Form**
  * **Success**
  * **Styling**
  * **Languages**
  * **Tags**
  * **Email notifications**
  * **Automations and workflows**


Learn more about the specific settings that you can configure for [popup](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings/popup) and [inline](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings/inline) forms.

## Settings that apply to both popup and inline forms
The following table describes the sections included on the **Create form** page for both popup and inline forms, and which settings you can configure in each section. Learn more about [creating a form](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/create).
#### Note
The **Form** and **Success** sections include multiple settings that need to be configured. Learn more about [**Form**](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings/all-forms#form) and [**Success**](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings/all-forms#success) settings.  
Settings that apply to both popup and inline forms.  
| Section  | Description  |  
| --- | --- |  
| Form  | The settings in the **Form** section determine the content of your form, such as the title, fields, marketing consent settings, [Sign in with Shop](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/manage#sign-in-with-shop), and disclaimer text. Learn more about [**Form** settings](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings/all-forms#form).  |  
| Success  | The settings in the **Success** section determine the content that's displayed to your customer after they successfully complete a form. Learn more about [**Success** settings](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings/all-forms#success).   |  
| Styling  | The **Styling** settings determine the look and feel of your form.   |  
| Languages  | You can use the **Languages** settings to translate your form into additional languages. Your form has to be saved before translations can be added.   |  
| Tags  | The **Tags** setting determines whether tags are added to customers that successfully complete a form. To add a tag, enter it in the **Find or create tags** field. Learn more about [using customer tags](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-admin/productivity-tools/using-tags).   |  
| Email notifications  | The **Email notifications** setting lets you determine whether to send an email to the store owner for each submission to a form.   |  
| Automations and workflows  | The **Automations and workflows** setting lets you integrate your form with an existing marketing automation in [Shopify Flow](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-flow). You can connect to an existing automation, or create a new one. Learn more about [creating marketing automations](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/shopify-messaging/marketing-automations/create).   |  
## Form settings
The following settings can be configured in the **Form** section of your popup or inline form.  
Settings for the form section of your form.  
| Setting  | Description  |  
| --- | --- |  
| Title  | The title of the form, where you can provide an incentive or a call to action to submit a response. Maximum of 50 characters.   |  
| Content  | The text displayed below the title. Consider offering an incentive for signing up and keeping the message simple. Maximum of 100 characters.   |  
| Fields  | The information to be captured by the form. Forms have a limit of 31 fields per form. Learn more about [managing form inputs](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings/all-forms#manage-form-fields).   |  
| Marketing consent  | You can capture email, SMS, and WhatsApp marketing consent by selecting either of the following settings: 
  * **On form submission** : Signs a customer up for marketing implicitly after they submit a form.
  * **On form submission if a box is checked** : Adds a marketing consent checkbox to your form that a customer needs to select in order to receive marketing from your store.

If you don't want to subscribe customers who fill in a form for marketing, such as "Contact us" or lead generation form, then select **Don't subscribe customers to marketing**. For email marketing consent, the email field is required on the form. For SMS and WhatsApp consent, the phone field is required on the form. If you use double-opt-in for SMS, then the customer's SMS marketing consent state is set to pending. Your SMS partner is responsible for confirming opt-in with the number. After receiving confirmation, your SMS partner needs to update the status from pending to confirmed.  |  
| Button label  | The text displayed on the submit button. The button should have a clear call to action, such as "Sign Up", "Subscribe", "Submit", or "Get my discount". Maximum of 25 characters.   |  
| Consent disclaimer (optional)  | A disclaimer that includes information about how you use customer data. For example, you can explain that by submitting their email address, customers agree to receive marketing emails. Consider [adding links](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/checkout-settings/refund-privacy-tos#add-links-to-your-policies-within-pages-or-on-social-media) to your store's terms and conditions or privacy policy.   |  
| Sign in with Shop  | When your store has [Sign in with Shop](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/shop/sign-in-features) activated and your form requires an email or phone number, a **Claim with Shop** button displays on the form for customers. This option is turned on by default for eligible forms. You can [turn off Sign in with Shop on a form](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/manage#sign-in-with-shop).   |  
## Custom fields
When you create a custom input field on a form, you can store it as a local field, or a customer or company metafield.
When you add a custom field to your form, you need to give the corresponding field a name. Optionally, you can set specific requirements for each field. Learn more about [managing form fields](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings/all-forms#manage-form-fields).
### Local fields
Fields stored locally are used only on the specific form that you add it to. To reuse a local field, you can [duplicate an existing form](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/create#duplicate-form) that uses that field.
You can change a locally stored field to a customer or company metafield at any time by checking **Also store as customer metafield**. However, only data submitted after the date that you changed the local field to a metafield is stored in the metafield.
### Customer or company metafields
Fields stored as customer or company [metafields](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/custom-data/metafields) are saved in the form field picker. If you have 20 or less customer or company fields saved, then these fields are visible and available for reuse on other forms. Only currently pinned metafields can be reused. If you have more than 20 customer or company metafields, then you can [manage your pinned metafields](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/custom-data/metafields/pinning-metafield-definitions).
After you save a custom input field as a metafield, the **Also store as customer metafield** checkbox no longer displays, and it can't be changed to a local field. If you edit a metafield, then Forms updates all forms that use that metafield to reflect those changes.
After you store a form field as a metafield, it's saved in the [**Custom data**](https://admin.shopify.com/settings/custom_data) settings of your Shopify admin, in the **Customers** or **Companies** section. You can use this metafield data to assess [B2B company requests](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/b2b/companies-and-customers/company-account-requests) or to create [customer segments](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/customers/customer-segmentation).
If a customer fills in a field that's already linked to their customer profile more than one time, then their customer profile displays metafield data only from the most recent form submission.
### Custom field data types
A custom field can be any of the following data types:  
Custom input field data types  
| Field data type  | Description  |  
| --- | --- |  
| Single-line text  | A short text field appropriate for a single sentence.  |  
| Dropdown list  | A list of options presented as a dropdown menu that the user can select one option from.  |  
| Radio buttons  |  A list of options presented as a series of buttons that the user can select one option from.  |  
| Multi-line text  | A longer text field appropriate for a paragraph.  |  
| Multiple choice  | A list of options presented as a series of checkboxes. Unlike the **Dropdown list** and **Radio buttons** , users can select more than one response.  |  
| Date  | A specific date.  |  
| Number  | A single number.  |  
| File upload  | A single file attachment of 20 MB or less. The file must be one of the following accepted file types:  

  * GIF
  * HEIC
  * JPEG
  * PDF
  * SVG
  * WebP
  * XML

## Manage form fields
#### Note
All forms must include either **Email** or **Phone** as a required input field. Company and customer forms also require a **Company name**. Learn more about [setting up a company account request form for B2B](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/b2b/companies-and-customers/company-account-requests#set-up-a-company-account-request-form).
By default, forms capture the customer's first name and email address. In the **Fields** section of your form, you can add or remove fields to customize the information that you collect from customers.
For example, if you want to create a field to capture the shoe size of your customers, then you can select the **Number** field and name the metafield `Shoe Size`. You can then set the field to accept values from 1-16.
#### Steps:
  1. Follow the steps to [create a form](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/create).
  2. In the **Fields** section, take any of the following steps to manage your form fields:
     * To add a new field, click **Add new field**. Select and configure the field that you want to add, and then click **Done**.
     * To remove a field, click the removal icon (⊖) next to the field's name.
     * To edit the details of a field, such as whether the field is required to complete the form, click the field's name. After you make your changes, click **Done**.
  3. Configure your other form settings, and then click **Save**.


## Success settings
The content in the **Success** section of your form displays after a customer successfully submits a form. The fields that you need to complete in the **Success** section depend on the **Confirmation behavior** that you select:
  * **Show message** displays a message to your customers after they submit a form.
  * **Redirect to another page** redirects your customers to the page of your choice after they submit a form.

  
Settings for the Success section of a form.  
| Setting  | Description  | Confirmation behavior  |  
| --- | --- | --- |  
| Discount (optional)  | The **Discount** setting lets you select a discount code to display to customers after they complete your form and reach the success step.  | Show message  |  
| Title  | The title of the success section for the form, where you can provide confirmation that a customer has successfully signed up to your email list. For example, `Here's your discount`, `VIP access starts now`, or `Thanks for signing up!`. Maximum of 50 characters.  | Show message  |  
| Content  | The body of the success section for the form, where you can provide more details or highlight the benefits of joining your email list. For example, `You're now the first to know about the latest drops and special offers`.  | Show message  |  
| Redirect URL  | The URL that you want to redirect your customer to after they complete a form. For example, a "Thank you" [page](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/add-edit-pages) that provides email subscribers with a special perk, or a "Next steps" page for new [B2B customers](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/b2b/companies-and-customers/adding-customers).  | Redirect to another page  |
