Hey @Stacy47728! Great question - and welcome to our community!
At this time there is no way to allow attachments on the booking page. That said, you can use [Invitee Questions On Your Booking Page](https://help.calendly.com/hc/en-us/articles/226893168) to gather all sorts of information. Then, you can use our [redirect](https://help.calendly.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040257613) tool to route invitees to another page, such as a form you use to prompt them to attach and upload a document (Google Forms is great for this!). If you use this tool, you can also include verbiage in the event type’s description telling invitee’s they’ll be redirected to a form asking for a resume submission after booking and to please follow those instructions.
Additionally, you can use [Workflows](https://help.calendly.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051017814) to send additional resources to invitees upon booking. You can include a hyperlink in an email requesting invitees go to a form to submit a resume, or, ask them to respond to the email with a resume attached.
I know this isn't exactly what you wanted to hear but do hope the information helps, at least somewhat! Let us know!
