## What can I do with a Free typeform account?
Create a Free account and hit your stride in Typeform with the following features: 
  * **Unlimited typeforms:** Create as many forms as you like to share however you want.
  * [**Design flexibility**](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/where-can-i-find-the-design-tools-to-add-images-360058370272/)**:** Use one of our pre-designed [templates](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/use-a-template-360029294412/) or add your own images and color palette to forms. 
  * **10 responses/month:** Collect 10 responses per month across all your forms. This means you can collect 10 unique submissions in total from your forms with your free account, every month.
  * [**Logic**](https://www.typeform.com/help/s/logic-360005963492/)**:** Use Logic, including [Branching and calculations](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/use-branching-and-calculations-in-logic-to-show-relevant-questions-360054770931/), [scores](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/add-scores-and-calculations-to-your-typeforms-360054424131/), and [variables](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/how-to-add-and-change-variables-360052365212/), to show your respondents different questions based on their answers or calculate scores or prices. 
  * [**URL parameters**](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/using-hidden-fields-360052676612/)**:** Save and show information about your respondents in your typeform URL.
  * **Custom**[**Endings**](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/endings-360051791392/)**:** Change your Ending screen to show any message you’d like. Add multiple Endings to show different outcomes to respondents. 
  * [**Embedding**](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/embed-your-typeform-360029249212/)**:** Embed your typeform in your website in a variety of formats.
  * **Download your**[**results**](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/explore-individual-responses-in-the-results-panel-360029253732/)**:** Export your results in a spreadsheet and present them however you like. 
  * **Basic reports and metrics:** Check your typeform’s performance in the Results panel (**Note:** drop-off rates are available as part of the [Business plan](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/business-plan-360060586751/) and up). 
  * **Integrations** : Boost your form power by setting up integrations with [Calendly](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/schedule-calendly-appointments-with-typeform-4406271242900/), [Zapier](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/connect-typeform-to-your-favorite-tools-with-zapier-360029258552/), [Google Sheets](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/google-sheets-for-typeform-360029256192/), [Slack](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/send-typeform-responses-to-slack-in-real-time-360029312832/), [Mailchimp](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/grow-your-mailchimp-lists-with-typeform-360029577371/), [Airtable](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/send-data-to-airtable-with-a-typeform-360029263292/), [HubSpot](https://help.typeform.com/hc/en-us/articles/28399440594324), [Pipedrive](https://help.typeform.com/hc/en-us/articles/53086602401556) and more. The full list of available integrations can be viewed in the Connect panel of any typeform. 
  * [**Import from Google Forms** and learn how to get started](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/create-new-typeforms-by-importing-from-google-forms-4402931979028/).
  * [**Developer tools**](https://www.typeform.com/developers/)**:** Use Typeform’s APIs to design forms and collect responses without using the Typeform builder. Learn more.
  * **Typeform AI** : Tell our AI chatbot what you want to build, and let it create your forms for you.

## Do you offer a free trial of paid plans/features?
Yes! You can try many of our paid features on our Free plan. When you do, your form will be put into **trial mode**. This means you can play with our features, and if you want to share a form using them, you can upgrade to one of our paid plans: [Basic](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/basic-plan-360060585211/), [Plus](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/plus-plan-360060147392/) or [Business](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/business-plan-360060586751/).
If you upgrade and then decide Typeform is not for you, or your project has finished, you can downgrade back to a Free plan. Here's how that works:
  * **Downgrade date** : Your downgrade doesn’t take effect until the end of your paid period (you keep paid access until then).
  * **Features/forms** : After the downgrade, features not included in your new plan are restricted; forms with paid features will go into trial mode, preventing publishing/collecting unless you upgrade or remove paid features. [This article explains out how to remove paid features from forms](https://www.typeform.com/help/a/why-is-my-typeform-tagged-paid-360029258192/).
  * **Your data** : Previously collected responses and forms aren’t deleted after the downgrade.
  * **Team seats** : If your new tier allows fewer seats, the most recently added seats are removed


[This article explains in more detail what happens when you downgrade from a paid plan](https://help.typeform.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038887692-What-happens-if-I-cancel-my-paid-account).
