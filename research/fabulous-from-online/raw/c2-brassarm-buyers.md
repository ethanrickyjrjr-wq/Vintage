# Buyers Information at The Brass Armadillo Antique Mall**Ⓡ**
Does the Brass Armadillo Buy Antiques?
We do not buy merchandise directly. However, we do have antique dealers at Brass Armadillo Antique Malls near you who buy antiques and sell them to shoppers daily. Do you find yourself with special antique or vintage items, and need a buyer to take them off your hands? Antique furniture, collectibles, jewelry, toys, dishware, signs and so much more can find their home with a dealer at the Brass Armadillo!

## How to Sell Antiques To Dealers
If you have an antique or vintage item(s) that you believe are of value, you might be able to sell them to a Brass Armadillo dealer!
We will connect you with an antique dealer at your preferred [Brass Armadillo Antique Mall location](https://brassarmadillo.com/locations/) who may buy your antiques.
We can not guarantee that a dealer will be interested in purchasing your items; however, hopefully, we can provide you with information on how to proceed.
**> >** Please also keep in mind that when selling antiques to a dealer, they are buying your item for **resale** **purposes**. This means that the local antique dealer will only pay 30-60% of the retail value of that item.
