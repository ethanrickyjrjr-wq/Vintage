## **What Moves the Price**
Three things move the price most: the size of the space, the volume of furnishings, and the commission attached to those furnishings.
  * **Size:** Per-square-foot pricing means larger spaces result in higher fees, according to HomeAdvisor.
  * **Furnishings:** HomeAdvisor reports that furnishings can add $2,000 to $5,000 to total costs on average. A commission applies to the purchase price, so a bigger purchase carries a bigger commission.
  * **Coordination:** Thumbtack notes that the designer coordinates orders, shipments, and installation. Ask how many shipments your quote assumes.
