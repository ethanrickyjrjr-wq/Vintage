##  Frequently Asked Questions 
##  FAQs 
###  Why should I use Acuity Scheduling?
Acuity Scheduling supports businesses with scheduling all types of appointments and services. With advanced features that take the hassle out of online booking, payments, calendar management, and client communication, you get time back to do what you love and focus on your growth. 
### Does Acuity Scheduling offer a free trial?
Yes! When you sign up for Acuity Scheduling, you’ll be offered a free trial. When your trial ends, you won’t be charged until you choose an Acuity Scheduling plan. Visit our [pricing page](https://acuityscheduling.com/pricing) to learn more. 
### Do you offer a free plan to Acuity Scheduling?
Acuity doesn’t offer free appointment scheduling at this time, but you can use the [free trial](https://acuityscheduling.com/get-started?type=professional&btn=pricing-page-faq) to test out our scheduling software before committing to a paid version. No credit card required. 
### Can I pay for my account annually? Is there a discount?
Yes. You'll even receive a discount if you switch to an annual plan of our online scheduling software. Once you start your [free trial](https://acuityscheduling.com/get-started?type=professional&btn=pricing-page-faq), switch to annual billing in your account settings, and you'll be all set until next year. 
### Are there any hidden fees?
We don’t like surprises either. Listed prices don’t include applicable taxes, but there are no unexpected fees. No set-up fees, no cancellation fees, no support fees—nada.
