Imagine transforming your space into a picture of elegance and style without the large price tag and lengthy process usually associated with luxury interior design. That's exactly what 'Designer for a Day' offers.
Our unique service brings professional design expertise right to your doorstep, for just one day. We'll work with you to select the perfect paint colors and furniture to maximize both beauty and function, and provide a comprehensive plan for sourcing new items you need to bring the design to life.
Whether you're looking to refresh a single room or revamp your entire home, 'Designer for a Day' makes luxury design accessible and absolutely tailored to you. Let's create something beautiful together.

## Bespoke Design from Start to Finish
#### Design Consultation
Every step of designing a home is crucial. REH can join your team of architects and contractors to help interpret the goals of your project from start to finish.
#### _Color Consultation_
We work with you to build a palette that complements both your unique aesthetic and the home’s character. A blend of hues that evoke harmony and individuality. 
#### Space Planning
Creating a layout, floor plan, or furniture plan can have endless options and opportunities. We make sure yours checks all the boxes.
#### Fixture and Finish Selection
Hard finishes such as flooring, countertops, and cabinetry create the foundation of a cohesive home design, while lighting and fixtures are the jewelry of every room. We’re here to make your design shine.
#### _Furniture Selection_
Let us help you find the ideal furniture for every room in your home. From your living room and bedroom to your outdoor space and home office, we can create a warm and inviting atmosphere that feels like home.
#### Installation and Styling
This is the moment when everything falls into place. Our meticulous planning and preparation culminate in a seamless white glove installation, where we ensure that everything is just right.
