### **Rental Policy: Fees, Delivery, and Damage Waiver, Tent Set-Up**
At Party People AZ, we’re committed to providing transparent and seamless rental services. Below, you’ll find a comprehensive breakdown of our fees, damage waiver policy, and delivery options.
#### **Damage Waiver Fee**
We offer a **5% damage waiver** to protect against minor accidental damage such as scuffs, scratches, or small stains. This waiver does **not cover complete damage, destruction, loss, or theft** of items. For major damages, replacement costs will apply.
####  **Tent Set-Up Fee-** Price Depends on tent/canopy size. Can vary from $55-$450. 
*Staking the tent/canopy is no extra charge. If we cannot stake, sandbags and/or concrete blocks will be required and is charged accordingly* 
*Depending on the city, we may need to file for a tent permit and possibly a safety package and will require a seperate fee*
#### **Pickup Fee (Includes Loading and Unloading)**
For a flat **$15 pickup fee** , we make your rental experience hassle-free by offering:
  * **Loading and Unloading Services** : Our team will assist with safely loading your rentals when you pick them up and unloading them when returned.
  * **Tie-Down Assistance** : We ensure your rental items, such as tables, chairs, linens, and specialty equipment, are securely tied down for safe transport.


#### **Delivery Service**
We provide reliable **rental delivery services** , starting at a **base price of $95-$150 depending on the order size** , with mileage-based adjustments for longer distances. Our delivery service includes unloading and placement of items at your event location.
#### **Why Choose Us?**
  * Transparent fees with no hidden costs
  * Professional assistance for loading and unloading
  * Flexible options for pickup and delivery tailored to your needs
  * Expertise in handling rentals for **weddings, corporate events, birthday parties** , and more


#### **FAQs**
  1. **What does the damage waiver cover?**  
The damage waiver covers minor damage, like scratches or stains. It doesn’t cover completely destroyed, lost, or stolen items.
  2. **What does the $15 pickup fee include?**  
This fee covers our team assisting with both loading your rentals at pickup and unloading them upon return, ensuring your items are securely handled.
  3. **How is the delivery fee calculated?**  
Delivery starts at $95- $150, with additional charges based on mileage. Contact us for a precise quote.
  4. **Do you offer same-day delivery?**  
Yes, we offer same-day delivery for certain orders. Please call us to confirm availability.
  5. **Can I rent just a single item for pickup?**  
Absolutely! Whether you need one table or an entire set of event equipment, we’re happy to accommodate.
  6. **What areas do you deliver to?**  
Our delivery service covers all major event locations. Contact us for more details.
  7. **What happens if I return items late?**  
Late returns may incur additional fees. Please contact us if you anticipate delays to avoid charges.
  8. **Can I cancel my rental after booking?**  
Yes, cancellations are allowed, but fees may apply depending on how close the cancellation is to your event date.
