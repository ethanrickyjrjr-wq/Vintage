## FAQs about interior designer insurance
Find answers to common questions about interior design liability insurance and other types of coverage.
### Which insurance policies protect interior designers against errors on design plans and specifications?
Even the most experienced interior designer can make a mistake.
You might give a client an inaccurate estimate for how long a project will take, or make an error in a design plan that leads to bigger problems down the road. If your mistake leads to a lawsuit, then you could end up facing thousands of dollars in legal fees that could sink your business.
[Professional liability insurance](https://www.insureon.com/small-business-insurance/professional-liability "Professional liability insurance") for interior designers helps pay for legal costs related to errors, oversights, and other instances of [professional negligence](https://www.insureon.com/blog/professional-negligence-vs-ordinary-negligence "Professional negligence vs. ordinary negligence: Is your small business at risk?"). It provides crucial coverage for potential budget overruns and delays while you help your clients achieve their vision.
You might also see this policy referred to as [errors and omissions insurance (E&O)](https://www.insureon.com/small-business-insurance/errors-omissions "Errors and omissions insurance") or [professional indemnity insurance](https://www.insureon.com/small-business-insurance/professional-liability/professional-indemnity "Professional indemnity insurance").
### As an interior designer, do I need both general liability and professional liability insurance?
[General liability insurance](https://www.insureon.com/small-business-insurance/general-liability "General liability insurance") and professional liability insurance policies aren't required by law, but they provide crucial protection for interior designers and other small business owners.
Here's why you should invest in both of these policies:
  * **General liability insurance** pays for legal costs if you accidentally harm a client. It covers [bodily injuries](https://www.insureon.com/insurance-glossary/bodily-injury "What is a bodily injury?"), [property damage](https://www.insureon.com/insurance-glossary/property-damage "What is property damage?"), and [advertising injuries](https://www.insureon.com/insurance-glossary/personal-and-advertising-injury "What are personal and advertising injuries?"). This policy usually includes [product liability insurance](https://www.insureon.com/small-business-insurance/product-liability "Product liability insurance"), which covers harm caused by products. It might be [required](https://www.insureon.com/small-business-insurance/general-liability/requirements "When is general liability insurance required?") for a [commercial lease](https://www.insureon.com/blog/why-your-commercial-lease-requires-liability-insurance "Why your commercial lease requires tenant liability insurance") or a [business loan](https://www.insureon.com/blog/how-does-a-business-loan-work "How does a small business loan work?").
  * **Professional liability coverage** pays for legal expenses if a client sues your business for a mistake. Though it's not often [required](https://www.insureon.com/small-business-insurance/professional-liability/requirements "When is professional liability insurance required?"), you might need this coverage to work for another firm or a client.


If you do any work as an [architect](https://www.insureon.com/blog/architect-licensing-requirements "Architect license requirements"), [engineer](https://www.insureon.com/blog/engineer-licensing-requirements "Professional engineer license requirements"), or [general contractor](https://www.insureon.com/blog/general-contractor-licensing-requirements "General contractor license requirements"), then you might need professional indemnity insurance for interior designers in order to get licensed in [your state](https://www.insureon.com/small-business-insurance/professional-liability/state-laws "State requirements for professional liability insurance").
### What other interior designer insurance policies should I carry?
For a comprehensive [risk management](https://www.insureon.com/insurance-glossary/risk-management "What is risk management in business?") plan, interior design firms often invest in the following policies:
  * [**Commercial property insurance**](https://www.insureon.com/small-business-insurance/commercial-property "Commercial property insurance") covers your office and everything in it, such as computers, equipment, and furnishings. It'll pay for losses from a [fire](https://www.insureon.com/blog/fire-insurance-for-small-businesses-protect-yourself-and-your-bottom-line "Fire insurance for small businesses: Protect yourself and your bottom line"), storm, or burglary.
  * [**Inland marine insurance**](https://www.insureon.com/small-business-insurance/inland-marine "Inland marine insurance") provides protection for [tools and equipment](https://www.insureon.com/small-business-insurance/contractors-tools-equipment "Tools and equipment insurance") that travel with you to clients' homes, such as sketching supplies, laptops, and measuring tape.
  * **[Installation floater insurance](https://www.insureon.com/small-business-insurance/inland-marine/installation-floater-insurance "Installation floater insurance")** is a type of inland marine insurance. It covers [wallpaper](https://www.insureon.com/retail-business-insurance/paint-wallpaper-stores "Paint and wallpaper store insurance") and other materials while they are being transported, stored, or installed at a client's property.
  * [**Products-completed operations coverage**](https://www.insureon.com/insurance-glossary/products-completed-operations "Products-completed operations") offers coverage for harm caused by a product, such as a dresser that tips over and injures a child. It's also called product liability insurance.
  * **[Business income insurance](https://www.insureon.com/small-business-insurance/business-interruption/business-income-coverage "Business income coverage")** , also called [business interruption insurance](https://www.insureon.com/small-business-insurance/business-interruption "Business interruption insurance"), covers financial losses when your business is forced to close due to a fire or other covered property claim.
