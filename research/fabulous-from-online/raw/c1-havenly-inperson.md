# In-Person Design
## In-person interior design, now within reach.
Get Started In-Person
### All the benefits of digital design, but in real life!
STEP 1
Tell us about your Home
Tell us what rooms you need designed, your budget, and we will match you with the perfect designer in your area
STEP 2
Collaborate in your Home
Work with your designer in-person to create a personalized, cohesive design for you and your space - iterate until you absolutely love it
STEP 3
Get the Home you Love
View your design in photorealistic 3D, get free samples, & buy anything you love with your ordering concierge team.
### Our Markets
We are currently expanding, and at this moment are thrilled to offer in-person interior design services in the following markets:
### Your perfect Havenly designer, in real life.
Sit back and start designing. Our designers will take your home measurements and capture the unique elements of your space. We create floorplans, bring paint and material samples, and even show you a photorealistic 3D rendering to take the guesswork out of what will look amazing.
### We do the hard stuff, so you can focus on the fun part.
We know that finding the right home design partner is incredibly personal. To start, we learn more about your project, style preferences, and how you prefer to collaborate with your designer. Then, we work our matchmaking magic to pair you with your perfect Havenly in-person designer in your area! We are committed to designing a home you'll love - with our Happiness Guarantee, you can switch designers if you don't absolutely love your design.
