# THE VIRTUAL DESIGN PROCESS
DWELLING ENVY MAKES DECORATING YOUR SPACE A BREEZE!
##  GETTING STARTED 
First and foremost, I want to make sure I’m a good fit for you! That’s why we will plan a Discovery Call, where you can talk to me about your space, design needs, and style.   
  
After your discovery call, you’ll complete Dwelling Envy’s online style survey – a fun, easy way to help define your style and direction for your space!  
  
I offer a helpful step-by-step Resource Guide to help you through the process of taking great photos and measurements of your space. You can view the guide below to make this part a breeze!   
,  
Once I receive your completed style survey, within 24 - 48 hours, you'll receive your LOA (Letter of Agreement) complete with your Custom Flat Rate Quote. 
##  COLLABORATE 
Once signatures and payment is collected - you'll be prompted to schedule your Onboarding Call, where together, we'll thoroughly examine how you envision your space. After our call - sit back and relax as Dwelling Envy gets to work!  
  
Once your initial designs are complete, you’ll get access to all of your information via a personalized, password-protected webpage, available to you 24/7 for 1 year!   
  
I’m here for you, in any communication style that works for you, from email to the occasional Zoom call. If something isn’t working out with your design, don’t worry! All flat-rate services include one round of minor revisions at no additional cost. 
##  SHOP YOUR DESIGN 
Once you are 100% satisfied with your design, you get to order your recommended products! The Shoppable Board makes it super easy to order. And I’m here to answer any follow-up questions for a full 30 days after your purchases are made! With access to your design page for 365 days post project completion, you can shop your designs on YOUR timeline and refer back to your design boards and walk-through videos anytime!
