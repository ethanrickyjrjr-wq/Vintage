# Embed options overview
Updated Aug 20, 2026·3 min read
## Quick answer
Calendly offers three embed options:
  1. Inline embed – Displays your scheduling page directly within your website.
  2. Pop-up text – Opens your scheduling page in a pop-up when a link is clicked.
  3. Pop-up widget – Adds a floating button to your page that opens a pop-up when selected.

## Select Your Embed Style
Choose the option that works best for your website:
  * **Inline embed** – Calendly appears directly on your page.
  * **Pop-up text** – A link opens the scheduling page in a pop-up.
  * **Pop-up widget** – A floating button stays visible and opens a pop-up when selected.
