#### About us
![](https://www.sillafineantiques.com/movie-prop-rental/)
silla was born out of a passion for beautiful objects: special pieces with aesthetic and historical significance. In 2009, after years of collecting, Andrew Silla and his wife Grace began to work privately with clients from their residence in Southern Maryland. Quickly outgrowing the space, the business was moved from Maryland to Pennsylvania in 2012 and after several warehouse location changes it was firmly settled in the present brick-and-mortar location in downtown Shippensburg.   
  
The 9000 square foot brick-and-mortar gallery is home to a large collection of works of art and estate jewelry. We specialize in sculpture circa 1860 through 1930 with a particular emphasis on the Animaliers and as such the gallery always has a very large collection of exceptional European and American sculpture available on display.

# Join Our Collector Mailing List
We take a great deal of pride at silla in offering a curated selection of treasures and it is only natural that these historical objects could be enjoyed by much broader audiences through prop rental to the film and theatre industry.We offer a large inventory of over 500 carefully curated and catalogued Vintage and Antique Furniture, Sculpture, Fine Art, Decorative Arts, and Rugs available at flexible daily, weekly and monthly rates. This extensive collection provides a unique and authentic touch to any production, enhancing the visual storytelling experience. As a dealer to the collector market, silla is not traditionally a "prop rental house", which means you will find the quality, condition and rarity are of a superior grade, are ready-to-place and truly raise the bar of any period set.  
  
Our Film and Theatre Industry Services Include:
  * Daily, weekly and monthly rental of vintage furniture, antique furniture from the 18th through 21st centuries, fine art, sculpture and rugs
  * Convenient in-house delivery options to New York City, Washington D.C. and Baltimore. Speedy LTL freight options available to meet even the tightest of schedules.
  * The possibility of purchase of unwanted props - contact us with details on specific items for fast purchase quotes.


Why choose silla?
  * Authenticity: We specialize in authentic vintage and antique items that bring history to life on screen. Our items have been carefully sourced and restored to their original beauty.
  * Diverse Inventory: Our large inventory allows you to find the perfect accents to finish your set efficiently
  * Expert Guidance: Our knowledgeable team can assist you in selecting the right props and provide historical context to ensure your production captures the essence of the era.
  * Flexible Rental Options: We offer flexible rental terms at industry rates to accommodate the unique needs of your film or theatre production, making the process seamless and hassle-free. 
  * Easy Consolidated Shipping: We offer very fast and highly competitive in-house delivery for New York City, Washington D.C., Philadelphia, Baltimore and most destinations in the tri-state area; for further destinations, LTL freight shipments can be packed in-house efficiently and competitively whether the rental is a single piece or several dozen objects.


We look forward to chatting with you about your project's requirements and are available by phone at +1 (717) 708-9017 or by email at sales@sillafineantiques.com.
