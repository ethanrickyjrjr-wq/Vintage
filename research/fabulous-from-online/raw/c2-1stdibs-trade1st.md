#  Trade 1st Program for Design Professionals 
As a design professional, 1stDibs is proud to be your partner in the design community — ready to help you delight your clients and grow your brand.  
Through the Trade 1st program, we offer design professionals a suite of complimentary benefits, including everyday net price discounts of up to 30%, personalized support, comprehensive purchase protection and more.
Click [here](https://www.1stdibs.com/trade/apply/) to join.
![Trade 1st Program.png](https://support.1stdibs.com/hc/article_attachments/27235555844891)
## Exclusive Benefits for Trade 1st Members
The design community has been an integral part of the success of 1stDibs since its founding. Interior designers and architects are the creatives and visionaries who bring our unique and extraordinary supply to life. Ensuring that the needs of these professionals are met and maintaining our best-in-class Trade 1st program are top priorities for 1stDibs.
Thanks to meaningful input from designers, we have continually evolved and enhanced our program to better meet the changing needs of today’s design community.
### How it works:
You’ll earn 1 point for every $1 spent on 1stDibs. Each point brings you closer to unlocking a higher membership level — and even richer benefits.
  * Bronze: 10,000+ points
  * Silver: 25,000+ points
  * Gold: 100,000+ points
  * Platinum: 250,000+ points


### Benefits tailored to you:
**Trade-Exclusive Pricing** : Unlock up to 30% in net price discounts, tax exemption and our lowest price guarantee  
**Expert Support** : Receive personalized support from our trade service advisers on sourcing, delivery and installation  
**Purchase Protection** : Experience broad purchase protection that includes waived restocking fees  
**Marketing Opportunities** : Share work on your Designer Portfolio for a chance to be featured in marketing and editorial placements  
**Cash-Back Rewards** : Receive points with every purchase you make on 1stDibs and earn cash back
![Screenshot 2025-04-01 at 3.08.29 PM.png](https://support.1stdibs.com/hc/article_attachments/35554799024667)
Learn more [here](https://www.1stdibs.com/trade/rewards/overview/).
Questions? Contact us [here](https://www.1stdibs.com/contact/).
