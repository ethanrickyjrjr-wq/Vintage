# If You Can Dream It, We Can Find It
A private sourcing service for diamonds, vintage jewels, and rare finds.
Looking for a specific diamond, a signed vintage piece, or the designer jewel you can't stop thinking about? ZHIVA sources it. Leveraging years in the trade and a deep, trusted network, we hunt down the exact stone or jewel you have in mind — and bring you honest options, not pressure.
[Start the Hunt](https://zhivajewelry.com/pages/sourcing#inquire)
How It Works
## Well connected. Quietly relentless.
No request is too small or too large. Diamond studs, a unique watch, an old-cut solitaire, the rare designer piece you've been drooling over — we treat each search with the same care. Our goal isn't a single sale. It's to become the person you call for the rest of your collecting life.
