# Event Rental Prices in Miami-Dade: What You Pay Beyond the Item Price
By the All Miami Party Rental team · Miami-Dade and Broward, Florida
Event rental prices in Miami-Dade rarely end at the number printed next to the item. A package listed at $398 turns into a different bill once 7% sales tax, a delivery fee set by your zone and a 3% card fee are added, and that gap is where a budget can quietly slip. The price of the item is not the price of the event.
Think of the total as four layers: the item or package price, the tax, the delivery fee for your address, and the charges that switch on by hour, floor or payment method. It isn’t a flat rate, and it isn’t a number anyone can read off a web page; it’s a sum you can check line by line before you pay a deposit. Each layer below uses figures All Miami Party Rental publishes, checked on October 12, 2026, and the written quote governs over every one of them.
## What Sits on Top of the Item Price
  * **The item or package price.** It’s the only number most rental pages show, and for many categories it isn’t posted at all.
  * **Sales tax.** The [FAQ page](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/faqs/) says a **7%** sales tax applies to all rentals. [How Florida sales tax on party rentals works](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/florida-sales-tax-party-rentals/) gets its own page, and the Florida Department of Revenue explains the state rate on its [sales and use tax page](https://floridarevenue.com/taxes/taxesfees/Pages/sales_tax.aspx), noting that many counties add a surtax on top.
  * **Delivery.** A fee set by zone: **$40 to $90** in Miami-Dade and **$50 to $80** in Broward, laid out in the table below.
  * **Conditional charges.** Time, place and staffing charges, plus a **3%** transaction fee on cards, Venmo and Cash App.


Four lines, all checkable.
## Which Event Rental Prices in Miami-Dade Are Published
As of October 12, 2026, All Miami Party Rental posts prices for four things. Its [formal packages](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/our-packages/) run from **$398** (40 guests, a 20×20 tent, 40 resin chairs and four tables) to **$1,970** for the 80-guest Luxury package, and its [fun packages](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/fun-packages/) run from **$200** to **$1,500** ; [kids party package prices](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/kids-party-package-prices/) get their own breakdown. The [tents and tent accessories page](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/tents-tent-accessories/) prices ten white-tent sizes, from **$100** for a 10×10 to **$600** for a 20×60, and [tent rental prices by size](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/tent-rental-prices-size/) lays out what the list does and doesn’t cover. Farm tables are **$60** at 30 inches and **$90** at 42 inches. The package pages print bare numbers without a currency symbol; they’re US dollars.
Most other prices aren’t posted. Chairs, round tables, dance floors, artificial turf and bar furniture show no price or a request button, and the company says most prices stay unposted because availability has to be checked for your date first. A missing price isn’t a zero. It’s a question for the written quote.
Package math needs the same care. The company says its packages were built around what most customers ask for, so a per-guest figure, like the $9.45 to $24.63 spread across the formal packages, is arithmetic on a posted price, not a rate published per head. [Rental package cost per guest](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/rental-package-cost-per-guest/) works through it, and [wedding table and chair rental cost](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/wedding-table-chair-rental-cost/) covers the chairs, where no unit price is posted.
For categories with no posted price the method is the same, and each of these covers what moves the quote: [ceiling draping cost](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/ceiling-draping-cost/), [dance floor rental cost](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/dance-floor-rental-cost/), [cocktail table and bar rental cost](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/cocktail-bar-rental-cost/) and [artificial turf rental cost](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/artificial-turf-rental-cost/).

## One Package Priced Line by Line
Take formal package #1: 40 guests, a 20×20 tent, 40 resin chairs and four tables, listed at **$398** on October 12, 2026. The page doesn’t say whether tax or delivery is included, and the [FAQ page](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/faqs/) says most large structures, such as inflatables, tents, flooring, lighting and cooling, have setup and installation already included in the price, so the example treats tax and delivery as separate lines to confirm.
  * Package price: $398.00
  * Sales tax at 7% on the package: $27.86, for $425.86
  * Delivery in the general Miami zone: $40.00, for $465.86 if the quote doesn’t tax delivery, or $468.66 if it does
  * Card fee at 3%: about $13.98 on $465.86, for roughly $479.84, but only if the fee applies to the whole total, which the site doesn’t spell out


Per guest, the posted $398 is $9.95. After tax and delivery the same package comes to about $11.65 per guest, and about $12.00 with the card fee. That’s roughly a fifth above the posted figure, and it came from lines the package page never shows.
None of this is a quote. Your address, items and payment method move every line, so the example shows the shape of the bill, not your number.
## Charges That Have No Published Price
The FAQ names several charges and puts a number on none of them. Every one needs a figure in your written quote before you pay.
### Time: Early Setup and Late Pickup
A setup before **10 AM** carries an early delivery charge. A same-day pickup after **7 PM** , when the venue requires it, carries additional delivery charges. Neither amount is posted.
### Place: Buildings, Upper Floors and Parks
A delivery to a building or an upper floor needs a call for an estimate. Parks add the zone fees above, and the site schedules park pickups for about **5 PM** , because most parks have a cutoff of about 6 PM. Access matters as much as distance.
### People and Power: Supervision and Generators
Each inflatable requires one supervisor, whom you can provide or the company can supply for an additional fee that isn’t posted. Parks don’t supply power, so inflatables there need a generator, and the [safety rules page](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/safety-rules/) lists it at **$70** , or $60 each when a setup needs more than one.
[Party rental hidden fees](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/party-rental-hidden-fees/) lists the questions that turn each of these into a number. Ask for the number.
## When You Pay, and When Dates Get Tight
The deposit is collected when you reserve, usually by Zelle or card. The packages page says a minimum deposit applies, and the amount isn’t posted, so ask for it in your first message. Orders over **$400** also get a credit card authorization form by email, checks can’t be personal checks and must arrive at least **7 days** before the event, and no check is accepted on the day. The final balance is due before setup on delivery day. [Party rental deposit and payment terms](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/party-rental-deposit-payment-terms/) covers the sequence.
Read the cancellation terms before the deposit, not after. Per the [cancellation policy page](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/party-rental-cancellation-policy/), a deposit is refundable in full more than four days out, with restrictions on custom items, food and entertainment, and it’s forfeited the day before or the day of the event.
The site flags holiday dates itself. Its package pages carry a note that holiday prices may vary and some package deals may not be available, without saying by how much or on which dates. With Thanksgiving week and the year-end holidays ahead, that’s a reason to ask early, not a reason to assume a surcharge. [Holiday party rental prices](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/holiday-party-rental-prices/) covers what can change at peak dates, and [hurricane season event planning](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/hurricane-season-event-planning/) covers the weather side of the booking window.
If your date is close, there’s still room to ask. The FAQ says a rental can be made up to three days in advance, with availability not guaranteed. A short runway is a risk, not a rule.
Two days instead of one? Extra days are usually priced at half rate with no additional delivery fee, provided the venue lets equipment stay overnight; [multi-day event rental](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/multi-day-event-rental/) explains how that works.
## Your Options, Side by Side

| Option  | What you can see before you call  | What you still have to ask  | When it tends to fit  |  
| --- | --- | --- | --- |  
| A posted package  | Package price, guest count, what’s listed  | Delivery, tax treatment, setup, deposit amount, substitutions  | A setup near 40, 60 or 80 guests under a tent  |  
| An itemized quote  | A few prices, like white tents and farm tables  | Prices for chairs, round tables, floors, turf and bars, plus availability  | A setup that doesn’t match a package  |

A quote from anywhere is a fair comparison only when it breaks out the same four layers. A low number with blank lines isn’t a lower price; it’s an unfinished one. For the quality side of that trade, see [weighing quality against cost](https://allmiamipartyrental.com/cheap-party-rentals-miami/).
## How to Check Any Quote Before You Pay
  1. **Ask for the quote in writing, one item per line.** A single total hides which layer moved.
  2. **Check the tax line.** The site says 7% applies to all rentals; ask whether the quote applies it to the items, to delivery, or to both.
  3. **Match delivery to your address or park.** Compare the line with the zone table above.
  4. **Ask which time, place and staffing charges apply.** Early setup, late pickup, upper floors, supervision and generators each need a number or a written “to be confirmed.”
  5. **Ask what the 3% applies to,** or ask what other payment methods the company accepts.
  6. **Ask for the deposit amount, the cancellation terms and the balance due date** before you pay anything.

### is there sales tax on party rentals in Florida?
All Miami Party Rental says it charges a 7% sales tax on all rentals. This page isn’t tax advice, so ask the company for the tax line in writing and a tax professional about your case.
### is delivery included in the package price?
The package pages don’t say, so treat delivery as a separate line until a written quote says otherwise. The FAQ lists fees by zone, from $40 to $90 in Miami-Dade and $50 to $80 in Broward, with park deliveries at the higher end.
### how much more does paying by card cost?
Debit and credit card payments carry a 3% transaction fee, whether you pay by phone or in person, and so do Venmo and Cash App. The FAQ doesn’t list that fee for Zelle, cash or approved checks, and it doesn’t say which total the 3% applies to, so ask for the card total in writing.
### how far ahead should you book?
As soon as you have a date and a venue. The site says a rental can be made up to three days ahead without a guarantee of availability, and holiday dates may carry different prices and fewer package deals, so a longer runway gives you more choice.
### what happens to your deposit if plans change?
It depends on how much notice you give.
