Good questions
## Asked before every booking.
Anything else, call or text us.
How long does virtual design take?
About a week from the day we receive your photos, dimensions, and style quiz. Larger packages and whole space projects can take a little longer, and we confirm the timeline before you pay.
What do I need to send you?
Phone photos of the room from each corner, basic measurements, and a ten minute style quiz. That's it. We send a simple checklist after you book.
Are revisions included?
Yes. Every package includes at least one revision round, and our larger packages include two. We keep going until the concept feels like yours.
Do I have to buy the furniture through you?
No. Your shoppable list is yours. That said, anything from our showroom or lighting catalog comes with member pricing on your project, insured shipping, and one point of contact if anything goes wrong.
