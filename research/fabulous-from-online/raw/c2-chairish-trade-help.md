#  Trade Program: Help Finding a Piece for a Project 
As a [Trade Program](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44210963551505) member, we’d be happy to help you source! We work with our curation team to locate items that are the right style, color, and are within your budget.
[Trade Program members](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44210963551505) can request sourcing assistance by sending an email to tradesupport@chairish.com with the following information:
  * Key item categories you’re looking for
  * Style direction
  * Budget range per piece and/or project total
  * Timeline and any key dates
  * Dimension goals/limitations
  * Desired materials
  * Desired colors
  * Destination (zip code)
  * Any other project details


  
Please give us a few days to find some nice pieces for you.
