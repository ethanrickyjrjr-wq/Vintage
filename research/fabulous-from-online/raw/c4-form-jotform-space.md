Once you reach your account upload limit, your form will show an Over Quota error that’ll block users from submitting it. Remember that the account Upload Limit is the total storage space available in your account to store uploaded files through form submissions, including documents, images, and other media collected directly through your forms. 
The amount of storage space varies depending on your Jotform plan. People with Starter plans have limited upload space, while those with Bronze, Silver, or Gold plans have more storage. You can check our [Pricing Page](https://www.jotform.com/pricing/) to see the upload limit for each one.
To free up more upload space, you can either delete the uploaded files attached to a submission or delete and purge that entire submission. 
## Deleting the Uploaded Files Attached to a Submission
If your form submissions include file uploads, you can remove individual files directly from the submission to free up storage space or manage sensitive data. Here’s how to do it:
  1. Open your **Jotform Submission Table** , and click on the uploaded file.
  2. In the **Preview window** that opens, click on the **Trash Can icon**.


![How to Free Up Space in Your Account’s Upload Limit Image-1](https://www.jotform.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/jotform-form-tables-selecting-uploaded-file-to-del-30x17.png)[](https://www.jotform.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/jotform-form-tables-selecting-uploaded-file-to-del.png "Download Image")
  1. Then, in the **Confirmation window** that pops up, click on **Delete File(s)**.


![Jotform file deletion confirmation window with the 'Delete File\(s\)' button](https://www.jotform.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/jotform-submission-tables-permanently-delete-uploa-30x17.png)[](https://www.jotform.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/jotform-submission-tables-permanently-delete-uploa.png "Download Image")
## Deleting and Purging Submissions With Uploaded Files
To free up space in your account’s upload limit, you can delete and purge submissions with uploaded files from your table. It’s easy, here’s how:
  1. Open your **Jotform Submission Table** , and select the submissions with the uploaded files.
  2. Then, click on **Delete** in the upper-right corner of the page.


![Jotform Submission Table, selecting multiple entries to delete](https://www.jotform.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/jotform-form-tables-selecting-multiple-entries-to-1-1-30x17.png)[](https://www.jotform.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/jotform-form-tables-selecting-multiple-entries-to-1-1.png "Download Image")
  1. In the **Confirmation window** that pops up, click on**Move to Trash**.


![Jotform submission tables confirmation window with the 'Move to Trash' button highlighted](https://www.jotform.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/jotform-submission-tables-move-2-entries-to-trash--30x17.png)[](https://www.jotform.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/jotform-submission-tables-move-2-entries-to-trash-.png "Download Image")
  1. Next, in the first tab of the table, click on the**Three Dots** **icon**.
  2. Then, in the **Dropdown menu** , select **Trashed Entries**.


![Jotform Submission Table with the first tab's Three Dots icon clicked, selecting Trashed Entries from the dropdown menu](https://www.jotform.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/jotform-form-tables-selecting-trashed-entries-from-30x17.png)[](https://www.jotform.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/jotform-form-tables-selecting-trashed-entries-from.png "Download Image")
  1. In the **Trash** **menu** on the right side of the page, click on **Select All** or select a deleted submission and then click on **Delete Forever**.


![Jotform Trash menu on the right side of the Submission Table, showing 'Select All' and individual deleted submissions with the 'Delete Forever' button highlighted to permanently remove them](https://www.jotform.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Jotform-How-to-Clear-Your-Account-Uploads-purging-submissions-6-30x17.png)[](https://www.jotform.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Jotform-How-to-Clear-Your-Account-Uploads-purging-submissions-6.png "Download Image")
  1. Finally, in the **Confirmation window** that pops up, click on **Delete**.


![Jotform confirmation window prompting permanent deletion, with the 'Delete' button highlighted as the final step to permanently remove trashed submissions from the Submission Table](https://www.jotform.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Jotform-How-to-Clear-Your-Account-Uploads-purging-submissions-7-30x17.png)[](https://www.jotform.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Jotform-How-to-Clear-Your-Account-Uploads-purging-submissions-7.png "Download Image")
That’s it. To avoid losing any data, remember to [Download Your Submission Files](https://www.jotform.com/help/356-how-to-download-the-submission-files/) before deleting them.
