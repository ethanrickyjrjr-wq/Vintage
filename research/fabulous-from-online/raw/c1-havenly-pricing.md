## Pricing questions, answered
How much does Havenly cost? 
Online design starts at $89 per room. In-Person design starts at $299. No hidden fees, no surprises.
How long does it take to get my design? 
Online designers typically deliver your first concepts in 3 to 14 days. In-Person timelines are set with your designer based on your project.
Can I get on a call with my designer? 
Yes. Online, you can hop on a video or phone call to talk through your space anytime. In-Person, your designer comes to you.
What if I don’t love my design? 
Your designer revises your plan until it feels right. And with our Happiness Guarantee, if something’s off, we make it right.
Can I keep my existing furniture? 
Yes. Tell your designer what’s staying, whether it’s a sofa you love or a hand-me-down, and they’ll design around it.
Do I have to buy everything at once? 
Not at all. Your shopping list is yours to tackle on your own timeline, piece by piece or all at once.
