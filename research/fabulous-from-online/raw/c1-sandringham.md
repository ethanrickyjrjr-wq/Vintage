#  E-Design & How it Works
![Coastal LR 4.jpg](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5d80f4c6160e82532fe421e7/1633492224030-7XRCXK3TDA1M33QB9FVK/Coastal+LR+4.jpg)
E-design has become the hip, new trend, phenomenon for individuals and family’s who are looking for a way to decorate and transform their [home](http://i.viglink.com/?key=5483115e4e3be4b9d9353d7270b7f181&insertId=8a38e65ef7cf2050&type=L&exp=60%3ACI1C55A%3A8&libId=k4ookvnn0102x9sb000DAqwp6eio&loc=https%3A%2F%2Fsandringham-interiors.squarespace.com%2Fedesign%23&v=1&iid=8a38e65ef7cf2050&opt=true&out=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.shelving.com%2F&title=Sandringham%20Interiors%20-%20Squarespace&txt=%3Cspan%3Ehome%3C%2Fspan%3E "Link added by VigLink"), quickly, effortlessly and inexpensively with a process that is efficient, simple and stress free! A personalized design experience that is done entirely online. If traditional Interior Design services are not within your budget and you are happy to tackle a little DIY but just need some ideas and a little guidance, we can help get you started.
## The E-Design Process and How It Works……
##  **1. CHOOSE YOUR ROOM & **[**DESIGN**](https://www.sandringhaminteriors.com/pricing-services)**SERVICE TYPE**
We offer a selection of Design Services to choose from, pick the online [E-Design ](https://www.sandringhaminteriors.com/pricing-services)Service that best suits your needs. Details of our [Design Services ](https://www.sandringhaminteriors.com/pricing-services) and options together with a description of what is included with each service is available on the [Pricing & Services Page](https://www.sandringhaminteriors.com/pricing-services).
* * *
## **2. COMPLETE THE CLIENT QUESTIONNAIRE**
Simply fill out the [Client Questionnaire](https://www.sandringhaminteriors.com/questionnaire). This helps us to gather some information from you as to your design style preference, budget and lifestyle and how you envision your space. Once you submit this information, we will contact you within 1-2 business days to schedule a 30-40 minute conference call where we can discuss your decorating needs further.
* * *
## **3. PROVIDE MEASUREMENTS**
Once we have touched base, we will ask you to send measurements of the space using our convenient [Measuring Guide Template](https://www.sandringhaminteriors.com/measuring-guide) as well as any [furniture](http://i.viglink.com/?key=5483115e4e3be4b9d9353d7270b7f181&insertId=f06384ca4862779b&type=L&exp=60%3ACI1C55A%3A4&libId=k5793wxm0102x9sb000DA1kjxdu8q&loc=https%3A%2F%2Fsandringham-interiors.squarespace.com%2Fedesign%23&v=1&iid=f06384ca4862779b&opt=true&out=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.wayfair.com%2Findex.php&title=Sandringham%20Interiors%20-%20Squarespace&txt=%3Cspan%3Efurniture%3C%2Fspan%3E "Link added by VigLink") items, including accessories you would like to keep in the final design.
* * *
## **4. TAKE PHOTOS OF THE SPACE**
To help us get a better understanding of the architectural nature of the space and a visual of the room, we will ask that you send over between 3-4 photos to help with planning. (ie: Windows & Walls; Fireplace Walls; Ceilings; flat, sloped, etc).
* * *
## **5. PAYMENT DUE**
Once you have submitted all the information we will collect payment via the [Paypal Shopping Cart](https://www.sandringhaminteriors.com/pricing-services) and we can begin. **_Design Services Fees will be required up front prior to commencing any Design Project._**
* * *
## **6. REVIEW YOUR CONCEPT BOARD**
Within 4-5 days, we will send over an initial concept board that will outline the design aesthetic of the space and suggestions of products and materials we recommend. ***** Two Revisions are included with a [Design ](https://www.sandringhaminteriors.com/pricing-services)Service Option”
* * *
##  **7.**[**DESIGN**](https://www.sandringhaminteriors.com/pricing-services)**PACKAGE**
Following revisions, we will put together your [Design Package](https://www.sandringhaminteriors.com/pricing-services) that will include an Initial Concept Board, a true to scale Floor Plan illustration, 3-D Rendering (if requested) and an itemized Shopping List with Product recommendations and Vendor links. Depending on the [Design ](https://www.sandringhaminteriors.com/pricing-services)Service you choose, the current turnaround time is approximately 10 - 15 days from [the date](http://i.viglink.com/?key=5483115e4e3be4b9d9353d7270b7f181&insertId=d3b54a017820b87f&type=KW&exp=60%3ACI1C55A%3A1&libId=k5795sca0102x9sb000DA1jvhw416&loc=https%3A%2F%2Fsandringham-interiors.squarespace.com%2Fedesign%23&v=1&iid=d3b54a017820b87f&opt=true&out=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.amazon.com%2FDate-unputdownable-psychological-thriller-breathtaking-ebook%2Fdp%2FB07BN8L5KB&title=Sandringham%20Interiors%20-%20Squarespace&txt=%3Cspan%3Ethe%20%3C%2Fspan%3E%3Cspan%3Edate%3C%2Fspan%3E "Link added by VigLink") of your completed questionnaire and receipt of residence photographs. 
Your final [Design Package](https://www.sandringhaminteriors.com/pricing-services) will be sent to you via email and your Product Recommendations will be provided via a Shoppable PDF file or link to a Personalized and Private Pinterest Board, depending on your preference. 
* * *
## **8. YOU ARE ALL SET!! Install at your leisure**
Now you can implement the design at your own pace, in your own time and as your budget allows.
If you would like to submit any “after” photographs of your design once it has been concluded, please let me know and I would love to share pictures, with your permission, on my[ Social Media Channels](https://www.facebook.com/ukdzynr/).
