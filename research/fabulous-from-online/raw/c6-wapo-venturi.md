# Robert Venturi, postmodern architect who argued ‘Less is a bore,’ dies at 93
September 20, 2018More than **8 years ago**
loadingLoading...
Architect Robert Venturi in 1991. (George Widman/AP)
![](https://www.washingtonpost.com/wp-apps/imrs.php?src=https://s3.amazonaws.com/arc-authors/washpost/25a0491c-0839-43ac-87ab-e1657952fbcb.png&h=196&w=196)
By [Matt Schudel](https://www.washingtonpost.com/people/matt-schudel/)
Robert Venturi, an iconoclastic architect often considered the father of postmodernism who rejected sterile, glass-cube structures in favor of an inclusive, eclectic style that embraced community values and a touch of vulgarity, died Sept. 18 at his home in Philadelphia. He was 93.
