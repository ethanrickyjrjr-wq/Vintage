_Meet The Founder_
#### Hi, I’m **Sara** — Founder and Interior Designer with **A Thing for Styling**.
Styling is at the core of my work. It’s the difference between a room that looks good and a home that feels finished. With a **BA in Interior Design** and more than a decade of experience, I bring professional training together with a globally influenced design sensibility.
My work blends structure and soul—clear layouts, intentional styling, and details that hold meaning. Since 2021, I’ve offered virtual interior design services, working closely with clients to create spaces that feel cohesive, personal, and lived-in—without overwhelm.
