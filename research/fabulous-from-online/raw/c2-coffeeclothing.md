# Sourcing Requests
Need something specific for your wardrobe, a fresh collection for your store, or pieces for a film project? We’ve got you covered—tell us what you’re looking for, and we’ll handle the hunt!   
  
Thanks to our large network of vintage, archival, and collector sources and years of vintage sourcing, no project is too niche. 
## Why work with coffee and clothing?
Our large network of vintage, archival, and collector sources paired with our quick turn around time is unparalleled. We can deliver a collection or rare find within 2 weeks. 
**Collection Details**  
Our showroom features a curated selection of period-specific vintage clothing, specializing in militia, leather, workwear, cottons, denim, and boots. Our collection primarily focuses on the 1960s and 1970s, with limited offerings from the 1980s and early 1990s. All items are available at wholesale prices. Discounts are not available beyond the marked wholesale prices.
## sourcing terms
**Appointments  
** You don’t need an appointment to find your dream item! Just fill out our Sourcing form, and we’ll handle the rest. If your project requires an appointment, we will schedule one at a time that works for you, whether that’s in person or virtually. 
**Project Turnaround**  
Once your request has been received and reviewed, we will assign your project a timeline. We aim to wrap things up in 30 days (shipping time excluded). This includes keeping you in the loop with updates—like when we find a match or need your input.
**Deposit and Payment Terms  
** Although a deposit is not required to work with us, priority is for projects that provide one. If you put down a deposit (usually 25% of the project cost, up to $200), your project jumps to the front of the line. The deposit goes straight toward your purchase—so it’s not an extra fee, just a head start. More details about deposits are included in the Sourcing form
