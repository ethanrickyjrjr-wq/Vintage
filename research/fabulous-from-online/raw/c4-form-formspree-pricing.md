# Formspree Plans Formspree handles form submissions for sites without a backend: point a form at a Formspree endpoint and submissions arrive by email, in the dashboard, and through integrations. Prices are in USD. Monthly plans are billed each month; yearly plans are billed once for the year, at a lower effective monthly rate. ## Plans ### Free For testing and development - Monthly: $0/month - Yearly: free ### Personal For personal or portfolio sites - **Forms**: Make as many forms as you want. You can even group them into separate "projects" to stay organized. - **Projects**: Group forms by project. Used to manage site-wide settings. Also used for automatic deployment by the Formspree CLI - **Team Members**: Add a separate login for your teammates so you can work together to create and share your forms. - **Monthly Submissions**: This is how many times a month someone fills out and submits one of your forms. Don't worry—if you need more submissions you can always upgrade. - **Submission Archive**: If you ever miss a submission email, you can login and read your latest submissions up to the per-form archive limit. - **Linked Emails**: Link your clients’ email addresses to your account so you can set up and manage all their forms in one place. - **File Uploads**: You can accept up to 10 files in each form submission (each limited to 25MB). They'll be stored safely on a remote server and you'll receive links to them in your submission emails. No files will be sent directly by email. - **Private Emails**: Hide your email address from spam-bots and human visitors: use a random string instead of your email address in the action attribute of your form. - **Custom 'Thank You' Page**: For non-AJAX forms: By default, a user is shown a Formspree "Thank You" page after submission. You can provide an alternative URL per form to use instead. Read More. - **Ajax Forms**: Use AJAX to submit forms — this even works cross-origin. Create a new form in your account dashboard and set the Accept header on your form to application/json. - **Submission Export**: From the dashboard, you can export and download all submissions data as CSV or JSON. - **Search Submissions**: Search submissions by keywords or phrases. - **Custom reCAPTCHA**: Skip our default recaptcha page by disabling it or providing your own reCAPTCHA secret key. - **Restrict to domain**: Restrict forms so they can only be submitted from your own website. - **Basic Spam Filtering**: Formshield uses machine learning to block spam submissions from entering your inbox. Turn on reCAPTCHA with a single click. - **Advanced Spam Control**: Customize Formshield to better fit your use case. Adjust the sensitivity of the automatic filter and block by categories such as profanity or cryptocurrency. - **Custom Honeypot**: Create a custom honeypot field to catch spammers using a unique fieldname that they won't likely recognize. Read more. - **Custom Spam Rules**: Create custom rules to discard spam that gets through our filters. Quickly neutralize a wave of spam with an identifiable pattern. Read more. - **Basic Plugins**: Send your form submissions automatically to third-party applications like Google Sheets, Trello and more. Includes all Productivity plugins. See all our Plugins. - **Premium Plugins**: Integrate with Premium plugins like Klaviyo, Hubspot, Zendesk and more. Includes all CRM, Newsletter, Support and Other plugins. See all our plugins. - **API Access**: Fetch all your submissions programmatically from a JSON HTTP endpoint without having to login or share your password. Read More. - **Autoresponses**: Send an email to submitters confirming that you've received their submission. Read More. - **Custom Email Templates**: Customize the content and style of your submission notification and autoresponse emails. Replace the Formspree branding with your own, or customize the email markup however you like. - **Custom Email Domains**: Make emails more recognizable and consistent with your brand by setting up a custom domain. Submission emails and autoresponses are sent from your domain instead of formspree.io - **Rules Engine**: Dynamically route submissions to different emails, flag spam, or change the form redirect with your own logic rules. Read more. - **Priority Support**: Support requests sent through our help center and in-app feedback forms will be placed at the top of our support queue. - **Service Level Agreement**: Service Level Agreements are available when required and may include uptime guarantees and support response times. - **Full-Service Onboarding**: Our engineers will roll up their sleeves and help you move your forms over to Formspree, including making the necessary changes to your website. - **Phone Support**: Our support team will be just a phone call away to help you with any issues you encounter during business hours. - **Auto-Verified Emails**: Skip the email verification step. New email addresses can be automatically verified allowing you to streamline new project setup and offer a fully white-labeled experience. Also removes the requirement for an unsubscribe link at the bottom of each notification email. - **Multiple To Emails**: Send submissions to multiple email addresses ## Signing up Create an account at https://formspree.io/register, then pick a plan at https://formspree.io/plans.
