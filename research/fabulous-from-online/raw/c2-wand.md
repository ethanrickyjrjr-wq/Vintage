## Why work with Wand Vintage?
![A shelf of brass and iron objects for interior design sourcing service](https://www.wandvintage.com/wp-content/uploads/go-x/u/01c7e305-00fa-422d-9804-69d30e7ef9bf/l733,t0,w922,h922/image-768x768.jpg)
**Flexible and personal**

**Character-driven approach**
I don’t deal in bulk lots or generic production-line items. Every piece is chosen for its detail, tone, and atmosphere to help your spaces feel layered, lived-in and truly individual.
**Projects big and small**
