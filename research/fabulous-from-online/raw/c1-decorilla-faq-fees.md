## How much does it cost to run a project?
Flexibility is key in our pricing at Decorilla. You have full control over your budget. For targeted, professional assistance, we offer an hourly rate of just $119 to $500, depending on the experience level of the designer. Alternatively, you can opt for one of our comprehensive design packages, with prices beginning at an affordable $599. If you have specific needs or budget constraints, we're happy to customize a package that aligns perfectly with your project requirements. Our goal is to provide you with high-quality design solutions that fit comfortably within your budget.
View more pricing and flat-rate e-decor package details on our [pricing page.](https://www.decorilla.com/pricing)
## What is the difference between pricing tiers?
We offer different pricing tiers to cater to a variety of budgets and design needs. Each tier corresponds to the level of experience and expertise of our designers, ensuring that you get the best value for your investment.
Our pricing tiers are as follows:
  * **Bronze** : Ideal for budget-conscious clients, this tier features our skilled junior designers. They bring fresh, versatile perspectives to various design styles, offering an affordable way to rejuvenate your space.
  * **Silver** : This tier includes our more seasoned designers with a robust portfolio of successful projects. It strikes a perfect balance for clients seeking exceptional quality at a reasonable price.
  * **Gold** : Representing our top-tier offerings, the Gold design package features top-rated, highly experienced designers. This tier is tailored for clients desiring a luxurious design experience and the highest level of expertise in a wide spectrum of areas.
  * **Platinum** : Platinum: Our Platinum tier is the ultimate choice for those who desire to collaborate with our very best and most experienced designers, while also seeking comprehensive assistance with design implementation. This tier includes architectural drawings, seamless coordination with contractors, access to luxurious design options, and the added benefit of a dedicated project manager who oversees order tracking and provides regular updates.


Regardless of the tier, every client receives a comprehensive design package that includes two initial design concepts, a finalized design with floor plan, 3D renderings, and a tailored shopping list. The primary difference lies in the designer's level of experience, allowing you to select a tier that aligns with your specific needs and budget.
