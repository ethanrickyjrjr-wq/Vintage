# About NZ Rent A Car Group
  * [Home](https://www.nzrentacar.co.nz/)
  * About Us


New Zealand Rent A Car is a co-operative of well-established independent companies providing low cost [car rentals](https://www.nzrentacar.co.nz/) in all main centres in NZ. Our company is unique, offering you the convenience of nationwide coverage, combined with the personal service of independent owner-operators.
![youtube-video-thumbnail](https://i.ytimg.com/vi/YR1cPCrbjKk/maxresdefault.jpg)
New Zealand [Rent A Car](https://www.nzrentacar.co.nz/locations/nelson/) began in 1991 and has grown from 10 offices into a network of 10 branches throughout New Zealand, from Auckland in the North Island to the south in Dunedin. We are the largest multi-depot off-airport car rental company in NZ.
Our focus is on providing a friendly, personal service and safe, clean, economical car rentals at affordable prices.
We have over 1,000 rental vehicles from small hatchback cars to 12 seater minibuses, and at some branches, specialist vehicles including sports cars, furniture trucks, and a range of 4 wheel drive vehicles. Most branches have budget backpacker vehicles available for longer term hires at excellent rates.
We offer a courtesy pick-up service at all main airports, railway stations, bus depots and accommodation within 5kms of our car rental depots.
We are also members of the Rental Vehicle Association, which imposes a strict code of conduct for all members.

## Our Promise
New Zealand Rent A Car was established to offer low cost car hire in New Zealand through a network of independent owner-operated businesses. We offer the convenience of nationwide coverage, with the personal service you expect to find in a small business.
##### Roadside Assistance
All vehicles are covered by a free Roadside Assistance programme.
##### Professional, Friendly Service
New Zealand Rent A Car is a nationwide network of independent car hire companies operating throughout New Zealand. Each branch manager has personally promised to provide you with an efficient, friendly, personal service and a safe, clean, quality car hire at an affordable price.
##### Exceptional Value
Our car rentals offer exceptionally competitive rates, backed up by a New Zealand wide network of independently-owned branches whose sole concern is ensuring you get the best possible service and support – wherever you travel.
##### Cook Strait Crossings
When crossing between the North and South Island, you can drop off your low cost car hire in Wellington or Picton and pick up a fresh vehicle from our branch on the other side. This option is seasonally dependent and needs to be requested and confirmed during the booking process.
##### Free Courtesy Pick Up
We also offer free pick-up within 5km of any of our branches during business hours. Minimum 3 day hire​ may apply, please ask the branch.​
##### Airport Service
A “Meet and Greet” service is available from our branches for most domestic flights. Call us on Free Phone 0800 800 956 for airport service from any city. An airport fee may apply where the airport is more than 5kms from our nearest branch or hire is less than 3 days​.
##### One Way Rentals
One way rentals are available between most locations. Minimum rental periods and restrictions to some vehicle types may apply.
