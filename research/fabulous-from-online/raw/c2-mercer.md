##  We find what others can't 
###  Sourcing is at the heart of what we do. Our founder has spent decades building the relationships, knowledge, and eye required to find exceptional pieces—and we're happy to put that to work for you. 
We source across categories: designer handbags, jewelry, accessories, outerwear, art, and home goods. If it's something worth owning, we'll look for it.
Every piece we source is personally vetted, Entrupy authenticated where applicable, and priced transparently. A $100 sourcing deposit is required to submit a request—this is applied in full toward your final purchase price.
**No obligation beyond the deposit. If we can't find your piece, your deposit is refunded.**
![](https://mercervintage.com/cdn/shop/files/LeMarcheeggplantdish1.jpg?v=1725482801&width=320)
##  How It Works 
### **Step 01: Submit your deposit**
Your deposit secures your request and gets us started. Applied in full toward your final purchase price—fully refunded in full if we can't find your piece.
[ PAY $100 DEPOSIT ](https://mercervintage.com/products/sourcing-deposit)
![](https://mercervintage.com/cdn/shop/files/IMG_2897.heic?v=1770755379&width=320)
### **Step 02: Submit your request**
Tell us what you're looking for—brand, style, color, condition, budget, and any reference photos.
**We'll follow up within 2–3 business days to confirm receipt and ask any clarifying questions.** From there, active sourcing typically takes 7–14 business days, though rare pieces may take longer.

### **Step 03: We present your options**
When we find a match, we'll send you photos, condition details, and pricing before you commit. You decide whether it's right.
![](https://mercervintage.com/cdn/shop/files/IMG_6477.jpg?v=1742330038&width=320)
### **Step 04: Authentication & delivery**
Every sourced piece goes through our standard authentication and inspection process before it reaches you. Once approved, your piece ships with full insurance and tracking.
