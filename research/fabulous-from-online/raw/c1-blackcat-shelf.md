## Description
This listing is for a digital customized styling for one set of shelves (one unit/bookshelf, or up to 6 shelves total). The process is simple — send photos of your shelves and a description of your style (and/or inspiration photos), and you’ll receive a fully customized digital design, drawn to scale, showing exactly how your bookshelves will appear when completed, which will include:
  * any existing items you’d like to include (decor, pictures, etc.)
  * recommendations for new items (decor, art, plants, etc.)
  * recommended overall style and color palette


Once finalized, I’ll provide a source/shopping list specific to your budget identifying all items in the design and where to purchase them.
Everything you need to decorate your set of shelves will be provided online in one convenient package.
Designs will be provided via email in image file format (.jpg, .pdf, etc.). Shopping lists will be provided as Pinterest boards.
