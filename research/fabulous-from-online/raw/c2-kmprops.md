# - Creating Magical Memories for a Lifetime - 
## - About Us - 
**My husband and I have always had a passion for antiquing ever since we met each other over 10 years ago. This has been the impetus behind the KM Props and Events, LLC.Even our home is filled with distressed and shabby chic furniture and our favorite treasures. We now look for and create new trends for our clients, always searching for “new” pieces to add to our collection. Our services include: Vintage and rustic rentals, design and styling, weddings, corporate events, and photo shoots.**
