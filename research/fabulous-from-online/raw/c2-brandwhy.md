## What are the PRICES like?  

##  _It’s about curating intentional and unique treasures that enrich your style story  
  
We are NOT pursuing labels for their own sake_  

BrandWhy Style’s Virtual clients will incur a **Sourcing Fee** on a per item purchased basis.   
  
Our fee is **12% of the item price** , with a minimum of $25 and a maximum of $175.   
  
Our Vintage Sourcing Fee is non-refundable for any reason as it covers the work performed by your personal stylist. **The Sourcing Fee must be prepaid via Zelle, Venmo or Paypal to initiate purchase process.** Once fee has been paid, details for direct purchase of your item will be passed along.  
  
Pickup can be coordinated complimentary from a location in 76107. Nationwide shipping will be a direct passthrough. Should the item require insurance for transportation, this will also be included in the price of shipment to protect your investment should damage occur.  
  
  

Our pricing is designed to remain proportional and transparent across all acquisitions.  
  
Smaller finds remain accessible.  
Mid-range pieces reflect the depth of sourcing involved.  
High-value acquisitions are thoughtfully capped.  
  
  

Examples of Vintage Sourcing Fee across various item price ranges -  
  
A $70 piece → $25 (Min Applies)  
A $500 piece → $60  
A $1,200 piece → $144
