# E-Design Questionnaire
Let me help you create a beautiful and eco-conscious home!
Step 1
Fill out the e-design questionnaire below. Most questions are optional. If you aren’t sure of an answer, you can leave it blank and we’ll chat about it later.
Step 2
Within 24 hours of submitting the questionnaire, you’ll receive an initial email with payment info and instructions on how to get the process started. (Unless you’ve already paid!)
Step 3
Send photos and measurements of your space, plus inspiration photos of spaces you love to larissa(at)ofhousesandtrees.com.
Step 4
Let the designing begin! Estimated delivery of your initial custom design is 7 to 10 days after I receive your questionnaire answers and photos/measurements. However, this timeline can be shorter or longer depending on the scope of the project. If you are in a time crunch, please let me know and I will do my best to accommodate your needs.
Step 5
Once you receive your final design board and shopping list, you purchase the products, set up your room and enjoy.
Have questions? Feel free to [contact me here](https://ofhousesandtrees.com/contact).
Looking forward to working with you!
