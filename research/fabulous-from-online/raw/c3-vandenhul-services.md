## How pricing works
The ranges above are real starting points, not final numbers. Every property is different: size, how long it's been on the market, and how much staging or design it actually needs all move the final price. You'll get a specific number during your consultation, before any work starts.
Step by Step
## What actually happens, start to finish
No guessing about what comes next. Here's the real path from your first call to a staged, market-ready property.
1
Consultation
### Schedule your consultation
Book a time that works for you, as the agent or the homeowner.
2
Consultation
### Walkthrough of the property
Mike and Deena walk the property with you and assess what's actually working against the sale.
3
Consultation
### Scope and quote
You get a specific plan for staging, and design if the property needs it, with a real number attached.
4
Staging
### Contract and invoice
Paperwork handled upfront, so there's no confusion once the work starts.
5
Staging
### Inventory selected for your property
Furniture and decor chosen specifically for the property's size, style, and the buyers it needs to reach.
6
Staging
### Staging day
Pack, load, delivery, and setup, handled by the team in one visit.
7
Design
### Deeper styling, if the property needs it
For listings that need more than furniture placement to show well, scoped at your consultation.
8
Delivery
### Follow-up check on the property
We check in during the listing period, not just on day one.
9
Delivery
### De-stage, scheduled around your timeline
Once the home is under contract or the staging period ends, pickup is scheduled around the closing, not the other way around.
