**I'll find your dream piece.**
From the perfect dress to a rare vintage staple, we'll track it down for you. This is for the girls who know what they want (or at least the vibe).
Read how sourcing works and fill out the request form. This is not a styling request, but a request to find a specific item.
Fill out the form with what you're looking for, sizing, your budget, and any timeline requests. We'll be in touch within a week. If it's a go, expect an 8–10 week turnaround to track it down.
