# Designer for a Day- Interior Design Service
$1250.00 USD
Need a designer for a day to come measure your space and tell you want you will need to make that space come to life? Purchase Designer for a Day and have the designer VIP treatment. Our Designer will come to your home after your discovery call and design consultation, and will walk you through purchasing items online **or** at your favorite store. The designer will help you make decisions **on the spot**. This service is **6 working hours** by your side to discuss problems and finds solutions to your space, selecting colors, patterns, and textures to bring joy to your space.  
By the end of the day all of your major items will be selected, and the whole design vision is easy to execute.  
We'll take a break half way for delivered **complimentary 30 min lunch**.  
This also includes **2 additional office hours** outside our day together. These hours can be for additional research, documentation creation OR follow up phone conversation.  
**How to Maximize Results:**
  * Be Ready to share all your inspiration images with us. Pinterest boards, Social Media Accts., Favorite places to visit, things/places that inspire you, etc..
  * Make sure everyone that will be involved in making design decisions will be present and have already discuss your investment amount for this project. You may not know it to the exact dollar amount, but you must have an idea.
  * Our main goal is to create a space that tells your story and brings you joy by mixing colors, patterns, and textures, so get inspired!


**FAQ**  
1. Can I use this for multiple rooms? During the Design consultation YES, but ONLY If you mentioned them during the discovery call and on the design questionnaire. In order for us to be able to get detailed information per room, the rooms must be prioritized.  
2. Can the consultation be online? YES, let us know if you would prefer it this way- it'll be a different package.  
3. Can I reschedule my time? Yes you can. There is no fee for rescheduling, we need a heads up of at least 2 hours .  
4. Are appointments refundable? No. If we do not hear from your to reschedule of at least 2 hours your forfeit your appointment and funds.
