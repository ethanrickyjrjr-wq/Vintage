How do customs duties/taxes work with items shipping from overseas? _Expand_
When goods are not shipped domestically (within your country) or within a single customs union, such as the European Union, you are responsible for any inbound duties and taxes which your local customs authority deems appropriate. 
These fees are in addition to the price and shipping cost you are charged when checking out. Applicable duties and taxes are calculated at the border and based on the nature of the item, final price of the item, and the destination country.
You can find more information, including import questions for the US, EU and UK [here](https://support.1stdibs.com/hc/en-us/articles/14491601612827-Customs-Duties-and-Taxes-for-International-Shipments).
How can I find out the lead time of an item?_Expand_
Most new and custom items on our site will have a production time.  
  
The production time is also known as lead time, it is the amount of time it will take for the piece to be created.  
  
If there is a production time, it will be posted on the main item page, as shown below: 
![](https://s29719.pcdn.co/about/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/pf-300x186.png)
The production time is separate from shipping and handling time. Please account for lead time in addition to the shipment time advised.
