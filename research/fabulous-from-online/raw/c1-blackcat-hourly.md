## Description
### **How This Fits Into Your Design Process**
This service is designed for the moments when you’re about to make a decision — and want to make sure you’re choosing the right option.
Instead of guessing or second-guessing, you get clear, professional input before you move forward.
* * *
### **What You Can Ask**
  * “Which of these options works better in my space?”
  * “Is this the right size or scale?”
  * “Do these finishes and materials work together?”
  * “Should I buy this — or keep looking?”
  * “Does this layout or placement make sense?”


* * *
### **What You’ll Receive**
  * A clear, personalized recommendation
  * The reasoning behind the decision
  * Guidance on what to do next
  * Optional links or quick visuals (if helpful)


* * *
### **Timing & Format**
This is a focused, one-question consultation.
Responses are delivered within 2–3 business days, giving you thoughtful, well-considered guidance you can trust.
* * *
### **Choose the Right Level of Support**
Different stages of your project call for different types of support:
**Finish My Room**  
→ For a complete plan to bring your space together
**Designer On Call (this service)**  
→ For help making a specific decision before you buy
**Designer Insider**  
→ For ongoing, real-time support as you shop and make decisions
* * *
### **Need Help While You’re Actively Shopping?**
If you want the ability to ask questions as they come up — whether you’re in a store or browsing online — Designer Insider gives you ongoing access to get input in real time.
→ [Explore Designer Insider](https://www.blackcatinteriors.com/product/designer-insider-2/)
* * *
### **Why This Matters**
Most design mistakes don’t come from big decisions —  
they come from small uncertainties that add up.
This service helps you:
  * Avoid costly mistakes
  * Make confident decisions faster
  * Keep your project moving forward
