**[Mary Jane West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mae%20West "w:Mae West")** ([August 17](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/August_17 "August 17"), [1893](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/1893 "1893") – [November 22](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/November_22 "November 22"), [1980](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/1980 "1980")) was an American actress and playwright, most commonly known as**"Mae" West**.
## Quotes
[[edit](https://en.wikiquote.org/w/index.php?title=Mae_West&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Quotes")]
[![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/c2/Mae_West_in_I%27m_No_Angel_2.jpg/250px-Mae_West_in_I%27m_No_Angel_2.jpg?utm_source=en.wikiquote.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/File:Mae_West_in_I'm_No_Angel_2.jpg)When I'm good, I'm very good. When I'm bad, I'm better. [![](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/57/Mae_West_Allan_Warren.jpg/250px-Mae_West_Allan_Warren.jpg?utm_source=en.wikiquote.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/File:Mae_West_Allan_Warren.jpg)When you got the personality, you don't need the nudity.
  * Goodness had nothing to do with it, dearie. 
    * Response to an exclamation, "Goodness! What beautiful diamonds!" in _[Night After Night](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Night%20After%20Night%20\(film\) "w:Night After Night \(film\)")_ (1932). She later used _Goodness had nothing to do with it_ as the title of her (1953) autobiography.


  * Why don't you come up sometime and see me? … Come on up, I'll tell your fortune. 
    * _[She Done Him Wrong](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/She_Done_Him_Wrong "She Done Him Wrong")_ (1933); this statement has widely said to be misquoted with the paraphrase: "Why don't you come up and see me sometime?" In fact, Mae West did say: "come up and see me sometime.” in these two movies _[I'm No Angel](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/I'm_No_Angel "I'm No Angel")_ and _[Sextette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sextette "w:Sextette")_ <http://quotationize.com/come-up-see-me-sometime-not-misquote-mae-west/>


  * Oh, Beulah, Peel me a grape. 
    * _I'm No Angel_ (1933)


  * I only like two kinds of men, domestic and foreign. 
    * _I'm No Angel_ (1933)


  * When I'm good, I'm very good. But when I'm bad, I'm better. 
    * _I'm No Angel_ (1933)


  * It's not the men in your life that count, it's the life in your men. 
    * _I'm No Angel_ (1933)


  * When women go wrong, men go right after them. 
    * _She Done Him Wrong_ (1933)


  * A man in the house is worth two in the street. 
    * _[Belle of the Nineties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belle%20of%20the%20Nineties "w:Belle of the Nineties")_ (1934)


  * When I'm caught between two evils, I generally like to take the one I never tried. 
    * _[Klondike Annie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klondike%20Annie "w:Klondike Annie")_ (1936)


  * You ought to get out of those wet clothes and into a dry [martini](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Martini "Martini"). 
    * _[Every Day's a Holiday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Every%20Day's%20a%20Holiday%20\(1937%20film\) "w:Every Day's a Holiday \(1937 film\)")_ (1937)


  * You take one thing and add it to another and you get two. Two and two is four; and five'll get you ten if you know how to work it! 
    * _[My Little Chickadee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My%20Little%20Chickadee "w:My Little Chickadee")_ (1940)


  * I used to be Snow White, but I drifted. 
    * [Interview](http://books.google.com/books?id=jU8EAAAAMBAJ&q=%22I+used+to+be+Snow+White+but+I+drifted%22&pg=PA64-IA1#v=onepage) in [_Life_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life%20\(magazine\) "w:Life \(magazine\)") magazine (April 18, 1969)


  * I'm the kinda girl who works for Paramount by day, and Fox all night 
    * _Sextette_ (1978)


  * To her British lover about to climb in bed with 80-something Mae: She said that she hoped soon to be able to say what Paul Revere said — 'The British are coming'. This was the last one-liner Mae ever uttered on film. 
    * _Sextette_ (1978)


  * Is that a gun in your pocket, or are you just happy to see me? 
    * _Sextette_[[1]](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Mae_West#cite_note-1)[[2]](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Mae_West#cite_note-2) (1978)


  * When you got the personality, you don't need the nudity. 
    * Quoted in "For Women, Monologues They Haven't Heard" by Susan Pomerance, Dramaline Publications (1985)


  * Marriage is a fine institution, but I'm not ready for an institution. 
    * #149 in _The 2,548 Best Things Anybody Ever Said_ (2006) by Robert Byrne


  * Give a man a free hand and he'll run it all over you. 
    * #684 in _The 2,548 Best Things Anybody Ever Said_ (2006) by Robert Byrne


  * I've been in more laps than a napkin. 
    * #685 in _The 2,548 Best Things Anybody Ever Said_ (2006) by Robert Byrne


  * She's the kind of girl who climbed the ladder of success wrong by wrong. 
    * #832 in _The 2,548 Best Things Anybody Ever Said_ (2006) by Robert Byrne


  * I've always had a weakness for foreign affairs. 
    * [Person-to-Person](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Person%20to%20Person "w:Person to Person") interview ([CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS "w:CBS")) with [Charles Collingwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles%20Collingwood%20\(journalist\) "w:Charles Collingwood \(journalist\)"), [September 1959](http://books.google.com/books?id=21R_KPMzH2EC&q=%22I've+always+had+a+weakness+for+foreign+affairs%22&pg=PA82#v=onepage)


## Misattributed
[[edit](https://en.wikiquote.org/w/index.php?title=Mae_West&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Misattributed")]
  * You only live once, but if you do it right, once is enough. 
    * "One lives but once in the world" (or "man lebt nur einmal") has roots dating back to at least 1774 in [Goethe](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Goethe "Goethe")'s _Clavigo_ , but the defining second clause "but if you do it right, once is enough" is attributed to West on social media. See [YOLO](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/YOLO "wikt:YOLO").
