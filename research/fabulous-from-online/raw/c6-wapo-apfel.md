# Iris Apfel, designer who became ‘geriatric starlet’ in 80s, dies at 102
As a senior citizen, she became an unlikely fashion celebrity for her outre style, including her trademark oversized, owlish spectacles.
March 1, 2024More than **2 years ago**
Loading...Loading...Loading...Loading...Loading...
Iris Apfel, left, and Ken Downing attend the New York Fashion Week Spring and Summer 2016 Delpozo fashion show in 2015. (Andy Kropa/AP)
![](https://www.washingtonpost.com/wp-apps/imrs.php?src=https://author-service-images-prod-us-east-1.publishing.aws.arc.pub/washpost/0fc42bf6-407b-4be7-8f2d-ec24944b2854.png&h=196&w=196)
By [Megan McDonough](https://www.washingtonpost.com/people/megan-mcdonough/)
Iris Apfel, a New York textile designer, socialite and self-described “geriatric starlet” who became an unlikely fashion celebrity in her 80s for her outré style, died March 1 at her home in Palm Beach, Fla. She was 102.
