## What Is E-Design?
E-design is a simple and affordable alternative to full-service interior design that is done online.
At Michele Safra, we make it easy by offering e-design packages. These packages include everything you will need in order to design a space that fits you in the best way. Each package will have a flat fee per room which varies depending on the size of the room. Therefore you will have the freedom to choose which package suits you best to meet your needs.

## FAQ
## _WHAT ARE THE ADVANTAGES OF E-DESIGN?_
E-design allows you to design your home no matter where you are or what your timeline and budget looks like. No time constraints, no appointments, no scheduling.
## _WHY DID WE START E-DESIGN?_
Satisfying the demand of social media followers and clients near and afar that request answers to specific design questions or whole projects in a simple, affordable and efficient manner.
## _How long will it take to get my room E-design?_
Once we receive all the material requested by us (questionnaire, payment, pictures, dimensions, etc.), you will receive an E-Design Presentation Package within 2-3 weeks via e-mail. The presentation will be sent as a PDF file that can be downloaded and printed.
## _Can some of my existing furniture pieces be incorporated in the design plan?_
Absolutely! All you will need to do is send us pictures and dimensions of the pieces you would like to keep and will incorporate them into the design.
## _If I have an open floor plan and two rooms are combined, which room do I select first?_
Our suggestion would be to purchase a design plan for both places in this case. Furthermore places which open into one another tend to feel like a single space. Therefore to get a truly transformative feel that you are looking for, you should take into consideration everything your eyes see in a single view.
## _DO YOU WORK WITHIN THE BUDGET GIVEN TO  
SPEND ON THE PLAN YOU’VE DESIGNED?_
We work within a given budget in the “Full Service” Plan. We can work with any given budget but just keep in mind, our ability to create a dramatic transformation will be determined by what you’ve set aside for this project.
## _What happens if I wait to purchase an item, and then it’s no longer available?_
You can always shop for a similar item if something becomes discontinued. Keep in mind that manufactured goods are rapidly changing and there are no guarantees that things will be in stock. Therefore we recommend that you wait until you are financially & practically ready to implement a design change before purchasing our services.
## _Will you make changes if I don’t like something you’ve selected?_
If you would like to change an item or two, you may send us your thoughts and we will provide up to two re-selections per design package at no charge.
## _IS E-DESIGN FOR YOU?_
E-design is ideal for clients who don’t mind doing a little leg work themselves to manage, prepare and implement a project with design guidance and expertise.
