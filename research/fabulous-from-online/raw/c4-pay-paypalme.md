## Frequently asked questions
### What is a PayPal link?
With a PayPal link, you can send and request money by sharing a unique, one-time payment link directly to your intended recipient via text, email, DMs or other apps. This makes paying and getting paid as simple as sending a message.
### How do I send money with a PayPal link?
  1. Open the PayPal app and log in.
  2. Tap “Send/Request”.
  3. Choose “Send and request money with a link”.
  4. Enter the amount, choose the currency, and add a note if you’d like. Select “Send.”
  5. Choose whether you’re “Paying for an item or service” or “Sending to a friend.”
  6. Select how you want to pay and choose “Next”.
  7. Add the name of your recipient and select “Next.”
  8. Review the payment details and select “Create PayPal Link”.
  9. Click “Share PayPal Link” to share it with your recipient using your preferred communication method.


### How do I request money with a PayPal link?
  1. Open the PayPal app and log in.
  2. Tap “Send/Request”.
  3. Choose “Send and request money with a link.”
  4. Enter the amount, choose the currency, and add a note if you’d like. Select “Request.”
  5. Review the request details and select “Create PayPal Link”.
  6. Click “Share PayPal Link” to share it with your recipient using your preferred communication method.


Recipients have 10 days to pay the request via the PayPal app or PayPal.com before the link automatically expires. A PayPal account is required to pay a payment request.
### How does the recipient accept sent funds or pay the request?
Recipients must click on the link and log into the PayPal app or PayPal.com to accept sent funds or pay the payment request. Recipients have 10 days to accept funds or pay the request before the link automatically expires. You can send a reminder or cancel the payment/request with the PayPal app before the link is accepted or paid.
### Does my PayPal link expire?
Manage your payment activity in the PayPal app. View link status, send a reminder or cancel if needed before the link is claimed with the PayPal app. Unclaimed links expire after 10 days. Send a reminder or cancel if needed before the link is claimed with the PayPal app.
