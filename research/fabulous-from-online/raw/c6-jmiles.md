## Principal Designer, Jessica Waters
Hi, I’m Jessica, founder of J. Miles Design — and I know exactly how overwhelming it feels to want a beautiful home but not know where to start. ## Frequently Asked Questions
### [What is virtual interior design?](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)[](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)
[What is virtual interior design?](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)[](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)
Virtual interior design — also called e-design or online interior design — is a professional home design service delivered entirely online. You share photos, measurements, and style preferences with your designer, and receive a complete, personalized design plan digitally: custom mood boards, floor plan layouts, curated furniture and decor selections, and a shoppable product list with direct links. No in-person appointments. No travel. Just a polished design plan delivered to your inbox.
### [How much does virtual interior design cost?](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)[](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)
[How much does virtual interior design cost?](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)[](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)
At J. Miles Design, we offer several package options depending on how many rooms you need and the scope of your project — from single-room à la carte packages to whole-home e-design covering up to 15 spaces. All packages are flat-fee with no hidden markups. You shop your curated product list at retail prices from stores like Wayfair, Amazon, West Elm, and Target.
### [How long does the design process take?](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)[](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)
[How long does the design process take?](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)[](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)
Most designs are delivered within 2 to 4 weeks after you complete your design questionnaire. Single-room packages tend to move fastest. Whole-home packages and new build designs — which involve finish selections, multiple rooms, and 3D renderings — typically take 3 to 6 weeks depending on scope and revision rounds.
### [Can you work with my existing furniture?](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)[](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)
[Can you work with my existing furniture?](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)[](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)
Absolutely. We start with what you already have and only suggest new pieces when needed to complete the look. This keeps your design cost-effective and ensures nothing goes to waste. You'll tell us what to keep and what you're open to replacing in your design questionnaire.
### [Do I have to purchase from your links?](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)[](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)
[Do I have to purchase from your links?](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)[](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)
Not at all. Our curated shoppable product lists are there for your convenience, but you are never required to buy through our links. You're free to source any item from wherever you prefer. We source from popular retailers so you're shopping familiar, trusted places — but the choice is always yours.
### [Are your designs suitable for families with kids and pets?](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)[](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)
[Are your designs suitable for families with kids and pets?](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)[](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)
Yes — kid-friendly and pet-friendly design is one of our specialties. We know how to select durable, stylish materials and furniture that can actually handle the demands of a busy household. Just tell us about your kids, pets, and lifestyle needs in your design questionnaire, and we'll factor everything in from day one.
### [What if I don't love the design?](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)[](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)
[What if I don't love the design?](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)[](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)
Our goal is for you to love every detail of your home. Each room includes up to 8 rounds of revisions — so we keep refining until it's exactly right. If you need additional edits beyond that, they're available for a small additional fee. Simply email us and we'll take care of it.
### [Do you recommend contractors?   
  
](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)[](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)
[Do you recommend contractors?   
  
](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)[](https://jmilesdesign.com/#/)
At J. Miles Designs we strive to provide a seamless design experience. In some cases, however, your project may require products or services that fall outside of our scope of work. When that happens, we are happy to connect you with one of our trusted third-party vendors. Once you choose to move forward with a third-party vendor, All communication, scheduling, measurements, orders, installation, billing, warranties, product quality and any related services or concerns will be handled directly between you and the vendor.   
  
While we only refer professionals we know and trust, J. Miles Designs is not responsible for the performance, workmanship, products, timelines, pricing, warranties, or any issues that may arise from services provided by third-party vendors. Our role is limited to making that introduction and that any agreements or contracts you enter into after that point are solely between you and the vendor.   
  
We appreciate your understanding and are always happy to continue assisting with the design aspects of your project.
