**Art department** in [filmmaking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filmmaking "Filmmaking") terms means the section of a [production](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_production "Film production")'s [crew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_crew "Film crew") concerned with visual artistry. Working under the supervision of the [production designer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Production_designer "Production designer") and/or [art director](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_director "Art director"), the art department is responsible for arranging the overall _look_ of the film (i.e. modern/high-tech, rustic, Victorian, etc.) as desired by the [film director](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_director "Film director"). Individual positions within this department include: [production designer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Production_designer "Production designer"), [art director](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_director "Art director"), [assistant art director](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_art_director?action=edit&redlink=1 "Assistant art director \(page does not exist\)"), [storyboard artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Storyboard_artist "Storyboard artist"), [concept artist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concept_artist "Concept artist"), [draftsman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drawing "Drawing"), [art department coordinator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_department_coordinator?action=edit&redlink=1 "Art department coordinator \(page does not exist\)"), [set decorator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Set_decorator "Set decorator"), [set dresser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Set_dresser "Set dresser"), makeup artist, [painter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Painter "Painter"), [property master](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Property_master "Property master"), [leadman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leadman "Leadman"), [swing gang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swing_gang "Swing gang"), [production buyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Production_buyer?action=edit&redlink=1 "Production buyer \(page does not exist\)"), [Film sculptor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_sculptor "Film sculptor"), and [property assistant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Property_assistant?action=edit&redlink=1 "Property assistant \(page does not exist\)").[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_department#cite_note-1)[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_department#cite_note-2)[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_department#cite_note-3)[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_department#cite_note-4)
