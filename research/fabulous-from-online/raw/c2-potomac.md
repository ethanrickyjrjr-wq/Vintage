# CUSTOM SOURCING
Whether you're furnishing a home with intention, replacing a beloved heirloom, or searching for a story-rich anchor piece, we’re honored to help.
Our custom sourcing service offers clients a personalized experience rooted in care, discretion, and design history. Let us search on your behalf—with purpose and with heart.
#### **Custom Sourcing Includes**
  * Personal review of your request
  * Access to our private estate and dealer network
  * 2–3 tailored proposals delivered via email
  * 30-day active sourcing window
  * First right of refusal on matched pieces
  * Styling suggestions upon request


**Sourcing Fee: $150**  
 _This fee is fully applied toward the purchase of any piece we source for you._
* * *
#### **How it works?**
  * **Step 1:** Submit your sourcing request form below
  * **Step 2:** We’ll review it within 3 business days
  * **Step 3:** If accepted, you’ll receive an email and payment link to begin
  * **Step 4:** Receive curated proposals within 7–10 business days
  * **Step 5:** Hold your sourced piece for 48 hours before public release


_Not every request is accepted—we prioritize sourcing projects that align with our aesthetic, sourcing reach, and timeline availability._
* * *
#### **Submit Your Request**
> Share your vision with us, and we’ll handle the search with care.
We review each request personally and do our best to respond within 3 business days. If your request feels like a good fit, we’ll follow up with next steps, timelines, and potential matches.
* * *
#### **Frequently Asked Questions**
Wondering how our process works? [Read our FAQs](https://www.potomaccollective.com/faqs) or scan below for quick sourcing-specific answers.
  * ####  Do you charge for sourcing?
Yes—$150, which is applied to any piece you purchase through us. It helps us protect our time and provide focused attention to your request.
  * ####  What if you don’t find anything I love?
The sourcing fee covers time, research, and vendor coordination. It is non-refundable but may be applied toward a future gallery purchase.
  * ####  Do you source for designers?
Yes! We welcome inquiries from interior designers and trade professionals. Please note that we currently offer piece-specific sourcing, not full project schemes… _yet._
  * ####  How long does sourcing take?
You’ll receive proposals within 7–10 business days from payment. Some items may take longer to locate depending on rarity or specificity.
