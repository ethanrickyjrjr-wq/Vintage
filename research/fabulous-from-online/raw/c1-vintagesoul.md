#  **E-Design Packages**
E-Design is a great way for us to service not only local clients but clients from all over. This is a great service if you are starting with a blank slate or if you are revamping an existing space. It is our goal at Vintage Soul Designs to help you create the home of your dreams! All of the services listed below can be tailored to your specific needs. We want you to have a wonderful experience working with us through this type of design. E-design is meant to be laidback and comfortable as well as affordable. We look forward to hearing from you about your next project! 
# **The Basic Package**
This package is to help you in the right direction with as room that you already have filled with things you love or a room that you are starting from scratch with
What is in the Basic Package :
  * Consultation email to discuss your project
  * Color Consultation
  * Mood board with inspiration photos- this is just inspiration, no direct shopping resources will be provided
  * Suggestions on implementing some of the inspiration into your space 


What we need from you:
  * Budget
  * A few photos of your space


_*Prices start at $350 and are subject to change based on the scope of your project_
* * *
#  **The Essentials package**
This is a great in between our other packages. With this package you will get:
  * Consultation email to discuss your project
  * Mood board with inspiration photos
  * Color consultation 
  * Furniture selection up to two or three pieces- We will provide direct links for you to purchase
  * Accessories- you chose what you would like us to source and we will provide direct links for you to purchase
  * up to 2 revision of the selections 


What we will need from you :
  * Budget
  * A few pictures from multiple angles of the room. Make sure (if possible) you capture the full length of each wall


_*Prices start at $500 and are subject to change based on the scope of your project_
* * *
#  **Full Design Package**
  * Initial email consultation to discuss your project
  * Moodboard with inspiration photos 
  * Floor plan with furniture layout 
  * Furniture Selection
  * Accessories Selection 
  * Lighting Selection 
  * Product Shopping List with Shoppable Links 
  * up to three revisions 
  * Paint Selection
  * Incorporating existing furniture
  * Plant Suggestions
  * Rendered Floor plan


What I am going to need from you:
  * Budget
  * A few pictures from multiple angles of the room. Make sure (if possible) you capture the full length of each wall
  * Measurements of your room for the floor plan- you can use this as a resource[ here](https://www.wikihow.life/Measure-a-Room)


_Prices are between $700-$1000 and can vary based on the scope of your project and the number of rooms_
