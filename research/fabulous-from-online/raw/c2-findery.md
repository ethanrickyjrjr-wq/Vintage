#  Personal Shopper 
Have you been tirelessly searching for that elusive vintage gem, but it seems like finding a needle in a haystack? Well, fret no more because we're here to make your dreams a reality.
Introducing our Personal Shopper service – your exclusive pass to a world of curated vintage treasures tailored just for you. Whether it's a specific piece of furniture, a rare collectible, or a unique decor item, we've got you covered.
Here's how it works:
  1. Submit your dream item: Tell us what you're looking for by filling out our easy-peasy inquiry form. Whether it's a description, a photo, or even just a vague idea, the more details you provide, the better!
  2. Sit back and relax: Once we receive your inquiry, our team of expert treasure hunters will swing into action. We'll scour our extensive network of suppliers, auctions, and flea markets to track down your dream item.
  3. Get notified: As soon as we find a match, you'll be the first to know! We'll send you all the juicy details along with photos and pricing, so you can decide if it's a match made in vintage heaven.
  4. Happy dance time: Once you give the thumbs up, we'll handle all the nitty-gritty details, from securing the item to arranging delivery right to your doorstep. All you have to do is sit back, relax, and bask in the glory of your newfound treasure.


So, what are you waiting for? Let's turn your wishlist into a reality! Submit your inquiry today and let The Findery Market be your personal shopper extraordinaire.
Get ready to embark on a treasure hunt like no other – because at The Findery Market, your dreams are our mission!
