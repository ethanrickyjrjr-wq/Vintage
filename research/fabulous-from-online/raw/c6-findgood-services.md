# INTERIOR STYLING
You don’t need a huge budget, a full renovation, or an entirely new house to create a space you love. Interior styling is customized to your space, your style, and your budget. Depending on what you need, we can create a visual design plan + shopping list for you to tackle yourself, collaborate on the design together, or work hands-on in your space.
We can utilize what you already own, bring in vintage and secondhand finds, source new pieces, rearrange, restyle, and make the room feel completely different without necessarily buying completely new things.
Big transformation or little refresh — let’s make it feel GOOD.
#### This can look like:
DIY Design Plan
A visual roadmap for your room with layout ideas, styling direction, sourcing recommendations, and a shopping list so you can make it happen yourself.
Collaborative Design
We dream it up together. I help pull the room together, make the big decisions, source the good stuff, and give you a clear plan to execute.
In-Home Styling
A hands-on styling session using what you already have, mixed with pieces we source or bring in to finish the space.

# CUSTOM SOURCING
You dream it. I’ll go find it.
Have a very specific piece in mind but no idea where to find it? Maybe it’s an oversized vintage mirror. An orange sofa. The perfect dining table. A collection of art for that one very blank wall.
Or maybe you know the feeling you want, but you need someone to track down the pieces that will get you there. Tell me what you’re looking for and I’ll put my professional treasure-hunting skills to work.
I can source from vintage shops, antique dealers, estate sales, local sellers, online marketplaces, and beyond to find pieces that feel unique, collected, and very you.
