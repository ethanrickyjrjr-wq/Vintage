## FAQs
How long does the sourcing process typically take?
The sourcing process can vary depending on location and specific requirements; typically, it takes 6-8 weeks within Texas and 8-10 weeks for the West Coast, East Coast, and in-between.
What are the pricing options available for furniture sourcing?
We offer two pricing models: 
  * **All-in** fixed rate option which covers up to 5 hours of design services and all other costs except shipping.
  * **Pay as You Go** option for more complex projects. 


Both options are billed at an hourly rate of $120 p/hour.
Are there any minimum budget requirements for your services?
Yes, we typically work with a minimum budget of $2,500 to ensure that we can source high-quality, unique pieces that meet our clients' expectations.
How do you handle shipping and delivery?
Shipping costs are estimated once a piece is selected and finalized. This cost is invoiced to you separately and must be paid prior to pickup.
