## Pricing Questions, Answered
### How much does Spacejoy cost?
Spacejoy has 3 online interior design packages, priced per room: Delight ($199), Bliss ($399) and Euphoria ($599). Furniture is optional: buy it with us, elsewhere, or keep what you own.
### What is included in a Spacejoy design package?
A Spacejoy designer works with you 1:1 and delivers 3D designs of your room, a floor plan and a shoppable list. Revisions are included in every package; the number depends on the package.
### How long does Spacejoy take to deliver a design?
Your first designs arrive within 10 business days of sharing all your room details.
### How is pricing worked out for more than one room?
Every package is priced per room. Add the rooms you want designed and your order total is shown before you pay. If a multi-room discount applies, it is taken off automatically.
### Can I get a refund?
If you placed an order by mistake and are not ready to get started on your design yet, you are entitled to a full refund, provided you request it within 48 hours of placing the order. Our refund policy has the full terms.
### How do I pay?
You pay by card at checkout. Payments are processed by Stripe, and Spacejoy never sees or stores your full card number.
### Can I change my package after choosing it?
You choose your package before checkout, and you can change it on the checkout step before you pay.
### Can I work within my budget with Spacejoy?
Yes. When you start your project, you share your furniture budget with your designer, who sources products that match it.
