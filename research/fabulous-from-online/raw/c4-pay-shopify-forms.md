# Create forms
You can create forms to help you grow your business in the following ways:
  * Prompt customers to subscribe to your email, SMS, or WhatsApp marketing
  * Approve wholesale retailers using a [company account request form](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/b2b/companies-and-customers/company-account-requests#set-up-a-company-account-request-form)
  * Collect data about your customers that's important to your business
  * Create contact us forms for support inquiries


Forms can be customized using the editor to match your online store and capture customer information in a privacy-compliant way. Before you create a form, review the various [form settings](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings) available to each different type of form.
You can display multiple popup forms, inline forms, or both types at the same time on your store.

## Considerations for creating Shopify Forms
Review the following considerations for creating Shopify Forms:
  * Forms display on your store only when they've been [activated](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/manage#form-activate).
  * You can have up to 25 forms per store.
  * Multi-step forms aren't supported.
  * Conditional logic isn't supported on forms.
  * When an existing customer submits a form, their email and phone number on file aren't modified, even when the form includes a different email or phone number. Other fields, such as marketing consent, also might not be updated. To change an existing customer's email or phone number, [edit their profile](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/customers/manage-customers#edit-customer-profiles) directly in your Shopify admin.


## Form types
You can create either a popup or inline form. Review the differences between popup and inline forms, and requirements to display them.  
Overview of form types, descriptions, and requirements.  
| Form type  | Description  | Requirements  |  
| --- | --- | --- |  
| Popup forms  | Display as content that floats as an overlay above the page. Learn about the [settings that you can configure for popup forms](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings/popup).  | To display popup forms on your store, you need to install the [Forms theme app embed](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/themes/customizing-themes/apps#app-embeds) in your theme.  |  
| Inline forms  | Display embedded in a specific section on your theme, such as on a page. Learn about the [settings that you can configure for inline forms](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings/inline).  | To display inline forms on your store, you need to use a [compatible theme](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/themes/managing-themes/versions#features) and install the [Forms theme app embed](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/themes/customizing-themes/apps#app-embeds) in your theme.  |  
## Create and add a popup form to your store
You can create a popup form to display on your store from the Forms app in your Shopify admin. After you create and activate a popup form, it automatically displays on your store.
Take the following steps to create a popup form and add it to your store:
  * [**Step 1: Turn on the Forms app embed for your theme**](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/create#embed-theme)
  * [**Step 2: Create a popup form**](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/create#create-popup)
  * [**Step 3: Translate your form to additional languages (Optional)**](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/create#translate-popup)
  * [**Step 4: Preview and activate your popup form**](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/create#activate-popup)


### Step 1: Turn on the Forms app embed for your theme
If you're creating a popup form for the first time, then you need to turn on the Forms app embed for your theme. As long as the Forms theme app embeds setting is toggled on, all active popup forms display on your online store.
If you've already turned on the Forms app embed setting, then proceed to [**Step 2: Create your popup form**](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/create).
#### Note
If you use both a popup form and an inline form on your store, and you deactivate the [Forms theme app embed](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/themes/customizing-themes/apps#app-embeds) setting, then the popup form continues to display on pages that also contain the inline form.
To prevent a popup form from being displayed on any page, you need to [deactivate the form](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/manage#form-deactivate).
#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to [**Online Store**](https://admin.shopify.com/themes).
  2. In your active theme, click **Edit theme**.
  3. In the theme editor sidebar, click the **App embeds** icon.
  4. Activate the **Forms** toggle.
  5. Click **Save**.


### Step 2: Create a popup form
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to **Apps** > [**Forms**](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/shopify-forms).
  2. Click **Create form**.
  3. Select **Popup form**.
  4. Enter a name for the form, and then click **Create**.
  5. In the **Popup display** settings, do either of the following:
     * To create a floating popup form, select **Floating** , and then select a position on your store from the drop-down menu.
     * To create an overlay popup form, select **Overlay** , and then use the color picker to set an overlay color.
  6. Configure the following parts of your form:
     * [**Teaser**](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings/popup#teaser) settings
     * [**Form**](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings/all-forms#form) settings
     * Manage your form [fields](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings/all-forms#manage-form-fields)
     * [**Success**](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings/all-forms#success) settings
     * [**Styling**](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings/popup#styling) settings
     * [**Image**](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings/popup#image) settings
     * [**Behavior**](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings/popup#behavior) settings
  7. Optional: To create a [customer tag](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-admin/productivity-tools/using-tags) after a customer submits your form, in the **Tags** field, enter a tag to select or create a new tag.
  8. Optional: To receive email notifications each time the form is submitted, in the **Email notifications** section, select **Send an email for each submission to this form**.
  9. Optional: To manage the automations and workflows associated with the form, in the **Automations and workflows** section, click **Manage** next to any workflow.
  10. Click **Save**.


### Step 3: Translate your form to additional languages (Optional)
#### Note
[Auto-translation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/translation#methods) for forms is supported on stores where the primary language is English. If your store's primary language isn't English, then you can instead add [manual translations](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/translation#methods) to your form.
You can add translations in [supported languages](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/translation#supported-languages) to your form. The steps to translate your form depend on the [translation method](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/translation#methods) that you want to use, and whether you've already [added additional languages to your store](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/languages/manage-languages#add-new-language).
If a translation doesn't have auto-translate available, then an "Auto-translation not available for this language" message displays.
#### Steps:
  1. In the **Languages** section of your form, do either of the following:
     * If you've already published additional languages to your store and they display in the **Published** section, then proceed to the next step.
     * If no available languages display in the **Published** or **Unpublished** section, then you first need to add a new language to your store:
       1. Click **language settings**.
       2. On the **Languages** page, click **Add a language**.
       3. Select a language from the drop-down menu, and then assign it to a domain.
       4. Click **Done**.
       5. Click **< -- Forms** to return to Forms, and then open the form that you want to continue editing.
  2. Depending on your preference and the translation method that's available, do either of the following:
     * Use auto-translate:
       1. Click **Translate** next to the language that you want to translate. If you have more than one language that you want to translate to, then you can select **Auto-translate all**.
       2. Click **Review** to preview your translations.
       3. Click **Save**.
     * Add your own translations manually:
       1. Click **Translate** next to the language that you want to translate.
       2. In each field in the column of the language you want to translate to, add your translation.
       3. Click **Save**.


### Step 4: Preview and activate your popup form
After you create a form, its status is **Inactive** by default. Before activating your form, you can use the preview to confirm how each part of the form will display to customers.
When you're ready to display your form on your online store, click **Turn form on**. After you turn the form on, its status displays as **Active**.
Learn more about [activating](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/manage#form-activate) or [deactivating](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/manage#form-deactivate) forms.

### Step 3: Choose the placement for your inline form
Inline forms can be displayed on either a new landing page or an existing page template. Learn more about [inline form placement](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings/inline#placement).
After you save your form changes, configure either of the following placement options depending on how you want your form to display on your store:
Add a new page to your online store for your form
When you create a landing page from the Forms app, you create a new page template and generate a new page using that template with a customizable handle, for example: `example.com/pages/your-custom-form-URL`.
#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to **Apps** > [**Forms**](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/shopify-forms).
  2. Click the inline form that you want to update placement for.
  3. In the **Placement** section of the form settings, click **Create landing page**.
  4. In the dialog, enter a title and URL for the landing page, and then click **Save**. A new page is created and added to the [**Pages**](https://admin.shopify.com/pages) list in your Shopify admin.
  5. Optional: To update the page title, content, or handle for your landing page, click **Edit page**. You can also make changes from the [**Pages**](https://admin.shopify.com/pages) list in the admin or by editing the [page template](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/themes/theme-structure/templates) in the theme editor at any time.

Add your form to an existing page template
You can add an inline form to an existing page template as a block in the theme editor by using the **Form ID** associated with your form. A form ID is a 6-digit number that represents a specific form in your store. The inline form displays on all pages that use the template you select.
  1. After you save your changes, in the **Placement** section of your form settings, select **Existing page**.
  2. Click the clipboard icon to copy the **Form ID** to your clipboard.
  3. Click **Go to theme editor**.
  4. In the **Template** section, add the inline form to your page template: 
    1. Click **⊕ Add section**.
    2. Select the **Apps** tab.
    3. Select **Forms**.
    4. In the block settings, paste the copied **Form ID** into the **Form ID** field.
    5. Optional: Adjust the colors, style, and layout settings of the block.
  5. Click **Save**.

## Duplicate an existing form
You can duplicate a form to create a new form that includes all of the fields and settings of an existing form, with the exception of translations and inline form placement.
After you duplicate a form, the new form is set to **Inactive** by default. Learn how to [activate a form](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/manage#form-activate).
#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to **Apps** > [**Forms**](https://admin.shopify.com/apps/shopify-forms).
  2. Click ![Horizontal menu](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/MenuHorizontalIcon.svg) for the form that you want to duplicate.
  3. From the drop-down menu, select **Duplicate form**.
  4. Enter a name for your new duplicate form.
  5. Edit any [form settings](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/forms-app/settings) that you want to change, and then click **Save**.
  6. Click **Turn form on** to display the form on your store.
