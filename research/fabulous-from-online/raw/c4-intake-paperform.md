### Streamline Your Interior Design Projects with a Professional Client Intake Form
Starting a new interior design project means gathering dozens of details—room dimensions, style preferences, budget constraints, timeline expectations, and visual inspiration. Instead of juggling emails, phone calls, and scattered notes, this **Interior Design Client Intake Form** brings everything together in one beautiful, on-brand experience that reflects the quality of your design work.
#### Designed for Interior Designers and Decorators
Whether you run a boutique design studio, work as a solo interior designer, or manage a growing team of decorators, this template helps you collect everything you need to deliver exceptional results. Capture room measurements, understand your client's aesthetic through a style quiz, set clear budget expectations, and collect inspiration photos—all before your first consultation.
The form replaces clunky PDFs and back-and-forth emails with a streamlined intake process that positions you as professional and organized from day one. Clients appreciate the clarity, and you get structured information that makes scoping, quoting, and project planning faster and more accurate.
#### Turn Submissions into Seamless Workflows
With Paperform's native integrations and **Stepper** (stepper.io) workflow automation, every submission can trigger your entire onboarding sequence. Send a personalized welcome email, create a project folder in Google Drive or Dropbox, add the client to your CRM or project management tool like Asana or Monday, and schedule a follow-up consultation—automatically. No manual data entry, no missed steps, just a smooth handoff from inquiry to active project.
You can also use **Papersign** (papersign.com) to send design agreements, terms of service, or mood board approvals for eSignature immediately after intake, keeping contracts and client information connected in one place for a clean audit trail.
#### Built for Your Brand
Interior design is all about aesthetics, and your intake form should reflect that. Paperform's doc-style editor lets you customize fonts, colors, backgrounds, and layouts to match your brand perfectly. Embed the form on your website, share it via social media, or send it as a standalone link that feels like a natural extension of your portfolio.
This template is trusted by interior designers, home decorators, staging professionals, and design consultancies who need a polished, professional intake experience that converts prospects into delighted clients.
