Why Staging Works
## Buyers decide before they finish the first room
Most of the time, someone walking through a home already knows whether they want it before they've seen all of it. Not from a checklist, but from a feeling. That feeling is what gets a house sold, and creating it is what we do.
Small, specific changes to the right rooms can put thousands of dollars back in your pocket. Mike and Deena have seen it firsthand: higher sale prices, faster sales, and smoother closings, both on their own properties and on the ones they've staged for others.
In today's market, buyers expect the best experience a showing can offer. The right staging makes a home feel warm and comfortable, and it helps buyers start to picture their own belongings in the space. That's what it comes down to: does the place actually feel like home.
Based on real market data for staged vs. unstaged homes (Florida Realtors, 2025). What a specific property gains depends on the property and the market.
