Prices for Christmas decorating services vary by project size, materials, and crew. Typical costs include labor, lighting materials, and equipment rentals, with regional differences driving the final price. This article breaks down the cost to help buyers plan a budget for the holiday season.  
| Item  | Low  | Average  | High  | Notes  |  
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |  
| Full Exterior Lighting (home)  | $1,500  | $3,200  | $6,500  | Includes design, installation, and removal  |  
| Tree Decorating Service (30–60 inch tree)  | $150  | $350  | $800  | Decor, lights, and placement  |  
| Mantel/Interior Garlands  | $80  | $180  | $350  | Per mantel or focal area  |  
| Labor (per hour, crew of 2)  | $60  | $90  | $140  | Includes setup and takedown  |  
| Materials (LED lights, decor)  | $150  | $600  | $2,000  | Dependent on quantity and quality  |  
Assumptions: Midwest labor rates, standard LED lights, typical curb appeal project, normal access.
## Typical total spending for holiday décor services
**Most households pay between $1,000 and $4,000** for a balanced mix of exterior lighting, a decorative tree, and some interior touches. The average tends to land around $2,500, with larger, multi-story homes or premium materials pushing higher. Per-unit pricing helps compare options for smaller jobs like a single doorway display or garland dressing.
## Cost breakdown: materials, labor, and equipment
**Material costs usually form the largest share of the quote, followed by labor.** A typical breakdown includes lights and decor (Materials), installation and removal (Labor), and any rental gear (Equipment). The table shows common ranges to expect in quotes.  
| Cost Component  | Low  | Average  | High  | Notes  |  
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |  
| Materials  | $150  | $600  | $2,000  | LED strings, bulbs, ornaments  |  
| Labor  | $120  | $300  | $1,400  | 2-person crew, 4–8 hours typical  |  
| Equipment & Rentals  | $50  | $150  | $500  | ladders, lifts, extension cords  |  
| Permits/Inspections  | $0  | $50  | $250  | Usually for street-anchored displays  |  
| Delivery/Removal  | $0  | $100  | $400  | Part of service or charged separately  |  
## Labor rates and crew configurations by region
**Hourly rates typically range from $60 to $140, depending on crew size and demand.** Urban markets with higher living costs and skilled installers can push rates toward the upper end, while rural areas may be lower. Typical options include a two-person crew for standard installs or a larger crew for multi-story homes.
## Per-unit pricing: lights, garlands, and ornaments
**Per-foot lighting costs commonly run from $1.50 to $4 per linear foot installed.** Garlands usually price by the linear foot of mantle treatment or by the focal area, while ornaments are priced per item or per tree decoration package. Use per-unit pricing to compare quotes efficiently.
## Project scopes that drive price differences
**Exterior lighting for a single-story home costs less than a full-sculpted display on a multi-story residence.** Interior focal areas like mantels or entryways add smaller line items, while commercial-grade displays or custom silhouettes increase both materials and labor.
## Seasonal demand and timing effects on price
**Prices rise during peak December weeks and weekends due to higher demand and limited contractor availability.** Scheduling early or after the initial blackout period can yield lower quotes, while rush requests may incur expedited-service fees.
## Ways to cut costs without losing impact
**Limit scope by prioritizing exterior lighting first and delaying interior décor.** Choose fewer premium lights and reuse some existing decor to reduce material costs, and compare multiple quotes to find bundled offers.
## Add-ons that commonly affect the final price
**Special effects and mounting hardware can add 10–30% to the total.** Examples include programmable light sequences, wind-resistant mounts, or extra-long run lengths that require more cables and power planning.
## Regional price differences in the United States
**Coastal metro areas show higher averages than inland rural markets.** The delta can be 10–25% depending on local wages, permit rules, and access challenges.
## Quantifying the biggest price drivers for Christmas decorating
**Key drivers include home size (square footage), number of trees, and outdoor mounting complexity.** A two-story house with multiple trees and garlands will push costs higher than a single-story home with a single tree.
## Practical ways to reduce price without sacrificing look
**Refine scope, time installs to off-peak slots, and use standardized decor packages.** Compare quotes that itemize materials and labor, and consider DIY-ready options like pre-lit trees from vendors that allow onsite assembly.
## Quote comparison essentials for Christmas decorating
**Ask for itemized bids with line-by-line costs for materials, labor, and equipment.** Ensure you understand removal timing and whether debris disposal is included in the base price.
