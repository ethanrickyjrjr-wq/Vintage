# 90 Minute Design Consult
**Considering hiring an interior designer but not sure where to start?** Our 90-Minute Consult is the perfect, low-commitment way to explore what professional design can do for you!
Our expert designers will meet with you at your home or office. We will maximize this time to deliver valuable ideas and tailored advice focused on your specific priorities.
How we spend the 90 minutes is up to you! Here are some examples. 
  * _For General Direction:_ We may discuss your budget, and timeline (and how realistic they are) and share our recommended vendors and tradespeople. 
  * _For Renovation Projects:_ We may focus on sketching out preliminary ideas for space planning and preliminary material recommendations. 
We may discuss preliminary space planning ideas and recommendations
  * _For Furnishing Projects:_ We may sketch out a preliminary furniture layout and advise on the best types of pieces for your space.


Since every consult is unique, our team takes detailed notes throughout the process. This ensures you can fully concentrate on your time with our designers.After the consult, you'll receive a Google Slides Presentation that summarizes our conversation, including actionable steps for you to get started. Should you wish to continue working with us, we'll also discuss the best next steps for your project.
THE DESIGN PROCESS
**Discovery Phase**
To kick off our consult, we need to gather some key information ahead of time. 
  * _Fill out your Design Questionnaire_ - You’ll receive a questionnaire that helps us understand your goals, lifestyle, and budget. Think about how you use your space and what you like and dislike in its current state. 
  * _Look for Inspiration Images_ - We want to align our design with your vision for the space. Gather inspiration images (or create a Pinterest board) with at least 3 images that you like. These images could include a general “feel” for your space, or built elements that you’d like to see incorporated. Don’t stress…they don’t have to be perfect! We just need a starting point. 


**The Consult - 90 minutes (In-Person)**
Our designers will arrive at the scheduled time and location for your consult. Every consult is a little bit different, but here is the loose structure. The time goes fast! 
  * _Step 1: Questionnaire Review & Walkthrough (30 minutes)_We review your questionnaire and walk through your prioritized spaces to understand your goals for the space. 
  * _Step 2: Design Time (1 hour)_ We put our heads down and work on the design for the spaces you indicated you need help with in priority order. 
    * Our designers will make periodic check-ins with you during this time to ensure we are on the right path for your design. 
    * We reserve the last few minutes to review the design presentation together. 


**What does my 90 minute Consult Include?**
Following our 90-minute session, you will be provided with a Google Slide Presentation based on your consult. It may include the following: 
  * Drawings or sketches created during your consultation. 
  * Design recommendations and curated resources discussed during the consult.
  * Industry tips and tricks from our expert team!
  * Trusted Trade References relevant to your project.


To maintain the affordability of this design package, this 90-minute consult does not include follow-up questions or additional advice afterward. However, if you wish to continue working with our team, please contact our team at hello@anniewise.com.
