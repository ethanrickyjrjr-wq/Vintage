# virtual interior design
## what is virtual interior design?
I’ll meet you virtually in the room you want designed for a flat fee using **ZOOM** or **FACE TIME**. The results are THE SAME as if I physically went into your home. 
[ get your free consultation now ](https://homestylingbyalexis.com/contact/)

## how does virtual interior design work?
  * Packages are sold in ten hours increments
  * Inspiration Board is designed.
  * Measuring appointment is scheduled.
  * Design Board is created using EXACT pieces for the room.
  * Furniture & decor links are sent and YOU purchase all items. 
  * Once items arrive, YOU place them in the room.


## is virtual interior design right for me?
Yes! If you…
  * You want to save money.
  * You don’t mind getting involved in the design of the space (this means comfortable taking measurements, talking with the designer frequently, disposing of furniture & decor not being used in the new design, hiring a handyman to hang art, wallpaper, mirrors, paint, or doing these things yourself)..
  * You are comfortable using Zoom/FaceTime.
  * You desire to be connected with the designer while working together via texting inspiration pics, ideas, questions, etc..
