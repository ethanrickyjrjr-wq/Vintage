## Our Philosophy  
Design Values & Aesthetic
![Designed to Endure](https://www.studio-mcgee.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcms.studio-mcgee.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F05%2FFullservice_Endure.jpg&w=3840&q=90)
Designed to Endure
Our aesthetic is rooted in New Heritage—a modern classicism grounded in how people actually live. We design with intention rather than trend, with an editorial point of view that leans into tension: old and new, warm and restrained, collected rather than matched. The result is a home that feels current today, and still reads as considered twenty years from now.
![A Commitment to Craft](https://www.studio-mcgee.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcms.studio-mcgee.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F05%2FFullservice_Craft.jpg&w=3840&q=90)
A Commitment to Craft
A home is built in the details. From custom millwork and considered architecture to the hand-knotted rugs, unlacquered brass, and one-of-a-kind antiques we source for every room—every element is chosen with intention and made to last. We partner with the makers, tradespeople, and workrooms whose craft holds up over time, because the soul of a home lives in what's often quietest: a clean miter, a hand-applied finish, a piece with a past.

## Our Process
01 | DISCOVERY
We begin with a deep conversation about how you live, what you love, and what your home should feel like. This is where we understand the vision—your aesthetic, your daily rhythms, your aspirations for the space. 
02 | CONCEPT
Our team develops mood boards, space plans, and a clear design direction. We present options, align on materials and palette, and refine the vision together before a single order is placed.
03 | DESIGN AND DOCUMENTATION
Detailed elevations, renderings, and specifications for every surface, fixture, and furnishing. We coordinate directly with your architect and builder to ensure the design translates flawlessly into construction.
04 | PROCUREMENT & INSTALLATION
We manage every purchase, specification, and delivery—handling logistics, claims, and timelines so you don't have to. On installation day, you walk into a fully realized home. Every piece placed, every detail considered.

## Working on something smaller?
Our Virtual Design Service is built for a smaller renovation or room refresh. Ideal for clients tackling one to six spaces, Virtual Design delivers full design direction: floor plans, material selections, and sourcing. From there, you handle the ordering, installation, and coordination with your builder on your own timeline.
