Connect with talented design students and professionals who create stunning spaces using curated second-hand finds. Save money, support sustainability, help kickstart careers, and get professional results.

## How It Works
From first contact to finished space in four simple steps
### For Clients
### 1. Browse Designers
Tell us your style, budget, and space details. We'll match you with designers who specialize in your aesthetic.
### 2. Connect & Discuss
Chat with your matched designers, share inspiration, and discuss your vision for the space.
### 3. Curated Shopping
Your designer sources beautiful thrifted pieces that match your style and budget perfectly.
### 4. Transform Your Space
Watch your space come to life with sustainable, one-of-a-kind pieces and professional styling.
### For Designers
### 1. Create Your Profile
Showcase your style, expertise, and portfolio. Set your rates and availability to attract ideal clients.
### 2. Connect with Clients
Get matched with clients who love your aesthetic. Discuss their vision and establish project scope.
### 3. Source & Curate
Hunt for perfect vintage pieces using our curated thrift directory. Build a collection that matches your client's vision.
### 4. Grow Your Business
Complete projects, build your portfolio, and earn income doing what you love with minimal overhead.
