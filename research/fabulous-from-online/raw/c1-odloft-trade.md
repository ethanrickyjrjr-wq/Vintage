Trade & Interior Design
Distinctive antique and vintage pieces for considered interiors.
Considered Pieces for Exceptional Interiors
At Odloft, we work with interior designers, architects, stylists and hospitality professionals seeking distinctive pieces with character and individuality. Our carefully curated collection of antique and vintage furniture, lighting, artwork and decorative objects is selected to complement both contemporary and traditional interiors.
Whether you’re sourcing a single statement piece or working on a complete project, our trade service offers a personal and flexible approach to help you find the right pieces for your scheme.
![](https://odloft.com/cdn/shop/files/ChatGPT_Image_Aug_7_2026_at_07_52_13_PM.png?v=1786128748&width=1254)
Sourcing for a Project?
Looking for something particular? Our sourcing service can help you find distinctive antique and vintage pieces beyond our current collection.
From statement furniture and unusual lighting to artwork and decorative objects, tell us what you’re looking for and we’ll use our network of dealers, auctions and private sources to seek out pieces that suit your project.
Whether you have a specific period, style, material or set of dimensions in mind, or simply an idea of the character you want to introduce to a space, we can work with your brief and present considered options as they become available.
Our approach is personal and selective. We focus on pieces with character, quality and individuality that will sit naturally within the wider scheme rather than simply filling a space.

## Who We Work With
We work with trade professionals across residential, commercial and hospitality interiors, offering a flexible and personal service tailored to each project.
**Interior Designers**
Sourcing distinctive furniture, lighting and decorative pieces for residential and commercial schemes.
**Architects**
Supporting projects where material, proportion and character are as important as function.
**Property Developers**
Helping add individuality and depth to restored, renovated and newly designed spaces.
**Hotels & Hospitality**
Antique and vintage pieces for hotels, restaurants, pubs, holiday properties and other character-led interiors.
**Stylists & Creative Professionals**
Furniture, artwork and decorative objects for editorial, photography, film and styling projects.
**Commercial Interiors**
Considered pieces for retail, office and hospitality environments where a more individual approach is required.
Trade Accounts Coming Soon
We’re currently developing a dedicated Odloft trade account service for interior designers, architects, stylists and hospitality professionals.
Once available, approved trade clients will be able to access preferential pricing on eligible pieces, manage their trade account and purchase directly through our website.
In the meantime, we’re very happy to work with trade customers personally. If you’re sourcing for a current project, looking for a particular piece or would like to discuss trade pricing, please get in touch with us directly.
