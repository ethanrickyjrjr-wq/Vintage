## Music

### Songs
[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Mirror_ball&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Songs")]
  * ["Mirror Ball" (song)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirror_Ball_\(song\) "Mirror Ball \(song\)"), a 2008 song by Alice Nine
  * ["Mirrorball" (song)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirrorball_\(song\) "Mirrorball \(song\)"), a song by Taylor Swift from her 2020 album _Folklore_

  * "Mirrorball", a song by Blur from their 2015 album _[The Magic Whip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Magic_Whip "The Magic Whip")_
  * "Mirrorball", a song by Elbow from their 2008 album _[The Seldom Seen Kid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Seldom_Seen_Kid "The Seldom Seen Kid")_
  * "Mirrorball", a song by Everything But the Girl from their 1996 album _[Walking Wounded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walking_Wounded "Walking Wounded")_
  * "Disco Ball" (ミラーボール _Mirābōru_ ; Mirror-ball), a 2007 song by Tokyo Jihen off the album [_Variety_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variety_\(Tokyo_Jihen_album\) "Variety \(Tokyo Jihen album\)")
  * "[There'd Better Be a Mirrorball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/There'd_Better_Be_a_Mirrorball "There'd Better Be a Mirrorball")", a song by Arctic Monkeys from their 2022 album [The Car (album)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Car_\(album\) "The Car \(album\)")
