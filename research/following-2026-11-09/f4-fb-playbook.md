## 1. TL;DR

- **The biggest risk is the account, not the tactics.** Meta says Marketplace access may be restricted for "a new or inactive account" or an "additional Facebook profile instead of your main profile" (`verified`, https://www.facebook.com/help/1968285150185577). Vendoo (Jul 2026, `verified`) says not to create a new account to sell, because new accounts have limited selling abilities. Buyers now see an AI summary with the account's age and friend count (`verified`, Meta, 2026-03-12). Ratings only become public at 5 or more (`verified`, https://www.facebook.com/help/915385548593204). A 0-friend, no-photo, days-old profile is the weakest possible seller identity, so weeks 0 to 4 are about trust, not volume.
- **Ask Ricky whether Hollie has a main personal profile she could sell from.** Whether "Hollie Wood" is an additional profile, and whether it matches her ID, is his call. Raised here, not resolved.
- **Two Meta facts from July 2026 are not in the repo yet.**
  - **Facebook Verified** is free. It matches a video selfie against the profile's existing photos, so Hollie needs a real face photo first. It is not available to Pages or ProMode accounts (`verified`, https://about.fb.com/news/2026/07/introducing-facebook-verified/). It is rolling out in phases and may not reach her yet. Turning on professional mode for Reels reach would probably make her ineligible (`inferred`; check f3).
  - **eBay already feeds Marketplace.** Eligible eBay listings that offer shipping appear on Marketplace with a "partnership" icon, with no opt-in and no cost. Buyers check out on eBay (`verified`, https://www.ebay.com/sellercenter/news/2025-april/facebook-marketplace). Hollie's shipped eBay items may already be there under the eBay seller, not "Hollie Wood."
- **Nobody offers hard proof that more listings, renewing or relisting grows reach.** Vendors say a new listing gets the most exposure and renew gives a small bump. Meta documents none of it. Treat cadence as a hypothesis and measure it.
- **Paid boost: skip it for cheap pieces.** The only first-hand number I could read is $10 buying 500 views and 0 messages (Closo, Dec 2025, `single anecdote`).
- **Safety: public meetups only.** The only SWFL designated exchange spot I found is Fort Myers PD, 2210 Widman Way, from a 2018 report. Nothing was found for Bonita Springs, Cape Coral or Naples. Confirm with LCSO before listing local pickup.
- **This reverses the standing social plan.** `docs/social-manual.md` says "Skip ... Facebook Groups (claims by comment), Marketplace except shipped checkout" and "November is a setup month, not a posting month for products." Its "Facebook Page" lane does not apply to Marketplace, because a Page cannot list (`docs/facebook-marketplace.md`).

## 2. Findings by topic

### 2.1 Account trust and the new, empty profile
- Meta: restrictions can hit new, inactive or additional profiles (`verified`, link above). Meta gives no age or activity threshold.
- Ratings go public at 5 or more eligible ratings, and can only be given in the iPhone or Android app. Meta does not verify that raters bought anything (`verified`, https://www.facebook.com/help/915385548593204). Sellers can request ratings from buyers; that help page is app-only, so I saw only its title (`verified` title).
- Meta's Mar 2026 post says the profile summary shows "how long you've been on Facebook and your number of friends," plus listing history, item types and ratings (`verified`, https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/facebook-marketplace-new-meta-ai-tools-make-selling-faster-and-easier/).
- Profile photo advice: "a clear photo of yourself (not a logo, meme, or blank)" (`unverified`: Underpriced, undated 2026, https://www.underpriced.app/blog/facebook-marketplace-flipping-guide-2026). A selling-profile article says a blank generic-name profile "looks like a scammer" and that a separate selling profile "often hurts you" (Closo, Dec 2025, `verified` read, https://closo.co/blogs/beginner-guides-how-tos/my-unfiltered-guide-real-tips-for-selling-on-facebook-marketplace-without-losing-your-mind).
- **Discrepancy:** Meta's restriction on new and inactive accounts is verified. "Needs an account several months old" (Closo) and "bans last 24 hours to 7 days" (search snippets) need review. Whether friends matter is disputed in snippets. No source could quantify "enough" friends.
- **Facebook Verified** is free, selfie-based, and shows on Marketplace, Dating, Groups and profile (`verified`, Meta, 2026-07-24). A snippet says Meta states it does not affect ranking (`unverified (search snippet)`). Meta also says it is "exploring the ability for small businesses to list on Marketplace" (`verified`, https://about.fb.com/news/2026/07/connecting-real-people-on-facebook/). Watch for it; don't count on it.

### 2.2 Cadence, renew and relist
- Vendoo (Aug 2026, updated Sep 2026, `verified`, https://blog.vendoo.co/facebook-marketplace-delete-and-relist): Renew may appear once a listing is 7 days old and may be usable up to 5 times. It keeps views and saves and gives "a little boost." Delete-and-relist gives a new ID and the most exposure but wipes saves; with 50 or more saves, drop the price first. Space relists out "to avoid looking like spam." If old and new stay live together, duplicate flags can cut visibility. Meta documents none of this. Vendoo sells a relist tool.
- Apartment Therapy (Apr 2024, older than 2025; one named seller): Facebook lets you repromote every seven days, but she prefers delete-and-relist, since "a new item is more likely" to show (`verified` read, https://www.apartmenttherapy.com/how-to-use-facebook-marketplace-37394670).
- Three Closo pages (renew, relist-when, boost) are "This article is no longer available." Everything from them is `unverified (search snippet)`.
- Reach drops: a Dec 2025 r/FacebookMarketplace mirror snippet describes two listings with zero views for a week, and a commenter reads it as deprioritisation, not a ban (`unverified (search snippet)`). A promotional 2026 guide claims views fell from mid-2025 (`unverified (search snippet)`).
- Volume: 430M items are listed monthly worldwide (`verified`, Meta, 2026-07-24). Meta's seller app is pitched at "listing thirty items a week" (`verified`). That is the established-seller ceiling, not a safe number for a day-old account.
- **Listing caps conflict:** "1 a day," "10 at a time," "20 a month," "same item every 5 to 7 days in groups" (all snippets or vendor claims). Check f3 for each.

### 2.3 Photos
- No controlled test of white cut-outs against in-context shots exists in anything I found. Advice is vendor and blogger opinion.
- Bovino (Apartment Therapy, Apr 2024): use your own real photos, not stock; lead with a static image, then add a video; styled or naturally lit shots catch the eye; show flaws up close. Her point is trust: stock photos make buyers doubt the item. A pure white cut-out on a 0-friend profile may read as stock. This is `inferred`, so use a cut-out cover plus at least one real room or on-form shot, plus label and flaw close-ups.
- Closo: natural light and a blank wall; "put it against a blank wall; open the curtains" (`single anecdote`).
- A snippet claims listings with 5 or more photos get 3 to 5 times the views. It is unsourced (`unverified (search snippet)`).

### 2.4 Pricing, offers and "Is this still available?"
- Do not price off 1stDibs or Chairish. A $2,000 1stDibs coffee table is "probably worth closer [to] $400 [or] $500" on Marketplace. Check eBay sold, Craigslist and Marketplace first (Bovino, Apr 2024, `verified` read).
- Closo suggests padding the price: "If you want $50 for an item, list it for $65." It also says never hold items ("No Holds"), and answer "Is this available?" with "Yes, are you available to pick up ... today?" to filter accidental taps (`single anecdote`).
- Snippets agree that availability-only messages signal little intent. One recommends a single polite "price is firm" and then ignoring repeat lowballs (`unverified (search snippet)`).
- **Auto-reply risk.** Meta now offers Meta AI auto-replies to availability questions built from the listing's description, availability, pickup location and price (`verified`, Mar 2026). For a one-of-one piece also on eBay, the reply can go stale after an eBay sale (`inferred`). Match d1's skip of DM-to-buy and Palm Beach Regency's "It's very difficult for us to hold an item as it could sell at any time" (`research/round-d/d1-social-video.md`). **Double-sell risk:** native Marketplace listings of pieces that are also live on eBay need a delist routine (`inferred`).

### 2.5 Response speed
- "Faster replies rank higher" and "Very Responsive badge" are widely repeated, but I found nothing from Meta for Marketplace (`unverified (search snippet)`). The "90% reply rate, 5-minute median" numbers describe the Page badge, not Marketplace (`unverified (search snippet)`).
- Closo claims early engagement in the first 60 to 120 minutes drives ranking (`single anecdote`). Practical read: reply within hours when you can; there is no proven penalty for slower replies.

### 2.8 Groups: member versus seller
- Meta: buy and sell groups follow Commerce Policies and admins choose how people sell (`verified`, https://www.facebook.com/help/319768015124786). Listings can be shared to groups at publish (Apartment Therapy, `verified` read).
- Groupboss (vendor, undated 2026): only Facebook's own "Sell something" flow cross-posts to several buy-and-sell groups; third-party posting tools risk bans; "avoid posting the same item more than once every 5 to 7 days"; identical posts to many groups fast is a common spam trigger (`single anecdote`, https://groupboss.io/blog/how-to-post-in-multiple-facebook-groups/). A search snippet cites "more than 10 groups in an hour" as a trigger, from one vendor (`unverified (search snippet)`).
- Ban causes: group rules vary by admin; I found no credible list. Join and post limits for a new profile belong to f3. Group lists belong to f1 and f2.

### 2.9 Shipping versus local
- Meta (via `docs/facebook-marketplace.md`, f3 to verify): 10% on shipped orders, $500 cap, ship within 7 days. 10% is `verified` (Meta merchant policy; read live in Jul 2026 posts by Just Being Resourceful and Value Added Resource, https://www.valueaddedresource.net/facebook-dedicated-seller-app/).
- **Discrepancy:** the 10% fee is verified. Closo's "5%, min $0.40" needs review and is probably stale. A snippet saying prepaid labels were removed for new listings needs review; Meta's Mar 2026 post says prepaid labels exist (`verified`).
- Seller app: iOS-only with Android in testing is verified (Meta, 2026-07-24, https://about.fb.com/news/2026/07/introducing-seller-app-facebook-marketplace/). Engadget's "iOS and Android" needs review. The app has bulk listing and performance insights (views, clicks, message threads, sold). It is free as far as Meta says.
- Closo avoids shipping on Marketplace ("seller protection is weak") and sells local only (`single anecdote`). A Reddit-mirror snippet suggests Depop beats Facebook for clothing, with the shipping that implies (`unverified (search snippet)`). **Clothing on local Marketplace: disputed, no evidence either way.** Decor, lamps, art and furniture fit local pickup (Apartment Therapy, Closo). **For shipped clothing, eBay's partnership route likely beats native FB shipping** (`inferred`).

### 2.10 Paid boost
- Closo: $10 on a dining set gave 500 more views and zero extra messages; "if the price and photos are good," organic is enough (`single anecdote`, Dec 2025 read).
- Snippets of removed Closo pages: $5 to $30 per boost, worthwhile for high-value or deadline items, wasteful on cheap ones (`unverified (search snippet)`). A podcast host says a $5 to $10 item is not worth promoting (`unverified (search snippet)`).

## 4. What not to do

- Don't mass-friend, mass-join groups or publish a batch of listings on day one. Meta can restrict new or inactive accounts, and restrictions take all listings down with the profile (`docs/facebook-marketplace.md`).
- Don't buy or request ratings or reviews. Don't use a VPN or a second account to "restart" (snippets link VPN use to limits; Meta ties restrictions to additional profiles).
- Don't use third-party group-posting bots. Don't paste links or the IG handle into listings (check f3).
- Don't ship from Marketplace anything priced above $500 (Meta cap) or promise holds on one-of-one pieces. Delete or mark sold the same hour a piece sells elsewhere.
- Don't use porch pickup, give out an address, or meet in private. Never use "stop by" or "our doors" language (no public showroom).
- Don't boost pieces under about $250. Don't solve a stale listing by posting a second copy while the first is live.
- Don't imply disco balls come with every order; they are occasional gifts.
- Don't assume "Hollie Wood" matches her ID. Don't treat the Facebook Page as a Marketplace channel.
