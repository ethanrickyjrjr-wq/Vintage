## 1. TL;DR

- **What this profile may and may not do.**
  - Meta now says: "You are no longer able to create additional Facebook profiles. Existing additional profiles have not been affected." (https://www.facebook.com/help/967154637433480). A profile created in 2026 is therefore almost certainly the main profile of its own, separate Facebook account (`inferred`). The "additional profile" Marketplace restriction probably does not apply. "New or inactive account" (also a verified restriction reason) does.
  - The question Ricky must settle is whether Hollie already has a Facebook account. Terms of Service 3.1: "Create only one account (your own) and use it for personal purposes." and "Provide for your account the same name that you use in everyday life." (https://www.facebook.com/legal/terms). A second account for Hollie would breach that (`inferred` consequence). Then confirm "Hollie Wood" is the name on her ID.
  - Allowed: listing as a US consumer seller, buy/sell group posting, shipped checkout up to the $500 cap already in the doc. Not allowed: listing from a Page, and listing services (so no "Hire Hollie" on Marketplace).
- **The 5,000-friend premise is out of date.** Meta says "You can have up to 6,000 friends on Facebook." and "You can join up to 6,000 groups." Meta publishes no per-day number for friend requests, group joins, listings or shares.
- **Pro mode vs the free badge is the real decision.** Pro mode makes followers public and adds insights, but Meta's July 2026 launch post says the free Facebook Verified badge "is not available for Pages or ProMode accounts." That badge shows on Marketplace. Recommendation (`inferred`): keep pro mode off at launch.
- **Marketplace has its own Follow.** Buyers can tap Follow on a seller's name and "get notifications when they create new listings." This is the cheapest following mechanism that exists.
- **New-profile signals are visible to buyers.** Since March 2026, buyers see an AI summary with "how long you've been on Facebook and your number of friends." A 0-friend, no-photo profile shows that. Ratings only go public at 5 or more.
- **Boost, Live Shopping and Meta Verified are not launch tools.**
  - Boost is a paid ad. Meta's explainer page is gone, so there are no verified cost rules.
  - Live Shopping Tools (June 2026) are advertiser features. The organic 2022 version is still dead.
  - Meta Verified is a paid subscription with no Marketplace-specific benefit on Meta's page.
- **Biggest red line.** Meta's Spam standard bans "Offering to provide anything of monetary value in exchange for engagement." A disco-ball giveaway tied to follows, likes or shares breaks it. Lane f4 should be told.

### Q3. Friends vs followers

- **Friend cap: 6,000.** "You can have up to 6,000 friends on Facebook." Also: "You won't be able accept a friend request from someone who has 6,000 friends." (https://www.facebook.com/help/211926158839933, `verified`). The 5,000 figure Ricky quoted is outdated.
- **Following.** "You can also follow Pages ... and people who aren't your friend on Facebook but allow everybody to follow them." The people you follow "can see that you're following them." You automatically follow friends. (https://www.facebook.com/help/279614732052951, `verified`)
- **Following instead of friending.** Meta says "To connect with people you don't know (like celebrities), follow them instead of sending them a friend request." (https://www.facebook.com/help/320587401313728, `verified`)
- **Public follow toggle without pro mode.** Search guides say a "Who can follow me" Public/Friends toggle sits under Settings > Followers and public content (`unverified (search snippet)`). Meta's page that would settle it was not found. The verified follow wording above implies profiles can allow everybody.
- **Friend-request privacy.** Users can limit incoming requests to friends of friends (https://www.facebook.com/help/211926158839933, `verified`). Hollie's own setting is worth checking.

### Q5. Sharing to buy/sell groups

- **How it works.** In a group: Sell Something, then pick Item for Sale, Vehicle for Sale, Home for Sale or Rent, or Sale event. Then "Choose your audience": tick other buy/sell groups you belong to, and tick Marketplace (https://www.facebook.com/help/1557451221155310, `verified`).
- **Page limit.** "if you're posting as a Page, you won't see the option to create a listing on Marketplace."
- **Cap per listing.** Meta publishes no cap on groups per listing.
- **Edits don't sync.** "you'll need to edit or delete each post in different buy and sell groups separately" (`verified`).
- **Group admin controls** (`verified`):
  - Only groups with the Buy & sell feature set allow Sell Something (https://www.facebook.com/help/776978302394267).
  - Admins can limit a member's posts per day and comments per hour (https://www.facebook.com/help/719854838671405).
  - Facebook may require admin approval of posts for restricted accounts (https://www.facebook.com/help/158630788063039).
- **Shipping in groups.** Sell with shipping exists in groups. Nothing was found on the shipping-in-groups cap.

### Q10. Meta Verified (paid), personal profile

- A personal Facebook profile is eligible, with "a government-issued photo ID", "Advanced Protection turned on" ("can't be disabled once your subscription is active") and a limit on recent profile changes (https://www.facebook.com/help/596285382333151, `verified`).

- No Marketplace-specific benefit is listed.
- Price: Meta's billing page says "The price of your subscription depends on the subscription plan". Numbers: Standard $14.99 a month in-app or $11.99 on the web per profile; Plus $49.99; Premium $149.99; Max $499.99. All `unverified (search snippet)`.

- Verdict: skip. The free badge does the seller-trust job.

## 4. Red lines (would get the profile or Marketplace restricted)

Quotes are from https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/spam/ unless noted (`verified`).

1. **Engagement-for-reward.** "Offering to provide anything of monetary value in exchange for engagement." Example Meta gives: "If you like my page, I will give you an iPhone!" Disco balls are not exempt, so no "follow and win a disco ball".
2. **Like/share-gating.** "Requiring users to engage (in the form of likes, shares, follows, or any other public-facing form of engagement) to gain access to specific, exclusive content."
3. **Selling permission to post.** "Attempting to or successfully selling, buying, or exchanging site privileges, such as admin or moderator roles, or permission to post in specific spaces." Do not pay groups for posting rights.
4. **High-frequency or repetitive posting.** See the Q6 table quote.
5. **Services.** "Commerce content may not promote the buying, selling, or trading of services, except on the WhatsApp Business app or through Appointments in Facebook or Instagram." (https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/commerce). On Marketplace: services "only allowed on Jobs on Marketplace" (https://www.facebook.com/help/130910837313345). Hire Hollie cannot be a Marketplace or group listing.
6. **Animal products.** "Selling animals or animal products isn't allowed on Marketplace." Vintage fur, ivory, tortoiseshell and feathers are at risk. Leather is unclear (`inferred`). Also screen for counterfeits and recalls, since vintage lamps with old cords may be recalled (https://www.facebook.com/help/130910837313345).
7. **Account identity.** One account only, a name that matches an ID, and no shared passwords (Terms 3.1).
8. **Reposting after rejection.** Meta's path is "Request review" (180 days). Do not delete and repost the same rejected listing; that advice comes from practitioner sites.
9. **Pretending.** "Pretending to be anything or anyone isn't allowed." Business use of a profile sits against "Profiles are for individual use only", though Meta also says Marketplace business restrictions apply only in the EEA, Philippines and India (tension, `inferred` risk).
