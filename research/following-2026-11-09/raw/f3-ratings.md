# How ratings work on Facebook Marketplace
Copy link
Buyer and seller ratings can only be provided from the Facebook app for iPhone or Android, not from facebook.com or Facebook Lite.
To share your feedback and help build a better community on Marketplace, you can rate buyers and sellers you’ve interacted with. Buyer ratings are ratings buyers receive from sellers. Seller ratings are ratings sellers receive from buyers.
Buyer ratings are only visible on the Facebook app for iPhone or Android. They are not visible when you visit facebook.com on a computer or if you use the Facebook Lite app. Seller ratings are visible in the seller’s profile on all platforms, except on Facebook Lite, where the seller’s rating is visible on the product details page. 
**Note:** We do not verify whether people have purchased the product or used the service they are reviewing or recommending on Marketplace.
## About buyer and seller ratings
Your ratings may become public once you receive 5 or more eligible ratings. This may include both the seller and buyer ratings you receive. Anyone who can see your [Marketplace profile](https://www.facebook.com/help/115419748941011?helpref=faq_content) will be able to see your ratings.
