## Group rules from the admins
* * *
1
Be Kind and Courteous
We're all in this together to create a welcoming environment. Let's treat everyone with respect. Healthy debates are natural, but kindness is required.
2
No Hate Speech or Bullying
Make sure everyone feels safe. Bullying of any kind isn't allowed, and degrading comments about things like race, religion, culture, sexual orientation, gender or identity will not be tolerated.
3
No Promotions or Spam
Give more than you take to this group. Self-promotion, spam and irrelevant links aren't allowed.
4
Respect Everyone's Privacy
Being part of this group requires mutual trust. Authentic, expressive discussions make groups great, but may also be sensitive and private. What's shared in the group should stay in the group.
5
No Affiliate Links
We do not allow affiliate links here in the group. If you need assistance with a product take a screenshot and post that for discussion purposes.
6
No direct linking to stores like eBay/Amazon etc.
Directly linking to product on eBay/Amazon/Etc will be viewed as spam and removed. If you are providing assistance or asking for it please take a screenshot and post that.
7
No link shortening services allowed
This is a tactic of spammers hoping to get their tracking cookie on unsuspecting persons devices so their purchases will result in a commission to the spammer. These links will be removed without further discussion. Do it a lot and get banned.
