# Manage membership for your Facebook group

Public groups
In public groups, people can become members without admin approval, and visitors (people who aren’t members) can post and comment if admins allow.
You can [choose to](https://www.facebook.com/help/131887213640898?helpref=choose) approve members and visitors as participants before they publish a post or comment for the first time. Once someone is approved as a participant, you can still require admin approval of their future posts.
Learn how to [approve participants and manage posts](https://www.facebook.com/help/131887213640898?helpref=approve_manage) in public groups and about [membership preapprovals](https://www.facebook.com/help/308412616488153?helpref=hca).
Private groups
In private groups, membership approval is required, unless limited membership is turned on. Learn more about [limited membership in a private Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/help/617811410580927?helpref=faq_content).
This means admins or moderators will have to approve membership requests before someone can join the group. Admins can also allow anyone in the group to approve requests from their friends.
People can join certain private groups without admin approval as limited members if the group has limited membership turned on. Limited members can see all group content and react but won’t be able to post, comment, or chat until they’ve been approved by an admin to participate.
To make approving members easier, you can use [Admin Assist](https://www.facebook.com/help/436275657385753?helpref=faq_content) to automatically approve or decline membership requests by setting up criteria such as automatically approving people who answered the [membership questions](https://www.facebook.com/help/200755420421098?helpref=faq_content) or automatically declining people with new accounts. If you turn on limited membership in your private group, any Admin Assist rules you set up to automatically approve or decline membership requests will apply to participation requests from limited members instead.
If you’re an admin of a private hidden group, you can choose to allow people to invite new members to your group [with a link](https://www.facebook.com/help/162550990475119?helpref=faq_content). If you allow invitations with a link, you can choose who can invite with a link — anyone in the group, only admins, or admins and moderators — and you can disable active links.
### Set who can approve member requests
  1. Go to [facebook.com/groups](https://www.facebook.com/groups) and select your group.
  2. Click **Group Settings** in the left menu.
  3. Below **Manage Membership** , click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=che_icons&name=comet-edit-desktop&density=1)**Edit** next to **Who Can Approve Member Requests**.
  4. Select who you want to be able to approve member requests: 
     * **Only admins and moderators**
     * **Anyone in the group:** Members can approve requests from their friends. If your private group has limited membership turned on, this setting allows members to approve participation requests from their friends.
  5. Click **Save**.


### Set up membership questions
You can set up membership questions to help you decide whether or not to approve a user as a member. If you turn on limited membership in your group, any membership questions you set up will become participation questions to help you decide whether or not to approve a limited member to participate in the group.
  1. Go to [facebook.com/groups](https://www.facebook.com/groups) and select your group.
  2. Click **Membership Questions** below **Badge requests** in the left menu.
  3. Click **Add Question** to add your first question, or **Create** to add a new question.
  4. Next to **Group Rules** , click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-toggleoff-desktop&density=1)**Toggle** to ask members to agree to the Group Rules.


### Approve or decline members
  1. Go to [facebook.com/groups](https://www.facebook.com/groups) and select your group.
  2. Click **Member Requests** above **Badge requests** in the left menu.
  3. You can then: 
     * **Approve** or **Decline** each request.
     * **Approve All** or **Decline All**.
  4. To leave feedback for someone you declined, click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-darkgreythreedots-desktop&density=1)**Options** next to their request, then select **Decline with feedback**. When you send feedback, it will say that the feedback came from “an admin,” but it won’t disclose your name.


### Automatically approve or decline membership requests with Admin Assist
  1. Go to [facebook.com/groups](https://www.facebook.com/groups) and select your group.
  2. Click **Admin Assist** in the left menu.
  3. Below **Manage people** , click **Add** next to **Approve member request if** and/or **Decline member request if**.
  4. Select criteria.
  5. Click **Add to Admin Assist**.


Admin Assist will only automatically approve a member request if all criteria are met. If a request meets both sets of criteria for approving and declining a request, the request will be approved.
### Allow or not allow invitations with a link to your private hidden group
  1. Go to [facebook.com/groups](https://www.facebook.com/groups) and select your group.
  2. Click **Group Settings** in the left menu.
  3. Below **Set Up Group** , click next to **Invite with link**.
  4. Click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-toggleoff-desktop&density=1)**Toggle** next to **Invite with link** to allow or not allow invitations with a link.
  5. If you allow invitations with a link, select who can invite with a link: **Only admins, Admins and moderators** or **Anyone in the group**.
  6. Click **Save**.


### Turn off an active link for invitations to your private hidden group
  1. Go to [facebook.com/groups](https://www.facebook.com/groups) and select your group.
  2. Click **+ Invite** at the top right.
  3. Select **Invite with link**.
  4. Select **Turn off link** , then **Turn Off** to confirm.


Learn how to [manage posts](https://www.facebook.com/help/131887213640898?helpref=faq_content) in private groups and about [membership preapprovals](https://www.facebook.com/help/308412616488153>).
