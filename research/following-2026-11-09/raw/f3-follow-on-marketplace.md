When you follow someone on Marketplace, you'll get notifications when they create new listings. You may also see their listings in your Feed.
## Follow someone on Marketplace
  1. From your Feed, click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=comet-marketplace-desktop&density=1)**Marketplace**.
  2. Find a listing from the person you want to follow.
  3. Scroll down to the seller's name and click on it.
  4. Click **Follow**.


## Unfollow someone on Marketplace
  1. From your Feed, click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=comet-marketplace-desktop&density=1)**Marketplace**.
  2. Click![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-buying-desktop&density=1)**Buying**.
  3. Click **Following**.
  4. Select the name of the person you want to unfollow.
  5. Click **Following**.


You can also learn how to [report someone on Marketplace](https://www.facebook.com/help/196126404168290?helpref=faq_content) or [block someone on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/help/168009843260943?helpref=faq_content).
