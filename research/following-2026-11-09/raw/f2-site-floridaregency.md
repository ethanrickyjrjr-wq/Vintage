Florida Regency is an upscale vintage furniture, lighting and home accessories store located on Antique Row in West Palm Beach, Florida. We specialize in Hollywood Regency, Mid Century Modern, 20th Century and Old Florida Style pieces from Circa 1950’s to the late 80’s. We carry Lucite, chrome, brass, rattan and faux bamboo to name a few. From Karl Springer, Milo Baughman, Charles Hollis Jones and Paul Evans we carry a variety of 20th century pieces. No matter what you are looking for your sure to find that special one of a kind piece at Florida Regency!
