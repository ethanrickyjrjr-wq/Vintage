## Group rules from the admins
* * *
1
This is a Research based Group.
Participants are require to do research on their items first. Even if you feel you don't know where to start, search generally for the item type with various dates (ie "1960s hats) on Google.
2
SHOW Your Work
When you ask for help, tell the group exactly what research you did & what came of it (even if you didn’t get much) - this saves members time, so they don't repeat work you've done & shows you have made an effort. **Describing an item does NOT count as research.** GOOGLE IMAGE SEARCH IS NOT ENOUGH.
3
When PROVIDING Answers, Be Specific & Fact Based
Please give reasoning behind your answers. Guesses without anything to back them up are not useful - if you don’t know and are interested in the answer, you may write “following.” Please *do not* rely on your memory of what you or family wore in previous decades. Double check your assumptions before responding! Read previous replies in a thread before responding. You don’t have to be right, just make the effort to contribute knowledgeably.
4
This is NOT a Traders or Thrifter's Forum
This is a strict rule, and is the reason this thriving group is here. Posting an item for sale, asking about value, or commenting you have private messaged someone is grounds for removal. We do not sell items here via post OR private message. Asking to buy or offering to sell an item via either method is grounds for removal.
5
WE DO NOT MENTION BUYING OR SELLING
Use “I have” instead of “I bought.” We do not in any way talk about commerce in any way as it leads to conversations outside the scope of dating items. Do not suggest you have purchased or will sell an item in the future.
6
NO Referral Links
Do not request or share links to locations or groups to buy, sell or estimate value.
7
NO Requests or Recommendations for Other Groups
OR links to them. If you think someone can help, invite them to our community. It is rude to send someone to another group. The rest of us might have something to offer or wish to learn here.
8
DO NOT BLOCK ADMINS
Admin may need to remove or lock your post for breaking the rules. DO NOT block the Admin, or you will be removed from the group. Admin can still see your posts/comments who has blocked them.
9
Play Nice. Be CIVIL to Each Other
Being factual and giving expertise is kind, please respect this when interacting. You may see two people assess a garment differently. See how the long term members do this politely even when vigorous
10
No Show-and-Tell
This group is purpose oriented. There are lots of vintage groups for sharing frivolous collection content or fun vintage photos, so we don’t need to indulge in it here. We keep the group’s feed focused on identification and learning. Off topic shares will be declined. Thanks for staying on task!
