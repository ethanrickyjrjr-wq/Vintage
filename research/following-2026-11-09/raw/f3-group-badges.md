Badges show people who you are or what you've accomplished in a group. If you have a badge and allow it to be displayed, it will appear on your group posts, comments and profile for the group.
Here are some of the badges you can get in groups:  
| Badge name  | Who can get it  | How to get it  | How long it's displayed  |  
| --- | --- | --- | --- |  
| Admin  | Group admins  | Accept an invitation to be an admin of the group  | Until an admin turns this badge off for the group  
  
Or  
  
Until an admin decides not to display the badge by their name  |  
| Moderator  | Group moderators  | Accept an invitation to be a moderator of the group  | Until an admin turns this badge off for the group  
  
Or  
  
Until a moderator decides not to display the badge by their name  |  
| [Group Expert](https://www.facebook.com/help/520382662510085?helpref=faq_content)  
 _Note: This badge isn't available in all groups_  | Eligible group members, admins and moderators  | Accept an invitation to be a group expert for demonstrating knowledge on topics the group cares about  | Until an admin turns this badge off for the group  
  
Or  
  
Until a group expert decides not to display the badge by their name  |  
| [Rising contributor, top contributor or all-star contributor](https://www.facebook.com/help/757687999245141?helpref=faq_content)  | Group members, admins and moderators  | Be among the most active participants in the group by receiving replies or reactions on your posts or comments, being an admin or moderator, and/or being a group expert  | Until an admin turns the Contributions feature off for the group  
  
Or  
  
Until a top, rising or all-star contributor decides not to display the badge by their name  |  
## Manage which badges appear next to your name
  1. Go to [facebook.com/groups](https://www.facebook.com/groups) and select your group.
  2. Click your profile photo to the left of "Write something..." to go to your member profile.
  3. On your member profile, click **Edit Profile**.
  4. Scroll down to **Badges** and click **Edit**.
  5. Choose which badges you'd like to appear on your group posts and comments, then click **Done**.


## Add your expertise if an admin made you a [group expert](https://www.facebook.com/help/520382662510085?helpref=faq_content)
  1. Go to [facebook.com/groups](https://www.facebook.com/groups) and select your group.
  2. Click your profile photo to the left of "Write something..." to go to your member profile.
  3. On your member profile, click **Edit Profile**.
  4. Click **Add Expertise** below **Expertise**. 
     * To add your topic of expertise, click **Add topic** below **Select your topic of expertise** , then search for and select your topic.
     * To show your expertise on your badge, click **Add to group expert badge**.
     * To add additional credentials, click **Add experience** and follow the prompts.
     * To let admins of other groups discover your expertise, click the option to be invited to other groups as an expert.
  5. Click **Save**.


To remove yourself as a group expert, go to the [member list](https://www.facebook.com/help/727473118066542?helpref=faq_content) and scroll down to **Group Experts** , then select your name and select **Remove as group expert**.
**Note:** Group admins can choose to [turn badges on or off in their group](https://www.facebook.com/help/383114938880295?helpref=managebadges).
## Badges appear in the tab for your nickname
You can view your points on your group profile.
  * Badge points are awarded per group. Your points do not carry over from one group to another.
  * Points are tracked separately. The points you earn on your main profile do not transfer to or count toward your nickname profile, and vice versa.
  * Your main profile and nickname each display their own points total and badges based on activity under that identity.
  * You can view your points on each of your group profiles. Your points total is shown on the appropriate group profile page. Main profile points appear on the group profile for your main profile, and nickname points appear on the group profile for your nickname.


## Adopting an admin badge while using a nickname
If you are the admin of a group, your group has nicknames turned on, and you have access to the feature, you can choose whether to display your admin or moderator badge while you use your nickname.
You may want to use your nickname without an admin or moderator badge if you want to act as a general member of a group, such as asking for or providing advice.
You may want to use a nickname with your admin or moderator badge if you are moderating content while you use a nickname and engaging in posts, comments or reactions.
## Adopt an admin or moderator badge while using your nickname
  1. Confirm that you have adopted a nickname in this group.
  2. Go to your group-specific profile.
  3. Select the tab at the top that represents your nickname.
  4. Click **Display badge** shown below the **Edit** button, or select **Manage** shown in the badge section.
  5. Check the box next to the admin or moderator badge from the badge list.
  6. Select **Save**.
  7. Refresh your profile page to confirm that the admin or moderator badge appears in the badge section.


## Turn off an admin or moderator badge for your nickname
  1. Click **Manage** shown in the badge section.
  2. Uncheck the box next to the admin or moderator badge from the badge list.
  3. Click **Done**.
  4. Refresh your profile page to confirm that the admin or moderator badge no longer appears in the badge section.
