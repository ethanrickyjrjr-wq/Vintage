You can add people you know personally as friends on Facebook. If you're unable to add someone as a friend on Facebook, you or the person you want to add may have various reasons for why you can't add them as a friend.
## They haven’t accepted your friend request yet or you're already friends on Facebook.
  * You may have already sent them a friend request. 
  * Check if the [friend requests you’ve sent](https://www.facebook.com/friends/requests?outgoing=1) are still pending.


## You or the person you want to add have reached the friend limit.
You can have up to 6,000 friends on Facebook. To connect with someone new you can:
  * [Turn on pro mode](https://www.facebook.com/help/2978174759158711?helpref=faq_content) to connect with more than 6,000 friends on Facebook.
  * [Unfriend someone else](https://www.facebook.com/help/172936839431357?helpref=faq_content) then send a new friend request.
  * [Follow them instead](https://www.facebook.com/help/279614732052951?helpref=faq_content) if they have following enabled. You’ll still be able to see their public posts.


**Note:** You won't be able accept a friend request from someone who has 6,000 friends.
## You or the person you want to add can only get friend requests from friends of friends.
  * One of you may have set your [privacy settings](https://www.facebook.com/help/217125868312360?helpref=faq_content) to get friend requests only from friends of friends on Facebook. 
  * Ask them to send you a request instead or change your privacy settings.


## The friend request was deleted.
[Send them a friend request](https://www.facebook.com/help/146466588759199?helpref=uf_permalink) or ask them to send you one instead.
## You’ve blocked them.
To send a friend request to someone you've blocked, learn [how to unblock them](https://www.facebook.com/help/115916435160582?helpref=faq_content). 
## You’ve been blocked from sending friend requests.
Learn why you’ve been [blocked from sending friend requests](https://www.facebook.com/help/320587401313728?helpref=faq_content).
