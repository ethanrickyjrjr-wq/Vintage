We may reject your listing if we find that it doesn’t follow our [Commerce Policies](https://www.facebook.com/policies/commerce) or [Community Standards](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Ftransparency.meta.com%2Fpolicies%2Fcommunity-standards%2F&h=AUBNwbD9ggj0nyNIezwoPy7sHpKgGuBB08yS4OrDBb0XJatRLq6w2o6pMZImUYUyHi2H3R0rTfy9RN64u8lGPhJ3mNF54AZrsiaFmX8uk7UAnmnS2sk1GVysscw6Oq5mixEivNbyxrgUvQ). For example, we might reject a listing for being a [counterfeit item](https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/commerce/third_party_infringement).
Your [Marketplace access](https://www.facebook.com/help/972392066266648/?helpref=faq_content) may be limited or suspended if you break our Commerce Policies. When you come close to having your access suspended, we’ll warn you that your access is at risk.
If your listing is rejected, you may have the option to:
  * **Request a review.** If we find that we made a mistake, we’ll restore your listing.
  * **[Remove your warning.](https://www.facebook.com/help/972392066266648/?helpref=faq_content)** If this is the first time you’ve broken a rule, and depending on the rule(s) you broke, you may be able to remove your warning if your access is at risk. However, your listing(s) that led to the warning won’t be restored.


You can also [learn about Marketplace listing rules](https://www.facebook.com/help/130910837313345/?helpref=faq_content) to avoid having your listings rejected and access suspended.
## Request a review
To request a review:
  1. Go to your profile.
  2. Click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=comet-blackthreedot-desktop&density=1) and select **Profile status**.
  3. Select **Marketplace** below **Extra features**.
  4. Click **See what happened** or **See issue** and select the listing.
  5. Click **Request review**.

  

If you don’t see an option to review, it may be because:
  * It’s been more than 180 days since your listing was rejected.
  * You already requested a review.
  * A review isn’t available for the rule you broke.


## Find out why your listing was rejected
To find out why your listing was rejected:
  1. Go to your profile.
  2. Click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=comet-blackthreedot-desktop&density=1) and select **Profile status**.
  3. Select **Marketplace** below **Extra features**.
  4. Select **Your listings**.
  5. Click the listing(s) below **Needs attention**.
  6. Click **See details**.


We review listings when our systems detect a potential violation or when someone reports them. Listings that do not comply with our policies are rejected. If your listing is rejected but you see similar listings on Marketplace, you can request a review if you believe we made a mistake, or report a listing that you think violates the rules.
