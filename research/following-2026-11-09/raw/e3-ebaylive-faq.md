## eBay Live FAQ
**What is eBay Live?**
eBay Live is a highly interactive livestream shopping experience. This real-time platform allows eBay buyers to shop curated livestream events, hosted by their favorite influencers, brands, and sellers. Visit the [eBay Live Hub](https://www.ebay.com/ebaylive) for more information.
**Do I need an eBay account to participate on eBay Live?**
Yes, you’ll need to be signed into your eBay account before you can interact with an eBay Live event. Currently, only users registered in the US can participate.
Download the eBay app or open [eBay.com](https://www.ebay.com/) and then create or sign into your eBay account to get started.  
  
**Where can I find and join eBay Live events?**
Check out the [eBay Live Hub](https://www.ebay.com/ebaylive) for a schedule of upcoming eBay Live events. You can set a reminder or follow your favorite sellers to be notified when they go live. For reminders about upcoming eBay Live events, enable push notifications from eBay in your account settings. [_Learn more_](https://www.ebay.com/help/account/changing-account-settings/notifications?id=4203).
My eBay > Settings > Notifications > eBay Live
Under **eBay Live** notifications, you can adjust your notification preferences:
  * **Event Reminders** – Alerts you when an event you registered for goes live
  * **Followed Sellers** – Notifies you when a seller you saved goes live
  * **Event Recommendations** – Recommendations for relevant live events


**Will my eBay Live activity be public?**
Yes, your username and eBay Live activity will be viewable to all livestream attendees. It’s possible that the livestream could be recorded or used by other users.
We welcome users to express themselves but please be respectful to the hosts and mindful of the community. Learn more about our Community Guidelines and eBay rules and policies.
**How do auctions work on eBay Live? What are extended auctions (also known as “soft-close auctions”)?**
Sellers can choose to list their items as fixed-price listings (Buy It Now) or as auction listings. If the Seller lists an item as an auction, the Seller may choose to enable extended auctions, which provide extra time if bidding is still hot at the scheduled close of a listing. If a bid is placed in the last 5 seconds of an auction on eBay Live, an extra 5 seconds will be added to the auction’s duration.
During the live auction, the high bid and bidder’s username will be displayed just above the comments section. The winning bidder will be automatically charged for the item and shipping cost and receive an in-app notification and a message in their eBay inbox.

**How do I place a bid during an eBay Live event?**
Before placing a bid on eBay Live, participants must first provide payment and shipping details. eBay Live participants can bid on the items showcased in the event by swiping the blue Bid slider to the right.
Placing a bid authorized eBay to automatically process your payment for the item and related costs if you win. All bids are final and cannot be retracted. Cancellations due to buyer’s remorse are not allowed. Canceled orders will be reviewed case by case.
If you are unable to bid, you may have restrictions on your eBay account. For any questions related to bidding restrictions, buyers should contact eBay customer support for assistance.
**How do I know if I won an eBay Live Auction?**
The winning bidder will receive an in-app notification and a message in their eBay inbox. eBay will automatically process payment for the item and related costs. If payment fails, payment must be completed through the normal checkout process in accordance with eBay policy. Learn more.  

**How can I sell on eBay Live?**
eBay Live is currently in beta with select sellers. Please fill out an [eBay Live Seller Interest Form](https://www.ebay.com/srv/survey/a/sellersvc.ebaylive.application) if you’d like to host an eBay Live event, and we’ll notify you as soon as you’re eligible to start selling live.
**What selling formats are supported on eBay Live?**
eBay Live offers Live Auctions with the option for an extended timer and Buy It Now listings.
**Why can’t I bid during a live event?**
If you see an error message or the bid button is grayed out when you try to bid during a live event, it may be due to one of the following reasons:
  * **Account restrictions:** Your account may have buying or seller restrictions that make you ineligible to bid in live auctions.
  * **Missing address:** You may not have a valid address on file.
  * **Missing payment method:** You do not have a valid payment method on file.


**What are buying restrictions?**
A buying restriction means you won't be able to make any purchases on eBay. It is usually placed on an account due to a policy violation.
If you have a buying restriction on your account, you won't be able to buy items or bid on auctions until the issue is resolved. Buying restrictions can be applied to your account if:
  * You have multiple accounts, and one or more has a buying restriction already placed on it
  * You are violating our Abusive buyer policy
  * Your account is under review for other policy violations


**What are seller-controlled restrictions?**
eBay sellers also have control over who can bid on and buy their items. They can block individual buyers or set buyer requirements based on specific criteria e.g., buyers whose primary shipping address is in a location where they don’t ship, buyers who caused multiple unpaid items or order cancellations.
**How can I avoid bidding restrictions?**
  * Only bid on or buy items you plan to pay for
  * Pay for items as soon as possible
  * Improve your feedback profile by leaving feedback for sellers
  * Establish a buying history by purchasing items that require immediate payment
