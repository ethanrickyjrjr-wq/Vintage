## FAQ
  * #### What is eBay Live?
eBay Live is an interactive live shopping experience where sellers can showcase products, engage with buyers in real time, and sell through live streaming events. Sellers can answer questions, highlight product details, and build a following while buyers discover and shop exclusive inventory during the event. Built on more than 30 years of ecommerce experience, eBay Live combines live shopping with the tools and protections sellers need to sell with confidence.


  * #### Who can sell on eBay Live?
eBay Live is available to eligible professional sellers, including registered businesses and sole proprietors.  
  
To sell on eBay Live, you’ll need an eBay business account and Business Policies for shipping, returns, and payments. If you’re new to selling on eBay, we’ll guide you through these steps during registration. If you already sell on eBay, make sure you have an eBay business account and have set up your Business Policies.  
  
Eligibility is determined based on a number of factors to help maintain a trusted and professional eBay Live community. We expect all our sellers to adhere to our [eBay User Agreement](https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/member-behavior-policies/user-agreement?id=4259&st=3&pos=1&query=User%20Agreement&intent=ebay%20Live%20user%20agreement&lucenceai=lucenceai&docId=HELP1313) and all eBay Policies. Category availability may vary by market seller eligibility requirements. [Learn more about eBay Live policies.](https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/member-behavior-policies/ebay-live-policy?id=5478&st=3&pos=3&query=eBay%20Live%20policy&intent=ebay%20live&lucenceai=lucenceai&docId=HELP1797)  
  
If you’re eligible, you’ll be able to access Live Studio or register to get started. If you don’t currently meet eligibility requirements, you may be placed on the waitlist.


  * #### How can I sell on eBay Live?
If you have a registered business, you can register to sell on eBay Live by completing the registration process. Once approved, you'll be able to access Live Studio, set up your first event, and start selling live.


  * #### Why should I sell on eBay Live?
eBay Live gives sellers a new way to showcase inventory, connect with buyers in real time, and build a loyal audience. By combining live interaction with the reach and trust of eBay, sellers can bring products to life, answer questions instantly, and create more engaging shopping experiences.


  * #### Do I need live selling experience to get started?
No. Whether you're new to live selling or already sell live on other platforms, eBay Live provides onboarding guidance, educational resources, and tools designed to help you prepare for your first show.


  * #### What kinds of products can I sell on eBay Live?
eBay Live supports a wide range of product categories, including collectibles, fashion, home & garden, health & beauty, electronics, sporting goods, and more. If you sell in one of our featured categories—such as trading cards, coins, comics, sports memorabilia, toys, watches, handbags, sneakers, apparel, or jewelry—now is a great time to register and connect with passionate buyers through live shopping. Category availability may vary by market and seller eligibility requirements. [Learn more about eBay Live policies.](https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/member-behavior-policies/ebay-live-policy?id=5478&st=3&pos=3&query=eBay%20Live%20policy&intent=ebay%20live&lucenceai=lucenceai&docId=HELP1797)


  * #### What do I need to start selling on eBay Live?
To get started, you'll need an eligible seller account, inventory to sell, and a device that meets eBay Live's technical requirements. Once you register, you'll be guided through setup, event creation, and preparing for your first live show. [Learn more in the eBay Live selling guide.](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/ebay-live/livestream-selling-ebay-live?id=5762&st=3&pos=1&query=Livestream%20selling%20with%20eBay%20Live&intent=livestream%20selling&lucenceai=lucenceai&docId=HELP1839)


  * #### How do selling fees work on eBay Live?
eBay Live uses a simple fee structure designed for live selling. There's no cost to get started, and your fees depend on the items you sell and the category you're selling in. You can review current rates, pricing details, and seller resources on our pricing page. [Learn more about eBay Live pricing.](https://www.ebay.com/ebaylive/seller-pricing)
