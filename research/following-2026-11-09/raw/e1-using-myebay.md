# Using My eBay
## On this page
  * [My eBay for buyers](https://www.ebay.com/help/account/using-ebay/using-ebay?id=5185#section1)
  * [My eBay for sellers](https://www.ebay.com/help/account/using-ebay/using-ebay?id=5185#section2)

You can track of all of your eBay buying and selling activity, send messages to other eBay members, and update your account details from My eBay.
On desktop, you can find My eBay at the top right of most pages, and once you’ve signed in, you’ll find everything you need under the **Activity** , **Messages** , and **Account** tabs. 
You can also hover over My eBay to see a menu of options, such as Bids/Offers, Watchlist, Messages and more.
In the iOS eBay app you can find My eBay at the bottom left-hand side of the home screen. On Android, simply tap the **Menu** button at the top left-hand side.
Select the button below to go to My eBay now: 
[Go to My eBay - opens in new window or tab](https://www.ebay.com/myb/Summary)
## My eBay for buyers
Whether you’re browsing or you’ve already made a purchase, the following My eBay pages will help you get the most out of your shopping experience. Select the links to read more. 
  * [Recently viewed](https://www.ebay.com/help/buying/search-tips/recently-viewed-items?id=4048) – find listings that you've looked at recently 
  * ​[Watchlist](https://www.ebay.com/help/buying/search-tips/watchlist?id=4046) – keep an eye on up to 300 items while you make up your mind about buying
  * [Purchase history](https://www.ebay.com/help/buying/search-tips/purchase-history?id=4047) – see all your orders from the last 3 years
  * [Saved searches](https://www.ebay.com/help/buying/search-tips/saved-searches?id=4051) – save the keywords for items you regularly search for and receive alerts when newly listed items match your search
  * Saved sellers – when you’ve had a good experience with a seller, select **Save this seller** in the listing to add them to your saved sellers list 


Find out more in our article about [how buying works on eBay](https://www.ebay.com/help/buying/buy-now/buying-works-ebay?id=4002). 
**Tip**   
If you're using the eBay app, make sure you enable notifications on your device so you don’t miss any important buying or selling reminders. See our page on [notifications](https://www.ebay.com/help/account/changing-account-settings/notifications?id=4203) for more information.
## My eBay for sellers
You’ll find information on all of your sales, fees, listings, and payouts in [Selling overview - opens in new window or tab](https://www.ebay.com/mys/overview) in My eBay. 
Keep track of your sales and listings by selecting the following options from the Selling menu on the left-hand side:
  * Sell an item – create new listings for items you’d like to sell
  * Drafts – listings you’ve started to create but aren’t live yet
  * Scheduled – listings you’ve scheduled to go live in the future
  * Active – listings that are live right now
  * Orders – completed listings where you made a sale
  * Unsold – listings where you didn’t make a sale
  * Payment options – add a payment method so we can pay you for the items you sell 
  * Shipping labels – buy and print shipping labels


Find out more about creating and managing your listings in our [listings overview](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/listings/listingsoverview?id=4072) article, or if you’re new to selling, have a look at our [guide to selling](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/selling/start-selling-ebay?id=4081) and [guide to getting paid](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/getting-paid/start-getting-paid-ebay?id=4795).
**Tip**  
You’ll also find your [seller standards dashboard - opens in new window or tab](https://sellerstandards.ebay.com/dashboard) in My eBay, which gives detailed information about your seller level and performance. Keep an eye on the dashboard to see if there’s any area you could improve on.
## Messages
You can receive and reply to messages from other eBay members by going to your [Messages - opens in new window or tab](https://mesg.ebay.com/mesgweb/ViewMessages/0). 
We’ll send important alerts about your account, as well as general news and announcements, to both your eBay Messages and your registered email address. 
Read our [page on Messages](https://www.ebay.com/help/account/messages/messages?id=4194) to find out how to organize your inbox, including how to add a signature or out-of-office reply for your eBay Store.
## Account settings
If you need to update your account information or preferences, for example if you need to change address or update your payment options, you can do so from the [Account tab - opens in new window or tab](https://accountsettings.ebay.com/uas) in My eBay.
Find out more in our article on [changing your account settings](https://www.ebay.com/help/account/changing-account-settings/changing-account-settings?id=4193).
Top Takeaway
### Go to My eBay to manage all of your buying and selling activity, check your Messages, and update your account settings.
