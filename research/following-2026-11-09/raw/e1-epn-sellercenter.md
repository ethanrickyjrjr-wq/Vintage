## What is the eBay Partner Network?
The [eBay Partner Network](https://partnernetwork.ebay.com/) is eBay’s affiliate marketing program. By signing up and sharing your listings outside of eBay, you can increase your earnings and diversify your revenue streams.
  

### How it works
#### Join
Complete our quick online application and we’ll do the rest. You’ll receive a notification when your application has been approved, usually within just a few hours.
#### Create
Use our free and simple tools to easily create trackable eBay Partner Network links for your listings.
#### Share

  2. A buyer places a bid on an Auction item within 24 hours after clicking your link and wins that auction within 10 days.
  3. The seller accepts a Best Offer placed within 24 hours after clicking your link.
