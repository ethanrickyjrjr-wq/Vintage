# What Resellers Get From Connecting With Other Resellers
  * September 22, 2026


[ ![Picture of List Perfectly Team](https://listperfectly.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Screen-Shot-2024-04-08-at-10.15.06-AM.png) ](https://listperfectly.com/author/listperfectlyteam/)

Reseller networking gives you knowledge you do not get from listing alone. You hear and watch how other sellers handle marketplace changes, inventory, shipping, slow sales, and the everyday decisions that come with running a resale business.
[Listing Party](https://listingparty.com/) is List Perfectly’s private live online reseller community, where moderated sessions give resellers a place to ask questions without second-guessing whether the question is too basic. Is anyone else having this problem? Did a marketplace change something? What are other resellers doing?
No reseller starts knowing every marketplace, shipping rule, inventory system, or what to do every time something changes. That knowledge grows by selling, asking questions, listening, and watching how other resellers run their businesses.
## **What Do Resellers Get From Reseller Networking?**
Reseller networking gives access to other sellers’ experience, processes, resources, reseller tools, worksheets, and opportunities to hear directly from marketplace or List Perfectly team members. Over the years, our reseller community has connected with teams from eBay, Depop, Etsy, Whatnot, Mercari, Grailed, and other selling platforms.
One reseller may know eBay inside and out but be completely new to Vinted. Another may be trying to understand what[ eBay’s acquisition of Depop](https://www.ebayinc.com/stories/news/ebay-completes-acquisition-of-depop/) means for resellers. Someone else knows vintage, jewelry, cards, clothing, or hard goods, while another reseller has figured out how to manage thousands of listings, improve sell-through rate or STR, sell more without simply listing more inventory, resell while working full time, or build income for retirement.
Those different experiences are what make a reseller community useful.[ Brian](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=esG3nFS_BWw) built reselling alongside his full-time career and into retirement.[ Kathy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hIFTJgEeGgM) took 30 years of experience as a music and theater teacher into her reselling business. You can also read her story in[ What to Do After Retirement: Reduce Clutter and Make Extra Income by Reselling](https://listperfectly.com/tips/what-to-do-after-retirement-reselling/).[ Debbie](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_IP-ompGn7c) built her business around a different set of goals and experiences.
## **A Safe Place to Ask Reseller Questions**
When something changes on eBay, Poshmark, Vinted, Whatnot, or another marketplace, one of the first things resellers want to know is: Is anyone else seeing this too?
A moderated reseller community gives you a place to ask about a shipping change, new fee, listing problem, pricing question, slow sales, inventory issue, or selling tool without feeling like you were supposed to know the answer already. At List Perfectly, we often say the only silly question is the one that was not asked.
![listing party events page](https://listperfectly.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Screenshot-2026-09-22-at-10.22.27%E2%80%AFAM-1024x515.png)
## **Online vs. In-Person Reseller Networking**
List Perfectly strongly supports remote work and online reseller networking. Resellers should not have to travel or commute somewhere just to connect, ask questions, or keep learning.
That is why Listing Party offers live online reseller sessions throughout the week, including daily live help, Mastermind, accountability sessions, seller socials, and other virtual events. Resellers can join from home, their office, warehouse, sourcing location, or wherever they run their business. Live streams, YouTube videos, social media, and virtual reseller communities also make it easier to hear marketplace updates, watch demonstrations, listen to other sellers, and ask questions without planning a trip around it.
In-person reseller networking has a different advantage: speed.
At an event like eBay Open, PoshFest, FlipCon, or BOSS Reseller Remix, you can learn in one day what might take weeks of virtual sessions, live streams, posts, comments, and online conversations to piece together.
Even the shyest reseller usually finds another seller who sells something similar, uses the same marketplace, has the same business problem, or is working toward a similar goal. Once that connection happens, questions that may have felt difficult to ask online become much easier face-to-face.
One conversation can turn into answers about pricing, inventory, marketplace changes, software, sourcing, retirement, or how another reseller solved the exact problem you have been trying to figure out.
List Perfectly supports both sides of reseller networking. Our history includes[ eBay Open sponsorship and participation](https://listperfectly.com/reseller-stories/list-perfectly-ebay-open-sponsorship/), going[ from PoshFest attendees to Poshmark sponsors](https://listperfectly.com/reseller-stories/from-poshfest-attendees-to-poshmark-sponsors-the-list-perfectly-story/), serving as a[ main sponsor of FlipCon](https://listperfectly.com/timeline/list-perfectly-main-sponsor-of-flipcon/), and supporting[ BOSS Reseller Remix as a main sponsor](https://listperfectly.com/uncategorized/boost-your-reselling-game-reasons-to-attend-the-boss-reseller-remix/).
Amanda and I were resellers before we built List Perfectly. We know virtual reseller communities, live sessions, marketplace teams, reseller events, and the questions sellers bring because we have spent years participating in all of them ourselves.

## **Frequently Asked Questions About Reseller Networking**
### **What is reseller networking?**
Reseller networking is connecting with other online sellers to share marketplace knowledge, experience, resources, processes, and answers to questions that come up while running a reselling business.
### **Do I need to be a List Perfectly customer to join its reseller community?**
No. The List Perfectly Facebook Group and other free reseller resources are open to resellers whether or not they use List Perfectly. Listing Party is included with every List Perfectly plan.
### **Can shy resellers participate in Listing Party?**
Yes. Camera use is optional, and resellers can listen, ask questions, join discussions, and participate at their own comfort level. The goal is to make it easier to connect with other sellers and get useful answers without feeling pressured to perform.
