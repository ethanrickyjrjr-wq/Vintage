# Types of verified badges on Facebook
Copy link
Verified profiles fall into a few categories: people who complete free Facebook Verified to show there's a real person behind their profile, people and businesses subscribed to Meta Verified, and notable people, brands, or entities.
  * **[Get Facebook Verified](https://www.facebook.com/help/1557824259344759?helpref=faq_content)**![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-verified-badge&density=1) : Facebook Verified indicates a profile has been verified based on activity across Facebook and other information provided. The Verified badge is free and can appear across Facebook surfaces, including Marketplace, Groups, Jobs, Facebook Dating and Profile.
  * **[Subscribe to Meta Verified](https://www.facebook.com/help/1206202490291214/?helpref=faq_content)![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-verifiedbadge-iphone&density=1)** : Meta Verified is a paid subscription that also involves verification and comes with benefits like the blue verified badge ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-verifiedbadge-iphone&density=1), enhanced support, impersonation protection, and more.
  * **[Apply for a blue (notable) verified badge](https://business.facebook.com/help/media/forms/?selectedSymptom=1100319309614978)![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-verifiedbadge-iphone&density=1) :** If you're a public figure, celebrity or brand, you can apply for the blue verified badge if you meet the requirements below. 


## Requirements for the blue (notable) verified badge
These requirements apply only to the blue verified badge for notable people, brands and entities – not to [Facebook Verified](https://www.facebook.com/help/1557824259344759?helpref=faq_content) or [Meta Verified](https://www.facebook.com/help/1206202490291214/?helpref=faq_content). Your Page or profile must follow our [Terms of Service](https://www.facebook.com/terms/) and [Community Standards](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Ftransparency.meta.com%2Fpolicies%2Fcommunity-standards%2F&h=AUCG7VfypNTQH--OIv-eMAkOREtgJSDywdPd6EHPga2ZTqALU6350L-gNvW625WJIjrY7-_Sj7YN6nqeH5FrqVe9UqsZ_ECeTa8wHu0IAngyX0_tG4uQYzNzEUUo6cpz594yh_0SRzyVFQ) and be:
  * **Authentic:** Represent a real person, registered business or entity.
  * **Unique:** Be the only presence of this person or business. Only one Page or profile per person or business may be verified, with exceptions for language-specific Pages and profiles. We don't verify general interest Pages and profiles (example: Puppy Memes).
  * **Complete:** Have an about section, Page or profile photo and recent activity, including at least one post.
  * **Notable:** Represent a well-known, often searched person, brand or entity. We review Pages and profiles that are featured in multiple news sources, and we don't consider paid or promotional content as sources for review.


If you don't meet the criteria, you can still show your Page or profile is authentic in other ways. For example, you can link to it from an official website or Instagram profile. You can also subscribe to [Meta Verified](https://www.facebook.com/help/1206202490291214/?helpref=faq_content), or, if you’re eligible, get a [Facebook Verified badge](https://www.facebook.com/help/1557824259344759?helpref=faq_content) to show there's a real person behind your profile — no notability or subscription required.
Note: If you already have an existing verified badge based on prior requirements, you can still subscribe to Meta Verified.
We encourage admins of verified Pages and profiles to use two-factor authentication.
