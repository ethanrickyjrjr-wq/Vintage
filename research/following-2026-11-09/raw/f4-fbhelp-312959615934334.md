# Facebook Dating
If you’re interested in using Facebook Dating to meet new people, you can create a Dating profile. In Dating you can send likes and messages to people you’re interested in. Once someone likes you back, you’ll match and can start chatting in Dating.
Note that all people on Dating must follow our [Community Standards](https://www.facebook.com/communitystandards/). Dating profiles and activity can't be promotional in nature or contain commercial offers.
