## What Is Facebook Marketplace?
Facebook Marketplace is Meta's online buying and selling platform built directly into Facebook. It allowes individuals and businesses to buy and sell physical items locally or ship eligible products nationwide.
Unlike traditional ecommerce websites, Marketplace combines shopping with Facebook's social network, making it easy for buyers to discover listings while browsing the app. It turns scrollers into  _shoppers._
Because Marketplace is integrated into Facebook, sellers can communicate directly with buyers through Messenger, share listings to Facebook Groups, and promote listings with paid advertising.
Facebook Marketplace is available in many countries and can be accessed through both the Facebook app and desktop website.
![How to Sell on Facebook Marketplace Opti](https://blog.vendoo.co/hs-fs/hubfs/How%20to%20Sell%20on%20Facebook%20Marketplace%20Opti.webp?width=851&height=551&name=How%20to%20Sell%20on%20Facebook%20Marketplace%20Opti.webp)
💡 **Tip:** You must have a Facebook account in your name to buy and sell on Facebook Marketplace. Do not create a new Facebook account to sell, because new accounts have limited selling abilities due to security measures.
## What Can You Sell on Facebook Marketplace?
You can sell almost anything on Facebook Marketplace as long as it’s not alive, illegal, perishable, dangerous, or too _mature_. Items must be tangible, so digital downloads or services are not allowed. 
[Top-selling items on Facebook Marketplace](https://blog.vendoo.co/top-selling-items-on-facebook-marketplace-in-2024) include cars and vehicles, electronics and media, home décor, and toys.

Examples include:

  * Sporting goods
  * Musical instruments
  * Pet supplies
  * Garden equipment

## Why sell on Facebook Marketplace?
Facebook Marketplace remains one of the largest online selling platforms because it combines a massive audience with a simple listing process and low selling costs.
Here are some of its biggest advantages:
### **Massive Audience Reach**
Facebook continues to be one of the world's largest social platforms, with billions of monthly active users across Meta's apps. Marketplace gives sellers access to a huge audience without paying listing fees.
Unlike traditional ecommerce sites where buyers intentionally search for products, Marketplace listings also appear throughout Facebook's shopping experience, helping shoppers discover items they weren't necessarily looking for.
### **Localized Selling**
One of Facebook Marketplace's biggest strengths is local selling. You can meet buyers nearby, avoid shipping costs, receive payment immediately, and sell large or fragile items that would otherwise be difficult or expensive to ship.
Furniture, appliances, outdoor equipment, and home décor often perform especially well as local pickup listings.
💡 **Tip:** Exercise caution when meeting with strangers, and learn [how to protect yourself from Facebook Marketplace scams](https://blog.vendoo.co/how-to-protect-yourself-from-facebook-marketplace-scam) before you meet up. It is best to meet in a public location. Many police departments offer designated Safe Exchange Zones.
### Nationwide Shipping
Eligible sellers can also ship many items across the United States, allowing you to reach buyers well beyond your local area.
Offering shipping significantly expands your customer base and makes Marketplace a viable option for resellers looking to increase sales volume.
### **FBMP Built-In Social Selling Features**
Facebook Marketplace integrates naturally with Facebook's ecosystem. You can share listings to Buy and Sell Groups, Boost listings with paid ads, and promote listings to local audiences. These built-in promotional tools help Marketplace listings reach more potential buyers than many standalone resale platforms.
### **Buyer Confidence**
Facebook profiles, seller ratings, and purchase history can help establish trust between buyers and sellers. While every transaction should still be approached carefully, buyers often appreciate being able to view a seller's profile and Marketplace ratings before making a purchase. Being able to physically see and inspect an item IRL prior to purchase provides further trust to buyers. Immediate cash-in-hand is convenient for sellers.
### **Ease of Use**
Marketplace is designed for simplicity, and it is one of the quickest and easiest ways to sell online.
Creating listings takes only a few minutes, and because most users are already familiar with Facebook, there is very little learning curve compared to some dedicated ecommerce platforms.

## Facebook Marketplace Fees & Payout
Facebook Marketplace remains one of the most affordable marketplaces for online sellers. There are no listing fees, and local sales arranged directly between buyers and sellers are free. If you sell an item with shipping through Facebook Marketplace, Meta deducts a selling fee from your payout, so it helps to understand [Facebook Marketplace fees](https://blog.vendoo.co/everything-you-need-to-know-about-facebook-marketplace-fees) before you list.
For shipped orders, the selling fee is:
  * 10% of the sale price, or
  * $0.80 minimum per order, whichever is greater.


💡 **Tip:** When selling locally, use safe and protected payment platforms like Facebook Pay, Venmo Business, or PayPal, which offer buyer protection. Note these transaction apps might have fees. 
### Facebook Marketplace Payouts: How Do I Get Paid on Facebook Marketplace?
To receive payment for shipped orders, you must:
  * Mark the order as shipped.
  * Enter a valid tracking number.
  * Have a verified bank account connected to your Marketplace seller account.


Meta initiates your payout **15 days after you mark the item as shipped** or **5 days after delivery is confirmed** , whichever comes first. After your payout is initiated, it can take up to 5 additional days for the funds to appear in your bank account, depending on your financial institution.
Be patient. In total, it may take **up to 20 days** from the date you mark an item as shipped for the money to reach your account.

## Selling Items on Facebook Marketplace: Step-by-Step Guide
If you're wondering how to sell on Facebook Marketplace, getting started only takes a few minutes. Once your listing is live, Marketplace makes it easy to communicate with buyers, manage listings, and track your sales from one place.
![How to Sell on FBMP Listings View](https://blog.vendoo.co/hs-fs/hubfs/How%20to%20Sell%20on%20FBMP%20Listings%20View.webp?width=851&height=471&name=How%20to%20Sell%20on%20FBMP%20Listings%20View.webp)
Follow these steps to start selling on Facebook Marketplace:
### 1. Access Facebook Marketplace
Open the Facebook app or visit Facebook.com and select the **M** arketplace icon from the navigation menu.
If Marketplace is available in your region and your account is in good standing, you'll be able to browse listings and create your own.
💡 **Tip:** You can hide listings from your Facebook friends if you don’t want them to see your listings.
### **2. Create a Listing**
Click Create New Listing and choose the listing type that best fits your item. Choices include list an item, list a vehicle, or list a home for sale. In most cases, you will select List Item for Sale. 
### **3. Add Photos**
Photos are often the first thing buyers notice, so quality matters. For the best results, use photos with bright lighting, photograph with a clean background, include all angles, and capture close-up pictures of important details. Be sure to disclose all flaws clearly.
![How to Photograph Items to Sell Online on FBMP](https://blog.vendoo.co/hs-fs/hubfs/How%20to%20Photograph%20Items%20to%20Sell%20Online.webp?width=851&height=225&name=How%20to%20Photograph%20Items%20to%20Sell%20Online.webp)
💡Tip: You can also use videos to show your items. If a picture is worth a thousand words, then a video is worth  _a million._
### **4. Write a Title and Description**
Titles and descripions help your listings be shown to buyers searching for similar items. When drafting titles and descriptions, use descriptive keywords that buyers are likely to search.
Include important details such as:
  * Brand
  * Size
  * Color
  * Model
  * Material
  * Condition
  * Measurements when applicable


Avoid keyword stuffing or using misleading titles. Accurate listings help attract qualified buyers and reduce questions after the sale.
The more specific your listing, the easier it is for buyers to find it. Here's an example 👀
![SEO on Facebook Marketplace](https://blog.vendoo.co/hs-fs/hubfs/SEO%20on%20Facebook%20Marketplace.webp?width=851&height=547&name=SEO%20on%20Facebook%20Marketplace.webp)
### **5. Set a Competitive Price**
Research similar sold and active listings before pricing your item. Facebook Marketplace is highly competitive, particularly for local sales. Buyers often compare multiple listings before making a purchase.
If you're willing to negotiate, consider pricing slightly above your minimum acceptable price to leave room for offers.
### **6. Choose Local Shipping or Pickup**
Facebook Marketplace allows many sellers to offer to ship worldwide. Local pickup is always an option as well. Or, you can do  _both_ local pickup and shipping.
Offering shipping expands your audience beyond your local area, while local pickup eliminates shipping costs and often results in faster transactions.
Choose the option that makes the most sense for the item you're selling.
### **7. Publish Your Listing**
Review all listing details before publishing. Double-check photos, title, description, category, price, and shipping options. 
Once published, your listing will appear in Marketplace search results and may also be recommended to buyers based on their shopping activity.
### **8. Respond Quickly to Buyers**
Fast response times can significantly improve your chances of making a sale. Watch for messages in your Marketplace inbox and answer questions promptly.
Common buyer questions include:
  * Is this still available?
  * Can you ship?
  * Would you take a lower price?
  * When can I pick it up?


Prompt, professional communication helps build buyer confidence.
💡Tip: Marketplace conversations appear in Messenger, but Marketplace also has its own inbox for buying and selling activity. Check both regularly so you don't miss potential sales.
### **9. Share Your FBMP Listings**
One advantage Facebook Marketplace has over many resale platforms is its built-in social sharing. You can increase visibility by sharing your listing to Local Buying and Selling Groups, Community groups, Neighborhood, Town, and County Pages, and your own Facebook Profile. 
![Facebook Marketplace Sharing](https://blog.vendoo.co/hs-fs/hubfs/Selling%20on%20FBMP.png?width=1446&height=1275&name=Selling%20on%20FBMP.png)Once your listing is published, select "List in more places" to share to groups and pages, or click "Share listing" to share on your own Facebook profile.
💡 Tip: Be sure each group's rules allow Marketplace listings before posting.

Instead of creating listings manually on every platform, Vendoo lets you create one listing and publish it to multiple marketplaces, including Facebook Marketplace.
You can also manage inventory, delist sold items, and relist stale inventory from one dashboard, helping you save time while reducing overselling.

## Facebook Marketplace Tips for Seller Success
Selling consistently on Facebook Marketplace requires more than simply posting listings. These best practices can help increase visibility and improve your sell-through rate.
  * **List consistently.** Adding new listings regularly helps keep your Marketplace account active and can improve your visibility in search results.  
  

  * **Use high-quality photos.** Bright, well-lit images with a clean background attract more buyers than dark or cluttered photos. Include multiple angles and close-ups of any flaws.  
  

  * **Write keyword-rich titles.** Include searchable details like the brand, size, color, model, and material so buyers can easily find your listing.  
  

  * **Price competitively.** Research similar listings before pricing your item. If you're open to negotiating, leave a little room for offers.  
  

  * **Renew eligible listings.** When Facebook allows you to renew a listing, take advantage of it to move your item back toward the top of search results.   
  

  * **Earn positive seller ratings.** Ship quickly, communicate professionally, and describe items accurately to build trust and encourage positive reviews. Sellers with high rankings are rewarded with additional exposure.  
  

  * **Relist stale inventory.** If a listing stops getting views, deleting and relisting it can give it new exposure. Vendoo makes this process much faster by allowing you to delist and relist in bulk. If you use Vendoo, make sure to delist and relist through Vendoo itself (rather than on Facebook) to avoid breaking the sync.  
  

  * **Boost high-value listings.** If you're selling an expensive item or want additional exposure, consider using Facebook's paid promotion tools to reach more buyers.


## How to Ship on Facebook Marketplace
Shipping on Facebook Marketplace allows you to reach buyers across the country instead of limiting your sales to your local area. When creating an eligible listing, you can choose to offer shipping, local pickup, or both.
Offering shipping can significantly increase your potential audience, especially for clothing, electronics, collectibles, and other items that are easy to package and mail.
### Choose Your Shipping Method
Depending on your listing, you can:
  * Purchase your own shipping label and upload the tracking information.
  * Offer free shipping and include the shipping cost in your item price.
  * Charge the buyer for shipping.


Facebook Marketplace supports major shipping carriers, including USPS, UPS, and FedEx.
💡 **Tip:** Take advantage of Facebook’s frequent free shipping promotions.
### Package Your Item Carefully
Proper packaging helps protect your item during transit and reduces the risk of damage claims. Use a sturdy box or mailer, protect fragile items, seal packages securely, and make sure your items are weather-proof. 
### Mark Your Order as Shipped
If you use a Facebook-provided shipping label, you do not need to manually enter tracking information. If you ship on your own, you must mark the order as "shipped" and provide the tracking number.
Tracking allows both you and your buyer to monitor the shipment and is required for Marketplace payouts on shipped orders.
### When Will You Get Paid?
After you mark an order as shipped and upload tracking, Meta will initiate your payout:
  * 15 days after you mark the item as shipped, or
  * 5 days after the item is delivered and delivery is confirmed... whichever comes first.


Once your payout has been initiated, it may take up to 5 additional days for the funds to appear in your bank account. Depending on the timing of delivery and your bank's processing time, the entire payout process can take up to 20 days from the date you mark the order as shipped.
💡 **Tip:** Upload tracking as soon as you ship your order. Prompt tracking updates help reassure buyers, reduce "Where is my order?" messages, and are required before Meta can begin processing your payout.
### Should You Offer Free Shipping?
Offering free shipping can make your listings more attractive to buyers, especially when competing with similar items. Rather than absorbing the shipping cost, many sellers simply build the estimated shipping expense into the item's price.
Whether free shipping makes sense depends on the item's size, weight, and competition. For lightweight items like clothing, offering free shipping can often increase buyer interest.
🧠 Did you know that buyers can filter their search to see only items with free shipping? This means that sellers who charge for shipping are negatively affecting exposure. 
### FBMP Shipping Tips
  * Ship orders as quickly as possible.
  * Upload tracking immediately after shipping.
  * Pack items securely to prevent damage.
  * Save your shipping receipt until the transaction is complete.
  * Communicate with the buyer if there are any unexpected shipping delays.

## Facebook Marketplace Local Selling
One of the biggest advantages of Facebook Marketplace is the ability to sell locally. Local pickup eliminates shipping costs, allows buyers to receive items immediately, and is ideal for furniture, appliances, home décor, sporting equipment, and other large or fragile items.
When creating a listing, you can choose to offer local pickup, shipping, or both. Offering both options gives buyers more flexibility while expanding your potential audience.

## Buyer and Seller Protection & Disputing Transactions
![Facebook Marketplace Seller Protection](https://blog.vendoo.co/hs-fs/hubfs/Facebook%20Marketplace%20Seller%20Protection.png?width=1580&height=557&name=Facebook%20Marketplace%20Seller%20Protection.png)
Facebook Marketplace offers purchase protection for many eligible shipped orders processed through Facebook Checkout. While local transactions are generally handled directly between buyers and sellers, shipped orders may qualify for protection under Meta's Purchase Protection Policies.
Buyers may be eligible to file a claim if:
  * The item never arrives.
  * The item arrives damaged.
  * The item is significantly different from the listing description.
  * Important parts or accessories are missing.


The easiest way to avoid disputes is to create accurate listings and communicate clearly throughout the transaction.
Best practices include:
  * Photograph the actual item from multiple angles.
  * Clearly disclose flaws or damage.
  * Write detailed descriptions.
  * Package items securely.
  * Upload tracking immediately after shipping.
  * Respond promptly if a buyer has questions.


If an issue arises, communicate professionally and try to resolve it directly with the buyer whenever possible. For the latest information about returns, refunds, disputes, and Purchase Protection eligibility, refer to Meta's Help Center. Learn more about protected purchases, disputes, and refunds[here](https://www.facebook.com/policies/purchase_protection).

## Facebook Marketplace FAQs
Here are some frequently asked questions about selling on Facebook Marketplace: 
### Is Facebook Marketplace free to use?
Yes. Facebook Marketplace does not charge listing fees. Local sales are free, while eligible shipped orders are subject to Meta's 10% selling fee (minimum $0.80).
### How much does Facebook Marketplace charge sellers?
Meta charges a 10% selling fee, with a minimum fee of $0.80 per order, on eligible shipped orders. The fee is automatically deducted from your payout before your earnings are deposited into your bank account.
### How do Facebook Marketplace payouts work?
Mark your order as shipped and upload a valid tracking number. Meta initiates your payout 15 days after shipment or 5 days after delivery confirmation, whichever comes first. It may then take up to 5 additional days for the funds to reach your bank account.
### Can I offer both shipping and local pickup?
Yes. Many sellers offer both options to reach more buyers. Local pickup works well for large or fragile items, while shipping expands your audience beyond your local area.
### What sells best on Facebook Marketplace?
Furniture, home décor, clothing, electronics, baby gear, sporting goods, toys, tools, appliances, and vehicles are consistently among the platform's most popular categories. Demand varies by location and season.
### Why can't I offer shipping on Facebook Marketplace?
Shipping eligibility depends on factors like your account status, the item category, and whether Meta has enabled shipping for your account. New or restricted accounts may have limited Marketplace features.
### What should you avoid when selling on Facebook Marketplace?
Avoid selling prohibited items, creating misleading listings, accepting suspicious payments, or meeting buyers in unsafe locations—knowing the signs of avoiding a Facebook Marketplace scam can help you steer clear of trouble. Always follow Meta's Commerce Policies.
### Is Facebook Marketplace worth it?
Yes. With no listing fees, free local selling, and access to millions of buyers, Facebook Marketplace remains one of the easiest and most affordable platforms for both casual sellers and professional resellers.
