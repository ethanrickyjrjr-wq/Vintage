## Group rules from the admins
* * *
1
LIST WITH ERA - items 60s-90s.
Please add era to all listings. If it is 90s, make it something worth having - this is about fashion and style.
2
FULLY DESCRIBE - PHOTOS and CONDITION
Listing should include photos of entire item, front and back, inside for lining/seams/to show authenticity and condition. Buyers cant handle the item, they rely on your words and images.
3
NO OUTSIDE LINKS OR ADS FOR OTHER VENUES
Sellers work for the traffic here, don't send it elsewhere.
4
ISO posts
They must have this at the top "ISO - Please post with price and photos, no teasers. Please do not start to describe without those things. Usual selling rules apply in ISO threads" (We mean the top, we look at the top line, if it isn't there, it is deleted.)
5
PAYMENT BY PAYPAL OR CASH
It protects buyer and seller. Terms of paypal available on their website.
