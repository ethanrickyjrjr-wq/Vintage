# Connected experiences across your profiles in your Meta Account
Updated:6 days ago
Some connected experiences aren't available in all countries. WhatsApp and Threads in a Meta Account aren't available in all countries.
After you add your profiles to the same Meta Account you can turn on and turn off different connected experiences, like:
  * ## Share posts, stories or reels across your profiles
You can share posts, stories or reels between your Instagram and Facebook profiles, share them from Instagram or Facebook to Threads, and share your WhatsApp Status to your Facebook or Instagram Stories.
    * To share from your Horizon profile, go to [Horizon Worlds.](https://www.meta.com/help/quest/articles/horizon/explore-horizon-worlds/take-and-share-photos-horizon/)
    * You can comment on Instagram reels recommended to you on Facebook with an Instagram profile you’ve added to the same Meta Account. You can change the Instagram profile used for commenting by selecting a different Instagram profile to share to.  

    * You can create a Status on WhatsApp and share it to your Facebook or Instagram Stories automatically. It's shared with the audience you last chose for a story on that app. Note: You can't share your Facebook or Instagram Stories to your WhatsApp Status.


  * Sync your profile picture across Facebook, Instagram and/or Meta Horizon and Threads profiles.
    * People may also see suggestions (like to add you as a friend or to follow you) associated with this profile picture across Facebook, Instagram and Meta Horizon.
    * If you sync your profile picture across your Facebook, Instagram or Meta Horizon profiles, changes you make to your profile picture will appear across all of these profiles. 
    * If you remove a profile from a Meta Account, your profile picture for profiles in that account will no longer sync.
    * Note: You can’t sync profile information from a Facebook, Instagram or Meta Horizon profile to another profile on the same product (for example, you can’t sync two Instagram profiles, or two Facebook profiles). 
  * Manage avatars. You can view all your avatars in one place, and easily assign the same avatar across multiple profiles. When you change your avatar for one of your profiles, it will update on all the profiles using that avatar. 
  * Sync your shopping activity across profiles. When your shopping activity is synced across Facebook and Instagram, you’ll have access to features like:  

    * Your wishlist will be visible to you on Facebook and Instagram. You’ll be able to add and see items in your wishlist from all connected profiles.  

    * Your recently viewed items will be visible to you across your profiles.
    * Note: Synced shopping activity isn’t available to everyone who has access to a Meta Account. Learn more about [Facebook shops](https://www.facebook.com/help/922407374933346?ref=faq) and [shops on Instagram.](https://help.instagram.com/240116693975803?ref=faq)
  * Connect Facebook Dating and Instagram. You’ll be able to:
    * Share Instagram posts by adding them to your Dating profile. 
    * Add Instagram followers as [secret crushes](https://www.facebook.com/help/347243103977573/).
  * Follow people on Meta Horizon. You’ll be able to: 
    * Send follow requests to your Facebook friends or people you follow on Instagram who also have Meta Horizon profiles and have chosen to show their Facebook and/or Instagram profiles with their Meta Horizon profile. 
    * Note: You can only turn on this connected experience if you have a Meta Account. 
    * Showing your other profiles in Meta Horizon. You’ll be able to: 
      * Choose which names or usernames are shown in each of your profiles. For example, you can update your Instagram profile to show your Facebook name or Meta Horizon username. 
      * Update your Meta Horizon profile to show your Facebook name or Instagram username, and who can see this information (for example, anyone or only your followers on Facebook and/or Instagram who also use Meta Horizon). 
      * Note:
        * You can only enable this connected experience if you have a Meta account. 
        * People will be able to see and search for you by your Facebook, Instagram, or Horizon profile information.
        * These profiles may be visible together when you’re suggested to others. 
        * You can always see your Facebook name and Instagram profiles in your own Horizon profile. This setting controls how others can see your Facebook name and Instagram profiles.
  * View media captured in Meta Horizon Worlds in your Instagram photo galleries through Meta Cloud Albums. You’ll be able to:
    * Choose which of your profiles in a Meta Account can access media captured from Meta Horizon Worlds from your Meta Horizon profile.


Starting early 2026, the connected experience, Logging in with accounts, will no longer be available, and all profiles will be able to log into each other by default. This means you’ll be able to use any profile to log into other profiles in the same Meta Account.
Due to this update, some Facebook, Instagram and Meta profiles will be moved into different Meta Accounts unless logging in with profiles is enabled for all profiles in the same Meta Account. 
To keep your profiles in the same Meta Account:
  1. Go to your Meta Account.
  2. Go to Connected experiences, then tap Logging in with profile.
  3. Select Allow all profiles to log into each other.


If you want to disable login access for a profile, you can [move that account out of your Meta Account](https://www.meta.com/help/accounts-center/589288436078759/?srsltid=AfmBOoq-tzmIKY6oPrytjqf6mgb5SUD4Sbiu22zCM0EpZH_SjfJGXKue).
If you want to turn on login access for a profile, you can [add that profile to your Meta Account.](https://www.meta.com/help/accounts-center/589288436078759/?srsltid=AfmBOoq-tzmIKY6oPrytjqf6mgb5SUD4Sbiu22zCM0EpZH_SjfJGXKue)
Note: Profiles can only access [connected experiences](https://www.meta.com/help/accounts-center/2862196384090214/), including Logging in with profiles, if they’re in the same Meta Account.
