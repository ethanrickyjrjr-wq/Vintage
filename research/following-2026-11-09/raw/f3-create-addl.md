You are no longer able to create additional Facebook profiles. Existing additional profiles have not been affected. 
This article is about switching into additional Facebook profiles. If you are trying to [switch between different Facebook accounts, read this article](https://www.facebook.com/help/804757836392387?helpref=faq_content).
## Switch between your profiles
  1. Click your profile photo in the top right of Facebook.
  2. Click **See all profiles**.
  3. Select the profile you want to use.


Learn how to [delete or deactivate an additional Facebook profile](https://www.facebook.com/help/1248946995836019/?helpref=faq_content).
