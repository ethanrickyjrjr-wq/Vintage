## Group rules from the admins
* * *
1
INTERESTED
To buy and take your place in line, please write interested. No other word will do. This is a first come first served sales forum.
2
STATE ERA - WE ONLY GO UP TO 1962
If your item is modette/mini-skirt, Jackie-O, Boxy 60s etc. it doesn't belong here. There is a group for later eras.
3
FULLY DESCRIBE
Buyers here will need Measurements, condition, materials, era, fastenings, and to assess if the items will fit them or suit their homes.
4
PHOTOGRAPHS
Please shoot in good light. Show items fully. Photos communicate authenticity. Include fastenings, labels, union tags, linings, damage. Buyers want to see what they are getting. OLL can't support 70s listed as 50s etc.
5
WE DON'T HOST DAMAGED WOUNDED ITEMS
If item needs repair, is shattering, has stains torn lining, fur/feathers shedding, if unironed or needs laundering - it cant be listed here. Try: https://www.facebook.com/groups/woundedbirds/
6
ISO - In Search Of
Please write this before anything else, at the VERY TOP of your call for items. "ISO - Please post with price and photos on OLL, as per usual rules. Do not start to describe without a fixed price. People posting teasers are removed from OLL"
7
PAYMENT BY PAYPAL OR CASH
Unless cash on collection, please use Paypal. It has buyer and seller guarantees to protect both sides of the transaction. Sellers - Produce an invoice. Buyers - Screenshot the listing. Do not send money F&F.
8
NO CROSSPOSTING
Items listed her may not be listed elsewhere at the same time. It doesn't work with the fundamental interested rule if an item is unavailable as it sold elsewhere.
9
NO LINKS
Please do not post links to ebay/etsy or outside selling platforms or pages. If you are using those platforms great, but they are being paid to market your items.
10
NO SELLER FORMAT
We do not use the marketplace seller format on OLL. It opens the door to auctions and disappointment. It doesn't accord with the interested rule.
