### Overview
#### Understanding Our Policies
The following policies, as well as our [Data Policy](https://www.facebook.com/about/privacy/), [Community Standards](https://www.facebook.com/communitystandards/), and [Terms of Service](https://www.facebook.com/legal/terms), apply to your creation and use of additional Facebook profiles.
  

Your main profile is the first profile you make when you create a Facebook account. The name on your main profile should match the name you go by in everyday life. After you create your main profile, you may become eligible to create additional profiles as well. Read more about how we define additional profiles [here](https://www.facebook.com/help/327202558928776). Unlike your main profile, your additional profiles are not required to use the name you go by in everyday life.
  

To become eligible to create additional profiles, you should continue to use Facebook and comply with our policies. You may become ineligible to create additional profiles if you violate our policies or take other actions that threaten the safety or well-being of others. If you repeatedly or egregiously violate our policies while using your additional profiles, one or more of your profiles (including your main profile) may be disabled. Your additional Facebook profiles may be disabled upon a lesser number of violations than the number required to disable your main profile.  

### General Policies for Additional Profiles
The following policies apply specifically to your use of additional profiles:
  1. #### [Impersonation](https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/additional_profiles/impersonation)
Do not impersonate, or falsely represent that you are authorized to speak on behalf of, another person, brand, business or organization. 
  2. #### [Misrepresentation](https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/additional_profiles/misrepresentation)
Do not misrepresent your identity by claiming or implying to be someone you are not. 
  3. #### Usernames
Do not provide your additional profiles with names or usernames that inherently violate our [Community Standards](https://www.facebook.com/communitystandards/) or consist solely of generic terms or locations. 
  4. #### Verification
Do not place a verified checkmark in your profile photo or cover photo, or in any other way falsely represent that your profile has been verified by Facebook.
