# Eligibility requirements for Meta Verified creator subscriptions
Copy link
We’re gradually updating people to [ Meta Accounts](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.meta.com%2Fhelp%2Fmeta-account%2F876751767182450%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUCQkPspZOp9OQlBMpUemBUtVVPaWYdbyUljgmrX4-gA_ZNbObubksnnCKWacNN9hj08aGhdTydjUqpPj9_qaBKOo2vbmp4iaz_VtkZ3LkveUu2dOCvBQ0yo2k_Fuu5djsC2GQL4mkYwsA). You may see either Accounts Center or Meta Account settings as the place to manage your account settings.
This article is about [Meta Verified for creators](https://www.facebook.com/help/565322618581808?helpref=faq_content) on Facebook and Instagram.
Looking for business information? Please visit the [Meta Verified for businesses Help Center](https://facebook.com/business/help/2876154822522394?helpref=faq_content).
Meta Verified for creators is a paid subscription that provides tools to help you build more confidence with new audiences and protect your brand. This subscription comes with benefits like the verified badge, enhanced support, impersonation protection, and more.
Learn about Meta Verified eligibility requirements for creators below.
  

You need to meet the eligibility requirements in order to qualify for Meta Verified.
  

## Subscriber eligibility
To purchase a Meta Verified subscription, you need to meet the following requirements:
  * Be at least 18 years old, or the age of majority in your country of residence.
  * Be located in a country where Meta Verified is available.
  * Have an eligible Instagram or Facebook profile.
  * Have a government-issued photo ID.
  * Not exceed the number of times you can make changes to your profile within a certain time period prior to applying for verification (examples: changes to profile name, profile picture, username, location, or profile type).
  * Agree to the [**_Meta Verified Terms of Service_**](https://www.facebook.com/legal/MV_terms) and follow the [**_Instagram Terms of Use_**](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F581066165581870%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUCQkPspZOp9OQlBMpUemBUtVVPaWYdbyUljgmrX4-gA_ZNbObubksnnCKWacNN9hj08aGhdTydjUqpPj9_qaBKOo2vbmp4iaz_VtkZ3LkveUu2dOCvBQ0yo2k_Fuu5djsC2GQL4mkYwsA), [_**Facebook Terms of Service**_](https://www.facebook.com/legal/terms), and [**_Community Standards_**](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Ftransparency.meta.com%2Fpolicies%2Fcommunity-standards&h=AUCQkPspZOp9OQlBMpUemBUtVVPaWYdbyUljgmrX4-gA_ZNbObubksnnCKWacNN9hj08aGhdTydjUqpPj9_qaBKOo2vbmp4iaz_VtkZ3LkveUu2dOCvBQ0yo2k_Fuu5djsC2GQL4mkYwsA).

  

## Profile eligibility
The following profiles are eligible for Meta Verified:
  * [_**Instagram professional creator profile**_](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F1158274571010880%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUCQkPspZOp9OQlBMpUemBUtVVPaWYdbyUljgmrX4-gA_ZNbObubksnnCKWacNN9hj08aGhdTydjUqpPj9_qaBKOo2vbmp4iaz_VtkZ3LkveUu2dOCvBQ0yo2k_Fuu5djsC2GQL4mkYwsA)
  * [_**Instagram personal profile**_](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F1717693135113805%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUCQkPspZOp9OQlBMpUemBUtVVPaWYdbyUljgmrX4-gA_ZNbObubksnnCKWacNN9hj08aGhdTydjUqpPj9_qaBKOo2vbmp4iaz_VtkZ3LkveUu2dOCvBQ0yo2k_Fuu5djsC2GQL4mkYwsA)
  * [**_Facebook professional creator profile_**](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/251342092572308?helpref=faq_content)
  * [**_Facebook personal profile_**](https://www.facebook.com/help/188157731232424?helpref=faq_content)

  

You can verify up to 2 eligible profiles that are [**_added to the same Meta Account_**](https://www.facebook.com/help/213446087365127?helpref=faq_content) (one Instagram and one Facebook). When you verify 2 profiles, both may need to have similar names and pictures.
  

**Note** : Profile eligibility requirements differ between Instagram and Facebook.
  

**Instagram profile eligibility**
Your selected Instagram profile needs to:
  * Be an Instagram [**_personal_**](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F1717693135113805%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUCQkPspZOp9OQlBMpUemBUtVVPaWYdbyUljgmrX4-gA_ZNbObubksnnCKWacNN9hj08aGhdTydjUqpPj9_qaBKOo2vbmp4iaz_VtkZ3LkveUu2dOCvBQ0yo2k_Fuu5djsC2GQL4mkYwsA) or [**_creator_**](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F1158274571010880%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUCQkPspZOp9OQlBMpUemBUtVVPaWYdbyUljgmrX4-gA_ZNbObubksnnCKWacNN9hj08aGhdTydjUqpPj9_qaBKOo2vbmp4iaz_VtkZ3LkveUu2dOCvBQ0yo2k_Fuu5djsC2GQL4mkYwsA) profile. This profile may need to have a profile picture and name that meets [**_Meta Verified naming standards_**](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F2994759677322122%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUCQkPspZOp9OQlBMpUemBUtVVPaWYdbyUljgmrX4-gA_ZNbObubksnnCKWacNN9hj08aGhdTydjUqpPj9_qaBKOo2vbmp4iaz_VtkZ3LkveUu2dOCvBQ0yo2k_Fuu5djsC2GQL4mkYwsA).
  * Have [**_Advanced Protection_**](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F945221763907783%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUCQkPspZOp9OQlBMpUemBUtVVPaWYdbyUljgmrX4-gA_ZNbObubksnnCKWacNN9hj08aGhdTydjUqpPj9_qaBKOo2vbmp4iaz_VtkZ3LkveUu2dOCvBQ0yo2k_Fuu5djsC2GQL4mkYwsA) turned on.
  * Meet minimum activity requirements.
  * Be located in a country where Meta Verified is available.
  * Follow the [**_Instagram Terms of Use_**](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F581066165581870%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUCQkPspZOp9OQlBMpUemBUtVVPaWYdbyUljgmrX4-gA_ZNbObubksnnCKWacNN9hj08aGhdTydjUqpPj9_qaBKOo2vbmp4iaz_VtkZ3LkveUu2dOCvBQ0yo2k_Fuu5djsC2GQL4mkYwsA) and [**_Community Standards_**](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Ftransparency.meta.com%2Fpolicies%2Fcommunity-standards%2F&h=AUCQkPspZOp9OQlBMpUemBUtVVPaWYdbyUljgmrX4-gA_ZNbObubksnnCKWacNN9hj08aGhdTydjUqpPj9_qaBKOo2vbmp4iaz_VtkZ3LkveUu2dOCvBQ0yo2k_Fuu5djsC2GQL4mkYwsA).

  

**Facebook profile eligibility**
Your selected Facebook profile needs to:
  * Be a Facebook [**_personal_**](https://www.facebook.com/help/188157731232424?helpref=faq_content) or [**_creator_**](https://www.facebook.com/help/251342092572308?helpref=faq_content) profile. This profile may need to have a profile picture and name that meets [**_Meta Verified naming standards_**](https://www.facebook.com/help/1502500157096706?helpref=faq_content).
  * Have [**_Advanced Protection_**](https://www.facebook.com/help/1052552578831700?helpref=faq_content) turned on.
  * Meet minimum activity requirements.
  * Be located in a country where Meta Verified is available.
  * Follow the [_**Facebook Terms of Service**_](https://www.facebook.com/legal/terms) and [**_Community Standards_**](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Ftransparency.meta.com%2Fpolicies%2Fcommunity-standards&h=AUCQkPspZOp9OQlBMpUemBUtVVPaWYdbyUljgmrX4-gA_ZNbObubksnnCKWacNN9hj08aGhdTydjUqpPj9_qaBKOo2vbmp4iaz_VtkZ3LkveUu2dOCvBQ0yo2k_Fuu5djsC2GQL4mkYwsA).

  

**Note** : Eligibility requirements are subject to change as we improve the product experience for subscribers.
