# Things that can't be listed for sale on Facebook Marketplace
Copy link
Products listed on Facebook Marketplace must comply with [ Meta's Commerce Policies](https://www.facebook.com/policies/commerce) and the [ Facebook Community Standards](https://www.facebook.com/policies/community-standards). Buyers and sellers are also responsible for complying with all applicable laws and regulations. 
## Examples:
  * **[Counterfeits](https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/commerce/third_party_infringement)** : Anything that violates intellectual property rights (examples: counterfeits, knockoffs, replicas of branded goods, pirated copies of a movie) isn’t allowed on Marketplace.
  * **Unauthorized or pirated copies of copyrighted works** : This includes videos, movies, TV shows and broadcasts, video games, CDs or other musical works, books, etc.
  * [**Services**](https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/commerce/services): Offering services (example: house cleaning) is only allowed on [Jobs on Marketplace](https://www.facebook.com/help/2203257093390031?helpref=faq_content). 
  * [**Animals**](https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/commerce/animals): Selling animals or animal products isn't allowed on Marketplace. This includes posting about animals for adoption,[ except by approved pet adoption matching services](https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/commerce/pet_adoption_matching_services).
  * [**Healthcare products**](https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/commerce/medical_and_healthcare_products): Medical and healthcare-related products aren't allowed on Marketplace (example: thermometers, first-aid kits). 
  * [**Recalled products**](https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/commerce/recalled_products): If you're unsure whether the item you're buying or selling has been recalled, look on the website of the item's manufacturer or relevant government entity. For example, the [US Consumer Product Safety Commission](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.cpsc.gov%2FRecalls&h=AUAtlG7puPAsm8A8vD5lys3ftHo5Wooskb-F0N6img4HWO7VuBnH_F6l9Ux_UJpOA7F30jdPmDFVTL6JSAlD4cZSrrNalerBJWpkuk-ZfPZ-ixQy97JGyZtuReZ6D7z2An5Idy7C2lKxSA) or the [EU Rapid Alert System for Dangerous Non-food Products](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fec.europa.eu%2Fsafety-gate-alerts%2Fscreen%2FwebReport&h=AUAtlG7puPAsm8A8vD5lys3ftHo5Wooskb-F0N6img4HWO7VuBnH_F6l9Ux_UJpOA7F30jdPmDFVTL6JSAlD4cZSrrNalerBJWpkuk-ZfPZ-ixQy97JGyZtuReZ6D7z2An5Idy7C2lKxSA).


Learn what options you might have if:
  * Your Marketplace [listing was rejected](https://www.facebook.com/help/2193854224216494?helpref=faq_content).
  * Your [Marketplace access](https://www.facebook.com/help/972392066266648/?helpref=faq_content) is at risk, limited or suspended.


Learn how to [report a listing on Marketplace](https://www.facebook.com/help/196126404168290?helpref=faq_content).
Note: The information above summarizes parts of the [Commerce Policies ](https://www.facebook.com/help/policies/commerce) for convenience, but you should consult the policies themselves for comprehensive and up-to-date information.
