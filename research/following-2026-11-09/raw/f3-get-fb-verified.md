# About the Facebook Verified badge
Copy link
This feature is not currently available to all users.
This article is about the free Verified badge on Facebook. Learn more about [Meta Verified](https://www.facebook.com/help/1206202490291214?helpref=faq_content).
The gray Verified badge on Facebook shows there's a real person behind a profile which can provide peace of mind in your interactions across Facebook. Once you complete the verification process, you'll get a gray Verified badge ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-verified-badge&density=1) on your profile, indicating your profile was verified based on your activity across Facebook and other information you provided.
Your Verified badge can appear next to your name across Facebook, including: 
  * Your profile
  * Marketplace
  * Groups
  * Jobs
  * Facebook Dating


**Note:** The Verified badge does not mean that Facebook endorses the user or guarantees their trustworthiness. All users are still subject to Meta’s policies, like [Community Standards](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Ftransparency.meta.com%2Fpolicies%2Fcommunity-standards%2F&h=AUAt1MsBBGW2afyxas_nKPHd5INOmgrJfzFrAvJn1ANWIHD6KmAuYAWxjMYv5a6uGu7XqRgH90VcUNd3rp_iHCTNKted_P4h8riHLXJEwE00QpWjAqjKRaPDg6L9uc5icLa_rwKv1H-6AA) and [Commerce Policies](https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/commerce/).
## How to get verified on Facebook
To start the verification process using the Facebook app for Android or iPhone: 
  1. Tap ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-hamburgerlight-android&density=1)**Menu** in the top left of Facebook. 
  2. Tap **Settings and privacy** , then tap **Settings**.
  3. Below **Tools and resources** , tap **Verified info and badge**.
  4. Tap **Get verified**.


If you don’t see **Verified info and badge** , this setting may not be available to you yet.
## How verification works
Verification is based on your activity on Facebook. Once you start the verification process, we may also ask you to submit a video selfie or a [government-issued ID](https://www.facebook.com/help/155050237914643?helpref=faq_content). This usually takes less than a minute and is a secure method for preventing online fraud. When you send us your selfie and/or ID, it will be stored securely. It won't be visible on your profile to friends or to other people on Facebook. We'll use your selfie and/or ID to help verify your profile. It will also be used to prevent abuse such as scams, phishing and fraud and to keep you and our community safe. To help ensure that your selfie and/or ID is genuine, we use both manual review and automated systems. We may also partner with trusted service providers to check your selfie and/or ID is authentic. This helps detect and prevent risks, such as impersonation. Learn more about [how Meta uses face recognition technology to verify you](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.meta.com%2Fen-gb%2Fhelp%2Fpolicies%2F3701579170107659%2F%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUAt1MsBBGW2afyxas_nKPHd5INOmgrJfzFrAvJn1ANWIHD6KmAuYAWxjMYv5a6uGu7XqRgH90VcUNd3rp_iHCTNKted_P4h8riHLXJEwE00QpWjAqjKRaPDg6L9uc5icLa_rwKv1H-6AA).
**Note:** Verification is not currently available to all users. Your Verified badge may not be visible to someone if they are not yet eligible for verification.
## How to show or hide your Verified badge on your profile
After you’ve been verified, your Verified badge will appear on your profile by default. You can choose to hide it at any time in your settings.
To show or hide your Verified badge using the Facebook app for Android or iPhone:
  1. Tap ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-hamburgerlight-iphone&density=1)**Menu** in the top left of Facebook.
  2. Scroll down and tap ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-settings-iphone&density=1)**Settings & privacy**, then tap **Settings**.
  3. Under **Tools and resources** , tap **Verified info and badge**.
  4. Tap the toggle next to **Show your Verified badge** to show or not show your badge.


## Why you may not be able to get verified
The Verified badge is not currently available to all users. If you don’t see the option on your profile to get verified, you may not be eligible yet. 
If you try to get verified but are unable to complete the verification process, you may be able to try again. If you’re submitting a video selfie, make sure that you’re in a well-lit area and that your face is clearly visible.
If we are unable to verify you based on your activity or detect that your photos cannot be verified against a selfie, you may be able to verify your profile with an ID.
## Why we may need to reverify you
We may ask you to verify again in some cases — for example, if you change key information on your profile, like your name, date of birth, or photos.
Your Verified badge may be removed if you don’t follow Meta’s policies, like [Community Standards](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Ftransparency.meta.com%2Fpolicies%2Fcommunity-standards%2F&h=AUAt1MsBBGW2afyxas_nKPHd5INOmgrJfzFrAvJn1ANWIHD6KmAuYAWxjMYv5a6uGu7XqRgH90VcUNd3rp_iHCTNKted_P4h8riHLXJEwE00QpWjAqjKRaPDg6L9uc5icLa_rwKv1H-6AA) and [Commerce Policies](https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/commerce/).
## How the Verified badge is different from Meta Verified
A gray Verified badge ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-verified-badge&density=1) is free for eligible users and is a way to show that your profile has been verified on Facebook. [Meta Verified is a paid subscription](https://www.facebook.com/help/1206202490291214?helpref=faq_content) that provides creators and businesses with tools and benefits across Facebook, Instagram and Whatsapp, including a blue verified badge![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-verifiedbadge-iphone&density=1) and impersonation protection for verified users. You may also see some profiles with a blue verified badge that represents our previous verification requirements for notable public figures.
Please also note that a Verified badge is different from [seller badges on Marketplace](https://www.facebook.com/help/1684084458520855?helpref=faq_content) or [badges in groups](https://www.facebook.com/help/1755743691177351?helpref=faq_content).
