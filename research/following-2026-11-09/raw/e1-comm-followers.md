Followers used to be public, not just seller-facing. So if I clicked on your profile I could see everybody you were following and everybody that was following you.
A lot of people abused it, and not just by trying to take sales offline. eBay eventually cut it off entirely.
Back when we had visibility I could see a lot of my followers were competitors (ones that liked to steal my photos ðŸ™„).
When you send newsletters and discounts to followers, you can't pick/choose - it's either all followers or none. So you've gotta assume there will always be some amount of competitors in there, along with legit interested shoppers. Whether you can see the list or not, that's almost always going to be true.
In addition, followers have the option to unsubscribe from your newsletter if they don't want unsolicited communications. In the past sellers were contacting followers about new products and other stuff and buyers were getting annoyed by the SPAM.
