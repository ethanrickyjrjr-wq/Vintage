Meta Verified for creators is a paid subscription that provides tools to help you build more confidence with new audiences and protect your brand. This subscription comes with benefits like the verified badge, enhanced support, impersonation protection, and more.
## Automatic renewal
Meta Verified subscriptions are billed on a recurring basis and renew automatically, unless canceled. You will be charged on the same date as your original purchase, until you cancel.
Learn more about [**_payments for Meta Verified creator subscriptions_**](https://www.facebook.com/help/620951066726781?helpref=faq_content).

## Verification process
When you subscribe to Meta Verified, you’ll follow a verification process and submit an application for review.
You’ll choose a subscription plan, select the profile(s) you want to verify, set up your recurring subscription payment, and confirm your identity.
  

**Step 1. Choose your profile**
You’ll choose the profile(s) you want to verify and set up your recurring subscription payment.
You can verify up to two [**_eligible profiles_**](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F2419286908233223%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUAtJdweU6W9869GbMvXzHwVxv3QKFD26Lhe_PmY9PK_rWxJo2un74h8rtRyTZi6wuhzT_u73jISjjJoy0xeaMsgrKqHUWQSQcsNxNKIahqOO0g1OQ_EeXrrbdOBovWbKSS-JnXXBUlppw) that are [**_added to the same Meta Account_**](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F559653651833249%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUAtJdweU6W9869GbMvXzHwVxv3QKFD26Lhe_PmY9PK_rWxJo2un74h8rtRyTZi6wuhzT_u73jISjjJoy0xeaMsgrKqHUWQSQcsNxNKIahqOO0g1OQ_EeXrrbdOBovWbKSS-JnXXBUlppw).
When you bundle your subscription and verify two profiles instead of one (example: one Instagram profile and one Facebook profile), you may receive a discounted price. Both profiles may need to have similar names and pictures.
**Note** : [**_Advanced Protection_**](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F945221763907783%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUAtJdweU6W9869GbMvXzHwVxv3QKFD26Lhe_PmY9PK_rWxJo2un74h8rtRyTZi6wuhzT_u73jISjjJoy0xeaMsgrKqHUWQSQcsNxNKIahqOO0g1OQ_EeXrrbdOBovWbKSS-JnXXBUlppw) needs to be enabled on the profile(s) you’re verifying. This security feature can’t be disabled once your subscription is active.
  

**Step 2. Confirm your identity**
You’ll confirm your identity.
To confirm your identity, you’ll need to upload photos of a [**_government-issued photo ID_**](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F1666863223752144%3Fhelpref%3Dfaq_content&h=AUAtJdweU6W9869GbMvXzHwVxv3QKFD26Lhe_PmY9PK_rWxJo2un74h8rtRyTZi6wuhzT_u73jISjjJoy0xeaMsgrKqHUWQSQcsNxNKIahqOO0g1OQ_EeXrrbdOBovWbKSS-JnXXBUlppw).
**Note** : If eligible, you may be verified using your activity across Meta products, such as name tenure and profile completeness including name and picture.
  

**Step 3. Get reviewed**
You’ll submit your information for review.
Once you complete the application process and submit your information, it’ll go into review. After review, you’ll get a notification with an update on your verification status. This process can take up to 3 business days.
Learn [**_how to troubleshoot issues when subscribing to Meta Verified_**](https://www.facebook.com/help/1237943377133914?helpref=faq_content).
## After you’re verified
