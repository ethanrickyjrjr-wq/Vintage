## Group rules from the admins
* * *
1
Sale posts only, no discussions
For discussions/showing off please join our sister group : https://www.facebook.com/groups/975922355847265/ Discussions are not for this group. just buying and selling.
2
Pattern descriptions
Pattern listings should include at least one photo, the pattern company and number, whether the item is complete or not, which pieces are missing (if any), and price, and shipping cost or location of seller. If you have listed elsewhere, aka “cross-posted”, it is common courtesy - and required - to say so in the post.
3
Claiming an item
Buyers should comment "interested" to claim an item. This can be pending questions, but this is where the line should begin forming. Other unequivocal statements of purchase intention (i.e., "I'll take it," "sold," "I'll buy it") are also acceptable.
4
Checking completeness
All patterns over $5 either need to be checked, offered to be checked upon request, or immediately refunded if they're incomplete. Patterns that are listed as Factory Folded will be among the exceptions and can be addressed on a case-by-case situation.
5
Sales posts are ONLY for lots or single items
The “Sell Something” tab should ONLY be used for single items or single lots. If you have multiple items to sell at one time, you MUST use an album in a discussion post. If you post more than one item in a 'sell something' post, it will be DELETED. (The reason for this is that multiple items in a “Sell” post cannot be commented on individually, and they have caused massive drama and confusion at various times.)
6
No teasers
NO teaser posts, no "how much interest would there be....." and no "how much is this worth?" posts. If you want to find out people's interest level, then just post it. If you're not sure about pricing, then either watch the page for a while to gauge values for different types of patterns, or just post at a price for which you can afford to sell it.
7
Payment
Paypal is recommended for the protection of buyers and sellers. Sellers, please invoice interested buyers with as much information as possible included on the invoice. Buyers, for your protection do NOT pay as gift/family. Only pay on a correctly written invoice.
8
No flooding
Please do not flood the wall or bump excessively. Sellers may post 5 new listings per hour or bump 3 existing listings per hour. If you have more than 5, you may post them all at once to an album but not individually to the wall.
9
Outside listings
Links to outside sources are not allowed; please do not link to your eBay, Etsy, or other places. If you are running a sale/coupon code and would like to share, please contact an admin.
10
Racially-insensitive items
We do not allow sales of patterns for dolls, household items and any other patterns that propagate racial stereotypes contributing to social inequality. If you have one of these patterns and would like to offer it for free to a verified educator or museum professional for educational use, please contact the admins. We are happy for these items to contribute to education, but we will not allow our group to contribute to the collectible monetary value of these objects.
