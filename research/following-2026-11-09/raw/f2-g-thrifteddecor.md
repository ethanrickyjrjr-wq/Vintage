## Group rules from the admins
* * *
1
FOR THRIFTED HOME GOODS STYLED IN YOUR DECOR ONLY
This group is for sharing how we style our THRIFTED treasures into our home decor only!!! NO GIFTED ITEMS. NO posts of your THRIFTED HAULS. NO CLOTHING. These types of posts will be automatically declined or removed.
2
POSTS MUST SAY WHAT IS THRIFTED IN THE TITLE
Please make sure when you post you state what is thrifted in the post, not just under the picture. Otherwise you risk having your post declined or removed.
3
NO PRICES PLEASE!
This group is to share how we display our thrifted finds, price is not relevant.
4
NO VIDEOS
Please, no posting of videos, these are for administrators only.
5
BE KIND!
If you see a post that isn’t your style just SCROLL BY. No negative, rude or passive/aggressive comments will be tolerated.
6
NO SHARING SOMEONES POST
This shouldn’t be an issue now that the group is private. This group is dedicated to protect peoples privacy.
7
No promotions or spam
NO links promoting any businesses, even if it’s your business, or any other promotions are allowed.
8
No political content
Political views and propaganda are not allowed as they are irrelevant to this group
9
NO QUESTIONS ON IDENTIFYING A THRIFTED ITEM
This group is solely for showing how we’re styling our thrifted items in our home decor only. Questions about how old an item is or the origin of an item are not for this group, thank you
10
NO POSTS ASKING TO “PLEASE SHARE” OR “SHOW ME”
These types of posts are reserved for Administrators only!
