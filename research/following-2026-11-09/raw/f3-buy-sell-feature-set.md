Groups focused on buying, selling or promoting products are subject to our [Commerce Policies](https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/commerce) and may have different features available for admins. 
You must be an adult in order to buy, sell or see listings in a Facebook buy and sell group (example: at least 18 years old in the United States). If you’re not an adult, you can still participate in the group, outside of listings.
Group admins can add or remove the buy and sell feature set, which includes:
  * **Sell something** [post format](https://www.facebook.com/help/144548630973199?helpref=faq_content).
  * Showing the **Buy and sell** tab when people first come to your group.
  * Seller badges: **Super seller** , **Highly rated** and **Very responsive**.


You can also only add the **Sell something** post format if you don’t want the full set of buy and sell features. Learn more about [feature sets and managing features](https://www.facebook.com/help/166599882185672?helpref=faq_content) in a group you admin.
## Add the buy and sell post format or feature set
  1. Go to [facebook.com/groups](https://www.facebook.com/groups) and select your group.
  2. Click **Add features** in the left menu.
  3. Below **Feature sets** , scroll until you find **Buy & sell**, then click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-blueplus-desktop&density=1)**Add set**.


## Remove the buy and sell post format or feature set
  1. Go to [facebook.com/groups](https://www.facebook.com/groups) and select your group.
  2. Click **Group settings** in the left menu.
  3. Below **Added to group** , click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=ofb-pencil-desktop&density=1)**Edit** next to **Added sets** , then click **Buy & sell**.
  4. Click **Remove** , then click **Remove set** to confirm.


## Keep in mind:
  * This feature set is only available in select groups and countries.
  * If you add the Buy & sell feature set, [anonymous posts](https://www.facebook.com/help/372188260706718?helpref=faq_content) won’t be available in your group.
  * You must be an adult in order to buy, sell or see listings in a Facebook buy and sell group (example: at least 18 years old in the United States). If you’re not an adult, you can still participate in the group, outside of listings.


If you have questions about Marketplace and where you can buy and sell items, visit the [Marketplace Help Center.](https://www.facebook.com/help/1889067784738765?helpref=faq_content)
