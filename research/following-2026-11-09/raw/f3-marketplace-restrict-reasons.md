# Who can use Facebook Marketplace
Copy link
Facebook Marketplace is available in many countries for adults with active Facebook accounts, and is available from the Facebook app for Android or iPhone.
To access Facebook Marketplace, you must:
  * Be an adult (example: at least 18 years old in the United States).
  * Live in a country where Facebook Marketplace is available.


If you are an adult and incorrectly blocked from using Marketplace, you can confirm your age on the Facebook app from your mobile phone to regain access. (**Note:** Some regions may have different verification methods available.)
Learn more about [confirming your age on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/help/958848942357089?helpref=faq_content), [acceptable types of IDs for age verification](https://www.facebook.com/help/159096464162185?helpref=faq_content) or [how to verify your age with a selfie.](https://www.facebook.com/help/1386337538619854?helpref=faq_content)

## Restricted access to Facebook Marketplace
We may fully restrict which Facebook accounts can access Marketplace. If you can't access or no longer have access to Marketplace, it may be because you:
  * Aren’t an adult.
  * Have a new or inactive account.
  * Are using an [additional Facebook profile](https://www.facebook.com/help/967154637433480?helpref=faq_content) instead of your main profile. Learn how to [switch between your Facebook profiles](https://www.facebook.com/help/5637701596308695?helpref=faq_content).
  * Went against our [Terms of Service](https://www.facebook.com/terms/), [_Commerce Policies_](https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/commerce/) or other policies.
  * Are not logged into Facebook.


Learn how to [_buy and sell responsibly on Marketplace_](https://www.facebook.com/help/1156544111079919?helpref=faq_content).
