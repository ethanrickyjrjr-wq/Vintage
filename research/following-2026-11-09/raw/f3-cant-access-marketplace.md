Marketplace isn't available to everyone right now. 
We may limit or suspend your Marketplace access if you break our [Commerce Policies](https://www.facebook.com/policies/commerce). When you come close to having your access suspended, we’ll warn you that your access is at risk.
If your listing is rejected, you may have the option to:
  * **Request a review.** If we find that we made a mistake, we’ll restore your listing.
  * **[Remove your warning](https://www.facebook.com/help/972392066266648/?helpref=faq_content).** If this is the first time you’ve broken a rule, and depending on the rule(s) you broke, you may be able to remove the warning that your access is at risk. However, your listing(s) that led to the warning won’t be restored. 


## Check your Marketplace status
  1. Go to your profile.
  2. Click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=comet-blackthreedot-desktop&density=1) and select **Profile status**.
  3. Select **Marketplace** below **Extra features**.


## Remove your warning
If this is the first time you’ve broken a rule, and depending on the rule(s) you broke, you may be able to remove the warning that your access is at risk.
  1. Open the Facebook app on a mobile device.
  2. Go to your profile.
  3. Tap ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=comet-blackthreedot-desktop&density=1) and select **Profile status**.
  4. Select **Marketplace** below **Extra features**.
  5. Select **Fix it now** then **Request review** and follow the instructions.


You’ll be prompted to read about our policies and what they mean to avoid issues on your account later. You’ll also have a chance to share what you think happened (example: “I didn’t know about the rules”) to help us continue to improve our products and policies. However, your listing(s) that led to the warning won’t be restored.
## Other reasons you might not have access to Marketplace
Learn about [other reasons you might not have access to Marketplace](https://www.facebook.com/help/1968285150185577?helpref=faq_content), like if you’re in a country where Marketplace isn’t available or you’re not an adult based on the laws in your country.
