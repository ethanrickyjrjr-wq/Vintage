If you can't join a group as your Facebook Page, it might be because the group admins don't allow [Pages to join](https://www.facebook.com/help/329227454611442?helpref=faq_content) their groups.
## Join a group
  1. Click **Groups** in the left menu of your Feed.
  2. In the search bar at the top, enter the group name.
  3. Select the group and then click **+ Join Group** below the cover photo.


You can join up to 6,000 groups. Once you reach this limit, you'll need to [leave some groups](https://www.facebook.com/help/172944012764072?helpref=faq_content) before you can join new ones.
To join a private group, you need to be approved by an admin, unless the group has [limited membership turned on](https://www.facebook.com/help/617811410580927?helpref=faq_content). Limited membership allows you to automatically join a private group without admin approval and to see and react to all group content, but you won't be able to post, comment, or chat until you are approved by an admin to participate.
Learn more about the [differences between public and private Facebook groups](https://www.facebook.com/help/220336891328465?helpref=faq_content).
## What happens next
You may have to wait for a group admin to approve your request, depending on the group settings. [Hidden groups](https://www.facebook.com/help/494687427966946?helpref=faq_content) you're not a member of won't appear in search results. When you join a group, group members will see that you're a member. Non-members may also see that you're a member, depending on the group's [privacy level](https://www.facebook.com/help/220336891328465?helpref=privacy).
## What to know about Pages joining groups
  * Multiple people may manage that Page. Page managers can see and interact with group posts and members, depending on the settings.
  * Group admins might not allow [Pages to join](https://www.facebook.com/help/329227454611442?helpref=faq_content) their groups.
  * Groups can't invite Pages to join. Pages can only request to join groups.
  * To join a group as a Page after joining as a profile, [change your interaction settings](https://www.facebook.com/help/215443548960829?helpref=faq_content).
