# About Facebook profile types
Copy link
Facebook offers different profile types for different purposes. 
Your main profile is the profile you create when you sign up for a Facebook account. You must have a main profile to create a Page. 
## Professional mode for profiles
[Professional mode](https://www.facebook.com/help/2978174759158711?helpref=faq_content) is a setting you can turn on for your main profile on your Facebook account. You might turn on this setting if you are a creator who wants to use your existing social network to grow a public presence. 
  * When professional mode is on, anyone can follow you and see your public posts. 
  * You get access to professional tools, such as insights and monetization features. 
  * Only you can manage your profile in professional mode, and your real name is still required.


## Additional profiles
**Note:** You are no longer able to create additional Facebook profiles. Existing additional profiles have not been affected. 
Additional Facebook profiles under your Facebook account represent different things about you like your interests or communities you belong to. Some features such as professional mode are not available on Additional Profiles at this time. 
## Page
A Page is a separate profile with access to professional tools. If you are a creator or have a business or organization you want to promote, [creating a Page](https://www.facebook.com/help/104002523024878?helpref=faq_content) is a good way to build your brand, engage with audiences and use professional tools to grow a separate public presence. 
  * Pages have public settings by default, so anyone can follow and see your posts.
  * More than one person can manage a Page. 
  * Pages do not require a real name and are designed for public use.
  * You can have more than one Page.
  * Pages and professional mode both offer professional tools, but only Pages can have multiple managers and are separate from your main profile.
