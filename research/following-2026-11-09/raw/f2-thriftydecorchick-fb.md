# With some patience, you can find gorgeous home decor finds for a fraction of the price!
  

Early last year I wrote about my absolute favorite place to shop. Facebook Marketplace is hands down the **best** spot to find unique home decor for prices that are hard to beat! 
  

I shared a [bunch of FB Marketplace shopping tips](https://www.thriftydecorchick.com/2025/02/my-Facebook-Marketplace-obsession-tips-for-finding-treasures.html) in that post, so check that out if you're new to the experience. (I also shared a few of my best finds at that time.)
  

Since then, I've become a (self-proclaimed) expert at this type of shopping. I've added _so_ many special finds to our home that I absolutely adore. I would take scrolling through the app over a store any day!
  

My furniture finds have been some of my best, but I think the smaller home decor items are my favorites. I'm always trying to keep our family history, places we love (and have visited) and our hobbies in mind when purchasing on Marketplace. 
  

> I want the things in our home to reflect us as a family whenever possible!
