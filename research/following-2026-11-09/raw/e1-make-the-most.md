# Your eBay.  
Your way.
## Make eBay work for you
eBay is a vibrant community where you can find exactly what you want, at a price you’ll love.
Your eBay adventure is as simple as you make it. So, here are a few things you can do to make the most of your shopping experience.
![Make eBay work for you](https://ir.ebaystatic.com/pictures/aw/pics/campaigns/GROWTHO-23387/Spring_Seller_C2C_Portrait_Milennial_Couple_0033_2_mobile.png)
### Register your eBay account
![Register your eBay account image](https://ir.ebaystatic.com/pictures/aw/pics/campaigns/GROWTHO-23387/v2/01_Mobile_Register.gif)
If you’re not registered yet, start by setting up your [eBay account](https://signup.ebay.com/pa/crte "eBay account").
  * Go to the eBay home page and select **Register** in the top left-hand corner. 
  * Enter your name and email address.
  * Choose a secure password and select **Create account**.


You must be registered to get exclusive coupons and rewards, speed through checkout, track past orders, and enjoy many more perks eBay has to offer.
[ Sign up now ](https://signup.ebay.com/pa/crte)
![Register your eBay account image](https://ir.ebaystatic.com/pictures/aw/pics/campaigns/GROWTHO-23387/01_Image_Register.png?v=2)
### Watch an item
![Watch Item image](https://ir.ebaystatic.com/pictures/campaigns/GROWTHO-23387/v2/02_Mobile_Watchlist.gif)
If you’re not quite ready to make a purchase, you can add it to your **watchlist**.
  * Simply select **Add to Watchlist** on the item page to add it to your watchlist. 
  * Access your watchlist via [My eBay](https://www.ebay.com/mye/myebay/WatchList).
  * From here, you’ll be able to receive up-to-date alerts and notifications when the price drops, stocks are low, or when the listing is about to expire.


Also, sellers might send you an offer or discounted price if you’re watching their item.
You can [learn more here](https://pages.ebay.com/watchlist/). 
![Watch Item image](https://ir.ebaystatic.com/pictures/aw/pics/campaigns/GROWTHO-23387/02_Image_Watch-item.png?v=2)
### Save a seller
![Save Seller image](https://ir.ebaystatic.com/pictures/aw/pics/campaigns/GROWTHO-23387/v2/03_Mobile_Save-seller.gif)
If you see a store or seller you like, you can always save them in [My eBay](https://www.ebay.com/myb/SavedSellers). This also comes in useful if you see a seller with a great feedback score displayed on their item or store page. 
  * Simply click the **Save this seller** link from an item or store page. 
  * Once you’ve done that, you’ll start seeing what they're selling in your feed.
  * You can also unsave a seller, just click the same link on the item or store page.


![Save Seller image](https://ir.ebaystatic.com/pictures/aw/pics/campaigns/GROWTHO-23387/03_Image_Save_seller.png?v=2)
### Save a search
![Save Search image](https://ir.ebaystatic.com/pictures/aw/pics/campaigns/GROWTHO-23387/v2/04_Mobile_Save-search.gif)
Save your frequent searches and save yourself valuable shopping time! This works wonders when you’re searching for rare items that aren’t listed often.
  * Click **Save this search** at the top of the search results page to add it to [My eBay](https://www.ebay.com/myb/SavedSearches). 
  * You can then organize your searches—filtering by price, color, or type of listing.
  * We can also alert you when there’s something new added, so you don’t miss out when that hard-to-find item you’ve been looking for gets listed.


You can [learn more here](https://www.ebay.com/help/buying/search-tips/saved-searches?id=4051).
![Save Search image](https://ir.ebaystatic.com/pictures/aw/pics/campaigns/GROWTHO-23387/04_Image_Save-search.png)
### Make an offer
![Make Offer image](https://ir.ebaystatic.com/pictures/aw/pics/campaigns/GROWTHO-23387/v2/05_Mobile_Make-offer.gif)
Making an offer gives you the opportunity to negotiate your way to a great deal. You can also filter your search for “Accepts Best Offer” to find listings where this type of scenario is possible.
  * Click **Make an offer** to enter your chosen amount.
  * Select when you’d like the offer to expire and add a message to the seller (optional).
  * Click **Review offer** and check all the information before submitting.


You can [learn more here](https://www.ebay.com/help/buying/buy-now/making-best-offer?id=4019).
![Make Offer image](https://ir.ebaystatic.com/pictures/aw/pics/campaigns/GROWTHO-23387/05_Image_Make-offer.png?v=2)
### Make a bid
![Make Bid image](https://ir.ebaystatic.com/pictures/aw/pics/campaigns/GROWTHO-23387/v2/06_Mobile_Make-bid.gif)
Bidding on auction-style listings is also a great way to buy for a bargain price.
  * Start by reviewing the starting bid amount, enter your chosen bid amount (must be more than or equal to the starting bid), and click **Submit bid**.
  * Once the auction ends, the highest bidder wins, so you may end up bidding multiple times to secure the item. 
  * You can also use **automatic bidding**. Simply enter the highest price you're willing to pay for an item, and we'll do the rest.


You can [learn more here](https://www.ebay.com/help/buying/bidding/bidding-works?id=4003).
![Make Bid image](https://ir.ebaystatic.com/pictures/aw/pics/campaigns/GROWTHO-23387/06_Image_Make-bid.png?v=2)
### Ask the seller a question
![Ask Seller image](https://ir.ebaystatic.com/pictures/aw/pics/campaigns/GROWTHO-23387/v2/07_Mobile_Ask-seller.gif)
Want to know a little more about an item? More clarity on shipping? Returns, maybe?
  * Click **Contact seller** on the item page.
  * Select a topic, and see if you can find your answer from a variety of pre-filled questions.
  * If you can’t spot the answer you’re looking for, click **Contact the seller** and send them a message.


You can [learn more here](https://www.ebay.com/help/buying/resolving-issues-sellers/contacting-seller?id=4021&st=12&pos=1&query=Contacting%20a%20seller&intent=ask%20question).
![Ask Seller image](https://ir.ebaystatic.com/pictures/aw/pics/campaigns/GROWTHO-23387/07_Image_Ask-seller.png)
