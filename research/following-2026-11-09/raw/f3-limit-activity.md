If you're a group admin, you can temporarily limit how frequently a member can post and/or comment in your group.
## Temporarily limit how frequently a member can post and/or comment
  1. Go to [facebook.com/groups](https://www.facebook.com/groups) and select your group.
  2. Click **Members** at the top. If you don't see Members, click **More**.
  3. Find the person you want to limit and click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-darkgreythreedots-desktop&density=1)**Options** , then select **Limit Activity**.
  4. To limit the number of posts a member can publish per day, click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-toggleoff-desktop&density=1)**Toggle** next to **Limit Posting** , then select how many daily posts to limit them to and for how long.
  5. To limit the number of comments a member can publish per hour, click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-toggleoff-desktop&density=1)**Toggle** next to **Limit Commenting** , then select how many hourly comments to limit them to and for how long.
  6. Click **Next**.
  7. Click **Save**.


## Remove or change the activity limits you’ve placed on a group member
Go to [facebook.com/groups](https://www.facebook.com/groups) and select your group.
  1. Click **Members** at the top. If you don't see Members, click **More**.
  2. Find the member and click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-darkgreythreedots-desktop&density=1)**Options** , then select **Limit Activity**.
  3. To remove limitations, click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-toggleonsettings-desktop&density=1)**Toggle** next to each limitation you want to remove.
  4. To change limitations, click the limitation you want to change and select the new number of daily posts, hourly comments and/or expirations.
  5. Click **Save**.


**Note:** When you set a limit on a group member’s activity, the next time they visit the group they'll see that their ability to post and/or comment in the group has been temporarily turned off.
