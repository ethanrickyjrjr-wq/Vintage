# How additional profiles work in Facebook groups
Copy link
You are no longer able to create additional Facebook profiles. Existing additional profiles have not been affected. 
Each additional profile has its own @username, Feed, friends list, Pages it follows, and groups it participates in. You can switch between profiles [without logging in and out of Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/help/967154637433480?helpref=faq_content).
## Limits on additional profiles in groups
  * Up to two profiles from the same account can join/participate in the same group. For example, someone with two additional profiles has three profiles total (their main profile and two additional profiles), but only two of their profiles can join/participate in the same group.
  * Only one profile can interact with a single group post. For example, if you post in a group, you can’t comment or reply on that post with a different profile.


## Membership/participation approvals for additional profiles
  * In groups where approvals are required ([private groups and any public group that requires participant approval](https://www.facebook.com/help/214260548594688?helpref=faq_content)), each profile has to be approved. For example, if a member of a private group has an additional profile in order to post in the group with a different name, that additional profile will have to request to join/participate. 
  * Additional profiles are considered to have joined Facebook at the same time as the account. For example, if someone joined Facebook five years ago then later created an additional profile and requests to join a group, [Admin Assist](https://www.facebook.com/help/436275657385753?helpref=faq_content) will consider the profile to have joined Facebook five years ago.
  * To see if someone is using an additional profile, go to their profile and look for an @username.


## Effects of suspending, banning and removing a profile  
| Admin/moderator action on a member’s profile  | Effect on that profile  | Effect on any of their profiles NOT in the group (main profile or additional profile)  | Effect on any of their other profiles already in the group (main profile or additional profile)  |  
| --- | --- | --- | --- |  
| [Suspend](https://www.facebook.com/help/387893081608612?helpref=faq_content)  |  **Cannot** participate in the group until the suspension ends  |  **Cannot** see, join or participate in the group until the suspension ends  |  **No effect. Can** still see and participate in the group.  |  
| [Ban](https://www.facebook.com/help/211909018842184?helpref=faq_content)  |  **Cannot** find the group in search, see any of its content, or be invited to the group again   |  **Cannot** find the group in search, see any of its content, or join/participate   |  **No effect. Can** still find the group in search, see its content, and participate in the group. Note that up to two profiles from the same account can join/participate in the same group.  |  
| [Remove](https://www.facebook.com/help/211909018842184?helpref=faq_content)  |  **Can** find the group in search, re-join and then participate  |  **No effect.** Can find the group in search, join and participate. Note that up to two profiles from the same account can join/participate in the same group.   | **No effect**  |  
Admins can also [prevent someone they banned from joining again under a different account](https://www.facebook.com/help/211909018842184?helpref=faq_content).
## Misuse of additional profiles
Keeping Facebook safe is a top priority. We have [policies for additional profiles](https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/additional_profiles), and our [Community Standards](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Ftransparency.meta.com%2Fpolicies%2Fcommunity-standards&h=AUCKc8nflZmFJGN9CRUtM-EVj1kMjNET2MqOxBo7qvxowd8h9BQct7dyIK_q0ZDWqBUOWp_PQ06HVjIlODlgtdu6MH62VtwPdXePgKVQvUkMjDL-1m7Zv5W1Gc7fApONC5yqq6FK-1GPzQ) apply to all profiles. If someone goes against our standards from any of their profiles, it will affect their Facebook account. If someone repeatedly or egregiously violates our policies while using their additional profiles, one or more of their profiles (including their main profile) may be disabled.
## If you’re concerned about additional profiles in a group you manage, consider:
  * [Allowing anonymous posts in your group](https://www.facebook.com/help/372188260706718?helpref=faq_content). People concerned about privacy might choose to post anonymously instead of creating an additional profile
  * Options for [managing membership](https://www.facebook.com/help/214260548594688?helpref=faq_content) (for private groups)
  * Options for [managing posts/participation](https://www.facebook.com/help/131887213640898?helpref=faq_content)
  * Setting up [Admin Assist](https://www.facebook.com/help/436275657385753?helpref=faq_content) to make approvals easier
  * [Tools that can catch spam in your group](https://www.facebook.com/help/938172370267447?helpref=faq_content)
