To help keep our Marketplace community safe, you may be unable to message on Marketplace for one of the following reasons:
  * You may be messaging someone in a country that doesn’t: 
    * Have the same access to Marketplace as you do.
    * Allow messaging between people in different global regions.
  * You may have sent too many messages on Marketplace.
  * Your access to Marketplace may have been removed.


If you used Marketplace in a way that goes against our [Commerce Policies](https://www.facebook.com/policies/commerce) or [Community Standards](https://www.facebook.com/communitystandards/), your access to Marketplace may have been removed.
## Appeal or delete a listing that wasn't approved
Please review our [Commerce Policies](https://www.facebook.com/policies/commerce) to see if the item is allowed on Marketplace. You can [delete the listing](https://www.facebook.com/help/514314075439323?helpref=faq_content) if it goes against the [Commerce Policies](https://www.facebook.com/policies/commerce).
If you think we made a mistake, please [fill out this form](https://www.facebook.com/help/contact/953354718105045) to ask us to take another look. Make sure to click or tap the box next to the item you want to appeal.
To see if your appeal was approved, please check your listing again in about 24 hours.
Learn more about our [Commerce Policies](https://www.facebook.com/policies/commerce).
