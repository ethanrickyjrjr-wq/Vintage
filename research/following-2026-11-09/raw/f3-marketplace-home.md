# 
Marketplace
Copy link
You can use Marketplace to [buy](https://www.facebook.com/help/1723922281209915?helpref=about_content) and [sell](https://www.facebook.com/help/561376580709359?helpref=about_content) items with people in your community on Facebook.
Learn more about:
  * [Buying and selling responsibly](https://www.facebook.com/help/1156544111079919?helpref=about_content) on Marketplace.
  * [Accessing Marketplace](https://www.facebook.com/help/972392066266648?helpref=about_content) on Facebook.
  * [Finding things to buy](https://www.facebook.com/help/222424444801203?helpref=about_content) on Marketplace by searching for a specific item or browsing categories.
  * [Selling locally](https://www.facebook.com/help/153832041692242?helpref=about_content) or [selling with shipping](https://www.facebook.com/help/773379109714742/?helpref=about_content) on Marketplace.
  * [How ratings work](https://www.facebook.com/help/915385548593204?helpref=about_content) on Marketplace.
  * [Tips for shopping safely](https://www.facebook.com/help/236079651241697?helpref=about_content) and [ avoiding scams](https://www.facebook.com/help/2374002556073992?helpref=about_content) on Facebook.


Keep in mind that items on Marketplace must follow our [Commerce Policies](https://www.facebook.com/policies/commerce).
