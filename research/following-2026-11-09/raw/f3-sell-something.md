When you sell something on Marketplace, you create a public listing that can be seen by anyone on Marketplace and in Feed, search and other places on or off Facebook. All listings on Marketplace and buy and sell groups must follow our [Commerce Policies](https://www.facebook.com/policies/commerce).
If you're meeting someone in person, we recommend arranging your meeting in a public, well-lit area or a police station. Let others know where you're headed. You can find a [local law enforcement agency](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.usacops.com%2F%3Ffbclid%3DIwAR0MishtMsNL9vKDMR3sdRP_O3xkYbgCheUA0bAQu9UwwlEQpk8ku4VQNqU&h=AUCZrnVuGNvBYR72NXEGP3eTmfFDRrwK3YJqjll9o7qrXKzPyeQW8LJlanLlgoRO5-sAseEDbEFbvtHDqCmbXJbS8NsCc4uTLRPLqkAlmLOVz7QTMm9Bu_GcaOpPT1iDA7SBmlvHE5HIgA) near you in the United States from the USACOPS directory. Read our tips for [buying and selling responsibly](https://www.facebook.com/help/1156544111079919?helpref=faq_content) on Marketplace and [meeting someone from Marketplace in person](https://www.facebook.com/help/2329750133711372?helpref=faq_content).
## Sell an item on Marketplace
  1. From your Feed, click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=che_icons&name=comet-marketplace-desktop&density=1)**Marketplace** in the left menu.
  2. Click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-blueadd-comet&density=1)**Create new listing** , then click **Item for sale**.
  3. Click **Add photos** to upload a photo of your item from your computer.  Or, click **Add Video** to upload a video of your item from your computer. We may select a cover photo for your listing based on photo/video quality and listing accuracy. 
**Note** : We may enhance your listing photos and videos to improve their quality. 
  4. Enter the info about your item. To mark an item as **Free** , you can enter 0 as the price.
  5. Click **Next**. If **Next** is grayed out, make sure you've entered the information that's required.
  6. Click **Publish** to post your Marketplace listing.


Learn how to [edit your listing](https://www.facebook.com/help/514314075439323?helpref=faq_content) or [mark an item as sold](https://www.facebook.com/help/1680504982210398?helpref=faq_content).
