You must be at least 13 years old to create a Facebook profile.
## Create a Facebook account
  1. Go to [facebook.com](https://www.facebook.com/) and click **Create new account**.
  2. Enter your name, email or mobile phone number, password, date of birth and gender.
  3. Click [**Sign up**](https://www.facebook.com/r.php).
  4. To finish creating your account, you need to [confirm your email or mobile phone number](https://www.facebook.com/help/223900927622502?helpref=faq_content).


## Troubleshoot login issues
  * If you're having trouble with your password, learn [how to reset your password](https://www.facebook.com/help/213395615347144?helpref=faq_content).
  * If you still can't log in, learn about [ ways you can recover your account](https://www.facebook.com/help/105487009541643?helpref=faq_content).
