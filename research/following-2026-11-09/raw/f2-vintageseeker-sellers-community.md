_Waitlist now open for Vintage Sellers Community, powered by The Vintage Seeker, a networking and learning group geared towards vintage and antiques vendors_
‍
_Ed. note: As of October 2025, the Vintage Sellers Community has transitioned to our_[ _Champion-level membership_](https://www.thevintageseeker.ca/support) _to The Vintage Seeker._
‍
Big news here at The Vintage Seeker! If you’ve noticed I’ve been a bit quiet in these parts lately, it’s with good reason. 
‍
Over the past several months, I’ve been cooking up something major, and I’m so excited to give you a sneak peek of what’s coming here over the next month. 
‍
**We’re launching a brand-new online community for sellers!**
‍
The Vintage Sellers Community is a private, members-only group for vendors of vintage, antiques, thrifted and resale goods. 
‍
This group will allow our selling community to:
‍
  * Connect and collaborate away from customers  
‍
  * Share resources, tips and best practices  
‍
  * Gain market insights and business advice from experts on a variety of topics including business  
operations, product knowledge, sales and merchandising, and more through webinars and events  
‍
  * Deepen industry conversations in a supportive and inclusive environment  
‍
  * Join a group committed to advancing the vintage and resale industry


‍
_Continued below_
‍
# Interested in promotional opportunities for your shop?
[Learn more](https://www.thevintageseeker.ca/support)
‍
_Continued from above_
‍
It’ll all be happening on an easy-to-use online platform that’s archivable, searchable and capable of housing all of our discussions, information and event recordings in one place. 
‍
For a monthly or annual fee, you can join the Vintage Sellers Community and have access to all of this and more. The community launches in October and we’re looking for our founding members who will help shape the tone of the community going forward. If this is something that **may** interest you, please[ sign up to the waitlist](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeH28wt2Uy8GxBjk2UN0SAeOO5mMYL_PSSoE3LICyVfwxwXIQ/viewform) to be notified when we go live. 
‍
Nothing like this has been created for this industry in Canada as far as I know, so together we’ll be building something cool and fresh and sustainable. What a thrill!
‍
It’s my belief that coming together as a community, sharing information and advice, and providing access to resources that will support your businesses will only help the industry grow. 
‍
Together, the secondhand goods industry is stronger. It’s my hope that we can make some big impacts as a group down the road. 
‍
I can’t wait to see you in the community!
‍
More details coming soon but for now, you can join the waitlist. _[Ed. note Oct 22: The community is now live!_[_Join here._](https://www.vintagesellerscommunity.ca/)_]_
