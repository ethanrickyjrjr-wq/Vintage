## Follow a profile
  1. Click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=comet-search-desktop&density=1)**Search** in the top left of any page on Facebook.
  2. Enter the name of the person and choose from the results.
  3. Go to the profile you want to follow.
  4. Click **Follow**.


## Follow a Page
  1. Click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=comet-search-desktop&density=1)**Search** in the top left of any page on Facebook.
  2. Enter the name of the Page and choose from the results.
  3. Go to the Page you want to follow.
  4. Click **Follow**.


Remember, the people and Pages you are following can see that you’re following them.
Learn how to [unfollow a profile or Page](https://www.facebook.com/help/190078864497547?helpref=faq_content).
When you follow a profile or Page, you may see updates from that person or Page in your Feed. If you're friends with someone, you automatically follow them. If you send someone a friend request who allows everybody to follow them, you'll automatically follow their posts.
You can make the posts of people and Pages you follow appear at the top of your Feed with[ Favorites](https://www.facebook.com/help/1634545223376778/?helpref=faq_content). Or, to temporarily unfollow a person, Page or group, you can [snooze them](https://www.facebook.com/help/538433456491590?helpref=faq_content).
**See who you're following**
You can see the people and Pages you're following by clicking **Friends** below your cover photo, then hovering over **More** in the Friends section and scrolling down to select **Following**. If you don't see **Following** , it means you're not following anyone yet. 
**Follow someone on Marketplace**
You can also learn how to [follow someone on Marketplace](https://www.facebook.com/help/265832487508648?helpref=faq_content).
