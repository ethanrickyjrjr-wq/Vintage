## Group rules from the admins
* * *
1
Kindness/Expectation of Behavior/Member Join Req
-Treat everyone with civility, kindness and respect. -Zero Tolerance for Bullying, Hate Speech, & Shaming - will result in immediate removal from the group. -Report Issues Directly to Admins via DM rather than public shaming. -Do Not Block Admins - will result in removal from the group. Reasons we will reject your join request: Locked profile Limited profile; no recent posts, really low friend count, low activity No profile picture Message us for a review if you are rejected.
2
Eras and Items
-Keep it vintage - 1999 or older -No Counterfeit, drug use related, illegal items or materials -No Culturally offensive items -No recent politics -Designer handbags need authentication proof and misspell names -Repro, modern, upcycled and handmade from new OR vintage materials can do great in our sister group Quirky Groovy 90s/Modern
3
Buyer Rules and Etiquette
-Buyer MUST use an approved claim word: Me/ Mine / Claim / Sold / Interested etc. -DM your payment info within 2 hours of claim, after 2 hours, goes to NIL -Pay invoice within 12 hours -Claim word with question holds the item for 2 hours AFTER question has been answered -Claims MUST be made in post - NO DM SALES -DO NOT - delete claims, pass often, ghost, or offer more for a claimed item
4
Payments
-PayPal or Venmo G&S ONLY -Quirky District Link is okay and trumps comment claims -Protection fees MUST be included in price -Sellers who charge tax MUST state in post -Local pickup is not recommended for safety - proceed with caution -Requiring unprotected payment method results in ban from QGV -QGV is a private group - we do not use FB sales platform -QGV ModMins are NOT responsible for any transactions. -DM admin with issues
5
Seller Rules and Etiquette
-Be 100% honest / transparent -Must state if Crossposted -Invoice in a timely manner unless communicated -No purge presales -No mention of hardships / donations -Ship w/in 5 business days of payment -NO DM sales -No interest gauging -Sales MUST be posted regardless of interest -Use your own photos/graphics -Delete sold items ONLY AFTER DELIVERED -Buyers are allowed to pass at any time
6
Sales Post Format
-Posts must include price, condition, description, measurements, and payment method for all items. -Measurements NOT required for items below $20 -Single items, lots, purges, auctions, shop with me allowed -Purges→ 2 hrs max, mixed era, and pre 1999. MUST contain start time. -Auctions → 6, 12, 24, or 48 hrs from set start time, bid increments must be stated and top bid must be accepted. -NO DOND -No minimum purchase requirement
7
BUMP Rules
-Post items ONCE -Okay to delete/repost item after 3 days and purges after 7 days after original post -Do not repost constantly -Do not manipulate / disrupt the algorithm in any way
8
Absolutely NO:
-ISO: Post in Quirky ISO Group -DOND -Outside links - once items is claimed, you can provide buyer with link to purchase on approved platforms -Linking to other posts/sales within Quirky is allowed -Quirky District Links are okay with all required information -Links to other groups are strictly prohibited -Interest gauging posts -Advertising -ID posts / questions → post to Quirky Bananas
9
Subscription to Sell
Seller Subscription Required. Contact Misty Felts, Sarah Ellison, Birdie Birdie, or Leann Baines to get set up before posting items for sale. Threads posted by admins and mods are free and can be utilized by everyone. Quirky ISO, Quirky Dill/Bananas and Quirky Goose are all free groups to post in.
