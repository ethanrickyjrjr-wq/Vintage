Admin Assist can help you automatically manage posts, comments, membership and more in your group based on criteria that you set.
## Here are some examples of how you can use Admin Assist
  * Automatically approve membership or participant requests from people who answered the [membership or participation questions](https://www.facebook.com/help/131887213640898?helpref=hca).
  * [Reduce spam](https://www.facebook.com/help/938172370267447?helpref=hca) by selecting preset criteria that automatically decline posts and comments that are likely to be spam.
  * Automatically [manage posts that contain false information](https://www.facebook.com/help/323862556429900?helpref=faq_content) as determined by [independent fact-checkers](https://www.facebook.com/help/1952307158131536?helpref=faq_content).
  * Manage conflict by automatically removing posts and messages that are reported a certain number of times, or turning off comments on posts that have been reported or may be contentious.
  * Automatically decline posts and comments that link to a specific site. You can also automatically notify the author why the post was declined and/or block them.
  * Set up [recurring posts](https://www.facebook.com/help/124331564818285?helpref=faq_content) or automatically-created [welcome posts that tag new members](https://www.facebook.com/help/2063362980550205?helpref=faq_content).
  * Automatically approve [pending posts](https://www.facebook.com/help/131887213640898?helpref=faq_content) if they pass your specific criteria and our systems are very confident about approving them based on the content of the post, the author, your past decisions about similar posts and more. 


## Set up Admin Assist
  1. Go to [facebook.com/groups](https://www.facebook.com/groups) and select your group.
  2. Click **Admin Assist** in the left menu.
  3. Below **Add or edit criteria** , click **Add** next to each automatic action you want Admin Assist to take.
  4. Choose a preset to customize or create your own criteria and follow the prompts.
  5. To turn on or off **automated moderation** , where Admin Assist will automatically approve [pending posts](https://www.facebook.com/help/131887213640898?helpref=faq_content) and/or [participant requests for public groups](https://www.facebook.com/help/131887213640898?helpref=faq_content) if they pass your specific criteria and our systems are very confident about approving them: Click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-settingsgearblack-weblite&density=1)**Settings** at the top of Admin Assist, then click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-toggleoff-desktop&density=1)**Toggle** next to **Pending posts** and/or **Participant requests** (for public groups). 


## Edit criteria
  1. Go to [facebook.com/groups](https://www.facebook.com/groups) and select your group.
  2. Click **Admin Assist** in the left menu.
  3. Below **Add or edit criteria** , click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=analytics-chartseditpencil-desktop&density=1)**Edit** next to the criteria, then click **Edit**.
  4. Edit the criteria, then click **Save criteria**.


## Delete criteria
  1. Go to [facebook.com/groups](https://www.facebook.com/groups) and select your group.
  2. Click **Admin Assist** in the left menu.
  3. Below **Add or edit criteria** , click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=analytics-chartseditpencil-desktop&density=1)**Edit** next to the criteria, then click **Delete**. Click **Delete** again to confirm.


## Undo an action completed by Admin Assist
  1. Go to [facebook.com/groups](https://www.facebook.com/groups) and select your group.
  2. Click **Admin Assist** in the left menu.
  3. Click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=novi-clock&density=1)**Activity log** in the upper right, then click **Undo** next to the action. Click **Undo** again to confirm.


## Learn how to use Admin Assist to automatically
  * Manage [posts and comments](https://www.facebook.com/help/131887213640898?helpref=faq_content) and [people](https://www.facebook.com/help/214260548594688?helpref=faq_content).
  * Manage [posts that independent fact-checkers have determined contain false information](https://www.facebook.com/help/323862556429900?helpref=faq_content).
  * [Reduce spam](https://www.facebook.com/help/938172370267447?helpref=faq_content) by selecting preset criteria that automatically decline posts and comments that are likely to be spam.
  * [Suspend someone](https://www.facebook.com/help/387893081608612?helpref=faq_content) if they’ve had multiple posts or comments removed.
  * Set up [recurring posts](https://www.facebook.com/help/124331564818285?helpref=faq_content) or automatically-created [welcome posts that tag new members](https://www.facebook.com/help/2063362980550205?helpref=faq_content).


## Note
  * Admin Assist will automatically decline an incoming post if any criteria is met.
  * Admin Assist will only automatically approve a membership or participant request if all criteria are met.
  * If you’re a public group admin, learn how to use [participant approval](https://www.facebook.com/help/131887213640898?helpref=faq_content) to review and approve people before they can post or comment for the first time.
  * If **automated moderation** is available and turned on in your group (see above): 
    * Automated moderation works with other criteria you set in Admin Assist to automatically approve certain posts and/or [participant requests for public groups](https://www.facebook.com/help/131887213640898?helpref=faq_content).
    * Admin Assist will only make a decision for you when it’s very confident. Our systems look at the person’s history, signals about potential risk or spam, specific content, past moderation decisions and more. If Admin Assist isn’t confident enough, the request will still appear in your [pending approvals](https://www.facebook.com/help/131887213640898?helpref=faq_content) queue.
    * You can review and undo any decisions you don’t agree with in the [activity log](https://www.facebook.com/help/151703058802391?helpref=faq_content), and Admin Assist will learn from your actions.
    * If you don’t see this feature, it could be because it’s not available in your group or because [post approval](https://www.facebook.com/help/131887213640898?helpref=faq_content) and/or [participant approval (for public groups)](https://www.facebook.com/help/131887213640898?helpref=faq_content) isn’t turned on.
