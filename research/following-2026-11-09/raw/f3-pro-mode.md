Pro mode gives you access to professional tools and insights that can help you build your professional presence from your Facebook profile. If you're eligible, you can [access monetization products](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/321041698514182?helpref=faq_content) to generate income from your content.
When you turn on pro mode, these tools are added to your existing Facebook profile experience. You don’t create a new profile. You can build your professional presence and maintain your personal friends and family experience from one place on Facebook.
You can turn pro mode on or off at any time. [Learn more about pro mode](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/2680340558863560?helpref=faq_content). 
## When you turn on pro mode
Things that stay the same:
  * Your current friends and profile content won’t change.


Changes when you turn on pro mode:
  * You'll be able to view content and audience insights to track your progress.
  * You'll gain access to enhanced safety features such as [Moderation Assist](https://www.facebook.com/help/1753036688579904?helpref=faq_content).
  * Your content will become eligible for discovery opportunities on Facebook.
  * Your **Who can follow me preference** will be set to Public, allowing fans to connect with you and see more of your content. You can change this in your settings.
  * If you’re eligible, you’ll be able to access monetization products that help you generate income from your content.


## Turn on pro mode
  1. Click your profile photo in the top right corner of Facebook, then click your name.
  2. Click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=comet-threedotsgreyrounded-desktop&density=1)**Options** below your cover photo.
  3. Click **Turn on pro mode**.
  4. Click **Turn on**.


## When you turn off pro mode
Some things stay the same:
  * Your current friends and profile content won’t change.
  * Your **Who can follow me preference** will change to **Friends**.
  * You will lose any followers who are not your friends. You can allow public followers again by turning on pro mode.


Some things change:
  * Your existing content or audience insights won’t be available.
  * You’ll lose access to enhanced safety features such as [Moderation Assist](https://www.facebook.com/help/1753036688579904?helpref=faq_content).
  * You’ll no longer be eligible for discovery opportunities on Facebook.
  * If you’re eligible for monetization, you’ll receive pending payouts or bonuses, but future bonuses and payouts will be paused.
  * If you have [content protection](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1024655109288450?helpref=faq_content) turned on, you lose the ability to protect your original content. Protection for all of your content will be removed, including any protected content that currently has an ownership issue.


## Turn off pro mode
  1. Click your profile photo in the top right corner of Facebook, then click your name.
  2. Click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=comet-threedotsgreyrounded-desktop&density=1)**Options** below your cover photo.
  3. Click **Turn off pro mode**.
  4. Click **Continue** , then click **Turn off**.
