# eBay Open 2026 On The Road: Smaller In Person Events, No Live Virtual Access

eBay is significantly scaling back annual seller conference as eBay Open goes on the road for 2026 with multiple smaller in person events across the country but no live virtual access, leaving many sellers with no option but a delayed recorded replay.
[Open went virtual during the pandemic](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/tag/ebay-open/), only returning to in person last year as part of eBay's big 30th anniversary celebration - but in those past years the virtual option was at least still offered "live" for the broader seller community to tune into during the event.
[ eBay Open 2025 Day One: New Features, Tools & Seller Protections eBay Open 2025 seller conference kicked off with keynote presentation & announcements about new features, tools & seller protections. ![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/ed/30/ed30d72b-9e06-4849-b9ff-511f2907ce30/content/images/icon/Value-Added-Resource-copy--3--2977.png)Value Added ResourceLiz Morton ![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/ed/30/ed30d72b-9e06-4849-b9ff-511f2907ce30/content/images/thumbnail/eBayopen2025-day1-7.jpg)](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/ebay-open-2025-day-one-recap/)
But for 2026, eBay has announced rather than one big event, they'll be holding four smaller "on the road" events with "limited spots" spaced out over the course of the year, with virtual programming only available as a replay at the end.
### [eBay Open hits the road!](https://community.ebay.com/t5/Announcements/eBay-Open-hits-the-road/ba-p/35538349)
> We’re thrilled to share that our biggest seller event is hitting the road this year for eBay Open: On the Road!  
>   
> 
> This one-day, in-person experience is built to deliver practical tools, clear guidance, and real conversations about what’s next for your eBay business. Start the day with a mainstage presentation and brunch before diving into hands-on workshops, networking, and more.  
>   
> 
> Throughout the day, you can:
>   * Book a one-on-one Seller Clinic with eBay Marketplace Advisors to ask questions and get personalized account support
>   * Learn about the power of eBay Live and the opportunities it’s creating for sellers
>   * Get hands-on guidance from product and policy experts
>   * Build real connections with other sellers and raise a glass together at happy hour

> Join us at one of our four stops—each city will feature the same experience, so please only register for one location:
>   * San Diego, CA – May 15
>   * Chicago, IL – June 25
>   * Dallas, TX – September
>   * South Florida – October  
>   
> 
> 

> eBay Open: On the Road is intimate, practical, and designed to help you grow—today and into the future. Spots will be limited at each location.  
>   
> 
> Registration will open roughly two months prior to each event date, so be sure to follow us @eBayforSellers on Instagram for news on each city’s event.
eBay says the same information will be presented at each stop, so sellers should only pick one city to attend, and for those who cannot make it to one of the in person events, a recorded replay will be provided as part of the Winter Seller Check In on October 8, 2026.
The agenda for the in person events has also been significantly scaled back from past eBay Open conferences, and unsurprisingly it looks like [AI](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/tag/ai/) and [eBay Live](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/tag/ebay-live/) will be the main topics of discussion.

Tickets for the events will be $25 and registration is currently open for the first one in San Diego:

RingCentral Events is your source for engaging events and experiences. ![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/ed/30/ed30d72b-9e06-4849-b9ff-511f2907ce30/content/images/icon/favicon-566.ico)San Diego ![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/ed/30/ed30d72b-9e06-4849-b9ff-511f2907ce30/content/images/thumbnail/497ae1ee-bde9-4c0d-8a37-e108ac928ca7.jpg)](https://events.ringcentral.com/events/ebay-open-on-the-road/registration)
Sellers were [absolutely ecstatic last year when eBay announced a return to a large in person event in Las Vegas](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/ebay-open-2025-august/), so this year's stripped back Open will likely be a let down for many.
The move away from engaging with the broader seller community is disappointing but not surprising as eBay has also recently [shut down their most popular social support channels](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/ebay-drops-social-support-us/), official participation in the eBay community forums continues to wane, and eBay's latest strategy pivot is clearly aimed at consumer to consumer (C2C) and Live selling even at the risk of alienating small, medium, and large business sellers from the platform.
[ eBay’s C2C & Live Pivot Gains Steam - But Does It Risk Alienating Business Sellers? eBay’s shift to C2C and live selling is driving growth—but could it risk alienating the business sellers who power its marketplace? ![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/ed/30/ed30d72b-9e06-4849-b9ff-511f2907ce30/content/images/icon/Value-Added-Resource-copy--3--2978.png)Value Added ResourceLiz Morton ![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/ed/30/ed30d72b-9e06-4849-b9ff-511f2907ce30/content/images/thumbnail/ebayseller32126-1.jpg)](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/ebays-c2c-live-pivot-gains-steam/)
Unfortunately, the writing was on the wall when [Director Seller Community and Engagement, Rebecca Michals,](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/ebay-expands-seller-led-events/) departed the company last month amidst broader layoffs and [Xiaodi Zhang, who returned to eBay in 2022 as VP Seller Experience](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/ebay-vp-seller-experience-xiaodi-zhang/) - a role that "spanned across all aspects of a seller's journey including C2C, B2C, and shipping - has been "promoted" to VP Global C2C.
![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/ed/30/ed30d72b-9e06-4849-b9ff-511f2907ce30/content/images/2026/03/2026-03-12_19-20-02-1-1.jpg)Source: [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/xiaodizhang/)
With no replacement yet taking on the broader VP Seller Experience role and a significantly reduced eBay Open experience, it would appear eBay is happy to leave their business sellers of all sizes on the sidelines in 2026 as they race to catch up to [competition from Vinted](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/ebay-zero-selling-fees-fashion-baby-home/) and [Whatnot](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/ebay-live-canada/).

Here's the important piece from the article:  
  
"These negotiated USPS rates can change independently of published retail and commercial pricing, allowing eBay and other shipping platforms to offer different discounts and adjust prices outside the regular postal rate schedule."  
  
USPS still has to go through the Postal Regulatory Commission (PRC) process for published retail and commercial pricing for both Market Dominant products (like Media Mail and First-Class Mail) and Competitive products (like Ground Advantage, Priority Mail and Priority Mail Express).  
  
The PRC has generally limited Market Dominant rate increases to once per fiscal year through 2030. Competitive rate changes can happen more frequently, but still require PRC review and public disclosure.  
  
Marketplaces and other third-party label providers usually have Negotiated Service Agreements (NSAs) or Non-Published Rate (NPR) contracts with USPS that establish different rates from those published retail and commercial prices.  
  
Those contracts can allow rates to change outside the regular published rate schedule. They are still subject to regulatory requirements, but some operate under previously approved pricing structures without requiring separate PRC approval for every adjustment.  
  
Because the specific contract terms and negotiated prices are generally confidential, those changes don't usually come with a public USPS rate announcement.
