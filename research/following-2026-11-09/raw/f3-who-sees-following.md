If you follow people or Pages on Facebook, learn how to control who else can see your follows.
The people you follow can see that you're following them. To change who else can see the people and Pages you're following on a computer:
  1. Click your profile picture in the top right of Facebook.
  2. Click **Settings and privacy** , then click **Settings**.
  3. From the left panel, scroll down to **Audience and visibility** , then click **Followers and public content**. 
  4. Next to **Who can see the people and Pages you follow?** , select the audience.


Remember that when you follow someone, a story about it may appear in your friends’ Feed. People and Pages you’re following may receive a notification that you're following them, depending on their notification settings. They'll also see your name on their list of followers and may choose to make that list visible on their profile. If you choose to [unfollow](https://www.facebook.com/help/190078864497547?helpref=faq_content) a friend, they won't get a notification that you've unfollowed them.
