## **If you can't access Marketplace**
Learn what to do if you [can't access Marketplace](https://www.facebook.com/help/972392066266648?helpref=faq_content).
## **If you have a negative experience on Marketplace**
  * [Block the buyer or seller](https://www.facebook.com/help/721253628232662?helpref=faq_content) on Facebook.
  * Report the [buyer, seller or listing](https://www.facebook.com/help/196126404168290?helpref=faq_content) to Facebook.
  * Leave a [rating](https://www.facebook.com/help/915385548593204?helpref=faq_content) for the buyer or seller.
  * Contact your local law enforcement if you're concerned about your physical safety, [theft](https://www.facebook.com/help/312500235963976?helpref=faq_content) or fraud.


## **If you need help with an order you purchased with checkout**
For items purchased with checkout on Facebook, you may be able to get support from our team. Learn how to [contact the seller or Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/help/390433814792564?helpref=faq_content) for help with your order.
