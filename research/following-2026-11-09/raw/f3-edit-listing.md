You can manage your listings on Marketplace by:
  * Editing the details of your listing.
  * [Deleting your listing](https://www.facebook.com/help/811868760104511?helpref=faq_content).
  * Marking your listing as [sold or pending](https://www.facebook.com/help/1680504982210398?helpref=faq_content).


Learn when you can edit your listing
## Edit your Facebook Marketplace listing
  1. From your Feed, click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=comet-marketplace-desktop&density=1)**Marketplace** in the left menu. If you don't see ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=comet-marketplace-desktop&density=1)**Marketplace** , click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=comet-arrowthinseemore-desktop&density=1)**See more**.
  2. Click **Selling** in the left menu.
  3. Click **Your listings**.
  4. Click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-darkgreythreedots-desktop&density=1)**Options** on the listing you want to edit.
  5. Click **Edit listing**.
  6. Edit your item's details and then click **Update**.


Note: You do not need to edit your listing to show the number of items you have left in a multi-quantity listing. If there are 10 items or less left of a multi-quantity listing, the listing will automatically show the buyer how many items are left.
If your listing only contains one item for sale, users will see "Only 1 for sale" on your listing.
**Note:** If you've enabled the **Checkout with shipping** option, please keep in mind that listings with active orders can't be edited or deleted. If the listing has an order that's pending, waiting to be accepted or declined, or has been shipped or sold, it can't be edited or deleted.
