## Group rules from the admins
* * *
1
Be Kind and Courteous
We're all in this together to create a welcoming environment. Let's treat everyone with respect. Healthy debates are natural, but kindness is required.
2
No Hate Speech or Bullying
Make sure everyone feels safe. Bullying of any kind isn't allowed, and degrading comments about things like race, religion, culture, sexual orientation, gender or identity will not be tolerated.
3
Respect everyone's privacy
Being part of this group requires mutual trust. Authentic, expressive discussions make groups great, but may also be sensitive and private. What's shared in the group should stay in the group.
4
No Harassing or Negative commenting
If you don't have a helpful or supportive response, move on or ignore the question or response. You will be banned and deleted otherwise without warning.
5
Business Advertising
🔹 Business Advertisements (Approved on Mondays ONLY): All business posts must include: Business name Address Phone number Posts missing any of the above details will not be approved All advertising posted other days will be deleted without notice.
6
Sales
✅ Allowed: Garage sales, community sales, and flea market sales. ❌ Not Allowed: Individual item sales, real estate listings, animal sales, or firearm sales. Please post these on Facebook Marketplace or other buy/sell groups instead. Posts that don’t follow these guidelines will be removed. Thank you for helping keep our group clean, relevant, and community-focused!🙌
7
Politics specific to Cape Coral
This group does not allow political posts of any kind, with the exception of local government–related information. Our goal is to maintain a respectful space that honors diverse opinions and keeps the focus on our community.
8
NO GO-FUND-ME POSTS
GoFundMe, crowdfunding, and fundraising posts are not permitted unless approved by an admin. Any unapproved posts will be removed.
