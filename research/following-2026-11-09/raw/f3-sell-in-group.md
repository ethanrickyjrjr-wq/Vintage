Groups focused on buying, selling or promoting products are subject to our [Commerce Policies](https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/commerce) and may have different features available for admins. 
You must be an adult in order to buy, sell or see listings in a Facebook buy and sell group (example: at least 18 years old in the United States). If you’re not an adult, you can still participate in the group, outside of listings.
You can only sell things in groups that have the buy and sell feature added. You must be an admin of the group to [add the buy and sell group feature](https://www.facebook.com/help/776978302394267?helpref=faq_content).
## Sell something in a Facebook buy and sell group
  1. Go to [facebook.com/groups](https://www.facebook.com/groups) and select your group.
  2. Click **Sell Something**.
  3. Click **Item for Sale** , **Vehicle for Sale** , **Home for Sale or Rent** or **Sale event** below **Create new listing**.
  4. Enter the details about the item you're selling and click **Next**.
  5. Choose your audience. To help more buyers see your item, you can:
     * Post the item in other buy and sell groups you're a member of. Click ![checkbox](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-pollscheckbox-desktop&density=1) next to the name of a group to add or remove that option.
     * Create a Marketplace listing for the item. Click ![checkbox](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-pollscheckbox-desktop&density=1) next to **Marketplace** to add or remove that option. Keep in mind that if you're posting as a Page, you won't see the option to create a listing on Marketplace.
  6. Click **Post**.


## Manage your posts
Learn how to [edit or delete](https://www.facebook.com/help/678743755557904?helpref=faq_content) your group posts, or [mark your posts as sold](https://www.facebook.com/help/661376560650221?helpref=faq_content). Keep in mind that you'll need to edit or delete each post in different buy and sell groups separately, even if they’re for the same item. If you created a Marketplace listing for the item, you’ll also need to [edit the Marketplace listing](https://www.facebook.com/help/514314075439323?helpref=faq_content) separately.
## Note
  * Items like animals, drugs, weapons, counterfeits and other items that infringe intellectual property rights [aren't allowed in buy and sell groups](https://www.facebook.com/policies/commerce) or on Marketplace.
  * You must be an adult in order to buy, sell or see listings in a Facebook buy and sell group (example: at least 18 years old in the United States). If you’re not an adult, you can still participate in the group, outside of listings.
