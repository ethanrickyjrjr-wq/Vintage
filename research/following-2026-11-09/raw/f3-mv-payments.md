# Billing and payments for Meta Verified creator subscriptions
Copy link
This article is about [Meta Verified for creators](https://www.facebook.com/help/565322618581808?helpref=faq_content) on Facebook and Instagram.
Looking for business information? Please visit the [Meta Verified for businesses Help Center](https://facebook.com/business/help/2876154822522394?helpref=faq_content).
Learn about billing and payments for Meta Verified creator subscriptions.
## Automatic renewal
Meta Verified creator subscriptions are billed on a recurring basis and renew automatically, unless canceled.
You will be charged on the same date as your original purchase, until you cancel. (Example: If you purchased your subscription on January 24th, you’ll be charged again on February 24th, March 24th, and so on.)
**Note** : If your billing date falls on a day that doesn’t occur in a given month (example: the 31st), you’ll be charged on the last day of the following month instead.
## Subscription price
The price of your subscription depends on the [**_subscription plan_**](https://www.facebook.com/help/729877456503583?helpref=faq_content) you choose.
## Accepted payment methods
Where you purchase Meta Verified determines which payment methods you can use. This is because your subscription is tied to the platform, device type, and account you use to sign up.
  

**If you purchase directly through Meta** , you can use:
  * **Credit Cards** : Visa, Mastercard, American Express
  * **Debit Cards** : Visa, Mastercard, American Express

  

**If you purchase through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store** , payment methods may differ:
  * [**_Apple payment methods_**](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fsupport.apple.com%2Fen-us%2F111741&h=AUA5gPb4vLz7hxBRKjlshKXMv2n0RtNtFNq7YSF-ZZzD0QQES6YEMkScNZbXwPHYFqdkl6FMnz_WP3OKoG1u03uDj7MbU8BRPlthGBAR016z4bRVlwR8UYE2xAco2WeZShseL8KZA2Fi_Q)

## Manage payments
Keeping your payment information up to date is important for maintaining your Meta Verified subscription and ensuring your business stays verified.
Where you purchase Meta Verified determines where you can manage your payment information:
  * **If you purchase directly through Meta** , you can update your subscription payment on Instagram or Facebook using a computer.
  * **If you purchase through Apple** , you need to manage your subscription payment through the Apple App Store.
  * **If you purchase through Google** , you need to manage your subscription payment through the Google Play Store.


**Note** : If you signed up through Apple or Google, make sure you’re signed into the same Apple or Google account you used for your purchase. If you use a different account, you won’t see your Meta Verified subscription or be able to update your payment.
## Update payment method
Any changes to your payment method take effect at the end of your current billing cycle. You need to have at least one valid payment method on file to keep your subscription active.
**Note** : We recommend adding a backup payment method to your account. If your default payment fails, your backup will be charged to keep your subscription active. If you don’t have a backup and your payment fails, you may receive a short grace period to resolve any payment issues before your subscription is canceled.
Learn [**_how to update your payment method for Meta Verified_**](https://www.facebook.com/help/697181472077676?helpref=faq_content).
## View payment history
You can view your Meta Verified subscription payment history, including payment amount, date, and status.
Learn [**_how to view your payment history for Meta Verified_**](https://www.facebook.com/help/584058520236369?helpref=faq_content).
## Refunds
Refunds for Meta Verified subscriptions are only available if you meet the [**_eligibility requirements_**](https://www.facebook.com/help/6143775985675073?helpref=faq_content).
Refunds are returned to the original payment method used to purchase your subscription. Refunds may take several business days, and in some cases up to 60 days, depending on your bank or payment provider.
Learn more about [**_refunds for Meta Verified subscriptions_**](https://www.facebook.com/help/6143775985675073?helpref=faq_content).
**Note** : Meta does not offer any credits or partial refunds for canceled Meta Verified subscriptions.
## Offers and discounts
We currently offer the following discount for Meta Verified creator subscriptions:
  * **Bundle discount:** When you bundle your subscription and verify two profiles instead of one (example: one Instagram account and one Facebook account), you may receive a discounted price.


**Note** : Offers and discounts may not be available to all regions or creators. Eligibility and availability may change.
## Cancel subscription
If you cancel your Meta Verified subscription, it’ll stay active until the end of your current billing cycle. After that, your subscription will end and you’ll lose access to all Meta Verified benefits.
To avoid being charged for another billing cycle, you need to cancel your subscription at least 24 hours before your next payment date.
Learn [**_how to cancel your Meta Verified subscription_**](https://www.facebook.com/help/947706246155091?helpref=faq_content).
## Payment issues
If you’re having trouble with payments for your Meta Verified subscription – such as failed payments, declined cards, or problems updating your payment method – please visit the troubleshooting article below for solutions.
Learn [**_how to troubleshoot payment issues for Meta Verified_**](https://www.facebook.com/help/303238179023189?helpref=faq_content).
  

Visit the [**_Meta Verified for creators Help Center_**](https://www.facebook.com/help/565322618581808?helpref=faq_content).
