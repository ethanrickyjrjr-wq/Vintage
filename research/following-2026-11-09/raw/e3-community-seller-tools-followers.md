"Followers" are people who have marked "save seller" or "save store" on a listing or search. The seller will not know who those followers are, and eBay will not remove them. They remain anonymous.
Followers are many types:
  * Competitors who want to keep track of their competition and prices.
  * Buyers who are interested in the type of items you are selling.
  * Repeat customers who collect what you are selling.
  * Buyers who accidentally click the 'heart' to follow a seller.
