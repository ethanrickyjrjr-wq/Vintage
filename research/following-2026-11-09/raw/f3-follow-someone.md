# What it means to follow a profile or Page on Facebook
Copy link
When you [follow a profile or Page](https://www.facebook.com/help/276458109035418?helpref=faq_content) on Facebook, you may see updates from that person or Page in your Feed, and that person or Page will be able to see that you're following them. Learn more about [changing who sees the people and Pages you follow on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/help/401227106619293?helpref=faq_content). 
You automatically follow people who you're friends with. You can also follow Pages (example: businesses, organizations, brands) and people who aren't your friend on Facebook but allow everybody to follow them.
Some Pages and well-known public figures with large followings are [verified by Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/help/196050490547892?helpref=faq_content) and have a blue badge next to their names to help you know that they're who they claim to be.
With [Favorites](https://www.facebook.com/help/1634545223376778?helpref=faq_content), you can make posts from Pages and people you follow appear at the top of your Feed.
You can also [follow someone on Marketplace](https://www.facebook.com/help/265832487508648?helpref=faq_content).
