# How to Post in Multiple Facebook Groups in 2026

How to post in multiple Facebook groups at once has really become a trick question after the latest API update by Meta!
This was simple before, but in 2024, Meta stopped allowing third-party tools, so the old automation methods no longer work.
The good news is that Facebook still offers a built-in way to do this for buy-and-sell posts, and you do not need any outside apps.
Here’s how you can post in multiple Facebook groups in 2026, along with some important limits to keep in mind.
## How to post in multiple Facebook groups
The first thing you should know is that this works for items you're selling, not for regular text or link posts. For those, see the limits section below. If you want to know how to add this buy and sell feature to your group, check the latter part of the content.
Here is the step-by-step process to post in multiple buy and sell groups.
**Step 1:** Click the **Sell Something** option.
![Tap on the Sell something box, the first step to make a post in multiple groups at once.](https://images.storychief.com/account_6213/image3_0536f595573a53b41f96f24644e088a7_800.png)
**Step 2:** Then tap on the type of product you are trying to sell. You’ll have **four options** available.
![Choose your listing types.](https://images.storychief.com/account_6213/image2_1abde9fa40b44270f8474cab77b14f0e_800.png)
**Step 3:** Further a new window will open where you have put all the details of the selling product, like pictures of the product, title, price, description, and others.
![Provide all the details of your listing post.](https://images.storychief.com/account_6213/image_813e984cf682fab11d35c315b1133b9b_800.png)
**Step 4:** Now tick mark the groups where you want to post, then click on the **Next** option. You can tick the other buy-and-sell groups you've joined that allow listings. The number you can reach varies.
![Choose the Facebook groups where you want to make multiple post.](https://images.storychief.com/account_6213/image5_f3a015aee4d83db68f9856a75132dd43_800.png)
So in summary, while making your post you can tick the other buy-and-sell groups you've joined that allow listings. The feature needs no third-party tools or any additional steps. It’s just a good feature by Facebook Meta.
## Posting in Multiple Facebook Groups by Sharing
To post in multiple groups by sharing you have to have a public group. Private groups do not have such features.
**1.** First, make a post within your or any other public group. Then tap on the share button.
![Tap on the share option of your post from the public Facebook group.](https://images.storychief.com/account_6213/image_6626e62e93861f8e164f77139c8c4fd0_800.png)
**2.** It’ll further open a new window and there you’ll see the **Group** icon. Click on it.
![Click on the group icon to make your post in multiple groups.](https://images.storychief.com/account_6213/image_1138f2078f927ee7266af276d273b3fa_800.png)
**3.** Another interface will open up with all the groups you are already connected with. You can even search your desired groups where you want to make the post. Choosing each of them will lead to making a new post to that specific group.
![Chose multiple groups to make your post.](https://images.storychief.com/account_6213/image_08e218cb4dd39cebdbaa177245e2a6a4_800.png)
And that is how you make a post in multiple Facebook groups by sharing. No need for third-party tools here.
## The Use of Third-Party Tools
With the update of Meta on 22 April 2024, the use of external tools to automate Facebook groups has been abolished. Meta has deprecated the group API endpoint. Ultimately, no more posts in multiple groups with the help of third-party tools.
All you can do is seek the help of available Facebook group features. Even though they are time-consuming, they are quite efficient as we have already mentioned in the step-by-step guide in the former part of the blog.
If you want to know more about the latest API updates on third-party tools by Meta, have a read to our blog [Facebook Groups API Deprecated: Meta Cuts off Third-Party Apps to Facebook Group](https://groupboss.io/blog/meta-cuts-off-third-party-apps-to-facebook-group/).
Overall if you are planning to use external tools to post in multiple groups, be careful! Meta might even put you in [Facebook jail ](https://groupboss.io/blog/facebook-jail/)permanently banning your existing groups and taking away the access.
## How to add buy and sell option in Facebook groups
You can add the Buy and Sell feature from [Facebook group settings](https://groupboss.io/blog/facebook-group-settings-learn-everything-in-details/).
![Start by adding the Buy and Sell feature set to post in multiple Facebook groups.](https://images.storychief.com/account_6213/image4_959eded2ea17cb4c60693e40d3b5eb7a_800.png)
Once you have added the feature set, go back to the group’s homepage. Now you’ll see a **Sell something** option instead of the **Write something…** option. You must look for that to make a post in multiple Facebook groups.
Only some group types support the Buy & Sell template, and only an admin can turn it on. If you don't see a 'Sell something' box after enabling it, your group type may not support listings.
## The Honest Limits: What You Can and Can't Do
Let’s be clear about what you can and can’t do in 2026.
Facebook does not have a built-in feature for posting regular text or link content to many groups at once. The option to post to multiple groups is only available for buy-and-sell listings. For regular posts, you need to share them with each group one at a time.
For normal posts, you have three honest options:
  1. As an admin, use Facebook's built-in scheduler inside each group to line posts up in advance.
  2. Save a draft and reuse the text and images quickly across groups.


None of these methods allows true bulk posting. This is the trade-off that came when Meta closed the API. If someone promises one-click posting to many groups, they are either breaking Facebook’s rules or putting your account at risk.
**A simple tip:** Write your post once in a notes app, then paste it into each group. This way, you save time and avoid using risky tools.

## FAQs
### What’s the best time of the day to make your Facebook group post?
Mid-week days are the best time to make your group posts. Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday are the best days of the week to make your Facebook group post. And if you are planning to make bulk posts, or post in multiple groups at the same time; consider posting from 12 pm-3 pm. Analytics show 7-9 am, 1-3 pm, and 7-9 pm are also good times to make a post. Some major groups suggest posting at 8 pm on Thursdays.
### Are there any external tools to make a post in multiple groups?
There are no approved tools that can post to Facebook groups now. Meta removed API access for all third-party apps in April 2024. Tools that claim to automate group posting often use browser bots, which break Facebook’s rules. Using these tools can get your account and groups banned. Only Facebook’s own features are safe to use.
### What to keep in mind while posting in multiple Facebook groups?
The first thing you should keep in mind is that posting relevant content. Suppose you are posting tutorial video content on a specific automation tool in multiple groups. You can not post that in groups that are not interested in automation tools or do not fall under the same niche of the content! So while selecting your groups be more deliberate. Ensure that you do not post marketing content in a sales group.
### Can I schedule posts to a Facebook group?
Yes, but only with Facebook’s built-in tools. If you are an admin or moderator, you can schedule posts inside each group. There is no way to schedule posts across multiple groups at once, so you still need to post to each group separately.
### How many groups can I post to in a day?
There is no official limit, but posting the same content quickly to many groups can look like spam. Space out your posts and avoid posting the same item more than once every 5–7 days, or Facebook may flag your account.
### Will I get banned for posting the same content in many groups?
Yes, it’s possible. Sending identical posts to many groups quickly is a common reason for spam bans. Change your wording, post only where it makes sense, and keep your posting pace natural.
Posting in multiple Facebook groups is more difficult now, but it’s still possible. Use the Buy & Sell method for listings, share with each group one at a time for other posts, and avoid any tool that promises shortcuts.
Taking the honest approach may be slower, but it keeps your account safe and ensures your reach is genuine.
