## Group rules from the admins
* * *
1
DO YOUR OWN RESEARCH & PROVIDE CLEAR PICTURES
And share with us where you researched so we don't waste time duplicating the research. When you hit a dead, then feel free to ask for help. Posts without pictures, blurry pictures or pictures that are sideways will not be approved
2
SHARE HAULS IN THE WEEKLY HAUL THREAD
We love when members share their awesome thrifted finds, but please do so in the weekly haul thread.
3
BOLOs MUST BE YOUR OWN
Be On the Look Out (BOLO) posts must be an item that you yourself sold. The point is to share something you bought that you were able to sell for a considerable markup. BOLOs for items/sales that were not yours will be deleted. Please state in your post what the item is, how much you paid, and how much you sold it for as a link will not stay active forever.
4
NO SELLING POSTS or LOOKING TO BUY
We are not a buy/sell group. If you're looking to buy, you may only post in our official ISO post.
5
NO SELF PROMOTION POSTS or OUTSIDE LINKS
Want to promote your business, your blog, your YouTube channel? You must get permission from Jason T Smith first and indicate in your post that it is approved. The only outside links allowed are links that will directly help with the question that has been asked.
6
NO RANT or POLITICAL POSTS
If the purpose of your post is just to complain or dwell on a negative experience and you're not actually seeking advice, it will be deleted. We are not the police of other people's bad behavior.Although we don't allow political posts, if you have a thrifted item that is political in nature and you need by all means bring it to the group. But the only replies acceptable to those type of posts is help, no soapboxing.
7
NO DISPARAGING BUYERS or PARTNERS
Stick to the facts and the facts only. We do not allow scam talk or promoting retaliatory actions such as blocking. We also do not allow attacks, name calling, or rants about our business partners. This includes but is not limited to shipping partners(USPS FedEx), sourcing partners such as thrift stores(Goodwill, Savers), payment partners (PayPal), and platform partners (eBay, Etsy, Amazon). If you have a problem you need help with, state the facts and we will do our best to help.
8
NO TAX QUESTIONS OR LEGAL ADVICE OF ANY KIND
We are all responsible for our own businesses and that includes being aware of legal requirements and tax law that impact our livelihoods. If you need help, reach out to a tax professional for guidance, not this group.
9
NO DELETING YOUR POSTS OR TURNING OFF COMMENTING.
Members are not permitted to delete their posts or turn off the commenting. If there is an issue with a post you have made please contact a Lifeguard for assistance.
10
PLEASE BLACKOUT ALL EBAY IDs THAT AREN'T YOURS
If you need help with a problem and have some screenshots, please blackout all names and IDs that aren't yours.
