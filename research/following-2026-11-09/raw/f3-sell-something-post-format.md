# Choose what people can post in a Facebook group you admin

This option is only available in some groups for now.
Admins of some groups can choose which kinds of posts people can create in their group. Admins can also customize the shortcuts available when someone goes to post in their group.
Note that Photo/Video, Camera, Check In, Tag Friends, Feeling/Activity and Background Color are available to everyone in groups and can’t be turned off.
## Choose which post formats to allow or not allow
  1. Go to [facebook.com/groups](https://www.facebook.com/groups) and select your group.
  2. Click **Group Settings** in the left menu.
  3. Below **Manage Discussion** , click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=comet-edit-desktop&density=1)**Edit** next to **Edit post formats**.
  4. Click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-toggleoff-desktop&density=1)**Toggle off** or ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-toggleonsettings-desktop&density=1)**Toggle on** next to each feature.
  5. Click **Save**.


## Customize which post formats are listed as shortcuts
  1. Go to [facebook.com/groups](https://www.facebook.com/groups) and select your group.
  2. Click **Group Settings** in the left menu.
  3. Below **Manage Discussion** , click ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=comet-edit-desktop&density=1)**Edit** next to **Edit post formats**.
  4. Click **Edit** next to **Post format shortcuts**.
  5. Click **Add shortcut** and select the format you want to add.
  6. To remove a shortcut, click the format you want to remove, then click **Remove**.
  7. Click **Save**.


**Note:** Only some formats can be added as shortcuts.
If you stop allowing a type of post, previous posts using that format will still be available in the group.
Learn how to [choose which kinds of features](https://www.facebook.com/help/166599882185672?helpref=faq_content) are available in your group.
