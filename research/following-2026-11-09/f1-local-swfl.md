## 1. TL;DR

- **Real SWFL buy/sell, vintage and estate *groups* are almost invisible to search.** `site:facebook.com/groups` returned nothing useful. Restricting to `facebook.com` returned mostly Pages and Marketplace category URLs. Only a few `/groups/` URLs surfaced. I found no local vintage, antique, MCM or reseller *group* in Lee or Collier. The honest list is Pages, businesses, events and media, plus 4 real groups, 2 of them fully read.
- **Two Cape Coral groups were read live, rules included.** Public: Cape Coral Community Information, 183.9K members. Private: Cape Coral, Florida, 52.9K members.
  - Community Information allows garage, community and flea-market sale posts but bars individual-item sales.
  - Business ads there run Mondays only and must give name, address and phone, which collides with Hollie having no public address.
  - The other group has a "Local Only" rule, so Bonita Springs residency may disqualify her.
  - Both are neighbor-building groups, not sales channels.
- **The strongest local tie is Flamingo Island Flea Market.** It is in Bonita Springs (11902 Bonita Beach Rd SE), not Fort Myers. It is open Fri to Sun 8am to 4pm all year, rents indoor and outdoor space, and its own site links its Facebook Page. `V` from its website today.
- **The right vintage event audience is Vintage Market Days South Gulf Coast.** The Facebook link `facebook.com/vmdsogulfcoastfl/` and Instagram link are on its own site (`V` today). The Jan 29-31, 2027 Lee Civic Center date is `V` from d6 (2026-10-29).
- **Best collab lead is a local thrift influencer.** Kelly Chenault runs the Instagram `@swflgulfcoastthrifts` with 10K+ followers. `V`: a June 2026 WINK News story, reposted on naplesshelter.org, calls it SWFLGulfcoastthrift. She is Instagram, not Facebook.
- **The Naples mom-group ecosystem has a real seller channel, but I could not get its URL.** A 2023 Naples Family article (`V`, dated Apr 29, 2023) names "Naples Moms Marketplace" (members sell personal items and advertise businesses) as a sub-group of Naples Moms 2.0 (21,100 members in 2023). It fits only if Hollie qualifies as a mom or caregiver. Find it by name in the signed-in browser.
- **Pacing is f3's.** `docs/facebook-marketplace.md` already notes Meta may restrict a "new or inactive account". Liking a few Pages is far lighter than joining groups, so the shortlist leads with Pages. Do not read "join first" as "do all of this on day one."
- **Rules are the biggest gap.** Only 4 entries have readable rules. No no-selling rule was found in any group I could read, but I could read almost none.
