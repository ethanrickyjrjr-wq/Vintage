## Tags and sources

**Tags**
- `verified-FB`: name, member count, activity and rules read from Facebook's logged-out group preview (crawl4ai, 2026-11-09). Counts are as of that day.
- `verified-site`: the Facebook link sits on the entity's own website, read this session. The Page itself is walled, so follower counts are not available and activity is not confirmed.
- `unverified (snippet)`, `unverified (roundup)`, `unverified (article)`: the claim exists only in a search-tool summary, a blog roundup or a news article, or the group's FB URL was walled.

## TL;DR

- **46 entries:** 19 buyer-audience groups, 9 reseller/peer groups, 18 Pages and creators. 16 groups are `verified-FB`.
- **Best sales channel found: Quirky Groovy Vintage BST.** It has 74.9K members, 2,645 posts in the last month and a vintage-1999-and-older rule, and its tags include Fashion and Interior Design. **Its rule 1 rejects join requests from locked or limited profiles, profiles with no recent posts, "really low friend count", "low activity" and "no profile picture".** Hollie's profile (0 friends, no photo) would be rejected today. Sellers also need a paid seller subscription, and payment must be PayPal or Venmo G&S.
- **The only era-matched BST is nearly dormant.** Ooh La La! 1963-1990s Vintage Hive Mind BST has 12.0K members but only 8 posts in the last month. Its active sibling (1920-1962, 117 posts a month) bars anything after 1962.
- **Most big thrift and vintage groups ban selling.** I Love Thrifting (611.6K), All Things Vintage & Thrifted (542.9K), Thrift Store Junkies (519.4K), Thrift Store & Antique Finds (489.6K) and Thrifted Home Decor & More (73.6K) are audience spaces. They suit genuine participation and seeing what buyers like, not listings. Treat them as inspiration, not as sales channels.
- **Empty categories.** I found no Facebook group for Hollywood Regency, Old Florida or Palm Beach style, vintage disco or mirror balls, vintage art or framed prints, or brass lamp buy/sell. Grandmillennial is only a paid private group ($29 a month, search snippet). Mid-century modern has a claimed 78K group with no Facebook URL found. Antique Lighting Collectors is the closest lamp match.
- **Pages are the low-risk first move.** Palm Beach Regency, Vintage Regency Boutique, Florida Regency and Vintage Palm Beach are on-aesthetic Florida shops. Follower counts are unavailable because all 14 Page URLs returned a 4-byte wall.
- **Roundups are unreliable.** The Sammy D list, "updated Oct 8, 2026", reuses one URL (`/groups/asmcuk/`) for three different groups and links one entry to a woodworking blog. The VintageDancer list is from 2016. I used both only as name leads and checked each group live.
- **Pacing is f3's call.** Order matters regardless: follow Pages first, join groups later.
