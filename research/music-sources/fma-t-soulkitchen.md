Description 
Bright minor-key soul funk at a relaxed tempo with a firm groove and a noticeably wider dynamic range than most of this album — it lifts and settles rather than running flat. Just over two minutes.  
  
For cooking and food content, kitchen b-roll, restaurant promos and warm lifestyle video. The dynamic lift suits picture that is building toward something.  
  
Two minutes seventeen, the shortest track on the album, and the widest dynamic range of the nine: it lifts and settles rather than holding one level, which the other eight do.
