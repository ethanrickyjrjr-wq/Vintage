### Frequently Asked Questions
## License Choice
What license/plan do I need for Social Media?
To share videos on social media, our Individual License (Pay-per-track or All Access Subscription plan) has you covered. However, if you're looking to amplify your reach through paid ads, you can purchase the Individual License.
What’s the difference between an All Access Subscription plan and a Pay-per-track License?
While a Pay-per-track License covers a single Music Track or a single Sound Effect, our All Access Subscription plans unlock our entire music and sound effects library. Enjoy unlimited downloads of tracks and sound effects for an entire year, and for each track and sound effect you use, download a license certificate authorizing perpetual usage (without any need for credits).
For client work, what are my licensing options?
When making projects for clients, they are the final project owners and publishers. You have two options:
- You can purchase a Pay-per-track License, with your client as the License Holder.
- Or, you can opt for the flexibility of a Pay-per-track Business License or a Business All Access Subscription plan, covering multiple client work. With these Business options, you have the freedom to sell an unlimited number of projects containing the licensed music track(s) and/or sound effects, to multiple clients for their commercial usage.
Can I use your music and sound effects in my podcast or audiobook?
Absolutely! To use our music and/or sound effects in your podcast or audiobook, you can purchase our **Individual Pay-per-track License** or our **Individual All Access Subscription** plan.
What license/plan are larger organizations (250+ employees) covered under?
If your organization has more than 250 employees, you’ll need an Enterprise License designed specifically to support your needs and usage requirements. To get started, select **Request a Quote** on our Pricing page or contact our support team directly.
