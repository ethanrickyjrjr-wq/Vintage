# What rights do I have with the free plan?
Edited
Songs made on the free plan are only intended for **personal, non-commercial use**. These songs cannot be monetized, but can be shared using your Suno link.
On the free plan, you can:
  * Create using 50 credits that renew daily
  * Keep the tracks set to Link-Only, or Publish them to your Suno page
  * Share your songs using the link to your song page, playlist, etc.
  * Use the song in a personal, non-monetizing project


As always, any material on Suno is subject to our [**Terms of Service**](https://suno.com/terms) and published tracks are subject to our [**Community Guidelines**](https://suno.com/community-guidelines)!
