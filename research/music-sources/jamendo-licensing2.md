### What is Royalty-free music or Rights included?
Royalty-free music licenses mean that you won’t have to pay any royalties on a per-use basis. Once you acquire the license and synchronize it with an audiovisual work or project, it’s yours forever no matter how many times your project is viewed. That’s what Real Royalty-free is about.
