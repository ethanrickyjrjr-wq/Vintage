# **About Us**
## **What Chosic is**
Chosic is a set of free tools to discover new music and understand it more deeply — similar-songs & playlist generator, music genre finder, BPM finder, lyrics search, playlist analyzer, music taste analyzer, and more — built for people who want to explore music beyond what streaming algorithms show them.
We also host a library of royalty-free music for creators to use in their own projects.
## **Who’s behind it**
Hi, I’m **Asoom**. Chosic is built and maintained by me and my wife — a couple based in Serbia who both work on the site. We design, code, and run every part of it — the tools, the backend, the infrastructure.
Chosic started as a way to combine two things I love — programming and music, though honestly music wins by a wide margin. If you’re wondering about the name — Chosic is short for “chosen music.”
Since I was young, I’ve enjoyed digging past the obvious recommendations to find genres and songs that only a small number of people seem to know about. Streaming platforms are good at giving you more of what you already like, but they’re not built for that kind of exploration. Chosic became our attempt to build the tools we wished existed — ones that make discovery feel like digging, not scrolling, and that make the data behind music (tempo, key, genre, mood) something anyone can actually play with.
I’m also just someone who spends an unreasonable amount of time thinking about music — you can see some of what I listen to on [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/user/22iaspg2vfcgr6vrghallsxty).
## **Why trust the tools**
  * Most of our tools — similar-songs finder, playlist analyzer, taste analyzer, BPM finder, genre finder — run on Spotify’s official API. Lyrics search and a few others pull from different, verified sources. Data on the site isn’t scraped or guessed.
  * Actively maintained — bugs get fixed by the same two people who built the feature, not routed through a support queue.
  * No AI-generated recommendations pretending to be curated; the tools are transparent about what data they’re using and how each result is generated.
  * Free to use, with no account required for most tools.
  * No hidden data resale — Chosic runs on ads and optional donations, not on selling user data.
