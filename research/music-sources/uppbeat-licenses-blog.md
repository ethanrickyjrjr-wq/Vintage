## Which licenses do you need to use music in videos?
![Image showing Uppbeat's music licensing platform alongside different examples of video content.](https://blog-cdn.uppbeat.io/blog/content/images/2024/06/Music-licensing-for-videos-1.jpg)
Before we get into how Uppbeat’s easy music licenses work, first we need to cover how licensing typically works. If you want to use music in your video, you need a master license and a sync license. 
The master license gives you permission to use a specific recording of a piece of music. Whereas the sync license outlines how you can use the music when it’s paired - or synced - with video footage.
While you technically need two licenses, copyright-free music platforms like Uppbeat sort out the legal side for you. All you need to do is sign up to Uppbeat and you’re ready to start downloading tracks from our catalog without worrying about copyright issues.
🎧

## How does Uppbeat's easy music licensing work?
![Images of Uppbeat's artists alongside different creators to show the relationship between the two that comes with music licensing.](https://blog-cdn.uppbeat.io/blog/content/images/2024/06/How-do-Uppbeat_s-music-licenses-work.jpg)
The important thing to know is all Uppbeat’s tracks are pre-cleared for you to download and use safely in your content. It’s as simple as it sounds, but how exactly does it work? Let us break it down for you.
Every time we start working with one of the incredibly talented artists featured on Uppbeat, we draw up an agreement for how their music can be used. This agreement allows Uppbeat to license their tracks directly to you, with master recordings available to download through the platform.
So how do you then get permission to use their music? When you make an account on Uppbeat, you sign a [_User Agreement_](https://uppbeat.io/user-agreement). As part of this you’re also signing up to terms that allow you to license music through Uppbeat. 
Every time you make a download, you’ve already agreed to the legal bits that grant you both the master license and sync license for that track. You essentially get the licensing out of the way so you can concentrate on picking your next soundtrack from [_Uppbeat’s_](https://uppbeat.io/) library.
### Can I use Uppbeat’s tracks in more than just online content?
Uppbeat’s licenses allow you to use our music in videos shared across platforms like YouTube, TikTok and Instagram - basically on what’s known as _open distribution platforms_. This means you’d need a different license if your content appears anywhere else. For example, if you were to show your content at a live event or sell physical copies of your video.
Need more coverage than our [_User Agreement_](https://uppbeat.io/user-agreement) gives you? We can still help you license music to use in your project. Simply fill out the [contact form](https://uppbeat.io/help-center/contact-form) and our friendly support team can help to modify your license.
🎶
You can learn more about the different types of licenses and what you’d need them for in our guide to [_music licensing_](https://uppbeat.io/blog/royalty-free-and-copyright-free-music/music-licensing).
### If the licenses are pre-cleared, why do I need a separate credit for each video?
Uppbeat Credits simplify the process of keeping your videos clear of copyright claims. They show platforms like YouTube you’ve correctly licensed that track and have permission to use it in your content. If you don’t use an Uppbeat Credit, platforms could hit you with a copyright claim.
A new Uppbeat Credit is generated each time you download a track. You can access your Credits by heading to your [_Download History_](https://uppbeat.io/my/downloads). Remember, you’ll need a separate Uppbeat Credit for each track you use and in every video it features in. That’s right, even if you’re using the same track in different videos! That’s because each Credit is specific to that particular use of the music.
Want to make your life even easier by pre-clearing tracks on YouTube? Why not try our [_Channel Safelist_](https://uppbeat.io/help-center/channel-safelist) feature. It tells YouTube you’ve already got permission to use our catalog, meaning you won’t even need to include an Uppbeat Credit.
### Which plan covers what?
Every Uppbeat download comes with the same pre-cleared master and sync licence. What changes between plans is where you're allowed to use it.  
  
Free, Essentials and Creator cover individuals publishing on their own channels. [Essentials](https://uppbeat.io/pricing) includes [music](https://uppbeat.io/music) and [sound effects](https://uppbeat.io/sfx), while [Creator](https://uppbeat.io/pricing) adds [stock video](https://uppbeat.io/stock-video), [motion graphics](https://uppbeat.io/motion-graphics) and [LUTs](https://uppbeat.io/luts).  
  
[Pro](https://uppbeat.io/pricing) covers freelancers and solo professionals, including client projects and commercial digital use.  
  
[Business](https://uppbeat.io/pricing) covers teams, companies and organizations, for company content and commercial projects.  
  
Safelisting scales with your plan too: one YouTube channel on Essentials, three on Creator, and ten on both Pro and Business.  
  
For broadcast, cinema, radio or paid streaming services, we offer custom licensing – [get in touch](https://uppbeat.io/pricing) and we'll sort it out.
