# Frequently Asked Questions
This page describes usage policy for our [Royalty Free Background Music](https://www.chosic.com/free-music/all/).
Music is FREE to use in all projects and media
Use for commercial purpose (monetization) allowed
Only tracks with symbol require attribution
Can I use the music on Chosic for free?–
Yes, all music in our library is free to use for commercial and non-commercial purposes. Just credit the artists in your video/project description.
Can I use the music for commercial purposes (monetization)?+
Yes. Just credit the artists in your video/project description.
I received a copyright claim on YouTube. What should I do?+
  1. Make sure you have included the required credits in the video description.
  2. If credits are provided, you can dispute the claim on YouTube. In the claim description, mention that the music is under a Creative Commons license and include a link to the music source. The claim should be removed in a few days in most cases.
  3. If the claim remains, please [report the track](https://www.chosic.com/report-problem-with-free-music/) or contact the artist directly to resolve the problem.


Where can I use this music?+
You can use our free music on YouTube, social media, websites, and anywhere else as long as you properly attribute the artist.  
If you can’t credit the artist, you need to buy a license. Please contact the artist in that case.
Can I use music in a radio/game/app/software/film?+
For these types of usage, some artists may permit the use of their work if they are properly credited, while others may require a license to be purchased. We recommend personally reaching out to the music authors for further information.  
You can find artist links on the track page.
What do you mean by “attribution”?+
Attribution means crediting the artist who created the music. You should copy and paste the provided text into your video description. This text will be available when you download any music track.
What is Creative Commons license?+
You can read about all the Creative Commons licenses on [this link](https://creativecommons.org/about/cclicenses/).
Can I modify the music?+
For most of the tracks here, yes, you can modify the music. For more details, click on the license link on the track page for each track you want to use.
How do I download your free music?+
We’ve tried to keep it simple: click the download button next to the track you want. You will be directed to the track page where you can read the track’s info and download it easily. Click the download button again on the track’s page, and it will be downloaded automatically.
Do I have to credit the artist if I use the music?+
Some tracks require you to credit the artist, while others don’t. You should read the description for each track on its page.  
For tracks that don’t need attribution, we’d love it if you could credit chosic.com! Just put a link to this page (<https://www.chosic.com/free-music/all/>) in your project so others can also find us.
How do I credit the artist in my video?+
Just copy and paste the text in the red rectangle on the track page into your video description, website text, or wherever applicable.
Can I download your free music in bulk?+
No, currently you can only download our free royalty-free music on a track-by-track basis.
Are the music tracks copyrighted?+
Yes, all the rights for the music tracks belong to their authors. You should credit the artist in your project description to ensure compliance.
Does a paid subscription protect you from copyright claims?+
A paid subscription doesn’t guarantee protection from copyright claims. You must still provide proper credits in the video description to avoid potential copyright issues.
How can I reduce the length of the credits if I use multiple tracks at once?+
Feel free to rearrange the credits, but ensure that they include the author’s name, the author’s link, the track title, and the license.
Can I produce my own songs using tracks from chosic.com and upload them to streaming platforms?+
No, it is prohibited to produce your own music using tracks from chosic.com and upload them to streaming services such as Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, or YouTube Music.
Does Chosic generate or provide any certificate for me in case of royalty strikes on YouTube or other social media?+
Chosic can’t provide such certificates, but you may contact the artists directly or check their website if they have one. You can see the artist name and site on each track page.
The description space of TikTok is very limited, so I couldn’t paste all of the text into the description. What should I do?+
We recommend pasting the artist’s name and site in the description space, but it is better to contact the artist to be 100% sure.
License Types+
There are two types of music in our library: 
  1. **Public domain music**  
You can use this music for free without attribution, even for commercial purposes.  
License example: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>


  1. **Creative commons music (CC-BY)**  
Attribution is required when you use this music, and you can use the music for free even for commercial purposes.  
License examples:  
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/>  
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/>


You can click on the license link from any track page to find more about what you can and can’t do with the music.
Can I buy a license?+
All our music is free to use, however, if you still want to buy a license, please contact the artist (link to artist site is provided in the track page).
Things you can’t do+
**You cannot:**  
**X** : You cannot claim music as your own or register it in any store/platform.  
**X:** You can use music for Facebook videos **but you cannot** register them to the **Facebook Rights Manager** Service.  
**X:** You may NOT use music in an audio track that you intend to put into the Content ID system.
Should I donate to use the music?+
No, you don’t have to donate in order to use the music! Donations are optional. All tracks are free to use on YouTube and other social media platforms. Just credit the artists and follow the instructions on each track’s download page.
  
If you have any problems or questions, you can [contact us](https://www.chosic.com/contact-us/)
