WHEN DO I GET PAID?
When your item sells, the sale remains pending for up to 22 business days. If the item isn't returned and the order isn't canceled, the sale is automatically confirmed at the end of the pending period. Your payout is then added to your Rebag Account within approximately 15 business days after the confirmation date.
## HOW DOES CONSIGNMENT WORK AT REBAG?
Before deciding between Consign, Trade, or Buyout, follow the same simple steps to get started:
  1. **Sign in to[Rebag.com ](https://www.rebag.com/) **- Log in or create an account before navigating to the Sell page
  2. **Share your item’s details** -Identify your item via AI, search, or image submission and provide basic information such as the model, material, and condition
  3. **Receive and accept your quote** - Our Clair technology will instantly generate an offer for most styles, or get your quote via email within 2 business days
  4. **Send in your item** - Use your free label or find a store near you
  5. **Your item is listed for sale on Rebag.com** - After inspection and verification, your item is live within 5-10 days
  6. **You get paid** - Once your sale is confirmed, your payout is added to your Rebag Account within approximately 15 business days.


## HOW DO I EARN MONEY THROUGH CONSIGNMENT?
  1. **Fair, accurate quotes** - Our data determines the fair value of your item, aiming to sell it for the maximum price
  2. **Your pre-approved payout** - You’ll always have visibility into your exact payout range
  3. **Fixed commission rates** - Regardless of the selling price, the approved commission rate will not change
  4. **Flexible ways to use your payout** - Shop with your Balance right away, or Transfer it to your bank. Balances transferred within 15 calendar days of deposit are subject to a 3% Early Transfer Fee.
  5. **Grow your Balance with Incentives** - Once your Balance becomes available, it automatically increases by 1% per month for up to 9 months, generating up to a 9% bonus.


## WHAT ARE REBAG'S COMMISSION RATES?
Commission rates, designed to provide visibility into your payout, are communicated upfront and remain fixed regardless of the selling price. All sellers enjoy the same low and lower rates for the most desirable items.
