## What is a clean out kit?
June 25 2026 11:41pm 
Our Cleanout Kit is a term we use for our physical cleanout bags _**OR**_ our printable labels, both of which expire within 3 months of being requested.
  * **For labels:** Sellers use their own packaging if they opt for a printable label


Sellers can choose to send in their gently used items to ThredUp for consignment **OR** for donation. We currently have 3 options for our cleanout service:
  * Standard
    * By selecting standard, all unaccepted items will be reused + responsibly recycled
  * Premium
    * If a premium item is purchased with a promo code, the payout will only be impacted by up to 20% max.
    * Sellers have the option to receive their unaccepted items shipped back to them.
  * Donation
    * In lieu of a payout, we donate $5 per donation bag to seller’s selected charity


**Why sell with ThredUp?**
  1. We give clothes a second life, keeping them in circulation & out of landfills
  2. We make it easy for Sellers to cleanout with us!
    1. We’ll provide the prepaid labels or physical bags
  3. We process, list, store and ship out items to our ThredUp buyers
  4. Our dynamic pricing system is designed to maximize the chance of selling items within their listing window
