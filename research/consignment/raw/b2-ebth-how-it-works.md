# How to Sell with EBTH
* * *
![](https://www.ebth.com/assets/how-it-works/the-call-c8e9b9584a5dab079520d57dd86223b5b9baee65a443d6e22b27f10f463c0deb.png)
## The Call
Individuals and families contact EBTH about selling a collection, whether it’s rare coins or a lifetime of household objects. Consultations are entirely free.
![](https://www.ebth.com/assets/how-it-works/the-prep-971f1091bff1ca7add70bd556a806389104625a7beac5d10b4d847368afc0bd3.png)
## The Prep
Our trained and knowledgeable staff sorts, catalogues, photographs and writes descriptions for each item, then uploads them to EBTH.com. If there are pieces not fit for sale, we arrange for donation and trash removal.
![](https://www.ebth.com/assets/how-it-works/the-sale-7d97259f1fcd5d164d220547ee7c93de98e6459fd269404ef490b01058d50ed5.png)
## The Sale
The sale goes live! All items are up for auction for up to 7 days, and all bids start at $1. After fast and furious bidding, the sale closes and we manage payment, pickup and shipping and delivery.
![](https://www.ebth.com/assets/how-it-works/the-proceeds-b55dce0df0f05fb06996e5efeda0c31af91dc5045980bb86cd489d414ad4b6b0.png)
## The Proceeds
We remove all auction items from the home and provide an itemized inventory list and settlement check within 30 days.
