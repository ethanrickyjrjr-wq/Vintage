## Instructions for Submission
Please include pictures for each of the items you would like to consign. Provide as many details as you can for each piece. For example, the original purchase price, any signs of wear & tear, and/or the age of an item.
You will receive a green confirmation box once you click submit to confirm your submission was sent. Please allow 2 business days for someone on our team to review your items and a contact you.
