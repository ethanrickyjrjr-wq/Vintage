## Designed for however yousell
  * ###### Consignment Stores
Shops that sell items for consignors, paying them a split once the item sells.
  * ###### Vendor Malls
Multi-vendor or booth-based retail spaces that need centralized checkout and vendor reporting.
  * ###### High-End Resale
Luxury purse, sneaker, and fashion stores where exceptional service is mission critical.
  * ###### Online-Only
Web-based stores that sell through Shopify or Square.
  * ###### Mixed-Retail
Shops that mix consignment and retail inventory under the same roof.
  * ###### Buy-Outright Stores
Resale shops that purchase items up front instead of consigning.
  * ###### Furniture Stores
Large-item resellers and estate shops that need simple inventory management and pricing.
  * ###### Gift Shops
Shops that bring together local makers and unique finds.
  * ###### Antique Malls
Collective spaces filled with unique, vendor-owned items.
  * ###### Art Galleries
Artisan and gallery-style spaces selling one-of-a-kind pieces.
  * ###### Pop-ups
Short-term or traveling shops that need quick setup, easy checkout, and lightweight inventory tools.

How can shoppers pay with their credit card?
To accept credit card payments, you’ll need a credit card terminal. We recommend using one of our integrated partners—Stripe or Gravity—to make processing payments simple and seamless. 
What kind of support do you offer?
ConsignCloud’s live Support team is available via our in-store chat and normally responds within just minutes! Our Support Hours are **8:00 AM - 5:00 PM PST, Monday - Friday**.

How do your Shopify and Square integrations work?
ConsignCloud connects directly with **Shopify** and **Square**. Once linked, any item sold through those platforms automatically updates in ConsignCloud—keeping inventory and consignor accounts in sync. 
Learn more about our [Shopify integration](https://help.consigncloud.com/en/articles/3212864-integrating-consigncloud-with-shopify)or [Square integration](https://help.consigncloud.com/en/articles/6688515-integrating-consigncloud-with-square). 
Can consignors log in to get their own updates?
