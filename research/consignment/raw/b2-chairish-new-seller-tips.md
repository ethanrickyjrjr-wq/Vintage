#  New Seller Tips 
Favorites build over time, so it can take 3-6 months for new sellers to gain traction on Chairish. In the meantime, below are some additional tips to help grow your sales:
  * Consistent newness is the best way to drive favorites and sales. 
    * Our [New Arrivals](https://www.chairish.com/collection/new-arrivals) page is our most trafficked collection. 
    * When you list new items they stay in this collection for 2-3 weeks. 
    * We highly recommend monthly (if not weekly) newness. 
    * Newness helps build [favorites](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44172122725777) and followers, which drive our triggered emails.
  * Triggered emails are powerful tools. Some ways to partake in these are:
    * **Promotions** : Participate in promotions. Every time your item goes on sale, an email is sent out to all of the item's [favoriters ](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44172122725777)and followers. 
    * **Reduce Prices** : [Mark your prices down](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44170940096657). Please note, if you mark your items down, you cannot bring the price back up again. If you don't want a permanent markdown, consider participating in a sale instead.
    * **Set Reserves** : Ensure you have compelling [Reserve prices](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44170397686801), as these drive our [Private Offer](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44173070844689) emails.
    * **Private Sales** : Participants of our [**Plus** program](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44173308382481) are able to participate in Private Sales, where they have the ability to create limited-time sales for their followers.
  * Promoted listings are a great way to drive visibility to your items. You can sign up [HERE](https://www.chairish.com/promoted-listings-setup). 
  * Make sure all listings are in the correct state and available for purchase by reviewing all of the tabs on your "My Listings" page.
  * Try to update [titles](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44202668491025), [descriptions](https://chairish2.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/44177598146065-Creating-a-Listing#h_01FE2JX2BXCPT9D7Y46CEHTARF), and materials to include more searchable terms, when possible. 
  * For wearable items, images on a person or mannequin (i.e. on-figure) are much more likely to sell, especially if the item is over $500.
  * Ensure you are reviewing and engaging with your [Offers ](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44173070844689)and [Messages ](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44171730260369)so they don't expire or get filtered to your spam folders.
