## What is posh authenticate
Posh Authenticate is our expert authentication service for select luxury items. Eligible items will have the “Authentication Included” badge on the listing page and will first be certified by Poshmark before they are delivered to you.
![Authentication badge example](https://d2gjrq7hs8he14.cloudfront.net/webpack5/section-what-is-cfbc66e26ee8dfea9f2d29ceb4e937d24ac2811a9dd9c1f09b2822c39c264967.png)

## FAQs
### What does the Posh Authenticate service mean for sellers?
When you make a sale, we will automatically route eligible items to Poshmark for authentication, while other items that are not eligible will ship directly to your buyer for faster delivery.
### How do I know if an item qualifies for the Posh Authenticate service?
Eligible items will have the “Authentication Included” badge on the listing page.
### What are the eligible categories for the Posh Authenticate service?
Only select luxury items sold for $500 or higher qualify for Posh Authenticate. All categories qualify, except for Electronics, Home, Pets, Toys, and Games.
### What if the item isn't eligible for Posh Authenticate?
If an item does not qualify for Posh Authenticate, it is still fully covered by [Posh Protect](https://poshmark.com/posh_protect).
### What if the item isn't approved by Posh Authenticate?
If our team cannot verify authenticity, the order is cancelled and a full refund will be immediately processed. The item will then be returned to the seller and it won’t be eligible to be relisted.
### How long will it take to complete the Posh Authenticate process?
Authenticating usually takes 1-3 business days once the item arrives at Poshmark. In some cases, it may take longer depending on volume or specific review needs.
### How can I check the status of an order that's being authenticated?
Your order tracking page will keep you informed as your order travels to Poshmark, as it undergoes the Posh Authenticate review, and then as it makes its way to the buyer.
### Are there additional fees for the Posh Authentication service?
No, Posh Authenticate is a free service for qualifying orders for both sellers and buyers.
