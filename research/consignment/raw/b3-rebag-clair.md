#  How do I sell using Clair? 
  * 3 months ago
  * Updated


Rebag makes it possible to move on from unwanted baggage the second you’re ready. Check out this video below for a quick tutorial! 
It's as easy as 1-2-3.
**1) Find your bag - select you bag's brand and model from your list**
**2) Tell its history - Tell us some details about your bag**
**3) Reveal its value - Generate its current resale value in an instant**
If we can’t provide you an instant offer, you can submit images of your item and our team will review and provide an offer it in 3-4 business days. Get an offer through our mobile app, online at Rebag.com, or in store at any [Rebag store location](https://rebag.com/store/).
