### Ways To Sell
## In Store
We make selling as easy and flexible as possible.
We evaluate and price clothing on the spot, so you can receive cash or trade right away.
When your buy is complete, your balance is automatically added to your Crossroads digital account and available at all of our store locations.
Even better, you can take _some_ or _all_ of your account balance at any time. Take cash on one visit and use trade on another. The choice is yours!
Join the waitlist from home using the [Crossroads Trading Waitlist App](https://crossroadstrading.com/buy-sell-trade/app/).
Must be 18 (or accompanied by an adult) [& present a valid photo ID to sell](https://crossroadstrading.com/privacy-policy/#:~:text=if%20You%20wish%20to%20sell%20merchandise%20in%20Our%20stores%2C%20we%20require%20You%20to%20provide%20Us%20a%20valid%20government%20ID%2C%20and%20we%20are%20required%20to%20maintain%20a%20copy%20of%20Your%20government%20ID%20to%20comply%20with%20local%20and%20state%20regulations%20governing%20resale%20businesses.). Not available at our outlet store in Stockton.

### Ways To Sell
## Drop Off
Most of our stores offer drop-off service. Simply let a buyer know you’d like to drop off your items, complete a brief form and be on your way. Sellers have the option to return for a cash or trade payout after 48-72 hours (varies by store) or have their payout held in their Crossroads digital account until their next visit. In some stores, sellers must return to collect their unpurchased items within 3 days or choose to have them donated. In other stores, sellers do not have the option to return to collect items. Ask your store for their policy.
Not all stores offer this service. Click “View All Locations” and select your store to check if they offer drop-off selling.
[View All Locations](https://crossroadstrading.com/store-locations/ "Locations")

#### Cash
When you sell your clothes for cash in our stores, you receive payment on the spot. We pay 25-40% based on what we price your items to sell for in the store. That means premium cash payouts for your premium pieces.
![cash payout table](https://crossroadstrading.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Website-Graphics-1-300x138.png)
#### Trade
When you trade at Crossroads, you’re choosing to receive trade credit that’s good at any of our stores. We pay 45% in trade of what we price your items to sell for in the store.

#### Clean
Do you buy dirty clothing? Neither do our customers. We can only accept clean, unstained, odor-free items in excellent condition.

#### Current
Just like you, our customers want on-trend, in-style items. So bring us that amazing designer dress you bought that doesn’t quite fit, or that jacket you loved but just don’t wear.

#### Condition
Details matter! If it’s missing a button, has a tiny tear, or the zipper’s broken, we can’t buy it. It pays to make sure your items are in great shape.
