## Frequently Asked Questions
Here are a few common questions about selling on Chairish 
  * How does payment work? 
Payment is issued via PayPal following delivery and the 48 hour return period. You can confirm that payment is being processed when an item shows as 'Delivered' in the 'My Sold Items' portion of the dashboard. 
  * How can I build my shop's trust and reputation? 
Be honest and transparent about your item's flaws. Quickly respond to Q&A and verified offers from buyers. 
  * What should I do if my listed item is no longer available for sale? 
If your item becomes unavailable for sale, deactivate it from your Chairish dashboard immediately so you don't end up having a very disappointed buyer. 


  * How can I ensure a hassle-free delivery? 
For smaller items include packing costs in your pricing when listing. Then carefully package your items for shipping & use the Chairish-provided shipping label to mail promptly. For larger items be available during business hours for furniture pickup. 
  * I'm a professional seller. Can I use Chairish to sell my items? 
Yes! If you are a professional seller who regularly buys and sells items at your own shop, flea markets, online, or through social media, then we'd love to connect you with a knowledgeable member of our Sales & Merchandising team. Please contact us at sales@chairish.com.
