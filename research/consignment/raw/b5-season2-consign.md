## FAQ
![](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0428/7378/2424/files/IMG_0782.png?v=1764723256)How long does the process take?
Once we receive your items, authentication and processing typically take 2–3 days. Most items sell in the first 30 days, although we make no guarantees.
![](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0428/7378/2424/files/IMG_0782.png?v=1764723256)How do payouts work?
You are paid electronically on the 10th day of the month after the item sells. Ex. January sales are paid on or before February 10th.
![](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0428/7378/2424/files/IMG_0782.png?v=1764723256)Items are subject to authentication & condition review.
Every item undergoes a multi-step verification process by our team of specialists before being listed. Counterfeits incur a $250 fee.
![](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0428/7378/2424/files/IMG_0782.png?v=1764723256)Commission rates as per chart above.
Your payout remains unchanged unless our team confirms such change with you or a change in required for sale.
![](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0428/7378/2424/files/IMG_0782.png?v=1764723256)What happens to items that don’t sell?
Items unsold after 90 days may be returned to you upon request. Items are considered abandoned on the 91st day after the initial agreement term is complete.
![](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0428/7378/2424/files/IMG_0782.png?v=1764723256)Does Season 2 accept every item?
We reserve the right to refuse pieces that do not meet our standards.
