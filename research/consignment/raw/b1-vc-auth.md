#  Seller: Selling with authentication 
If an item is purchased with authenticated shipping, you'll ship it directly to our warehouse to undergo Authentication & Quality Control.
You’ll be notified of sales via email and in the app. If we've provided you with a shipping label, you must ship your item within 7 days. If you do not ship the sold item within this time, the sale will be cancelled and the buyer refunded.
Find out how to print your prepaid shipping label 🔍**[here](https://faq.vestiairecollective.com/hc/en-us/articles/208531049)**, or click**🔍[here](https://faq.vestiairecollective.com/hc/en-us/articles/209245105)** for a guide to preparing your item for shipping. We should receive your item within 2-7 days of shipping.
![](https://faq.vestiairecollective.com/hc/article_attachments/34589310460177)
**During Authentication & Quality Control**
Once your item arrives at the warehouse, our expert teams will check, approve, package, and ship it to the buyer within 2 business days. (During busy periods of the year, it may take a few more days.)
Their findings will be available in the Authentication & Quality Control Report, which we send to you via email and within app once the review is complete. Learn more about Authentication & Quality Control.
If our experts notice your item differs from its listing in regards to authenticity or conformity, your sale could be cancelled. To avoid cancellation, follow our [**catalogue rules**](https://faq.vestiairecollective.com/hc/en-us/articles/16540741718033).
Important: Please do not include any liquids in your description, photographs, or in any packages you ship.
Learn more about **[Authentication& Quality Control](https://www.cjoint.com/doc/22_10/LJzm7kKEnIb_TRUST-REPORT-OCT2022-EN-final+.pdf).**
**Payment**
We issue payment the next business day after Authentication & Quality Control is complete (excluding Saturdays, Sundays, and bank holidays). It will reflect in your account within 5 business days depending on your payout method.
To learn more about payments 🔍**[click here](https://faq.vestiairecollective.com/hc/en-us/articles/209304765)**.
