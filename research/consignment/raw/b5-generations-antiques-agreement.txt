The Consignor hereby warrants, represents and attests that he/she is at least 18 years of age, is the owner of all goods cons igned to 
Generations; and has the legal right to enter into this Consignment Agreement; that the goods are saleable and/or free & clear of liens,

Generations offers two consignment options. By circling and initialing the option below, the consignor agrees to that consignment 
option offered by Generations: 
 
Option A 50/50 split.  50% of the selling price to the consignor and 50% of the selling price to 
Generations.  This option is for those consignors who have a set amount that they want to 
receive for their item and will not allow Generations to accept any offers lower than the 
asking price. 
 
Option B 60/40 split.  60% of the selling price to the consignor and 40% of the selling price to 
Generations. This option is for those consignors who have allowed Generations to 
negotiate and accept offers from buyers that are 80% or greater of the asking price for their 
item. 
 
Generations reserves the right to determine the actual selling price of all items. The price will not be changed during the initial 44 day  
consignment period unless agreed to by the consignor by choosing option 2.  All prices are subject to a 30% reduction on day 45. 
 
Consignor acknowledges that Generations is not a certified appraiser of antiques and/or collectibles. Every effort will be ma de to 
fairly price and profitably consign items based on a number of factors including age, condition, quality , quantity, brand name and 
market prices for comparable merchandise.  
 
Generations reserves the right to attempt to sell any item through any internet source.  Any and all proceeds above the store  tag price 
shall be added to the proceeds of Generations.  The consignor agrees that they are not due any additional portion of proceeds above the 
store tag price.  Items sold on the internet shall not be sold below the store tag price (or discounted price after 44 days).

Consignor agrees that any item consigned to Generations may be displayed whenever and wherever Generations chooses.  
 
Consignor acknowledges that Generations will have a monetary investment in time, space and advertising in selling consignors items. 
Consignor agrees not to remove consigned items prior to the end of the 60 day consignment period. If a consigned item is removed 
prior to the end of the consignment period, the consignee agrees to pay Generations a fee of 15% of the asking sale price of the 
consigned item plus any additional costs incurred by the consignment. 
 
Consignors will be paid after their item has sold. Payment will be made on the 10th of each month for the previous month’s sales for 
balances over $25.  Payments are available for pickup in person at Generations or used at any time for store credit.  Cash payments 
may be available at the discretion of Generations.  We do not automatically mail checks .  If you prefer to have a check mailed to you, 
there is a $1.00 mailing fee.  Payments not picked up after 3 months will be expired and forfeited.  Checks that are picked up will not 
be reissued under any circumstance. 
 
Generations has set up an access account document via google docs so that you may track your sales and payments.  You will be 
added to this account via email notification from Generations.  Others may call Todd at 440-628-818 to inquire if an item has sold or 
not. 
 
Consignor acknowledges that Generations maintains fire and liability insurance coverage only. All reasonable care will be tak en to 
ensure the safety of items consigned with Generations, however, items are left at the consignor’s risk. Consignor acknowledges that 
Generations is open to the public and is not responsible for any loss due to damage, theft , fire, water, casualty or natural disaster. 
 
It is the responsibility of the consignor to notify Generations of any change of contact telephone number or address.  
 
All consignment items must be clean, in displayable condition and ready for resale.  Generations may charge a fee up to $10 i f an item 
requires additional cleaning or repair.  The cost of this work, if necessary, will be deducted from the consignor’s portion of  the sale 
proceeds. 
  
Although we cannot guarantee that each item will sell, Generations will make every effort to correctly price each item and sell each 
item consigned within the consignment period. 
 
It is the responsibility of the consignor to know when the consignment period expires. Upon expiration of the consignment per iod, the 
consignor has seven calendar (7) days to pick up any unsold items.  
 
You must notify us at least one calendar day in advance that you are coming to retrieve your unsold item(s). We need this tim e to pull 
your item(s) and have them ready for you to pick up. 
 
Unless prior arrangements have been made and agreed upon in writing by both the consignor and Generations any item left for more 
than seven (7) calendar days after the end date (SEE LAST PICKUP DATE ABOVE)  of the consignment period will be considered 
abandoned and become the sole property of Generations and may be disposed of or donated to any charity of choice. After 67 days 
from the consigned date, Generations has NO FURTHER OBLIGATION TO THE CONSIGNOR for account inquiries, accounting 
information or contract settlements.
