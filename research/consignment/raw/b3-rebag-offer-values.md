#  How do you determine offer values? 
  * 6 years ago
  * Updated


After multiple years of operation, we have accumulated an extensive proprietary database that enables us to provide competitive offers.
We consider a variety of factors including, but not limited to: brand, condition size, color, material, resale price, popularity, our own inventory and how well it sells on the resale market.
