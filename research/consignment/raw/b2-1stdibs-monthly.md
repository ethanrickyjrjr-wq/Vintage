#  What Is the Monthly Fee for 1stDibs? 
By bringing together many of [the world’s best sellers and galleries](https://www.1stdibs.com/seller-directory/) specializing in [furniture](https://www.1stdibs.com/furniture/), [art](https://www.1stdibs.com/art/), [fine jewelry](https://www.1stdibs.com/jewelry/) and [fashion](https://www.1stdibs.com/fashion/), our global marketplace offers buyers unparalleled access to the most beautiful things on earth. As a seller on 1stDibs, you’ll reach millions of discerning collectors, sophisticated customers and top interior designers around the world.
The monthly fee for a seller on 1stDibs can vary depending on the components of a given membership plan. 1stDibs offers multiple membership plans from which a seller can select to best suit their business. A seller can select either a low monthly subscription fee and a higher per-sale commission rate or a plan with a higher monthly subscription fee and a lower per-sale commission rate.
The 1stDibs scale enables us to provide competitive shipping rates for sellers worldwide. We also handle logistics, which saves you valuable time. Our fraud and chargeback protection helps ensure safe online transactions and reduces risk for businesses that sell merchandise on 1stDibs. And of course, our team is here to support sellers throughout the selling process and to assist in resolving any disputes.
We provide the tools that sellers need to grow a successful online business, remain organized and optimize their offerings. 1stDibs also offers expert coaching and resources that allow sellers to optimize their business on our marketplace.
If you're interested in becoming a seller on 1stDibs, you must [submit an application](https://www.1stdibs.com/dealer/application-form/) and provide at least two references who can verify you are a seller in good standing and can adhere to strict standards for service and quality. All prospective sellers must then be approved and selected to become a [vetted seller](https://www.1stdibs.com/about/vetted-sellers/) on our marketplace.
