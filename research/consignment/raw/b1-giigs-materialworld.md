  * Sell used clothing, handbags, shoes, and accessories for cash, or trade in for Material World shopping credit, paid upfront, without having to wait for items to sell.
  * How it works:
    1. Get a free trade-in kit with prepaid, insured UPS shipping label, poly-mailer bag, and closet clean out guidelines.
    2. Drop your package off at any UPS office.
    3. Material make you an offer upfront, up to 70% of the estimated resale value (no consignment - no having to wait for your items to sell).
    4. You accept or pass on the offer.
    5. Payment by PayPal (3% transaction fee), or Material World shopping credit.
    6. For items not accepted, either donate to charity and get a tax deductible receipt, or get them returned to you, free of charge.
  * Refer a friend:
    * You earn 10% commission on their trade-in values for a year.
    * Your friend earns an additional $25 on their first trade-in.
  * Material World want:
    * women's clothing, handbags, shoes, and accessories
    * men's clothing, handbags, shoes, and accessories
    * maternity clothing
    * excellent to like-new condition
    * freshly laundered, stain and defect free
  * Material World do not want:
    * jewelry
    * watches
    * swimwear
    * intimates
    * leggings
    * children's wear
  * 230+ accepted designer and contemporary brands, such as:
    * Alexander Wang
    * Balenciaga
    * Chanel
    * Louis Vuitton
    * Missoni
  * Quality standards:
    * Clothing:
      * Excellent condition
      * Styles within the last 5 years
      * No stains or marks
      * No holes or tears
      * No alterations
      * No replicas or missing brand labels
    * Handbags:
      * Excellent pre-owned condition
      * Styles within the last 10 years
      * No broken handles, straps, or zippers
      * No scuffed corners, darkened handles, or tears
