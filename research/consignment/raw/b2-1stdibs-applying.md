#  Applying to Sell on 1stDibs 
1stDibs works with more than 4,000 trusted sellers from around the world. Unlike other online marketplaces, we only allow sellers who are professionals in their fields. All sellers undergo a comprehensive evaluation by our in-house experts to ensure the integrity of their listings and the quality of their service.
## How the Process Works
Prospective sellers should submit an [application](https://www.1stdibs.com/dealer/application-form/) to 1stDibs and provide at least two references who can verify they are a business in good standing, with top-quality inventory to match our standards.
Applications are reviewed by our experts in contemporary and vintage furniture, fine art, jewelry and fashion. All sellers are evaluated on the appropriateness and quality of their inventory. This may involve requests for samples to assess a collection, in-person visits to prospective sellers’ workshops, audits of social media accounts and industry editorial coverage — as well as a review of their interactions with customers to gauge their commitment to service and their reputation within the industry.
