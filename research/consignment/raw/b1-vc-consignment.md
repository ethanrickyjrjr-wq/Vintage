#  Seller: The Consignment Service - What It Is and How It Works 
**If you’re looking for a seamless experience and a zero hassle way to sell your incredible pre-loved wardrobe, then our Consignment Service is for you.**
Currently available in the EU, UK, and US, our Consignment Service takes all the effort out of selling. Here’s everything you need to know about and how it works.
### We take care of everything 
With the Consignment Service, we offer a 360° service for each and every item you want to sell.
First, we’ll arrange an appointment (digitally or in person) to select the items most likely to sell. From then on, your Account Manager will be on-hand every step of the way to organise:
  * The pick-up of the items you want to sell
  * Expert photography of your items that will grab buyers’ attention
  * Pitch-perfect item descriptions, with all the details that buyers love
  * Market-rate pricing, based on our pricing expertise
  * Safe storage of your item until it sells
  * Shipment of sold items to your buyer


For a guide on which items we do (and don’t) accept for Consignment, [**click here**](https://faq.vestiairecollective.com/hc/en-gb/articles/5837220788369).
**Good to know** : At the moment, our Consignment Service is only available in the EU, UK, and US.
### Am I eligible for the Consignment Service?
Let’s find out! Just answer these quick questions:
  * Are you based in the EU, UK, or US? 
  * Do you have at least 5 items you want to sell?
  * Are the items from luxury or designer brands?
  * Are they in very good condition?


If you answered **yes** to all of the questions above, then you’re eligible for our Consignment Service. Just fill out [**the contact form**](https://fr.vestiairecollective.com/consignment/) and one of our dedicated account managers will reach out to start the process.
**Good to know** : If you prefer your account to be completely anonymous, we can take care of that for you, too.
### How much will I earn with the Consignment Service?
You will earn up to **80%** of the item price. To get an idea of our Consignment Service Commission rates, see the latest information below.

### What if my Consignment Service item doesn’t sell?
It’s rare that Consignment Items don’t sell, (who wouldn’t want your gorgeous pieces?!) but here are your options in this unlikely event.
If you’d like your item returned, we will charge**€20 per item.**

Submit a consignment request through our [Consignment Service page](https://www.vestiairecollective.com/consignment/).
