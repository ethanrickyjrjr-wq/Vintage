#  Determining Shipping Method 
At Chairish, we want you to know exactly how your item is being shipped, whether you're still deciding on a purchase or already have one on the way.
  * [Before Purchase](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/46388694404881-Determining-Shipping-Method#h_01KQWCQ3MZ1S0BHT8NXYXXAT6H)
  * [After Purchase](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/46388694404881-Determining-Shipping-Method#h_01KQWCSAFDXAQQMFWWZ8BF9FXN)


## Before Purchase
* * *
Before purchasing an item, you can see the shipping methods available by clicking on the **Calculate Shipping Costs** link under the **Details** section of the item listing.
![ShippingMethod1.png](https://support.chairish.com/hc/article_attachments/46388694402321)
In the example below, this item uses Seller-Managed shipping, or can be picked up from the city listed:
![ShippingMethod2.png](https://support.chairish.com/hc/article_attachments/46388678985489)
In this example, the item can be sent via Chairish-Managed shipping, Seller-Managed shipping, or can be picked up from the city listed: 
![ShippingMethod3.png](https://support.chairish.com/hc/article_attachments/46388694403345)
Click [here to learn more about the available shipping methods](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44246519895697).
## After Purchase
* * *
After purchasing an item from Chairish, you will receive an email confirming your order and listing the shipping method you selected at checkout. 
![PurchaseConfirmation1.png](https://support.chairish.com/hc/article_attachments/46389310664209)
**Didn't receive a purchase confirmation email?** Click [here](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44253762105105-My-Purchases) for troubleshooting steps.
You can also view the shipping method by logging into your Chairish account and navigating to your 'My Purchases' page. Click on the **Details** button to the right of the applicable order.
  * Click [here to learn more about the available shipping methods](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44246519895697).


Items shipped via Seller-Managed shipping will say 'Seller Managed Delivery' in the **Shipping Method** field. 
![ShippingMethod4.png](https://support.chairish.com/hc/article_attachments/46392346651409)
Items shipped via Chairish-Managed shipping will either list the carrier's name or say 'Pending Shipment' in the **Shipping Method** field.
![ShippingMethod5.png](https://support.chairish.com/hc/article_attachments/46392565638673)
