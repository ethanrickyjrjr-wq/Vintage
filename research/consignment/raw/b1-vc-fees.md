#  Seller: Selling Fees 
Listing your items is free on Vestiaire Collective. Once you make a sale, a Selling Fee of 17% will be deducted from the final price of your sale.
**What you get**
  * Access to a global community of fashion lovers
  * Hassle-free shipping with prepaid shipping labels
  * Advanced pricing and selling tools
  * Expert customer support at every step of the selling process

**For Sellers in the US listing in USD**  
| 
#### **Price range**
 | 
#### **Selling fees**
 | 
#### **Payment processing fees**
 |  
| --- | --- | --- |  
| **[0 USD - 75 USD]**  | **12 USD**  | **3 USD**  |  
| **[75 USD - 15 000 USD]**  | **17%**  | **3%**  |  
| **[15 000 USD +]**  | **2 500 USD**  | **3%**  |  
All items priced in USD between 75 USD and 15 000 USD have a selling fee of **17%**. Items priced under 75 USD have a fixed selling fee of **12 USD**. Items costing more than 15 000 USD have a fixed selling fee of **2 500 USD.**
A payment processing fee of 3% (min 3 USD) will also be charged on your sale to cover the external processing costs of your transaction.
**Good to know:** We cannot accept items listed for sale on Vestiaire Collective for **less than $18 USD.**
**For Sellers in Canada listing in CAD**  
| 
#### **Price range**
 | 
#### **Selling fees**
 | 
#### **Payment processing fees**
 |  
| --- | --- | --- |  
| **[0 CAD - 120 CAD]**  | **19 CAD**  | **5 CAD**  |  
| **[120 CAD - 24 000 CAD]**  | **17 %**  | **3 %**  |  
| **[24 000 CAD +]**  | **4 000 CAD**  | **3 %**  |  
All items priced in CAD between 120 CAD and 24 000 CAD have a selling fee of **17%**. Items priced under 120 CAD have a fixed selling fee of **19 CAD**. Items costing more than 24 000 CAD have a fixed selling fee of **4 000 CAD**.
A payment processing fee of 3% (min 5 CAD) will also be charged on your sale to cover the external processing costs of your transaction.
**Good to know** : We cannot accept items listed for sale on Vestiaire Collective for **less than $34 CAD.**
Learn more about minimum amounts for international bank transfers by clicking[ **here**](https://faq.vestiairecollective.com/hc/en-us/articles/27765027362833-Seller-Minimum-amounts-to-receive-wire-transfers)
*These selling fees apply to all new items listed from October, 15th 2026 onwards.
