#  Problem with an Item Received

## Wrong Item Received/Item Not as Expected
* * *
If you received the wrong item, or the item's features differ from the listing (ex. missing lever, different cushions, incorrect dimensions), [contact us](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44209356634257) to report this within 48 hours, per our [Return Policy](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44246824574865).
What to include:
  * Clear full-item photos from multiple angles.
  * Clear close-ups showing the differing features.
  * Photos of the box, internal packaging, and shipping label.
  * Screenshots or links to the listing photos that show what you expected.


Next steps:
  * We’ll review for misrepresentation. If confirmed, we'll arrange a return at no cost to you and a full refund. A replacement may be offered if available.
  * If the difference is limited to subjective color variation due to lighting or screens, see [below](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/45740860530193-Problem-with-an-Item-Received#h_01KP69YVGJAGAWK95TKHME6DDP).


For issues with the shipping or delivery of an item (for example, if an item arrived damaged), click [here](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44246709644689).
## Color/Finish Discrepancy
* * *
To assess a color/finish discrepancy fairly, we review both the listing and your evidence. Complete, clear documentation helps us evaluate color/finish concerns quickly.
When contacting Support with color/finish concerns, please provide the following: 
  * Full-item photos from at least two different angles in neutral, natural light (ex. near a window, midday) or under white (5000-6500K) bulbs. Avoid filters or flash if possible.
  * Avoid heavy shadows, colored walls reflecting onto the item, or strong warm bulbs that shift color.
  * Close-up photos of the areas where the color/finish looks different, taken in the same lighting as the full-item photos.
  * A simple neutral reference in-frame (ex. a plain white sheet of paper) to help gauge color balance. If you have a color card, you may include it, but it’s not required.
  * A screenshot or link to the listing image(s) you’re comparing against, and a note identifying the specific photo(s).
  * Any labels, manufacturer tags, color/finish codes, or product markings visible on the item.
  * Photos of the shipping box and internal packing materials. Keep these until we confirm next steps.
  * Contextual details that may affect appearance (ex. type of indoor lighting used for your photos, time of day, camera/device used).


What we consider:
  * **Listing text vs. images** : We weigh the written description and any stated color/finish name alongside listing photos.
  * **Your photo conditions** : We rely on photos taken in neutral, natural light or under white bulbs, with no filters, to reduce lighting bias.
  * **Material behavior** : Reflective metals, high-gloss lacquers, natural woods, and hand-dyed textiles can shift with angle and light; minor shifts are expected.
  * **Device variability** : We account for known differences among screens and color profiles.


Our standard:
  * We look for a material difference between the delivered item and the listing description/images, not a subtle variance attributable to lighting or screens.
  * When the listing clearly states a specific color/finish and the delivered item does not match, we will treat this as a discrepancy and work toward a remedy.


Outcome:
  * If the evidence supports a material discrepancy within the return window, we'll authorize a [return ](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44246824574865)and provide next steps.
  * If the variance appears consistent with normal lighting or screen differences, we may decline a misrepresentation claim but can still discuss return options subject to shipping and packing costs.


## Questioning Authenticity
* * *
We take authenticity seriously. If you suspect an item is not genuine, contact us within 48 hours of delivery and provide detailed evidence. Click [here ](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/45470683260561)to learn more about our Authenticity Guarantee and how to report concerns.  

## Replacement Parts
* * *
Unfortunately, Chairish does not sell replacement parts. If you need a part for an item you previously purchased on Chairish, you can perform a search on Chairish again to see if similar items are being sold that may provide the parts you need.
## Disposal Authorization Process
* * *
Our priority is to protect buyers, sellers, and our carrier partners. Items with active mold/mildew, pest infestations, strong or persistent odors, or other biohazards are not eligible for sale on Chairish and will not be re-sold if discovered post-purchase.
If you receive an item that appears to contain mold, mildew, infestation, biohazards, etc., contact our Support team right away and include the order number, photos, and a brief description of the issue (ex. odor, visible growth). 
Do not dispose of any item until you receive written authorization from Chairish. In many cases we will instruct disposal rather than return shipment to prevent contamination.
If Chairish authorizes disposal, refunds or other resolutions are handled per our [Buyer Guarantee](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44253417033617).
#### _**Item Disposal**_
When disposal is appropriate, Chairish will issue a disposal authorization ("disposal ticket"). This documents the item, order, and instructions so that everyone is aligned.
You may be asked to provide:
  * Photos/video of the item showing the issue and full views
  * A photo of the item immediately prior to disposal (ex. sealed for transport)
  * A simple receipt or confirmation from the disposal site, when available


Follow local regulations for disposal, such as:
  * **Textiles** : Seal in plastic before moving. Avoid shaking or compressing.
  * **Upholstery and rugs** : Wrap in plastic sheeting/tape. Keep separate from clean items.
  * **Wood or case goods** : Wrap, minimize handling indoors, and dispose per municipal rules.
