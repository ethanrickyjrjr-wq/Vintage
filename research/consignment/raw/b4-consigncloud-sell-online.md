## Everything you need to sell online
###### Real-Time Inventory Sync
Item quantities sync between ConsignCloud and your online store, so nothing sells twice.
###### Online-Ready Listings
Photos and rich text descriptions sync straight to your storefront.
###### Splits on Every Sale
Online sales credit the consignor’s balance, just like sales in your store.
###### More Sales Channels
Through Shopify, list on Facebook, Instagram, eBay, and more.
###### Included on Every Plan
Shopify and Square integrations come with every ConsignCloud plan.

## FAQs
Which online platforms does ConsignCloud work with?
Shopify and Square. Through Shopify, you can also reach other platforms like Facebook, Instagram, and eBay.
Do online integrations cost extra?
No. Shopify and Square integrations come with every ConsignCloud plan. You’ll need your own Shopify or Square account.
Will an item that sells online still show as available in store?
No. Item quantities sync in real time, so every item lives in one place.
How do consignor splits work on online sales?
The same as in store. ConsignCloud calculates the consignor’s share and updates their balance automatically.
Can I use the Shopify or Square POS in my store?
Yes. Once you connect Shopify or Square, you can ring up sales on their POS instead of ConsignCloud’s, and everything stays in sync.
