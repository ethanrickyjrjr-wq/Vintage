#  Seller Expectations 
  * You are responsible for the quality of your item and its listing, including accurate product information, photos, and condition. 
    * You agree to accurately describe each item to the best of your ability. Chairish reserves the right to revise your listing for grammatical errors, photo editing, and other small corrections.
  * You must keep inventory current (de-listing items that are no longer available); ship in a timely manner; mark shop in [vacation mode](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44173926349329) when away for extended periods; respond to [offers ](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44173070844689)in a timely manner.
  * You must ensure that the condition and quality of your item is suitable for resale. 
    * Please ensure that your item is clean and odor free, with the exception of any condition you have clearly disclosed in writing or per photographs in the listing description.
  * Chairish reserves the right to inspect or reject any item as unsuitable for sale at any time.
  * If Chairish finds a buyer for your item and you circumvent or go around Chairish in an attempt to avoid payment of the Commission to Chairish, by selling to the buyer developed by Chairish directly, this will be treated as a sale and the Commission will be due and payable to Chairish.
  * You covenant, represent, and warrant that you have all rights, authority, and clearances necessary for Chairish to sell the item on your behalf, and perform your rights and obligations under our [Terms of Service](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44208742405649).
  * You will give Chairish or a buyer any documents necessary to transfer the ownership of the item to the buyer.
  * You are solely responsible for all aspects of dealing with your clients and your business (ex. paying or getting paid by your design clients, handling logistics between you and your clients, and managing your business), and handling all related inquiries and disputes.
  * You as a seller are aware of and understand the Chairish[ cancellation policy](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44246643739537) & [Return Policy](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44246824574865).
  * You as a seller are aware of and abide by our [Terms of Service](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44208742405649)[.](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/115007071507-Terms-of-Service)
