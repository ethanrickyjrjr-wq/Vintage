#  How and when do I get paid? 
  * 18 days ago
  * Updated


**Buyout**
Processing is estimated to take approximately two to three weeks after we receive your item. Your payout is then added to your Rebag account within approximately 40 business days after Processing is complete.
**Consignment**
When your item sells, the sale remains pending for up to 22 business days. During that window, if the item is returned or the order is canceled, the sale is not confirmed and your item is relisted. If neither happens, the sale is automatically confirmed at the end of the pending period. Your payout is then added to your Rebag Account within approximately 15 business days after the confirmation date
**Using your balance**
Once added to your account, your balance can be used immediately to purchase items on Rebag online, in the app, or in our stores. Applying your balance toward a purchase also earns you a 10% Spending Bonus.  

**Bonus**
Once your balance becomes available, it automatically increases by 1% per month for up to 9 months for up to a 9% Bonus.  

**Transferring your balance to your bank**
Transfers are not instant and can only be operated during business hours. Once a transfer request is accepted, funds are estimated to reach your bank within approximately five to seven business days. Transfer requests are subject to daily processing limits. In the limited circumstances in which your request is not processed, please resubmit the following business day. You can also log in to Rebag.com and chat with our customer service team, who can provide assistance. Please note that bonus payouts continue to accrue and remain fully available until your balance is transferred.  

**Early Transfer Fee**
Balances transferred within 15 calendar days of being added to your account are subject to a 3% Early Transfer Fee, subtracted from your requested amount. After 15 calendar days, your balance is eligible for a free transfer.  

**What if my transfer doesn't show up?**
If funds don't appear in your bank account within the expected window, your bank may have encountered a processing issue, most often a mismatch between the bank details on file and your bank's records. Check that your account and routing information are correct, and that your account accepts electronic transfers in the currency of your country of residence; if necessary, remove your bank account and add it again with the correct details. You can update your bank account and routing information in the payments section of your account. If a transfer fails, we'll add the funds back to your Rebag account and notify you by email. Still missing funds after that? You can also log in to Rebag.com and chat with our customer service team, who can provide assistance
  
**A note on timing**
All timeframes above are estimates. Actual timing may be shorter or longer. Review our [Terms of ](https://rebag.com/terms/)
[Service](https://rebag.com/terms/) for full details.
