# How can I get paid by Rebag?
Written by Marcos
Updated over a week ago
# **Seller Payment Methods**
**WHAT PAYMENT METHODS DO YOU OFFER?**
Rebag offers payment as Rebag Credit or as Proceeds deposited to your Rebag Account.
  1. Rebag Credit is available once payment is processed.
  2. Proceeds are deposited to your Rebag Account Balance on the timeline below. You can shop with your Balance right away or Transfer it to your bank account.


Keep your Proceeds working for you: once your Rebag balance becomes available it automatically increases by 1% per month for up to 9 months, generating up to 9% bonus.
**HOW LONG DOES IT TAKE TO GET PAID?**
**Buyout**
  * Processing is estimated to take approximately two to three weeks after we receive your item. Your payout is then deposited to your Rebag account within approximately **40 business days** after Processing is complete.


**Consignment**
  * When your item sells, the sale remains pending for up to 22 business days. During that window, if the item is returned or the order is canceled, the sale is not confirmed and your item is relisted. If neither happens, the sale is automatically confirmed at the end of the pending period. Your payout is then added to your Rebag Account within approximately **15 business days** after the confirmation date.


**Incentives**
  * Once your Rebag balance becomes available it automatically increases by 1% per month for up to 9 months, generating up to 9% bonus.


**Transfer**
  * Transfers are not instant and can only be operated during business hours. Once a transfer request is accepted, funds are estimated to reach your bank within **approximately 5 to 7 business days**.


**Early Transfer**
  * Balances transferred within 15 calendar days of deposit are subject to a 3% Early Transfer Fee.


**Timeframes**
  * All timeframes are estimates, and actual timing may be shorter or longer. Please review our Terms of Service. Buyout orders accepted before October 8, 2026 are not affected by this change.


**WHAT INFORMATION IS NEEDED FOR BANK TRANSFERS?**  
We need your bank account and routing numbers. Please note, Rebag will only issue payments to accounts that match the name of the person completing the order.
If your estimated window has passed, log in on Rebag.com and chat with our Client Services team, or email contact@rebag.com.
