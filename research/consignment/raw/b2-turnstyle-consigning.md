## Consignment Hours
Consignments are taken on a first-come, first-served basis — except for Thursdays and Saturdays, which are reserved for appointments only. Consigning begins when we open and ends 90 minutes before we close. Please limit walk-ins to 25 items or fewer. Walk-in consignment hours are subject to change based on inventory levels.
If you’d like to make an appointment to consign on Thursdays or Saturdays, please call your [**preferred Turn Style location**](https://www.turnstyleconsign.com/locations/). They’ll be happy to set up your consigning appointment.
Have questions? Check out our FAQ section below.
[Learn More](https://www.turnstyleconsign.com/consigning/#faq)
## How to Consign
Before bringing in your items, take a moment to familiarize yourself with our consignment process. This will help you prepare, giving you the best shot at having your items accepted. Learn how to consign clothes, accessories, furniture and home decor items in five easy steps.
## Delivery and Pickup of Large Items
Turn Style offers [pick-up and delivery](https://www.turnstyleconsign.com/delivery/) of used consignment furniture, for all of our Minnesota, Colorado and Arizona locations. Fees begin at $85 and vary depending on the number of items, size of items and distance traveled. Please call your local store for more information.
If you are bringing in your used furniture into one of our furniture consignment stores on your own, you are solely responsible for carrying your furniture items into and out of the store. Sales associates cannot assist in moving furniture. Call and speak to an associate prior to bringing in your furniture item(s).
![Customer and employee reviewing items](https://www.turnstyleconsign.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/consignment-image.jpg)
## Terms and conditions
Store personnel will determine the selling price of your items, usually one-third to one- fourth of retail value taking into account quality, condition, demand, and age of the item. You must choose the option to receive 45% of the selling price before tax and item fees if paid by check, or 55% of the selling price before tax and item fees if you choose to be paid as a store credit. For any item(s) which sell for $200.00 or higher, the consignor will receive 60% of the selling price before tax and items fees.
All merchandise may be marked down an additional 50%, 60 days after receipt or during clearance events. Furniture items may be discounted an additional 25%, 30 days after receipt. After 90 days or the expiration date indicated on the consignment receipt, items that have not been sold or picked up by you will become the property of Turn Style. If you choose to pick up your unsold merchandise prior to the expiration date indicated on the consignment receipt, you may locate and retrieve your items from the sales floor and there will be a $1 per item pull charge. You are solely responsible for locating goods on the sales floor.
Items that are discovered to be damaged or flawed after initial inspection and acceptance will be sold at a discount or donated to charity. Please note that we do not contact you in the event this occurs. Turn Style reserves the right to discount merchandise during sale events.
All items are left at your risk. Turn Style is not responsible for missing or damaged items.
If you choose the check option, checks can be picked up the store of consignment at your request.
