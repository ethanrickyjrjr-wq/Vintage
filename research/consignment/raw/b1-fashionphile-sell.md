The Details 
###  What can I expect when I submit an offer? 
###### **Online Offer Steps:**
  1. Fill out our quick online form using a few photos and selecting your item’s designer, category and item name, along with a brief description of the condition.  
  

  2. We’ll email you with an offer within 7-10 business days. Accept your offer.  
  

  3. Send us your items with a complimentary shipping label or a free UPS Pick-Up.  
  

  4. Once received and authenticated, your payout will be deposited in your FASHIONPHILE Wallet, where you can choose to cash out at anytime or enjoy a 10% bonus by converting your funds to store credit.   
  



**When will you receive your offer?**
Emailed to you 7-10 business days after submitting your item(s) for an offer.  
  

**How do you get the items get to us?**
Ship to us with a complimentary label, schedule a free UPS Pick-Up, drop it off at a Selling Studio near you.  
  

**How long does payout take?**
Estimated 2-4 days after your item is received and authenticated.
###  What can I expect when I visit a location? 
###### **Visit Us Steps:**
  1. Find a [location](https://9b6844-68.myshopify.com/pages/locations "Locations") near you and schedule an appointment.  
  

  2. Bring us your items and we will give you offers on the spot.  
  

  3. Get paid as soon as your item is authenticated.  
  



**When will you receive your offer?**
Live, during your appointment.  
  

**How do you get the items get to us?**
We can take your items during your appointment.  
  

**How long does payout take?**
Your payment is made as soon as your item is authenticated.
###  What can I expect from Luxury Sales? 
###### Luxury Sales Steps:
  1. Fill out our form to schedule your Luxury Sales appointment.  
  

  2. We’ll come to you and help you submit your luxury items and assist with finding your next “holy grail” accessory  
  

  3. You will receive a buyout offer for you to accept in your My Account dashboard. We will take the items you accept from the buyout offer and send them to our headquarters to be authenticated.  
  

  4. Once the items are authenticated, you get paid!  
  



**When will you receive your offer?**
Live during your appointment or shortly thereafter.  
  

**How do you get the items to us?**
We do the heavy lifting during the appointment and pick up the item from the location of your choosing.  
  

**How long does payout take?**
Estimated 2-4 days after your item is received and authenticated.
