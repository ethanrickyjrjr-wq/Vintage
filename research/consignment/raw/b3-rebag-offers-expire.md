#  Are offers free? Do offers expire? 
  * 6 years ago
  * Updated


Yes! Rebag is a free service, you are welcome to submit as many items as you like.
As the resale market is constantly fluctuating, our offers expire after two weeks. If you would like to sell your bag to Rebag after this timeframe, please get another offer for your item
