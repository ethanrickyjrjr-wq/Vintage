#  What is Rebag’s commission structure? 
  * 3 years ago
  * Updated


Commission rates, which are the fees that you pay, are based on your item's listing price and category. You’ll pay less for in-demand, high-value pieces (as low as 8%) and more for lower-value items. Most items will typically fall in the 15-25% range. Take a look at our Commission Rates chart for an indicative overview of rates broken out by category and net sales price. Actual commission rates will be be communicated individually for each quoted item
