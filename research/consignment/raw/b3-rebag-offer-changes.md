#  What happens if my offer changes after Rebag receives my item? 
  * 5 days ago
  * Updated


After we receive and inspect your item, we may adjust your offer based on its condition or other details confirmed during inspection. 
If your Buyout or Consignment offer changes, you’ll have 7 business days to accept or decline the revised offer. If no action is taken within that time, the revised offer will be automatically accepted.
