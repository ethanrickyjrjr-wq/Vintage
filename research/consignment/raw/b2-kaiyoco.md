## Kaiyo: Your Questions, Answered
### Everything You Need to Know About Shopping Pre-Owned Furniture the Smart Way
######  What exactly is Kaiyo, and what makes it different from a regular furniture store?
Kaiyo is a **pre-owned furniture marketplace** that connects buyers with high-quality, second-hand pieces — think designer sofas, mid-century chairs, dining sets, and more — all at a fraction of the original retail price. Unlike a typical thrift store, Kaiyo carefully curates and inspects every item, so you’re not digging through clutter. You’re browsing a **thoughtfully organized online catalog** of furniture that’s ready to find a new home.
######  How much can I actually save compared to buying new furniture?
Quite a lot, honestly. Many customers have reported savings of **50% to 90% off the original retail price** on items from well-known brands. Whether you’re hunting for a budget-friendly accent chair or a high-end designer sofa, Kaiyo’s pricing makes premium furniture genuinely accessible — without sacrificing quality or style.
######  Can I search for furniture by specific dimensions or price range on the website?
Yes, and this is one of Kaiyo’s most loved features! The website has a **powerful search engine that lets you filter by dimensions, price, style, and more**. So if you need a chair that’s exactly 40 inches tall, or a coffee table under $300, you can find it without endlessly scrolling. It’s a huge time-saver compared to hunting through estate sales or flea markets in person.
######  Does Kaiyo offer delivery, and what does the delivery experience look like?
Absolutely. Kaiyo offers **white glove delivery service** , meaning their team brings the furniture directly to your desired room, assembles it, and removes all packaging. Delivery teams like Raymond, Chi, Oscar, and others have been praised by customers for being **punctual, professional, and genuinely careful** with every piece. You’ll typically receive email notifications, a morning-of text, and a call when they arrive at your door.
######  Is there a warehouse pickup option if I want to save on delivery costs?
Yes! If you’re local or don’t mind making the trip, you can **schedule a pickup appointment at the Kaiyo warehouse** in Chino, California (14628 Yorba Ave, Chino, CA 91710). The warehouse is clean, well-organized, and staff are friendly and helpful. The appointment system is smooth — you’ll receive reminder emails leading up to your date, and your order will already be staged and ready when you arrive.
######  What are Kaiyo's warehouse hours?
The Kaiyo warehouse is open **Monday through Saturday, 6:30 AM to 3:00 PM** , and is closed on Sundays. It’s best to schedule your pickup appointment in advance through their website to ensure everything is ready for you when you arrive. Walk-ins during scheduled hours may be accommodated, but pre-booking is highly recommended.
######  Does Kaiyo have a sustainability mission behind it?
Yes, and it’s a big part of what makes the brand special. Kaiyo’s core mission is to **reduce furniture waste going into landfills** by giving pre-loved pieces a second life in new homes. They also **plant a tree for every purchase** made through the platform. So when you buy from Kaiyo, you’re not just getting a great deal — you’re actively participating in a more sustainable approach to home furnishing.
######  Can I sell or consign my own furniture through Kaiyo?
Yes! Kaiyo operates as a **two-sided platform** — they don’t just sell furniture, they also help you rehome yours. You can reach out to Kaiyo for an evaluation of your piece, and if accepted, they’ll schedule a pickup. Their pickup crews (like Lawrence, Joaquin, Shane, and Eliseo, who’ve been praised by past consignors) **handle everything carefully and professionally** , so you don’t have to worry about logistics.
######  Are the item descriptions and dimensions on the website accurate?
Based on consistent customer feedback, the answer is a strong yes. **Online dimensions and condition descriptions have been reported as accurate** and reliable by multiple buyers. One longtime customer specifically noted that the dimensions listed for a mid-century chair matched real life perfectly — which is especially important when you’re trying to coordinate furniture with pieces you already own.
######  How do I get in touch with Kaiyo if I have questions or issues?
You can reach Kaiyo by phone at **(646) 844-8585** , or visit their website at **kaiyo.com** for online support. They’re also active on social media including **Instagram (@getkaiyo), Facebook (@GetKaiyo), and TikTok** , where you can browse their latest finds and updates. For in-person inquiries, the warehouse in Chino is your best bet during operating hours. Their team is known for being **responsive, professional, and genuinely passionate** about what they do.
