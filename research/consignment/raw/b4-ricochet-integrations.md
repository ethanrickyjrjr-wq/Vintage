Software Integrations
# Integrations Make Everything Possible
Utilize the power of cloud-based software with built-in, third-party integrations. Use the tools you love most and expand the capabilities of Ricochet for your store.
[Start Free Trial](https://www.ricoconsign.com/sign-up)
![](https://www.ricoconsign.com/website/assets/content-images/integrations-hero.webp)
![](https://www.ricoconsign.com/website/assets/content-images/integrations-hero-image.webp)
![](https://www.ricoconsign.com/website/assets/content-images/quickbooks.webp)
### Quickbooks Online
Connect to your QuickBooks account and automatically synchronize your payouts and product sales history. Reduce the time spent manually creating checks and balancing your books. QuickBooks integration is free and built right into Ricochet.
![](https://www.ricoconsign.com/website/assets/content-images/mailchimp.webp)
### Mailchimp
Market to your customers and consignors for free with Mailchimp. By connecting Ricochet with Mailchimp, you can build out powerful marketing campaigns, advertise sales, communicate with your top consignors, and grow your online business.
![](https://www.ricoconsign.com/website/assets/content-images/google-analytics.webp)
### Google Analytics
Connect your valuable web data to Google Analytics for real-time tracking. Expand your marketing efforts and optimize your website with one of the most powerful and useful tools on the web. G4 analytics, classic analytics, and Google Tag Manager are all available.
![](https://www.ricoconsign.com/website/assets/content-images/avalara.webp)
### Avalara
Say goodbye to tax-related headaches. Utilize the ultimate online tax compliance solution for tracking taxes on online sales. Easily monitor and manage your tax obligations, ensuring accurate reporting and compliance every step of the way.
![](https://www.ricoconsign.com/website/assets/content-images/facebook.webp)
### Social Verification
Ensure your social media users that your products are legitimate and that your website is safe and secure. Free and simple verification with Facebook Pixel as well as Pinterest verification is all included out of the box.
![](https://www.ricoconsign.com/website/assets/content-images/ship-station.webp)
### ShipStation
Natively we include basic shipping options to help stores get started. However, large volume stores may need a bit of a bump in their shipping capabilities. With ShipStation, you get access to many carriers, label printing and more.
![](https://www.ricoconsign.com/website/assets/content-images/legit-grails.webp)
### LegitGrails
Traditionally, authenticating high-end items can be a major challenge. With Ricochet's integration with LegitGrails, stores can now access fast, AI and human powered authentication, with certified results in as little as 30 minutes.
![](https://www.ricoconsign.com/website/assets/content-images/tango.webp)
### Tango
Provide your consignors with a streamlined and unique payout process. Consignors can choose to receive their payouts utilizing popular cash apps like Venmo, or to have their payout balance transferred to a gift card from over 1000 major retailers.
