# Sell Your Vintage Furniture, Art, and Home Decor
> We are always on the hunt for fabulous finds. Our primary focus is furniture, but we also buy art and home decor. We have an appetite for all things exceptional. Although vintage is our main squeeze, we also purchase and resell newer designer pieces. If you have something beautiful or interesting, show us! The worst we can say is “no.”
> We buy single pieces or entire estates. Do not be hindered by your location or the condition of the piece. Our trucks travel nationwide and our team of restoration experts are great at fixing bumps and dings.
> We also pay finders fees for people who spot something in the wild and [**let us know.**](https://furnishmevintage.com/contact/)
## How it works
  * **E-mail us** photos and information about the piece(s) you are selling. Our e-mail address is **shopfmv@gmail.com**
  * Your e-mail should include the zip code where the furniture is located, condition information (broken, missing or damaged parts, smoke, mold, pet or other smells?), asking price (if you have it). If you do not have an asking price, you can leave that out.
  * We will take a look and respond within a few days
  * Our buyer will work out a price for purchase
  * Movers will come to your home to pick up your item(s) and payment is remitted. Some sellers offer to deliver the pieces to us, but you are not required to do so.
