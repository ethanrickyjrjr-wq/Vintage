​JOIN OUR VENDOR COMMUNITY
​
At Florida’s Antique Mall, becoming a vendor is more than just renting a booth—it’s joining a vibrant community of collectors, curators, and creators who share a love for timeless treasures. Our mall is home to hundreds of unique vendors, each bringing their own style and story to the mix.
​
When you set up here, you’re not just selling products—you’re reaching customers who come looking for one-of-a-kind finds, nostalgic gems, and pieces that spark memories. You’ll be part of an inviting atmosphere where shoppers return again and again because of the variety, quality, and charm they discover.
​
As a vendor, you rent your booth space, provide and maintain your merchandise, and use our consignment system to easily input your inventory and set your prices. From there, we take care of the rest—we provide the labels for your items, handle all point-of-sale transactions, and make sure your customers enjoy a smooth shopping experience.
​
We make it simple so you can focus on what you love—finding, creating, and sharing amazing pieces. From daily foot traffic to large events, we’re dedicated to helping our vendors succeed.​​​​​
##### APPLY TO BECOME A VENDOR
We’re excited you’re interested in joining our vendor family! Before you get started, please take a few minutes to look over our Vendor Guidelines (button below). They’ll give you a clear picture of how things work and what to expect. After that, just fill out the application form below. Once we’ve received it, someone from our team will reach out to you to walk through next steps. We can’t wait to connect with you! Your treasures could be the next thing a customer falls in love with here.
