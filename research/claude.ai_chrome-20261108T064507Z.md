# Claude in Chrome
## A helping hand across all your tabs
Claude in Chrome reads the page you’re signed in to, then clicks, types, and fills forms while you decide what happens next.
[Add to Chrome (opens in new tab)](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/claude/fcoeoabgfenejglbffodgkkbkcdhcgfn)
Available on all paid plans. Not on a paid plan? [Upgrade now](https://claude.com/pricing)
##  A refreshing way to work on the web
No more copying, pasting, and switching tabs.
ClaudeClaude CodeEveryday toolsBackground tasks
### From browser research to finished deliverables
Claude researches and takes action in Chrome. It can click, type, and navigate websites. And it hands back deliverables like Excel models, comparison decks, and reports without having to copy and paste.
### Works with Claude Code
Connect development workflows to Chrome for a partner that tests and iterates with you.
### Uses your everyday tools
Bring Claude into your workflow to make apps work better, without leaving the browser.
### Runs tasks in the background
Start a workflow and move on. Claude completes tasks while you focus elsewhere.
##  Speed up everyday tasks with Claude in Chrome

Claude can navigate your analytics dashboard, extract the numbers you need, and compile them into a summary. No exports, no tab-switching, no manual copying.

### Organize files in Google Drive
Claude can sort through your Drive, create a folder structure, move files where they belong, and flag duplicates and old files for you to review. You approve the changes instead of doing the sorting yourself.

### Prepare and plan from your calendar
Claude can read your calendar, pull context from email threads, flag which meetings you need to prepare for, and book rooms if they’re missing. Run it every evening and start each day knowing what's coming.
[Learn more (opens in new tab)](https://academy.claude.com/use-cases/prepare-and-plan-from-your-calendar)
### Research competitors, get a comparison deck
Claude in Chrome visits competitor sites and pulls pricing, features, and positioning. Claude compiles everything into a formatted PowerPoint ready for your next strategy meeting.
[Learn more (opens in new tab)](https://academy.claude.com/use-cases/size-a-market-using-your-research)
### Log sales calls to your CRM
Claude can read your calendar, match attendees to Salesforce contacts, and draft activity logs for each call. You add notes and approve before anything gets created.
[Learn more (opens in new tab)](https://academy.claude.com/use-cases/log-sales-calls-to-your-crm)
### Clean up promotional emails
Claude can scan through your inbox, identify marketing emails, newsletters, and automated notifications, then present them as a list for you to review before deleting in bulk.

##  Stay in control
Pages can hide instructions meant to redirect Claude. Here’s how we defend against that, and what you can do too.
[Read the safety blog (opens in new tab)](https://www.anthropic.com/news/prompt-injection-defenses)
Built-in safeguardsHabits that help
#### Choose how Claude gets approval
Let Claude work with a safety check on each action, or approve one site at a time in Permissions Mode.
#### Claude asks before sensitive actions
Purchases, financial actions, and anything you can’t undo wait for you.
#### Admins set org-wide rules
Team and Enterprise admins control access and set site allowlists and blocklists.
#### Skip sensitive workflows
Avoid banking, health records, and anything involving credentials.
#### Review requests before approving
Read each one closely, especially for financial, personal, or work actions.
#### Pause and report unusual behavior
A site may be hiding instructions. Your reports help us catch new attacks.
## FAQ
### What is Claude in Chrome and how does it work?
Claude in Chrome is a browser extension that lets Claude work on the web pages you have open. Claude can read a page, then click, type, or fill forms on the sites you’re already signed in to. You choose whether Claude asks before it acts or works through a task with a safety check before each action. Common use cases include testing web pages during development, gathering research from across the web, and helping with repetitive tasks like data entry. Open it from the Chrome side panel, from Claude, or from Claude Code.
### What are the ways that Claude uses a browser?
Claude uses a browser two ways:
If you already use Claude in Chrome, that stays your default and Claude keeps working in your Chrome browser, where you're already signed in. Switch anytime in Settings → Capabilities → Preferred browser.
Claude now includes its own built-in browser, so it can spin up work on its own without taking over your screen, and your work stays safe. There’s nothing to install. If you're new to browser use, Claude uses the built-in browser by default.
### What browser activity is not recommended?
Avoid financial transactions, password management, or anything involving sensitive personal data. Start with trusted sites and familiar workflows where you're comfortable having Claude take actions. Make sure your use complies with our [acceptable use policy](https://support.anthropic.com/en/articles/12005017-using-agents-according-to-our-usage-policy). [Learn more.](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12902428-using-claude-for-chrome-safely)
### What are the security risks?
Browser AI faces unique security risks, like prompt injection attacks, where malicious actors might try to trick Claude into unintended actions, such as sharing your bank information or deleting important files. While we’ve implemented protections, they aren’t foolproof. Attack vectors are constantly evolving and Claude may hallucinate, leading to actions that you did not intend. We’ve shared our testing results, including possible attack scenarios, so you can make informed decisions, and strongly encourage you to [read about the risks (opens in new tab)](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12902428-using-claude-for-chrome-safely) before using this product. [Read blog post. (opens in new tab)](https://anthropic.com/research/prompt-injection-defenses)
### Does it work with Claude or Claude Cowork?
Claude Cowork is now just Claude, so you don't have to choose where to get work done. The update is rolling out to Pro and Max plans, with more to follow. If you're on a different plan, you'll be walked through the update in the product. We hope you enjoy the simpler experience. [Read what changed](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-is-now-claude)
