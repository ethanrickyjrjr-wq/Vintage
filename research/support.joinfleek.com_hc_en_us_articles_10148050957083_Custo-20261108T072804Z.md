# **Is shipping, customs, and logistics included in the price?**
**Shipping costs** are included in your order total at checkout.
**Customs duties and taxes** vary by country and destination. In many cases prices already include standard customs duties — but depending on your location, additional charges may apply (see the country breakdown below).
**What this means for you:**
  * The price you see at checkout includes shipping.
  * Depending on your destination and the seller's region, you may or may not be charged customs duties on delivery.
  * For full details, see **Fleek Customs, Duties & Taxes** below.


**Need help?** In-app chat • Email: support@joinfleek.com

## **Fleek Customs, Duties & Taxes**
Fleek aims to make international shopping smooth and transparent. In most cases, prices already include standard customs duties. However, depending on your location and recent regulatory changes, additional charges may apply.
## 🇺🇸 United States
**What's covered:**
  * Standard customs duties
  * All U.S. tariffs introduced under recent trade regulations


**What this means for you:**
  * The price you see at checkout is the final amount. Shipping, duties, and delivery are all bundled in.
  * Your parcel is pre-cleared before it lands, so there are no customs delays or hold-ups.
  * Since all payments go through Fleek, any returns or refunds are clean and straightforward.


Note:  
For certain courier services, you may be contacted directly for orders that are placed with Sellers from Pakistan and asked to pay customs duties before delivery.  
If this happens, please pay the required duties and share the payment invoice/receipt with us. Fleek will reimburse you for the eligible customs duties paid.  
If you have any doubts about a customs payment request, please contact us for confirmation before making the payment.
## 🇬🇧 United Kingdom
**If buying from Pakistan or India:**
  * Customs duties are covered.
  * If you're charged, Fleek will refund the non-VAT portion.


**If buying from other regions:**
  * You may be charged customs duties.
  * Fleek will **NOT** refund these.
  * You may be able to reclaim VAT from local tax authorities.


## 🇪🇺 European Union
_(Germany, Netherlands, Luxembourg, Austria, Belgium, Czech Republic, France, Italy)_
**If buying from Pakistan, India, or within the EU:**
  * Customs duties are covered.


**If buying from other regions:**
  * Customs charges may apply.
  * Fleek will **NOT** refund these.


## 🌍 Other countries
  * Customs duties are not covered.
  * You are responsible for all applicable charges.
  * VAT may be reclaimable through your local tax authority.


## 🧾 VAT rebates (for businesses)
If you're a registered business, you may be eligible to claim VAT refunds from your local tax authority.
## 📦 If your order is stuck in customs
  * Customs delays are sometimes outside Fleek's control.
  * Clearance can take up to 60–90 days in rare cases.
  * Fleek works closely with shipping partners and will keep you updated.


**Need help?** In-app chat • Email: support@joinfleek.com

# **Are customs duties and taxes included in the price when I order from abroad?**
It depends on your destination and the seller's region (see the full **Fleek Customs, Duties & Taxes** breakdown above).
**What you need to know:**
  * Where charges apply, import duties and taxes are determined by your local customs authority.
  * Rates vary depending on the product type and total order value.
  * For the US, all duties/tariffs are pre-paid and bundled into the checkout price.


**Non-VAT reimbursement:** In some cases (e.g. UK orders from Pakistan/India), Fleek reimburses the **non-VAT portion** of customs charges. Eligibility depends on your country and the seller's region.
