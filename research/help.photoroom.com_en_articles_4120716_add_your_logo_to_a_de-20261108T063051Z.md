# Add your logo to a design
Learn how to add your logo to any of your Photoroom designs.
Written by Matt Rouif
October 20, 2025
Table of contents
[On iPhone and iPad](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/4120716-add-your-logo-to-a-design#h_acdd1e349d)[On Android](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/4120716-add-your-logo-to-a-design#h_d0a0bc43aa)[On Web App](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/4120716-add-your-logo-to-a-design#h_99ef20d711)
It's important to add your business logo to your designs. It increases brand awareness and it can also help to deter other people from using your designs without permission.
[![Side-by-side images showing Photoroom designs with logos.](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h69nsblw/1544620040/a1567a9932bb989b7eb2575ac713/logos-on-designs.jpg?expires=1794121200&signature=05dc6349ceaea69cba151ee6e69276f3d80958c73951b45409781d18060e561d&req=dSUjEs98nYFbWfMW1HO4zZFuVfiu667LFOlu9vMOMq%2FVtjnewaBpAkKrETHJ%0ALJwq7vO1rhT2s8zmjBE%3D%0A)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/h69nsblw/1544620040/a1567a9932bb989b7eb2575ac713/logos-on-designs.jpg?expires=1794121200&signature=05dc6349ceaea69cba151ee6e69276f3d80958c73951b45409781d18060e561d&req=dSUjEs98nYFbWfMW1HO4zZFuVfiu667LFOlu9vMOMq%2FVtjnewaBpAkKrETHJ%0ALJwq7vO1rhT2s8zmjBE%3D%0A)
Photoroom provides several ways to add your business logo to designs:
  * Add a logo to your brand kit and then insert from your brand kit to your designs (recommended)
  * Add a logo by uploading an image file
  * Add a logo by taking a photo of your business logo.


For instructions, see the sections below.
* * *
## On iPhone and iPad
To add a logo to a design on the iPhone or iPad version of Photoroom, follow the instructions below. Note that the video shows how to add a logo from your brand kit and also how to insert a logo from an iPhone's camera roll.
(Video shows Photoroom on iPhone)
  1. Select **Content**.
  2. Select the workspace that contains the design you want to edit.  
​  
​**Note:** If you are going to add a logo from the brand kit, the logo image has to be available in the brand kit for your chosen workspace. Each workspace has its own brand kit. For instructions, see [Add a logo to your brand kit](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/10173745-add-a-logo-to-your-brand-kit).  
​
  3. Select a design to open it in the editor.
  4. Select Insert.
  5. Either:
     * Select a logo image from the **brand kit** for the workspace
     * Select **Camera roll** to upload a photo of your logo. If you have recently saved the logo image to your camera roll, you can also select it in the **Latest from camera roll** section.
     * Select **Files** to upload an image file of your logo.
     * Select **Camera** to take a photo of your logo with your device and then use that image.  
​
  6. Use gestures to resize and position the logo image.
  7. Select **Done** to close the layer settings for the logo image.
  8. [Optional] Select **Export**. Use the export options to save your design as an image file or share it with other people.
  9. Select **X** to save your design and exit the editor.

# On Android
To add a logo to a design on the Android version of Photoroom, follow the instructions below. Note that the video shows how to add a logo from your brand kit.
  1. Select **Content**.
  2. Select the workspace that contains the design you want to edit.  
​  
​**Note:** If you are going to add a logo from the brand kit, the logo image has to be available in the brand kit for your chosen workspace. Each workspace has its own brand kit. For instructions, see [Add a logo to your brand kit](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/10173745-add-a-logo-to-your-brand-kit).  
​
  3. Select a design to open it in the editor.
  4. Select **Insert**.
  5. Either:
     * Select a logo image from the **Brand** kit for the workspace
     * Select **Gallery** to upload a photo of your logo. If you have recently saved the logo image to your gallery, you can also select it in the **Latest from Gallery** section.
     * Select **Files** to upload an image file of your logo.
     * Select **Camera** to take a photo of your logo with your device and then use that image.  
​
  6. Use gestures to resize and position the logo image.
  7. Select **Done** to close the layer settings for the logo image.
  8. [Optional] Select **Export**. Use the export options to save your design as an image file or share it with other people.
  9. Select **Done** to save your design and exit the editor.​

# On Web App
To add a logo to a design on the Web App version of Photoroom, follow the instructions below. Note that the video shows how to add a logo from your brand kit and also how to insert a logo image file.
  1. Log in at [app.photoroom.com](https://app.photoroom.com).
  2. Select the workspace that contains the design you want to edit.  
​  
​**Note:** If you are going to add a logo from the brand kit, the logo image has to be available in the brand kit for your chosen workspace. Each workspace has its own brand kit. For instructions, see [Add a logo to your brand kit](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/10173745-add-a-logo-to-your-brand-kit).
  3. Select a design to open it in the editor.
  4. Select the logo from your **Brand kit** (shown in the sidebar). If the Brand kit is not shown, select **Brand kit** in the header menu.
  5. Drag your logo image to your preferred position.
  6. Use the handles to resize the logo image.
  7. [Optional] Select the **Download** button to download your design as an image file. Enter a name for the image file and choose the file type, then select **Download**. The image file downloads in your browser.  
​
