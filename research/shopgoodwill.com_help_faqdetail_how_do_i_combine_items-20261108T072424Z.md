##  [](/help "Back to help") How do I combine items? 
Auction items may be combined for payment and shipping if they are from the same Goodwill location, close within 7 days of each other, are not marked as Cannot Be Combined For Shipping, do not exceed 20 lbs in combined weight and are being shipped via the same carrier.
  

To combine eligible auction items into one order, you must wait until all of the items have ended before paying.
Once the auctions have ended, please sign into your ShopGoodwill.com account and proceed to your Open Orders. Click on the Pay link next to one of the items, all auction items that may be eligible for combination will then be displayed. Click on the box next to each item and follow the instructions to complete your payment.
  

**_Please note that Buy It Now Non-Auction items and Stock Items are not eligible for combined shipping._**
