### **USPS shipping rates come in 3 pricing tiers:**
  1. **Retail Pricing** is the most expensive shipping rate pricing tier, and it’s what you’ll pay if you purchase postage directly from USPS at the Post Office or on USPS.com.
  2. **Commercial Pricing** ® offers discounts off Retail Pricing.
  3. **Connect eCommerce™** is a new pricing level that's automatically available on Pirate Ship, offering additional savings that are cheaper than Commercial Pricing!


In 2019 USPS changed their pricing structure, merging the two Commercial Pricing discount tiers they previously had, Commercial Base Pricing and Commercial Plus Pricing. You may see other shipping software continue to advertise with those names in an attempt to confuse you or conceal hidden markup on top of the actual costs.
In 2022 USPS launched the new Connect eCommerce pricing level, which offers additional discounts below Commercial Pricing when using Pirate Ship.
Some services (like [Media Mail](https://www.pirateship.com/usps/media-mail)®) do not have any discounts available. But no matter which shipping service you use, with Pirate Ship you'll get the the cheapest rates USPS offers, with no markup or fees.
