# Fashion pricing comps (group: fashion)

Date fetched: 2026-10-26. Items B1, B5-B7, B9-B15, B18. Photos not seen; item descriptions came from Ricky.

## Method and caveats

- **Sources that worked:** Poshmark sold-listing search (`availability=sold_out`) via crawl4ai. Every SOLD row below was read from the Poshmark page named in the URL column (permalink where the crawl exposed one, otherwise the sold-search page).
- **Walled, not used:** eBay sold (captcha for crawl4ai, 403 for WebFetch) and Etsy search (empty response). So there are no eBay or Etsy sold prices. One ASKING data point (B1, ASOS retail) comes from a WebSearch summary and is marked.
- **SOLD on Poshmark means** the last list price shown on a listing flagged Sold. Offers and bundles can make the real payout lower, so treat each figure as a ceiling for that sale. Poshmark skews low versus Etsy for vintage and costume pieces.
- Recommended ranges are list prices for Etsy/Shopify, which leave room for 10-20% offers. Where a range sits above the sold comps because of that, the rationale says JUDGMENT.
