## A10 Pink glass stemware (about 6 flutes + 6 coupes/sherbets)

**ID notes.** Real Depression-era pink (Anchor Hocking, Jeannette, Hocking, Federal, Tiffin, Fostoria) vs 1980s-90s pink stemware. Flutes are the odd piece: tall pink flutes are scarce in true Depression patterns; most "pink flutes" on the market are later or French (Arcoroc). What resolves it: pattern name (etched floral, optic, block, hex), base marks (Anchor Hocking anchor, Jeannette "J"; Tiffin/Fostoria are usually unmarked), mould seams, small bubbles and uneven rims (period) vs clean machine finish (later), colour (soft peach-pink = 1930s; hot or bubblegum pink = later). Replacements.com pattern ID from a photo of the bowl and foot.
