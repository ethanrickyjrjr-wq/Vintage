# Print group pricing (C1-C8)

Fetched: 2026-10-26. Raw crawls are in `research/` (dated, never rewritten).

**Etsy note:** Etsy requires vintage adult magazines (Playboy, Penthouse) to be listed as mature content. Flag C1-C6 accordingly (per Ricky's brief; policy text not re-verified in this pass).

**Method and limits:** eBay (search and item pages) walled crawl4ai (captcha or 173-byte error) and WebFetch (HTTP 403); Amazon walled crawl4ai. So almost every comp below is an ASKING price from AbeBooks, Mercari or a dealer page. The only SOLD price found is C1 (icollector auction). Prices are item price, shipping excluded unless noted. Asking prices run above sold prices, so targets sit at or below the lower-middle of the asking cluster. Call count ran past the ~25 budget (about 38) because eBay walls forced fallbacks and C4/C8 needed identification.
