## A5. Elvis 1956 stage photo, signed by Scotty Moore and D.J. Fontana (not Elvis)

**Identification notes**
- 8x10 B&W, Elvis with acoustic guitar at a stand mic, Scotty Moore behind him. Signed "Scotty Moore" and "D.J. Fontana" in silver/gold pen. Thin gold metal frame. No COA known.
- Value is in the two signatures, not the print. Moore died 2016 and Fontana 2018 (per the Autograph Boss page), so the signing predates 2018 and no new ones exist.
- Probably a later reprint of a 1956-era image (the closest eBay comp is titled "Wertheimer", the 1956 Elvis photographer). Unconfirmed. Check the back for a photographer stamp or credit.
- What resolves the uncertainty: (1) take it out of the frame and photograph both signatures, the back, and any inscription; (2) get a third-party opinion. Without a COA the buyer pool is smaller and discounted.
- Elvis's own signature is not on it. Elvis-signed Blue Moon Boys photos sell for thousands (see context rows), so the listing title must say "signed by Scotty Moore and D.J. Fontana", never imply Elvis signed.

**Authenticated vs unauthenticated gap (asking prices only, one data point on the unauthenticated side).** JSA-certified dual-signed asks: $290 to $573 (three listings, two of them the same photo). Unauthenticated: about $180. Gap is roughly $110 to $390, midpoint about $250. The single-signer rows point the same way (JSA'd Moore EUR 156 vs an ended unauthenticated Fontana ask of $44.50): a certificate adds meaningful value.

**Authentication options**
- **JSA Basic COA** (likely cheapest): about $25 to $35 for a single autograph plus $5 per extra signature, so about $30 to $40 for two signers, for items worth under $300. Source: search snippet of https://www.spenceloa.com/fees, but **that page did not render in the crawl (nav only), so this is not verified on JSA's own site.** No "quick opinion" price found, none stated here. JSA submission rules for photos not verified.
- **PSA/DNA** (verified on https://www.psacard.com/pricing/autographs, read 2026-10-26): multi-signed fee depends on the "premier" signer's tier; the 2-signature column runs from $20 (lowest tier) up to $125 (the $100 tier). Moore's and Fontana's tier was not retrieved (PSA's lookup is a form). PSA says band-member items are priced by the band name, which may change the tier; unresolved. Turnaround about 30 business days. Items under $50 authentication price get a small 3x3 certificate; full Premium Letter upgrade is +$15. PSA's separate "Original Photograph" service ($50 for declared value up to $999) authenticates the photo print itself and does not apply here.
- Per a search snippet of PSA's submission forms (not verified), framed photos are not accepted, so unframe before submitting either way.
- Net: a roughly $35 to $60 certificate plus shipping and insurance buys a roughly $110 to $390 asking-price uplift. Risk: if the signatures are rejected, the piece is a decorative reprint worth far less.

**Recommended list price (as-is, no COA):** low $120 / target $180 / high $250. Rationale: anchored on the one ended unauthenticated ask ($179.99); no sold comps; accept offers near $140.
**If JSA-certified:** low $250 / target $300 / high $400. Rationale: lower-middle of the $290 to $573 JSA asks, because Hollywood Memorabilia's $573 is a dealer ask with no sale evidence.

**Verified / need review:** JSA-certified asking range verified (read on 3 dealer pages). Unauthenticated value and any actual sold price need review (eBay sold walled). JSA fee needs review (page did not render).

## A3. Large cubist acrylic on stretched canvas, about 24x36 in, unsigned, unframed

**Identification notes**
- Cubist / Picasso-style face with yellow hat and cityscape; cream, yellow, teal, brown, black. No visible signature, unknown artist.
- Biggest open question: is it a hand-painted original, or a printed or factory-painted decor canvas? That can swing the value several times over. Resolve with raking light (real brushwork and impasto vs flat print texture), then check the back for labels, stamps, stretcher bar marks, a date, or a signature that is not visible from the front.
- Reverse-image-search the front: it may be a copy of a known Picasso (which makes it "after Picasso" decor).

## A4. Framed original ink-and-wash face drawing, about 11x14 in frame, unsigned

**Identification notes**
- Face with black ink drips over the eyes, stitched mouth, pen hatching on the neck. Black wood frame, white mat, frame about 11x14. No visible signature.
- To resolve original vs print: look for ink sheen and pooling, paper tooth, pen-pressure indentation, and dot patterns under a loupe. Open the back of the frame to check for an inscription, date or signature hidden under the mat edge, and measure the image size (mat opening) versus the frame.
