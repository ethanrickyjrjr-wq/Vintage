# The Ultimate Guide to Bidding & Buying
[Bidding & Buying](https://blog.shopgoodwill.com/english/category/Bidding%20%26%20Buying)[Thrifting Tips](https://blog.shopgoodwill.com/english/category/Thrifting%20Tips)

![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5ed5374fe7e06943c8a085e0/699ee398-f16a-4b96-91a4-0d3bcb086e03/Screenshot+2026-06-11+at+6.45.20%E2%80%AFAM.png)
If you’re a thrifting fan, you’re likely familiar with the hustle, getting up early on the weekends to sort through bins in the search for one-of-a-kind finds. While we still love a good in-person experience, today, you now have options for your treasure hunt through online thrift stores. That means browsing [_vintage clothing_](https://shopgoodwill.com/categories/clothing?p=1), [_antique jewelry_](https://shopgoodwill.com/categories/jewelry-gemstones?p=1), [_rare home decor_](https://shopgoodwill.com/categories/home-decor?p=1), and more truly unique items, all right from the comfort of your couch.
## What Is ShopGoodwill.com
Unfamiliar with the digital side of thrift retail and wondering, _is ShopGoodwill.com legit_? It most certainly is, and you’re in for a real treat. More than 135 Goodwill organizations across North America use ShopGoodwill.com to connect donated items with interested buyers online. From everyday finds to unique, collectible, and higher-value pieces, the goal is simple: be thoughtful stewards of every donation, maximize its impact, and help fund Goodwill’s mission of creating jobs, training, and opportunities in local communities. And you’ll find these treasures that help make a real difference on our online auctions. 
## Getting Started with ShopGoodwill.com
Diving headfirst into our massive digital marketplace doesn’t have to be overwhelming. [_Getting started_](https://blog.shopgoodwill.com/english/getting-started-with-shopgoodwill) simply means setting up your buyer profile, so you’re ready for whatever major wins come your way. 
### Creating Your Account and Setting Preferences
Before you hop in and participate in any active online bidding, you’ll need to [_register for a free account on ShopGoodwill.com_](https://shopgoodwill.com/register). You’ll want to immediately set up your shipping and payment configurations, too, so you don’t miss out on any fast-paced online auctions once you start shopping. 
### Navigating the Search and Filter Tools
With hundreds of thousands of secondhand items active and available at any given time, you’ll likely want to give your search game an extra boost. When you shop our online thrift store, consider filtering your searches by location, category, and item condition to find those hidden gems fast (and avoid any pesky extra scrolling).
### Setting Up Watchlists and Alerts
Have your sights set on a few special gems and want to keep track of multiple auctions simultaneously before you’re ready to bid? Be sure to take full advantage of the “My ShopGoodwill” tab. Here, you’ll find your “favorites” section, where you can bookmark and monitor all those online auctions you don’t want to miss out on. 
## Advanced Bidding Strategies to Win More Auctions
We make it easy to browse, shop, and score big. But if you really want to snag some amazing deals, you’ll need a bit of a strategy when it comes to your online bidding. The best part? You can craft an experience that’s all your own.
### The Power of Proxy Bidding
One of the most effective tools you can use on ShopGoodwill.com is [_automatic (proxy bidding)_](https://shopgoodwill.com/help/faqdetail/automatic-proxy-bidding), and there’s good news: it’s built right into the platform. No more waiting around by the computer or stressing about going over budget. 
Taking over the bidding process for you, this system automatically raises your bid by small increments so that you can stay competitive during bustling auctions. Simply enter your max bid limit when you place your initial bid and watch this tool hard at work. 
### Sniping: The Magic of the Last-Second Bid
Expert bidders on our online thrift store typically like to keep it stealthy, often practicing a bit of “sniping.” Catch the competition off guard by swooping in at the last second to score big. Pro tip: Check out the [_Last Chance_](https://shopgoodwill.com/categories/lastchance?st=&sg=LastChance&c=&s=&lp=0&hp=999999&sbn=&spo=false&snpo=false&socs=false&sd=false&sca=false&caed=5%2F23%2F2023&cadb=7&scs=false&sis=false&col=1&p=1&ps=40&desc=false&ss=0&UseBuyerPrefs=true&sus=false&cln=1&catIds=&pn=&wc=false&mci=false&hmt=false&layout=grid&ihp=true&utm_source=sgw_website&utm_medium=banner&utm_campaign=bottom_tile&utm_content=last_chance) section, where you can find auctions that are closing soon, still open for online bidding, and all yours for the shopping. 
### Knowing Your Maximum Walk-Away Price
While we love the thrill of the hunt, it’s also important to stay practical. Make sure you set a realistic budget before you hop into any online auctions, as that is the key to true online thrifting success. 
## Mastering the Buyer Dashboard and Account Rules
Whether you’re tracking your favorites, looking for shipping updates, or making a payment, the “My ShopGoodwill” buyer dashboard is your go-to resource. Learn how to navigate this space, so you can stay on top of everything all in one place (and keep that good buyer standing).
### Tracking Deals via "Auctions in Progress"
Your “My ShopGoodwill” buyer dashboard also has another handy feature you’ll want to take full advantage of. Under the "Auctions in Progress" tab, you can find all the active online auctions you’re currently bidding on, so you can easily monitor their progress. Look for the trophy icon to track your success, as that indicates you’re the current highest bidder!
### Instant Gratiﬁcation with "Buy It Now" Listings
In the mood for an instant win and want to skip the online bidding altogether? We’ve all been there, and we have options for that, too. When you spot the “Buy It Now” icon next to an item, that means you can score it at a fixed price, in one speedy click. Head to the [_Buy It Now_](https://shopgoodwill.com/categories/buyitnow?st=&sg=BuyItNow&c=&s=&lp=0&hp=999999&sbn=&spo=false&snpo=false&socs=false&sd=false&sca=false&caed=5%2F23%2F2023&cadb=7&scs=false&sis=false&col=1&p=1&ps=40&desc=false&ss=0&UseBuyerPrefs=true&sus=false&cln=1&catIds=&pn=&wc=false&mci=false&hmt=false&layout=grid&ihp=true&utm_source=sgw_website&utm_medium=banner&utm_campaign=bottom_tile&utm_content=buy_it_now) page to see these pieces all in one place and get one step closer to scoring a treasure you’ll cherish forever. 
### Avoiding Non-Payment Penalties and Account Bans
Each bid on our online thrift store [_makes a big difference_](https://blog.shopgoodwill.com/english//goodwill-week-how-your-bids-make-a-difference), directly supporting the Goodwill mission and funding life-changing community programs for those who need a helping hand. Every purchase makes an impact, with 85 cents of every dollar going directly back to local Goodwill agencies. In order to keep your good standing (and avoid any suspensions), you’ll want to make sure you pay for your items on time. 
And it’s super easy to do just that with the [_payment method_](https://shopgoodwill.com/help/faqdetail/checkout-process) of your choosing. Have questions? We’re always here to help. [_Learn how to get in touch_](https://shopgoodwill.com/help/faqdetail/contact-us) with our support team. 
## Start Online Thrifting Like a Pro
Whether you’re looking for a [_special gift,_](https://blog.shopgoodwill.com/english/thrifted-gift-ideas?rq=gift) love to [_upcycle_](https://blog.shopgoodwill.com/english/creative-upcycling-spotting-quality-auction-finds), are a collector, or simply live for the hunt, ShopGoodwill.com is your go-to for all things rare, valuable, and unique. By mastering our platform and becoming a pro at online bidding, you’re saving money and contributing to a more [_sustainable future_](https://blog.shopgoodwill.com/english/earth-day-why-thrifting-is-sustainable)—all without ever having to leave your house. So, what are you waiting for? [_Start browsing_](https://shopgoodwill.com/home) today and find a hidden gem you can’t believe you ever lived without.
## FAQs:
**_Is ShopGoodwill.com legit?_**
Yes! We are a fully legit online auction platform, where Goodwill organizations from around the U.S. and Canada list their unique donated items. Hesitant to enter your payment details? Shop away! Rest assured that we always use secure payment gateways to safeguard all your personal and financial information. 
**_How is ShopGoodwill.com reliable?_**
Since each item is being sold directly by non-profit Goodwill organizations, you never have to worry about scammers or fake postings. We fully vet each piece, ensuring it is authenticated and meets quality and condition standards. We also provide measurements, thorough product descriptions, and other details in each listing. Shopping with us is an easy, eco-friendly, and sustainable way to find unique items. 
**_Why can ShopGoodwill.com shipping be expensive?_**
While we work hard to ensure the rare items on our site are accessible and affordable, ShopGoodwill.com [_shipping rates_](https://shopgoodwill.com/help/faqdetail/how-are-shipping-and-handling-charges-determined)can vary, as individual Goodwill locations handle this process. However, you can always view shipping costs before bidding, so you know what to expect. Want to save big on shipping?
