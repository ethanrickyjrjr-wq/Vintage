## About our stores
Goodwill Industries of Southwest Florida offers programs to individuals with disabilities and disadvantages in Southwest Florida that includes an Adult Day Life-Skills Training site, adult and youth employment services, connections to community resources and jobs, small business training classes, free digital skills training, English as a Second Language (ESL), citizenship, employability workshops and My ACCESS Florida assistance. 
Goodwill SWFL is also the largest provider of disability-accessible housing in the state of Florida. The retail stores collect and sell donated items and new goods, which help fund the services and programs offered in Southwest Florida. 93 cents of every dollar goes back into supporting Goodwill’s programs and services in our Southwest Florida area.

## Pickup details
**Pick Up Orders:**
  * **By appointments only- Please schedule an appointment online all appointments are scheduled on the website 2 business days in advance.**
  * **Pickup Days are on Wednesday and Friday. Holidays may be closed. If there are no appointment slots we may be closed due to holidays.**
  * **Enter the East entrance, go to back parking lot and a sign will be on the building for ShopGoodwill Pickups**
  * **Call 239-995-2106 Ext. 2275 when you arrive for the order.**
  * **Orders need to be picked up within 7 days from payment.**
  * **No items can be stored for longer time periods with space limitations.**
  * **_NO Add on orders will be allowed. Only orders scheduled will be available for pick up_** _._
  * **_Orders not picked up within 7 days will become unavailable and will be considered a donation_. _Pickups scheduled and not picked up will be considered donations after 7 days._**


  

**_Pickups are only at 5100 Tice St, Fort Myers, FL 33905._**
