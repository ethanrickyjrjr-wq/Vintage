# How to fix: Image too small
Submit images that are at least 500 x 500 for all products to be compliant with new requirements
**Note** :
  * **We recently announced new image size requirements of at least 500 x 500 pixels for all product images. Enforcement of the new requirements will begin January 31, 2027**.
  * Starting July 2026 you will see warnings on images larger than the minimum requirements but smaller than 500 x 500.


Submit images that are at least 500 x 500 for all products to be compliant with new requirements. Until January 31, 2027, you can use an image that's at least 100 x 100 pixels for non-clothing and 250 x 250 pixels for clothing products. For YouTube Shopping ads, images must be at least 500 x 500 pixels to serve on TV.

* * *
##  Reasons why your issue is happening
The image submitted for this product is too small to be used in an ad or a free listing.
#### Why is this important?
Customers rely on product images to make decisions when shopping online. Since images are so important, we disapprove products when they don't provide an image with the right dimensions. By staying within the size dimensions we provide, you can make sure your product is ready to appear to customers who view your ads or free listings.
* * *
##  How to fix the issue
Update your images so that they meet our image requirements:
  * For apparel products, images must be at least 250 x 250 pixels and can't be larger than 64 megapixels.
  * For YouTube Shopping Ads, images must be at least 500 x 500 pixels to serve on TV.
  * For all other products, images must be at least 100 x 100 pixels.
  * Images cannot be larger than 64 megapixels or 16 MB in file size.
  * Avoid submitting thumbnails or scaling images. We recommend that the product take no less than 75% but not more than 90% of the full image.


You can submit links to your updated product images by adding them to the [image link `[image_link]`](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/12472547) attribute.
**Note** : We recently announced new image size requirements of at least 500 x 500 pixels for all product images. Enforcement of the new requirements will begin on January 31, 2027. Until that date, you will find warnings on images larger than the above requirements but smaller than 500 x 500. In some cases, we may serve an optimized version of images below 500 x 500 when possible.
### Fix products one by one directly in Merchant Center
**Note** : To view images that will be impacted by the new 500x500px requirements: From the Needs attention tab, select “View history” and review the orange warnings on the right hand side of the page to find ”Image too small for upcoming enforcement” and “Image resolution optimizations applied”. To download a list of impacted products, click the “View fix” and then “Download impacted products" on the page that loads.
  1. Go to **Products** in Merchant Center, then select the **Needs attention** tab.
  2. Use the **filter** icon ![](https://storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/KNBfKo2b7Jb49LtVM7NE7nN3F3hEA7gN8UXs) above the table to find all products affected by your issue.
  3. Select the title of the product you want to fix, then select **Edit product**.
  4. Replace your current product images with images that meet our requirements.
  5. Select **Save**.


**Note:** You can increase resolution, remove backgrounds, and generate a scene for your products using [Product Studio](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/13708167) which is available for Merchant Center users and within the Google & YouTube app in Shopify.
### Fix multiple products at once
**Note** : To view images that will be impacted by the new 500x500px requirements: From the Needs attention tab, select “View history” and review the orange warnings on the right hand side of the page to find ”Image too small for upcoming enforcement” and “Image resolution optimizations applied”. To download a list of impacted products, click the “View fix” and then “Download impacted products" on the page that loads.
  1. Go to **Products** in Merchant Center, then select the **Needs attention** tab.
  2. Use the **filter** icon ![](https://storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/KNBfKo2b7Jb49LtVM7NE7nN3F3hEA7gN8UXs) above the table to find all products affected by your issue.
  3. Select the **Download** icon ![](https://storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/v7a2eV7CaaJjjQ5Htv5OM1yx8o7Fq8aEiU5f) to download a list (.csv) of all affected products for all issues or this particular issue.
  4. Cross-reference the downloaded list with your uploaded product data, and make sure that the affected products have image links to updated product images (using the [image link `[image_link]`](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/12472547) attribute).
  5. Reupload your corrected product data to Merchant Center using your chosen upload method.


* * *
##  Next steps
After making the requested changes, your changes may take 24–72 hours to be reflected on the “[Needs attention](https://merchants.google.com/mc/products/diagnostics)” page.
