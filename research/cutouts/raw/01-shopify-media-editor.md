# Using the media editor
You can use the media editor to edit your images, [3D models](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/product-media/3d-scanner), and videos. The media editor is available on both desktop and mobile devices, but some media editing features are available only on the desktop version. Currently, on the mobile version of the media editor, you can only edit image files.
You can use the media editor to edit media wherever the edit icon displays on your media. This includes [product media](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/product-media), media in your online store editor, media in Shopify Messaging emails, and media in **Content** > **Files**. You can't use the media editor to edit images in the [rich text editor](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-admin/productivity-tools/rich-text-editor).
Before you edit your media, you should understand how the aspect ratio of your images affects how they display on websites.

## Understanding image aspect ratio
An image's aspect ratio is the ratio of its width to its height. For example, an image that is 200 px by 400 px has an aspect ratio of 1:2. An image that is 150 px by 450 px has an aspect ratio of 1:3.
Images can be different sizes and still have the same aspect ratio. To calculate whether images have the same aspect ratio, divide each image's width by its height, and then compare the results.
#### Note
You can use Shopify's [product camera](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/product-media/add-media#add-media) to take photos and videos of your products in the ideal aspect ratio, directly in the Shopify app.
Using a consistent aspect ratio for all the images of a particular type makes them display better side by side, because they all display as the same size. For example, if you want your product images to display as the same size within a collection, then they need to have the same aspect ratio.
You can use the media editor to [crop your images](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-admin/productivity-tools/media-editor#crop-an-image) to have the same aspect ratio.
Aspect ratio also determines how some theme sections display an image. Sections such as slideshows, image banners, and full-width sections crop your image to fit the shape of the section's image area, so the aspect ratio that you crop to controls how much of the image stays visible. Learn more about [how to reduce image cropping](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/images/theme-images#reducing-image-cropping).
## Add or edit alt text
Alt text describes an image and is important for accessibility and SEO. You can add or edit alt text for images in the media editor.
The media editor might suggest alt text for images that don't already have it. These suggestions are generated using AI to help describe your image content. You can accept, edit, or replace the suggested alt text. Keep your alt text brief and descriptive. Around 125 characters or less is recommended. Learn more about [adding alt text to media](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/product-media/add-alt-text).
#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to the media file that you want to edit.
  2. Click the thumbnail to open the file editor.
  3. In the **Edit information** section, click in the **Alt text** field.
  4. Do one of the following:
     * If there's a suggested alt text, review it and click **Accept** to use it, or edit it as needed.
     * If there's no suggested alt text, or if you want to write your own, enter a brief description of the image.
  5. When you're done making edits in the media editor, click **Save**.


## Crop an image
You can crop an image to remove part of the image. This is useful when you want to display only a part of the image, or when you need to change the image's aspect ratio.
You can crop an image on both desktop and mobile devices. Saving a cropped image on a mobile device creates a new copy of the image.
#### Steps:
Desktop
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to the media file that you want to edit.
  2. Click the thumbnail to open the file editor.
  3. In the media editor, click **Crop and transform**.
  4. Frame the area of the image that you want to keep:
     * To crop the image with a custom aspect ratio, click **Freeform**. You can click and drag the corners or edges of the highlighted area or click inside the highlighted area and drag it to a different position.
     * To crop the image using the same aspect ratio as the image, click **Original**. You can click and drag the corners of the highlighted area to resize it, or click inside the highlighted area and drag it to a different position. The aspect ratio stays the same as the original image when you resize it.
     * To crop the image into a square, click **Square**. You can click and drag the corners of the square to resize it, or click inside the square and drag it to a different position. The aspect ratio of the square stays the same when you resize it.
  5. Click **Apply** to crop the image.
  6. When you're done making edits in the media editor, click **Save**.

Mobile
  1. From the [Shopify app](https://www.shopify.com/install/detect), go to the media file that you want to edit.
  2. Tap on the image you want to edit.
  3. In the media editor, tap **Crop**.
  4. To crop the image into a custom size, tap and drag the corners of the highlighted area or tap inside the highlighted area and drag it to a different position.
  5. Optional: To reset the image or undo a change, use the 
![Undo icon](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/UndoIcon.svg) or ![Redo icon](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/RedoIcon.svg) at the top of the editor. 
  6. When you're done making edits in the media editor, tap **Save** to save a new copy of the image.


## Draw on an image
You might want to draw on an image to add a line, highlight part of the image, or add a design.
#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to the media file that you want to edit.
  2. Click the thumbnail to open the file editor.
  3. Click **Draw**.
  4. Select a color and a brush size.
  5. Click and drag on the image to draw.
  6. Click **Apply** to add the drawing.
  7. When you're done making edits in the media editor, click **Save**.


You can use the eraser to remove all or part of a drawing. The eraser doesn't remove anything from the original image.
## Resize an image
You can resize an image to change its actual size. If you try to adjust the aspect ratio by resizing the image, then your image might be distorted. Instead, [crop the image](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-admin/productivity-tools/media-editor#crop-an-image) to the aspect ratio that you want to use.
#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to the media file that you want to edit.
  2. Click the thumbnail to open the file editor.
  3. Click **Resize**.
  4. Enter the new width for the image. By default, **Lock aspect ratio** is selected, which will update the image height automatically to maintain the images aspect ratio. If you want to adjust only the height or width of an image, then click **Lock aspect ratio** to deselect the setting. Resizing your image with **Lock aspect ratio** deactivated can distort your image.
  5. Click **Done** to resize the image.
  6. When you're done making edits in the media editor, click **Save**.


## Change an image's orientation
You can flip or rotate an image to change its display orientation. You can also tilt the image within its frame.
#### Note
If images display in unexpected orientations after upload, this is usually caused by EXIF (Exchangeable Image File Format) metadata. Different applications interpret EXIF orientation data differently. To fix orientation issues, use the media editor to rotate the image, which permanently rearranges the pixels rather than relying on metadata.
#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to the media file that you want to edit.
  2. Click the thumbnail to open the file editor.
  3. Click **Rotate**.
  4. To change the image's orientation:
     * Click **Rotate clockwise** to turn the image.
     * Click the **Flip vertically** or **Flip horizontally** buttons to flip the image.
  5. Click **Apply** to change the image's orientation.
  6. When you're done making edits in the media editor, click **Save**.


## Removing or replacing an image's background
You can use AI to [remove or replace the background of an image](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/ai-powered-tools/media-generation) in the media editor. You can remove or replace an image's background in the media editor on both desktop and mobile devices.
Learn more about [media generation](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/ai-powered-tools/media-generation).
## Revert changes to an image
If you modified an image using the Shopify media editor, then you can choose to revert all changes back to the original image, even after the changes have been saved. Reverts to a modified image can’t be undone, so if you want to re-apply or keep any modifications to the image, then they need to be redone manually.
#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to the media file that you want to edit.
  2. Click the thumbnail to open the file editor.
  3. Modify the image.
  4. Click **Revert to original**.
  5. In the **Revert image to original?** dialog, click **Revert**.


A **Reverted to original** message displays indicating the change is complete.
## Adjust a 3D model
You can adjust the following for your 3D models:
  * environment
  * background
  * initial camera position


You can't adjust a 3D model on mobile.
#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to the media file that you want to edit.
  2. Click the thumbnail to open the file editor.
  3. In the **Environment** section, click the environment that you want to apply to your 3D model, and use the slide bar to adjust the exposure of your 3D model.
  4. In the **Background** section, use the color picker to select the background color or input a hex code.
  5. Click the camera icon to adjust the **Initial camera position**. You can adjust the X and Y rotation by entering in the degree of the rotation into either the **X Rotation** field or the **Y Rotation** field, and you can zoom into your 3D model by moving the slide bar to a higher percentage.
  6. Click **Done**.


If you adjust the X or Y camera rotation on a 3D model and the changes revert when you save, then adjust the camera zoom first before setting your preferred camera rotation.
## Replace the thumbnail for a video
When you upload a video, the thumbnail defaults to the first frame in the video, but you can upload your own thumbnail to replace the default.
You can't replace the thumbnail for a video on mobile.
#### Steps:
  1. From your Shopify admin, go to the media file that you want to edit.
  2. Click the thumbnail to open the file editor.
  3. Click ![Horizontal menu](https://cdn.shopify.com/shopifycloud/help-center/manual/admin-icons/MenuHorizontalIcon.svg) > **Replace thumbnail**.
  4. Select the file from your computer.
