# Product media
Product media can include images, 3D models, and videos. Using media such as 3D models for your products provides your customer with a better understanding of the function and size of an item, and increases your customers' confidence in the quality of your products.
You can add product media directly to your product pages, or by using [metafields](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/custom-data/metafields). If you have a [compatible theme](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-store/themes/managing-themes/versions#features), then you can add references to your product metafields through the theme editor.
You can also access product media and other files by navigating to **Content** > [**Files**](https://admin.shopify.com/content/files) within the Shopify admin. You can filter your list of files using the `Used in` search dropdown and selecting **Product media**. The **Files** section of the admin allows you to view all your files used across every product in your store.
#### Caution
You should only ever upload files to the **Content** > **Files** section of your Shopify admin when they're intended for public access on your store, such as product media, downloadable content, and other storefront images. Never upload any documents that contain confidential, personal, or sensitive information about you, your business, or your customers.
#### Tip
For a complete list of requirements and information about uploading files and using the file system in your Shopify store, refer to [_Uploading and managing files_](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-admin/productivity-tools/file-uploads).
To display 3D models or videos in your online store, you need to do one of the following:
  * Use a [theme that supports 3D models and videos](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/product-media/product-media-types#supported-themes). You might need to update to a newer version if you're currently using one of these themes and 3D models and videos don't display.
  * Modify your current theme. If you're comfortable reading and editing theme code, then you can learn more about [adding media support to your theme](https://shopify.dev/themes/product-merchandising/media/support-media) and [media UX guidelines](https://shopify.dev/themes/product-merchandising/media/media-ux) in Shopify's developer documentation. If you're not comfortable reading and editing theme code, then you can [hire a Shopify Partner](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/partner-directory).
