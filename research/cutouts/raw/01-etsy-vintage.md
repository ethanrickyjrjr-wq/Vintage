# How to Add or Edit Your Shop's Location 
Adding a location helps your shop and listings appear in local searches.
## To add or edit your shop's location
  1. Sign in to Etsy.com and go to [**Shop Manager.**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/dashboard)
  2. Select **Settings** , then select [**Your shop**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/settings/your-shop).
  3. Select **Shop team**.
  4. Under **Location** , select **Add** to add a location or **Edit** to edit your location.
  5. As you begin typing your city, a list of suggestions will appear. Select a location from the list.
  6. Select **Save**.


You can also add or edit your location in your [Shop home visual editor](https://www.etsy.com/shop/me/edit), which can be found by going to **Sales Channels** in your [**Shop Manager**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/dashboard) and selecting the pencil icon next to your shop name. You can also access your Shop home visual editor within [**Your shop**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/settings/your-shop) settings.
If your exact location does not appear in the dropdown menu, please choose the nearest city or town that does appear.
## How to update the location on your public profile on Etsy
You may also want to update your location in your Etsy profile.
  1. Sign in to Etsy.com and go to [**Account settings**.](https://www.etsy.com/your/account)
  2. Select **Edit public profile** or select the **Public Profile** tab. 
  3. Enter your location in the **City** field. If your city isn't there, please choose the nearest city or town that does appear.
  4. Select **Save Changes**. 


On your listings, buyers in your local area may see your address without the name of your country. For instance, buyers from within Türkiye may see a location as "Izmir, Izmir," instead of "Izmir, Türkiye," but buyers outside of Türkiye will still see "Izmir, Türkiye."
