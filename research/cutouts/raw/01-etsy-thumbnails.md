# How to Edit Your Listing Photos 
Did you know you can add videos of your items? [Learn how to attract shoppers with listing videos](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/how-to-take-listing-videos-with-your/821069114343?ref=vg20sh).
When you add photos to [your listings](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015628707?segment=selling), you can change their order, crop them, edit them, and adjust the thumbnail. The thumbnail is a cropped version of your primary listing photo that may be seen in different places across Etsy.
Having multiple clear, high-resolution images in your listing is an important factor for your listings’ quality. Having a main image that is too dark, blurry, or are part of a photo-collage may result in your listing appearing lower in Etsy search results. 
  * Learn more about [Etsy’s Customer Service Standards](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360036207794-What-are-Etsy-s-Customer-Service-Standards?segment=selling).
  * Learn more about [requirements for listing photos](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015663347?segment=selling).


While your listing photos upload to the Etsy Seller app, you can complete the remaining listing information. You'll receive a notification if any of your photos failed to upload and you need to try again. Once your photos have successfully uploaded, you can begin to edit them.
## How to change the order of your listing photos
The first photo will be the thumbnail for your listing. You can change the order of your photos on your listing if you’d like a different one to be first.
To change the order of your photos on Etsy.com: 
  1. On Etsy.com, open **Shop Manager**.
  2. Choose [**Listings**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/listings).
  3. [Edit your listing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015692667?segment=selling) or create a new listing.
  4. After you’ve added at least 2 images to the **Photos** section, select and drag them to change the order.


To change the order of your photos on the Etsy Seller app: 
  1. Open the listing and tap a photo or the **Listing** heading.
  2. Tap **View all**.
  3. Tap **Reorder**.
  4. Tap and drag a photo.
  5. Choose **Save**.


## How to crop your listing photos
You can crop your photos after you add them to your listing. Cropping can change how the thumbnail looks, so try cropping before adjusting your thumbnail.
To crop listing photos on Etsy.com: 
  1. Open **Shop Manager.**
  2. Choose [**Listings**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/listings).
  3. [Edit your listing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015692667?segment=selling) or create a new listing.
  4. Hover over the photo.
  5. Choose the crop icon.
  6. Select any one of the suggested sizes or drag the grid's corners to choose your own size.
  7. Select **Save**.


To crop listing photos on the Etsy Seller app: 
  1. Open your listing and tap a listing photo.
  2. Tap **Edit**.
  3. Drag the corners to resize the image or choose **Original** for preset sizes.
  4. Slide the image to choose what part you want to display.
  5. Tap **Apply**.


You won’t be able to undo the crop after you save it. To undo the crop, upload the original image again.
## How to edit your listing photos
Etsy has partnered with Img.ly so you can edit your listing photos on the Etsy Seller app with just a few taps.
To edit your listing photos on the Etsy Seller app: 
  1. In**Listings** , select the listing you wish to edit or create a new one. 
  2. Go to **Photo and video** and select the photo or videos you wish to edit, or add a new one. Select **Edit**.
  3. Select **Transform** to crop or rotate, or **Adjust** to edit brightness, contrast, saturation, sharpness, and more. 
  4. Select the check mark in the top right corner when finished editing.


If you have preferred photo edits, save them as a custom photo filter. Saving your custom photo filters can make editing easier and help give your listing photos a cohesive look.
To save your edit as a custom photo filter on the Etsy Seller app: 
  1. After editing your listing image, select **Create a filter** to save it.
  2. Provide your custom photo filter with a name under **Filter name**.
  3. Select **Save**.


To apply a custom photo filter to your listing image on the Etsy Seller app: 
  1. When editing an image, select **Apply a photo filter**. 
  2. Choose the filter you want from your list of custom photo filters.


Your custom photo filters will show up in order from most to least recently used. 
If you delete a custom photo filter, the images that have been edited with the filter will not be impacted.
## How to adjust your thumbnail
To adjust your thumbnail on Etsy.com: 
  1. Open **Shop Manager.**
  2. Choose [**Listings**](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/tools/listings).
  3. [Edit your listing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015692667?segment=selling) or create a new listing.
  4. Choose **Adjust Thumbnail**.
  5. Slide the circular button left and right to zoom in and out.
  6. Position your image so your subject is clear in all 3 views used across Etsy.
  7. Select **Apply**.


## **How to fix your listings’ images for Etsy search visibility**
To view which of your listings have images that may be causing Etsy search visibility problems: 
  1. From your **Shop Manager** , visit your [Etsy Search visibility page](https://www.etsy.com/your/shops/me/search-visibility?ref=seller-platform-mcnav). 
  2. In the **Your listings** section, you’ll see personalized insights and recommendations on how to improve your listings. 
  3. Choose an issue you want to correct and select **View listings**. This shows all the listings in your shop with that issue. 
  4. Multiple issues may affect an individual listing. Once all the issues are resolved, they will no longer appear in your **Etsy Search visibility** page. 


Once improvements have been made, the insights will typically disappear within 24 hours.
