# About Product Studio for Merchant Center
Google’s Product Studio is a suite of free, AI-powered tools that empowers you to enhance and create high-quality product images and videos in just a few clicks, saving you time and resources. Product Studio helps you quickly optimize your product image assets within Google Merchant Center using AI tools. The tools can help you create images, change backgrounds, increase image resolution, remove backgrounds, and generate product videos. Your new images and videos can help you attract more customers when you scale them across your marketing channels.
Merchant Center users can [get started now](https://merchants.google.com/mc/productstudio) or read below for more information. If you don’t yet have a Merchant Center account, you can [create one for free](https://www.google.com/retail/). [Product Studio is also available via the Google & YouTube app on Shopify](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/13709443). If you’re interested in participating in other AI features or staying updated on the latest features, express your interest by [completing the form](https://forms.gle/FNSbH6TGdTVXR5cW6).

##  Benefits
"80% of surveyed users already have been more efficient or expect to be more efficient by using Product Studio.1"
With [Google’s AI Principles](https://ai.google/responsibility/principles/) in mind, Product Studio brings you the benefits of generative AI, helping you easily create unique and tailored product imagery and videos for free and get more value from the images you already have.
  * **Save time, money and resources** : Product Studio is free and helps reduce the time, resources and budget needed to plan, shoot, and edit high quality product images and videos.
  * **Increase customer engagement** : With more images and video, your listings may perform better on Google Shopping and generate a higher return on investment.
  * **Seamlessly update your images across multiple channels** : When you save your new or updated images to Merchant Center, they're eligible to appear across a wide range of Google platforms, including search results, Shopping ads, Performance Max (PMax) campaigns, and anywhere else that utilizes feed data.
  * **Refresh your product images for holidays, seasons, and sales** : Quickly and effortlessly create high-quality promotional images in one click using our inspiring themes to appeal to shoppers throughout the year.

## What to expect
Product Studio is available to all Merchant Center users except those based in RU and FR. However, the feature still works best with Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, and Spanish language descriptions. The Generate Video feature is only available for merchants in Australia, Canada, India, Japan, the UK, and the US. We continue to make the product better by improving the user experience, output quality, feature set, and more.
To make sure we're creating the best possible images and videos for your offers, we have a team of quality reviewers who may review your inputs and new images or videos. Here's how it works:
  * **We only share the basics** : Reviewers view your original offer images, the new images or videos created by our system, and the instructions given to create those new images or videos.
  * **Reviewers assess quality** : This helps us understand what's working well and where we can improve.
  * **Your privacy matters** : We only share the information needed to assess the image or video quality. Your confidential business information remains protected.


This process allows us to continuously refine our image generation technology and deliver high-quality visuals that will help your offers shine.
As this feature is still experimental, it may generate unexpected images and videos and works best with certain products. For example, consumer packaged goods with products front and center. This feature doesn’t support images with hands, walled art, and light fixtures. It also doesn’t support image or video creation for regulated goods such as alcohol, drugs, pharmaceuticals, gambling-related products, weapons, tobacco, fireworks, and health and medical devices. Learn more [Product Studio Additional Terms of Service](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/14579601).

##  How to access Product Studio
Product Studio is available in Merchant Center or the Google & YouTube app in Shopify. To access Product Studio in Merchant Center, follow the steps below or simply go directly to the [Product Studio page](https://merchants.google.com/mc/productstudio).
  1. Go to the "Creative content" section on the left-side navigation menu of your Merchant Center account.
  2. Select **Product Studio**.


You’re now able to explore the full suite of creative tools in Product Studio, where you can enhance product images and generate brand new videos with the power of AI.
For Shopify users, learn more [About Product Studio for Shopify](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/13709443). Additionally, some advanced features, like AI-powered text suggestions, are also available via the [Product Studio Text API for developers](https://developers.google.com/product-studio/docs/onboarding).

## Change background
The change background feature provides a fast and cost-effective solution for generating high-quality product images that showcase products in natural, lifestyle-like settings. Review the [best practices](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/13708167?hl=en-EN#bestpractices) section below to learn more about writing a good scene description and what product images work best with Product Studio.
  1. Go to [**Product Studio**](https://merchants.google.com/mc/productstudio) within the "Creative content" on the left-side navigation menu.
  2. Select the **Generate image** button under "Generate better product images in seconds" card.
  3. Select the product image that you would like to use or upload a new one. 
     * Enter a description of your product.
     * Describe the scene in which you want your product placed in. 
       * For the best results, be sure to include a detailed description of the product, placement, surroundings, and background you’d like to be generated.
       * Use a template to populate a scene description with just one click. You can find seasonal, holiday, and studio templates under the "Or start with an inspiring theme” section. Select a theme to automatically populate the "Describe the scene" text box and generate a scene for you.
  4. Select **Generate image**. It may take a few moments for the images to load.
  5. Review your newly generated images and take any of the following actions: 
     * **Review the images** : Select on any of the four output images to expand the image for a better view.
     * **Animate an image** : Select the checkbox in the top left corner of an image to select it. Then select **Animate image** to be brought into the image to video tool. 
       * Note that you can only animate one image at a time. If you would like to animate multiple images, we suggest you download each image and upload them to the [Animate images](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/13708167?hl=en-EN#Animate_images) tool separately.
     * **Download an image** : Select the checkbox in the top left corner of any image you would like to download, then select the **Download** button.
     * **Add to Merchant Center** : If you’re satisfied with any of the images check the box in the top left corner of the image, and select **Add to Merchant Center**. 
       * A confirmation dialog box titled "Add this image to Merchant Center?" will appear.
       * Checkbox "Use this image as your main product page" option and then select **Add image** to add the image to Merchant Center. 
         * The "Add this image to Merchant Center?" option will only appear if one image is selected in Product Studio. If you select multiple images, each image will be added as an additional image, not as your main image.
       * Select **Add image** to add the image to Merchant Center.
  6. If you’d like to generate a new background, you can add a new description and select **Regenerate scene**.


**Note** : If there are 70 images of a product, you won't be able to save a new image and will find an error message. Also, when submitting lifestyle images, be sure you follow the guidelines found in the[ lifestyle image article](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/9103186).

##  Remove background
Remove background allows you to remove the background of your product image with one click. Only your product will remain on a white background to help shoppers view your product clearly.
  1. Go to [**Product Studio**](https://merchants.google.com/mc/productstudio) within the "Creative content" on the left-side navigation menu.
  2. Select the **Remove background** card.
  3. Select the image you wish to edit or upload a new one, then select **Remove background**. 
     * You can download or undo your changes.
  4. Select **Add to Merchant Center**. A confirmation dialog box titled "Add this image to Merchant Center?" will appear.
  5. Checkbox "Use this image as your main product page" option and then select **Add image** to add the image to your Merchant Center account.

## Increase resolution
This feature quickly increases the quality and size of your product images to help you create high-resolution product imagery that looks crisp.
  1. Go to [**Product Studio**](https://merchants.google.com/mc/productstudio) within the "Creative content" on the left-side navigation menu.
  2. Select the **Increase resolution** card.
  3. Select the image you wish to edit or upload a new one, then select **Increase resolution**. 
     * You can now slide the Before/After toolbar to view the changes.
  4. Select **Add to Merchant Center**. A confirmation dialog box titled "Add this image to Merchant Center?" will appear.


Checkbox "Use this image as your main product page" option and then select **Add image** to add the image to your Merchant Center account.

##  Animate images
Effortlessly transform your static product photos into engaging, short videos using AI. This powerful tool helps you capture customer attention. It adds dynamic motion to your images, making your products stand out across your online store, social media, and marketing campaigns. Whether you start with an existing photo or a newly generated scene, animating your image is just a click away, helping you create compelling visuals to attract more customers.
### Instructions
  1. Go to [**Product Studio**](https://merchants.google.com/mc/productstudio) within the "Creative content" on the left-side navigation menu.
  2. Navigate to the "Animate images" card and select the **Get started** button.
  3. Select the "Upload image" icon ![import](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/CBSwsyG_fk2milw7BnMHQXsKlh1hfzXKijq9ZHL3CgeiiKMi95VvBCUlVWo16CAqZiY=w36) or select the **+Product** to add a product you’d like to generate a video for. 
     * If you select a product, the main product image will be used to generate a video. If you’d like to use a different product image, we recommend you upload that image directly into the tool.
  4. After you upload an image, you'll find an automatically populated prompt based on your uploaded image, which you can also modify. Select **Generate**. 
     * For the best results, ensure your video generation prompt includes a comprehensive description of the desired visual elements, such as placement, surroundings, and background.
  5. After a few moments, 4 videos will be generated. Hover over any of the videos to have it automatically play. If you would like to save the video, hover over the video and click the download icon ![](https://storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/v7a2eV7CaaJjjQ5Htv5OM1yx8o7Fq8aEiU5f) in the top right corner of the video.

## Create images
Generate new product visuals using text prompts with the Google product image generator. This interactive feature in Product Studio lets you describe your desired outcome and refine images step by step. To guide the AI, combine your descriptions with product images from your Merchant Center library or your own uploaded images.
**Note** : This feature is currently available for Merchant Center users only.
### Instructions
  1. Go to the "Creative content" section on the left-side navigation menu of your [Merchant Center](https://merchants.google.com/) account.
  2. Select **Product Studio**.
  3. On the Product Studio landing page, choose the card that says, "Create images".
  4. The text and image input box will appear. You can generate a new image or edit existing ones by combining text descriptions, uploaded images, and images from your product library.
  5. **Provide your inputs** : You can use any combination of the options below: 
    1. **Describe the image or edits** : Type a description of the image you want to create or the changes you’d like made in the text box. For example, "Show this product on a beach at sunset" or "Make the background blue."
    2. **Upload your own images** : Select the upload icon ![import](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/CBSwsyG_fk2milw7BnMHQXsKlh1hfzXKijq9ZHL3CgeiiKMi95VvBCUlVWo16CAqZiY=w36) to add images from your computer. You can upload a maximum of 3 images and the images must be a JPEG or PNG file.
    3. **Select a product from Merchant Center** : Select the **+Product** icon to choose a product from Merchant Center and then select **Next**.
  6. After entering your text prompt and/or adding images, select the **Generate** button.
  7. **Refine your newly generated image** : 
    1. Review the generated image.
    2. To make further adjustments, type new instructions in the text box and select **Generate** again. You can repeat this process to fine-tune the result.
  8. After you’re satisfied with the image, hover over the image to save it 
    1. Select the**download button**![](https://storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/v7a2eV7CaaJjjQ5Htv5OM1yx8o7Fq8aEiU5f) to save it to your device.
    2. Select **+ Add to Merchant Center** to choose a product to save the image to. If you would like to save the image as the main product image for this offer check the "Main image" box.
    3. Select **Add image**.


**Note** :The conversation will be lost when you leave the page.

##  ****Explore
The "Explore" section is your source of inspiration for creating fresh and engaging product assets. Here, you'll discover new and creative ways to showcase your products using Product Studio. This section is designed to help you stay ahead of the curve and generate timely, relevant content for your marketing campaigns.
### Image themes
The "Image themes" tab provides a gallery of pre-made scene suggestions that you can apply to your product images. Browse through a wide variety of themes, such as "Celebrations and holidays," "Seasons," "Studio settings," "Interiors," "Events," and "Nature" to find the perfect backdrop for your products. You can filter the themes by category to narrow down your options. When you find a theme you like, simply select it, and you'll be taken to the "Change background" feature with a pre-populated prompt. This makes it easy to create professional-looking product photos without having to write your own descriptions.
###  ****History
The "History" tab shows your 20 most recently generated scenes, allowing you to easily revisit your work. You can copy the description from a previous generation if you'd like to create a similar scene again.
**Note** : When you use Product Studio, data such as your descriptions, generated images, and the times of generation are stored on the device you’re using. If you’re on a shared device, other users may be able to access this information. You can clear this data at any time by selecting "Clear history" in the top-right corner of the page.

### Writing a good scene description
When describing your scene, consider the following and provide these details within your description to get the best results:
  * **The style of image**. For example, product photography, illustration, macro photography, etc.
  * **The type of product you are adding to the scene**. For example, a bottle of cologne.
  * **The placement of the product**. For example, on a stone platform.
  * **The scene around the product**. For example, surrounded by white and yellow flower petals.
  * **The background**. For example, large tropical leaves and foliage in the background.
