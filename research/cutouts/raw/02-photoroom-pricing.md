## Frequently asked questions
#### Which image formats are supported on each plan?
**Image Editing API (Plus Plan)**
For the input and result images, the Image Editing API supports four formats: PNG, JPEG, WEBP and AVIF. By default, the result image will be in PNG format.  
  
**Remove Background API (Basic Plan)**
For the input image, the Remove Background API supports four formats: PNG, JPEG, WEBP and HEIC. For the result image, the Remove Background API supports three formats: PNG, JPEG and WEBP. By default, the result image will be in PNG format.
#### What is the max resolution of images on each plan?
**Image Editing API (Plus Plan)**
We recommend limiting images to 25 megapixels for optimal performance.
  * **Maximum File Size** : 30MB
  * **Maximum Resolution** : 5,000 pixels on the widest side


Images with a larger resolution are supported (if they stay below 30MB), but will be resized down to 5,000 pixels on the widest side.
**Remove Background API (Basic Plan)**
We recommend limiting images to 25 megapixels for optimal performance.
  * **Maximum File Size** : 50MB
  * **Maximum Resolution** : 6,000 pixels on the widest side


#### I was subscribed to the previous pricing, how do I transition to a new plan and maintain my credits? 
You can subscribe to plans from[ your API dashboard.](https://app.photoroom.com/api-dashboard)
[Discover more details for how to make your transition. ](https://www.photoroom.com/api/api-pricing-transition)
#### How do I find my API dashboard? 
You can access your API dashboard under the "Team" section in your account. [Watch this video to see the process. ](https://youtu.be/2D8ovhsxiyo)
#### How do I subscribe to a plan? 
From [your API dashboard:](https://app.photoroom.com/api-dashboard)
  * Create a new team
  * Enable a new API key
  * Select your new plan (Basic or Plus) and proceed to payment 


Note: for existing customers, you will have 2 teams - one subscribed to the new pricing plan and the other to our old pricing. 
  * If you prefer to continue using your previous API key, contact us at api-help@photoroom.com with:
  * Your previous API key
  * New API key (first 10 characters)
  * Your switch date, so we can update your account. [Learn more](https://www.photoroom.com/api/api-pricing-transition)


#### Is the Photoroom API free to try?
Yes, and you don't need a card. There are two free ways to test before you commit: a monthly sandbox allowance on the Image Editing API, and free production calls on the Remove Background API when you create an account.
#### What do I get in the sandbox?
1,000 sandbox calls a month on the Image Editing API. Sandbox results are watermarked, so you can build and test the full workflow end to end before you go live. No card, no commitment.
#### Do I get any free production calls?
Yes. Create an account and you get 10 free production calls on the Remove Background API. These are not watermarked, so you can ship a real result and see the output quality you'll get in production.
#### What's the difference between sandbox and production calls?
Sandbox calls are for building and testing: you get 1,000 a month on the Image Editing API, and the output carries a watermark. Production calls return a clean, watermark-free image. Your first 10 production calls on the Remove Background API are free with an account; after that you move to a paid plan.
#### If I subscribe to a Basic plan can I make Plus calls and vice versa?
Yes. 
  * If you’re on the Basic Plan (which is Remove Background API) and make a Plus call (which is the Image Editing API), you’ll be charged the Plus price per image of $0.10. 
  * If you’re on the Plus Plan and call the Remove background API, you’ll be charged the Basic price per image of $0.02.


#### Can I upgrade my plan from a free trial to a paying plan?
Yes - from [your API dashboard](https://app.photoroom.com/api-dashboard).
- **From free trial:** select Plus or Basic from the API dashboard.
#### What if I run out of my plan before it renews?
To prevent service disruptions, update your plan from [your API dashboard ](https://app.photoroom.com/api-dashboard)by clicking on “change plan.” 
**Note:** we send a notification when you’ve used 80% and another notification when you're at 100% of your plan.
#### Can I downgrade or cancel my plan?
Yes, from [your API dashboard](https://app.photoroom.com/api-dashboard). Your plan will be downgraded or canceled on your next billing date. 
You can use your remaining plan until then.
  * If you canceled your plan, we won’t bill you.
  * If you downgraded your plan, you will be billed your new plan amount at the time of your renewal.


#### Can I change the number of images in my plan?
Yes, you can increase or decrease the number of images from [your API dashboard](https://app.photoroom.com/api-dashboard). 
  * If you **add more images** , the change applies **immediately.**
  * You can immediately use the images in your new plan.
  * Your new subscription starts from the day you increased your plan images.
  * 30 days after your upgrade, your new plan will renew as long as you don’t cancel it.


Example:
  * You subscribed to (& pre-paid for) a $20 Basic plan (1000 images).
  * You increase your plan to the $40 Basic plan (2000 images)
  * We’ll immediately charge you**$40**. 
  * Please note that you will be moved to your new plan and therefore lose any existing unused images. Email api-help@photoroom.com if you notice any discrepancies.
  * If you **reduce images** , the change will **apply on your next billing date.** You can use your remaining images until then.


#### I canceled or downgraded my plan by mistake, can I correct this?
  * Cancelled plans: please contact our customer support team to assist you.
  * Downgraded plans: from [the API dashboard](https://app.photoroom.com/api-dashboard), select, “change plan” then select your new plan. Your new subscription date starts from the day you updated your plan.


#### Can I subscribe to an annual plan?
At this time, you can subscribe only to monthly plans. If you’re processing more than 200k images per year, contact sales@photoroom.com for custom pricing options. 
#### Do you have preview images?
For prototyping/testing you can sign up for a free trial with 10 images and the sandbox (1,000 images per month with Image Editing API). 
#### Can I rollover any of the plans?
Unused images will not roll over month-to-month.
#### Can I select any amount of images or is it fixed amounts?
You can purchase a fixed amount (for example, 1000, 2000, etc.) from your API dashboard.
#### What's the return on investment for the API plans?
Use our [ROI calculator](https://www.photoroom.com/api/roi-calculator) to discover how much additional revenue you can earn by improving your product photos with Photoroom's API. Enter a few numbers, and we'll run the estimate.
#### I have another question
For any other questions, please review our [technical FAQ](https://docs.photoroom.com/getting-started/frequently-asked-questions) or [join our Slack community](https://www.photoroom.com/join-api-community).
