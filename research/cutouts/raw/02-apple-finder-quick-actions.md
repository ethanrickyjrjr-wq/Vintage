# Perform quick actions in the Finder on Mac
Quick actions help you perform certain tasks right from the Finder or your desktop, without opening an app. For example, you can create a PDF or rotate an image.
The types of tasks available in the Quick Actions menu and at the bottom of the Preview pane depend on the file type and any enabled Finder (Quick Actions and Preview Pane) extensions.
![The bottom of the Preview pane showing the Quick Actions buttons.](https://ipcdn-web.apple.com/assets/v2/web/ae9f8fc8-4779-4609-ac37-75b0a487bd4b)
  1. On your Mac, click ![the Finder icon](https://ipcdn-web.apple.com/assets/v2/web/5054cf62-c8e5-4672-a8b0-120a387ebe79) in the [Dock](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/aside/glosc3855c74/27/mac/27) to open a Finder window.
  2. Select the file.
Alternatively, you can select a file on your desktop.
  3. Control-click the file, then choose Quick Actions. Depending on the file type (options vary), you can do the following:
     * _Rotate an image or movie:_ Choose Rotate Left or press and hold the Option key, then choose Rotate Right. Repeat to continue rotating the item.
     * _Mark up a document or image:_ Choose Markup. The file opens in a Quick Look window, where you can use the markup tools to add notes, edit text, and more. See [Mark up files](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mark-up-files-mchl1fd88863/27/mac/27).
     * _Trim a movie or audio file:_ Choose Trim, then drag the yellow handles in the trimming bar. To test your changes, click Play. You can start over by clicking Revert. When you’re ready to save your changes, click Done, then choose to replace the original file or create a new one.
     * _Create a PDF:_ Click More, choose Create PDF, then enter the name. To combine multiple files into a PDF, see [Combine files into a PDF](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/combine-files-into-a-pdf-mchl21ac2368/27/mac/27).
     * _Convert an image to another format or a smaller size:_ Click More, choose Convert Image, then choose a format and a size. For example, you can convert an .heic photo from your iPhone to a .jpeg or .png image.
     * _Remove the background of an image:_ Click More, then choose Remove Background.
     * _Customize and add more options:_ Click More, then choose Customize. Extensions settings opens, where you can add extra Finder extensions, such as Remove Background. See [Change Login Items & Extensions settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/change-login-items-extensions-settings-mtusr003/27/mac/27).
You can [use the Shortcuts app to add your favorite shortcuts](https://support.apple.com/guide/shortcuts-mac/apd163eb9f95) to the Quick Actions menu. You can download your favorite quick actions from third-party developers in the Mac App Store. You can also use the Automator app to [create workflows for Quick Actions](https://support.apple.com/guide/automator/aut7cac58839) that you can enable as extensions and access in the Finder, the Touch Bar, or the Services menu.


**Tip:** You can use the Quick Actions buttons at the bottom of the Preview pane in a Finder window. If you don’t see the Preview pane on the right, choose View > Show Preview.
**See also**[Use the Preview pane in the Finder on Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/use-the-preview-pane-in-the-finder-on-mac-mchl1e4644c2/27/mac/27)
