## Tips for Getting the Best Valuation
  * The 6-digit creed serial number (e.g., 9XXX-1234) dates the bag — pre-1994 codes are highly desirable
  * Hangtag leather quality matters: thick, butter-soft, naturally darkening = retail vintage; thin & uniform = outlet/factory
  * Outlet/factory bags (signature C canvas, lightweight hardware, "Coach Factory" creed) rarely resell above $40
  * Photograph the inside creed stamp, serial number, and the hardware backplate — those identify production era
  * Original dust bag and care card add 10–25%; the hangtag (round leather oval) is the strongest authenticity signal


## Frequently Asked Questions
### How do I know if my Coach bag is a real vintage piece or an outlet/factory bag?
Vintage retail Coach (1970s–1990s) is heavy glove-tanned leather, brass hardware, a creed stamp inside that doesn't say "Coach Factory", and a serial number starting with a single digit (not letters). Outlet/factory bags say "Coach Factory" on the creed, use lightweight hardware, and often have signature C canvas. The retail vintage market pays $100–$2,000; outlet bags rarely clear $50 on resale.
### Are Bonnie Cashin Coach bags really worth thousands?
Yes, the strongest pieces. Coach's 1962–1974 collaboration with designer Bonnie Cashin produced bucket bags, kisslock clutches, and crossbody bags in vegetable-tanned leather. Authentic Cashin-era bags with the original hangtag, brass turnlocks, and undamaged leather regularly sell $200–$2,000+. The largest market is on eBay, Vestiaire, and dedicated vintage Coach Instagram dealers.
### Where should I sell a vintage Coach bag?
Vestiaire Collective and The RealReal handle authentication well for retail pieces over $200. eBay works for everything below that with clear creed and hardware photos. Poshmark and Mercari move outlet/factory bags fastest at lower price points. Dedicated vintage Coach resellers on Instagram pay premium for Bonnie Cashin pieces.
