**Autograph Grading:** A single signed item (which includes autographed baseballs) would receive one grade - a grade reflecting the condition of the autograph only. Read about the [PSA/DNA Autograph Grading Process](https://www.psacard.com/services/autographgradingprocess) for more details. 
**Baseball Grading:** Autographs on a baseball will receive two individual grades for the condition of the autograph and baseball, averaged to an overall grade.

#### ENCAPSULATION:
Turnaround time is approximately 30 business days once we receive your submission into our system. Tamper-evident, sonically-sealed cases are used to encapsulate autographed trading cards, photographs, checks, index cards, postcards and cuts. The attractive, hard plastic cases provide protection from pressure and most damage.
  1. All autographs submitted for encapsulation must be submitted on an Autograph Encapsulation Submission form as a separate order from items not being encapsulated (separate shipping charges apply). 
  2. If encapsulation in our tamper-evident holder is selected, the submitter will forgo the Certificate or full Premium Letter, PSA/DNA tamper-evident label and DNA daub. The customer may request to receive a Premium Letter of Authenticity in addition to encapsulation for an additional $15. 
  3. Please note that encapsulation is only available for items that fit in [Holders Currently Available](https://www.psacard.com/services/cardspsagrades#holders). Any item requiring a Supersized or T3 holder will be subject to an additional $7 holder charge and any item requiring a Jumbo holder will be subject to an additional $30 holder charge, excluding Reholder Services.
