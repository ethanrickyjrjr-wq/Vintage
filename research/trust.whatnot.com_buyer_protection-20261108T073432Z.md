## Whatnot Buyer Protection
![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/69f897418b033a4eff9befd3/fa021685-99e7-4dcf-adc7-01108a704950/Whatnot_Buyer_Protection_EN_.jpg)
  * ## See it. Buy it. We’ve got your back.
Buying from someone new online can come with questions.
_Is that the actual item? What condition is it in? Can I trust this seller? What if it never arrives? What if what shows up isn’t what I expected?_
On Whatnot, you can get answers before you buy. In a live show, you can see products on camera, talk to the person selling them, and ask questions in real time. You get a better look at what you’re buying and who you’re buying from before you decide to purchase.
And once you buy, [_Whatnot Buyer Protection_](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/articles/360061194552-Whatnot-Buyer-Protection) is there in case you need it. Here’s how it works.
  * ## Before I buy: I can see what I’m getting
You spot something you want in a live show. Want a closer look at that corner? Curious about the condition, size, or what comes with it? Wondering where a vintage piece came from? Ask the person selling it.
That’s part of what makes shopping live different. You can see the item, talk to the seller, and get your questions answered before you buy. It brings more of the buying experience out into the open and gives sellers a chance to earn your trust in real time.
  * ## I bought it. Now what?
You made your purchase, and your order is on its way. If something doesn’t go as expected, you can feel confident knowing you’re not on your own. Your [eligible](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/articles/360061194552-Whatnot-Buyer-Protection) purchase is backed by Whatnot Buyer Protection.
You’re covered if your order:
    * **Doesn’t arrive**
It wasn’t shipped, gets lost or delayed in transit, or never reaches you.
    * **Arrives damaged**
It’s broken, defective, expired, or unusable.
    * **Isn’t what you purchased**
It’s the wrong item, something is missing, it doesn’t match what was described, or it isn’t authentic.
Get what you purchased, or we’ll make it right.
  * ## Something went wrong. What do I do?
Most orders arrive just as expected. But if something isn’t right with yours, you can contact Whatnot for help. Click the Activity button in your app, tap the order, and select “Get help with this purchase.” Tell us what happened, including as much detail as possible and any relevant photos, and we’ll guide you through the next steps.
  * ## Protection at every step
A lot happens behind the scenes at Whatnot to keep every purchase secure. Sellers are verified before they can sell, and our systems continue monitoring for fraud even after they’re approved. 
Our Trust & Safety team operates 24/7, and one in three Whatnot employees works on buyer and seller trust, from keeping bad actors off the platform to supporting you when something goes wrong. And eligible purchases are backed by Whatnot Buyer Protection, so you can shop and buy with confidence.
We’re building Whatnot to be the most trusted marketplace to buy and sell. That means working to earn your trust with every purchase.  
  
[**Learn more about Whatnot Buyer Protection →**](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/articles/360061194552-Whatnot-Buyer-Protection)


### Frequently Asked Questions
  * What is Whatnot's Buyer Protection? 
Buyer Protection covers eligible purchases if an item doesn't arrive, arrives damaged, or shows up differently from how it was described. If something goes wrong, you can file a claim through the app and get a refund.
  * How do I get a refund on Whatnot? 
Open the order in the app, tap ‘Request Help,’ and share what happened along with a photo if the item arrived damaged or wasn't as described. Most claims are resolved by the seller first; if they can't make it right, our team steps in.
  * How long do I have to file a claim? 
You have up to 30 days from purchase or 14 days from delivery, whichever comes first. Some categories have shorter windows—check the [_full policy_](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/articles/360061194552-Whatnot-Buyer-Protection-Policy) for details.
  * What happens if the seller doesn't respond?
If the seller doesn't respond within two business days or can't resolve the claim, Whatnot support takes over and reviews the claim directly. You'll get updates as the case moves forward.
  * Does Buyer Protection cover counterfeits?
Yes. Counterfeits are illegal, and they are not allowed on Whatnot. If an item turns out to be inauthentic or not as described, it's covered through [Buyer Protection](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/articles/360061194552-Whatnot-Buyer-Protection-Policy).
