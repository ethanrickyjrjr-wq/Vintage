## Description
### _Rare Speer portrait from the Hillbilly Cat’s first professional publicity shoot_
Vintage glossy 8 x 10 photo, signed on the reverse in red ballpoint “Love ya, Elvis Presley.” Also signed on the reverse in blue ballpoint by the Blue Moon Boys: Scotty Moore (adding “Best of Luck”), Jimmy Day, and Don Fontana, and in pencil by Bill Black. The original owner has also written Elvis’ name above the autographs. In good to very good condition, with light toning, soiling, and two old tape remnants on the signature side affecting nothing, and old tape, two areas of missing emulsion and paper, missing lower right corner, spotting, and surface impressions from signatures on the reverse. The signatures were obtained by the original owner at the Bono, AR. High School gymnasium, September 1955. This photo is from the Speers photo session—Elvis’ very first publicity photos session captured between November 1954 and March 1955—with a solemn Elvis portrayed with his hair curled. Pre-certified Roger Epperson/REAL and RR Auction COA.
