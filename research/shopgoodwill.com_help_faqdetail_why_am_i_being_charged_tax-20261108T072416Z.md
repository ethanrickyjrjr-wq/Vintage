##  [](/help "Back to help") Why am I being charged tax? 
ShopGoodwill.com collects sales and use tax on purchases made through the site in accordance with the 2018 U.S. Supreme Court South Dakota v. Wayfair decision and/or marketplace facilitator regulations enacted by individual states.
  

Sales tax is determined based on the property being purchased, and the ship-to location.
All taxes are calculated at the rate and in compliance with local and federal mandates.
In some states, shipping and/or handling is taxable.
  

Further information on the taxability of internet purchases can be found at your state's department of taxation or revenue website or via your tax provider.
