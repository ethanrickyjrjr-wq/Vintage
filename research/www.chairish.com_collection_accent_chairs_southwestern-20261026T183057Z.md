Few things add ambiance like a Southwestern accent chair. Whether you own an authentic adobe home, or you’re simply looking to imbue your house with the spirit of the southwest, nothing can conjure a desert mood like a southwestern style accent chair. Ranging from primitive and rustic to colorful and playful, southwest style chairs run the gamut, making it easy for designers and DIY-ers to find one that fits the bill perfectly. 
**Capture Hacienda-Inspired Style with Southwest Style Chairs**
Southwest chairs are ideal for adding flourish to not only southwestern-style homes, but Spanish-style, Mediterranean-style, Arts and Crafts-style, cabin-style, or ranch-style homes. Southwestern accent chairs intermix seamlessly with [Monterey](https://www.chairish.com/maker/monterey-furniture)-style furniture as well as Mission and Arts and Crafts style furniture. 
**Discover Sarape Upholstery, Leather Upholstery, and More**
There is no limit to the materials used in the construction of southwest chairs. Wood and leather chairs are perhaps the most common materials, but those desiring unexpected texture and whimsy will find plenty of alternatives as well. Try an Equipale Barrel chair, which is traditionally made of leather and dried Mexican cedar strips, or a cowhide steer and horn chair. The latter, featuring a frame made entirely of polished steer horns is an undeniable statement piece that can be put to work solo, or in pairs, to cue up a show-stopping moment in a living room or den.
