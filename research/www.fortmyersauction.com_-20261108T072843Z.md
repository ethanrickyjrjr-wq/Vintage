#  **Welcome To Fort Myers Auction**
##  **Turn your assets into cash through our online auctions**
Fort Myers Auction is a respected online auction company founded in 2012. We specialize in online auctions, working with private individuals, financial institutions, businesses and local governments to liquidate repossessed or unneeded assets. We frequently sell firearms, cars, boats, campers, equipment, heavy machinery and more! We host regular Tuesday night auctions that attract thousands of buyers each week. Our loyal bidders are ready and prepared to bid on your items! Our other major business and estate auctions are held on a regular basis, you can check our online auction site for upcoming events.
We operate out of Fort Myers but host online auctions all throughout South West Florida. If you are interested in selling your personal or repossessed items with us, read through our seller information as well as this list of items we will not accept in our online auctions. If you have any questions about selling with us or participating in one of our auctions please contact us! We are here to meet your auction needs.
