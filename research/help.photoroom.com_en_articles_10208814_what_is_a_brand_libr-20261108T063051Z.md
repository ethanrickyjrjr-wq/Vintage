# What is a Brand Library?
Learn how to create and use a Brand Library to save time when editing images in Photoroom.

A Brand Library is a shared collection of your brand’s design elements. It stores them in one central location so that they can be used by everyone in a Space. 
There is one Brand Library per Space and it can include:
  * Brand info, such as brand name, description, website address and brand style
  * Logos
  * Colors
  * Fonts
  * Models for AI Fashion Models
  * Backgrounds
  * Cutouts (text layers, graphic layers, and more).

# Why use a Brand Library?
With a Brand Library, your team shares one set of logos, colors, fonts, and other brand assets across the Space. Everyone uses the same assets in their designs. This helps you:
  * Keep designs consistent and professional across your Space
  * Spend less time editing, as the right assets are always in one place.
