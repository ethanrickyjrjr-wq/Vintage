## Where does Fleek's stock come from?
Most of Fleek's stock passes through South Asia, especially India and Pakistan, because the region has become a global hub for secondhand clothing. Donations collected in wealthier countries generate more volume than local thrift shops can absorb or sell profitably, so excess stock is shipped in bulk to South Asia, where the infrastructure exists to process it at scale.
  * Donations in the US, UK, and Europe exceed what local resale channels can handle, leaving large volumes to be exported overseas (Brooks & Grüneisl, 2025).
  * Massive import capacity - Pakistan imported ~809,000 tonnes of used clothing in 2023, worth around US$390 million, ranking among the world's top importers (Shahid et al., 2023).
  * Efficient sorting and redistribution - India and Pakistan have decades of experience grading garments by quality and style, supported by lower costs and established re-export networks.


For Fleek customers, this means access to stock that is cheaper than sourcing from European wholesalers, while benefiting from the variety and scale of South Asia's sorting hubs.

## What to expect when you start reselling
![Vintage clothing quality grading system showing A, B, and C grade categories for wholesale reselling](https://d29qs7eov4xhfc.cloudfront.net/c8e00710-9576-11f0-8784-176cac1b786a_quality-and-condition.png)
### Quality and condition
  * ●~20% of items may need minor cleaning or repair (normal in wholesale)
  * ●A-grade items are ready to sell immediately
  * ●B-grade items may have minor flaws but are still highly sellable
  * ●C-grade items need some TLC but offer the highest profit margins


![Tips for selling on Vinted](https://d29qs7eov4xhfc.cloudfront.net/cbb239e0-9576-11f0-8784-176cac1b786a_tips.png)
### Tips for selling on Vinted
  * ●List 15+ items across at least 2 platforms (Vinted, Depop, eBay)
  * ●List up to 5 items daily (Vinted and Depop's algorithm rewards consistency over batch uploads)
  * ●Accept your first 5 offers to boost your profile visibility
  * ●Optional: Use a free cross-lister tool to list faster e.g. SellerAider

## Fleek's shipping & delivery
Most Fleek orders arrive within 21–28 working days, with faster delivery for domestic and regional routes. Tracking is shared by email and in the app once activated, and our support team is here if delays occur.
### Domestic delivery
  * ●Orders shipped within the same country move the fastest.
  * ●Deliveries within the UK or US are usually completed in 6-7 business days.
  * ●Deliveries within the EU are typically completed in 7-9 business days.


### Cross-border delivery within Europe
  * ●When stock is shipped between the UK and EU, the delivery window is slightly longer.
  * ●These orders usually arrive within 10-11 business days.


### International orders
  * ●For shipments between regions such as Europe, the US, and South Asia, the average timeline is 21-28 working days.
  * ●Orders from India and Pakistan follow a two-hop process:
  * ◦The first stage is a local cargo flight, which may not show tracking and can take up to 10 days.
  * ◦Once the shipment reaches the destination country, it is handed over to a major courier such as DHL, UPS, or DPD, where full tracking becomes available and final delivery is usually fast.
