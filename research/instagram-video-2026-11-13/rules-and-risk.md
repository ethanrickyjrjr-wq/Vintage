## 1. Meta AI labeling

Source (fetched): https://www.meta.com/help/artificial-intelligence/1783222608822690/ ("How to identify AI content on Meta products", updated "2 weeks ago" per the page)

- Rule for video/audio: "On Facebook, Instagram and Threads, Meta may require an AI label when content has photorealistic video or realistic-sounding audio that was digitally created, modified or altered, including with AI. This requirement does not apply to images. However, images may still receive a label if the systems detect that they were AI-generated or if they were modified using AI."
- Penalty: "There may be penalties for content shared without a label when it is required." (Penalties are not specified on the page.)
- Examples needing a label: "A video that appears realistic of a group of people walking around an outdoor market"; "A reel narrated with a realistic AI-generated voiceover"; "A song created using AI-generated vocals". Not needing one: "Video of an outdoor landscape, created in a style resembling a cartoon".
- Auto labels from metadata: "Some content that is made or modified using one of these tools may have an industry-standard signal that identifies the content as being created or modified using AI tools... These signals are read by Meta's systems to determine if a label is applied." "Content with signals we can identify indicating that it was created with AI will be labeled AI content. Content with signals indicating that it was modified using AI will not have the AI content label directly on the post... [details sit under] Options." The page does not name C2PA; "industry-standard signal" is its wording (C2PA link = unverified from this page).
- Meta's own tools: content made or edited with Meta AI "may be automatically labeled as AI content or in some cases, feature a visible watermark."
- Self-label: people "may also be able, and in some cases required, to label their content". Caveat: "Not all AI content contains the information needed to identify it."
- Instagram's own "Label AI content on Instagram" page (https://help.instagram.com/761121959519495) fetched but only a shell (steps are app-only); how-to-toggle steps unverified.
- Does a label hurt reach? No official statement found on either page. Unverified; any claim either way is opinion. Separately, Meta's "reducing distribution" page does not list AI labels as a demotion category.

Implication for us: a reel with a realistic AI voiceover or realistic AI-generated video needs the label. A real-footage reel with stock music needs none. An AI-altered still image is not required to be labeled, but may be labeled automatically.

## 2. Promotion, branded content, comment-to-DM, engagement bait

- Branded content: https://help.instagram.com/1372533836927082 covers creators/advertisers using paid partnerships. Eligibility lines: "Share accurate information" and "Comply with our Community Standards". This is about partnership ads; promoting our own items in our own account is ordinary organic posting. The page says nothing on own-product promotion specifically. A paid-partnership label only matters if we work with a brand or creator (inference).
- Engagement bait (official): https://transparency.meta.com/enforcement/taking-action/lowering-distribution-of-problematic-content/ (updated Apr 25, 2025): "Meta may reduce its distribution... Low-quality content, such as clickbait and engagement bait." It does not define engagement bait and does not mention "comment for the link".
- Spam policy, https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/spam/: prohibits "Requiring or claiming that users are required to engage with content (e.g., liking, sharing) before they are able to view or interact with promised content" and "Like/share-gating: Requiring users to engage (in the form of likes, shares, follows, or any other public-facing form of engagement) to gain access to specific, exclusive content." Also bars giveaways of "cash or cash equivalents in exchange for engagement" and "anything of monetary value in exchange for engagement."
- Recommendations (official, creators.instagram.com): content breaking Recommendation Guidelines "can still be distributed normally to your followers" per the page; repeated breaches or a bio/profile photo that breaks them can make "your entire account... ineligible for recommendation." Same page: avoid "visibly recycled content" with watermarks, post original content, 3 minutes or less. https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators
- Comment-to-DM (ManyChat style "comment LINK"): COULD NOT VERIFY. No Meta page fetched addresses it. The nearest rule is the like/share-gating line above, which concerns gating "exclusive content" behind public engagement. A comment-for-link automation arguably resembles that, but that reading is mine, not Meta's. Safer pattern: put the eBay link in bio/story link sticker and say "link in bio"; if automation is used, stay with Meta's approved messaging tools (not verified).

## 4. Truth in advertising and eBay

FTC (fetched mirror: https://search.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business, "Advertising FAQ's: A Guide for Small Business"):
- "The FTC looks at the ad from the point of view of the 'reasonable consumer'... Rather than focusing on certain words, the FTC looks at the ad in context - words, phrases, and pictures - to determine what it conveys to consumers."
- Deceptive if it "contains a statement - or omits information" that is likely to mislead and is "material - that is, important to a consumer's decision to buy"; material claims include "performance, features, safety, price, or effectiveness." (An item's condition and appearance are plainly material; that application is my inference.)
- Omission example: ad is deceptive if it fails to disclose the buyer would get abridged books.
- The FTC pages specifically on endorsements and on AI images returned "Page Not Found" or walls on the ftc.gov host. No FTC statement on AI-generated ad imagery was fetched: UNVERIFIED. Blogs found by search claiming FTC AI-image rules are secondary.

eBay picture policy (fetched): https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/listing-policies/picture-policy?id=4370
- Not allowed: "Photos that don't accurately represent the item"; "Stock photos for used, damaged, or defective items"; "Photos with added borders"; "Photos with added text, artwork or marketing material"; "Watermarks of any type". Penalties: "Removing the listing or other content, issuing a warning, restricting activity or account suspension."
- The page has no AI-specific wording. eBay's rule is about accuracy, not the tool. (Search-result blogs say the same; unfetched.)
- Applies to listing photos. Instagram videos are not listing photos, so the picture policy does not govern them directly (my reading). It does govern what we put in the eBay listing: no AI-regenerated garment photos, no promo graphics baked into listing images.
- Driving traffic off eBay and eBay links policy (https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/listing-policies/links-policy?id=4277): WALLED (verification page). Linking from Instagram TO eBay is the reverse direction and that page covers links inside listings anyway; eBay's rules on external promotion: UNVERIFIED.
- "Item not as described" return rules (https://www.ebay.com/help/buying/returns-refunds/item-not-described-returns?id=4666) and seller non-performance policy: both WALLED. UNVERIFIED from eBay text. The shop's own approved policy (CLAUDE.md, Ricky): seller pays return postage for "damage, faults, wrong items or description errors", 30 days.
- Risk reasoning (inference, not eBay text): if a promo video shows an AI-brightened, smoothed or recoloured garment, hides a flaw, or shows a different fit/colour than the listing, a buyer can claim the item did not match what they saw. The listing is the contract; a video cannot fix mismatch and can create a second source for the claim. Keep color, fit, flaws and condition faithful to real photos, and keep video claims the same as listing text.

## 6. Real person's likeness and voice

ElevenLabs Prohibited Use Policy (fetched, https://elevenlabs.io/use-policy): prohibits "Cloning, synthesizing, or impersonating the voice, identity, or likeness of any living individual without their explicit and informed consent or other valid authorization". Also: "infringing the intellectual property or publicity rights of others, for example by... a person's voice or likeness without authorization." Minors: "You may not submit voice recordings of anyone under 18".
ElevenLabs help (fetched, https://help.elevenlabs.io/hc/en-us/articles/36842751624209-Can-I-create-a-Professional-Voice-Clone-of-someone-else-s-voice): "You can only create a Professional Voice Clone of your own voice. Even with their consent, you cannot clone someone else's voice. All Professional Voice Clones require a verification process to confirm that the voice belongs to you."
Terms of Use (fetched, https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use) 4(b): a voice model of "your voice or a voice you are authorized to share"; 4(d) licence lets ElevenLabs use "your voice, and other indicia of your persona" in your Content to provide and improve the service. So Hollie cloning her own voice is allowed, but she personally must do the verification and accept that licence. Only this one tool was checked; other tools' terms (face-swap, image-to-video) were not.

## Rules for our videos
1. Label AI realism: if a reel has a realistic AI voiceover, AI vocals or photorealistic AI-generated video, turn on Instagram's AI label. Cartoon-style or real footage needs none. Source: Meta AI-content page (section 1).
2. Show the real garment. Do not use AI to change color, fit, fabric or hide flaws; the eBay listing photo must "accurately represent the item" and stock/AI substitutes for used items are out. Sources: eBay picture policy; FTC FAQ ("pictures" judged in context).
3. Flaws in the listing must appear in the video's captions or footage too; omissions count as deception. Source: FTC FAQ (omissions, material claims).
4. Keep video claims identical to the listing (size, condition, price). A mismatch is a description error that, under our policy, the shop pays to return. Source: shop returns policy (CLAUDE.md); eBay INAD page unverified.
5. Use music from Meta's Sound Collection (or music we hold a licence for); avoid trending commercial tracks on the business account. Source: Meta music blog; business-account limits unverified.
6. No "comment/like/share to get the link or item" gating. Use link in bio, story link sticker or a plain "ask me" and answer manually. Source: Meta Spam policy (like/share-gating). Comment-to-DM bots: unverified, avoid until checked.
7. Post original, unwatermarked, vertical, under 3 minutes video; no recycled clips with other apps' watermarks. Source: Instagram creators recommendations page.
8. Hollie's voice clone only with her own verified recording and consent, on a tool whose terms allow it (ElevenLabs: own voice only, her own verification). Never clone someone else, or a minor. Source: ElevenLabs use policy and help page.
9. Do not add text, borders or watermarks to eBay listing images (promo overlays belong only on the Instagram video). Source: eBay picture policy.
10. Before any Florida-specific AI claim or paid use, check separately; this lane did not verify Florida law.
