## Frequently asked questions
#### What is an AI video ad maker?
An AI video ad maker is a tool that creates short video ads for social media without filming or editing. You give it your product, and it handles the concept, script, visuals and voiceover. There are three main types: general AI tools that generate video from a text prompt, AI avatar tools that create talking-head videos, and e‑commerce tools built specifically for product ads. [Photoroom’s AI video ad maker](https://app.photoroom.com/video-ad-maker) is built for e‑commerce: it turns your product photos into UGC-style ads with AI characters, product showcases and TikTok trend videos for Meta, TikTok and YouTube.
#### What is the best AI video generator for making ads?
It depends on the type of ad you need. General AI assistants like ChatGPT and Gemini can write scripts, but you still need another tool to make the video. Video editors like CapCut and Canva give you control, but you assemble the ad yourself. AI avatar tools create talking-head videos where the product usually stays a static image. For e‑commerce brands, Photoroom’s AI video ad maker is built specifically for product ads. It starts from your own product photos, recommends the best setup for what you sell, writes the script and shows you a shot-by-shot video plan before you generate. You get UGC-style ads with AI characters, product showcases and TikTok trend videos in 9:16 and 16:9, with voiceovers in multiple languages, without filming, editing or writing prompts.
#### Can Photoroom make video ads from product photos?
Yes. Photoroom turns your product photos into short video ads for Meta, TikTok and YouTube. Add photos of your product, choose a UGC, product or TikTok trend video, and Photoroom recognizes your product and suggests the best length, style and creative angle. You then review a shot-by-shot video plan, the script and the AI voiceover before generating. You can import photos from your computer, Shopify store, Google Drive or Dropbox, so there’s nothing to re-upload.
#### How can I make a video ad without filming?
There are three ways to make a video ad without filming: animate product photos with an image-to-video tool, assemble stock footage in a video editor, or use an AI video ad maker that creates the full ad for you. Animating photos gives you motion but no story, and stock footage won’t show your actual product. Photoroom’s AI video ad maker creates the complete ad from your product photos: an AI character presents your product, an AI voiceover reads the script, and you get a finished UGC-style ad or product showcase without a camera, studio or actors.
#### How long should a video ad be?
Most social media video ads work best when they’re short, because viewers decide in the first few seconds whether to keep watching. Short ads of under 15 seconds suit feeds, Reels, Stories and TikTok. Hook viewers in the first seconds and show the product early. Photoroom creates video ads of 5, 8 or 10 seconds and recommends the right length for your product: a quick 5-second beat, a balanced 8-second showcase or a full 10-second product overview.
#### How do I make a Facebook video ad for my product?
To make a Facebook video ad, create a short vertical video (9:16) that shows your product in the first seconds, then upload it to Meta Ads Manager to run on Facebook and Instagram feeds, Reels and Stories. You can film it yourself, hire a creator, or use an AI video ad maker. With Photoroom, you make the ad from your product photos: choose a UGC video with an AI character for a creator-style ad, or a product video to put the item centre stage. Make a few versions with different angles and test which one performs best.
#### Can ChatGPT make video ads?
ChatGPT is useful for brainstorming ad ideas, writing scripts and drafting captions, but it isn’t built to produce finished video ads for e‑commerce. You would still need a video tool to turn the script into an ad featuring your product. Photoroom’s AI video ad maker does the whole job in one place: it starts from your product photos, suggests creative angles, writes the script and creates the finished ad with an AI character, voiceover and the right format for Meta, TikTok or YouTube.
#### Is an AI video ad maker worth it for a small business?
For most small businesses, yes. A professional video shoot, UGC creators or an agency can cost hundreds or thousands per video and take weeks, which makes it hard to test enough ads to find a winner. AI video ad makers cut both the cost and the time, so you can make new creatives every week. Photoroom’s AI video ad maker is included in the Max and Ultra plans, so there’s no separate video subscription. You can also edit your product photos and make your video ads in the same tool.
#### How do I create a video ad in multiple languages?
If you sell to customers in other countries, ads in their language usually perform better than English-only ads. Traditionally that means hiring translators and voice actors for every market, which is out of reach for most small sellers. You can also translate on-screen captions, but captions alone feel less personal than a voice speaking the customer’s language. With Photoroom, you choose the voiceover language before you generate, and an AI voiceover reads a script written to fit your video. Create the same ad in several languages, keep the same AI character and voice across every version, and test new markets without extra production costs.
