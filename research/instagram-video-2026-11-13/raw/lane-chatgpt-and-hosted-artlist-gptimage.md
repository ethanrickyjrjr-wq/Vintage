#  GPT-Image-2 Family 
6 min read
_Updated: November 8, 2026_
**Image Model — generation & editing**
**UPDATE — November 8, 2026:** GPT Image 2.6 Vector makes SVG files from a prompt: logos, icons and simple illustrations, in Flare or Sunburst at Low, Medium or High quality. 2.6 also covers text-to-image and editing (Low to High quality, Reasoning control, 1K to 4K, up to 16 references) and a GIF option for short loops.
This page covers the full [GPT-Image-2](https://artlist.io/ai/models/gpt-image-2) family: the original GPT-Image-2 release, GPT-Image-2.5 and GPT-Image-2.6.
GPT-Image-2 is OpenAI’s flagship image model, strongest on photorealism and on text that stays legible — signs, UI, packaging, infographics.
Since 2.5 it ships in two variants: Flare for everyday speed, Sunburst for precision work, both covering generation and editing.
Pick it over Nano Banana or Flux when a job depends on rendered text or on repeated edits to the same subject.

## Key Features
### Image Editing
  * **Scoped Edits:** Changes only what’s asked; subject, composition and background stay untouched
  * **Subject Consistency:** Distinctive features carry through across new settings, styles and compositions
  * **Long Sessions:** Quality holds over many rounds of revision instead of degrading
  * **Multi-Reference Input:** Up to 16 reference images in one edit, each taggable in the prompt


### Text-to-Image
  * **Text Rendering:** Near-perfect text on signs, UI and infographics, the model’s standout strength
  * **Instruction Following:** Handles detailed visual instructions and real-world information accurately
  * **Reasoning Control:** On 2.6, choose how much the model plans before rendering; Medium by default, High for the most careful layouts
  * **Transparent Backgrounds:** On 2.5, the background can be set to transparent or opaque for compositing work
  * **Extra Detail Tiers:** On 2.5, XHigh and Max spend more effort on the image for extra detail


### Animated GIF and Vector
  * **Prompt to GIF:** One prompt becomes a short loop of 2 to 12 frames, timed from 20 ms to 2 seconds per frame
  * **Prompt to SVG:** One prompt becomes a scalable vector file with clean flat shapes, ready to edit in design tools

## Credit Cost
Credits are charged per image, or per frame for GIFs. Flare and Sunburst cost the same. On 2.6, editing costs more than text-to-image; on 2.5 the two cost the same.

## Limitations
  * Sunburst buys precision with generation time, so it runs slower than Flare
  * 2.6 tops out at High; XHigh and Max are only on 2.5, and they add time on top of render time, so use them for finals, not for iterating
  * Seven preset aspect ratios only; panoramas and thin banners have to be composed wider and cropped
  * No mask or brush: edits are steered by the prompt alone, so name the region you want changed
  * GIFs are prompt-only and top out at 400px, so they suit stickers and loops, not hero animation
  * SVG is prompt-only and suits flat graphics; detailed scenes come out simplified and can take several minutes
  * 2.6 always renders an opaque background, SVG included; use 2.5 for cutouts
  * No seed control, so a prompt won’t reproduce the same image; lock a result by editing it forward


## Prompting Tips
  * Conversational full sentences beat keyword lists; the model reads a prompt the way a person would
  * Put rendered copy in quotes and say where it sits: "the sign reads 'OPEN LATE' in bold condensed sans"
  * For edits, name what should stay as well as what changes: "keep the background and lighting, replace only the label"
  * With several references, tag each image in the prompt and say what it contributes: subject from one, setting from another
  * For a GIF, describe the motion across the frames: "the cup fills from empty to full, then the steam rises"
  * For SVG, ask for flat shapes and a small palette: "flat two-color logo of a coffee cup with steam"
  * Iterate at Medium on Flare, then rerun the winner on Sunburst at High quality and High reasoning for the shipping frame
  * For a cutout headed into a composite, generate on 2.5 and ask for a transparent background up front
