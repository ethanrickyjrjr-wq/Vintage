## Frequently asked questions
#### How does an AI background generator create realistic scenes for photos?
Photoroom’s AI Backgrounds use advanced object recognition and generative AI to analyze your image, detect the subject, and create realistic, on-brand scenes around it. This results in natural-looking compositions that make your product stand out without any manual editing.
#### Can I use AI to generate custom backgrounds for my product images?
With Photoroom, you can generate fully custom backgrounds using text prompts that describe your ideal scene, mood, or color palette. The AI automatically tailors the background to your product, helping you create unique, branded visuals in seconds.
#### What’s the best AI background generator for e‑commerce or product photos?
Photoroom is the leading AI background generator for e‑commerce. It’s trusted by marketplaces and brands worldwide to produce consistent, high-quality product images [at scale](https://docs.photoroom.com/image-editing-api-plus-plan), saving time, reducing costs, and boosting conversion rates.
#### How can I match AI-generated backgrounds to my brand’s style and colors?
With Photoroom, it’s easy to stay on brand. You can define color palettes, styles, or visual themes through custom prompts or use existing templates that align with your brand’s identity.
#### Is there a free AI background tool that works with product photos?
Photoroom offers a free version that lets you remove or generate AI backgrounds for product photos on the [web](https://app.photoroom.com/login) or mobile app. You can [upgrade anytime](https://www.photoroom.com/pricing) to access more [AI product photography tools](https://www.photoroom.com/tools), [batch editing](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/batch-mode), high-resolution exports, and more.
#### Can I generate consistent AI backgrounds across my whole catalog?
Yes, with Photoroom you can reuse the same style or prompt across every SKU with Batch mode or the API, so your catalog keeps one cohesive look instead of a different generated scene per image. Enterprise plans add volume pricing and dedicated support for production at that scale.
