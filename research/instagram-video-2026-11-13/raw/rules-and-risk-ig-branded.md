Branded content ads are now called partnership ads.
To post organic [branded content](https://help.instagram.com/128845584325492?helpref=faq_content) or run [partnership ads](https://help.instagram.com/292748974937716/?helpref=related_articles), creators, advertisers, publishers, and other professional accounts must pass and remain compliant with our eligibility requirements.
## Eligibility requirements
In order to post branded content or run partnership ads, you must:
**Create content on an eligible surface**. At this time, partnership ads are available for public content on: 
  * Facebook Pages or profiles in professional mode
  * Instagram professional accounts


**Comply with our Community Standards.** These rules apply to all posts and interactions on Instagram and Facebook. Any content posted by publishers or creators that violates our [Instagram Community Guidelines](https://help.instagram.com/477434105621119?helpref=faq_content), [Facebook Community Standards](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards), and policies regarding intellectual property, authenticity, and user safety may make publishers or creators ineligible to monetize or cause them to lose their monetization eligibility.
If you believe you may have violated our policies regarding intellectual property, you can learn more about [copyright or trademark infringement](https://help.instagram.com/1445818549016877?helpref=faq_content).
**Share accurate information**. Creators and publishers posting content flagged as [misinformation and false news](https://about.fb.com/news/2017/04/working-to-stop-misinformation-and-false-news/) may be ineligible or may lose their eligibility to run branded content or partnership ads.
**Develop an established presence**. Creators and publishers must have an authentic, established presence on Instagram or Facebook. This also includes maintaining a sufficient follower base.
**Follow our rules for politicians and governments**. The following political and government entities are ineligible to run branded content:
  * Current elected and appointed government officials
  * Current political candidates
  * Political parties
  * Registered political committees
  * Government agencies and departments


More specifically, in the United States, the branded content tool may be used in branded content posts if the paying partner has been authorized to run ads about social issues, elections or politics with a connected Facebook Page. The branded content tool is not available for political entities outside of the United States. However, political advertisers can run partnership ads if they and their creator partner have authorization to run ads about social issues, elections or politics.
**Avoid systemic violations.** Monetized creators and publishers, and those that have not been onboarded to a monetization product, may lose the ability to monetize if an entity connected to it has repeatedly violated these requirements.
**Note:** Meta reserves the right to modify, suspend or terminate access to, or discontinue the availability of its content monetization features at any time.
For people residing in [the EU, the EEA and Switzerland](https://help.instagram.com/help/681634310058367?helpref=faq_content), your ability to advertise and monetize with ads is limited if your account is in an Accounts Center with a [subscription to use Meta Products without ads](https://help.instagram.com/help/262038446684066?helpref=faq_content) or a [less-personalized ad experience](https://help.instagram.com/business/help/825123042877166?helpref=faq_content). Learn more about [these limitations on your ability to advertise or monetize](https://help.instagram.com/business/help/1563729837497242?helpref=faq_content).
