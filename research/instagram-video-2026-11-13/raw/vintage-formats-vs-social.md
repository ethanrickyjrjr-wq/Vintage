_Even if it feels like you are in views jail, social media isn’t dead — here’s how these vintage sellers are making it work_
Despite the ever-changing algorithms and the constant hum of “is social media over?” — many vintage sellers are still using platforms like Instagram and TikTok to their full advantage. 
‍
In this practical, honest panel chat, Vintage Sellers Community members Lesley Metcalfe, Alex Ure and Brigid Milway talked about what’s working and what’s not when it comes to social media for resellers.
‍
From which content types perform best (spoiler: it's not the same for everyone!) to how they’re each approaching Reels, TikToks and building community, the panel shared real experiences, helpful tactics, and even a few social media boundaries they’ve put in place to avoid burnout.
