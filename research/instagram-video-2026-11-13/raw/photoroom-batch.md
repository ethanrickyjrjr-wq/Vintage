## Frequently asked questions
#### How can I edit multiple photos at once with AI?
Editing multiple photos at once usually requires a tool that can apply the same corrections across an entire batch. This avoids repeating the same steps for each image and helps keep colors, lighting and backgrounds aligned. [AI editors](https://www.photoroom.com/blog/ai-photo-editors-small-businesses) are especially useful because they can detect the product, adjust the subject and apply cleanup or framing across all files in a single flow. Photoroom uses AI to process full batches together. You upload your images, choose the edits you want and the tool applies them across every photo at the same time while keeping product details sharp and accurate.
#### What is the best way to edit product photos in bulk?
The most reliable approach to bulk editing is to use a batch editor that lets you apply backgrounds, lighting adjustments, cleanup and framing across all images with the same settings. This keeps your catalog visually aligned and removes the need to repeat corrections for each SKU. Bulk editing is especially helpful for ecommerce teams handling product drops, seasonal refreshes or marketplace updates. Photoroom supports bulk editing for small and large image sets. You select your preferred edits once and the platform applies them across full batches, giving you consistent results across every product photo with far less manual work.
#### Can I apply the same background to all my photos at once?
Yes. Many photo editors allow you to set one background and apply it across your full batch of images. This is an effective way to keep your catalog uniform, especially if you sell products in multiple colors or categories. The goal is to maintain the same visual identity across your entire storefront, which can be difficult when editing images one by one. Photoroom lets you apply a [single background](https://www.photoroom.com/batch/ai-backgrounds) across all your photos in batch. The AI isolates the product, keeps edges clean and maintains shadows so the final result looks natural and consistent across every SKU.
#### How do I keep my product photos consistent across a catalog?
Catalog consistency depends on using the same lighting, background, framing and cleanup style across all product photos. Doing this manually can introduce differences that make your listings look uneven. Batch editing solves this by applying identical settings across entire collections so every SKU follows the same visual rules. Photoroom gives you a batch workflow that keeps your product photos aligned. You choose the edits once and the AI applies them across all images, preserving product shape and detail while creating a unified catalog ready for marketplaces and ads.
#### Can I edit thousands of product photos in one go?
Large catalogs can be edited together as long as the tool supports high-volume uploads and bulk adjustments. This makes it possible to update lighting, backgrounds and framing across thousands of images without handling each file manually. Teams that manage frequent drops or seasonal changes rely on batch editing because it keeps the entire catalog up to date with far less effort. Photoroom is built to support large batches. You upload your full set, choose your edits and the platform applies them across every file with the same settings. The result is a consistent catalog that stays aligned even at scale.
#### What makes Photoroom’s AI effective for product photography in batch?
[AI product photography](https://www.photoroom.com/ai-product-photography) has different requirements than general image editing. It needs accurate subject detection, stable edge recognition and consistent lighting interpretation across hundreds of images. Photoroom’s AI is trained specifically for ecommerce products, which helps the system understand shapes, materials and silhouettes more reliably. This is why batch edits stay consistent across full catalogs, even when SKUs vary in size, color or packaging.
