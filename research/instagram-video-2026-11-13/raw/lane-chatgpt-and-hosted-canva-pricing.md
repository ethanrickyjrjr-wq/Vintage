## Frequently Asked Questions
  * * * *
  * Can I use Canva for free?
Canva Free is available to anyone. If you want to unlock more premium tools, Canva Pro is perfect for solo creators who want to design like a pro, with premium templates, and AI-powered features that make it easy to create standout content.
  

For individuals, marketers, and growing teams ready to scale their brand, Canva Business builds on Pro with even more powerful tools - smarter ad performance insights, enhanced brand management, more AI access, and more. Everything is designed to help you stay on brand, collaborate seamlessly, and grow your business with ease.
  

Want to see for yourself? You can start a free trial of Canva Pro or Canva Business.
  * * * *
  * How does AI usage work across Canva plans?
Canva's AI tools work in two ways, depending on how they're included in your plan.
  

    * **AI design tools:** Some AI tools like Translate and Magic Write can be used abundantly, and don’t count towards your monthly AI allowance. **This includes all ‘Standard AI’ tools for paid plans (excluding Pro Lite plan, where Standard AI draws from your monthly AI allowance).** Fair use limits apply.
    * **AI tools that share an AI allowance:** These draw from a shared monthly allowance that varies by plan, and resets each month.
  

Your total number of uses depends on:
    * The type of AI tools you use (higher-tier tools use more of your allowance)
    * How complex your task is
    * Whether you use a mix of different AI tools
  

**Estimated AI allowance by plan**
  

The ranges below show what’s possible if you use one type of AI tool (either Standard, or Premium, or Ultra - not a mix). Completing more complex tasks with a single tool or using a mix of tools may reduce your total uses to the lower end of the range.
  

**Your allowance by plan:** AI usage is pooled across all AI tools listed below, and resets monthly:
    * **Canva Free:** Limited access to Standard and Premium AI tools. No access to Ultra AI tools or Affinity’s Premium AI tools.
    * **Canva Pro:** 10x more AI allowance than Canva Free, for Premium and Ultra AI tools.
    * **Canva Business:** 20x more AI allowance than Canva Free, for Premium and Ultra AI tools.
    * **Canva Enterprise:** 20x more AI allowance than Canva Free, for Premium and Ultra AI tools.
  

If you want extra AI allowance, find more info under ‘How can I get more AI on my plan?’.
  

AI allowance during trial is the same as Canva Free. Access the full allowance (10x more) when you start your paid plan.
  * * * *
  * How can I get more AI on my plan?
There are a few ways to get more AI allowance:
    * Upgrade your plan for a higher monthly AI allowance.
    * **AI Pass** - a monthly add-on for Pro and Business users who subscribe online. Get Canva's largest AI allowance, refreshing every month. [Learn more](https://www.canva.com/help/ai-pass/).
    * **AI top-ups** - a one-time purchase for Business users who subscribe online. Get the same amount of AI as your monthly allowance, valid for 30 days. [Learn more](https://www.canva.com/help/ai-top-ups/).
  * * * *
  * Is Canva Pro available for teams?
Canva Pro is the best plan for solo creators who want to design like a pro and create visually stunning content with ease. Only one person can use a Canva Pro account.
