Understanding Canva's AI tools will help you maximize your monthly AI allowance, and design with confidence. Canva's AI tools work in two ways, depending on how they're included in your plan:
  * **AI design tools:** Some AI tools like Translate and Magic Write can be used abundantly, and don't count towards your monthly AI allowance. This includes all 'Standard AI' tools for paid plans (excluding Pro Lite). For Canva Free and Pro Lite, Standard AI tools still draw from the monthly AI allowance.
  * **AI tools that share an AI allowance:** These draw from a shared monthly allowance that varies by plan, and resets each month:
    * Standard AI tools (for Canva Free and Pro Lite)
    * Premium AI tools
    * Ultra AI tools


Your monthly AI allowance determines how much you can use AI tools each month. Higher plans include more AI allowance. Learn more about each AI tool type and how much AI usage your plan includes below.
### What are Standard AI tools?
On paid plans, Standard AI tools don't count towards your shared AI allowance*. On Canva Free and Pro Lite, Standard AI tools are part of your shared allowance and use the least amount of it. Standard AI tools include:
  * Image Upscaler
  * Magic Formulas


### What are Premium AI tools?
Premium AI tools are part of your shared AI allowance. AI tools in this tier are more advanced, and use more of your AI allowance for image, video, interactives, design generation, and more. Premium AI tools include:
  * Magic Layers
  * Generate a design with Canva AI
  * Create an image with Canva AI
  * Build interactive experiences with Canva Code
  * Generate elements with Canva AI (shapes, 3D, charts, forms, and more)
  * Music and Sound Effect Generator
  * Form generator
  * Premium Al tools in Affinity, including Generative Fill, Expand, Edit, Generate Images and Vectors. Premium AI tools in Affinity are not available to Canva Free accounts.


### What are Ultra AI tools?
Ultra AI tools are part of your shared AI allowance. AI tools in this tier use the latest cutting-edge AI models, and use the most AI allowance. Ultra AI tools include:
  * Conversational design with Canva AI 2.0
  * Create a video clip with Canva AI


### Plan Eligibility for Premium and Ultra AI Tools
Premium and Ultra AI tools are available with:
  * Canva Pro
  * Canva Teams
  * Canva Business
  * Canva Enterprise
  * Canva Nonprofit


See the plan comparison table on our [_pricing page_](https://www.canva.com/pricing/) for full details.
### What AI tools are available to me?
Availability of your AI tools depends on your plan. Your shared AI allowance can also be used with AI features available in Affinity. See the plan comparison table on our [_pricing page_](https://www.canva.com/pricing/) for full details.
### How much AI usage do I get with my plan?
The size of your shared AI allowance and your level of access to each type of AI tools (Standard, Premium, Ultra AI) [_depend on your plan_](https://www.canva.com/pricing/).
All AI tools share the same AI allowance. The ranges below show you what’s possible if:
  * You only use one type of AI tool (either Standard, or Premium, or Ultra - not a mix), and
  * Your prompt or task is simple, and
  * You use an AI tool that uses the least power of AI tools in its category


When you use more advanced AI tools and prompts frequently, your actual usage will typically be on the lower end of the estimated ranges.
For examples on which AI tools use more or less AI power in Standard, Premium, and Ultra AI, read **How does my AI allowance work?** below.
To learn more about how Canva 2.0 uses your AI allowance, see [**How does Canva 2.0 use my AI allowance?**](https://www.canva.com/help/ai-access/?query=ai%20usage#how-does-canva-2.0-use-my-ai-allowance-) below.
### AI allowance access
  * **Canva Free and Pro Lite** : Limited access to Standard and Premium AI tools. No access to Ultra AI tools or Affinity’s Premium AI tools.
  * **Canva Pro and Nonprofits** : Use Standard AI tools as much as you like*, plus 10x more AI allowance than Canva Free, for Premium and Ultra AI tools.
    * **Canva Pro trial** : Limited access to Standard and Premium AI tools. No access to Ultra AI tools or Affinity’s Premium AI tools.
  * **Canva Business** : Use Standard AI tools as much as you like*, plus 20x more AI allowance than Canva Free, for Premium and Ultra AI tools.
    * **Canva Business trial** : Limited access to Standard and Premium AI tools. No access to Ultra AI tools or Affinity’s Premium AI tools.
  * **Canva Enterprise:** Use Standard AI tools as much as you like*, plus 20x more AI allowance than Canva Free, for Premium and Ultra AI tools.
  * **Canva Education:** Use Standard AI tools as much as you like*, plus 10x more AI allowance than Canva Free, for Premium and Ultra AI tools.


For Canva Business, Nonprofits, Education, and Enterprise, each member has their own allowance. Allowances aren't shared or pooled across the team or organization.
*Fair use limits apply.
### How does my AI allowance work?
Your AI tools share a single monthly allowance, with some tools using more of it than others. The complexity of your prompt or task will also influence how much AI allowance is used. The ranges provided above are estimates. More complex prompts and tasks use more of your monthly AI allowance, so the number of uses you get will vary.
  * **Standard AI tools:** Magic Formulas can use less AI allowance than Magic Write for SEO
  * **Premium AI tools:** Style Match can use less AI allowance than creating interactives with Canva Code
  * **Ultra AI tools:** Generating and iterating on multi-page designs through conversation with Canva AI 2.0 will use more AI allowance than text-to-image


To get the most out of your monthly allowance for Canva’s most popular AI tools, see our [_AI prompting tips._](https://www.canva.com/help/ai-prompting-tips/)
## How does Canva AI 2.0 use my AI allowance?
Canva AI 2.0 works as a conversational design partner that can take multiple editing and design actions autonomously when prompted. Because of this, it uses more AI power than single-prompt features.
It uses your shared AI allowance. The amount of AI allowance used depends on the task. Your usage may be higher based on:
  * **Designs referenced:** When you include designs in a task, larger files use more AI allowance. For example, a 40-slide presentation will use more than a 5-slide presentation.
  * **Task complexity:** More complex tasks require more steps, which increases AI allowance usage.
  * **Tools used:** Tasks that use multiple AI tools consume more allowance than those that use fewer tools.
  * **Models used:** More complex tasks or higher quality outputs may use more advanced AI models, than simpler tasks. The more powerful the AI tool, the more AI allowance used.


To get the most out of Canva 2.0 while maximizing your AI allowance, explore our [_AI prompting tips._](https://www.canva.com/help/ai-prompting-tips/)
## How do I track my AI allowance?
#### Quick tip
You can check the AI usage tracker anytime from the **Billing** page in **Settings**.
[Go to Billing page](https://www.canva.com/settings/billing-and-teams)
### How to track AI allowance
DesktopDesktop
MobileMobile
  1. From the homepage, select your **profile icon**.
  2. Select **Settings**.
  3. Select **Billing**.
  4. Under **AI usage,** you can see your AI usage and remaining balance.


**Note:** Your Canva AI allowance covers Canva's own AI tools, including Canva tools you use in a connected AI assistant. If a message about allowance comes from another app rather than Canva, check that app's settings or contact its support team. 
### What happens when I reach my limit?
DesktopDesktop
For Standard, Premium, and Ultra AI tools, Canva will notify you as you near and reach your allowance limit. For ways to increase your AI allowance, see **How do I get more AI access?** below.
### AI allowance notifications
You’ll get a notification in Canva as you approach your AI allowance limit, so you can plan ahead.
### AI feature usage pauses
Paid plans won’t experience a pause with Standard AI tools as they don’t draw from your monthly AI allowance*.For Canva Free, if you reach your limit you’ll see short pauses between generations for Standard and Premium AI tools. 
Ultra AI tools aren't included in short pauses, so they won't be available until your monthly AI usage resets.
**Tips to maximize your AI allowance:**
  * Check out Canva’s guide on [_writing better prompts to maximize your AI allowance._](https://www.canva.com/help/ai-prompting-tips/)
  * Track your AI balance to plan ahead. See **Understanding your AI allowance** to learn how to check your AI balance.


### How do I get more AI access?
DesktopDesktop
If you’ve reached your limit, below are some of your options for getting more AI access. 
  * **Canva Free and Pro Lite:** Upgrade to another plan (for example, Canva Pro or Canva Business) to access more AI tools and a higher AI allowance. 
  * **Canva Pro and Canva Teams:** Upgrade your plan for more AI access, or purchase AI Pass as an add-on to your subscription. If you hit your limit, you can keep using AI tools with with short pauses between tasks.
  * **Canva for Business:** Purchase AI Pass or an AI top-up as an add-on to your subscription. 
  * **Enterprise and Canva Education:** If you hit your limit, you can keep using premium AI features with short pauses between tasks.
  * **Canva for Nonprofits** : Purchase AI Pass as an add-on to your subscription. If you hit your limit, you can keep using premium AI features with short pauses between tasks.


Otherwise, wait for your allowance to reset.
## Using your AI allowance in AI assistants
When Canva is connected to an AI assistant like ChatGPT or Claude, creating designs, generating images, and using Magic Layers use the same Canva AI allowance as they do in Canva. In ChatGPT and Claude, Canva shows your usage as you get close to your limit, and gives you a link to upgrade if you reach it. Learn more about [_connecting Canva to AI assistants_](https://www.canva.com/help/mcp-canva-usage/) to generate designs.
## How does this work with AI assistants connected to Canva?
Your AI allowance can also be used to generate designs with Canva AI within AI assistants that are connected to Canva. AI tools that you can generate designs within include ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, and more. Learn more about [connecting Canva to AI assistants](https://www.canva.com/help/mcp-canva-usage/) to generate designs.
## How does this work with Canva’s Apps Marketplace?
Generations made through third-party AI apps in the Marketplace aren’t included your AI allowance. Third-party subscriptions or limits may apply.
### Third-party app allowances and support
To find out about an app's usage limits or get support, you first need to identify who created it.
### Check who created the app
  1. Go to the app you want help with.
  2. Select the **More** on the app thumbnail.
  3. Look for the **Created by** information. 


**Get help with app usage and limits**
  * If you see **Created by Canva** , the app is supported by our team. For any questions about its usage [contact Canva Support. ](https://www.canva.com/help/ai-access/)
  * If a different name appears, the app is made by a third party. To ask about their specific usage limits or subscription requirements, select **Get help with this app** from the **More** to contact the developer directly. Canva support cannot assist with third-party app policies.


If you've seen apps that generate offensive or inappropriate content, report them by selecting the **More icon** on the app thumbnail, then selecting **Report**. You can also [report inappropriate content here](https://www.canva.com/help/report-content/).
