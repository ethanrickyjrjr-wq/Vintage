## Frequently asked questions
#### How does an AI virtual model generator work for clothing and fashion photos?
An AI virtual model generator uses generative AI to place clothing and other fashion items like jewelry, eyewear, and footwear onto lifelike digital models. With Photoroom, you can upload a product image, choose your preferred model type, and our AI automatically creates high-quality virtual model images with natural poses and lighting.
#### Can I use AI to create virtual models wearing my brand’s products?
Yes, you can easily generate virtual fashion models wearing your brand’s products. Upload a photo of your clothing item: a dress, a top, a pair of jeans, or whatever you sell, and Photoroom’s AI model generator automatically places it on a realistic model. 
#### What’s the best AI fashion model generator for e‑commerce or lookbooks?
Photoroom’s AI fashion model generator is the best solution for e‑commerce and fashion lookbooks. We combine AI virtual model generation with [background removal](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/background-remover) and [background generation](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/instant-backgrounds), [lighting adjustment](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/image-brightener), and more [AI photo editing tools](https://www.photoroom.com/tools) to help you create high-quality, consistent, and affordable e‑commerce visuals at scale.
#### How realistic are AI-generated models compared to real photoshoots?
AI virtual model tools are trained on natural poses, lighting, backgrounds, and material textures to produce realistic results that match the look and feel of a professional fashion photo shoot. With Photoroom, you can choose model styles, backgrounds, poses, and image dimensions, ensuring your AI fashion photography looks realistic, diverse, and on-brand.
####  How does Photoroom’s AI virtual model generator help fashion brands create realistic product photos?
Photoroom’s virtual model tool uses generative AI to automatically place clothing items on photorealistic models, adjust shadows and textures for accuracy, and create cohesive imagery for all your sales channels. 
#### Is AI Fashion Models part of the Photoroom API?
Yes. Virtual Model is fully accessible via the Photoroom API, allowing enterprise teams to integrate model photography directly into their existing workflows and automate it across their entire product catalogue.
