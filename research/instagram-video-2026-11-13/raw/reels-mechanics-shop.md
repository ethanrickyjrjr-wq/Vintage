Some features related to Shops and checkout on Facebook and Instagram are no longer supported. Learn more about [recent changes](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1314349509894768?helpref=faq_content).
With [Shops](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/2343035149322466?helpref=faq_content), you can display and sell products on Facebook and Instagram. People who visit your shop can browse your products, add products to their cart and get to know your brand. You’ll create your shop in [Commerce Manager](https://www.facebook.com/commerce_manager), a tool you use to manage your products and sales on Facebook and Instagram.
If you’re a Shopify seller you can easily [create shops and use Shops ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/139029445945711?helpref=faq_content) to promote your products.
## Before you begin
  * [Create a Commerce Manager account](https://business.facebook.com/business/help/842191386156027?helpref=faq_content).
  * **If you don’t have a Facebook business Page or catalog yet** : You can create both when you set up your shop.
  * **If you already have a Page and catalog you want to use for your shop** : You must have full control of the business portfolio if you use one to manage your Page and catalog. Make sure you also have full control of both the Page and catalog. [Learn more about managing access to a business asset.](https://business.facebook.com/business/help/299504287548592?helpref=faq_content)
  * **If you want to make your shop visible on Instagram** : You’ll need an [Instagram business account](https://www.facebook.com/help/502981923235522?helpref=faq_content) in your business portfolio. If you only want to sell on Instagram, you don't need to create or provide a Facebook business Page when creating your shop.


## Set up a shop on Facebook and Instagram
Start by going to [Commerce Manager](https://business.facebook.com/commerce). You should see **Set up your shop** at the top if you've successfully created a commerce account. You will need to complete a number of tasks to finish setting up your shop. This article will take you through each task.
## Create shop
This should already be marked as completed as your shop was created during the [Commerce Manager account creation](https://business.facebook.com/business/help/842191386156027?helpref=faq_content) process.
## Checking your shop info
![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=not-completed&density=1)**Not completed** indicates that your commerce account is in review. Our review process can take up to 4 weeks. We'll update the status of your reviews in Commerce Manager. We'll also send you an email if there are any actions you can take based on the results of your reviews.
You will see ![](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=completed&density=1)**Completed** when the review is completed.
## Optimize your catalog
Click **View details** to complete syncing tasks and access additional functionalities:
  * **Select catalog** : This option may or may not be visible. It allows you to select which catalog to use with your shop.
  * **Add product variants** : Upload a data feed that will be checked against products in your catalog to ensure you don’t lose product events data when adding variants.
  * **Change your dataset**(optional)**:** You can [add a dataset](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/750785952855662?helpref=faq_content) (if you don’t have one), replace an existing dataset or keep an existing dataset.


## Set up shipping profile
Click **Set up** to go to the **Set up default shipping profile** page. Here you can change a number of settings:
  * **Profile name**. Change the name of your shipping profile.
  * **Country** and **States**. Select the areas you will ship to.
  * **Shipping speed**. You must add at least one type of shipping speed. You can customize each speed by estimated delivery days and cart minimum for free shipping (if offered).
    * **Standard shipping** typically takes 5-8 days including handling time.
    * **Expedited shipping** takes 3-5 days including handling time.
    * **Rush shipping** takes 2-3 days including handling time.


When you’re finished, click **Save**.
## Add checkout URL
A checkout URL transitions customers from your shop on Facebook and Instagram to the checkout process on your website. You must [add a checkout URL](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1324891268731018?helpref=faq_content) for your shop to be visible.
## Optimize your shop
Click **Optimize shop** to select a few optional enhancements:
  * **Automatically personalize the layout of your shop’s home page** : Optimize your shop’s layout to show people a personalized version based on their shopping history and interests.
  * **Display ratings and reviews** : Show customer ratings and reviews from your website and third-party sources.
  * **Display offers** : Meta will [crawl](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/441625284575556?helpref=faq_content) your website and ads to detect offers, then display them in your shop to help drive conversions.


## Publish shop
When all the steps under **Set up your shop** are completed, click **Publish shop** to publish your new shop.
Once your shop is approved and published, your shop will also display a footer that shows your shop’s name, a featured image, customer ratings and the **Follow** and **Shop all products** buttons.
Next, you can:
  * [Add products to your catalog](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/384041892421495?helpref=faq_content) that you want to sell. All products from your catalog are included in your shop by default, but you can also [control which products appear](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/836266307249612?helpref=faq_content).
  * [Create a collection](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/2658959294372394?helpref=faq_content), a group of products you want to feature in your shop.
  * [Customize your shop ](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/300074297741275?helpref=faq_content)by featuring collections, products or promotion banners on your shop's home page. You can also control whether your shop is visible on Facebook and Instagram.
