## Frequently asked questions
#### How can I turn a product photo into a video with AI?
Photoroom turns product photos into videos with two AI tools. In [Product Animator](https://app.photoroom.com/u/ai-images/video-generator), upload 1 to 4 product photos, pick [one of 300+ templates](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/video-generator/templates), and download the video in under a minute. In [Video Ad Maker](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/video-generator/ad-maker), upload your photos and the [AI guides you](https://app.photoroom.com/video-generator) step by step through the shots, script, and voiceover. You can refine the result in chat. No filming or editing software is needed.
#### Why should I use video for e‑commerce products?
Product videos show what photos can't: movement, texture, shine, scale, and different angles. That helps shoppers understand a product before they buy. With Photoroom's AI video generator, sellers can make these videos from existing product photos for product pages, [marketplace listings](https://www.photoroom.com/industry/marketplaces), and [social media](https://www.photoroom.com/use-cases/social-media), without organizing a video shoot.
#### Can I create a product video with AI from one image?
Yes. Photoroom's Product Animator and Video Ad Maker both work from a single clear product photo. Adding up to four photos from different angles gives the AI more detail about the product's shape, materials, and branding, which keeps the video closer to the real item.
#### What makes Photoroom’s AI Video Generator different from other tools?
Photoroom's AI Video Generator is built for product videos, not general-purpose video. Sellers can remove the background or enhance a photo and then animate it, all in one platform. Product Animator gives fast, consistent results from 300+ templates. Video Ad Maker guides you to a custom video with a shot plan, script, and voiceover. Teams on Enterprise plans can also automate video creation with the [image to video API](https://www.photoroom.com/api/image-to-video-api).
#### What types of product photos can I turn into videos?
Photoroom's AI video generator works with clothing, footwear, bags, beauty, jewelry, furniture, food and drink, and most other product categories. Product Animator has category-specific templates with motions suited to each product type. Video Ad Maker can plan a custom video for any product you upload.
#### What should I look for in an AI product video generator?
For e‑commerce, look for a tool that works from existing product images, gives you control over the result, supports different product categories, and fits into your existing content workflow. [Product fidelity](https://www.photoroom.com/glossary/fidelity) also matters, especially when colours, materials, logos, and shape need to remain recognisable. Photoroom covers these with Product Animator and Video Ad Maker. If you’re comparing options, see our guide to the [best AI product video generators.](https://www.photoroom.com/blog/best-ai-product-video-generator-e-commerce)
#### What's the difference between Product Animator and Video Ad Maker?
Photoroom's two AI video tools differ in how much the AI guides you. Product Animator turns product photos into videos using 300+ ready-made templates, which suits fast, consistent results across a catalog. Video Ad Maker is a guided AI workflow: upload your photos, and it plans the shots, script, and voiceover with you. You can refine the result in chat and export 5, 8, or 10-second videos in 9:16 or 16:9. Both tools are available on [Max and Ultra plans](https://www.photoroom.com/pricing).
