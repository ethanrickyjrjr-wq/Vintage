## How to make AI-generated video
### Launch Canva
Open Canva on the app or web browser to start generating a video right where you work—be that from the homepage or in the editor.
### Access the AI video generator
From the homepage, select “Canva AI” and choose “Video clip” to start a conversation. If you’re in the editor, open the Elements tab to access the prompt box.
### Create AI video
Write your prompt to generate a video clip for up to eight seconds. Make it detailed — include your preferred style, framing, and lighting. Then hit “Generate video” from the dropdown menu.
### Customize your AI-generated video
Add [stickers](https://www.canva.com/features/stickers/), graphics, and other elements from our library. Explore our editing tools and trim, [rotate](https://www.canva.com/features/rotate-video/), or crop clips as needed. You can also apply [video transitions](https://www.canva.com/features/video-transitions/) or filters.
### Present, download, or share
Once you’re satisfied with your work, download it as a high-resolution MP4 file. Share your AI video via social media or email. Or, present your video directly from the editor.

## Frequently Asked Questions
  * * * *
  * What is an AI video generator?
An AI video generator is a tool that generates videos from descriptions using artificial intelligence algorithms. These text-to-video generators use AI to create or enhance video content from text inputted by users. Need help writing your content first? Use an [AI script generator](https://www.canva.com/features/ai-script-generator/) to craft your narrative, then bring it to life with [AI video generation](https://www.canva.com/features/script-to-video/). This helps craft high-quality videos without the need for expensive equipment, actors, or even video editing skills.
  * * * *
  * What is the best AI video generator?
Canva AI’s Create a Video Clip, powered by Google’s Veo-3, can effortlessly generate high-quality, cinematic footage with synchronized sound, making it one of the best text-to-video generators. Just type your script and instantly generate platform-ready videos with synchronized audio for a cinematic, lifelike effect.
  * * * *
  * What is the output of my AI video?
With Canva AI’s Create a Video Clip, powered by Google’s Veo-3, you can use your text prompt to generate a 16:9 video for up to eight seconds, with synchronized audio — that includes dialogue, sound design, and even music. One video is generated per prompt. After the generation, just add your video to a Canva design for further customization in the editor.
  * * * *
  * Is Canva’s AI Video generator free?
Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Nonprofit users can use Canva AI’s Create a Video Clip to create a limited number of cinematic video clips per month.
  * * * *
  * Can I use the videos for commercial purposes?
  
Yes, you can use the designs generated with Canva AI for any purpose, including personal or commercial projects, as long as you follow our [AI Product Terms](https://www.canva.com/policies/ai-product-terms/) and [Terms of Use](https://www.canva.com/policies/acceptable-use-policy/).   
  
Keep in mind that if you’re going to use AI output for your commercial projects, you may not have exclusive rights to your AI-generated designs. It’s your responsibility to ensure that your AI-generated design is suitable for commercial use. This includes determining whether permission is required to depict works of art, photographs, trademarks, or logos.   
  
Please be mindful that Canva doesn’t guarantee that the AI-generated images, designs, and text you generate are cleared for use (particularly if the image or design you create looks like someone else’s work).   

  * * * *
  * How are you moderating Canva AI and ensuring safe use?
Our values define how we build our products, treat our community, and impact the world. We want to ensure our community feels safe when creating and interacting with content on our platform.
  

To ensure our community can enjoy Canva AI safely and responsibly, we've implemented various measures, including automated reviews of input prompts for terms that might generate inappropriate content. Users can [report unsafe content here](https://www.canva.com/en_au/help/report-content/), and we also encourage you to contact our support team to provide feedback at any time.
