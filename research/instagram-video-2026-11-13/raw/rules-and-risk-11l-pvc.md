# Can I create a Professional Voice Clone of someone else's voice?
Copy page
No. You can only create a Professional Voice Clone of your own voice. Even with their consent, you cannot clone someone else’s voice. All Professional Voice Clones require a verification process to confirm that the voice belongs to you.
If someone wants to share their voice with you, they can create and verify a Professional Voice Clone on their own account, then share it with you privately using a sharing link. Learn more in our article: [How do I share a voice?](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/product/voices/my-voices/how-do-i-share-a-voice)
