## Motion content without a production pipeline
The Image to Video API turns product photos into short videos automatically. Send one or more images of a product and get back a video with natural camera motion, animation or effects you set up. No filming, studio or editor needed. Use it as a video generator for your whole catalog, not one product at a time.

## Frequently asked questions
#### What is the Image to Video API?
It turns product photos into short videos automatically. You send one or more images of a product and get back a video with natural camera motion, animation or effects you set up. No filming, studio or editor is needed.
#### What can I use the videos for?
Product detail pages, social posts, paid ads and marketplace listings. The output is sized for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, product pages and ad platforms. Teams use it to turn a static catalog into video at the scale those channels need.
#### Which products does it work best for?
It's built for fashion and apparel, food, retail products and cars. Check out our [Video API documentation](https://docs.photoroom.com/video-api-enterprise-plan/overview) for more details.
#### Do I need several photos of the product?
No, one image is enough to make a video. Sending several angles helps the video stay true to the real product. For example, our [car demo](https://docs.photoroom.com/video-api-enterprise-plan/overview#cars) uses six photos taken from different angles.
#### Who can use the Image to Video API, and how much does it cost?
It's available on [Enterprise plans only](https://www.photoroom.com/enterprise). Contact our team to book a demo and get pricing for your volume.
