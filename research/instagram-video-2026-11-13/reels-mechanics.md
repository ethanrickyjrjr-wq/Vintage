## Findings

### 1. Reels ranking and small accounts (OFFICIAL)
- Mosseri, "Instagram Ranking Explained" (May 31, 2023; page dated, not 2026): "The most important predictions we make are how likely you are to reshare a reel, watch a reel all the way through, like it, and go to the audio page". Reels are "Much like Explore, the majority of what you see is from accounts you don't follow." Source: https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained (raw/reels-mechanics-ranking.md)
- Same page, on what gets suppressed: "We aim to make certain reels less visible for other reasons, such as low-resolution or watermarked reels, reels that are muted or contain borders, reels that are majority text, or reels that have already been posted on Instagram." Also: "We're experimenting with new notifications to help creators understand when the reach of their reel may be limited due to a watermark."
- Small-account fairness (creators.instagram.com, dated April 30, 2024): "every piece of eligible content (e.g., content that is original, does not violate our community guidelines, has no visible watermarks and satisfies our recommendation guidelines) is shown to a small audience that we think will enjoy it, regardless of whether they follow the account... the top performing set of reels are shown to a slightly wider audience, then the best of these are shown to an even wider group". https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality (raw/reels-mechanics-rm-originality.md)
- Originality: original content = "Photos or videos you took", "Content you designed", "Content you materially edited". Immaterial edits listed: "borders, watermarks, speed changes, just crediting the original creator". Enforcement: "If your account primarily posts unoriginal reels, photos, or carousels... your account may not be seen in recommendations to new audiences" and eligibility returns "when a significant percentage of your recently posted reels, photos, and carousels are considered original in a 30-day period. This is calculated on a rolling basis". https://creators.instagram.com/original-content-guidelines (raw/reels-mechanics-ocg.md). Note: the official page covers reels, photos and carousels (a search summary dated the photo/carousel extension to April 30, 2026; I could not confirm that date, the official page is undated).
- Recommendation guidelines (same 2024 page) list "Content that is associated with low-quality publishing, such as unoriginal content that is largely repurposed from another source" and "clickbait or engagement bait" as not eligible for recommendation.
- Implication of "watermarked" + "already posted on Instagram": a TikTok/CapCut-exported watermarked file is explicitly a reach penalty; our own Photoroom/own-footage clips are "Photos or videos you took".

### 3. Trial Reels (OFFICIAL)
- https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels (raw/reels-mechanics-trial.md): "Trial reels will be shown to non-followers first." Toggle 'Trial' before sharing; "your followers won't see it in Feed or Instagram's Reels tab. Your trial reel will not appear in your profile's reels tab or your main grid unless you later decide to Share to everyone." "Approximately 24 hours after you share a trial reel, you can view key engagement metrics". Auto-share option "if we determine it's performing well based on the views it receives within the first 72 hours."
- Eligibility, creators.instagram.com/reels: "You must have a professional account to use trial reels. Select creators without a professional account may have access to trial reels as part of ongoing testing." (raw/reels-mechanics-reels.md). No follower threshold is stated on either official page. Third-party sources say 1,000 followers (SECONDARY, conflicting: one says 200 for professional accounts). With 347 followers, check whether the Trial toggle appears in the app; that is the only reliable test.
- Caveat: Trial reels do not show on the profile grid, so they are for testing, not for showing a listing to existing followers.

### 5. Carousel vs Reel vs Story for one item
- Official ranking page: Feed is "a mixture of videos, photos and carousels"; Reels and Explore are discovery surfaces where most content is from non-followers; Stories are ranked from accounts you follow only ("The stories you see are from people you've chosen to follow"). So: Reels = reach to strangers; Story = existing followers (and a link sticker, see below); carousel/feed = followers plus Feed recommendations. Content Publishing API allows carousels of "up to 20 images, videos, or a combination of the two" with the first item's crop deciding the ratio (https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/content-publishing.md, raw/reels-mechanics-cpapi.md). No official page I fetched ranks these formats against each other for a single product; that comparison is marketer opinion.

### 6. Shopping / product tags for a small eBay seller (OFFICIAL, mostly bad news)
- https://help.instagram.com/1187859655048322 (raw/reels-mechanics-shop.md): "Some features related to Shops and checkout on Facebook and Instagram are no longer supported." Shops are built in Commerce Manager with a catalog; to show on Instagram you need an Instagram business account; "You must add a checkout URL for your shop to be visible" (transition to checkout on your own website). The linked "recent changes" page would not load (facebook.com/business/help/1314349509894768 returned nothing), so what exactly was dropped is UNVERIFIED. Third-party pages claiming US in-app checkout (BigCommerce etc.) were not crawled and may be outdated.
- Product tagging API doc (https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-facebook-login/product-tagging.md, raw/reels-mechanics-tag.md): the account "must have an approved Instagram Shop with a product catalog containing products"; "Product tagging is not supported for Instagram Creator accounts"; "Product tagging is not supported for Stories and Live" (API); the content-publishing doc also says "Shopping tags are not supported" via API publishing. Warning: "Beginning August 10, 2023, some businesses without checkout-enabled Shops will no longer be able to tag their products".
- Conclusion: tagging products needs a Business account + Commerce Manager shop + catalog + a checkout URL. A seller whose only storefront is eBay has no official path shown on these pages; eBay-to-Meta catalog feed was not researched (UNVERIFIED). Treat shopping tags as not available; use the link in bio.

### 12. Business vs Creator, and the API
- Reach: no official page fetched says account type changes Reels reach. Official: Account Status works for "a Creator or business account"; Trial Reels need "a professional account" (either type).
- Content Publishing API (raw/reels-mechanics-cpapi.md): applies to "Instagram professional accounts"; `media_type` can be `VIDEO`, `REELS` or `STORIES`; media must be on a public URL (or resumable upload to rupload.facebook.com); limits "100 API-published posts within a 24-hour moving period"; permissions `instagram_content_publish` / `instagram_business_content_publish`; needs Meta app and App Review for non-test users (not detailed here); Page Publishing Authorization may block publishing. Product tagging: Business only, not Creator. Note the doc's own text "Shopping tags are not supported" and trial-reel posting via API is not mentioned (UNVERIFIED).

## Implications for "post a new eBay listing as a video" (ranked)
1. Make it original, watermark-free, unique per listing: export from Photoroom/Edits with no watermark (Edits exports "with no watermark"); never post a CapCut/TikTok-watermarked file; never reuse the same clip twice (reposted and watermarked reels are explicitly demoted).
2. Use Reels for discovery (strangers), Stories for existing followers. A new-listing Reel plus the same day's Story (listing photo, link sticker) covers both; Story link sticker is the only click path that is not the bio (sticker details SECONDARY).
3. Do not plan around Instagram product tags or checkout. They need a Business account, Commerce Manager shop, catalog and checkout URL, and "some features... are no longer supported". Drive buyers via a link in bio (to the eBay store or a single page) and put "link in bio / search HV-01 on eBay" in the caption and on-screen.
4. Optimise for what Mosseri states ranks Reels: watch to the end, reshare, like. Short loops built around one clear reveal (form turning, tag/price close-up) beat long tours; give a reason to send it ("send to the friend who'd wear this"). Sends-per-reach is secondary-sourced but consistent with the official "reshare" prediction.
5. Keep the video full-frame, with sound and little text: avoid borders, muted audio and text-heavy frames (all named as reasons for lower visibility). Add voice-over or original room audio; avoid licensed library tracks unless confirmed for business use.
6. Try the Trial toggle on the next few listing Reels; if it is missing at 347 followers, that is expected under conflicting eligibility reports. If present, test two hooks, read the 24-hour metrics, then "share with everyone". Trial reels stay off the grid, so do not use them for listings that sell quickly.
7. Automation: the Content Publishing API can post `REELS` from a public video URL to a professional account (100 posts/24h), so a pipeline listing-photo to video to post is possible, but needs a Meta app, a Business/Creator account linked as required, and no shopping tags via API. Decide Business vs Creator on shopping/music needs, not reach (no official reach difference found). Hollie should check Account Status in the app, since it states recommendation eligibility.
8. Fix the bio first: Instagram's recommendations and Profile visits matter only if the bio matches the new Southwest Florida vintage and resale positioning and carries the eBay link. (Repo context: bio still says Loveland OH, Bonita Springs FL and studio rental.)
