# The Coffee Table Styling Tricks Designers Swear By (And Actually Use at Home)
By
[Kristin Hohenadel](https://www.thespruce.com/kristin-hohenadel-4768148)
[Kristin Hohenadel](https://www.thespruce.com/kristin-hohenadel-4768148)
Kristin Hohenadel has written on design for publications including the New York Times, Interior Design, Slate, Fast Company, and the international editions of Elle Decor.

Styling your coffee table with the right mix of decor will [elevate your living room design](https://www.thespruce.com/living-room-decor-ideas-5442837) without adding visual clutter. Create an inviting display with books, flowers, candles, decor objects, and more to make it both beautiful and functional. 
Check out these designer-approved ways to style a coffee table for inspiration. 
  * [**Valerie Darden**](https://www.brextoncoleinteriors.com/about) is an interior designer and the founder of Virginia-based Brexton Cole Interiors.
  * [**Jessica Nelson**](https://www.jessicanelsondesign.com/) is an interior designer and founder of Seattle-based Jessica Nelson Design.


01 of 17
##  Use the Rule of Thirds 
[Brexton Cole Interiors](https://brextoncoleinteriors.com/)
As an interior designer, [Valerie Darden](https://www.brextoncoleinteriors.com/about) loves to rely on the [rule of thirds](https://www.thespruce.com/rule-of-thirds-interior-design-8732947) for styling coffee tables. 
"This means dividing the table up into three sections and creating a visual triangle of 2 to 3 books, other decorative objects such as a vase with flowers, or a stylish tray," she says. 
02 of 17
##  Mix Materials 
[Desiree Burns Interiors](https://www.dburnsinteriors.com/)
When choosing objects to display, be conscious of mixing materials for added interest and dimension. Top a [round marble coffee table](https://www.thespruce.com/how-to-style-a-round-coffee-table-8738108) with greenery in a clay vase and [a strand of decorative wood beads](https://www.thespruce.com/most-overused-coffee-table-styling-trick-11937857), or brighten a wooden coffee table with metallic accents. 
03 of 17
##  Style With Colorful Flowers 
[Studio Peake](https://www.studiopeake.com/)
"I enjoy adding fresh flowers whenever I can to a coffee table," Darden says. "This gives the room an instant boost." 
04 of 17
##  Use a Variety of Shapes 
[Studio Peake](https://www.studiopeake.com/)
Style a long rectangular coffee table with a round tray to break up straight lines. Use a mix of shapes on a round, square, or organically shaped coffee table to add texture and variation to your display. 
05 of 17
##  Vary Heights of Accessories 
[Brexton Cole Interiors](https://brextoncoleinteriors.com/)
"Add in different heights, such as two coffee table books and a taller vase next to it," Darden says. "This creates dimension, interest and beauty."  

06 of 17
##  Layer Textures 
[Yael Weiss Interiors](https://www.yaelweissinteriors.com/)
Create visual interest by layering multiple textures. Mixing materials and shapes when styling your coffee table is especially important when designing a neutral space. 
07 of 17
##  Stack Coffee Table Books 
[Brexton Cole Interiors ](https://brextoncoleinteriors.com/)
"I love adding interesting books," Darden says. "It's a fun way for guests to not only see how beautifully they are styled, but also open them up and check them out." 
08 of 17
##  Incorporate Natural Elements 
[Forbes + Masters](https://www.forbesmasters.com/)
Style your coffee table with organic shapes and natural elements to create a lively display. A bowl of preserved greens and a sculptural piece of driftwood will look good at any time of year and add warmth to a contemporary living room. 
09 of 17
##  Consider Color Palette 
[Jessica Nelson Design](https://www.jessicanelsondesign.com/) / [Carina Skrobecki Photography](https://www.cassphoto.net/)
"Think about the books you are layering together as a cohesive palette," says interior designer [Jessica Nelson](https://www.jessicanelsondesign.com/about). Choose books that complement each other or create a fun combination.  

10 of 17
##  Stick to Odd Numbers 
[Desiree Burns Interiors](https://www.dburnsinteriors.com/)
If you want to incorporate more than three objects into your coffee table design, stick to odd numbers. For a larger table or a more [maximalist aesthetic](https://www.thespruce.com/what-is-maximalist-style-4685629), style the surface with five objects for a balanced feel that is pleasing to the eye. 
11 of 17
##  Make It Family-Friendly 
Design by [Side Angle Side](https://www.sideangleside.co/) / Photo by [Casey Dunn](https://caseydunn.net/)
"Use things that are functional and fun for your family," says Nelson. "Favorite books, little baskets that contain toys, or even a chessboard are all great things to include." 
12 of 17
##  Utilize Negative Space 
[Yael Weiss Interiors](https://www.yaelweissinteriors.com/)
Don't crowd the coffee table in a [minimalist living room](https://www.thespruce.com/minimalist-living-room-ideas-5213203) with too many objects. This table is styled with a small vase of flowers and vintage candleholder for a light and airy feel that leaves plenty of negative space as well as drinks, books, and other ephemera. 
13 of 17
##  Use Book Stacks for Display 
[Jessica Nelson Design](https://www.jessicanelsondesign.com/) / [Carina Skrobecki Photography](https://www.cassphoto.net/)
Display decor items on book stacks for a layered look with extra height. "One of my favorite tips for anywhere in your home—add a small plant or vase of flowers," Nelson says. "This always elevates the space." 
14 of 17
##  Top an Ottoman With a Tray 
Design by [Kibler & Kirch](http://www.kiblerandkirch.com/) / Photo by [Lucy Call](http://www.lucycall.com/)
If your coffee table is [an ottoman](https://www.thespruce.com/best-ottomans-11774370), make it functional with a round metallic or wooden tray that mimics its shape. Style it with crystal decanters filled with your favorite beverage when entertaining. 
15 of 17
##  Leave a Board Game Out 
Design by [Side Angle Side](https://www.sideangleside.co/) / Photo by [Casey Dunn](https://caseydunn.net/)
Encourage family and friends to engage with one another by leaving your favorite board game out on the coffee table. This backgammon set doubles as decor on an upholstered coffee table, while the coffee table books are decoratively stacked next to the sofa. 
16 of 17
##  Boost the Mood With Candles 
[Jessica Nelson Design](https://www.jessicanelsondesign.com) /[ Carina Skrobecki Photography](https://www.cassphoto.net/)
Candlelight will add ambiance to your coffee table while creating a flattering glow. Use a pair of taper candlesticks, a scented candle, or [flameless LED candles](https://www.thespruce.com/best-flameless-candles-5080285) depending on your preferences.  

17 of 17
##  Leave a Book Open 
[Yael Weiss Interiors](https://www.yaelweissinteriors.com/)
Leave a favorite coffee table book open to encourage people to browse. Place it on a stylish book stand for an elegant look.
