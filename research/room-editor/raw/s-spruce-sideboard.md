# 30 Stylish Sideboard Decor Ideas for Any Room
By
[Rabekah Henderson](https://www.thespruce.com/rabekah-henderson-5202618)
[Rabekah Henderson](https://www.thespruce.com/rabekah-henderson-5202618)
Rabekah Henderson is an NC-based contributing writer for The Spruce covering design, decor, and all things home.

The term '[sideboard](https://www.thespruce.com/dining-room-sideboards-and-buffets-1976662)' can seem a like catch-all for describing a range of furniture items, but they're often small tables or buffets that offer space for decor, storage, and art. Sideboards are great additions to spaces like the [living room](https://www.thespruce.com/living-room-decor-ideas-5442837), dining room, and entryway, and they can work with any style too. Keep reading to discover 30 great ways to style and decorate a sideboard. 
[The 10 Best Places to Buy Dining Room Furniture](https://www.thespruce.com/the-best-places-to-buy-dining-room-furniture-4587352)
01 of 30
##  Use Sideboards for Storage 
[Cathie Hong Interiors](https://cathiehonginteriors.com)
Sideboards in living rooms can be [for more than just looks](https://www.thespruce.com/tips-for-making-a-living-room-storage-look-expensive-8772433)—they can act as storage too. Use your sideboard to store books, movies, photos or even [records](https://www.thespruce.com/cd-storage-ideas-7550823). The possibilities are nearly endless. 
02 of 30
##  Cover It Up 
[Cathie Hong Interiors](https://cathiehonginteriors.com)
If you'd like to keep your sideboard storage a bit more hidden, look for a sideboard that features cabinets or covered shelves. This is especially helpful if your storage is a bit more cluttered than clean. 
03 of 30
##  Go Vintage 
[Afro Bohemian Living](https://www.instagram.com/afrobohemianliving/)
[Vintage](https://www.thespruce.com/vintage-vs-antique-vs-retro-6541571) sideboards add plenty of charm and storage to your home, and are a great addition to spaces with a retro or farmhouse style. To add one to your home, look for them online, at thrift stores, estate sales, or antique stores. 
04 of 30
##  Add a Houseplant 
[Cathie Hong Interiors](https://cathiehonginteriors.com)
The perfect pairing for the space next to your sideboard? An oversized houseplant. The height of the plant will contrast well against a short sideboard, and a houseplant makes any space feel lush and vibrant. 
05 of 30
##  Lean Some Art 
[Cathie Hong Interiors](https://cathiehonginteriors.com)
Struggling with what to put on top of your sideboard? Try adding some oversized art. [Lean](https://www.thespruce.com/wall-decor-ideas-7090571) it against the wall on top of your sideboard, or hang it a few inches above. 
06 of 30
##  Stack a Few Books 
[Cathie Hong Interiors](https://cathiehonginteriors.com)
[Books](https://www.thespruce.com/how-to-organize-books-5093674) are another great sideboard topper. When selecting books, look for oversized coffee table books rather than magazines or smaller paperbacks. These coffee table books will most likely be sturdier and have a more eye-catching exterior compared to other choices. 
07 of 30
##  Use Similar Materials 
[Cathie Hong Interiors](https://cathiehonginteriors.com)
To ensure your sideboard visually fits the rest of your space, try picking one in a similar material to what's found in the rest of your home. The light oak sideboard in a room full of oak and birch above from [Cathie Hong Interiors](https://cathiehonginteriors.com) is a great example. 
08 of 30
##  Take Advantage of Awkward Spaces 
[Desiree Burns Interiors](https://www.dburnsinteriors.com)
Sideboards are a wonderful way to use the awkward wall space that sits beneath a stairway. Add a few pieces of decor, a mirror for your on-the-way-out-makeup-checks, and bowl for keys and you've got yourself a winning use of that space near the stairwell. 
09 of 30
##  Make It a Media Center 
[Sarah Fultz Interiors](https://sarahfultzinteriors.com/gallery#project-index)
Sideboards can also serve as media centers that sit underneath televisions. Pick one with cabinets or drawers to hide cords and cables away. 
10 of 30
##  Go Semi-Circular 
[Emily Henderson](https://stylebyemilyhenderson.com/)
Semi-circle sideboards are a charming addition to any space, as their unique shape adds a sweet, vintage touch, and it also means they can easily fit in all kinds of spaces. 
11 of 30
##  Make It Match 
[Emily Henderson](https://stylebyemilyhenderson.com/)
For a built-in sideboard look, pick a sideboard in the same material as the wall next to it. You can also DIY it too, by painting both the sideboard and bordering wall in the same color. 
12 of 30
##  Install Paneling 
[Emily Henderson](https://stylebyemilyhenderson.com/)
One of the best backdrops to a sideboard is [beadboard or paneling](https://www.thespruce.com/wainscotting-vs-beadboard-paneling-5093363). It adds a subtle touch of character that makes your sideboard look instantly classier. Pick a sideboard that's at least six inches shorter than the paneling for the best look. 
13 of 30
##  Add a Mirror 
[Emily Henderson](https://stylebyemilyhenderson.com/)
Mirrors are a common addition to sideboards, and it's easy to see why. They're a great way to fill wall space without adding visual clutter, they match with anything, and they reflect light. 
14 of 30
##  Switch It Out Seasonally 
[Emily Henderson](https://stylebyemilyhenderson.com/)
To dress up your sideboard all year round, rotate your decor seasonally. Add pastel blooms in the spring, pops of lush green in the summer, and deep autumnal hues in the fall. 
15 of 30
##  Layer Your Art 
[julia robbs](https://juliarobbs.com/Lifestyle-and-Interiors)
Sideboards don't need to be limited to just one piece of art—instead, art can be layered for a dynamic and complex look. To make this decor work, pick art in complementary colors and patterns. 
16 of 30
##  Anchor Your Seating Area 
[Julia Robbs](https://juliarobbs.com/Lifestyle-and-Interiors)
Living room sideboards can be excellent anchors for a seating area and serve as conversation-friendly alternative to a TV or sound system. Place one against a wall and center your seating around it to try out the look for yourself. 
17 of 30
##  Hang Lots of Art 
[Julia Robbs](https://juliarobbs.com/Lifestyle-and-Interiors)
Another way to add lots of art to your sideboard is by hanging pieces of similar sizes, subjects, and color palettes, like the set above photographed by [Julia Robbs](https://juliarobbs.com/Lifestyle-and-Interiors). 
18 of 30
##  Store Your Dishes 
[Bespoke Only](https://www.bespokeonly.com/)
Looking for a better place to store all your holiday dishes? Try a sideboard. These do-it-all pieces provide a great place to hide away your out-of-season dishes while still ensuring they're easy to access when the time comes. 
19 of 30
##  Light a Candle 
[Devon Grace Interiors](https://www.devongraceinteriors.com/)
[Candles](https://www.thespruce.com/decorating-with-candles-ideas-6824614) are another winning sideboard topper. They provide ambiance and a certain _je ne sais quoi_ , and they can provide plenty of style too when paired with some luxe candle holders or candelabras. 
20 of 30
##  Leave Space 
[Bespoke Only](https://www.bespokeonly.com/)
In narrow rooms, be sure to pick a sideboard that's not too wide. This will ensure that there's plenty of space to walk between seating and the sideboard. 
21 of 30
##  Make Some Drinks 
[Liljencrantz Design](https://liljencrantzdesign.com/)
An extra-tall sideboard can also double as a [bar cabinet](https://www.thespruce.com/bar-cart-ideas-5188364). Look for a vintage one online or at an antique store to find a piece with unique features and ornamentation. 
22 of 30
##  Keep It Minimal 
[Devon Grace Interiors](https://www.devongraceinteriors.com/)
For a sideboard that's sleek and simple, use one that doesn't have any drawers or shelving underneath. This will keep its silhouette minimal and understated, and is perfect for small spaces. 
23 of 30
##  Get Built-ins 
[Devon Grace Interiors](https://www.devongraceinteriors.com/)
For a sideboard look that's extra luxurious, consider adding a built-in one. This works really well if you want your sideboard to double as a mini bar or drinks area. 
24 of 30
##  Fill Awkward Space 
[Cathie Hong Interiors](https://cathiehonginteriors.com)
Do you have an awkward wall space next to an off-center fireplace or window? Add a sideboard. These multipurpose furnishings provide a way to fill the space and some storage too. 
25 of 30
##  Use Rattan 
[Devon Grace Interiors](https://www.devongraceinteriors.com/)
[Rattan](https://www.thespruce.com/what-is-rattan-furniture-made-from-2736315) sideboards can give your space a down-to-earth look, and they're great in homes that have a touch of tropical or desert-inspired style. 
26 of 30
##  Add Oversized Florals 
[Devon Grace Interiors](https://www.devongraceinteriors.com/)
To add a structural element to your sideboard, add an oversized vase and some extra-tall florals. It's a great focal point for your sideboard, and it's easy to switch out the blooms depending on the season or occasion. 
27 of 30
##  Add a Sideboard to the Bedroom 
[Desiree Burns Interiors](https://www.dburnsinteriors.com)
For bedroom storage that's more versatile than a dresser, try a sideboard. Some pieces have cabinets or pull-down compartments, making them a great spot to store extra shoes or linens. 
28 of 30
##  Get Creative With Placement 
[Desiree Burns Interiors](https://www.dburnsinteriors.com)
Sideboards don't need to sit against a wall—they can also rest against a large piece of furniture, like a sofa, for a chic and modern look. Try setting your sideboard against a sofa if you're trying to better define a transitional or open-plan space, like the space between a living room and a dining area. 
29 of 30
##  Add Lighting 
[Desiree Burns Interiors](https://www.dburnsinteriors.com)
Don't forget to add some lighting to your sideboard. This can be in the form of overhead spotlights or a table lamp. Either way, the extra ambience secondary lighting adds will be a welcome addition. 
30 of 30
##  Don't Forget a Bench 
[Desiree Burns Interiors](https://www.dburnsinteriors.com)
When adding a sideboard to the entryway, try adding a bench too. Not only is it a helpful place to sit down and put on or take off shoes, but it will also help a bare entryway feel more complete.
