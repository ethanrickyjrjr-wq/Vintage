One-click staging vs a real 3D scene
## Most AI staging paints furniture on. This one places it.
A one-click tool repaints your photo and hands back a picture. If the sofa sits two feet too far left, your only move is to generate again and hope. 3DSpace AI rebuilds the room as a 3D scene first, so every object is a real object you can pick up and put down exactly where you want it.
### One-click AI staging
You describe a style, the model redraws the whole photo. Fast and often beautiful — but the layout is the model's decision, furniture cannot be nudged, and a client note like "move the rug, keep everything else" means starting over. Perfect when you want a look, not a plan.
### 3DSpace AI
Your photo becomes a navigable 3D scene with real depth, real floor and real walls. Furniture is dropped into that scene as 3D geometry, so it sits on the floor, respects perspective and casts the right shadow. Move one chair a few centimetres and nothing else changes.

How it works
## Photo to furnished 3D scene, in one window
Three stages, and the editor walks you through them in order: upload the image, choose the area you want to work in, then customise and generate.
1
### Upload your photo
Drop in a picture of the room or the building. If it already has furniture in it, clear the space first — the option is right there in the flow, so an occupied room and an empty one both start the same way.
2
### Pick the area to edit
Mark the part of the image you are furnishing. Luw.ai models that space in 3D and maps the model onto your photograph, so the scene keeps the real perspective of the original shot.
3
### Furnish it and generate
Drag objects into the scene, place each one exactly, then render. Undo and redo are there the whole time, and the project saves so you can come back and move a single chair tomorrow.

Photo to 3D model
## Turn a product photo into a 3D model
You do not need a 3D file to put one specific piece of furniture in a room. Drop in a flat photograph — a catalogue shot, a picture from a retailer's page, a photo of the chair your client already owns — and Luw.ai gives you back a 3D model of it, ready to place in the scene.
### One flat photo is enough
No turntable, no scanning rig, no measurements and no 3D software. A single ordinary product image is the whole input.
### You get real geometry
What comes back is a 3D object with actual depth, not a cut-out pasted on the picture. It stands on the floor, turns as you turn it and takes the light of the room.
### It lands in your scene
The model drops straight into the room you are working on and behaves like any other object: position it, rotate it, scale it, undo it.
This is what makes a specific product possible instead of a lookalike. The sofa in the render can be the sofa the client is actually buying — and once you have generated it, it is saved to your projects and can be dropped into the next room without generating it again.

  * Objects respect the floor, the perspective and the light of the original photo

## 3D virtual staging — frequently asked
What is 3D virtual staging?
Ordinary virtual staging edits a photograph: software paints furniture over an empty room and returns a new picture. 3D virtual staging rebuilds the photographed space as a three-dimensional scene first, then places actual 3D furniture models inside it. Because the room has depth, objects sit on the floor, obey the camera's perspective and can be moved after the fact.
How is this different from one-click AI virtual staging?
One-click staging gives you the model's idea of a furnished room in a few seconds, and you take it or generate another. 3DSpace AI hands you the room as a scene and lets you furnish it yourself, so the layout is a decision rather than a lottery. Use one-click when you want a look fast; use 3DSpace when the placement has to be exactly right.
Can I add furniture to a photo of a room I already have?
Yes — that is the whole idea. Upload any photograph of a real space, including one you took on your phone, and 3DSpace AI turns it into the scene you furnish. You do not need a floor plan, a measurement or a 3D file of the property.
Can I turn a picture of a piece of furniture into a 3D model?
Yes. Drop a flat image of an object into the editor and it comes back as a 3D model you can place, rotate and scale in the scene. This is how a product photograph from a catalogue ends up as a real object in a customer's living room.
Which 3D file formats can I upload?
GLB, GLTF and OBJ — what most 3D software exports. You only need them if you already have 3D models to bring in. If you do not, you never touch a file format: drop in a photograph of the piece and the editor builds the 3D model for you.
Do I have to empty the room first?
Not in a separate tool. If the photo you upload already has furniture in it, 3DSpace asks whether to clear the space and does it inside the same flow, so an occupied room and a vacant one both start the same way.
Does it work on exterior photos?
Yes. A facade, a terrace or a garden can be uploaded the same way as an interior, mapped to 3D and furnished with outdoor pieces.
Is virtual staging worth it?
Physical staging means renting furniture, moving it in and paying for the weeks it sits there. Virtual staging replaces that with an image, at a fraction of the cost, and lets you show the same room in several styles instead of committing to one. What it cannot do is change how the property feels during an in-person viewing — it works on the photographs, which is where most buyers form their first impression.
Does virtual staging help sell a house?
Empty rooms photograph badly: they read as smaller than they are and give a buyer nothing to judge scale against. Furniture in the photograph gives the room a purpose and a sense of size, which is why staged listing photos generally attract more attention than bare ones. Treat it as a way to make the listing photos do their job, not as a guarantee about price.
Is virtual staging legal?
Yes, as long as you disclose it. Say plainly in the listing that the photographs are virtually staged, and never use staging to hide or invent a permanent feature of the property — a wall, a window, a fireplace, damage. Most MLSs have a specific rule about this; check yours and put the disclosure in the remarks.
Do I need to install anything?
No. 3DSpace AI runs in the browser on a normal laptop or desktop — there is no download, no plugin and no CAD software to learn.
Do I need an account?
Yes, you sign in to use the editor, but you do not need a credit card to start. Free output carries a Luw.ai watermark and is licensed for non-commercial use; a paid plan removes the watermark and covers commercial use.

## About
### What is Luw.ai and what does it do?
Luw.ai is an AI rendering platform designed to help architects and designers transform their ideas into stunning visuals within seconds. Our platform specializes in generating high-quality renders and redesign options for both exterior and interior designs. 
### What 3D software is compatible with Luw.ai?
Our AI tools can generate renderings from any 3D modeling software, including Autodesk 3ds Max, Revit, SketchUp, Blender, and more. Simply screenshot your 3D view and our AI will transform it into a professional rendering. If you are macOS user, [download Luw Screenshot](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/luw-screenshot/id6737491946?mt=12&src=Luw.ai) from Mac App Store for better region detected screenshots for Luw.ai. 
### Can I modify specific parts of my design?
Yes, using our Magic Wand tool, you can mask and highlight specific areas for modification. Simply describe the changes you want, and our AI will generate a modified design while preserving the rest of the image. 
### Can I use Luw.ai for commercial projects?
Yes, all generated designs can be used for commercial purposes when you have an active paid plan. Free version outputs include watermarks and are limited to non-commercial use. 
### How do I use the HD - 4K - 8K Export or Supersize feature?
To export in high-definition, click the *Export* button over any image and select *Download Resized Image* . This ensures your designs maintain their quality and can be scaled up to 4X (Basic) or 8X (Pro). 
### Can I use hand-drawn sketches?
Yes, you can upload your freehand sketches and describe your desired outcome. Our AI will transform your sketches into fully rendered designs within seconds. 
### How can I open GLB, OBJ or STL files without installing anything?
You don't need to install anything. Our free browser tools open 3D files locally on your machine — nothing is uploaded, no account, no watermark: [GLB viewer](https://app.luw.ai/tools/glb), [glTF viewer](https://app.luw.ai/tools/gltf), [OBJ viewer](https://app.luw.ai/tools/obj), [STL viewer](https://app.luw.ai/tools/stl), [FBX viewer](https://app.luw.ai/tools/fbx), [PLY viewer](https://app.luw.ai/tools/ply), [3MF viewer](https://app.luw.ai/tools/3mf), [DAE viewer](https://app.luw.ai/tools/dae), [USDZ viewer](https://app.luw.ai/tools/usdz). Every model Luw.ai's 3D AI generates can be checked there in seconds, and you can put two versions side by side. [See all free browser tools](https://app.luw.ai/tools). 
### Can I view DXF, IFC or SVG floor plans in my browser?
Yes. The same free tools open 2D, CAD and scanned formats too: [DXF viewer](https://app.luw.ai/tools/dxf), [IFC viewer](https://app.luw.ai/tools/ifc), [SVG viewer](https://app.luw.ai/tools/svg), [360 panorama viewer](https://app.luw.ai/tools/panorama), [Gaussian splat viewer](https://app.luw.ai/tools/splat), [floor plan area calculator](https://app.luw.ai/tools/floor-plan-area). The DXF viewer keeps your layers and measures distances, the IFC viewer opens BIM models without Revit, and the floor plan area calculator measures rooms straight from a plan image. Everything runs in your browser — no upload, no queue, no account. 
### Who can use Luw.ai?
Luw.ai is designed for everyone, from professional designers and architects to students and individuals with no prior experience. Our intuitive interface makes it easy to generate design ideas and renderings without requiring special software or coding knowledge.
