How it works

Drag a pick into the photo, or choose Place. Drag a find to move it; use its corner or the size slider to resize. Arrow keys move the selected find; Shift moves it farther. Remove find takes out the selected find; Undo brings it back. Proportions are approximate.

The Decorate buttons suggest a layout from your picks or favorites. If a layout is available, it replaces your current one; Undo brings it back. Pieces that don’t fit are listed under “Left out of this layout.”

Your photo and arrangement stay in this tab. The sample room is ready to decorate. For your own photo, Decorate downloads a room guide once (about 45 MB plus its runtime) and runs on your device. Closing or reloading clears your room. Previews don’t guarantee fit.
