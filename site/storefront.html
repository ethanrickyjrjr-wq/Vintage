Where the prices come from

Targets are set from comparable sold and asking prices gathered on 2026-10-26 (eBay sold, Etsy, Chairish, Poshmark, auction records). The full comps tables with URLs are in the repo under research/pricing/. Low–high is the comp spread, not a discount range.

Shop tags already on items (the chandelier reads $60) are shown where seen.

Before listing

  • Re-shoot the two paintings square-on, no flash; shoot the Elvis front, back and signatures.
  • Everything with a blue chip exists only as a video frame. Share the original iPhone video or shoot stills.
  • Check the lamp base for a Stiffel stamp; it moves the price.
  • Look under the white ribbed chair for a Vitra or Easy Edges label. A real Gehry Wiggle sells for $700 to $1,150.
