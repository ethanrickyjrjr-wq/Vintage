        How it works.

        1. The Logline. Email Hollie. Free.
        2. The Screen Test. A video call, booked online, so Hollie sees the real room. Photos work too.
        3. The board. Pay your billing online, and in 7 to 10 business days you get a plan and options, each labeled From the collection or Sourced for you. Two rounds of changes included.
        4. You approve. In writing, piece by piece. You pay for a sourced piece before Hollie buys it. Collection pieces hold for 48 hours.
        5. That’s a wrap. Hollie places it in person, or you get the list and the pieces, shipped.

        Yes, Hollie sells things too.

        Some of what you’re shown comes from the Holliewood & Vine collection. Some is found elsewhere. Every option is labeled From the collection or Sourced for you, so you always know whose shelf it came from. Collection pieces are the listed price. Sourced pieces carry a 10% sourcing fee on its own line, and you see the full price before anything is bought. No mystery math.

        Photo checklist
        • A wide shot from the doorway.
        • One photo of each corner.
        • Each wall, straight on, with a little ceiling and floor.
        • Daylight, lamps off. No wide-angle lens.
        • Don’t tidy. Hollie needs the real room.
        • Rough measurements: room length and width, windows, doors.
        • What you’re keeping, what’s a maybe, what’s a hard no.
        • A budget number.
        The fine print
        • Homes only: houses, condos, apartments and studios.
        • Book and pay online before any visit. No money changes hands in your home.
        • Fees cover Hollie’s time and taste and are paid upfront. The Casting Call fee is credited toward the piece.
        • If Hollie comes up empty, the fee becomes store credit for 90 days.
        • Rush applies to the service fee, never to item prices, and uses pieces already in the collection.
        • In person, Hollie places furniture, hangs art and mirrors, sets lamps and styles. No electrical work, no plumbing, nothing that needs a permit, and no hiring trades for you: your own trades work for you directly.
        • Sourced pieces are final once you approve them against condition photos, unless they arrive damaged or different from what you were shown. Collection pieces follow the shop’s usual policy.
        • Prices are starting points, plus applicable sales tax.
