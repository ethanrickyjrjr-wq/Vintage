## The short version

1. **On Facebook, the profile itself is the bottleneck.**
   - Buyers see a Meta AI summary of every seller that shows account age and friend count (Meta, March 2026).
   - Ratings only become public after 5.
   - The best vintage buy/sell group found (Quirky Groovy Vintage, 74.9K members) turns away profiles with no photo or a low friend count.
   - So no following or group work matters until the profile is filled in: her face as the photo, real friends, the free badge (see "Before anything else" below).
2. **The follower that counts on Marketplace is Marketplace's own Follow.** A buyer taps the seller's name, then Follow, and will "get notifications when they create new listings" (verified, f3). Listing steadily is what grows it. Following other people does not.
3. **Liking Pages is low risk (inferred: Meta publishes no limit, and a like is far lighter than a group join). Joining groups should be slow.** Meta publishes no daily number for friend requests, group joins, listings or shares. It does name the triggers: lots of friend requests, requests that go unanswered, and repeated content posted at high frequency (verified, f3).
4. **eBay following is one-way.** Saving another seller only adds them to our own feed. Our followers are buyers who save us. Every tool that markets to them (newsletters, coupons, sale events, buyer groups) needs a Store (verified, e1 and e2). On the free account, growth comes from the free levers listed under "eBay: do now" below.
5. **The single best promotion channel found:** Colourful & Maximalist Home Decor Inspo, 662,779 members, checked signed-in today. Its rules allow maximalist small businesses to post about themselves on Tuesdays, Saturdays and Sundays, at most twice a month, with a handle and a photo carousel. The look is exactly the shop's.

## Facebook: the first four weeks

f4's plan, capped by f3's rules (f4 section 3 has the full table):
- **Week 0, now:**
  - Ricky settles the account questions above.
  - Hollie adds her face as the profile photo and tries Settings > Verified info and badge.
  - She uses Facebook normally and adds only people she knows.
  - Finish the item 01 draft.
  - Phone the Lee County Sheriff's Office to confirm a current exchange spot. The only one found is Fort Myers PD, 2210 Widman Way, from a 2018 report.
- **Weeks 1 to 2:**
  - About 3 listings a week, one a day at most. Cover photo is the cut-out, plus one real on-form or room shot, plus label and flaw close-ups.
  - Reply within hours. No holds.
  - After each sale, ask that buyer for a rating in the app.
- **Week 3:**
  - Renew a 7-day-old listing that has had no messages, once.
  - Join the first one or two groups from the table.
  - One share per item, 5 to 7 days apart.
- **Week 4:**
  - Review views, messages and sales per listing.
  - Optional boost test only on a piece of $250 or more that is already cut in price. The only first-hand data point: $10 bought 500 views and 0 messages.

Red lines (Meta's own words, f3):
- No "follow or like to win a disco ball". Meta's spam standard bans "Offering to provide anything of monetary value in exchange for engagement".
- Hire Hollie can't be a Marketplace or group listing. Services are barred.
- No fur, tortoiseshell, ivory or feathers. "Selling animals or animal products isn't allowed on Marketplace."
- Don't post the same listing to many groups at once.
- Don't delete and repost a rejected listing; request a review instead.

Skip for now:
- **Promoted Listings.** Since Jan 13, 2026 a General ad fee is owed if any buyer clicked the ad in the previous 30 days, and a watchlist add counts as a click. It is also gated to Above Standard or Top Rated sellers "with enough account activity".
- **eBay Live.** It needs a business account and approval, and giveaways need eBay's prior approval.

**Overlap with Facebook:** eligible eBay listings that offer shipping already appear on Facebook Marketplace through eBay's partnership, at no cost, with checkout on eBay (eBay Seller Center, April 2025, verified by f4). Before listing a piece natively on Facebook, check whether it already shows there. Two live copies of a one-of-one piece means double-sell risk.

## eBay: after the Store upgrade

- A Store unlocks the newsletter (audience "your followers (saved you as a seller)", at most one email per buyer per 14 days), coded coupons (printable as a package insert, the eBay-approved way to bring buyers back), sale events and rule-based offers.
- **No US page confirms that Starter gets these tools.** Confirm on the Store comparison at checkout before choosing Starter over Basic (e1).
- An Australian snippet limits newsletters to higher tiers (e3).

## Shop-kit fixes from e1's audit

e1 found no file that breaks an eBay rule. Two small fixes, not yet made:
- `customer-replies.txt` reply 11 says "Agree the address privately through the order conversation". Change it to "Share the pickup address in eBay Messages after the buyer has paid", per eBay's local-pickup page. "Privately" reads as off-platform.
- `thank-you-card.txt` asks for "honest feedback". That alone is allowed. But a disco-ball gift in the same parcel can read as an incentive for feedback, which is banned. Drop the feedback line from cards that go with a gift.

Never put the Instagram handle, a URL or contact details anywhere eBay displays: listings, photos, profile, Store, messages, feedback.

## Discrepancies (X verified, Y need review)

- **eBay monthly limits:** 75,000 items and $750,000, read in Seller Hub > Overview on Nov 9 (7 items and $1,202 used). The "about 10 items or $500" figure does not apply to this account.

- **Facebook selling fee:** 10% verified (Meta merchant policy). Closo's "5%" needs review and is probably stale.
- **eBay Live eligibility:** the seller page (eligible professional sellers, waitlist) is verified. The FAQ's "beta with select sellers" needs review (e1 treats it as stale; e3 relied on it).
- **Flamingo Island's Instagram:** `@flamingoflea` verified on its own site. `@flamingoislandfleamarket` from the Nov 8 alt text needs review.
- **eBay followers:** saved sellers get a go-live alert (verified, Live FAQ). Whether they get alerts for new listings needs review: one vendor guide says yes, a UK community post says no.
- **eBay pickup address:** the contact policy bans addresses in messaging. The local-pickup page says to share the location through Messages after payment. Follow the pickup page, which is the more specific.
