Holliewood & Vine on camera

A friend with great taste, showing you what she just found. One corner, one steady take.

The shows

  • Just in20 to 35 s
    Hold one piece up. Say what it is and why you grabbed it. The main show.
  • Fit check15 to 40 s
    Held up, worn, full-length turn. Or one piece, three ways.
  • Click10 to 20 s
    Lamp switched on, glass turned in the light, a bare shelf then dressed.
  • Found it30 to 60 s
    The carry-in, cleaning, measuring, packing.

Later, once Hire Hollie opens: Hollie has notes, where she styles someone's space.

Shoot it

  • Phone vertical, locked off, eye level, waist-up. Full-length for fit checks.
  • One corner, shot on the diagonal, with 2 or 3 objects that always sit there.
  • Step back and crop in. Skip the 0.5x lens up close.
  • Window light from the side or front, never behind you. Keep the lamp you're showing on.
  • Sound matters most. A clip-on mic if you can. A rug or a clothes rack cuts the echo.
  • Put the hook line on screen from the first frame. Captions on.
  • Keep street windows, house numbers, mail and labels out of frame.

Say it

  1. Hook. Two seconds or less, also on screen.
  2. What it is. One fact: era, maker, material.
  3. Why you. "I have a thing for ___."
  4. Proof. A flick, a click, a turn, or a measurement shown on screen.
  5. One honest flaw. Say it out loud and show it close.
  6. Close. See below.

30 to 45 words is about 15 seconds. 70 to 90 is about 30. Talk to one person.

Pick the lines that sound like you

  • Hook

    A. "I have a thing for [x], and this might be the best one I've found."

    B. "Okay. This one."

    C. "Would you wear this to [place]?"

  • Why you

    A. "Anything [colour, material] is an immediate yes for me."

    B. "I saw it across the room and walked straight to it."

    C. "I'd put it next to [x]. Tell me I'm wrong."

  • Sign-off, now and then

    "Do it for the plot." or "Hollie dresses the set."

Close, no link yet

  • "I'm finding more like this. Follow so you see it first."
  • "Save this for the drop."
  • "Tell me what you'd put next to it."

Once pieces are listed: "Piece [No.] is on the listing page. Link in bio."

Never say

Visit, in store, stop by. DM to buy, claim, comment SOLD. Discount, code, offer, giveaway. "Interior design" (say style). Name a maker or "signed" only if it is marked. No safety claims on wiring or glass. Cut "hey guys" and "welcome back."

One example

Just in, about 30 seconds. Swap the brackets for real facts.

On screen: I have a thing for brass lamps.

"I have a thing for brass lamps, and this might be the best one I've found. It's [era], [maker or unmarked], brass and cream ceramic. [One proof only you can give: the weight in your hand, a flick, the click of the switch.] Here's the flaw: [real flaw, close-up]. And when you turn it on, the whole corner changes. Follow so you see the next one first."

One shoot, 90 minutes

  • Set15 min
    Pick the corner. Clear the frame. Lock exposure. Record 10 seconds of your voice and listen on earbuds.
  • Wearables, 6 pieces40 min
    Hold to camera 6 s, worn 10 s, full-length turn 8 s, detail 5 s.
  • Objects, 6 pieces30 min
    Hold and talk 20 s, one click or turn 8 s, flaw close-up 5 s, dressed corner 8 s.
  • Talk5 min
    Each hook twice. Each close once.

About 60 clips make 8 to 10 posts. Film some pieces both ways, talking and silent. We don't yet know which works better for this shop.
