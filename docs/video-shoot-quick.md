# To the Point Videos

The easy shoot, on a two-page card. More in **In depth Video making** and **Full Picture**.

**A friend with great taste, showing you what she just found. One corner, one steady take.**

---

## 1. Pick a show

| Show | What | Length |
|---|---|---|
| **Just in** | Hold one piece up. Say what it is and why you grabbed it. The main show | 20 to 35 s |
| **Fit check** | Held up, worn, full-length turn. Or one piece, three ways | 15 to 40 s |
| **Click** | Lamp switched on, glass turned in the light, a bare shelf then dressed | 10 to 20 s |
| **Found it** | The carry-in, cleaning, measuring, packing | 30 to 60 s |

---

## 2. Set up (15 minutes)

1. **One corner**, shot on the diagonal, with 2 or 3 objects that always sit there.
2. **Phone vertical**, locked off on a clamp or tripod, eye level, waist-up.
3. **Light:** a window to the side or front, never behind you. Keep the lamp you are showing on.
4. **Lock exposure** (touch and hold until AE/AF Lock) and white balance.
5. **Clear the frame:** street windows, house numbers, mail, labels.
6. **Sound:** clip-on mic if you have one. Record 10 seconds and listen on earbuds. A rug or a rack of clothes cuts echo.
7. **Lens:** wipe it. Use 1x. Skip the 0.5x lens up close.

---

## 3. Film it

| Block | What | Clips |
|---|---|---|
| **Wearables, 6 pieces** (40 min) | Hold to camera 6 s, worn 10 s, full-length turn 8 s, detail 5 s | about 24 |
| **Objects, 6 pieces** (30 min) | Hold and talk 20 s, one click or turn 8 s, flaw close-up 5 s, dressed corner 8 s | about 24 |
| **Talk** (5 min) | Each hook twice. Each close once | about 12 |

About 60 clips make 8 to 10 posts. Film some pieces both ways, talking and silent, and compare after a month.

---

## 4. Say it

1. **Hook.** Two seconds or less, also on screen.
2. **What it is.** One fact: era, maker, material.
3. **Why you.** "I have a thing for ___."
4. **Proof.** A flick, a click, a turn, or a measurement shown on screen.
5. **One honest flaw.** Say it out loud and show it close.
6. **Close.**

30 to 45 words is about 15 seconds. 70 to 90 is about 30. Talk to one person.

**Hook, pick one:** "I have a thing for [x], and this might be the best one I've found." · "Okay. This one." · "Would you wear this to [place]?"

**Why you, pick one:** "Anything [colour, material] is an immediate yes for me." · "I saw it across the room and walked straight to it." · "I'd put it next to [x]. Tell me I'm wrong."

**Sign-off, now and then:** "Do it for the plot." or "Hollie dresses the set."

**Close, no link yet:** "I'm finding more like this. Follow so you see it first." · "Save this for the drop." · "Tell me what you'd put next to it."

**Close, once pieces are listed:** "Piece [No.] is on the listing page. Link in bio."

---

## 5. Leave out

Visit, in store, stop by · DM to buy, claim, comment SOLD · discount, code, offer, giveaway · "interior design" (say style) · a maker or "signed" unless it is marked · safety claims on wiring or glass · "hey guys", "welcome back".

---

## 6. One example

Just in, about 30 seconds. Swap the brackets for real facts.

On screen: "I have a thing for brass lamps."

"I have a thing for brass lamps, and this might be the best one I've found. It's [era], [maker or unmarked], brass and cream ceramic. [One proof only you can give: the weight in your hand, a flick, the click of the switch.] Here's the flaw: [real flaw, close-up]. And when you turn it on, the whole corner changes. Follow so you see the next one first."

---

## 7. After

1. **Copy the clips off the phone** the same day.
2. **Edit in Instagram Edits:** trim to the first and last word, hook text on screen, captions on, fix wrong words.
3. **Audio:** keep your voice. Music only from Meta's Sound Collection, low under the voice.
4. **Export clean:** no watermark, no black bars.
5. **Caption:** a searchable phrase first, then era, material, size and the flaw. Up to 5 hashtags. City or region only.
6. **Aim for 10 or more reels a month.**
