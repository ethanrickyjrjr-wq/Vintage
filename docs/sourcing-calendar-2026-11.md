## Quick buy ceiling (one-pound item sold on eBay with free shipping)

- Expected sale $25: pay at most $5. $35: $9. $45: $13. $60: $20. $90: $33. $120: $45.
- Rule: half the net after fees and a $9.29 label at the low end of the sold range, not the asking price. Heavier or bigger than a 12 x 12 x 12 box: the label eats the margin. Jewellery, watches and women's bags pay 15% on eBay instead of 13.6%.
