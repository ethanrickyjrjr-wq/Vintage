Holliewood & Vine · Photo Checklist

The pro look comes from light, a plain backdrop and the same framing on every shot. A phone is enough.

1 · Kit (once)

  • White seamless paper or poster board (pure white, not cream)
  • 2 white foam boards (fill) + 1 black foam board (edges)
  • Phone tripod · painter's tape · tape measure
  • Microfiber cloth, brass polish, glass cleaner
  • Optional: iPhone Settings › Camera › Formats › Most Compatible (JPEG). The cutout script reads HEIC too.

2 · Set up

  • Room with one big window. Every other light off. Flash off. No direct sun on the set.
  • Tape the paper to the table and curve it up the wall: no seam, no corner.
  • Item side-on to the window (90°). White board on the shadow side.
  • Pale item? Black boards just outside the frame for a dark edge.
  • Phone on the tripod. Tape-mark the tripod and the item spot. Don't move the tripod all session. Turn the item, not the camera.
  • Main 1x lens, never 0.5x. Lens at the item's mid-height, straight on.

3 · Every item

  • Dust it, polish the brass, wipe fingerprints off glass
  • Off: price tags, stickers, strings, props, anything that isn't for sale
  • Item fills 70–80% of the frame, with margin on all four sides. Nothing touching the edge.
  • Tap the item to focus

Shot list

  • Front, straight on (the main photo)
  • Three-quarter angle
  • Back
  • Maker's mark, label, signature
  • Every flaw, up close (never hide one)
  • Tape measure across the widest point
  • In the burgundy room, unedited (Etsy needs one real-setting photo)
Seen in the test run: the chandelier shot still had its $60 tag and a mini disco ball on it. The cutout keeps whatever is attached to the item, so take it off before you shoot.

4 · Special cases

  • Big pieces (chair, dress form): plain wall + floor, item pulled 2–3 ft off the wall
  • Rugs: lay flat, shoot from overhead
  • Glass (chandelier, stemware, decanter): white sheet 2–3 ft behind, lit from behind; black cards just outside the frame. Don't cut these; list the original.
  • White, frosted, pale: black backdrop or black side boards
  • Fringe, feathers, chains: hang or lay flat on a plain contrasting backdrop
  • Never use a backdrop the same colour as the item

5 · After the shoot

  • One folder per shoot; name files by item ID, e.g. A13-front.jpg
  • Run the cutout script (or hand the folder to Claude):
    C:\Users\ethan\dev\Vintage\.venv\cutout\Scripts\python.exe C:\Users\ethan\dev\Vintage\tools\cutout\cutout.py "PHOTO FOLDER"
  • Read each report line: OK · PIXELS CHANGED (check by eye) · see-through (use the original) · shoot closer
  • cutouts/listing/*.jpg → Etsy, eBay, Shopify (2048 px, white)
  • cutouts/site/*.png → our site only. Never upload a transparent PNG to Etsy: it turns black.
  • Keep every original
