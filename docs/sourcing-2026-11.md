## 2. The buy-ceiling rule (use it at every sale)

From lane C, checked by hand. Assumes free shipping to the buyer, 6% sales tax on the sale, a 1 lb USPS Ground Advantage commercial label at Zone 5 ($9.29, Notice 123 effective 2026-10-04), $1.50 packing. - **Ceiling = half the net at the bottom of the middle-half sold range** for the exact variant, not the median and not the asking price. Shortcut at 13.6%, 1 lb, Zone 5: net = 0.856 × price − 11.19.
- Heavier items use the real label: 2 lb $10.50, 3 lb $12.12, 5 lb $14.53, 10 lb $17.81, 25 lb $48.69 at Zone 5. Over one cubic foot the label is billed on dimensional weight (divisor 139 since 2026-07-12); a 24×12×12 lamp box bills as about 25 lb, so a $60 lamp in that box nets nothing.
- Etsy Offsite Ads take another 15% when a sale comes through an ad (opt-out allowed under $10k a year).
- Sources: eBay's selling-fees page (crawled 2026-11-08), USPS Notice 123, Pirate Ship's July 2026 rate-change note, a checkoutpage.com Etsy fee summary dated 2026-07-19 (Etsy's own fee page was not read; *needs review*).

### Do not buy

- Heavy anonymous glass: Fire-King sets, Depression glass, Anchor Hocking, CorningWare (category medians $18 to $20), common Snowflake Pyrex singles. A $30 five-pound piece nets about $8 before cost.
- Furniture (antique furniture median $22 on the sold report), large framed glass, any lamp whose box is over one cubic foot, anything over 70 lb ($200 overweight fee since 2026-07-12).
- Modern Lilly Pulitzer (brand median down 29% year on year, *needs review*), Tori Richard above $4, loose modern Hot Wheels, sports cards, Funko.
- Unauthenticated Fendi, Louis Vuitton or Ray-Ban; unmarked "Murano".
- Pens, cameras and electronics that cannot be tested; unsorted LEGO; costume lots with no signed pieces (the signed-name premium is not proven by sold data: a signed Coro brooch sold for $12).
- Lighters with fuel unless shipped empty and declared ($50 hazmat non-compliance fee since 2026-07-12).

### Seasonality

Vintage overall sells through at 13.6% in 30 days on eBay (ZIK, Sept 2026): about 86% of listings do not sell in a month, so price to the sold range, not the asking range. List light giftables (sunglasses, watches, bags, Levi's, Zippo, Hot Wheels) by about 20 November for Black Friday (11/27) and Cyber Monday (11/30). After mid-December, buy Christmas stock for next year.
