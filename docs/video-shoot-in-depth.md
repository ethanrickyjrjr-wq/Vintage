# In depth Video making

Everything for one shoot, start to posted: gear, phone settings, light, sound, the corner, shot lists, editing and posting. The short version is **To the Point Videos**; the direction and the lines to say are in **Full Picture**.

## 2. Phone settings

Set these once, before the first shoot.

| Setting | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Formats | Settings > Camera > Formats > **Most Compatible** | Plays and edits everywhere |
| Resolution and speed | **1080p at 30 fps**. Use 4K at 30 fps for a clip you may crop in on | Bigger files fill the phone and slow the edit |
| Never | PAL or 25 fps | US power runs at 60 Hz; 25 fps flickers under LEDs |
| Auto FPS | **Off** | It drops to 24 fps in low light |
| Lock White Balance | **On** (Settings > Camera > Record Video) | Keeps burgundy, brass and lamp light from drifting |
| HDR Video | **Off** | Other phones receive a flatter copy |
| Exposure | Touch and hold a mid-tone surface until "AE/AF Lock" shows, then slide exposure down until bright lamps keep their colour | Stops the picture pumping as you move |
| Lens | **1x** for talking, 2x for tight details. Skip 0.5x | Ultra-wide bends faces and the room |
| Stabilising | Tripod. Action mode only in bright light | Action mode needs light |
| Slow motion | 120 fps for glass turning or light through facets, under warm bulbs. Test first; LED panels can band | |
| Before each shoot | Wipe the lens. Turn on Do Not Disturb. Free some storage. Charge, or plug in | |

## 3. The corner

- **Pick one corner** and shoot on the diagonal, so the walls make lines that pull the eye back. A flat wall looks boxed in.
- **Two or three objects live there always.** That repetition is what makes it recognisable.
- **A plain wall or a fabric behind the piece.** Burgundy fits the house look.
- **Distance:** put the phone far enough back to crop in. Stand a few steps in front of the wall so the background softens.
- **Clear the frame.** Street windows, house numbers, mail, parcels, labels and a car in a window all come out. Tag a city or region only.
- **Mark the floor** with tape where the tripod and where Hollie stand, so the framing is the same every shoot.

---

## 4. Light

- **One soft light**, slightly above eye level, about 45 degrees to one side. That adds shape without lighting the wall behind.
- **Window light** from the side or front. Never put the window behind Hollie.
- **Colour temperature.** Set the soft light to 3200K next to warm lamps and brass. Use 5600K when window light is the main source. Do not mix the two on one face.
- **Keep the lamp you are showing switched on.** It is the warm accent in the frame.
- **Lock exposure and white balance** after the light is set (section 2).
- **Keep the light off the glass.** Light the piece and the surface it reflects separately; keep the camera side dark.

---

## 5. Sound

Sound is the difference between everyday and sloppy.

1. **Clip the mic** about a hand-span below the chin, cable under the shirt, nothing brushing it.
2. **Plug it in before opening the Camera app.**
3. **Record 10 seconds and play it back on earbuds.** Check for echo, hum and rustle.
4. **Cut echo:** hard walls and bare floors bounce sound. A rug, a curtain or a rack of clothes soaks it up. Switch off fans and air conditioning for each take.
5. **Record 10 seconds of the room being quiet** at the end, to patch gaps in the edit.
6. **Without a mic:** phone close, mouth within an arm's length, room quiet.
7. **Keep the audio on every talking clip.** For Click clips, keep the room's own sound: the switch, the glass.

---

## 6. Framing

- **Vertical, 9:16.** On a 1080 x 1920 frame, keep faces, the piece and all text inside **x 90 to 990 and y 420 to 1248**. The edges are covered by the app's buttons and by the crop on the profile grid.
- **Talking:** waist-up, eyes about a third of the way down the frame.
- **Fit check, full length:** the whole body inside the box with room above the head and below the feet.
- **Pieces:** fill the box, don't float in the middle of empty space.
- **Mirror and glass:** camera off the mirror's centre line, farther back, at 2x or 3x. A sheet of black foam board with a lens hole, or black cloth, where the reflection lands. Dark clothes, no white shirt.

---

## 7. Prep

**Wearables**
- Steam or press. Lint-roll. Check tags, hooks and closures.
- Measure laid flat: pit to pit and length for tops; waist and hips for bottoms; shoulder to hem for dresses. Write the numbers on a card.
- Note the label and the one flaw. Photograph both close up.
- Hang them on the rack in outfit order.

**Objects**
- Dust and wipe glass with a lint-free cloth. Straighten the shade. Tuck the cord.
- Test every light and every switch before you film.
- Measure height and width. Note any mark and the one flaw.
- Do the proof once off camera (the flick, the weight, the click) so you know what it sounds like.

**Voice**
- Write each hook on a card. Read it aloud twice. If it does not sound like you, pick another line from Full Picture.

## 8. Shot lists

Raw seconds. A finished post is shorter after trimming.

### Just in (post 20 to 35 s)

| # | Shot | Raw | Camera | Say |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook, to the lens | 2 s | Waist-up, 1x | The hook line |
| 2 | Hold the piece up to the lens | 6 s | Same | What it is; why you |
| 3 | Proof | 5 s | Close, 2x | The flick, weight, click or measurement |
| 4 | The flaw | 5 s | Close | Say it, show it |
| 5 | The piece in its place | 3 s | Wide | Nothing, or one line |
| 6 | Close, to the lens | 3 s | Waist-up | The close from Full Picture |

### Fit check (post 15 to 40 s)

| # | Shot | Raw | Camera |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hold the piece to the camera, say what it is | 6 s | Waist-up |
| 2 | Wear it, talk | 10 s | Waist-up |
| 3 | Full-length turn, slow | 8 s | Full-length, phone farther back |
| 4 | Detail: label, closure, flaw | 5 s | Close, 2x |

**Three ways:** pick one anchor piece, then shoot shot 3 three times, each in a different outfit, 8 seconds each. Say the anchor's one fact once, at the start.

### Click (post 10 to 20 s)

| # | Shot | Raw |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dark corner, hand reaches the switch | 3 s |
| 2 | Light comes on, at about 2 to 3 seconds in the finished clip | 4 s |
| 3 | Hold on the lit piece | 4 s |
| 4 | Flaw close-up, if there is one | 2 s |

For glass: a hand turns the piece on a lazy Susan; light moves through the facets. Shoot at 120 fps once.
For a dressed corner: film the bare shelf for 4 seconds, then the dressed shelf for 4 seconds from the same spot. Do not move the phone.

### Found it (post 30 to 60 s)

| # | Shot | Raw |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The carry-in: bags, boxes, the door | 5 s |
| 2 | Cleaning or steaming | 10 s |
| 3 | Measuring, with the number on screen | 8 s |
| 4 | The first look at the finished piece | 6 s |
| 5 | Packing: tissue, tape, the note card, the box | 15 s |

Blur address labels. Talk over it, or let the sounds carry it.

### Check before you move on

- Can you hear the hook clearly on earbuds?
- Is the flaw visible, and said?
- Is the text and the piece inside the box (section 6)?
- Is the lens clean, the exposure locked?

## 9. One shoot, 90 minutes

| Min | Block | What to film | Clips |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 to 15 | **Set** | Pick the corner. Clear the frame. Tape the floor. Light, then lock exposure. Mic on, 10-second test | none kept |
| 15 to 55 | **Wearables, 6 pieces** | Hold to camera 6 s, worn 10 s, full-length turn 8 s, detail 5 s | about 24 |
| 55 to 85 | **Objects, 6 pieces** | Hold and talk 20 s, one click or turn 8 s, flaw close-up 5 s, dressed corner 8 s | about 24 |
| 85 to 90 | **Talk** | Each hook twice. Each close once. Ten seconds of the quiet room | about 12 |

About 60 clips make 8 to 10 posts. Film some pieces both ways, talking and silent, and compare after a month. Copy the clips off the phone the same day.

## 10. Edit in Instagram Edits

Free, exports in 4K with no watermark, makes captions automatically. Needs iOS 16.3 or later.

1. **Import** the clips for one post. Drop the bad takes.
2. **Trim** to the first word and the last. Jump-cut the breaths, about every 3 to 5 seconds on talking clips.
3. **Hook text** on screen from the first frame, inside the safe box (section 6).
4. **Captions on.** Read them through and fix wrong words. Keep them inside the box.
5. **Audio.** Keep Hollie's voice. For a music bed, use Meta's Sound Collection (open Audio in the Reels camera on the real account): it is royalty free for commercial use. Keep it low under the voice. Skip CapCut's music; it is non-commercial only. For a Creative Commons track, credit the artist in the caption as the licence asks.
6. **Export clean:** no watermark, no black bars. Post the exported file; do not re-save it in another app.

## 11. Cover

One cover per post, 1080 x 1920, text and piece inside the safe box. Pick a frame where Hollie is holding the piece, or use a text card in cream (#f6efdf) on burgundy (#451c2b). A real photo of the real piece only; no AI imagery. No price, discount, "visit" or claim wording. Pink (#e5a6b4) works for accents on burgundy; never on white or cream.

---

## 12. Caption and post

- **Open with a searchable phrase**, then the facts: "Brass tulip lamp, 1980s."
- **Short (8 to 30 words):** keyword phrase. One feeling line. Piece number and link in bio.
- **Medium (31 to 60 words):** keyword phrase. Era, material, size. One honest flaw. Where the page is.
- **Gone edit:** "Gone." Piece. One line on where it came from. The next drop day.
- **Up to 5 hashtags**, niche ones only.
- **City or region tag only.**
- **Close** from Full Picture: the no-link version until pieces are listed.
- **Pace:** aim for 10 or more reels a month. Reply to comments within 7 days.

---

## 13. Before you post

- Nothing says visit, in store, stop by, DM to buy, claim, discount, code, offer or giveaway.
- No maker or "signed" unless it is marked.
- No safety claims on wiring or glass.
- Nothing identifying in the frame: street, house number, mail, labels.
- The flaw is shown.
- Captions are correct.
