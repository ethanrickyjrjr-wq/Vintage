# eBay drafts: paste-ready measurements, smoke-free line and packed weights (2026-11-12)

Measurements are taken from `docs/ebay-measurements-2026-11-12.md`. Widths that file marks (?) are **left out** because the photo can't be trusted for them. Re-tape them across the chest before adding.

**Smoke-free.** Ricky confirmed on 2026-11-12 that every piece comes from a non-smoking home. Add this line to every description, just above the shop footer:

> From a smoke-free, non-smoking home.

**Weights are estimates. Nothing was put on a scale.** Each one is the packed weight in a poly mailer (about 2 oz with a tissue sheet and thank-you card), set at the top of the usual range for that kind of garment and then rounded up again, as Ricky asked. HWV Parcel uses calculated USPS Ground Advantage, so a high weight means the buyer pays a little more postage instead of HWV losing money on the label. If a scale reading comes in lower, edit the figure down before listing.

Ranges checked against: [Shopify packaging weights](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/setup/packaging/packages-and-weights) (blouse 8–16 oz, jacket and dress pants 1–2 lb), [ParcelPath dress weights](https://parcelpath.com/shipping-terms-glossary/glossary-weights-and-measurements/how-much-does-a-dress-weigh/), [ParcelPath jeans weights](https://parcelpath.com/shipping-terms-glossary/glossary-weights-and-measurements/how-much-do-jeans-weigh/) (0.8–1.3 lb) and [ParcelPath jacket weights](https://parcelpath.com/shipping-terms-glossary/glossary-weights-and-measurements/how-much-does-a-jacket-weigh/).
