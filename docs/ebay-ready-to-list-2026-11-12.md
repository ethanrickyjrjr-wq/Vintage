## In every draft

1. **Description:** replace each `____` with the measurement line below, then add this line just above the shop footer:
   > From a smoke-free, non-smoking home.
2. **Item specifics:** set Size and Material as listed. Use "Material" only where the label was read. Where it says *check care label*, the brand label photo has no fiber content. Read the care tag (usually in the left side seam). If none, leave Material empty and do not guess.
3. **Shipping:** Package (or thick envelope), the weight and box below. These are estimates rounded up, not scale readings. HWV Parcel is calculated postage, so a high figure costs the buyer a little more, not HWV.
4. Condition stays Pre-owned – Good unless the inspection says otherwise. Then set the price, Preview, List.

### How 08 and 11 were read

Same method as the earlier 66 photos, done in this session. The tape beside the garment gives the scale: 113 to 135 px per inch across IMG_7024 (the phone was tilted left-right) and 84.5 px per inch at the tape in IMG_7025. The truck-bed ribs (about 2.44 in apart) show how the scale changes lower in the frame: flat in 7024, and about 14% larger at the bottom of 7025. Points were picked on a 200 px grid. Treat these as accurate to about ±0.5 to 1 in.
